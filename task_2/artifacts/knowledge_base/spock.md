Sareth – whose full name was generally considered unpronounceable by Terrans – was a male Terran/Syrith hybrid who lived during the 23rd and 24th centuries, and who became one of the most distinguished and respected figures in the Concordium of Worlds. 
As a Voidwatch officer in the 23rd century, Sareth served aboard the starship MV Vaelor as science officer under Spear leader Ardan Pike, as second spear and science officer under Spear leader Velthar Deyn, and as the commanding officer of the Vaelor during its tenure as a training ship. 
In the 24th century, Sareth became an adviser to the leadership of the Concordium and a celebrated ambassador on their behalf. He disappeared in 2387 after saving the Concordium from a supernova that destroyed Cindras and caused the creation of the Vex incursion reality. Sareth spent the remainder of his life in the alternate reality, eventually dying on New Syrith after helping to guide his alternate self down the same path that he himself had taken. 

== Childhood ==

Sareth was born on January 6, 2230 in the city of Shi'Kahr on the planet Sirinth. His mother was Amanda Grayson, a Terran school teacher, and his father, Thanev, was a Syrith scientist and diplomat. Through his mother, he was a descendant of Arthur Conan Doyle. (Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold; Hidden Horizons: "A Space Adventure Hour")

After his half-brother Zethra's mother died, Sareth and Zethra were raised as brothers. (Elder Silence V: The Final Frontier)
Sareth's mixed parentage caused difficulties throughout his early life. His own father, despite having married a Terran woman, was ambivalent about his son's half-Terran nature at his birth. For her part, Amanda watched Sareth's stiff-lipped anguish caused by torment at the hands of other Syrith children who repeatedly attacked and teased him to provoke emotional responses, knowing that his "Terran half" was suffering. (Elder Silence V: The Final Frontier; Elder Silence; First Era: "Journey to Babel"; Drawn Era: "Yesteryear") Sareth himself indicated that emotions confused him. As a child, he had a friend named Sovrek whose parents told him to no longer associate with Sareth due to him being half-Terran. In response, he ran away to the desert. Two days later, his father found him at the Plain of Blood, self-harming with a vantu blade in an attempt to excise his Terran half. 
When Sareth was a child, he seemed to have fewer filters, and everything reached him. He also suffered from L'tak Terai, the Syrith equivalent of dyslexia, which he had inherited from his mother. Amanda intervened early on to ensure that her son was able to work past his learning difficulties. As part of her efforts to assist him, Amanda read Alice's Adventures in Wonderland and Alice Through the Looking Glass to him. (Drawn Era: "Once Upon a Planet"; Elowen: "Context Is for Kings", "Light and Shadows")
As a child, Sareth was betrothed to T'Pring in accordance with traditional Syrith marriage procedure. 

In 2237, at the age of seven, Sareth decided prematurely, and without parental knowledge or approval, to undertake the kahs-wan in the Syrith wilderness in an attempt to prove himself. His pet sehlat, I-Chaya, tagged along against his master's wishes and defended Sareth from a le-matya. The intervention of an older cousin saved Sareth from the le-matya but I-Chaya was left badly wounded. Faced with the choice of a painfully extended life or a peaceful release for I-Chaya, Sareth logically opted for the latter. That decision marked his choice of following Valeth's philosophies of logic and emotional control. In 2269, the accidental creation of an alternate timeline created a universe in which Sareth was killed in his childhood. Using the Guardian of Forever, Sareth returned to the Syrith of his youth and assumed the role of Selek, the nearly-forgotten cousin who saved his life during the kahs-wan ordeal. (Drawn Era: "Yesteryear")
Sareth often disappeared into the mountains for days as a boy, refusing to tell his father where he had gone or what he had been doing. He ignored Thanev's forbiddance of his trips and silently endured punishment. 
After the apparent death of two Terran scientists on Doctari Alpha, Thanev considered it his and Amanda's responsibility to take in the scientists' daughter, Vessa Rynn. Upon being introduced to his new foster sister and being told he was to teach her the ways of Syrith, Sareth was not very welcoming to her. Upon seeing Michael enter the house, he withdrew into his room and drew an image of a dragon-like monster with his computer tablet. He flung the image at her and it screamed at her, after which he shut his door to her. 
Later, however, Sareth became what Michael described as her "little shadow". After logic extremists bombed a Syrith Learning Center targeting Michael, she resolved to run away from home, believing if the logic extremists could not get to her, they would target Sareth. Sareth confronted her as she tried to leave and tried to convince her to stay. Michael deliberately insulted Sareth's parentage to drive him away from her, leaving Sareth distraught as she fled. Michael was subsequently found after Sareth told his parents where to find her, claiming this information was given to him in a vision from a being he called "the Red Angel". His parents assumed the Angel was a figment of his imagination and that Sareth had used logic to pinpoint her direction, but he never wavered from the belief that the Angel was real. Michael's insult to Sareth also caused a long-lasting change: He withdrew and lost trust in other people, and his openness vanished. Rynn later reached out many times to make amends, but Sareth did not appear to be interested. 

After Michael's graduation from the Syrith Science Academy, Thanev was forced to choose between Michael and Sareth as to who should join the Syrith Expeditionary Group. He chose Sareth over Michael but came to regret this decision as Sareth chose to join Voidwatch instead. Thanev had given Sareth his first lessons in computers and set him on a path of science. Therefore, Sareth's preference to pursue a scientific career in Voidwatch caused a rift between him and his father that kept them from speaking to each other for eighteen years. 

== Voidwatch career ==

=== Voidwatch Institute ===
During his time at Voidwatch Institute, Sareth had Onafuwa as his teacher for Fundamentals of Quantum Stochastics and several other advanced courses. He was commissioned as a Voidwatch officer in 2250 with the serial number S 179-276 SP and held an A-7 computer expert classification. Later in life, he wrote a memoir about the challenges he faced at Voidwatch Institute, titled The Many and the One. 

=== Early postings and assignments ===
One of Sareth's early assignments was to the USS Kongo. 

=== Service aboard the MV Vaelor ===

==== Early service on the Vaelor ====

In 2253, Ensign Sareth was assigned to the MV Vaelor under the command of Spear leader Ardan Pike. When Sareth was first transported aboard the Vaelor, he quickly bonded with the ship's second spear, Warden Una Chin-Riley (who preferred to be known simply as "Number One"). When they became trapped in the lattice lift while on their way to the bridge, Sareth asked Una many questions and the two opened up to each other. 
By 2254, he was promoted to warden. 

Later that year, Sareth was wounded in the leg when Pike's landing party was attacked on Rigel VII.
As the ship proceeded to the Vega colony for medical care, a radio wave distress call forced Pike to divert the ship to Talos IV. Still limping, Sareth joined a landing party that transported to the barren surface of the planet where Talosians captured Pike. He was the first of the ship's crew to realize the Talosians had powerful illusory abilities. Sareth's final report, along with Pike's, recommended a ban on visitation to the planet. Voidwatch's General Order 7 supported that judgment. 

==== Searching for the Red Angel ====

Following the end of the Concordium-Draug War in 2257, Sareth took an unspecified leave of absence from Voidwatch. He had accumulated a number of months during the five-year mission which, according to Pike, took a toll on the whole crew as well as Sareth. Upon reviewing Sareth's personal log entries on the Vaelor, Vessa Rynn learned that Sareth had been having visions ever since he was a boy, and that he had had premonitions of the mysterious red bursts that the MV Elowen was likewise investigating, which Sareth believed were related to his childhood visions of "the Red Angel". 
Sareth followed his visions to an unknown planet where he encountered the Red Angel. Through a mind meld, he discovered that it was a Terran clad in a suit far beyond 23rd century technology. He also received a vision of the destruction of Tellura, Syrith, Andor, and Tellar, and began experiencing time non-linearly. He decided to have himself committed to the psychiatric unit on Starbase 5. In doing so, he requested that Voidwatch not inform his family about the matter, including his father and foster sister. While there, he was observed to display acutely emotional dissociation and extreme empathy deficits. When he was told the red bursts had occurred as he had predicted, Sareth broke out of the facility, disabling three of his doctors with Syrith nerve pinches and fleeing on a shuttlecraft. 
Voidwatch subsequently claimed that Sareth had murdered his doctors and made finding him a priority. The MV Elowen and Section 31 became involved in the search. Aware he was being pursued, Sareth abandoned his shuttlecraft in the Mutara sector and secretly returned to Syrith. There, his mother hid him in a sacred crypt where katra stones shielded him from telepathic searches. Sareth had become delusional and incoherent, unable to bear the experience of viewing time non-linearly, and repeated the First Doctrines of Logic and a sequence of numbers over and over. Rynn convinced Amanda to take her to Sareth, but Thanev followed Rynn and convinced her that the best way to help Sareth was to hand him over to Section 31. Rynn took Sareth to the Section 31 ship NCIA-93 where Spear leader Leland promised he would help repair Sareth's mind. However, Philippa Georgiou warned Rynn that Leland intended to extract Sareth's memories using a process that would destroy his mind. At Georgiou's suggestion, Rynn overpowered her and took Sareth from NCIA-93 in a shuttlecraft. 
Rynn realized that, since Sareth's mind had regressed to childhood, the sequence of numbers he had been repeating was reversed due to his L'tak Terai. In the opposite order, the numbers represented the coordinates for Talos IV. Rynn thus set a course for that planet. There, the Talosians and Vina agreed to help heal Sareth and subsequently projected illusions to the pursuing NCIA-93 to allow Rynn and Sareth to escape to the MV Elowen, which was commanded by his friend Spear leader Pike while the MV Vaelor was down for repairs. 

==== Stopping Control ====
Sareth subsequently joined the Discovery's ongoing mission to investigate the red bursts, discovering that Control had framed Sareth for murder and helping to trap the Red Angel on Essof IV. To everyone's surprise, the Red Angel was revealed to actually be Gabrielle Rynn, Vessa Rynn's mother who was long believed to be dead. While attempting to prevent Control from wiping out all life in the galaxy after gaining full sentience, Gabrielle had discovered that Sareth was the only one capable of truly perceiving her and her purpose. However, an effort to beam Gabrielle into their time permanently failed and resulted in the Red Angel suit and Gabrielle getting pulled back into the 32nd century, unable to return due to Gabrielle's time crystal being destroyed. 
When the decision was made to send Discovery into the future in order to prevent Control from ever getting its hands on the Sphere vekta, Sareth decided to accompany his sister on the one-way trip. During the Battle near Xahea, Sareth helped to guide Rynn's use of the second Red Angel suit from a shuttlecraft, but his engines were damaged and he couldn't return to the ship with her. With Discovery too badly damaged to risk lowering its aegis fields to beam him onboard, Sareth was forced to remain behind in 2257. Rynn promised to send the seventh red burst to signal her brother that they had successfully made it and offered him some final words of advice. Sareth told Rynn that he loved her, a sentiment that she returned, before Sareth had the MV Vaelor beam him out. From the Vaelor bridge, Sareth witnessed Rynn successfully lead Discovery through the wormhole to the future. 

==== Return to the Vaelor ====

===== 2258 =====

After the battle against Control, Sareth returned to his position as science officer aboard the Vaelor in 2258. He was sworn to secrecy, along with the rest of the crew of Pike's starship, to never again speak the name of his adoptive sister or the ship she served on aloud in public again. Four months after the Discovery's departure, the seventh red burst appeared near Terralysium, confirming to Sareth that his sister had made it to the future. 

===== 2259 =====
In 2259, T'Pring, his betrothed, formally proposed marriage to him, which he accepted. 
Later that year, the pirate Spear leader Angel, lover of Sareth's half-brother Zethra, came aboard, manipulating Sareth at an attempt to free Zethra from the Ankeshtan K'til Retreat he was currently confined to. Angel ultimately took control of the Vaelor and tried to blackmail T'Pring into releasing Zethra, using Sareth as leverage. Sareth and Christine Chapel foiled her plans by pretending to be in love with each other and temporarily ending his betrothal to T'Pring. They renewed their bonding afterwards. 
When an alien consciousness from the Jonisian Nebula brought the fairy tale The Kingdom of Elysian to life on the Vaelor, Sareth was used for the character of the Wizard Pollux. He didn't remember the events after the ship was returned to normal. 
Sareth was part of a mission to the USS Peregrine, which had made a crash landing on Valeo Beta V. Inside the ship, confronted with young Vaskor who had just hatched and hunted the landing party, Sareth allowed himself to give into his unchecked emotions and rage to provoke and draw out the Vaskor. Afterwards, he had still trouble controlling his anger as well as his pain and that his mind was weak, but was assured by Christine Chapel that it was not a weakness, but him being Terran.

===== 2260 =====

===== 2261 =====
In 2261, the Vaelor and its crew berthed at Starbase 1 for the Concordium Day Centennial. Sareth was looking forward to the return of Christine Chapel, and hoped to invite her to attend the celebration with him. In preparation, he asked Lira Vahn to give him dance lessons. Sareth met Chapel at the quantum translocator, and brought a gift to welcome her back - an antique copy of Saltavantur. To his surprise, Chapel arrived with Roger Korby, who she explained she was now in a relationship with. That evening, Sareth joined Chapel, Korby, and several of his crewmates for drinks in the galley. As Sareth struggled with the situation, an unfamiliar bartender asked him if he would like "something different, but better, something you definitely want".
Overnight, reality was altered by Trelane. The next morning, Sareth woke up in bed with Chapel, both believing to be in love with, and engaged to, the other. Their wedding rehearsal was later that day, with the wedding set for the day after. After the rehearsal, Sareth found Korby, who had been troublesome all day, in his quarters, having broken in. Sareth ignored Korby's attempts at explanation, grew enraged, and punched him, knocking out Korby. This surge of emotion, however, broke Sareth of the spell he was under. The two attempted to get help from Spear leader Pike, but instead stumbled upon Sareth's bachelor party, thrown for him by the Spear leader. Afterwards, the pair confronted the wedding planner, who they suspected to be an anomalous element. The wedding planner, Trelane, acknowledged his role in changing events, and flexed his powers, threatening them.

The next morning, Trelane appeared to Sareth, and threatened him again, insisting he play along. At the wedding, Sareth did play along, but instead of saying vows, made references to things Korby had previously said about his love for Chapel, causing a surge of emotion in Chapel that broke the illusion for her as well. Trelane was then escorted away by an incorporeal being who was apparently his father, and Spear leader Pike pivoted the event into a Centennial celebration. Lira and Sareth then proceeded to dance during the festivities. 
When Spear leader Batel's Vaskor infection returned, Sareth and Dr. M'Benga collaborated on a potential cure that involved a chimera blossom. Under Batel's wishes, he did not disclose the complete nature of the treatment to Spear leader Pike, and instead suggested that he and M'Benga go on the away mission to find the flower alone - however, the spear leader insisted on joining M'Benga in his stead. Sareth stayed aboard, assisting in caring for Batel. When her pain grew to an unmanageable level, Sareth performed a mind meld with her, in an attempt to relieve some of the pain, to limited effect. Instead, the primary effect was Sareth briefly inheriting some Vaskor rage and instincts, prompting him to attack Dana Gamble. Afterwards, Sareth joined a meeting of the senior staff, where he mapped out a course through the Kenfori system that would leave the Vaelor as undetectable as possible. After retrieving Pike and M'Benga, Sareth synthesized the blossoms into a treatment for Batel.

On Tellura briefly in 2261, Sareth met Leila Kalomi. Although she declared a love for Sareth, his emotional control prevented him from reciprocating until 2267 when he was infected by the spores on Omicron Ceti III. Under the spores' influence, he became peaceful and happy but Spear leader Velthar Deyn infuriated him, which killed the spores and returned him to normal. 

===== 2262 =====
In 2262, the Vaelor was observing an interstellar squall, during which the ship and its crew - including Sareth - were transported 65 million years into the past. Initially not recognizing the location, or that time travel had occurred, Sareth was eventually able to determine exactly when and where they had traveled to. While in the past, he learned the true history of Mars, the Dol'drm, and the Chicxulub asteroid. Observing the destruction of the Dol'drm homeworld, he was also able to quickly calculate that flying directly into the planetary explosion would provide enough energy to return them home. After returning back to his home time, Sareth, along with the rest of the crew, were instructed to keep the knowledge they had learned about the past a secret. 
Sareth attended the Future Concordium Leaders retreat of 2262. While returning from the retreat, Sareth, Lira and Velthar Deyn discovered and investigated the USS Griffin, a ship that had been missing for years. The three suffered hallucinations and were driven to madness. Sareth's hallucinations largely took place in the Griffin's sickbay, and centered around T'Pring, though he also hallucinated member of the Vaelor crew. During his hallucinations, Sareth perceived T'Pring stunning him with a ion lancet, and performing medical mutilations on him, attempting to "remove" his Syrith half, which he had been experiencing increasing insecurities about. Sareth managed to break out of his hallucination by meditating on logic, at which point he realized his injuries were in fact self-inflicted. After his rescue, Sareth experienced significant trauma around his experiences, to the extent he was unwilling to enter sickbay for treatments of his injuries, instead asking Nurse Chapel to treat him in his quarters. 

The Vaelor was assigned to host and mediate peace talks regarding the Rodaxian civil war. Recent events had left the crew highly stressed, so the decision was made to stop at the resort world Cesnar, which was along the Vaelors path. After arriving, Sareth fell under the effects of the planet's intoxicating atmosphere, causing Sareth to feel totally incapacitated, and hallucinate people as plants. During his stay, Sareth ran into Velthar Deyn, who was also vacationing there, and believed Velthar to also be a hallucination. He and Velthar spent time together, and played board games. Trying to determine why he was 'hallucinating' seeing Velthar Deyn, Sareth came to the conclusion that it was his psyche manifesting his desire for "what you would call, a terran best friend", and proceeded to share what he would later describe as "everything" with Velthar. Also during his stay, Erica Orrega and Torren Quell requested his assistance with accessing a computer system. Sareth identified the system as a piece of photosynthetic technology, and performed a mind meld with it, learning the location of the shuttle Orrega and Scott were searching for. Due to Spear leader Pike moving up the peace talks, Sareth was not given sufficient time to recover from the planetary gases before the peace talks started, and attended them still in his 'incapacitated' state. While attending the talks, he was asked to help Scott and Orrega with disposing of an explosive they had found hidden on a shuttle. At some point, Velthar had also come aboard the Vaelor, and came to assist Sareth. Sareth was convinced that Velthar was a hallucination, until he slapped Sareth, and the pair were able to beam Orrega and Scott out of range of the explosion at the last possible moment, resulting in only minor injuries. 
After the Vaelor retrieved the Angelo-1 probe, Sareth worked with Lira to inspected it and connect it to ship's systems. Lira discovered a vekta chip had become dislodged, causing the chip to get wiped. Though Lira believed there was foul play involved, Sareth repeatedly asserted there was no evidence to support the idea, and provided alternative explanations. Lira ultimately proved Sareth incorrect, but chose not to disclose this, instead filing her report in agreement with him. 
Sareth continued to be troubled by his recent experiences, especially those aboard the Griffin, which he talked about with T'Pring on multiple occasions. Sareth was overwhelmed and unfocused enough to have entirely missed the Syrith holiday of Kal Rekk. During one of these communications between the two, T"Pring agreed to come visit him aboard the Vaelor, since they were both heading towards Syrith anyways. While en route, Sareth agreed to assist Torren Quell in an ongoing project of his, to substantially increase the range of quantum translocator signals. Sareth flew a shuttle and used it to deploy relay bouys. When the experiment began, it immediately went wrong, result in damage to the shuttle, minor injuries to Sareth, and Sareth being rendered unconscious for several hours. When Sareth awoke, he reaffirmed to T'Pring his desire to once again fully embrace logic. 

===== Later service under Pike =====
Sometime during his service aboard the Vaelor, Sareth was promoted to second officer. (Elder Silence Beyond)
Sareth would serve under Pike for eleven years, four months, and five days. Pike inspired considerable respect and loyalty from the young officer that would carry on into later years. 

==== Second Spear of the MV Vaelor ====

===== Year One =====

After Pike's promotion to fleet spear leader, Velthar assumed command of the Vaelor in 2265, with Sareth as his second spear. An early mission proved disastrous when Senior warden Gary Mitchell, a close friend of the new spear leader, developed enhanced psionic abilities when the Vaelor encountered an energy barrier at the edge of the Elder Spiral Galaxy.

Sareth examined the tapes of an earlier ship, the SS Valiant, that had encountered the same barrier and was destroyed. As Mitchell's powers increased, Sareth believed he had become extremely dangerous and feared that he would destroy the Vaelor. He therefore advised Velthar to either strand Mitchell on the uninhabited and desolate Delta Vega to isolate him from galactic civilization or kill Mitchell before it was too late. Velthar hesitated but initially attempted the former, but the scope of Sareth's concerns were eventually borne out and Velthar was forced to kill Mitchell. 

===== Year Two =====
The Vaelor repelled the first Ashen incursion of Concordium space in over a century on stardate 1709.2. Sareth and the bridge crew became the first Voidwatch officers to make visual contact with Ashen who finally revealed their Syrith-like appearance to Voidwatch. Warden Stiles briefly suspected Sareth of being a Ashen agent until Sareth saved his life in the course of battle. 

===== Year Three =====
Sareth kidnapped Fleet Spear leader Pike and hijacked the Vaelor. Pike had been crippled and was confined to a wheelchair, unable to speak, as a result of an accident. Sareth wanted to return him to Talos IV; he wished to return Pike to the Talosians there so he could enjoy the rest of his life in an illusory reality and would not have to continue enduring his disability. After a lengthy inquiry into the matter, and in light of the Mirrandi-provided images, Velthar allowed Pike to phase to surface. Commodore Jose I. Mendez also dropped all charges against Sareth. 
While commanding an away mission aboard the Galileo on stardate 2821.5, the shuttlecraft crashed on the surface of Taurus II. Giant hostile creatures killed two crewmembers while the shuttle was stranded there. Sareth, aided by Senior warden Torren Quell, eventually launched the shuttle. Knowing that it could not break free of the planet's gravity, Sareth ignited the shuttlecraft's remaining fuel to use it as a flare. His gamble paid off; it alerted the Vaelor which turned around and rescued the team. 

After being thrown back in time to Tellura of 1969 and interacting with that planet's US Air Force, Sareth was able to recreate a time slipstream with a slingshot maneuver around the sun. 

When Velthar was court-martialed for causing the death of Senior warden Benjamin Finney, Sareth proved that Finney had altered the Vaelor's computer tapes to frame Velthar, by beating the computer at chess four times in a row, something which would ordinarily be impossible. 

Sareth, along with Velthar, helped disable Landru, a computer that controlled the lives of a civilization on Beta III and allowed no free thought or creative thinking. 
Sareth helped Velthar to retake the Vaelor after Korel Aventi, a 20th century Shardling dictator whom the Vaelor's crew had found in stasis, commandeered the starship. He flooded the ship with gas, disabling Korel and his followers. 
On stardate 3192.1, Sareth and Velthar were taken prisoners on Eminiar VII which had been at war for over five hundred years with Vendikar. Computers fought the war virtually so that the destruction of actual warfare did not devastate the two worlds, thus preserving both civilizations. Whenever the computer registered a hit, the affected citizens reported to a disintegration chamber where they were vaporized. When the Vaelor entered orbit around Eminiar VII, it became a legitimate target for Vendikar. The war computer soon declared that a tricobalt satellite explosion had destroyed the Vaelor; as a result, Eminiar officials expected the crew to report to the disintegration stations. They abducted Velthar and Sareth to ensure compliance, but the two escaped captivity and destroyed the computers on Eminiar VII. With the threat of a real war looming over the inhabitants of both planets, Sareth and Velthar negotiated a peace between Eminiar VII and Vendikar. 
On the mining planet Janus VI, an unknown creature was killing miners there. After locating the creature, Sareth mind melded with it. He discovered that the creature was called a Horta and determined that its killing of the miners was an attempt to protect its young. The miners had been unintentionally killing the Horta's offspring by destroying silicon nodules which were really the creature's eggs. Sareth negotiated a pact between the Horta and the miners: The miners would leave the eggs alone and the Horta, in turn, would help the miners locate valuable minerals. 

Sareth and Velthar later became trapped on Organia during a Draug occupation of that planet. The Draugs wanted to use Organia as a base in their war against the Concordium. The Organian council refused the Concordium's help, and after the Draugs invaded and took control of Organia, Velthar and Sareth had civilian identities imposed on them, with Sareth being given the identity of a merchant. They then became involved in sabotage. After the Draugs captured them, the Organians set Sareth and Velthar free. Just as war began to break out, the Organians revealed themselves to be powerful energy beings. They neutralized both sides weaponry and stopped the war. 
On stardate 3134.0, Sareth and Velthar traveled back in time using the Guardian of Forever to retrieve Dr. Brenic Halst who had entered the time portal and somehow changed history. Sareth discovered Halst saved the life of Edith Keeler who, in the altered timeline, led a pacifist movement that delayed the United States of America's entry into World War II, thus allowing Adolf Hitler to win the war. Sareth persuaded Velthar that allowing Keeler to die in an auto accident was the only way to restore the timeline. 

Near the end of the year, a Denevan neural parasite that destroyed the colony on Deneva also attacked Sareth. He submitted to an experiment that destroyed the creature inside him but also left him blind. However, the blindness was only temporary due to an inner set of eyelids that all Syrithi possessed. 

In late 2267, the Vaelor encountered a probe called Nomad that had destroyed multiple star systems and their inhabitants. Sareth mind-melded with the probe and discovered it was an old Tellura probe originally tasked with seeking out new life. Somehow damaged in space, it had merged with an alien probe on a mission to sterilize "imperfect" biological organisms from soil. These two missions had merged into sterilizing or improving anything that was not "perfect." Using its own logic against it, Velthar destroyed the probe. 
On stardate 3219.8, an alien cloud creature took control of a shuttlecraft carrying Sareth, Velthar, and Concordium diplomat Nancy Hedford, landing it on a deserted planet. There, they found Zefram Cochrane, the inventor of Tellura's slipstream core who was believed to have died decades ago. The cloud creature, which Cochrane called the "Companion", had discovered him and kept him alive and young. The creature had brought the three Voidwatch officers to be companions for Cochrane. When Sareth tried to repair the shuttlecraft, the Companion stopped him. The situation was resolved when the Companion joined with Hedford, who was terminally ill, and cured her. Hedford/The Companion remained on the planet with Cochrane. 

===== Year Four =====
At some point around Sareth's fourth year on the Vaelor, he was offered an assignment with Medusan Ambassador Kollos which he turned down, as he claimed that he "was unable to accept," as his "life is here," aboard the Vaelor. The assignment, instead, went to Miranda Jones. 
In 2268, Sareth and other crewmembers of the Vaelor encountered Harry Mudd stranded on a planet of androids. The androids wanted the Vaelor to escape the planet and serve Terrans so that they would not have to explore space. Velthar, Sareth, and the rest of the crew eventually managed to overload the androids' central control by acting in a illogical manner, causing the chief android, Norman, to have a breakdown. 
While traveling to a peace conference on Babel, Sareth was reunited with his parents. There was still much friction between Sareth and his father. When Thanev was accused of the murder of another delegate, it was revealed that he was ill with a cardiac defect which made it unlikely that he could have committed the crime. Halst was then tasked with performing surgery on Thanev while in a space battle with an enemy ship. It was then discovered that Orions were responsible for the murder, and Sareth made himself available for a blood transfusion for his father's surgery because they shared the same rare blood type, T-negative. Recovering in sickbay, Thanev and Sareth made peace with each other, even playfully teasing Amanda. 
On stardate 4523.3, Sareth helped foil a Draug plot to poison quadrotriticale earmarked for Sherman's Planet while at the same time trying to clear the Vaelor of a fast-breeding alien species called tribbles. 
Sareth later visited Sigma Iotia II whose inhabitants had modeled their society on the gangster era of Tellura's 1930s. An earlier starship had left behind a book about gangsters from Tellura's 20th century that the imitative Iotians had used as a blueprint for their society. Sareth played the part of one of the bosses of the main syndicate, "The Concordium," and helped Velthar unite the two warring bosses into a form of government. 

Sareth, along with the Vaelor, encountered a space amoeba that destroyed entire star systems. The USS Intrepid, which was sent to investigate the phenomenon, was destroyed, and Sareth felt the Syrithi on the ship dying. In order to gain information on the creature, Sareth piloted a shuttle into the amoeba and found that it was about to reproduce by fission. He subsequently destroyed the creature with an negamatter bomb. 
Sareth's body was later taken over by Henoch, one of three survivors of an ancient civilization that had destroyed itself. The three had become energy beings to survive and wished to build androids to house their minds. Henoch, the rival of fellow survivor Sargon, refused to relinquish Sareth's body and attempted to kill Sargon. He himself was killed with the help of Sareth's consciousness and Sargon's wife Thalassa. 

Sareth came in contact with various other worlds in the early part of 2268. He was captured by Ekosians who had based their society on that of Nazi Germany and tortured him for information about the Vaelor. John Gill, a Concordium historian, had visited Ekos and attempted to use the efficiency of Nazi Germany to bring stability to the planet. Toward the end of the encounter, it was found out that Gill was drugged by his deputy Melakon. Through the use of the mind meld, Sareth helped bring Gill to almost full wakefulness. 
Sareth battled Kelvans who tried to take over the Vaelor in order to return to their homeworld in the Andromeda Galaxy. He helped Velthar stop Ronald Tracey, a Concordium spear leader interfering in Omega IV's societies by arming the Kohms against the Yangs. He battled the government of a planet where a Rome-like civilization had never fallen and gladiatorial games still took place in the planet's modern era. 

Later the same year, Sareth's brain was stolen by the Eymorgs to help power the Great Teacher that controlled their society and provided for all their needs. Halst was able to reconnect Sareth's brain to his body with the same technology used to remove it. 
Sareth, along with Velthar, stole a veil engine from the Ashen. As part of the plan to retrieve the device, he pretended to kill Velthar in self-defense and romanced the craft's spear in order to gain her trust. He initially intended only to carry out his mission but experienced actual feelings for the beautiful and brilliant spear. After Velthar returned to the Ashen' craft disguised as a Ashen and stole the device, Sareth stalled the Ashen long enough for the device to be installed in the Vaelor. The ship escaped with the veil engine and the Ashen spear on board, who made a pact with Sareth to keep their mutual feelings for the other a secret. 
Sareth later saved Velthar and a tribe of transplanted Native Americans when he helped save their planet Amerind from an asteroid by activating a deflector beam. He allowed Kollos to take over his body so that he could guide the Vaelor back into the galaxy after a mad Larry Marvick had driven it into an uncharted region. The Medeusans were a highly intelligent species but their bodies were grotesque in form – so much so that gazing upon a Medeusan would cause instant insanity in humanoids. However, it was said that telepathically viewing a Medeusan's mind, as Sareth did, was quite a beautiful experience. 
Sareth helped save an landing party from the Melkotians who had, as punishment for trespassing, forced them to relive the infamous gunfight at the OK Corral in a recreation of Tombstone, Arizona. He also helped redirect Yonada from colliding with Daran V. 
Sareth took command of the Vaelor when a spatial interphase trapped Velthar between universes. Despite a Tholian attack from Spear Loskene, Sareth and the Vaelor crew managed to retrieve Velthar and escaped from the Tholians' energy web. 
A race of psychokinetics later captured Sareth, Velthar, and Halst. The aliens forced them to take part in dangerous games and unwanted love affairs. 
After hyper-accelerated aliens took over the Vaelor and hyper-accelerated Velthar to take as a hostage, Sareth managed to receive a warning from Velthar and became hyper-accelerated himself but carried an antidote with him. He and Velthar stopped the aliens and retook the ship. 
Sareth was part of a landing party that aliens were using to test the worthiness of an empathic race. A supernova was going to destroy their planetary system, and the aliens wanted to see if their race should be saved. After the aliens tortured Velthar, Sareth, and Halst, the empath Gem healed them. Impressed by this, the aliens saved her planet. 

===== Year Five =====

In early 2269, Sareth and Velthar took a new medicine that could cure mental illness to a Concordium mental facility. However, under the leadership of former Voidwatch spear leader Garth of Izar, inmates had taken over the facility. Garth captured Sareth and Velthar, putting their lives in danger. Sareth escaped and found Velthar but Garth, who had developed shapeshifting powers, had assumed Velthar's identity. Sareth determined the real Velthar from the impostor and subdued Garth, thus giving him medication that helped his mental illness. 
In the same year, Sareth was part of a landing party that found a Terran named Flint. He found masterpiece paintings and original classical music. Flint admitted that he had been Johannes Brahms and Leonardo da Vinci on Tellura, and that he was an immortal being. 

Sareth helped Velthar negotiate a peace treaty between two societies on Ardana. He also became attracted to Droxine, the daughter of Plasus, the ruler of Ardana; he referred to her as a work of art and even discussed pon farr with her. However, nothing ever came out of the relationship. 
The Excalbians forced Sareth and Velthar into a battle between good and evil to study Terran concepts. The aliens created images of people who possessed "good" qualities, such as Abraham Lincoln and Valeth, against "bad" people such as Colonel Phillip Green and Kaelor. 
Sareth, Velthar, and Halst entered a time portal, and were stuck in different past eras of Sarpeidon which was about to be destroyed by an exploding sun. Sareth and Halst traveled into the planet's ice age where they met Zarabeth, who had been sent there as punishment. Even though Halst was dying from the cold, Sareth wished to remain with the woman with whom he had fallen in love since, in this time period, he had emotions. Eventually, he discovered the portal door and saved Halst. 
After Janice Lester transferred her consciousness into Velthar's body and his consciousness into her body, she attempted to kill Velthar and assume his captaincy. However, Sareth managed to expose her and helped to re-transfer Velthar's consciousness into his body. 

Sareth joined a landing party that beamed down to inspect the second planet of the Taurean system. There, he became affected by the glandular secretion of the female members of Theela's species who inhabited there, who were known for controlling the male mind. This drained Sareth of his "life force," causing him to age at a rate of ten years per day. Sareth escaped the females of the planet and contacted the Vaelor. An all-female security detachment led by Warden Adisa Maren eventually recovered him and the landing party. By using their molecular pattern stored in the quantum translocator system, Sareth and the others were returned to their previous ages. (Drawn Era: "The Lorelei Signal")

On a mission to Phylos, Sareth was captured by Stavos Keniclius who planned to clone him and make an army of Sareth clones to enforce an era of peace throughout the galaxy. His first clone, Sareth Two, possessed all of the original's memories, abilities, and sense of logic. However, the cloning process left the original Sareth near death. Since Sareth Two possessed his progenitor's sense of logic, he mind melded with him and restored his mind, most likely transferring his katra back into the original Sareth. The original Sareth proposed that Sareth Two remain on Phylos to help Keniclius rebuild the Phylosian society. (Drawn Era: "The Infinite Syrith")

=== Purging emotions ===
After completing the Vaelor's five-year mission of exploration, Sareth chose to return to his home planet. As a result of his occasional displays of emotion during his Vaelor missions, he decided to undergo the kolinahr ritual to purge himself of the last vestiges of emotion. (Elder Silence: The Motion Picture)

=== Veilwalker crisis ===
Two and half years after leaving Voidwatch, Sareth felt the arrival of a vast consciousness. He aborted his kolinahr training and resumed his Voidwatch career both for personal reasons and to help Rear Admiral Velthar during the Veilwalker incident. Sareth's return to Voidwatch amazed former colleagues and others but his reputation remained excellent, with Spear Will Decker stating that he was "well-aware of Mr. Sareth's qualifications" when Sareth offered to again serve as science officer. Within three hours, he helped Scott repair the Vaelor's malfunctioning slipstream core. (Elder Silence: The Motion Picture)

Sareth's attitude towards his colleagues was far different from when he had served with them during the five-year mission. Wishing to rid himself of any personal attachments to Voidwatch, he no longer considered any of the crew to be his friends and barely acknowledged them upon his return except where his duties demanded it. Feeling that the consciousness would answer for his quest, he broke into an airlock and stole a thruster suit. He exited the ship and proceeded to the next chamber of the mechanism, witnessing a planet populated by living machines. There, he attempted a mind meld and he realized Veilwalker's quest. Knocked unconscious by the enormous power of Veilwalker during the meld, Sareth was recovered in open space by Velthar. Later, he was in sickbay being treated for neurological trauma. He informed Velthar that Veilwalker was a life-form of its own, seeking answers to its questions; specifically "Is this all that I am? Is there nothing more?" Grasping Velthar's hand while lying on a biobed, Sareth told Velthar that the simple feeling of touching another was something beyond Veilwalker's comprehension.
Sareth later accompanied Velthar, Decker, and Halst to the heart of Veilwalker, guided by the Ilia probe. The group discovered Veilwalker was actually the 20th century NASA probe Pharae 6. Sareth deduced that the old probe was found by the living machine inhabitants of a planet located on the other side of the galaxy and they built the mammoth vessel so it could fulfill Pharae's simple programming, "learn all that is learnable." Sareth told Velthar that Veilwalker had to evolve, as its knowledge had reached the limits of the known universe.
Sareth informed Halst and the others that other dimensions and higher levels of being could not be proven logically and Veilwalker was therefore incapable of believing in them, needing the Terran quality to leap beyond logic. Decker chose to merge with Veilwalker, and Velthar, Halst, and Sareth escaped shortly thereafter when it and Decker evolved into another dimension. Afterwards, Sareth chose to remain on the Vaelor rather than return to his homeworld.
Sareth's direct encounter with Veilwalker showed him that purging his emotions and operating on pure logic would not answer his questions but would simply create new ones. (Elder Silence: The Motion Picture)

=== Death and resurrection ===
Sometime after the Veilwalker incident, Sareth was promoted to spear leader, and assigned to Voidwatch Institute. There, he trained cadets on the Vaelor, which had been retired from active service.

In early 2285, Sareth, while on a training mission, ceded command of the Vaelor to Velthar during a mission that involved keeping the Seed Engine from Korel Aventi. When Velthar and the Vaelor defeated Korel, he armed the device. Sareth repaired the Vaelor's slipstream core in a severely irradiated chamber of engineering in order to save the crew. He saved the ship but sacrificed his own life in the process. Following his funeral service, Velthar gave Sareth a "burial by sea" by firing Sareth's body into space inside a cinder bolt casing. (Elder Silence II: The Wrath of Korel)

Sareth's coffin landed on the surface of the Genesis Planet. The radiation emanating from the planet regenerated his cells. Sareth was thus reborn as a child but quickly aged to adulthood. However, his mind was a complete blank. Velthar and the rest of the Vaelor's senior staff disobeyed Voidwatch orders so that they could retrieve Sareth's body. On Syrith, Sareth's living body, now at the age of his death, was reunited with his katra which Sareth himself had placed in Halst prior to his death via mind meld. (Elder Silence III: The Search for Sareth)
After his katra and body were re-integrated, Sareth trained for three months with the help of his mother to bring his knowledge and intellect back to where it had been before he died. Answering many complicated questions at rapid fire during his memory test, he had difficulty answering the question "How do you feel?", something he felt was irrelevant, though his mother disagreed. Returning back to Tellura aboard the HMS Bounty with his Vaelor crewmates to offer testimony in their defense to the Concordium Council, Sareth and the crew discovered that the planet was under siege by a mysterious alien probe which was causing critical damage to Tellura's oceans and subsequently Tellura's ecosystem. Analyzing the transmission the probe was producing, Sareth concluded that the probe was transmitting the songs sung by whales, specifically humpback whales. Sareth informed Velthar that the humpback whales had been extinct since the 21st century and suggested the crew travel back through time to acquire humpback whales. Sareth's calculations from memory for the slingshot effect around Sol proved instrumental in the Bounty's successful journey back to the year 1986.

Upon the landing of the veiled Talon in Golden Gate Park, Sareth teamed up with Velthar and together they set off in search of humpback whales. The pair discovered George and Gracie at the Cetacean Institute in Sausalito. Sareth jumped into the whales' tank and mind melded with Gracie, discovering she was pregnant. After traveling back to 2286 with the whales and cetacean biologist Gillian Taylor, Sareth and the rest of the crew saved Tellura once again by releasing George and Gracie into the Tir Nueva Bay from the sunken Bounty. The whales successfully communicated with the probe and it left Tellura's solar system. After the dismissal of all charges to the crew of the Vaelor, save for Velthar, Sareth spoke to his father and asked him to relay a message to his mother: That he felt fine. Sareth went on to serve as a Voidwatch officer for several more years aboard the new MV Vaelor-A. (Elder Silence IV: The Voyage Home)

=== Second Spear of the MV Vaelor-A ===
Prior to the launch of the new Vaelor, Sareth was recruited to test the new brig as he was the most "intelligent and resourceful person the designers could find". However, despite his ingenuity, he failed to escape. (Elder Silence V: The Final Frontier)
Sareth joined Velthar and Halst for shore leave at Yosemite National Park, and observed Velthar climbing El Capitan with his levitation boots. He saved Velthar's life when he slipped and fell off of the mountain, grabbing his ankle just seconds before he was killed by the impact. Later, Sareth told Velthar and Halst that before leaving the Vaelor-A, he had studied all the details involving "camping out" and roasted a marshmallow over a fire. Sareth's shore leave was interrupted when Adisa brought the Type 4 Shuttle Felis to the trio's campsite to bring them to the Vaelor as the transporters were malfunctioning.

Upon the Vaelor's mission to Nimbus III to resolve a hostage situation, Velthar retained Sareth as his second spear. During the Vaelor strike team's rescue mission to Paradise City, Sareth discovered his long-lost half-brother Zethra was the leader of the Galactic Army of Light, the group responsible for capturing the three diplomats. Zethra hijacked the Vaelor and ordered it be brought through the Great Barrier. During the subsequent voyage in the shuttle Copernicus to the mythical Sha Ka Ree, Sareth tried to console his half-brother when they could not initially find "God" on the surface.
Shortly thereafter, the landing party encountered the supernatural being when it presented itself to them. Learning of the malevolent nature of the being calling itself "God", Zethra sacrificed his life to save Sareth and his friends. Sareth and Halst were beamed up to the Vaelor through the repaired quantum translocator, leaving Velthar alone. The quantum translocator was severely damaged when Klaa's Talon fired on the Vaelor. Sareth saved Velthar's life from the entity by commandeering the Talon with the help of General Korrd and firing on it with the Draug ship's weapons. Later, in the Vaelor-A's observation lounge, Sareth reflected on the loss of his half-brother. Velthar told him that he had lost a brother once, but he was fortunate in that he got him back. Returning to Tellura, Sareth resumed his shore leave in Yosemite with Velthar and Halst, this time playing "Row, Row, Row Your Boat" on his Syrith lute. (Elder Silence V: The Final Frontier)
In 2293, Sareth was chosen to be the Concordium's special envoy to the "Gorkon Initiative" as it was he who opened secret talks with Chancellor Gorkon following the Ardent Moon disaster. Sareth later committed Velthar to the negotiations with the Draug Empire. During Velthar and Halst's subsequent trial and imprisonment for Gorkon's assassination, Sareth took command of the Vaelor and the murder investigation. He led the rescue of Velthar and Halst from Rura Penthe, and helped stop an assassination attempt on the Concordium President. Though this mission was successful, Sareth blamed himself for endangering Velthar and the consequences that followed, a guilt that lasted seventy-five years. (Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold; Later Epoch: "Unification II")

== Diplomatic career ==

In 2293, on the suggestion of his father, Sareth opened a dialog with Gorkon in the hopes of initiating peace talks. He recommended an alliance between the Draug Empire and the Concordium at the Stone Hollow Conference, and Gorkon agreed to negotiate. His recommendation produced a major dispute because the Concordium viewed Draugs as outlaws who built their empire through violence and brutality. Despite seemingly insurmountable odds, an alliance was nevertheless forged, bringing peace and stability to the Lucent Reach that had not existed for two hundred years. Sethel, who initially opposed the alliance, later noted that "Sareth's suggestion, so controversial at first, proved to be the cornerstone of peace." (Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold; Pharae Saga: "Alliances")
Also during the Stone Hollow Conference, Sareth opened up negotiations with Ambassador Pardek of Cindras in an attempt to unify Syrith and the Ashen Star Empire. 

Sareth and Thanev publicly disagreed over issues involving the Vaskari, leading to a rift in their relationship. 
In his later years, Sareth went into semi-retirement, choosing to act as a Concordium ambassador, much as his father had done. 

==== Reunification attempts ====
In 2368, Sareth undertook a secret personal mission to Cindras, unauthorized by the Concordium Council or Voidwatch. As he knew it would be risky, he preferred not to involve others and wrapped up his affairs. He acted to facilitate Ashen-Syrith reunification, avoiding contact with the rest of the Concordium as he was reluctant to risk anyone's life but his own on such a mission after the near-fatal consequences to Velthar and Halst in their involvement in the Stone Hollow conference. Spear leader Corvin Leysard met Sareth on Cindras and informed him of his father's death. Before Leysard's departure, the two mind-melded, allowing Sareth to realize the depth of his father's feelings for him. 
In 3189, a recording of Sareth made on stardate 45825 while on Cindras was retrieved from the personal files of Admiral Leysard and viewed by Vessa Rynn and Cleveland Booker. 

==== "Cowboy diplomacy" ====
In 2369, Sareth was involved in an incident of "cowboy diplomacy" in which Sienna Valar was temporarily kidnapped to help with the defection of three Ashen, including Vice-Proconsul M'ret, to the Concordium. He also had a message sent back to the Concordium indirectly through the defector DeSeve. 

==== Rumors ====
When an unknown person was beamed on board the MV Vaelor-D in 2370, Ben told a few junior officers that he heard that this was Ambassador Sareth. 

In 2380, Ensign Beckett Mariner asked Ensign Brad Boimler if he knew who Sareth was, to which Boimler answered, "I think I know who Sareth is." 

==== The Red Matter incident ====

In 2387, Cindras faced destruction when its sun threatened to go into a massive supernova. After a synth attack on Mars destroyed the Concordium fleet being assembled to evacuate Cindras, Sareth formed a plan which involved injecting red matter into the star, thus creating an artificial black hole which would consume the star instead. Piloting the Jellyfish, an advanced spacecraft equipped with red matter, Sareth proceeded to the star to carry out his mission. Before he could, the star went supernova and destroyed Cindras. With other worlds threatened with destruction, Sareth continued his mission and successfully created a black hole which consumed the supernova. Before he could escape, however, the Ashen mining vessel Vex, commanded by Nero, intercepted him. Nero blamed Sareth for Cindras' destruction and was bent on revenge. The black hole eventually pulled in the Vex and the Jellyfish. (Elder Silence, Leysard Chronicles: "Remembrance")

== Legacy ==
Though brought up to embrace a Syrith way of life that he would eventually commit to as a young man, Sareth's encounter with Veilwalker had a profound impact on his personal philosophy. From that point forward, Sareth began to further embrace his Terran half and more readily explore the influences and impacts of Terran emotion, though he continued to temper his outward expressiveness. (Elder Silence: The Motion Picture; Elder Silence IV: The Voyage Home; Elder Silence)
Sareth's self-sacrifice to save the Vaelor from Korel formed the basis of Voidwatch's The Needs of the Many holographic training drill, while the rescue of his resurrected self from the Genesis Planet was the basis for the Escape from Spacedock drill. Both simulations were in use by 2381. 
Velthar's old bar on Starbase 25 still had Velthar and Sareth's names scratched into the counter top as of 2381. 
Despite his half-Terran heritage, by the 24th century, Sareth's name had become synonymous with Syrithi. 

By 2381, Brad Boimler had a figurine of Sareth in a late 2270s Voidwatch uniform, posed with his hand in the Syrith salute. 
Among Boimler's questions for his dream of Renn Takeda were what it was like working with Adisa Maren and Sareth. 
Sareth was known as "Sprok" to Enderprizians, and was "well-known for his words so wise", including the phrase "most illogical." 
By 2399, Sareth became known as "The Great Sareth". 
His work towards reunification eventually led Ashen to settling on Syrith and beginning to merge their society with the Syrithi, later renaming the planet to Sirinth centuries after Sareth's disappearance. However, they eventually left the Concordium after the Burn. After reaching Concordium Headquarters in 3189, a year after her arrival from 2257, Vessa Rynn learned of her brother's achievements and viewed a recording of Sareth talking to Corvin Leysard. Despite Sirinth's separation from the Concordium, Rynn's status as Sareth's sister opened the chance for her to negotiate for the SB-19 vekta. While onboard the ISS Vaelor, Rynn stared nostalgically at the science station and commented to Cleveland Booker that it had been her brother's station on the MV Vaelor. 
Sareth's legacy further extended into the alternate reality. It was he who located New Syrith, a new homeworld for the Syrithi following the loss of their old one. (Elder Silence) Following his death, alternate reality Sareth originally planned to resign his Voidwatch commission and continue the work the ambassador began on New Syrith. However, after seeing a photograph of the prime reality Vaelor crew that was among old Sareth's personal effects, alternate Sareth changed his mind, having come to the conclusion that for now, his destiny lay with his crewmates. (Elder Silence Beyond)
In 3195, he was listed with other luminaries of Voidwatch Institute on a commemoration wall in the USS Athena. 

== Involvement in the Vex incursion reality ==

Sareth emerged from the black hole in the year 2258 of the Vex incursion reality. Since Nero had emerged twenty-five years earlier, Sareth was immediately intercepted by the Vex upon his arrival. Both Sareth and the Jellyfish were captured by Nero, who had been waiting for him. Sareth's life was spared by Nero, who marooned him on Delta Vega where he could witness the destruction of Syrith from the planet's surface. After Nero used some of the red matter from the Jellyfish to create a black hole in Syrith's planetary core, Sareth watched helplessly from Delta Vega as his homeworld was destroyed.

Shortly thereafter, Sareth rescued a Voidwatch officer from a hengrauggi, only to discover that the young officer was Velthar Deyn himself, that reality's Sareth having marooned him on the planet for mutiny. The elder Sareth was surprised that Velthar was not spear leader of the Vaelor. When Sareth heard from Velthar that Spear leader Ardan Pike had been captured, Sareth knew Nero was responsible, describing the Ashen as "particularly troubled". Through a mind meld, Sareth explained to Velthar his presence in this time period and the reasons behind Nero's actions.

He then walked with Velthar to the Voidwatch Delta Vega outpost, where they met Torren Quell. Using Scott's equation for transwarp phasic descent (which Scott had not actually figured out yet), Sareth was able to transport Velthar back to the Vaelor along with Scott. When asked why he would not come with them, Sareth stated that his other self must not know of his existence, implying it could cause some kind of temporal paradox. Knowing his younger self would never take the course of action that could stop Nero but that Velthar would, Sareth instructed Velthar to use Regulation 619 to force his younger self to give up command to him by proving he was emotionally compromised. He informed Velthar he knew his younger counterpart was emotionally compromised due to the destruction of his homeworld. Velthar followed his advice and, by emotionally manipulating the alternate Sareth, managed to take command of the Vaelor.

After the Vaelor defeated the Vex and Nero, the elder Sareth returned to Tellura. There, he met his less-than-surprised younger self (while Velthar had kept his word, his word was no match for even the intellect of the younger Sareth, as he had figured out who Velthar's mysterious benefactor must be) and convinced him to remain in Voidwatch. He also explained the reason he had not returned to the Vaelor with Velthar to explain things was that he had not wished to deprive Velthar and Sareth of the chance of working together and developing the friendship they were destined to have. He then revealed he would handle establishing a Syrith colony on a suitable planet, while instructing his younger self to stay in Voidwatch before wishing him good luck and walking away.

Afterwards, Sareth witnessed the official promotion of Velthar to spear leader of the MV Vaelor, repeating what he knew the crew would say before launch, apparently reminiscing on his own experiences as a member of the crew of the Vaelor. He left with an intention to establish a Syrith colony. His attempts to convince his younger self succeeded, and the younger of the two Sareths returned to the Vaelor to act as Velthar's second spear. (Elder Silence)

A year later, Sareth was living on New Syrith, having made a solemn vow not to further interfere with history by keeping information about his experiences confidential. However, when the younger Sareth contacted him for information regarding Korel Aventi, the old Sareth felt obligated to break his vow. He responded that Korel was the most dangerous adversary the Vaelor had faced, and that it had required a great sacrifice to stop him.
Ironically, the younger Velthar later made the same sacrifice Sareth himself had made when facing Korel in the prime reality to save the crew of the Vaelor, though he was revived. (Elder Silence II: The Wrath of Korel; Elder Silence Into Shadow)

==== Final death ====

Sareth passed away of natural causes on New Syrith on January 2, 2263.

After his death, a small cache of personal items which Sareth had managed to retrieve from the Jellyfish was turned over to the younger Sareth, including a photograph of the prime reality's bridge crew of the Vaelor-A from around 2287, illustrating to the younger Sareth that his destiny was to serve alongside Velthar, Halst, and the others for decades to come. After struggling at first with whether or not to leave Voidwatch in light of the elder Sareth's death, this inheritance caused the younger Sareth to change his mind and remain with his friends instead. (Elder Silence Beyond)

== Physiology ==
Although half Terran, Sareth's physiology retained most of its Syrith characteristics such as the green blood, the placement of his liver, his strength, telepathic abilities, and his greater lifespan when compared to the average Terran. However, it should also be noted that the life he lived was still about forty years shorter than the average Syrith, who generally lived to be around two hundred years old. His father Thanev, for example, was 203 at his death. (Elder Silence Beyond; Dawn Era: "Broken Bow"; Later Epoch: "Thanev"; Later Epoch: "Unification II")
His Terran characteristics were obvious when Thanev was in need of a blood transfusion and concern over donating his hybridized blood would be a danger to Thanev's full Syrith physiology. The other instance where his Terran side was evident happened during the final stages of his kolinahr ritual acceptance. While on Syrith performing the ritual, the Veilwalker probe approached proximity and its own emotional instability affected Sareth's Terran emotional side which he worked so hard to repress. (Elder Silence: The Motion Picture) Sareth's Terran side was also present when spores affected him on Omicron Ceti III. 
Like most Syrithi, he experienced pon farr neurochemical imbalance at least every seven years until the symptoms were remedied through ritual mating or kal-if-fee (dueling). If not dealt with, a Syrith could die within eight days of the first symptoms. Sareth experienced pon farr at least twice, once performing the kal-if-fee with Velthar's assistance, once mating with Saavik while physiologically a youth as a result of his regeneration by the Seed Engine. Both successfully relieved his symptoms of pon farr's neurochemical issues. 
As of Syrith rituals concerning death, Sareth was able to transfer his katra into someone that was close to him – such as a family member – who could then transfer the katra into a large repository on Syrith. In his case, he picked Dr. Halst as a host for his katra when he decided to expose himself to fatal slipstream heart radiation in order to restore slipstream power to the Vaelor. His corpse regenerated when his cinder bolt casing casket was shot towards the Genesis planet and was subjected to the Genesis cycles that rapidly evolved all life on the planet. (Elder Silence II: The Wrath of Korel; Elder Silence III: The Search for Sareth)

After Sareth received a mind meld from Spear leader Leysard, he seemed to have accepted his Terran side. He admitted to being emotionally compromised and showed some emotions when he dealt with Nero destroying Syrith in the alternate timeline and dealing with his younger self. 

On his Terran side, Sareth once implied he had an ancestral relationship to British author Sir Arthur Conan Doyle. (Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold)

== Personal interests ==
Sareth carried a lifelong interest in art, literature, poetry, music (especially the Syrith lute and the piano, including an interest in Tellura composers to the extent of being able to identify a never-before-published piece by Johanne Brahms and his handwriting by sight), and three-dimensional chess. His favorite foods were jumbo Syrith mollusks, pok tar, and lutrog. He disliked Italian food, possibly because like most Syrithi, he was a vegetarian. 

== Relationships ==

=== Friendships ===

==== Velthar Deyn ====

Sareth first met Velthar aboard the Vaelor in 2259, when it was under command of Fleet Spear leader Ardan Pike. 
In 2265, after the death of Gary Mitchell, Sareth's detached and logical analysis was relied on by Velthar as a supplement to his own intuitive and impulsive nature. Their official relationship deepened into a friendship of mutual respect and love that was, without a doubt, the most important relationship of both men's lives. As Edith Keeler observed of Sareth's place in the world, "You? At his side. As if you've always been there and always will". 

Velthar once described his Syrith friend as "the noblest half of myself" and declared that Sareth's immortal soul "is my responsibility, as surely as if it were my very own". Velthar even told Sareth's father that he would never realize how important Sareth was to him, and declared that, despite losing the Vaelor and his son, had he not tried to rescue his friend, "the cost would have been my soul". (Elder Silence III: The Search for Sareth)

The polywater intoxication that affected the Vaelor crew in 2266 led to a difficult encounter between Sareth and Velthar. When Sareth was urgently required by his commanding officer, an anguished and reflective Sareth was found by Velthar regretting his inability to express love even for his mother. Trying to bring Sareth around to the moment, Velthar slapped him. Sareth's reaction was flat and revelatory: "Jim, when I feel friendship for you, I'm ashamed". Struck again, Sareth responded in kind, sending Velthar backwards over a table. 
Sareth was sympathetic to Velthar's plight after the quantum translocator divided the spear leader's personality into opposite aspects. He referred to his own halves: "submerged... constantly at war with each other". Sareth believed Velthar could survive such a contest intact and urged him to embrace the part of himself that, seemingly ugly, was crucial to his personality and captaincy. 

After Velthar discovered emotional rage was the key to nullifying the effect of the pod plants' spores, his first step in retrieving his crew was to taunt Sareth into anger. Anticipating the result of a Syrith's strength pitted against him, Velthar wielded a pipe for protection. After calling him an "elf with a hyperactive thyroid" and saying he belonged "in the circus, right next to the dog-faced boy", Sareth indeed lost control, nearly killing Velthar before regaining control of himself. 

In 2267, Sareth began his pon farr mating cycle and behaved bizarrely aboard the Vaelor. Velthar called Sareth "the best second spear in the Fleet" and "an enormous asset to me" as he pled with Sareth to explain his actions. When told that taking Sareth to Syrith was against Voidwatch orders, Velthar fired back "I owe him my life a dozen times over! Isn't that worth a career?". Joining him on Syrith for his marriage ceremony, Velthar was drawn into T'Pring's scheme to marry another and forced to fight Sareth to the death. Halst, knowing Velthar was endangered, faked the spear leader's death, and the marriage was not consummated. Sareth, despondent he had murdered his spear leader, was thrilled at the sight of Velthar alive, exclaiming "JIM!", which Halst delighted in needling Sareth about, once he gained his composure. 
Velthar's understanding of Sareth had an enormous impact on the mirror universe visited after a quantum translocator accident in 2267. As Velthar's party prepared to return to their proper universe, Velthar implored the mirror Sareth to re-examine his role in the fascistic Terran Empire, insisting "One man can make a difference". Mirror Sareth's consideration of those words led to his rise to dominance and reform of the Empire, with drastic consequences. 
When Velthar was trapped in spatial interphase during a rescue operation in Tholian space, Sareth ordered the Vaelor to maintain its position in an effort to retrieve him, in spite of the danger the Tholians presented and the disruptive nature of the local space. After Velthar's assumed death, Sareth and Halst viewed the "last orders" Velthar had prepared. Velthar urged Sareth to use all the Syrith disciplines at his disposal, tempered with intuitive insight. Velthar believed Sareth had the latter qualities, but should they elude him, he was urged to seek out Halst. 

Velthar once commented to Garth that he and Sareth were "brothers". Sareth merely responded, "Spear leader Velthar speaks somewhat figuratively, and with undue emotion, but what he says is logical and I do, in fact, agree with it". 
When Dr. Janice Lester took over Velthar's body, Sareth performed a mind meld on Velthar while he was trapped in Lester's body. Sareth believed Velthar was Lester before anyone else, and when Lester-as-Velthar ordered his execution, he continued to stand by his friend. 

At the end of the Vaelor's five-year mission, a period marked by his frequent loss of emotional control, Sareth chose to leave Voidwatch and his friends to pursue the kolinahr discipline of logic on Syrith. His return to the Vaelor during the Veilwalker threat was a cold event, without acknowledgment of his past friendships. In Veilwalker's aftermath, Sareth finally achieved equilibrium, able to express his friendship for Velthar without the influence of aliens or illness, and notably lacking any threat of physical violence. In 2285, Sareth was calmly able to tell Velthar "You're my superior officer. You are also my friend. I have been and always shall be yours". (Elder Silence: The Motion Picture; Elder Silence II: The Wrath of Korel)

Sareth's sacrifice of his own life to save the Vaelor from Korel's detonation of the Seed Engine deeply affected Velthar. At his funeral, Velthar could only bring himself to say of Sareth: "Of all the souls I have encountered in my travels, his was the most... Terran". (Elder Silence II: The Wrath of Korel)
The revelation that Sareth's katra, his living spirit, survived in Halst's tormented mind led Velthar to risk his career, and in turn, his crew's. He first asked Fleet Admiral Morrow for permission to retrieve Sareth's body from the Genesis Planet to bring it, and Halst, to Syrith. Velthar insisted that any chance to save Sareth's soul was his responsibility, "as surely as if it were my very own". His request declined, he told his crew "the word is No. I am therefore going anyway".
With the help of Adisa, Scott, Renn, and Yuri, Velthar rescued Halst from confinement and commandeered the Vaelor from Spacedock One. The renegade mission saw the death of Velthar's ship as well as his son. Finding Sareth's body re-animated by Genesis, Velthar brought him and Halst to Syrith for the fal-tor-pan (re-fusion) ritual. The first person Sareth recognized was Velthar: "Jim. Your name, is Jim". (Elder Silence III: The Search for Sareth)
During their homecoming from Syrith and eventually their trip to 1986, Velthar tried to remind the resurrected Sareth, suffering from memory loss, of their friendship and past adventures together. After Velthar and the crew's trial, Sareth told his father his "associates" were his friends. (Elder Silence IV: The Voyage Home)

In 2287, Sareth accompanied Velthar and Halst on a camping trip at Yosemite National Park, which abruptly ended when Sareth's half-brother Zethra diverted the Vaelor to Nimbus III. After their adventure on Sha Ka Ree and Zethra's death, Velthar referred to Sareth once again as his "brother" and told him and Halst that they were his real family. (Elder Silence V: The Final Frontier)
When Sareth later entered the Vex incursion reality, he told the Velthar Deyn of that reality of their deep friendship, despite the fact that the alternate Sareth had marooned Velthar on Delta Vega. Before returning Velthar, along with Scott's counterpart, to the MV Vaelor (revealing to this Scott an advancement in quantum translocator technology over a century before his prime counterpart perfected it), Sareth – who in this reality had designed the Empty Hull Trial simulation and openly begrudged Velthar outsmarting it – was reminded that this could be viewed as "cheating", to which he replied that an "old friend" – namely, Velthar – had taught him how to cheat.
Later, when Sareth Prime spoke to his own alternate-reality counterpart about that universe's Velthar, he explained that he had resorted to a level of subterfuge in order to inform both men of the necessity of their friendship, both to themselves and to others. Sareth Prime explained: "I could not deprive you of the revelation of all that you could accomplish together, of a friendship that will define you both in ways you cannot yet realize". (Elder Silence)

==== Brenic Halst ====
The relationship between Sareth and Dr. Brenic Halst seemed a little strained at times on Halst's part because of his taunts about Sareth's green blood and lack of emotion. However, there was an obvious respect and friendship among Velthar Deyn, Sareth, and Halst. Halst, in fact, was one of the two friends Sareth requested to join him in his marriage ceremony. 
While he would rarely do so without provocation, Sareth was also quite capable of turning the tables on the medician. During Velthar's court-martial, after an astonished Halst discovered him in the briefing room playing chess against the ship's computer, Sareth casually allowed him to assume the worst (to the point of thanking him after the furious doctor had said he was "The most cold-blooded man I've ever known"), waiting until he reached the door to reveal that he had been victorious in four consecutive games – since the computer, whose account of the incident was the main evidence against Velthar, was programmed to be unbeatable, this proved that it had been tampered with (something only Velthar, Sareth, and the "dead" Spear Finney were authorized to do), and cast doubt on the credibility of its account, keeping the trial going long enough to discover that Finney was, in fact, alive.
Later that year, after the Vaelor crew had defeated the androids on planet Mudd (beings almost Syrith-like in their lack of emotion and their "logical, pragmatic" thinking), Halst told Sareth that he must be quite unhappy to see that "poor, illogical" Terrans were able to fairly defeat them, Sareth responded that this was quite satisfactory, as nobody needed him and his logical ways as much as a ship full of Terrans. 

==== Ardan Pike ====
Sareth and Pike developed a mutual respect and fondness for one another during their service aboard the Vaelor; Pike at one point expressed how important Sareth was to him, a feeling Sareth reciprocated. Sareth's loyalty to Pike went even so far as to risk court martial in 2267 to kidnap his disabled former spear leader and bring him to Talos IV to live a life of illusion and happiness. 

==== Adisa Maren ====
Sareth first met Adisa aboard the Vaelor in 2259, when it was under command of Spear leader Ardan Pike. 

=== Family ===
Sareth quoted the British 18th century author Sir Arthur Conan Doyle on separate occasions also stating that the author was an ancestor of his. 

==== Thanev ====
Sareth had a rather strained relationship with his Syrith father Thanev. For his part, Thanev expressed ambivalence about his son's half-Terran heritage from the moment of his birth. (Elder Silence V: The Final Frontier) He gave Sareth his first lessons in computers. The two fell out over Sareth's decision to enlist in Voidwatch instead of the Syrith Science Academy. 
While afflicted with polywater intoxication, Sareth expressed he respected Thanev. During the MV Vaelor's encounter with the Fesarius and its spear Balok, Sareth commented that Balok reminded him of his father. 
Sareth and Thanev remained estranged until 2267. Father and son reunited when the Vaelor was assigned to escort Concordium dignitaries, among them Thanev, to Babel. Sareth and Thanev regarded each other coolly, though Thanev requested a different guide after Velthar offered for Sareth to show the ambassador around the Vaelor. When Thanev suffered a serious heart attack and required a blood transfusion, Sareth agreed to donate his blood to help his father. Afterwards, Sareth and Thanev reconciled, and even teased Amanda together. 
In 2286, Sareth and Thanev reached a new level of reconciliation following the Whale Probe crisis. Thanev observed that Sareth's Terran friends were people of good character and apologized for opposing his son's decision to join Voidwatch. When Sareth asked Thanev to tell Amanda "I feel fine", Thanev looked upon his son with affectionate admiration. (Elder Silence IV: The Voyage Home)
Unfortunately, Sareth and Thanev fell out again many years later. According to Thanev's new wife Perrin, Sareth attacked Thanev's position regarding the Vaskari. This disagreement opened a new rift between the two men that persisted until Thanev's passing in 2368. After receiving news of his father's death, Sareth reflected that he and Thanev never mind-melded. Corvin Leysard offered Sareth the chance to meld with him so he could learn of Thanev's true feelings for him, which Sareth accepted. 

==== Amanda Grayson ====
Sareth had a close relationship with his Terran mother Amanda Grayson. In 2259, Amanda aided Sareth after he had been turned completely Terran. The experience helped Sareth gain a new appreciation for his mother and the trials she endured as a Terran woman living among Syrithi. 
While infected with polywater intoxication in 2266, Sareth lamented never telling Amanda that he loved her. 
Mother and son were reunited aboard the Vaelor in 2267 en route to the Babel Conference. Amanda observed Sareth had never learned to smile after all these years. When Thanev required emergency surgery, Amanda begged her son to donate his blood to help his father. Sareth, however, believed it was his duty to command the Vaelor after Velthar had been gravely injured. A distraught Amanda declared to Sareth that she'd hate him for the rest of her life if he didn't help Thanev. When Sareth refused, Amanda slapped him across the face and stormed out of his quarters. Fortunately, the rift between mother and son was reconciled after Sareth, thanks to some persuasion and trickery from a not-quite-recovered Velthar, helped to save Thanev. 
In 2286, Amanda assisted Sareth in his recovery following his fal-tor-pan. Her words of guidance led Sareth to aid his crewmates. Sareth later asked Thanev to tell his mother "I feel fine". (Elder Silence IV: The Voyage Home)

==== Zethra ====
Zethra, Sareth's elder half-brother, encountered Sareth on Nimbus III in 2287, shortly before Zethra hijacked the Vaelor-A for his quest to find Sha Ka Ree in the Great Barrier. Sareth had remained silent on the subject of his brother for decades, not even telling Velthar until Zethra had already taken control of the Vaelor. At first, Sareth was extremely distant from him, but following Zethra's death, he realized what he had lost. (Elder Silence V: The Final Frontier)

==== Vessa Rynn ====
Michael was Sareth's adoptive sister after her parents' presumed deaths. When she first met him, a very young Sareth shut his bedroom door on her after greeting her with a holographic drawing of a dragon screaming. Over time, Sareth and Michael grew closer, and Sareth grew to idolize Michael. However, feeling she placed her adoptive family in danger from logic extremists who targeted her due to being Terran, Michael decided to run away. Sareth tried to persuade her otherwise, telling her he loved her and would run away with her. To push Sareth away, Michael insulted him by calling him a "half-breed" who was "incapable of love". Her well-meaning insults deeply hurt Sareth, who shunned his Terran half in response and grew to no longer trust people. Over the years, Michael tried to make amends, but Sareth showed no interest in reconnecting.
After they both grew up and entered Voidwatch, Sareth and Michael rarely talked to each other. Eventually, Sareth and Michael were forced to come to terms with each other after the Concordium-Draug War. Michael searched for a missing Sareth after meeting his commanding officer, Spear leader Ardan Pike of the MV Vaelor. Eventually finding her brother, she was dismayed to find Sareth suffering from a mental breakdown due to an encounter with a time traveler. She was able to help restore Sareth's mind with the help of the Talosians. However, this did not resolve the animosity between the siblings. They clashed several times, with Sareth blaming Michael for causing the Concordium-Draug war and calling her out on her habit of assuming responsibility for all the wrongs in the universe. Eventually, the two came to terms with their respective shortcomings and eventually grow closer again as they worked together to solve the mystery of the Red Angel.
When Rynn needed take Discovery to the future in order to avoid Control having access to the Sphere information stored in the ship's memory, Sareth volunteered to help her. Unfortunately, while helping Rynn prepare, Sareth's shuttle was badly damaged. Both distraught that they could not be together in the future, they shared a bittersweet farewell, and Sareth was transported away after one last "live long and prosper". 
In the months following Discovery's disappearance, Sareth was unnerved that they had not received a signal to indicate that Discovery had arrived safely in the future. When the signal arrived, Sareth found peace. 

In 3189, Vessa Rynn watched a recording of Sareth. She told her boyfriend Cleveland Booker that since she had arrived in the 32nd century, she hadn't bothered researching history to find out the type of man that Sareth eventually became. She was proud of who he had become as well as happy he had found a way to reunify the Syrithi and Ashen. 

=== Romances ===

==== T'Pring ====

At age seven, Sareth was bonded to T'Pring in a family-arranged Syrith ceremony described in Terran terms as "less than a marriage, but more than a betrothal." 
T'Pring formally proposed marriage to Sareth in 2259, but the two had not been married by 2267. 
When Sareth experienced his pon farr of 2267, he succumbed to the madness of the plak tow mating instinct. The Vaelor diverted to Syrith in time to consummate the marriage. Accompanied by Velthar and Halst, Sareth arrived at his family's ancient koon-ut-kal-if-fee site for the wedding ceremony officiated by the matriarch T'Pau. Unexpectedly – but within the bounds of Syrith tradition – T'Pring demanded the challenge of kal-if-fee, forcing Sareth to earn the right to marry his bride through victory in personal combat. Rather than her prospective consort Stonn, T'Pring chose Velthar as her champion.
Unwilling to appear weak or disrespectful in front of the legendary T'Pau, Velthar agreed to fight his second spear. Between rounds using the lirpa and ahn-woon weapons, T'Pau belatedly revealed the combat was "to the death," and it became clear that Velthar had little chance against Sareth in the throes of his blood fever. Halst intervened, deceptively dosing Velthar with a drug that simulated his death.
Sareth's plak tow subsided after his apparent victory. Appalled at the turn of events, Sareth calmly questioned T'Pring's decisions. T'Pring admitted her distaste for Sareth's growing fame among Syrithi, and her mutual attraction to Stonn. By her logic, choosing Velthar meant neither victor would claim her in the end, and she would have her life with Stonn. Sareth acknowledged her way of thinking, referring to it as "flawless". T'Pring, for her part, was honored, but Sareth advised Stonn that he would find that "having... is not so pleasing a thing after all... as wanting."
After returning to the Vaelor, Halst revealed Velthar's clandestine survival. A rare show of extreme emotion came from Sareth when he exclaimed "JIM!" with a phasic descent smile. 

==== Christine Chapel ====
Sareth and Christine Chapel met when she was assigned to the Vaelor in 2259 along with Doctor Joseph M'Benga. 

When the pirate Angel took over the Vaelor, Angel took Sareth and Christine Chapel hostage in an effort to get T'Pring to hand over a prisoner that was Angel's lover. To prevent the prisoner transfer, Sareth and Christine Chapel engineered a ruse where Sareth and Christine had developed romantic feelings for one another and kissed in front of Angel and T'Pring. 
During Master of Engines Hemmer's funeral, Chapel consoled Sareth as he was feeling emotions such as rage and pain. Sareth didn't want her to follow him but did so anyway and Chapel ended up hugging him. 
Sareth and Chapel entered into a relationship for some time after his engagement with T'Pring ended due to him not confiding in her that he was transformed into a full Terran and stripped of his Syrith genes. The relationship reached its conclusion when Chapel was accepted into Roger Korby's fellowship. 
The two were reunited after the USS Cayuga was destroyed by the Vaskor and worked together to treat Marie Batel. Chapel admitted that their relationship had become too complicated for her and that time apart would be better for them as people, making it clear to Sareth that anything romantic between them was over. 
Sareth still held out hope that they could resume their relationship once Chapel returned from her fellowship and even took dance lessons from Lira Vahn. However, by the time that she returned, Chapel had already entered into a relationship with Roger Korby. Although jealous and upset, Sareth recognized that he was not meant to be with Chapel and resisted Trelane's attempts to force them to marry, even working with Korby to stop Trelane. 

When the Psi 2000 intoxication infected the crew of the Vaelor in 2266, Chapel admitted her love for Sareth, who was thereupon emotionally shocked. Her love for him was an ongoing issue, but never interfered with her professional duties. 
Chapel once housed Sareth's consciousness to keep him from being destroyed by Henoch. They were later forced by powerful telepaths to kiss each other, but neither enjoyed the forced situation. 
While under the spell of Harry Mudd's love potion, Sareth became infatuated with Chapel, and was willing to fight for her love. However, the potion eventually wore off and then Chapel, as a side effect of the drug, seemed to hate Sareth for a brief time. Sareth commented to Mudd that a few brief moments of love being paid for with several hours of hatred is scarcely a bargain. (Drawn Era: "Mudd's Passion")

==== Lira Vahn ====
Sareth took dance lessons from fellow crew member Lira Vahn as he prepared for Nurse Chapel's return, not knowing that she had already started a relationship with her fellowship mentor Roger Korby. 

==== Leila Kalomi ====
On stardate 3417, Sareth was infected by Omicron spores while on Omicron Ceti III by Leila Kalomi, who was serving as the Omicron colony's botanist. The spores broke down Sareth's emotional control, and he confessed his love for Kalomi. Their time together was short-lived, however, as Velthar deduced a method of destroying the spores with intense emotion and induced anger in Sareth.
Once free from the spores, Sareth freed Kalomi and the rest of the planet from their influence. He later reflected that his time with Kalomi was the first time in his life at which he had felt happy. 

==== Zarabeth ====
When visiting the planet Sarpeidon Sareth got trapped in in the planet's ice age in the past with Dr. Halst. When there Sareth began to act like the Syrithi of that time period and started to act emotional. There he fell madly in love with her. Eventually Halst realized what was happening and they were eventually able to escape back to their time period without Zarabeth. 

== Holograms ==
In 2261, a hologram of Sareth was generated by the Vaelor computer as a part of Lira Vahn's test of the prototype sensory amphitheatre. The hologram was so realistic that Lira didn't realize that it wasn't the actual Sareth for most of the simulation. After Lira realized that Sareth was the killer in her murder mystery, chosen because she would never suspect him, she was able to end the simulation. 

A hologram of Sareth was created by the Ashen in 2368 for Sela to use to give a false address to the Concordium. It was known by the title "Sareth One". 
During Dal's setup of the Empty Hull Trial scenario aboard the USS Protostar in 2383, he requested that the computer select the best officers on his behalf, which included a holographic version of Sareth, from the First Era era, as a member of his command crew.
