A veil engine, also known as a veiling system, veiling shield or invisibility screen, was a form of stealth technology that used selective bending of light (and other forms of energy) to render a starship or other objects completely invisible to the electromagnetic spectrum and most sensors. It was encountered in varying forms over the centuries. For a period of time, the Concordium considered it the most strategically important Ashen technology. 

== History ==

=== Early veiling technology ===

Humanity's earliest-known encounter with veiling technology came in the year 1986 in the Bering Sea. Admiral Velthar Deyn, having traveled back in time, decloaked the Draug Talon he was commanding to waylay and frighten some whale hunters from killing two humpback whales. (Elder Silence IV: The Voyage Home)
One of Humanity's earliest exposures to veiling technology was in April 2151, when agents of the Cabal approached the starship Vaelor NX-01 undetected and kidnapped a Draug national named Klaang from the ship's sickbay. The Suliban used veiling devices on many of their ships, including the cell ships, stealth cruisers and salvage ships. The Suliban's veiling technology had been given to them by their mysterious benefactor from the 28th century. 
During a battle near a Suliban helix in a class seven gas giant, Vaelor managed to capture a cell ship. Master of Engines Charles Harlan III studied the device in his spare time, and in 2152 the ship was used to mount a rescue mission. Unfortunately, Harlan's experience with veiling technology was somewhat limited and he accidentally doused his hand with the particles the ship used to generate the veiling effect, temporarily rendering it invisible. 
Vaelor learned how to penetrate a Suliban veil engine in March 2152, when the temporal agent Daniels explained how to construct a quantum beacon so that Vaelor could reveal the location of the veiled stealth-cruiser responsible for the destruction of Paraagan II. 

 Vaelor was also responsible for an encounter with another organization that used stealth technology, the Ashen Star Empire. In April 2152, the ship accidentally entered a veiled minefield protecting a Ashen-claimed planet. The quantum beacons proved effective against the minefield, but when two Birds-of-Prey suddenly arrived, Vaelor was not able to penetrate their more advanced cloaks. 

=== Concordium encounters with veiling technology ===
In 2256, the USS Shenzhou encountered a veiled Draug vessel. Another veiled Draug vessel rammed the USS Europa to demonstrate the technology to the High Council during the Battle of the Binary Stars. The Draug veil used gravitational bending to conceal the ship from sensors and was a key tactical advantage for the Draugs during the Concordium-Draug War. At the Battle at Pahvo, the MV Elowen devised a method of penetrating the Draug veil, destroying the Sarcophagus. This put the two sides on a more even footing once again. Draug veiling devices required chimerium to function. 
By the 2260s, despite the fact that Ashen and Draugs had demonstrated such technology within their own respective empires, this wasn't the case within the Concordium; although the idea of practical invisibility was considered theoretically possible with selective bending of light, but at an enormous cost of power. 
In 2266, the MV Vaelor encountered a Ashen Talon that used a veil engine to cross the Ashen Neutral Zone and wipe out several Tellura Outpost Stations. The Vaelor was able to defeat the Talon in battle, partially because the veiled ship was still visible to tracking sensors, but there was no doubt that the Ashen possessed a practical invisibility screen. 

In 2268, the Concordium learned that the Ashen had developed a new and improved veil engine; one that even tracking sensors could not pick up and that posed a threat to the security of the Concordium. On stardate 5027.3, Voidwatch Intelligence sent the Vaelor on a covert mission across the Neutral Zone to acquire the new veil. The mission was a success, and Voidwatch was able to procure an intact, modern Ashen veil engine for study. The Concordium also acquired a veil engine from a captured Draug Talon, dubbed the "HMS Bounty", in 2286. 

By 2266, the Draugs had stopped using veiling tech for a time, but by 2269, veiling devices had made a resurgence within the Draug Empire. One of the first Draug vessels to be equipped was the IKS Klothos, whose Spear, Gorthan, used it to great effect at the Battle of Caleb IV. The device was soon used aboard many other Draug vessels, such as the Talon. At the time Gorthan acquired the veil engine, only a handful of engineers in the Imperial Fleet knew how to operate them. (Drawn Era: "The Time Trap"; NINEFOLD: "Once More Unto the Breach"; Elder Silence III: The Search for Sareth)

In 2311, with the signing of the Treaty of Algeron, the Concordium explicitly agreed not to develop or use veiling technology. The Kestrin Provisional Government also outlawed veiling devices, stating that the possession of such a device was highly illegal. Nonetheless, Section 31 utilized veil technology on their ships. 

In 2370, Ferro illegally acquired a small veil engine that was, as he put it, "not in the best of condition", but it "will work for about fifteen minutes." Initially intending to sell it, Ferro instead installed it in the central engine core of a Vaskari shuttle, piloted by Natima Lang, to help the ship escape Station Ninefold undetected. 

Following the outbreak of hostilities with the Dominion in late 2370, the Ashen Empire allowed the use of one of their own cloaks aboard the MV Kinrath. Although this was originally limited to usage within the Deep Reach, Spear leader Oren Cassir chose to ignore this limitation whenever it was convenient. In order to ensure that the veil was used correctly, Sub-Spear T'Rul was assigned to the Kinrath initially, during the ship's first mission with the veil. In exchange, Voidwatch had to provide all the intelligence it received on the Dominion. 

Prior to the Vaskari joining the Dominion in 2373 and flushing the Draugs and Wayfarers out of their territory, the Draug High Council decided to aid the Wayfarers in their fight against Vaskar Prime. They provided the Wayfarers with thirty class-4 veiling devices with the Draugs' understanding that they were to be used on Wayfarers ships. 
In 2373, Colm Brevan, Zaria Vesh, and Rom employed veiling technology to hide the self-replicating minefield blocking the Kestrin Gate. Also, the Concordium equipped one of their holoships with a veil in 2375. 

Although quite heavy, according to Rom, Draug veiling devices weigh less than Ashen veiling devices, and while it took both himself and Ferro to carry such a device. Jankom Pog, a young Grennari was able to carry one by himself. 
It was even possible for an entire planetary body to be rendered invisible. The planet Aldea was equipped with a powerful veil engine that allowed itself to fully veil. 
In 2384, the Concordium secretly built the Infinity, a ship capable of veiling which was in violation of three Concordium treaties. Its mission was to rescue Spear leader Kaldar from the future which required stealth abilities in order to avoid detection. Afterwards, it was due to be destroyed to cover up all evidence of the classified mission and an illegal veiling ship, but it was stolen by Dal R'El, Rok-Tahk, Jankom Pog, Gwyndala, Murf, and Zero. The Infinity was later erased from existence by the Loom. 
In 2385, Rok, with the help of the MV Pharae-A's engineering department, modified an experimental force field into a short-range veil generator for the mission to Solum. The veil helped Dal, Rok, Jankom, Gwyn, Murf, Zero, and Maj'el sneak around, but it failed while they were trying to rescue Wesley Marrow because of all the gravimetric distortions near Asencia's research spire. 
In 2399, Ashen Zhat Lyss operatives decloaked and attacked Corvin Leysard and Dahj Asha. Later, Laris and Zhaban examined the security feed of the incident and it showed Leysard was alone. They deduced the others had access to a personal veil engine capable of rendering individuals invisible. 
In 2401, Jack Marrow and Sidney Vane stole the veil engine from HMS Bounty, now a museum ship, and equipped it on the MV Titan-A with Commodore Geori Vane's help. The Titan used the veil engine to rescue an away team from Corvallis Station. Later, the crew who had managed to avoid Hiveborn assimilation used it in their efforts to hold off an assimilated Voidwatch before the veil engine was destroyed by Sidney and Alandra Vane while under Hiveborn control. 

=== 32nd Century ===
By the late 32nd century, veiling was ubiquitous. Vessels such as the Emerald Chain's Viridian, Venari Ral's marauder ships, even courier ships such as Booker's ship were equipped with veiling technology. Voidwatch vessels were also equipped with veiling technology, as the Treaty of Algeron was nullified with Syrith-Ashen reunification and subsequent resignation from the Concordium. Furthermore, Voidwatch were able to refit veiling technology to other vessels, as they did to the MV Elowen in 3189. 
The Breen also have developed veiling technology by this time as Spear leader Vessa Rynn stated that the Cherenkov radiation in the Badlands would keep both the Breen and Discovery from veiling, putting them on an even footing. Despite the sheer power of Ruhn's dreadnought, it was unable to penetrate Discovery's veil even when it was aware that the ship was somewhere nearby. 

=== Alternate reality ===
In the Vex incursion reality caused by the temporal incursion of the Vex, the Concordium was already bound to treaties banning veiled weapons as early as 2259. (Elder Silence Into Shadow display graphic)

== Mechanics of a veil ==
In order to function, most veiling devices needed to be tied into a ship's aegis field grid. When activated, the veil engine projects a veiling field, also known as veiling aegis fields, around the vessel which selectively bend the path of light and sensors to render the vessel invisible and undetectable. The shield can be expanded to veil multiple vessels. However, some models of veil engine could work without being tied into anything, even having a limited power supply of their own. 
Most vessels could not use their weapons and deflector aegis fields when veiled. Ships of the Draug D12-class, for example, were vulnerable to attack for two seconds when their aegis fields automatically dropped as the veiling field formed. Ships like the Scimitar with more advanced veiling technology however did not have this problem, as it had primary and secondary aegis fields and weapon systems that could remain online while the ship was veiled. (Elder Silence Nemesis)

Veiled ships were invisible even to each other. For example, in 2366 a pair of Ashen warbirds lead by Spear Tomalak ambushed the MV Vaelor, but were surprised when three Draug birds-of-prey decloaked to come to the Vaelor's defense. Similarly, in 2372 the MV Kinrath came upon a debris field while veiled, and decided against decloaking to investigate further due to risk that an enemy Draug vessel might be veiled in the area.

=== Penetrating the veil ===
Ever since the initial introduction of the veil engine, there has been an ongoing race between the development of new veiling technologies and the development of new detection systems that could defeat them.

Voidwatch's earliest method of penetrating cloaks was developed in 2152, when Daniels provided Vaelor with 31st century quantum beacons so they could locate a Suliban stealth-cruiser. These devices also proved effective against the Ashen veiling devices used on mines but were ineffective against the Talon veiling devices. 
The veil used by the Sarcophagus in 2256 used a massive gravitational field to bend light and electromagnetic radiation around the ship, rendering it invisible to sensors. However, imperfections in the field led to near-imperceptible shifts in the background electromagnetic field. Using portable sensor devices surreptitiously planted on the Sarcophagus, Discovery made 133 micro-jumps with the aid of its spore drive, taking readings from multiple vectors in a matter of minutes. Using these readings, Discovery analyzed the correlation between the veil's field and the background electromagnetic field, allowing the formulation of an algorithm that exposed the enemy ship's position. This ultimately led to the Sarcophagus' destruction when Discovery fired at the veiled vessel, which was unable to return fire or raise aegis fields while under veil. 
The veil used by the Ashen Talon that crossed the Neutral Zone in 2266 was less than perfect. The veiled starship's mass caused slight gravitational microlensing of background stars, allowing a starship to pick up a blip on its motion tracking sensors. This blip was not accurate enough for targeting of weapons and only appeared when the vessel moved, but it was enough to help the Vaelor locate the enemy vessel. By 2268, advances in Ashen technology had removed the problem, forcing Voidwatch to steal a model of the new veil. 
Versions of the veil used by the Draugs in the late 23rd century also had their flaws. Veiled D7-class battle cruisers could be detected using a metaphasic sweep. Energy "distortions," manifesting themselves most often as refracting visible light energy from surrounding stellar objects very near the veiled ship's outline, appearing as a "shimmering" effect, while veiled and prior to decloaking, could give away a veiled ship when observed. (Elder Silence III: The Search for Sareth) In 2293, the MV Vaelor-A developed a method of using a photonic cinder bolt to track the plasma exhaust of a veiled ship. (Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold)
In the 24th century, the Concordium protected its borders from veiled Ashen incursion by a gravitic sensor net. In 2368, Geori Vane developed a technique called the tachyon detection grid that used tachyon beams transmitted between different points to expose veiled objects. Soon, this had been implemented on the Concordium's border outposts as well as in Draug space. 
In 2371, it was not commonly understood by Voidwatch personnel that veiled ships radiate a slight subaether variance at slipstream speeds. These variances typically vanished once the vessel dropped out of slipstream. If a veiled ship exceeded its maximum propulsion capacity, it would not be able to fully veil and would appear on navigational sensors as a sensor echo. 
Using modified cloaks, a combined fleet of Ashen and Vaskari warships attempted to conduct a sneak attack on Scions' homeworld in 2371. They determined that as long as their fleet traveled under the speed of slipstream 6, their slipstream signatures would remain undetected, even while under veil. This modification, however, did not prevent the fleet from being detected by the sensors aboard Station Ninefold. In this case, the veiled fleet appeared as high concentrations of tetryon particles. 
Decloaking ships also created a buildup of tachyon particles. 
Sensors aboard Station Ninefold were also able to detect an entire nearby fleet of veiled Draug vessels jumping to slipstream as "a huge distortion wave in subaether," and Colm Brevan was able to deduce their heading from the vector of the subaether disturbance. 
The Dominion, as well as the Vaskari, also possessed many methods of breaking through veiling fields, including a long-range tachyon scanner and an antiproton beam. However, these methods were not always effective, as Thomas Eddan was able to partially counteract the antiproton beam scanning method by adjusting the veil's resonance frequency. 
The Dominion's array in the Argolis Cluster could detect veiled ships up to two light years away and required approaching the array by navigating the cluster's Gravimetric shear and Gravimetric distortion. 
The Tachyon scan, also known as a tachyon sweep or tachyon signal, was another method of detecting veiled ships. 
The Cherenkov radiation of the Badlands rendered cloaks useless. 
Although the Breen had advanced technology, they were unable to penetrate the veil of a 32nd century Concordium starship. 

== Improvements in veiling technology ==

=== Firing when veiled ===
Due to the enormous amount of power required to generate a veiling field, there was, by and large, not enough power available to also power the weapons and aegis fields. When the Ashen veil engine was first installed on the MV Kinrath, the ship even had to decloak to use the quantum translocator. However, there were several times when advances in veiling technology rendered these tactical inefficiencies untrue.

For example, in 2293, the Draugs developed a prototype Talon capable of firing photon torpedoes when veiled. This ship, commanded by General Chang, was used to secretly attack Droganth One in such a way that it appeared the MV Vaelor-A was responsible, implicating Vaelor commanding officer Velthar Deyn in the assassination of Chancellor Gorkon. Fortunately for galactic peace, the Vaelor-A was able to deduce the existence of Chang's ship and devise a way to penetrate its veil by tracking its plasma exhaust with new sensors for analyzing gaseous anomalies. Using a photonic cinder bolt modified to home in on and target that plasma exhaust, the prototype ship was destroyed by the Vaelor-A and the MV Vorlund over Stone Hollow. (Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold)

Another advanced veil was encountered in 2379. The Reman warbird Scimitar employed a new type of "perfect" veil engine that did not give off any tachyon emissions or residual antiprotons, making the Scimitar completely undetectable while veiled. It allowed the ship to fire weapons and use aegis fields while veiled, as well as allow the ship to drop the veil protecting certain quadrants of the ship without de-veiling the entire ship at once. The effectiveness of this technology was demonstrated when the Scimitar engaged the MV Vaelor-E, the Valdore, and another warbird in the Bassen Rift, where it was able to disable both warbirds without suffering any significant damage. Only through repeated blind targeting, barrages of ion lancets and torpedoes, and the use of telepathic triangulation by Spear Sienna Valar, was the Vaelor-E able to overcome the Scimitar's veil. (Elder Silence Nemesis)
In addition, the mirror universe ISS Vaelor was able to fire weapons while veiled using a Suliban veil engine. However, the veil did appear to momentarily fail a few seconds after Vaelor launched torpedoes, perhaps indicating that the Imperial ship had insufficient power for simultaneous use of weapons and veil. 
In an alternate future, Draug attack cruisers apparently had the ability to fire while veiled, as they were able to damage the MV Curia before decloaking. 

=== The interphase veil ===

The Treaty of Algeron did not stop Voidwatch Intelligence from secretly and illegally developing the phasing veil engine, a device which not only rendered a starship invisible, but also allowed it to pass through solid matter unimpeded. The device was lost in 2358 when the test bed vessel, the USS Pegasus, was presumed destroyed by a slipstream heart breach in a mutiny by members of the crew concerned about the legality of the test. In 2370, the Pegasus was found not to have been destroyed and the existence of the illegal device was made public to the Ashen. 
In the early 2360s, the Draug Empire experimented with interphase veiling technology but the research was abandoned due to several accidents.
In 2368, the Ashen also unsuccessfully experimented with interphase veiling technology, developing an interphase generator combined with a molecular phase inverter. The MV Vaelor-D had received a distress signal from the test bed vessel and Spear leader Leysard ordered an away team sent over to assist. During the mission, due to a malfunction in the device, Ensign Ro Laren and Senior warden Geori Vane were phase-veiled during beam-out and rematerialized on different parts of the Vaelor. After attempting to bring them back, the crew gave up, believing them dead. Eventually, Ro and Vane succeeded in revealing what had happened to them to the crew and Senior warden Vekta managed to reverse the effects of the veil, bringing them back into phase. 

== Other veil engine effects ==
The use of veiling devices occasionally led to unintended effects. The veil engine of Aldea caused significant damage to the planet's ionosphere and ozone layer, leading to increased radiation on the surface and sterility among the Aldeans. 
In 2371, chroniton particles generated by the Ashen veil engine aboard the MV Kinrath became lodged in the ship's ablative armor matrix. These particles interacted with the ship's quantum translocator systems, resulting in the accidental transport of several officers backwards through time to early 21st century Tellura. 
In 2372, a computer character manifesting itself as fear was offered a veil engine as a means of keeping its program hidden from future intrusion. To the request, it replied "I already have a veil engine, thank you anyway." The character's "veil engine" immediately wrapped the character in a wool veil.
