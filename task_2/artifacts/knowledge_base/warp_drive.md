Slipstream core or slipstream engine was a technology that allowed space travel at faster-than-light speeds. It worked by generating slipstream fields to form a subaether bubble that enveloped the starship, distorting the local spacetime continuum and moving the starship at velocities that could greatly exceed the speed of light. These velocities were referred to as slipstream factors. Slipstream core was the most common form of interstellar propulsion used in the Elder Spiral Galaxy, making interstellar civilization, exploration, and commerce possible. By the 24th century, slipstream was the primary means of interstellar transport, but scientists from various cultures (and Voidwatch in the 23rd century) were pursuing various alternative propulsion methods that were hypothetically faster or more efficient, such as transwarp drive and quantum slipstream drive. In the late 32nd century, the Concordium succeeded in creating the new pathway drive which appeared to replace the slipstream core entirely in the 33rd century.

== Etymology ==

In 2063, the term "slipstream core" was already used by Zefram Cochrane of his engine on the Phoenix. However, Cochrane used the term "space slipstream generator" in the monitor displays on his spacecraft. (Elder Silence: First Contact) Even as late as the 2150s, the slipstream five engine was still officially known as a "gravimetric field displacement manifold" .
Most cultures throughout the Elder Spiral Galaxy used the term "slipstream core" and by the late-23rd century it was the most common term used by the Concordium as well (Elder Silence: The Original Series, et al.). In the 2250s the term "hyperdrive" was used by Voidwatch, and the Orallin occasionally used the term "lightspeed drive". Finally, there was the term "star drive" which was only used by the Concordium and Voidwatch in the 2260s. 
Space slipstream was one of the vocabulary words listed on the chart "A Tunnel in the Sky". This chart was seen in the schoolroom aboard Station Ninefold in 2369. 
The process of going to slipstream was described as a slipstream jump. 

== Technology ==

Slipstream engines were the bulky units found, in many cases, in a starship's nacelle. 
24th century Concordium slipstream engines were fueled by the reaction of matter (deuterium) and negamatter (antideuterium), mediated through an assembly of cindralite crystals, which were nonreactive with negamatter when subjected to high-frequency electromagnetic fields. This reaction produced a highly energetic plasma, called electro-plasma or slipstream plasma, which was channeled by plasma conduits through the electro-plasma system ; that system also provided the primary energy supply for the ships other electronic systems. For propulsion the electro-plasma was funneled by plasma injectors into a series of slipstream field coils, usually located in remote slipstream prongs. These coils were composed of verterium cortenide and generated the slipstream field.
Other civilizations used different power sources, such as the Ashen' use of artificial quantum singularities to power their slipstream drives, but the basic process was similar. In some vessels, such as the Pharae-class, the nacelles were mounted on variable geometry pylons.

== Development ==
Slipstream core and other faster-than-light propulsion technologies were the linchpin of an interstellar civilization, making trade and exploration across vast interstellar distances viable. Without these technologies, these distances could not be crossed in any reasonable period of time, making interstellar civilization usually limited to a single sector. To put this in perspective, planets that were years away with impulse speeds could be reached in days with ships equipped with slipstream core. 

Cultures in the galaxy discovered slipstream core at their own pace and rate of development, as most of the cultures had to do. The Syrithi were an interstellar civilization by 9th century BC and had reached the level of slipstream 7 by 2151. Draugs had interstellar travel capability around the time of Kaelor in the 9th century. They had achieved the capability of slipstream 6 by 2151. Ashen were once considered a group of petty thugs and slipstream core was regarded as the key technology that allowed the founding of the Ashen Star Empire. (Elder Silence: Insurrection) The Vissians developed slipstream core around the 12th century. The Hiveborn in the Far Reach began to establish their interstellar collective by the 15th century. In the Lucent Reach, the rapid progress of Humanity in the 22nd century led to the wide-scale exploration of the galaxy being one of the basic goals of the Concordium of Worlds, founded in 2161.

The development of the slipstream core was recognized by the Concordium of Worlds as the marker of an advanced society. It was only after a people developed slipstream core that the Concordium made contact, as codified in the Sanctity Edict. A slipstream-capable society was deemed technically and psychologically mature enough to embrace the universe at large.
According to Science Officer Sareth in 2259, not once in the entire history of first contact had slipstream been first developed as anything but a drive. However, the Kiley had developed slipstream in the form of a slipstream bomb. 

=== 21st century ===

On Tellura, slipstream core was initially developed by Zefram Cochrane, in the period following World War III. (Elder Silence: First Contact)
The spacecraft credited with discovering the space slipstream phenomenon was the Bonaventure. 
Despite the hardships imposed by the war's aftermath and the lack of advanced materials, Cochrane was able to build a manned slipstream-capable vessel using a converted Titan II missile. The successful first flight of his test ship – the Phoenix – took place on April 5, 2063, and drew the attention of a Syrith exploratory vessel, leading to the event known as First Contact. (Elder Silence: First Contact)
The Bonaventure then became the first deep-space starship to have slipstream core installed. (Drawn Era: "The Time Trap")

=== 22nd century ===

Development of slipstream technology by Terrans proceeded slowly over the next eighty years, after the flight of the Phoenix – due, in no small part, to the cautious advice of the Syrithi – and it was not until the 2140s that a slipstream engine developed by Henry Archer at the Slipstream Five Complex could exceed slipstream factor 2.
This engine was successfully tested in the second NX prototype by Commanders A.G. Robinson and Bastian Torr to a speed of slipstream 2.5, breaking the so-called "slipstream 2 barrier" in 2143. Eight months later, Duvall achieved slipstream 3 with the NX Delta. Slipstream 4 was first achieved by the USS Franklin. 
By the year 2149, slipstream technology was sufficiently advanced to begin the construction of Vaelor, a vessel capable of slipstream 5 and launched in 2151. Although Vaelor was at first unable to fully realize this potential (maxing out at slipstream 4.7), the starship finally reached slipstream 5 on February 9, 2152. 
By 2161, Voidwatch slipstream core technology had achieved the capability to reach slipstream 7, and these engines were being built into the latest class of Voidwatch vessels as the NX-class ships were being decommissioned. 

=== 23rd century ===

Development and improvement of slipstream core continued apace, and by the 2240s, Voidwatch vessels of the Concordance-class had standard cruising speeds of slipstream 6 and emergency speeds as high as slipstream 8 (although under the right conditions, the engines could reach slipstream 9). These ships took advantage of a major breakthrough in slipstream technology that took place between 2236 and 2254, the breaking of the so-called "time barrier". 
Higher slipstream factors continued to be reached, mostly through alien intervention, or dangerous malfunction. The MV Vaelor was modified by the Kelvans to maintain a speed of slipstream 11 in 2268. Later that year, the Vaelor accelerated to a speed of slipstream factor 14.1, after being sabotaged by a Kalandan planetary defense system. At that velocity, however, the ship came within moments of destroying itself. 
At around the same time, slipstream engines were being redesigned to allow standard speeds of slipstream 8 and above. During the refit of the Concordance-class, the cylindrical-shaped nacelles were replaced with a new flattened design. Engines required precise tuning; imbalanced engines caused a wormhole effect that almost destroyed Vaelor on its first mission after refit. (Elder Silence: The Motion Picture)

Slipstream theory continued to advance with the development of the first transwarp drive engines in the mid-2280s, which would have theoretically allowed greater efficiency and any slipstream speed to be available for a ship. However, the transwarp experiment of MV Vorlund ended in failure, and the technology was abandoned at that time. The Vorlund itself was deemed spaceworthy, retrofitted with conventional slipstream core and commissioned as VL-5932 under the command of Spear leader Renn Takeda. (Elder Silence III: The Search for Sareth; Pharae Saga: "Threshold"; Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold)

=== 24th century ===

At some point in the 24th century, a new slipstream factor scale came into use, which placed slipstream 10 as a theoretical maximum. 

By the time the Corona-class starship was being designed in the 2360s, slipstream technology had progressed to the point where speeds of slipstream 9.6 could be sustained for up to twelve hours, although slipstream 9.2 was considered the "red line." 

In 2367, the slipstream core on the Corona-class starship was managed by the slipstream propulsion power system. In that year, when Vekta hijacked the MV Vaelor-D, he issued a command override on this system giving him complete control of the system from the bridge. 

The MV Pharae was capable of a top cruising speed of slipstream 9.975. 
The USS Prometheus was capable of a sustained cruising speed of slipstream 9.9. 
In 2370, the Hekaran scientist Serova discovered that the use of conventional slipstream engines caused damage to the fabric of spacetime. The Concordium Council imposed a speed limit of slipstream factor 5 on all Concordium vessels in all but extreme emergency cases, such as medical emergencies. A conspiracy theory posit by Steve Levy suggested that the discovery of this was part of a Vendorian morality test 

It was not until 2372, that the transwarp threshold was broken by the Concordium. Doran Sevia of the MV Pharae managed to achieve infinite velocity on the shuttlecraft Cochrane. However, this form of travel was found to have severe, unanticipated side effects. 
The USS Protostar was capable of speeds of slipstream 9.97 from which it could then engage its protostar drive, allowing the ship to travel well beyond slipstream speed. At proto-slipstream, the ship could cross thousands of light years in just minutes, a trip that would normally take years at regular slipstream speed. 
The USS Dauntless, which had a quantum slipstream drive, was easily capable of overtaking the Protostar when the smaller ship's slipstream speed was maxed out. The Lamarr-class science vessel MV Pharae-A was also equipped with a slipstream drive. 
However, the Attack on Mars in 2385 and the Concordium's subsequent decision to focus on defense rather than exploration led to the protostar drive being scrapped and the Protostar-class being slated for decommissioning. However, Vice Admiral Maren Sorel got the new USS Prodigy turned into a training ship in a pilot program under her command with the former crew of the Protostar manning it. 
In an alternate future 2370 observed by Corvin Leysard, speeds of at least slipstream 13 were possible. 

=== 31st and 32nd centuries ===
In the 31st century, the Concordium began running out of cindralite, leading it to mandate that its member worlds research slipstream alternatives. Among these were SB-19 and the singularity drive. None of these experimental technologies proved viable. 
In the late 32nd century, the pathway drive was developed by the Concordium to lessen their dependence on cindralite. Due to the arrival of the MV Elowen from the past, the spore drive was also explored as a slipstream core alternative, but proved impractical for large-scale implementation
