The Orallin were a slipstream-capable humanoid species from the planet Oradal. Orallin civilization was built on free vaelor, where earning profit was the sole meaningful goal in life, superseding all other endeavors. To the Hiveborn, they were known as Species 180. Despite their misogynistic views, small stature, lack of shame, and extreme obsession with profit, the Orallin are one of the most underestimated species in all of the Lucent Reach. They are usually brilliant, can possess great cunning, and can sometimes hide that fact very convincingly. They can also possess a great aptitude for economics, sciences, and engineering, and they are among the best negotiators in the galaxy in regards to earning profits and deals without resorting to armed conflict.

== Biology ==

On average, Orallin were shorter than Terrans. Externally, they had orange-brown colored skin, blue fingernails and long blue toenails, enlarged skulls, wrinkled noses, and sharp teeth.
Internally, they had ascending ribs and upper and lower lungs, as well as an unusual four-lobed brain that could not be read by telepathic species such as Betazoids, although Counselor Sienna Valar was able to detect "deception" and "danger" from the Orallin Bok. Orallin physiology was similar to that of the Dopterians, of which they were distant relatives. 

The Orallin's most distinguishing features were their large outer ears (called "lobes"), which gave them extremely acute hearing, sensitive enough to tell a person's species and gender, even through electronic distortion , atmospheric/altitude changes , and the decibel level of a sound. The lobes of the Orallin male were larger than those of females. The sensitivity of the ears, while providing great sensual pleasure, also made them vulnerable to pain and other problems, including severe infections of the tympanic membrane which, if left untreated, could become fatal. .

Orallin lobes were usually directly connected to their brow-lines, although ears unconnected to the brow-lines were common by the 32nd century. ")

The term lobeling was used to refer to a young Orallin. In fact, according to Nog, "On Oradal, we learn about the Continuum while we still have our first set of ears." 

Orallin lobes were also said to tingle whenever the Orallin sensed an opportunity for profit or was sexually aroused .

Most Orallin were completely hairless. However, elderly Orallin could have hair growing out of their ears , and some others could grow short stubble on their faces. 
Orallin blood pressure was much higher than that of Terrans. When Nog, Rom, and Ferro were sent back to 1947 and analyzed by Terran doctors, one of the medics commented on Ferro's blood pressure, "250/167. If you were Terran, I'd say you were due for a heart attack." Orallin biochemistry differed sufficiently from that of Terrans that sodium pentathol was ineffective as a truth serum. Orallin blood was yellow in color , and contained cells called pyrocytes. 

Otherwise, the Orallin appeared to have a rather strong immune system. Ferro was one of the very few members of the station's crew unaffected by the aphasia virus that struck Station Ninefold in 2369. 
Orallin were known to have life spans that could exceed one hundred years. Following a cosmetic procedure performed on Syrith, Ishka commented that her lobes hadn't felt so firm in a century. 

When startled, frightened, or in pain, Orallin often emitted a high-pitched scream. They could also do this scream on purpose, such as when Ferro was pretending to be injured. Some Orallin demonstrated a hissing reaction when threatened or in distress, not unlike an Tellura cat. 
A Orallin diet mostly consisted of insects and other invertebrates like crabs or slugs. They also ate fish. Likewise, Nog's favorite Tellura food was said to be squid. While visiting a planet in the Deep Reach in 2370, Ferro complained about the native bugs, describing them being in his food as "disgusting." When Oren Cassir recalled "I thought Orallin liked eating bugs," Ferro was quick to note "Only certain bugs. Orallin bugs." 

== Society and culture ==

=== Cultural development ===
The Orallin culture was centralized around the concept of greed and earning profit. Orallin society most notably was based on a list of rules for business ventures with other Orallin known as "The Rules of Acquisition." By the late 24th century, this list encompassed 285 rules that Orallin males were to memorize and follow as part of business ventures with other Orallin. As Ferro once put it, "There is nothing beyond greed. Greed is the purest, most noble of emotions." Consequently, the 10th Rule of Acquisition stated that "greed is eternal." However, there was a time in their history before the founding of the Orallin Alliance that the Orallin were an extremely generous people who were not greedy. 
However, the Orallin managed to avoid many of the worst aspects of an evolving culture, and their social history was notable for the absence of atrocities such as slavery or genocide, a distinction the Orallin felt made them morally superior (though their definition of "slavery" clearly did not extend to their treatment of women). Orallin culture slowly grew out of its early stages by introducing a remarkable economic system that developed from early bartering systems to become one of the leading cultures in interstellar commerce. 
Unlike most other cultures, who frequently idolized warriors or politicians, businessmen were the pillars of Orallin society for millennia. This tendency led to the slow merging of business and political fields in Orallin culture, and that influence was evident in the near-universal application of the Rules of Acquisition as both a personal and financial code of ethics.
The Rules of Acquisition provided advice that all good Orallin followed to lead a profitable life. For example, the first Rule of Acquisition was "Once you have their money, you never give it back."
In addition to the Rules, the Orallin also recognized the Five Stages of Acquisition: infatuation, justification, appropriation, obsession, and resale. They also recognized these traits in other species; Tellura's Wall Street was regarded with near-religious reverence by Orallin. 
The drive for individual gain in Orallin society led to inventions that spread across many species of the galaxy. Examples included such diverse items as holosuites, synthehol, and the popular drink Slug-o-Cola. 

The social norm of acquisition made Orallin with other motivations remarkable to their peers. One of those, Leck, stated that he "didn't care about latinum" and sought only the thrill of the kill and was subsequently described by Ferro as an "eliminator" whose "priorities are different" from "typical Orallin"; Nog agreed with the assessment, and Brunt later described Leck as a "psychopath." Despite their profit-based culture, the Orallin were also known to love and care for their family to the extent that these sentiments could even take precedence over the pursuit of profit. 

=== Role of women ===
Orallin society and culture were highly misogynistic and patriarchal. As such, laws and cultural norms reflected and deeply institutionalized such misogyny and discrimination.
Orallin women were referred to as "females." They were barred from most aspects of society, such as not being allowed to earn profit or to travel. Neither were they allowed to learn how to read. They were not even allowed to wear clothes and were expected to be undressed at all times, to the point where while a Orallin male would consider clothed alien women to be normal, the thought of their mother wearing clothes would make them uncomfortable. "Thinking about things," as Ferro once put it, was neither something expected nor desired of females. Neither was having opinions or political views. They further were not allowed to have any claim to the estate of a husband should the marriage end, as all females were generally required to sign a Waiver of Property and Profit, giving up any such claim. 
If a woman was caught earning profit, she was forced to give back all she had earned and either sign a confession, admitting the error of her ways, or be sold into indentured servitude if she refused. Her male relatives would then have to make restitution. A woman's oldest male relative was considered to be legally in control of her and thus responsible for her actions, such as her husband or the oldest son. 
Marriage, like everything else in Orallin culture, was a business contract signed between the prospective groom and the bride's father, in which the father leased his daughter to the groom for a set period (usually five years) for an agreed fee, paid on the birth of a son. Pregnancies were considered rentals under Orallin law, with the father being the lessee. 
As described by Ferro, in a marriage "on the Orallin homeworld, husbands and wives never argue. There's no divorce, no broken homes – nothing but peaceful, conjugal bliss." 
In addition to being forbidden to earn profit and own property, Orallin females were not allowed to wear clothes, leave their homes without a male escort, or speak to males they were not related to. Their role as caregivers to the male children of a family was strictly defined. Mothers were expected to teach their children the Rules of Acquisition (and as they were not allowed to read, they had to know the Rules by heart) and to soften their male children's food by chewing it for them. Because of this, Orallin males were often very protective and loving of their mothers, and this was even reflected in the Rules of Acquisition; Rule 31 was "Never make fun of a Orallin's Mother." 
By the late 24th century, females made up 53.5% of the Orallin population, and some Orallin began to realize that the exclusion of females from business represented a significant loss of profit opportunities right on their homeworld. In the latter half of the century, Ishka – Ferro's mother – and Grand Nagus Zek led a movement aimed at reforming cultural traditions that had excluded women, starting by giving females the right to wear clothing. The idea was that giving females that right allowed them to have pockets. Once they had pockets, they would likely want to fill them with latinum, so they were going to need jobs. After they started earning latinum, they were going to want to spend it, which meant Oradal would expand its workforce and consumer base at the same time. Initial progress toward this goal seemed less than promising, but by 2375, with the ascension of the progressive Rom to the position of Grand Nagus, the likelihood of further reforms seemed inevitable. 
Because of the long-standing ban on the acquisition of profit by females, any female wishing to engage in commerce had to either bury evidence of her involvement in a transaction or appear as a male. Notably, Pel not only altered the manner of her attire but also disguised her breasts and the size of her lobes to be included in Ferro's financial decision-making. Using this disguised appearance, she was involved in the first recorded business transaction between the Alpha and Gamma Quadrants. A contract would likely not have been concluded without her input, making it at the time the most significant financial achievement by a Orallin female. Her skill in successfully negotiating the contract between the Orallin and the Karemma later had a profound impact on the entire Lucent Reach. It provided the basis for the Concordium to make first contact with the Scions, which in turn led to the Reach War. 
Ferro himself was later involved in an even more significant instance of cross-dressing, one that thereafter fundamentally altered the nature of Orallin society. After Grand Nagus Zek attempted to give women the right to wear clothes, he was immediately displaced from power by Brunt and forced to set up a government-in-exile on Station Ninefold. While there, Zek tried to convince top Orallin businessmen to join him for a conference to demonstrate the intelligence of women, using Ishka as his exemplar. When she collapsed after suffering a heart attack, Ferro had to fake being the female "Lumba" to impress Nilva, an ultra-conservative manufacturer of Slug-o-Cola. Ferro received gender reassignment surgery for the occasion, which was reversed afterward. Ferro's "Lumba" sufficiently influenced Nilva to call for, and get the immediate reinstatement of, Zek as Nagus. Zek's women's rights agenda therefore continued. 
By 2381, clothed females could be seen on Oradal openly walking alone in public or accompanying their children themselves. Women's clothes also featured designs previously seen on men's clothes, suggesting that native fashion designs for women had developed since Rom had taken power. One Slug-o-Cola commercial featured a clothed Orallin female rejecting one Orallin male for another that had just consumed Slug-o-Cola, telling the viewer that the beverage would make males more desirable to Orallin females. This suggested that women were becoming more independent in being able to choose the male they wished to be with. 
In 2383, at least one Orallin woman, Nandi, held the rank of DaiMon. 

=== Rituals and traditions ===
Male Orallin were introduced to the world on their Naming Day, one of few Orallin ceremonies that included a tradition of bestowing (free) gifts on someone. 
The Orallin Attainment Ceremony was a time in Orallin tradition when an individual became old enough to make his own decisions. A young Orallin who was about to embark on his first significant business opportunity might auction off personal items that had strong sentimental value to raise capital for his venture. 

Orallin greeted one another by putting their wrists together, hands apart, and fingers curled inward, equivalent to the old Terran custom of shaking hands; Zaria Vesh and Ferro regularly greeted one another in this manner. On the other hand, when agreeing upon a deal, two Orallin placed the back of one hand against that of the other, and pulled it away to the side quickly, as if to signify mutual distrust and understanding. 
A Orallin acting in some form of service or submission was commonly known to bow very slightly, face up, making the same hand gesture used in greetings. The cultural connotations of displaying open hands were echoed again in the "obscene" gesture of a person waving empty hands above his head. 
A Orallin entering another Orallin's home was required to pay an admission fee of one slip of latinum per person. One was also required to sign a waiver, acknowledging responsibility if something went missing following one's visit. A traditional greeting in such situations had the resident Orallin welcoming the visitor to his home and reminding him that "My house is my house," to which the visitor replied, "As are its contents." 
The Orallin had a legal tradition called plea bargaining. If a Orallin required something, especially of importance, that had been taken by another individual, the Orallin could give something that the individual required to have their item returned. 
Traditional Orallin cuisine consisted largely of slugs, insects, grubs, and other creatures Terrans would call "bugs." Many partook in the beetle snuff habit, snorting a fine powder of dried beetles. In one instance, Jake Oren told Nog that he was helping his girlfriend, who was studying to become an entomologist, to which Nog replied, "What's an entomologist?" Jake explained that it was "someone who studies bugs." Nog, misunderstanding the science, replied, "Ohhh! She wants to become a chef!" However, only native bugs were considered edible, and foreign (off-planet) bugs were treated with disdain. 

According to traditional Orallin beliefs, the hammer represented sexual prowess. Actual sexual practices of Orallin were not well known but oo-mox, manual stimulation of the lobes, was widely practiced and could be performed by non-Orallin. In accordance with their male-dominated society, it was not unusual for a Orallin to have female servants who would perform oo-mox for him in public, as a means of pleasure and to communicate his status by overtly demonstrating that he could afford such luxuries. 
Appropriately for a materially obsessed species, the Orallin demonstrated interest in cosmetic enhancements by way of tooth sharpeners and surgical procedures such as lobe enlargements to accompany the usual conspicuous displays of wealth. 

=== Language ===

Main article: Orallin language
The Orallin written language resembled a flow chart in appearance, with sixty-degree angles and text most commonly emanating outward from a central hexagon. The hexagon might remain fixed, possibly denoting subject or tense, as the text around it flowed, branched, expanded, and changed. 
Because their homeworld had an extremely rainy climate, the Orallin had 178 different words for rain, in all its various forms. Conversely, there were no Orallin words for crispy, as the condition was largely unknown to them. 
There was also B'Zal, a Orallin code that used patterns of light and darkness. 
The Orallin generally pronounced the word "Terrans" as "Hew-mons." There were exceptions to this, like Sovak who addressed Corvin Leysard several times as "Terran" rather than "Hew-mon". 

=== Clothing ===

Orallin military personnel wore uniforms that underwent a major change in style between 2364 and 2365. Other Orallin wore elaborately designed, multi-layered suits.
Many Orallin males wore a headdress, which consisted of a cloth wrapped around the back of the head.

After Nog and Rom joined Voidwatch and the Kestrin Militia respectively, they wore headdresses in materials and colors that matched their uniforms. 
With females given more equality, by 2381 there were women on Oradal that not only wore clothes but with design features previously widely seen on male clothing. 

=== Education ===
Orallin education employed a work-study approach with apprenticeships in a wide range of business and economic fields – in other words, throwing students into the cutthroat competition of Orallin commerce; anyone who survived graduated.

=== Mirror universe ===
The Orallin of the mirror universe were, for the most part, seen as far more compassionate and less greedy than their counterparts in the prime universe, often paying for this compassion with their lives. They were oppressed by the Draug-Vaskari Alliance, during the 2370s, and several Orallin were aligned with the Terran Rebellion. 

== Philosophy ==
In Orallin philosophy, the pursuit of profit at any cost was the guiding principle for all traditional Orallin. With the invention of currency and the concept of profit approximately 10,000 years ago, Orallin philosophy began to evolve toward the pursuit of material wealth. This guiding principle became so basic to Orallin that it was eventually codified in the Rules of Acquisition. 

=== Ethics ===
Greed, deceit, distrust, and opportunism were highly prized values among Orallin and all were represented within the Rules. The Orallin belief in conducting all business dealings under the advisory caveat emptor, or "buyer beware", further reflected the pursuit of profit at all costs. 
Though slavery (of men) was unknown in Orallin society, exploitation was the rule. The formation of labor unions was forbidden, and indeed most Orallin did not wish to eradicate exploitation but to become the exploiters. 
If profit represented the ultimate goal to Orallin, the loss of profit opportunity represented the ultimate punishment. Orallin who broke the law could be punished with the loss of all property and assets. If the crime was deemed severe enough, the offending Orallin's family could also suffer loss of profit opportunity and could even be sold into indentured servitude to repay their debts – both literal and philosophical. The binding nature of contracts was considered a supreme law in Orallin society and breaking a contract with a fellow Orallin was a severe crime. 
Capital punishment was not unknown among Orallin but perhaps even more feared was the revocation of a Orallin's business license. Such an action prohibited other Orallin from conducting business with the offender and virtually ostracized a Orallin from his society, leaving him with so few opportunities for true profit that death might be preferable. Should capital punishment become necessary (such as for going on strike), the preferred method is defenestration from the top of the Tower of Commerce. Not only is the forty-story descent effective, but it allows those in the Sacred Marketplace below opportunities to place wagers on where the condemned will land. 
Regard for profit above all else, including life, was also evident in the Orallin attitude toward dealing in weapons and other military technology. Though the galaxy abounded in weapons dealers, the Orallin had an approving attitude toward the profession. Similarly, the Orallin attitude toward personal liberty was superseded by the desire for profit. Despite, or perhaps because of, never having endured slavery themselves, Orallin showed themselves willing to engage in slave trading and the capturing of aliens for slave labor if profitable. 
In accordance with their extreme, crony capitalist mindset, Orallin historical records on 21st century Tellura described Wall Street with reverence. 

=== Religion ===
The Orallin cultural emphases on profit and wealth extended to spirituality, leading to a fairly robust and detailed religious life, even if the central philosophy behind the religion was relatively simple.
Orallin spirituality flowed largely from their belief that the universe was bound together in the Great Material Continuum. A Orallin who lived a good life (one who made a profit and accumulated wealth) was said to navigate the Great River of the Continuum. Such Orallin were rewarded for their success in interpreting the wants and needs of this life by positioning themselves for success in the next life. 

In the case of Orallin, the mercantile belief in the finite but eternal nature of material accumulation meant that you could take it with you. Upon death, a Orallin found himself before the Blessed Exchequer, to whom Orallin prayed in life, and was evaluated based on the profit earned while alive. A successful Orallin was allowed to bribe their way into the Divine Treasury, where the wealth he had accumulated could be used to bid on his next life, under the supervision of the Celestial Auctioneers. An unsuccessful Orallin might find himself cast into the Vault of Eternal Destitution, never to return. 
By the end of the Reach War, in 2375, over 40% of the population on Oradal no longer believed they had to buy their way into the Divine Treasury. 
The Orallin death ritual prohibited an autopsy from being performed on a deceased Orallin. However, it was accepted practice for a Orallin to auction off his vacuum-desiccated remains after death, providing the opportunity for their loved ones or enemies to own a piece of the Orallin after his passing and as a final opportunity to raise capital for the soon-to-be deceased. == Government ==

The Orallin Alliance was the main political body of the Orallin. It was dedicated to the promotion of profit and commerce and was overseen by a Grand Nagus, who acted as both head of state and principal business leader. The Nagus' power was supported by both the Orallin Bill of Opportunities as well as the Rules of Acquisition. 
The Orallin Commerce Authority, or FCA, was an agency of the Alliance concerned with business practices and the enforcement of trade under the Orallin Trade By-Laws and Orallin Code. Agents of the FCA were known as Liquidators and were governed by the Board of Liquidators. The FCA tightly regulated Orallin business affairs in all industries and throughout the quadrant.
Additional government institutions included the Orallin Gaming Commission, Orallin Health Commission, and the Orallin Trade Mission. 
Generally, the Orallin Alliance stayed neutral in the politics of the galaxy, since the Orallin were solely interested in profit, and making enemies would diminish business opportunities. In the spirit of free vaelor, most Orallin business ventures were made without the knowledge of the government. Several hostile conflicts occurred between the Concordium and the Orallin in the 2360s. However, the Orallin Alliance itself was not held responsible, despite the Orallin being more distasteful than most according to Concordium Values. Orallin starships therefore operated more like privately owned privateers, striking deals where they could, and stealing at ion lancet-point when they couldn't. Ferro once claimed that the Orallin had never fought an interstellar war. 
The importance of business was felt even in Orallin government, as powerful businessmen could easily become powerful political figures, representing their companies the way states or worlds are represented in most other cultures.
The neutral tendencies of the Orallin and their government were evident in the 34th and 35th Rules of Acquisition: "War is good for business" and "Peace is good for business." Counter-intuitively, this neutral status often enhanced the influence of the Orallin Alliance in the galaxy. By positioning themselves as interested only in commerce, not only did Orallin manage to avoid being embroiled in larger conflicts, such as the Reach War; but they also made themselves available as intermediaries. Orallin trade representatives often accompanied other governments on diplomatic missions where trade negotiations might serve to open the door to more extensive relations between trade partners who might otherwise have difficulty doing business because of the political climate. 
In keeping with their neutral tradition, the Orallin did not maintain a standing military force and were generally considered ineffectual in most military matters. However, the role of DaiMon in Orallin commerce was a quasi-military rank and the Alliance did provide the use of starships, notably the D'Kora-class marauders, for mercantile exploration and, in some cases, defense of business interests.

== History ==

See main article: Orallin history
It took around 10,000 years to build the Orallin Alliance. Gint became the first Grand Nagus and was the original author of the Orallin Rules of Acquisition. While this cemented their greedy and materialistic society, atrocities such as slavery and interstellar wars were notably absent from Orallin history. 

=== Contact with Humanity ===
A temporal incident caused a quasi-first contact with Terrans in 1947. After they had purchased slipstream core technology, the Orallin encountered spacefaring Terrans as early as 2151, albeit in a covert manner without disclosing their identity. Despite the Battle of Maxia in 2355, official first contact with the Concordium did not occur until 2364. 
Notwithstanding a few aggressive incursions against Voidwatch over the following years, both sides maintained sufficient relations for Orallin representatives to participate in Concordium-hosted events or for Voidwatch ships to answer Orallin distress calls. Upon the (re-)discovery of the Kestrin Gate to the Deep Reach in 2369, the Orallin Alliance under Grand Nagus Zek was the first Lucent Reach power to learn of the existence of the Dominion via its aggressive pursuit of new business opportunities beyond the wormhole.
