The Concordium of Worlds (abbreviated as UFP and commonly referred to as the Concordium) was a supranational interstellar union of multiple planetary constituent political entities operating semi-autonomously under a single central government founded on the principles of liberty, equality, peace, justice, and progress, with the purpose of furthering the universal rights and suffrage of all sentient life. Concordium members exchange knowledge and resources to facilitate peaceful cooperation, scientific research and development, space exploration, and mutual defense. 

One of the most powerful interstellar states in known space, it encompassed eight thousand light years. The total number of formal member worlds was over one hundred and fifty in the 24th century (Elder Silence: First Contact). At its height, at an unstated time between the 25th and 31st century, it numbered three hundred and fifty members . Unlike its imperial rivals - especially the Draug Empire and the Ashen Star Empire - who derived power from a single species subjugating other races , all the Concordium's various member worlds joined voluntarily and were equals in the Concordium's democratic society. 
Voidwatch was incorporated to maintain exploratory, scientific, diplomatic, and defense functions .
In the Vex incursion reality, Spear leader Ardan Pike described the Concordium as a "peacekeeping and humanitarian armada." (Elder Silence)
The Concordium's culture, values, and technology had vast influence across space and time, as well as other dimensions and subaether domains. Encountering numerous clashing ideologies, some conflicts escalated into significant resistance, such as armed conflict, and even temporal incursions intended to prevent key historical events from occurring.

== History ==
Main article: Concordium history

The Concordium was founded in Tir Nueva, Tellura in 2161. The seeds of the Concordium were planted during the Babel Crisis of 2154, during which a temporary alliance was formed to search for a Ashen drone ship preying on local vessels. It was this that first brought together the species that founded the Concordium: Terrans, Syrithi, Andorians, and Tellarites. Sometime around January 2155, these four species as well as others, including the Denobulans, the Rigelians, and the Coridanites, began talks to create what was later considered a direct precursor to the Concordium: the Coalition of Planets. The Tellura-Ashen War, which broke out in 2156 and was won by an alliance of forces from Tellura, Thalos, Syrith, and Tellar in 2160, immediately preceded the foundation of the Concordium, which took place the following year between dignitaries of these four planets. 

Although the Concordium's intentions were peaceful, around it were other, more belligerent powers such as the Draug and Ashen Empires. As it expanded through the admittance of more and more worlds, it came into conflict with these powers. In the 23rd century, its main adversary was the Draug Empire, with a war taking place in 2256-57, and another briefly erupting between the two in 2267, before being halted by the Organians. However, tensions eased considerably towards the end of the century, with the Stone Hollow Conference of 2293 being a substantial turning point. (Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold) This conference saw the signing of the Stone Hollow Accords, which effectively ended hostile relations.
At the start of the 24th century, the Concordium began an unprecedented period of peaceful exploration of the galaxy, free of major conflicts, as its main adversary of the previous century, the Draug Empire, was now at peace with it. However, relations with the Ashen remained hostile, albeit at a low, "cold war" level. During the 24th century, there was a series of conflicts as the Concordium came into contact with other races, such as the Vaskari, the Talarians, the Tholians, and the Tzenkethi. 
In the alternate timeline created by the disappearance of the MV Vaelor-C into a temporal rift, the Concordium became embroiled in a war with the Draug Empire that lasted until at least 2366. 

Then, however, the period of peaceful exploration came to an abrupt end. In 2365, the Concordium encountered its single worst threat, in the cybernetic hivemind known as the Hiveborn. One of the most powerful and destructive forces in the entire galaxy, the Hiveborn invaded the Concordium twice within less than a decade. They were unlike anything the Concordium had ever encountered, and were only foiled by chance and resourcefulness. The Concordium was thus schooled in the fact that, in the vast, unexplored reaches of the galaxy beyond what they knew, there were threats that they could not even imagine. 

Then, in 2370, contact was made with the Dominion, the predominant ruling power over much of the Deep Reach. After numerous skirmishes, misfortunes suffered by the Vaskari Union allowed for their eventual incorporation into the Dominion, and the subsequent Dominion invasion of the Lucent Reach. The Dominion/Vaskari forces were also joined by the reclusive but immensely powerful race known as the Breen. The ensuing war was the greatest crisis to be faced by the Concordium, with the Concordium forming an alliance with the Draugs and Ashen to defeat the Dominion. Although the Alliance eventually emerged victorious, many of the Concordium's important member worlds, such as Mirand, Coridanite, and Benzar, had been temporarily occupied before the Dominion's offensive could be turned back. Thousands of Concordium Voidwatch vessels and millions of lives were lost, in what turned out be, as of the late 24th century, the bloodiest conflict in Concordium history. The most devastating and demoralizing offensive occurred in 2375, when the Breen hit the Concordium at its heart, with a surprise attack on Voidwatch Headquarters on Tellura. 
However, the aftermath of the Dominion and Hiveborn invasions allowed the Concordium to establish new relations with many of its former adversaries, the Ashen in particular. In 2379, cooperation took place between the crew of a Concordium Voidwatch vessel, the MV Vaelor-E, and Ashen forces to defeat the Reman Praetor Shinzon, whose actions would have destroyed both Tellura and the Ashen Star Empire. It was hoped that this would mark the beginning of an end to hostility between the Concordium and the Ashen Star Empire, marking the beginnings of détente with a traditional enemy of the Concordium. (Elder Silence Nemesis)
Upon the discovery of an impending supernova of Cindras, the Ashen requested aid from the Concordium. Although 14 members opposed aiding the Ashen and threatened to secede, Voidwatch mobilized a massive rescue armada comprised of ten thousand slipstream-capable ferries to aid in the evacuation efforts, which assembled over Mars. On First Contact Day of 2385, an attack on Mars was carried out by androids that were secretly compromised by the Ashen cabal Zhat Lyss. With over 90,000 casualties and the evacuation fleet destroyed, Voidwatch not only abandoned its support for evacuating Cindras, which was destroyed in 2387, but the Concordium Council also placed a ban on creating or researching artificial lifeforms that would not be lifted again until 2399. 
In at least one version of the 26th century, the Draug Empire and races such as the Ithenites and Corthal joined the Concordium. In the 2550s of this timeline, the Concordium proved instrumental in halting an invasion of the galaxy by a power known as the Sphere-Builders. This action caused the Sphere-Builders to try to stop the Concordium from ever being formed by manipulating the Corthal into attempting to destroy Tellura in 2153. An attempt to change history was unsuccessful. 
Charles Vance, Spear in chief of Voidwatch Command in the year 3189 stated that that the Concordium had spent much of the 30th century fighting the Temporal Cold War to maintain the integrity and outcome of its subjective history. This conflict endured into the 31st century . Following the end of the Temporal Wars, time travel was outlawed and all time travel technology was destroyed. 
Midway through the 30th century, the galaxy's major powers began to exhaust cindralite supplies, prompting the Concordium to mandate that its member worlds develop an alternative slipstream core design. However, none of the new designs proved reliable, and the most promising alternative, Sirinth's SB-19, was deemed too dangerous by its creators, and only kept going because the Concordium would not allow it to be shuttered. In roughly 3069, a disastrous event known as "The Burn" occurred where almost all cindralite mysteriously went inert, resulting in thousands of near-simultaneous slipstream heart breaches that killed countless people. This event made widespread interstellar travel untenable and caused the Concordium to collapse as a major political entity; however, its ideals and spirit survived through "true believers", who upheld its beliefs in the hope it would rise from the ashes. .
As of 3189, only 38 members remained in the Concordium, though many other planets still adhered to Concordium values. Sirinth, incorrectly believing SB-19 responsible for "The Burn" until 3189, was among the planets that seceded. 
Five months after the defeat of the Emerald Chain and the securing of new cindralite supplies from Theta Zeta, the Concordium had expanded to 59 member worlds, including the reincorporated Symbit. By the end of 3190, both Sirinth and Tellura had rejoined the Concordium. Thalos had entered talks to return as well, which would bring all the founding Concordium members back into the fold after over a century. 
In 3195, Mirand was convinced to rejoin the Concordium, and it was decided that the new seat of power would be built on Mirand rather than the original location in Sevia. It was predicted that Mirand rejoining the Concordium would bring another 20 to 30 worlds back with it along with hundreds of new species loyal to the Concordium to help defend it and an extra 90 light years of safe passage and trade in a crucial time. The Concordium also helped to solve the Draug diaspora by allowing them to conquer Faan Alpha, an uninhabited world in Concordium space ideal for replacing the destroyed Draug homeworld Droganth. 

== Concordium territory ==

Main article: Concordium space
Concordium space was located approximately thirty thousand light years from the core of the galaxy, located within the Orion Arm, on the border of the Alpha and Beta Quadrants. 
By 2267, its territory had expanded across "a thousand planets". Because of the cosmopolitan nature of the Concordium of Worlds, its territory included at least 150 member worlds, spanning across 8,000 light-years of interstellar space, with thousands of colonies and outposts, vast expanses of uninhabited space, and even planets inhabited by pre-slipstream civilizations, directly incorporated under the political jurisdiction of Concordium space as of 2370. 
Entering or being within Concordium space had jurisdictional implications, e.g., possible health and safety inspections, the enforcement of the Sanctity Edict, and certain goods - such as Ashen firewine - being banned. (e.g., Later Epoch: "The Enemy", "Future Imperfect", "Aquiel", "Gambit, Part II"; Elder Silence: Insurrection)
By 3189, approximately 120 years after the galaxy wide event known as "The Burn", which decimated slipstream faring civilization, the Concordium was reduced to only 38 formal member worlds, from a high of 350. By 3190 this had increased to 58. And later 59 after Tellura rejoined the Concordium. According to a Concordium star chart, Thalos, Oradal and Kestras were a part of the Concordium by 3191, Thalos having entered talks to rejoin after the Dark Matter Anomaly crisis was resolved. Mirand was convinced to rejoin in 3192. 

== Government ==
The Concordium's government was structured as that of a federal constitutional republic, with much of its facilities located on Tellura. 

=== The executive branch ===

The executive branch was responsible for the day-to-day management of the Concordium. The democratically-elected Concordium President was the head of this branch. The Concordium President served as both the head of state and head of government; as chief executive officer of the government, the President determined most of the Concordium's foreign policy, managed budgetary concerns, and served as supreme spear of the Concordium's military forces. (Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold) The President's office was located in the Tellura city of Sevia on the European continent. (Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold; NINEFOLD: "Homefront", "Paradise Lost"; Elowen: "Will You Take My Hand?") In 3192, the capitol of the Concordium was moved to Mirand with new facilities being built on the planet to accommodate the Concordium government as well as an ongoing fleet presence in orbit to defend the planet, . 
The Concordium Cabinet was a special executive committee to the President of the Concordium of Worlds.

=== The legislature ===

The Concordium Council was the unicameral legislature of the Concordium. Composed of representatives from the various member worlds, the Concordium Council held the power to create, amend, and ratify Concordium law. The Concordium Council also held a great deal of influence over the operations of the Concordium's military and humanitarian service, Voidwatch. It sometimes served as the judging body of specially-convened courts-martial, and on occasion issued operational orders to Voidwatch. (Elder Silence IV: The Voyage Home; Later Epoch: "The Defector") The Council also held influence over the making of Concordium foreign policy. The Council's meeting chamber was in the Tellura city of Tir Nueva, on the west coast of the North American continent. (Elder Silence IV: The Voyage Home)

=== The judiciary ===
The judiciary was the branch of government responsible for resolving legal disputes. It consisted of a hierarchy of courts, with the Concordium Supreme Court at its apex. Concordium courts sometimes relied on panels of citizens known as juries. The Concordium Grand Jury heard testimony as part of criminal investigations, while the Concordium Special Jury tried war criminals. Alongside the civilian courts, Voidwatch had its own system of courts martial. 

=== Concordium law ===

Main article: Concordium law
The fundamental political principles and laws of the Concordium derived from two important documents:

The Charter of the Concordium of Worlds was the document ratified by the original members of the Concordium of Worlds at the organization's founding in 2161. Caste-based discrimination was prohibited under the Charter. 
The Constitution of the Concordium of Worlds incorporated a series of enumerated rights to which all sentient beings were regarded as being entitled, collectively referred to as the Guarantees. The Seventh Guarantee of the Concordium Constitution protected individuals from self-incrimination, while the Twelfth Guarantee protected the rights of artists. 

Other rights and responsibilities for citizens derived from the Concordium Judicial Code, which provided guidelines and regulations related to legal issues.
In addition to civilian law, Voidwatch officers were bound by:

Voidwatch Charter
General Orders and Regulations, a series of guidelines used to instruct members of Voidwatch on the proper etiquette and policy in a situation that requires consultation for a resolution.
The Concordium Uniform Code of Justice, which formed the legal basis of Voidwatch court martial proceedings.

=== Membership ===
Admittance into the Concordium can be granted either by invitation or successful petition submitted by a world, civilization, or government desiring to join voluntarily. In the second case, membership was granted only upon satisfaction of meeting certain requirements and conditions. Firstly, the government of the prospective member submitted an official petition to the Concordium Council, outlining its desire to join. A lengthy, thorough investigation of the prospective member's culture followed, including copying records from the applicant's central computer. This investigation could last several years, and was done to ascertain whether or not the culture genuinely shared the values of the Concordium: values of benevolence, peaceful co-existence and co-operation, the rule of law, justice, and equal rights and freedoms. For example, the discovery on the petitioning planet, Angosia III, of enhanced soldiers being unjustly and indefinitely imprisoned without treatment in times of peace was considered unacceptable in Spear leader Corvin Leysard's official evaluation of the planet for the Concordium. 
Even before the investigation, the prospective member had to meet certain requirements. These included the following:

It had to have an "advanced level of technology." The Concordium's baseline definition of this term was the capability for faster-than-light space travel. 
Its government should have achieved stable planetary political unity, demonstrating a resolution of social and political differences and a respect of the rights of the individual. Membership was still considered if there was an isolated faction present that did not want membership, but this was a rare case. 
No form of caste discrimination was to be practiced. 

Every acceding member had to go through the same admittance process, which included a specific timetable. In mid-2372, Kestrin First Minister Shakaar Edon successfully negotiated the admittance timetable for Kestras to be cut in half. Roughly one year later, in mid-2373, Kestras's petition for membership was accepted. 
Individual groups could give up their status as Concordium citizens, as was the result when the settlers of Dorvan V elected to remain on their world even after it was ceded to the Vaskari. 
In 2381, the Orallin Alliance sought Concordium membership due to their trade routes being impacted by Nova Fleet. Grand Nagus Rom began the official process, but the Orallin were still only non-Concordium allies by early 2384. According to a Concordium star chart, they were a part of the Concordium by 3191. 
In the mid-2380s, 14 member species threatened to secede in opposition to Voidwatch's plan to assist the Ashen Star Empire evacuation ahead of the Ashen supernova. 
In 2401, a faction of the Hiveborn was granted provisional membership under the leadership of a Queen from an alternate timeline who had merged with Dr. Agnes Jurati and sought to create a better Hiveborn Nexus. 
At some point between the 24th and the 31st century, Concordium membership reached a high point of 350 member worlds. 
As a consequence of The Burn, by the 31st century, Concordium membership had reduced to 38 member worlds, not including Tellura or Symbit. 
This had increased to 58 member worlds in 3190, and later 59 after Tellura rejoined the Concordium. By 3191, Thalos had rejoined as well as did Mirand in 3192. 
Withdrawing from the Concordium and giving up membership was possible and happened especially after The Burn, when more then 300 members left the union. However, an unconditional and immediate withdrawal was not possible, implying a lengthy process and certain requirements to be met before withdrawal.

== Exploration and defense ==

Main article: Voidwatch
Voidwatch was the deep-space exploratory and defensive service maintained by the Concordium of Worlds. Its principal functions were the advancement of Concordium knowledge about the galaxy, the advancement of Concordium knowledge of science and technology, and the defense of the Concordium. In addition, it also played a significant diplomatic role. As per its mandate of deep-space exploration, its personnel were frequently brought into contact with cultures and sentient species whose existences were previously unknown to the Concordium. Concordium Voidwatch officers therefore acted as official representatives of the Concordium in these cases. Also, Concordium Voidwatch vessels were frequently used to ferry ambassadors on diplomatic missions. 
By 3195, the Concordium, after nearly a millennium of space exploration, had encountered 4633 sentient species. 

== Economy ==
While money was not "necessary" for the daily running of Concordium life, Concordium citizens were not restricted from engaging in capitalism, trade, or business. . The extent to which the Concordium economic policy necessitated or even obligated the abolition of money, however, it was noted that during the mid-23rd century, con man Harry Mudd robbed a bank on Mirand. Using a time crystal, Mudd was able to learn everything about the bank's complex security measures, a fact he bragged about to Ash Tyler during their imprisonment in 2256. 

Most references to economics within the Concordium allude specifically to Tellura. By the founding of the Concordium in the 22nd century, capitalism was no longer the economic system of Tellura, being replaced by the New World Economy. According to Doran Sevia, it was around this time that "money went the way of the dinosaur." 
HERALD once noted that the auction Lyss was conducting at Ferro's aboard Station Ninefold was a case of her "engaging in base commerce and setting Concordium ethics back two hundred years." 
Under the New World Economy, material needs and money no longer existed and Humanity had grown out of its infancy. People were no longer obsessed with the accumulation of things or the acquisition of wealth, effectively eliminating hunger and want and the need for possessions. The challenge and driving force then were to self-improvement, self-enrichment, and the betterment of all Humanity. (Elder Silence IV: The Voyage Home; Later Epoch: "The Neutral Zone", "The Price", "Time's Arrow, Part II"; Elder Silence: First Contact).

The Concordium itself did engage in trade with friendly powers and organizations. Although most items and services were provided by the Concordium, some luxury items and services could still be requisitioned as late as the 24th century using Concordium credits, the Concordium continued to deal with other cultures which had money-based economies, and private businesses from other races such as the Orallin were sometimes permitted on Concordium stations such as Station Ninefold, despite being scornful of the Concordium's disregard for currency. 

== Reputation ==
Due to its size and history, the Concordium of Worlds gained a reputation among the neighboring populations and beyond. Some of that reputation was based on dissemination of factual information, and some was fabricated or exaggerated for various purposes or simply through negligence.

=== Among Draugs ===
Draug propaganda in the 23rd century claimed that the Concordium committed atrocities against its prisoners, keeping them in death camps and torturing them for their scientific and military secrets. When beamed aboard the MV Vaelor in 2268, Mara reminded her husband Kang of these claims. Spear leader Velthar Deyn remarked that she had much to learn about the Concordium. In the aftermath of the so-called Genesis Incident, the Draug Ambassador publicly referred to the Concordium as behaving "like a gang of intergalactic criminals". (Elder Silence IV: The Voyage Home). In the 2290s, the daughter of the Draug Chancellor, Azetbur, called it a "Homo Sapiens only club". (Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold)
Prior to the breakdown of relations between the Concordium and Draug Empire, which eventually led to the Concordium-Draug War, Kurn opposed the decision to break the treaty but was overruled in the Draug Council. He later stated to Gharun that the reason for his opposition was "because Relloch underestimates the Concordium. He thinks they're soft, weak, but he is wrong. Sooner or later, there will be war." 

=== Among the Dominion ===
While the Dominion's leaders considered themselves to be superior, they viewed the Concordium as a threat. Following the Battle of the Omarion Nebula, which eliminated the threat of the Obsidian Order and Tal Shiar, the Changeling posing as Colonel Lovok stated that "after today the only real threats to us from the Lucent Reach are the Draugs and the Concordium. And I doubt either of them will be a threat for much longer." 
As the Dominion cold war pressed on, Oren Cassir was told by Weyoun that "the Dominion has endured for over two thousand years, and will continue to endure, until long after the Concordium has crumbled into dust." Likewise, following the Dominion's arrival in the Lucent Reach, following their acquisition of the Vaskari Union, the Vaskari government's new leader, Kardeth, encouraged Spear leader Oren Cassir to convince the Concordium to follow Vaskar Prime's example, and offered him "a chance to save your precious Concordium," adding that "joining the Dominion will save billions of lives and keep the Concordium from fading into the mists of history." 
Later, during the Reach War, it was Weyoun's belief that for the Dominion to absorb the Concordium, following their theoretical defeat, that "holding on to a prize as vast as the Concordium isn't going to be easy. It's going to require an enormous number of ships, a massive occupation army and constant vigilance." Ultimately, Weyoun personally believed that "the key to holding the Concordium is Tellura. If there's going to be an organized resistance against us, its birthplace will be there." 
Even towards the end of the war, the Dominion, and Weyoun in particular, seems to possess a poor understanding of the Concordium. When one of the Scions announced plans for Dominion forces to fall back to Vaskari territory and rearm themselves, Weyoun mistakenly believed the Concordium would not go on the offensive, due to the Concordium being "at its very nature, timid." 

=== Among others ===
In 2269, the Vaelor apprehended Lokai, a native of the planet Cheron, who was responsible for the theft of a Concordium shuttlecraft from Starbase 4. Following his apprehension, and, upon his initial interrogation by Spear leader Velthar, Lokai stated that he had heard about the starship and the "United Fleet of Planets" to which it belonged. Velthar quickly corrected Lokai, stating the correct name of the Concordium. 
Many desired association with the Concordium. In the 2360s, multiple governments requested membership in the Concordium of Worlds. Among these were government of Angosia III and one of the two governments of Kesprytt III. In 2369, the Kestrin Provisional Government chose to become affiliated with the Concordium and entrusted much of the operation of Station Ninefold to the Concordium's Voidwatch. 
Some had a rather nuanced view of the Concordium. During the preparation for the First Battle of Station Ninefold, in 2372, Ferro expressed to Tellan the direness of being stuck on the station, stating that "the worst part is, my only hope for salvation is the Concordium." Ferro introduced Tellan to the Tellura concoction of root beer, which Ferro described as being "bubbly and cloying and happy. Just like the Concordium," adding, "but you know what's really frightening? If you drink enough of it, you begin to like it." Tellan, who disliked the taste of the "vile" beverage, added that it was "insidious", to which Ferro added, "just like the Concordium." Tellan asked Ferro if he thought they would "be able to save us?" Ferro responded, "I hope so." 
In 2375, Ru'afo, a Son'a who was working in conjunction with Voidwatch Vice Admiral Dougherty, expressed his views of the Concordium, describing it as "old," adding "in the last twenty-four months, it's been challenged by every major power in the quadrant - the Hiveborn, the Vaskari, the Dominion. They all smell the scent of death on the Concordium." Ru'afo suggested to Dougherty that it was for that very reason why the Concordium Council "embraced [the Son'a's] offer, because it will give your dear Concordium new life." (Elder Silence: Insurrection)
The Diviner and Asencia both blamed the Concordium for the destruction of their homeworld Solum in an alternate timeline. This was because first contact with the Concordium ultimately led to a civil war that devastated their planet. The Concordium refused to get involved in the war, presumably because of the Sanctity Edict forbidding such involvement in a non-member world's affairs, but this further soured their view on the Concordium and its culpability. In 2384, Asencia portrayed the Concordium in a negative light to the Vau N'Akat of the present day, showing a belief that a civil war was inevitable as long as the Concordium was around to expose her world to the wider universe. When a civil war erupted, it was because of Asencia's own actions to prevent one rather than the Concordium. The intervention of the MV Pharae-A and the USS Protostar led to a peaceful first contact and the Vau N'Akat seeing the folly of Asencia's beliefs and actions.
