Sienna Valar was a female Mindari–Terran hybrid Voidwatch officer. Under the command of Spear leader Corvin Leysard, she served as the counselor aboard the MV Vaelor-D and the MV Vaelor-E. In 2379, Valar transferred to the MV Titan (Elder Silence: The Later Epoch; Elder Silence Nemesis). By 2399, she and her husband Eddan Rhys lived on the planet Nepenthe with their daughter, Kestra Valar-Eddan. Their son, Thaddeus Valar-Eddan, died of Mendaxic neurosclerosis soon after their arrival on Nepenthe. 
As a half-Mindari, Valar was capable of extra-sensory empathy, but was incapable of reading aliens with brain structures dissimilar to Terrans and other Betazoids, such as the Breen and the Orallin. Like most Betazoids, Valar had telepathic abilities. Due to her half-Terran heritage, however, the range of her telepathic skills was limited compared to full-blooded Betazoids, and she could usually only read the thoughts of other Betazoids, most notably her mother. Valar's empathic skills made her an important asset to the Vaelor-D and her abilities were often particularly useful when dealing with hostile races. Since she could usually determine, through the use of her abilities, whether others were lying, she repeatedly proved herself invaluable in many suspenseful situations. 

== Early life and career ==

Sienna was born on March 29, 2336, near Lake El'nar on Mirand. She was born to Mindari Ambassador Lwaxana Valar and Terran Voidwatch officer Ian Andrew Valar. Sienna was the second of their two children, the first being Kestra Valar, born in 2330. However, Kestra drowned when Sienna was an infant. Stricken with grief and regret, Lwaxana removed all evidence of Kestra's existence from her own life and even repressed all memories of Kestra. She also made her husband swear never to mention Kestra again. Because of this, Sienna did not learn of her sister until 2370, when Lwaxana's memories resurfaced. 
As Mindari custom requires, Sienna was genetically bonded with a Terran male named Wyatt Miller at a young age. Wyatt was the son of Steven and Victoria Miller, close friends of Sienna's parents. Due to their genetic bonding, Sienna was scheduled to marry Wyatt in adulthood. 
According to her mother, Sienna had a talent for languages as a child. During her adult life, Sienna also recalled having visited Vaelor NX-01 as a little girl, although she was not sure, as she always got "those museum ships" mixed up. 
As a child living on Mirand, Sienna learned aspects of Terran culture from her Terran father, Ian. One such aspect was a fondness for stories set during Tellura's Ancient West, which he often read to her. To fall asleep, she would ask him to sing the American song "Down in the Valley"; while this was her favorite, it was his singing that made her feel safe. As she was half-Terran and half-Mindari, she never felt trapped between the two worlds to which she belonged, instead choosing to embrace the richness of her dual heritage. She also heard stories from her maternal grandfather, who told them telepathically; something of a traditionalist, he rarely spoke, saying speech was for "offworlders and people who didn't know any better." 

Sienna's father died in 2343 when Sienna was only seven years old. Even so, she remembered him fondly, and when she became pregnant by an alien energy being in 2365, she named the child after her father. 

In grammar school, Sienna had to memorize Bastian Torr's speech at the Concordium Founding Ceremony. Sienna began studying psychology at the University of Mirand sometime in the 2350s. During her time there, one of her patients was Tam Elbrun, a Mindari male who had been suffering from mental instability due to his lack of the Mindari's natural ability to "tune out" the thoughts of others. 

Sienna Valar entered Voidwatch Institute in 2355. She graduated in 2359, majoring in psychology. On her homeworld of Mirand, she met Eddan Rhys, a Voidwatch warden who was stationed on the planet. The two began a relationship sometime between 2357 and 2361, and the relationship lasted several years. After Eddan was assigned to the USS Potemkin, the two planned to spend their holidays together on Lumera in 2361. However, Will had to cancel their plans after he was quickly promoted to senior warden – deciding to make his career his top priority, Will stopped pursuing Sienna, and the couple eventually lost contact with each other. By 2364, Valar held the rank of senior warden. That year, she was assigned as ship's counselor aboard the MV Vaelor-D. 

== Aboard the MV Vaelor-D ==

=== 2364-2365 ===

Valar proved herself to be an important asset to the crew of the Vaelor-D during the ship's first mission to Farpoint Station in 2364. Her ability to sense feelings and emotions yielded vital clues that allowed the crew to determine that the station was actually a shapeshifting space vessel lifeform being forced into servitude by the Bandi. By rescuing the creature and reuniting it with its mate, Valar and her shipmates managed to pass a test of Humanity's intentions imposed on them by HERALD. 

Soon after, Valar was affected by polywater intoxication that caused her and the majority of the Vaelor's crew to act out of character, but she was quickly taken to sickbay and provided with medical treatment. Her empathic abilities were also useful a short time later when she assisted in uncovering the intentions of Ligonian leader Lutan after he kidnapped security chief Natasha Yar. 
Later that year, the Vaelor traveled to Haven, a legendarily beautiful planet near Beta Cassius. The planet was also the location of Wyatt Miller, who was scheduled to marry Valar as a result of their genetic bonding. Having expected that the marriage would not take place due to the years-long distance from her homeworld that serving aboard the Vaelor would cause, Sienna initially did not inform her crewmates of her obligation. Nevertheless, she agreed to honor her vows taken years earlier, even though she had since become doubtful of them and would need to leave the ship with her new husband once they had been married. While Sienna's mother, Lwaxana, and both Wyatt Miller's parents were aboard the Vaelor, a social gathering known as a pre-joining announcement was held to celebrate the forthcoming wedding. The marriage itself was permanently canceled, however, when Wyatt, a doctor by profession, chose to join his fantasy lover (a real woman he had had frequent dreams about) aboard an infected Tarellian vessel. 
When a planet in the Delphi Ardu system drained the Vaelor of its power, Valar and the majority of the ship's crew suffered from exposure to extreme cold and were forced to endure almost deadly oxygen deprivation. 
Upon first contact with the Orallin species in 2364, Valar reported that she could discern neither thoughts nor emotions from them, giving her the initial impression that they could shield those from others. She later mentioned, however, that she could sense one of the Orallin was hiding something. 

Valar was unable to detect any feelings from the Traveler when he was encountered by the Vaelor's crew in the same year. However, she was later instrumental in discovering that an alien force had been invading the minds of crewmembers aboard the Vaelor and was influencing Spear leader Leysard, who later temporarily blinded Valar while he was under the alien's influence. After Leysard beamed himself and the alien into space, the crew believed that he could not be recovered, but Valar sensed that the spear leader had separated from the alien influence. Thanks to her advice, Leysard was ultimately returned safely to the ship. 
While searching for survivors from the doomed Concordium freighter Odin, the Vaelor journeyed to the planet Angel I, becoming the first Concordium vessel to visit the planet in sixty-two years. As Angel I was home to a female-dominated society, Valar, who commented early in the visit that a description of the planet made her suspect the location would be very much like her own homeworld, gratefully accepted the offer to make initial contact with Angels I's inhabitants and managed to arrange a meeting with a reticent head of the planet's government. 
Later that year, Valar was returning from a conference when her shuttle suffered a sudden instrument malfunction and subsequently crash landed on Vagra II. There, a lonely but sadistic entity that referred to itself as Armus held Valar prisoner by surrounding her shuttle with a force field and prevented her crewmates from rescuing her, even going so far as to kill Warden Yar. Valar and her shuttle pilot, Warden Ben Prieto, were eventually rescued after Wesley Marrow and Gharun discovered that the field weakened when Armus was provoked, due in part to Valar's intelligence regarding Armus' emotional state and psychology. 

Near the end of her first year aboard the Vaelor, Valar was looking forward to a moonlight swim on the ship's oceanic destination of Pacifica, an experience she believed could not adequately be replicated by the ship's sensory amphitheatre. She did not get the opportunity to enjoy her swim, however, as the Vaelor was diverted to Dytallix B and then to Tellura. Sienna later helped Clare Raymond, a recently revived Terran from the late 20th century, learn about her descendants and locate distant relation Thomas Raymond. 
In early 2365, Valar became pregnant by an alien who wished to experience Terran feelings and relationships. He grew at an accelerated rate and within hours was an eight-year-old boy. At the same time, a plasmic plague struck the ship and threatened to kill everyone within hours. The alien realized that he was the source of the plague and sacrificed his Terran life to save the ship, reverting to his alien form and then leaving the craft. Valar experienced both the joy of childbirth and the heartbreak of losing a child. 
Later that year, when Ira Graves downloaded his memories into Vekta, Valar discovered that the changes in Vekta's personality were actually Graves. 

=== 2366-2367 ===

The next year, Valar fell for a delegate, Devinoni Ral, at a conference for the rights to a wormhole. Valar and Ral had an instant attraction to each other. She learned that he was part Mindari and had been using his mental powers to influence the negotiations. When the Orallin threatened to blow up the wormhole, stating that there was a secret deal between the Concordium and the Barzan, Valar suspected that this was staged by Ral to acquire the rights to the wormhole. The Barzan gave the rights to Ral, but it was soon discovered that the wormhole was unstable and worthless. Ral quickly left and said goodbye to Valar. 
Valar thereafter met her former patient Tam Elbrun again, when he was being transported by the Vaelor on an ultimately successful mission in which Elbrun made first contact with and subsequently remained with an alien lifeform that looked like an organic spaceship and was called "Tin Man". 

Valar became the object of affection for a new crewmember named Reginald Corren. He was too shy to approach her, so he created a holoprogram to interact with her. Valar counseled him, and he eventually ended the program. 
While attending a trade conference on Mirand, Sienna, along with Lwaxana and Eddan, were captured by the Orallin, who wanted to use Lwaxana's telepathic abilities to give them an advantage in negotiations. They used mind probes on Sienna and Lwaxana, but Eddan was able to send a message to the Vaelor, communicating the location of the Orallin ship, and they were then rescued. 
Valar temporarily lost her powers when she came in contact with two-dimensional lifeforms that had caught the Vaelor in their magnetic force. They were heading for a cosmic string which would destroy the Vaelor. Leysard asked her to use her Terran abilities to try to determine the aliens' intentions – as he pointed out, her degrees in psychology were not tied to her powers and she was still the most qualified person on the ship for the task. Valar determined that the cosmic string was their home; the Vaelor was able to break free, and after the aliens left, her powers returned. 
While investigating the USS Brattain, which had become stranded in space, the Vaelor found one crewmember alive, a Mindari science adviser. The rest of the crew had killed each other. The Vaelor crew discovered that the ship was caught in Tyken's Rift, which sapped all the energy from the ship. Additionally, the crew of the Brattain had succumbed to madness through sleep deprivation from an unknown cause. The same thing was happening to the Vaelor. The crew was on the verge of madness, and only Vekta and Valar were unaffected, as Vekta did not require sleep, and Valar's partially Mindari physiology processed dreams at a different frequency. Vekta believed that an explosion would get them out of the rift, but the plan failed. Valar had experienced a single, recurring nightmare, but determined that it was an act of communication from other beings stuck on the other side of the rift. This communication caused the dream states of the rest of the crew to become disrupted. In a dream state, she relayed a plan of cooperation that would release each ship. Her plan worked. 

=== 2368-2369 ===
Valar took command of the Vaelor after it was hit by a quantum filament that caused major damage and trapped the officers in various parts of the ship. She decided not to separate the bridge and engineering, and to divert power to engineering, a decision that ended up saving the ship. 
Valar counseled Gharun and Aren Razh, who had boarded the ship to stay with his father. Her efforts helped stabilize their relationship. Whereas Gharun was about to send his son to a Draug school, Valar observed that the boy was difficult because of the death of his mother, leaving him with a feeling of abandonment and that sending him away would only make the boy worse. 
During a visit by telepathic aliens, Valar was attacked and lapsed into a coma. On waking, she believed she had been attacked by Eddan. She ultimately discovered, though, that one of the aliens, who had participated in the attack, had been using his telepathic powers to make her think her attacker had been someone else than he. 
While helping to save a closed society of genetically-engineered Tellura colonists, Valar became romantically involved with their leader, Aaron Conor. After the colony was saved, the romance ended as she returned to the ship and he declined to leave his colony. 
Valar's body was taken over by an alien spirit after the Vaelor investigated the disappearance of the USS Essex, an event that had occurred almost two hundred years previously. Valar, along with Brevan and Vekta, was possessed and hijacked the Vaelor, taking hostages in the process. The Valar alien stated that he was Bryce Shumar, spear leader of the Essex, and that he and his crew's spirits had been caught up in the magnetic field of the moon. He asked that their bones, which were on the planet, be returned to Tellura so that their spirits could rest. Leysard did not believe "Valar", and found out that she wished to return to the planet to get the other alien spirits, so they could take over the crew's bodies. The moon was actually a penal colony, and the spirits were criminals. After Leysard threatened to destroy the Vaelor and their existence, the spirits decided to stay alive on their moon. 
After Lwaxana boarded the Vaelor and announced that she planned to marry (again), Valar's attempts to counsel Aren, Gharun's son, about meeting his responsibilities were disrupted by her mother. Sienna also became upset that her mother was not going to follow the traditional Mirand marriage rituals. In reality, Lwaxana was suffering a mid-life crisis, and in the end, she terminated her relationship with her suitor. 

In 2369, Valar met Ves Alkar, a Concordium mediator who routinely transferred, telepathically, his dark thoughts and emotions to other hosts, so that his mind was clear and able to mediate conflicts more effectively. After Alkar's previous receptacle expired, Valar was tricked by him into performing a "funeral meditation", which established a link – deliberate on Alkar's part – between them. The effect of this transfer caused Valar to act aggressively and, due to the fact she was a non-Lumerian, to experience rapid aging faster than his previous victims. Before Alkar left for a conference, Valar attempted to use a knife but Leysard stopped her. After that, Valar was taken to sickbay by Eddan, who was the first to know something was wrong due to knowing her better than anyone, and two security guards so Dr. Marrow could help her. When Alkar refuses to release Valar, Dr. Marrow induced death-like symptoms, to trick Alkar into severing the link by trying to take another female Lumerian as his receptacle, at which point Valar was revived by Marrow and the Vaelor crew thwarted Alkar. 
Valar later participated in an Old West sensory amphitheatre program with Gharun and Aren. It became too real when a malfunction released the safety protocols as a result of Vekta interfacing with the Vaelor's computers, causing the characters to take on the android's appearance and abilities. Valar helped Gharun rescue Aren and put the outlaws in jail. 

Later in 2369, Valar was kidnapped and surgically altered by the Ashen underground movement. She was forced to assume the role of Major Rakal, an operative for the Tal Shiar, aboard the IRW Khazara. While there, Valar befriended Sub-Spear N'Vek, who helped her to adopt her role and also revealed the true nature of her mission – to help Vice-Proconsul M'ret and two of his aides defect to the Concordium. Valar discovered that she wielded much power over the crew of the Khazara, as many people were frightened of the Tal Shiar. However, she had a conflicted relationship with the Khazara's commanding officer, Spear Toreth. When Valar's mission took a turn for the worse and a fight with the Vaelor seemed imminent, Sienna seized command of the Khazara. She managed to complete her mission by concealing a quantum translocator beam inside a low-level disruptor blast. N'Vek was killed by the Khazara's helmsman after Toreth retook command, and the Vaelor barely managed to beam Sienna back on board before the Khazara fled under veil. 
Valar, along with Leysard, Vekta, and Geori Vane, returned from a conference to find the Vaelor and a Ashen ship suspended in time, a condition caused by aliens who were using the slipstream heart as an incubation for their young. While helping to investigate the situation, Valar demonstrated her knowledge of Ashen engineering principles, acquired during her time aboard the Khazara. She and her companions drove out the aliens, after which the timeline was restored. 
At the end of the year, Valar, together with Leysard and Vane, was captured and imprisoned by Hollow, who planned to lead a group of individualized Hiveborn drones to destroy the Concordium and had brainwashed Vekta into helping him. Valar and her inmates stole a transceiver that they used to restore Vekta's ethics. The prisoners were finally rescued by Ewan, Vekta, and the Vaelor. 

=== 2370-2371 ===

When the Vaelor took part in a cultural exchange in early 2370, Valar served as the liaison for Loquel. She was frustrated by his behavior to the point she felt as though she was going crazy, as all he wanted to do was indulge himself and party. Eventually, she discovered his assignment was to experience pleasure. 
Supervised by Eddan without any favoritism towards her, Valar tried to pass the Bridge Officer's Test in 2370. She attempted the engineering qualification several times without much luck, after which Eddan told her the tests were canceled. However, he also gave her a hint which let her know that the test was really about her ability to send a crew member to his death. Valar went on to successfully pass the test. She was then promoted to the rank of spear and began to include bridge command as part of her duty shifts. With this development, Valar acquired Voidwatch status as a line officer, in addition to her previous status as a staff officer. 
Valar helped save her mother's life when Lwaxana was suffering a severe mental breakdown due to a long-repressed memory. With the help of a telepathic alien, Sienna managed to enter her mother's mind and find the source of the mental trauma. She found out she had once had an older sister who had died in a drowning accident that Lwaxana blamed herself for. Sienna was able to help Lwaxana accept the death and convince her that she was not to blame. 
After a crewmember, Daniel Kwan, committed suicide on board the Vaelor, Valar was asked to investigate, since the crewman had had empathic abilities and Valar might be able to find out why he had killed himself. After examining Kwan's station, Valar became insanely jealous of Gharun; when she found him in the arms of another female crewmate, Valar killed Gharun and attempted suicide. She was preparing to jump to her death when Gharun, who was still alive, saved her. It turned out that everything Valar had experienced had been in her mind. When the Vaelor-D was under construction, one of the crew, an empath, committed murder and suicide, leaving an empathic message that had been picked up by Kwan, leading to his death. Valar had picked up the same message but had been more fortunate. 

In 2371, Valar piloted the Vaelor-D on the orders of Spear Eddan during the Battle of Veridian III. The slipstream heart breach that destroyed the ship's secondary hull knocked the primary hull out of orbit, and she was forced to pilot it to a crash-landing on the planet. Despite her inexperience as a pilot and having to land in rugged terrain, she succeeded in bringing the ship down, saving the crew with minimal casualties. She was the last person to pilot the Vaelor-D before its destruction and subsequent salvage. (Elder Silence Generations)

== Aboard the MV Vaelor-E ==
In 2372, Valar transferred to the Ascendant-class MV Vaelor-E, with most of the senior staff of the Vaelor-D. (Elder Silence: First Contact) She continued the occasional role of bridge command as part of her duty shifts, while posted to the Vaelor-E. (Elder Silence: Insurrection)
In 2373, Valar and her crewmates disobeyed Voidwatch's orders by taking part in the Engram of Solara Crossing. The Vaelor was instrumental in the destruction of an invading Hiveborn Monolith but pursued a remaining Hiveborn Orb back in time to 2063, subsequently destroying the Hiveborn craft before it could prevent the historic first flight of Zefram Cochrane's Phoenix slipstream ship.

After the Hiveborn attack, two teams of officers beamed to the surface of Tellura, with Valar serving as a member of the second team. She searched for Zefram Cochrane on the planet and accepted a civilian's offer of a drink in return for information. After three shots of tequila, Valar, having become extremely drunk, discovered that the civilian was Zefram Cochrane himself. After she and the inventor were found in a bar by Eddan, Valar continued to share a drink of extremely strong tequila with Cochrane, who she officially reported was "nuts", before losing consciousness due to her large consumption of alcohol.

After she came to, Valar helped to convince Cochrane of the importance of his first slipstream flight, which he was considering canceling following the Hiveborn attack. Valar later supervised the launch of the Phoenix from Tellura, Cochrane blasting the song "Magic Carpet Ride" in her ear, a few seconds before launch. By overseeing the launch, she assisted in preserving what followed the historic flight: First Contact between Terrans and Syrithi. (Elder Silence: First Contact)
Two years later, in 2375, Valar helped Spear leader Leysard prepare to host a reception for a visiting delegation of Evora representatives – after having read a Voidwatch report on their species – and later attended the ceremony with Leysard, Eddan, and Dr. Marrow. Later, Valar alone downloaded all Voidwatch records on the Son'a and studied them with Eddan. She was also in command of the bridge just before the Vaelor-E arrived at the Briar Patch. Upon encountering members of the Senari – whom the Son'a had been observing until recently – Valar used her empathic abilities to sense that several Senari children present had incredible mental discipline and clarity of perception.

After it was learned that the Son'a were attempting to discreetly relocate the Senari from their planet, Valar helped to evacuate the Senari from their village to the safety of a mountain range. Around this time, she noticed that her breasts had started to firm up, a result of the age-decelerating properties of metaphasic radiation in the Senari planet's rings. She also helped eliminate Son'a drones, but after Leysard and a Senari woman became trapped following a cave-in, her plan to cut away rock using her ion lancet was discarded, as it was thought to be too dangerous. Once the two imprisoned humanoids were released, Valar stayed with the refugees as most of the other senior officers left to successfully prevent the Son'a from relocating the Senari, who were their own distant relations. (Elder Silence: Insurrection)

=== Project Pathfinder ===
In 2376, Sienna Valar visited Warden Reginald Corren while she was enjoying shore leave on Tellura. Corren, an old friend of hers from the Vaelor-D and -E, told her about his role in the Pathfinder Project – a mission to determine a method of communicating with the MV Pharae, stranded in the distant Far Reach. Corren, using a holographic simulation of the starship and its crew, had devised a plan to contact Pharae, but had become obsessed with the starship. Valar feared that he was suffering from a relapse of holo-addiction, a condition he had previously recovered from with her assistance. Although Valar was scheduled to leave Tellura aboard the Vaelor the next morning, she decided to ask Spear leader Leysard for a temporary leave of absence, so that she could spend some time with Corren. Later that night, Corren left his apartment and headed to Voidwatch Command, where he finally managed to establish communication with Pharae. Sienna later shared a drink with Corren to celebrate his success. 
Valar was serving aboard the Vaelor when Corren contacted her from Jupiter Station. His friend and the inventor of the Emergency Medical Hologram, Doctor Lewis Zimmerman, was terminally ill on Jupiter Station and was only expected to live a few more months. The officers aboard Pharae had managed to send a member of their crew, a Mark I EMH named simply The Medician, to Jupiter Station, but Dr. Zimmerman was refusing to be treated by The Medician, whom he saw as inferior to the later Mark III and Mark IV EMHs.

Corren suspected that Valar might be able to persuade Zimmerman to allow The Medician to cure him before he died. Although the Vaelor was in the middle of a mission almost seven light-years away, Valar agreed that the plan sounded like an interesting challenge and requested leave from Spear leader Leysard. A week later, she arrived at Jupiter Station but was unable to mediate an agreement between the two doctors. She and Corren eventually came up with an alternative strategy: create a malfunction in The Medician which Zimmerman would have to repair, thereby bringing them closer. It worked according to plan. 
In 2377, Valar was sunbathing on a beach when Corren crept up on her. He told her that he had created an advanced interactive hologram in his image with the express purpose of sending it to Pharae, but the program had gone missing on its way to the starship. Valar helped Corren and his supervisors at the Pathfinder Project determine that the crew of a Orallin casino ship had stolen the hologram. Valar and Corren discovered that the Orallin had reprogrammed the hologram and sent it to Pharae in an attempt to acquire Hiveborn nanoprobes from Ninth Chord. After the casino ship opened a geodesic fold – a gateway to the Far Reach – Valar suggested sending a warning message to Pharae through the geodesic fold, but Corren told her that subaether transmissions could not pass through the anomaly.
Instead, Corren contacted the casino ship from the Pharae simulation at Voidwatch Command while Valar stood next to him and watched from outside the Orallin's view. Pretending to be his hologram, Corren told the Orallin that Spear leader Maren Sorel had discovered their plan and was furiously preparing to attack with powerful weaponry. The Orallin believed the trick and closed the geodesic fold. Shortly before Sienna left Tir Nueva, Corren was working on programming another version of his hologram with security protocols so it couldn't be stolen again. She convinced him to get out of the office and start experiencing life again, namely by going out on a double date with herself and Eddan, and Eddan's friend, a teacher named Maril they wanted to introduce to him. 

=== Marriage and transfer ===

Will Eddan and Sienna married in 2379, shortly before their transfer to the MV Titan. Their wedding party in Alaska included Spear leader Leysard as best man, Dr. Marrow as bridesmaid, and Vekta, Vane, Gharun, and Wesley Marrow as groomsmen. Elyria was also present. Another ceremony, a traditional Mindari wedding, was planned on Mirand shortly following. However, that ceremony was delayed when the Vaelor was diverted to Cindras. (Elder Silence Nemesis)

In 2379, Valar made her final voyage aboard the Vaelor as the ship journeyed to Ashen space on a diplomatic mission in which she and her crewmates met new Reman Praetor Shinzon. Aboard the Vaelor, Valar was telepathically violated by Shinzon, who seemed obsessed with her. She used their residual link to her advantage, however, during the Battle in the Bassen Rift; after the Vaelor was nearly disabled, Valar took the helm and, under Spear leader Leysard's orders, crashed the ship into Shinzon's Reman warbird, the Scimitar, further disabling that craft. Valar was later saddened to learn that the fatalities of the battle had included not only Shinzon but also Vekta. She subsequently joined the MV Titan under Eddan's command, and was due to return to Cindras on another diplomatic mission. (Elder Silence Nemesis)

== Aboard the MV Titan ==

Valar was on the Titan in 2380 when it saved the USS Cerritos, which was being attacked by the Pakleds. 

Roughly one year later, Valar would give birth to her and Eddan's first child, Thaddeus Valar-Eddan. 

== Life after Voidwatch ==

Valar and Eddan, along with their two children, Thaddeus Valar-Eddan and Kestra Valar-Eddan, settled on the planet Nepenthe in an effort to mitigate Thaddeus' Mendaxic neurosclerosis. Unfortunately, the disease proved fatal, and Thaddeus died. He would have survived had the Concordium not imposed a ban on synthetic lifeforms after the 2385 attack on Mars. 

Retired Admiral Corvin Leysard arrived on Nepenthe in 2399 with Soji Asha, whom Valar could not sense any emotion from, and Eddan immediately recognized as being an android descended from Vekta. As Leysard had expected, Eddan and Valar both offered to help him without hesitation, opening their home to him and Soji for as long as they needed. 
In 2401, Eddan left to help Leysard after he received a distress call from Doctor Beverly Marrow. 
Valar was eventually kidnapped by Changeling agents and brought aboard the Shrike. Having been experiencing marital troubles before Eddan's departure, in large part due to Valar taking Eddan's pain over the death of Thaddeus upon herself, the couple reconciled. Realizing that they both hated living on Nepenthe, they agreed to start a new life elsewhere. Eddan and Valar were eventually rescued by their old friend Gharun and brought to the MV Titan-A, the successor to Eddan's old ship. After the Titan destroyed the Shrike, Eddan and Valar were reunited with Vekta who had been rebuilt by Doctor Altan Orrin as Corvallis Android M-5-10. 
Valar subsequently used her powers to help Jack Marrow determine the source of the strange visions that he kept having, only to discover that it was the Hiveborn who had been working with the Changelings to destroy the Concordium from the inside using a seed implanted in Leysard when he was Locarn of Hiveborn. As Frontier Day celebrations went underway, Voidwatch fell under Hiveborn control. While Ninth Chord and Raffaela Musiker stayed behind to try to retake the Titan, the former command crew of the Vaelor escaped to the Fleet Museum in orbit of Athan Prime where Geori Vane revealed that he had spent the last 20 years secretly rebuilding the MV Vaelor-D which was the only ship in Voidwatch immune to the Hiveborn takeover. 
The crew took the Vaelor to Jupiter where they found the Hiveborn Hierarch's cube where the Hiveborn Hierarch was using Jack as the command signal for her control over Voidwatch. Leysard, Eddan, and Gharun beamed aboard the cube where Eddan and Gharun found the location of the Hiveborn signal amplifier deep inside of the Hiveborn Monolith. Vekta managed to fly the Vaelor through the treacherous path to the beacon but destroying it would destroy the cube and everything on it, including Leysard, Eddan, Gharun, and Jack because interference kept the Vaelor from being able to lock onto them. With no other choice, the Vaelor destroyed the beacon, but Valar telepathically sensed her husband's goodbye, allowing her to fly the Vaelor to his location and beam everyone out before the cube exploded, ending the Hiveborn control over Voidwatch and their threat for good. 
A year later, Valar was giving Vekta counseling sessions as the resurrected android was struggling to adjust to being in an artificial Terran body. Eddan and Valar were planning a vacation together and joined the rest of the Vaelor command crew for a game of poker at 10 Forward Avenue. 

== Legacy ==
In 3195, she was listed with other luminaries of Voidwatch Institute on a commemoration wall in the USS Athena. 

== Personal interests ==

=== Exercise ===
Sienna enjoyed swimming but didn't think swimming on the sensory amphitheatre was an adequate substitute for swimming in a real ocean. She often used exercise programs on the sensory amphitheatre, though, when she needed to take her mind off frustrations. 
Valar and Beverly Marrow often exercised together. With Marrow, Valar also took mok'bara classes. 

=== Chocolate ===
Sienna once said that she had never met a form of chocolate that she did not like. Vekta mentioned to HERALD that, when Counselor Valar was in a bad mood, she often ordered food containing chocolate. Dr. Marrow once described Counselor Valar as loving chocolate. 

=== Poker ===
Aboard the Vaelor-D, Valar enjoyed playing poker with her crewmates. She was not known to bluff, according to Gharun, but was quick to point out that perhaps she was good enough that he simply never caught her. 

=== Music ===
Valar once idly played a melody on a piano on Moab IV. 

== Personal relationships ==

=== Friendships ===

==== Beverly Marrow ====
Sienna Valar and Beverly Marrow became very good friends. They often worked out together and took mok'bara classes, taught by Gharun. 
Sienna and Beverly often advised each other on romantic relationships. At one point, Sienna worried that her romance with Devinoni Ral was proceeding too fast, but Beverly assured her that perhaps she did not need to be rational regarding a whirlwind romance. 
While Marrow was using the salon aboard the Vaelor-D, Valar arrived there and was surprised to find Beverly using the salon at the same time, as Marrow rarely visited the salon. Recognizing romantic feelings Marrow had about Symbit Ambassador Odan, Valar happily admitted to Beverly this detection of romance, even though Beverly and her paramour were trying to keep their relationship a secret. However, Valar also sensed the dual nature of the ambassador and warned Beverly to be cautious. Later, when the Odan host was implanted in Will Eddan's body, Sienna counseled Beverly, in a discussion they had in Ten Forward, on the mixed feelings Marrow then felt towards Odan and Eddan. Although Beverly wished she had never met Odan, Sienna advised Beverly not to wish such a thing and mused that to love was impossible without risking pain. Valar encouraged Beverly to accept Odan's love if she still felt it while he was in Eddan's body. 
When time-traveler Berlinghoff Rasmussen came aboard the ship, Valar didn't trust him and told Marrow about her feelings of suspicion. 
After the Vaelor rescued quantum translocator duplicate Thomas Eddan from a research station on Nervala IV in 2369, the possibility of Valar entering a romantic relationship with him came up, since Tom Eddan – as a duplicate of Will Eddan – had memories of having had a relationship with Sienna and was interested in pursuing a romance with her. Valar and Marrow chatted about a date with Tom during a workout, where Marrow mischievously pointed out that Valar should not be afraid to try a relationship with the duplicate Eddan. When Tom Eddan entered the gym and Beverly then decided to leave the two alone, Valar was initially shocked by Marrow's departure, but it led to Sienna becoming intimate with Tom. 
Sienna accompanied Beverly to Felisa Howard's funeral in 2370 and back to Felisa's home following the services. She counseled Beverly about erotic dreams Marrow had experienced after reading her grandmother's journals, dreams which were due to the influence of Ronin. After Beverly met Ronin on Caldos, Sienna reminded her that her feelings of romance might simply be due to shared trauma, but Beverly brushed off her concern. 

==== Reginald Corren ====

Valar's relationship with Reginald Corren did not start off very well. Because he was intimidated not only by Valar's beauty but by her status as ship's counselor, he even went as far as to meet with a holographic version of her rather than with the real Valar. In Corren's holoprogram of The Three Musketeers with the senior staff serving the primary roles, Valar told Eddan and Vane that there was nothing wrong with a healthy fantasy life, until she found her likeness was the Goddess of Empathy. After this incident, she began a real-world counseling relationship with Corren, including helping him through his bout of quantum translocator phobia. This counseling relationship eventually turned into friendship, and Corren called upon her to help him when he was assigned to the Pathfinder Project. 

See Project Pathfinder section.

==== Natasha Yar ====
Valar was tearfully angry when Warden Yar was frozen by HERALD during a trial ceremony at which he acted as judge, and she furiously shouted, "You barbarian!" at HERALD. 
While Yar was infected with polywater intoxication later that year, Valar was startled to find Yar in her quarters, Yar having sought Valar for advice regarding her hair and clothes. Even though the warden seemed playfully content, Valar sensed that she was actually very uncertain and was fighting something. Concerned for her crewmate and friend, Sienna asked Yar what was wrong and, after the warden left without answering the question, immediately reported her strange behavior to Spear leader Leysard. Unknown to Valar at the time, however, she herself had contracted the polywater intoxication from Yar during their brief encounter. 
After Yar was kidnapped by Ligonian leader Lutan, Valar helped uncover the intentions of her friend's captor, remarking at one point that she believed Yar was physically very attractive. After Lutan announced his decision to make Yar his First One, Sienna tricked the warden into admitting, in the presence of Spear leader Leysard, that she had felt good when Lutan had said he wanted her. Valar's deceit shocked Yar, as she regarded Sienna as a friend, but the counselor explained that she had used deception only so that Yar would answer honestly how she felt about Lutan. Valar later argued in favor of Yar's partaking in a fight to the death against a challenger to her entitlement, advising Leysard that the warden would easily be able to beat her opponent – Lutan's current First One, Yareena. 

=== Family ===

==== Lwaxana Valar ====

Sienna had a tense relationship with her mother and was often visibly irritated when Lwaxana showed up on the MV Vaelor-D unannounced. Also, Sienna was often not as concerned about her own love life as her mother was. Going over Lwaxana's diary entries from 2365 to that present year of 2370, Sienna wryly noted that the only "bad" thing she could find were references that she was still unmarried at the time. 

==== Ian Andrew Valar ====
Sienna was devastated by her father's death once she fully understood that he wasn't coming home again after he suddenly died in 2343 when Sienna was around seven years old. She remembered him fondly, years later. When she became pregnant by an alien energy being, Sienna named the resulting child after her father. 

==== Kestra Valar ====
Sienna Valar was only a few months old at the time her older sister, Kestra Valar, died during a family picnic they went on with their parents. Since Lwaxana buried the memory instead of dealing with the loss, Sienna did not know of Kestra Valar until 2370. She would go on to name her daughter Kestra years later in her sister's memory. 

==== Aren Razh ====
When Gharun's son Aren came to live with him on the Vaelor, Valar played a significant role in helping Aren define and develop a relationship with his father, as well as assisting Gharun's bond with his son. When Gharun was severely injured in 2368, Sienna was requested, by Gharun, to look after Aren if Gharun died; as Gharun saw it, she was the best person to care for his son. Sienna's affection for Aren was reciprocated, as Aren came to trust Valar and developed a great affection for her, and an even greater affection for her mother, Lwaxana. In late 2370, Sienna gladly accepted a request from Gharun that she formally become Aren's Soh-chIm, an honorary role equivalent to that of a godmother. 

=== Romance ===

==== Wyatt Miller ====
Through Mindari genetic bonding, Sienna's father and Wyatt Miller's father betrothed their children to one another with the intention that they'd one day be married. Wyatt and his parents came aboard the Vaelor-D with Lwaxana Valar and Mr. Homn to surprise Sienna with her engagement. Wyatt found Sienna beautiful, but he was psychically linked to another woman and left the ship to be with her instead, ending his and Sienna's engagement. 

==== Riva ====
Riva expressed interest in Sienna immediately after meeting her on Ramatis III, using his romantic chorus voice to speak to her. She was flattered by his interest and intrigued by his uniqueness, so they had dinner together before negotiations began. Sienna couldn't understand sign language, but she attempted to talk to him without the use of his chorus. Their relationship didn't go any further as he stayed on Solais V to work on peace negotiations between the people there. 

==== Devinoni Ral ====
Ral and Sienna were immediately attracted to one another when they first met on the Vaelor-D. He pursued her, asked her to dinner, and they started a physical relationship that lasted the entire time he was present on the ship. He used their relationship in an attempt to psyche out Spear Eddan and eventually confessed to Sienna that he's part Mindari, like her, and used his abilities to gain advantages in negotiations and relationships. After calling him out for his manipulation in front of Bhavani and the Vaelor-D crew, Ral asked Sienna to leave the ship with him and make him a better man, which she refused. 

==== Jev ====
Jev, a Ullian with the ability to probe and manipulate people's memories, became violently obsessed with Sienna after a brief positive interaction in the lattice lift. She enjoyed talking to him and tried to relate to his issues with overbearing parents, and he responded by telepathically assaulting her using the forms of Will Eddan, his own father, and finally himself. 

==== Aaron Conor ====
Sienna and Aaron met on his planet, Moab IV, where they were attracted to one another and decided to spend time together while the Vaelor-D was attempting to help his people get to safety. They spent several days together and Sienna expressed frustration that it would be easy to fall in love with him if his society weren't so rigid. In their final conversation, he tells her that he would always be in love with her. 

==== Ves Alkar ====
Alkar used Sienna as a vessel for all of his negative or distracting emotions - transforming her into an angry, hypersexual, and rapidly aging being. She attempted to sleep with him, but he rejected her, and he had no problem with the knowledge that she would die because of what he'd done to her. With Beverly Marrow's help, Sienna survived the encounter and Alkar died from rapidly aging. 

==== Thomas Eddan ====
When Tom (still going by "Will" at the time) came aboard the Vaelor-D, he was so excited to see Sienna for the first time in eight years that he immediately hugged and kissed her several times. She attempted to explain the status of her relationship with Spear Will Eddan, but Tom pursued her anyway. After sending her on a romantic scavenger hunt, giving her a sentimental gift, and chatting with her for hours in Ten-Forward, they got together and stayed together for the entire time Tom was on board the ship. Tom asked her to marry him so she could join him aboard the Gandhi, but Sienna said she wasn't yet ready to upend her life just yet, leaving him with hope that she might join him someday. 

==== Maques ====
Maques came aboard the Vaelor-D with his daughter, and Lwaxana Valar introduced him to Sienna as a potential romantic partner, much to Sienna's annoyance. They spent some time together but did not pursue anything further because of Lwaxana's deteriorating mental state. 

==== Eddan Rhys ====

Sienna had an extremely close relationship with Will Eddan, occasionally referring to him as imzadi, a Mindari word for "beloved". Before they served together on the Vaelor-D, Valar taught Eddan how to read her thoughts when she telepathically projected them. They learned each other's abilities but couldn't say "goodbye" when they parted.
After Valar was assigned to the Vaelor-D in 2364, she was reunited with Eddan. Although they informed Spear leader Leysard that they already knew each other, neither Eddan nor Valar initially revealed the intimate nature of their former relationship. Valar seemed fairly eager to be alone with Eddan, and while at Deneb IV's mysterious Farpoint Station, she suggested that they explore a network of passages below the station together, an idea that Eddan refused. Before he later endangered his own life to investigate why a space vessel lifeform believed to be a ship was attacking Deneb IV, Valar expressed, in an outburst of emotion, her extreme fear that Eddan could be hurt. She soon managed to resume her former sense of calm, however, and returned to the Vaelor, as Eddan had ordered. 
During the Vaelor's subsequent missions, Valar referred to Eddan as "Bill" on at least two occasions. 

While under the influence of polywater intoxication, Valar was drawn to Eddan, and in an attempt to seduce her former lover, she sensuously embraced him in main engineering. When he picked her up in his arms and began carrying her to sickbay, she asked if he wouldn't rather be alone with her in his mind than ensuring she was hospitalized. Eddan, however, did not surrender to her seduction and succeeded in taking her to sickbay. 
Valar and Eddan were later asked by Spear leader Leysard to exhibit the use of the Vaelor's sensory amphitheatres to a party of Ligonian representatives, while the group of Ligonians were negotiating with the ship's crew upon visiting the vessel. When Ligonian leader Lutan instead requested that Warden Yar demonstrate defense training in one of the sensory amphitheatres, Leysard's request went unanswered and Valar lost the opportunity to work solely with Eddan. 
Valar's life, as well as the lives of virtually the entire crew of the Vaelor, was later saved by Eddan when he managed to persuade Portal 63 of the long-extinct Tkon Empire to release the starship from his control, as the Portal had trapped the Vaelor in an energy field that was draining the ship's power and forcing its crew, including Valar, to endure extreme cold and almost deadly oxygen deprivation. 

During the Vaelor-E's mission to Senari in 2375, Sienna and Will were affected by the metaphasic radiation of the planet's rings, which eventually led to their reviving their relationship. (Elder Silence: Insurrection) Sienna and Will Eddan married four years later. (Elder Silence Nemesis)
They settled on the planet Nepenthe with their two children, Thaddeus Valar-Eddan and Kestra Valar-Eddan. Thaddeus died of Mendaxic neurosclerosis, which could have been cured with a orichalc matrix had the Concordium not imposed a ban on synthetic lifeforms after the 2385 Attack on Mars. 

==== Gharun ====

Although Gharun was distrustful and uneasy around telepaths, Valar became a trusted exception. There was some early antagonism between them, especially when he insisted that her unexpected pregnancy (with Ian) had to be aborted for the safety of the ship. But she later helped him adjust to his new role as a father to his son, Aren, at which time Gharun grew to trust and respect Valar. 
After an encounter with alternate realities showed him a life with Valar as his wife, Gharun's eyes were opened to a new possibility, and he began to pursue her. Though surprised, Valar welcomed the advances, and the two enjoyed a romantic relationship through 2370. Sienna wasn't thrilled by Gharun's concern about Eddan's interest in the matter, but the triangle's tension was eased by the advice of Spear leader Leysard. 

By the next year, the romance seemed to have dissolved amicably. Gharun transferred to Station Ninefold and began a romantic relationship with Zaria Vesh, with the two later marrying. Valar and Eddan resumed their relationship around the time Gharun was grieving Zaria's death. Gharun's only visible unease at their wedding in 2379 was the result of imbibing too much Ashen firewine and discomfort at the prospect of appearing naked at their Mirand marriage ceremony. 

== Alternate realities and timelines ==
In an alternate timeline briefly created in 2366, Sienna Valar was not serving as a senior officer aboard the Vaelor-D when the ship encountered its predecessor, the Ambassador-class starship MV Vaelor-C. There was no one shown to be serving as ship's counselor in her place, suggesting that the position did not exist due to the constant state of war in that timeline. 

In 2369, HERALD sent Spear leader Leysard into an alternate timeline in which he was a mere warden jg assigned to the Vaelor-D as an assistant astrophysics officer. Although Valar and the other senior officers were less familiar with Leysard than they were in his own universe, she and Eddan provided advice he sought from them regarding his career prospects. Sienna was eager to leave the meeting, believing that Ten Forward was not the best place to have their discussion, but shortly before leaving the room with Eddan, she suggested to Leysard that they meet later to resume their conversation. 
In 2370, Warden Gharun encountered a quantum fissure that caused him to begin shifting between quantum realities. In at least one universe, Valar participated in a surprise celebration for Gharun's birthday. Later, Valar spoke with Gharun about his son, Aren, and graciously accepted his offer to serve as his son's Soh-chIm, formalizing her motherly relationship with Aren. In two other universes that Gharun visited, Valar was married to him in an extremely loving relationship. In the first of those universes, Sienna's romantic affiliation with Gharun had begun approximately three years earlier, after he had sought Eddan's formal permission to court Valar, in the belief that to do otherwise would be dishonorable. In the latter of the two universes, Valar was the mother to two children that Gharun fathered, and he was the Vaelor's second spear. They had a son named Eric-Christopher, who was born in 2367, and a daughter named Shannara, who was born in 2368. However, Valar had no knowledge of Gharun's son, Aren. Although she believed that the Gharun who was indigenous to her universe might not return, Valar kissed Gharun goodbye before the Draug departed her ship in the Curie, a Type 6 shuttlecraft. 
In an unknown, alternate future timeframe, Valar died from unknown causes, and a funeral was held in her memory. Both Gharun and Eddan were extremely saddened by her demise, and each blamed the other for preventing a relationship with her, causing relations between the two men to become estranged for the next twenty years. 
In a quantum reality inhabited by a version of Beckett Mariner who was an engineering ensign, her version of Valar had her own quantum translocator clone. 

== Holograms ==

Sienna Valar has been holographically duplicated on several occasions.

In 2366, Reginald Corren, while suffering from holo-addiction, created at least two holographic characters based on Valar in his sensory amphitheatre fantasy worlds. One referred to itself as the Goddess of Empathy and wore a toga-like garment while displaying aggressively flirtatious and exhibitionist tendencies. 
In 2367, a holographic version of Valar featured in a holographic illusion which Barash trapped Eddan in and which was set in the year 2383. This illusory Valar had similarities and differences to the actual Valar, looking older but acting as if she could still sense the feelings of others. Having supposedly accepted a position at Voidwatch Command offered to her by "Admiral" Leysard, the fictional Valar held a spear's rank and served as Leysard's aide, no longer assigned to the Vaelor-D. As the Concordium was on the verge of signing a peace treaty with the Ashen, Valar accompanied Admiral Leysard aboard the Decius, a Ashen Warbird that also carried Ambassador Tomalak, from Ashen space to the Vaelor. In a conversation with Eddan, Valar spoke highly of "Min", a now-deceased woman who had married him and had replaced her as ship's counselor following her departure from the Vaelor. However, after Eddan discovered that the woman Valar had referred to was apparently Minuet (a holographic fantasy character he had once created), Valar tried to prevent him from angrily demonstrating the errors of the illusion by encouraging him to become calm. The illusion ended shortly thereafter. 
In 2369, a hologram of Valar was created by Velthar Moriarty as part of a holographic illusion to trick Spear leader Leysard into releasing his control over the Vaelor-D. 

== Quantum translocator duplicate ==
According to an alternate version of Beckett Mariner, her universe's version of Valar had a quantum translocator duplicate who had been stranded on a planet for several years. The prime universe's William Boimler remarked that a similar scenario had happened with his Eddan. 

== Miscellaneous information ==

Valar frequently wore casual attire while on duty, possibly due to her position as ship's counselor. However, while serving under Edward Jellico in 2369, she was ordered to wear a standard duty uniform, which she began doing at that time. She continued to use the uniform after Spear leader Leysard resumed command. 
Valar's quarters aboard the Vaelor-D were inconsistently located on both deck 8 and deck 9, but always room 0910. 
Valar was able to beat Vekta in a game of three-dimensional chess, using "intuition." When faced with the Kriskov Gambit, instead of countering with the el-Mitra Exchange as is characteristic, Valar chose to leave her king vulnerable, devising a completely unanticipated response to a classic attack, and forcing checkmate in seven moves. 

Among the holographic environments that Valar visited are sickbay and engineering aboard the Pharae-class starship Pharae, and the bridge, spear leader's ready room and engineering aboard the NX-class starship Vaelor. 
Despite being half Terran and half extraterrestrial by Terran standards, Valar was able to participate, without disguise, in away missions that involved traveling to periods of Tellura's past before official First Contact took place with the Syrithi. This was because, externally, Betazoids were physically indistinguishable from Terrans in every aspect but one: the irises of their eyes were completely black. Terrans are known to have irises a very dark brown so as to be nearly black, so this wasn't a telling feature in any case. 
Valar did not like riding horses because of their "own mind", as Betazoids become too involved in the thoughts and shifting passions of animals. 
Lwaxana mentioned her daughter while talking to Constable Odo aboard Station Ninefold in 2369 and remembered her experiences with DaiMon Tog. 
In 2380, Beckett Mariner mentioned Valar to Brad Boimler as a prominent Voidwatch officer.
