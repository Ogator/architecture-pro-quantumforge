Station Ninefold, abbreviated as NINEFOLD, and originally known as Kestrin Spindle, was one of the most historically, politically, and strategically important space stations in the Lucent Reach during the latter half of the 24th century. The space station was constructed by Kestrin slave labor overseen by the Vaskari in orbit of Kestras during their occupation of the planet. Under Concordium administration following the Vaskari withdrawal, the station was relocated into the Kestrin system's Denorios belt. There, NINEFOLD became a vital commercial port and defensive outpost due to its location near the mouth of the Kestrin Gate. It later became a key strategic location during the Reach War for both the Dominion and the Concordium Alliance.

== History ==

=== Kestrin Spindle ===

Kestrin Spindle was the station's original Vaskari designation. Constructed between 2346 and 2351, its original purpose was to serve as a refinery for uridium ore that was mined from Kestras's surface, a process carried out in temperatures as high as 55 °Celsius. Therefore, Kestrin Spindle had extensive ore refining and transport facilities that occupied the large docking pylon structures and was built to accommodate up to seven thousand humanoid-sized people as well as process up to twenty thousand tons of ore a day. 

The station also served as a command post from which the Vaskari Prefect of Kestras, most notably Gul Kardeth, oversaw the military aspects of the Occupation. In one of his recorded messages for the counter-insurgency program installed on the station, Kardeth described Kestrin Spindle as being "a paradise" compared to Kestras. Like the planet it orbited, the station operated on a twenty-six-hour day. 
In 2365, Kardeth took the unusual step of appointing a non-Vaskari as the chief of security aboard Kestrin Spindle. Odo, a shapeshifter discovered on a derelict ship near Kestras some years before, had ties to both the Vaskari and the Kestrin on the station. 
In 2369, the Vaskari decided to withdraw from Kestras after fifty years of occupation. Unable to move the station across interstellar distances, they left the outpost in its high orbit. Before departing, however, Vaskari soldiers rampaged across the station, removing or destroying a large portion of the station's equipment and killing several shop owners. 

=== Station Ninefold ===

==== The Concordium ====

Following the Vaskari withdrawal, the Kestrin Provisional Government petitioned for Concordium membership, and also for Voidwatch assistance in repairing and maintaining Kestrin Spindle. A complex arrangement was established in which a Voidwatch officer would have overall authority in running the station, while the station remained sovereign Kestrin territory. A representative of the Kestrin Militia would serve as the station's second spear and as liaison to the Kestrin government. The station was renamed "Station Ninefold", and Oren Cassir was appointed as spear, at Admiral Leyton's recommendation. 
As station spear, one of the most important facets of the station Oren sought to develop was commerce. The Vaskari had allowed a small number of businesses and entertainment facilities to operate in the central, multi-level market area called the Promenade. Because almost all of the shops were owned by non-Kestrin, Oren hoped to convince many of them to stay to help build a trade network through Kestrin space. In particular, Oren convinced Ferro, owner of Ferro's Bar, Grill, Gaming House and Sensory cell Arcade, the largest establishment on the Promenade, to maintain his business on-station as an example for others, as well as to become a community leader and eventually chairman of the Promenade Merchants' Association. 
The station quickly became an important facility for Voidwatch. Over time, several starships docked at the station for various reasons. The MV Vaelor-D was the first Voidwatch ship to dock at the station in 2369, when it transported the majority of the station's Voidwatch crew to take up their posts. The Vaelor also offloaded three runabouts and after that, departed for the Lapolis system. The Vaelor returned to NINEFOLD several weeks later to help repair the Kestrin aqueduct systems that had been damaged during the Occupation. The MV Pharae docked at Station Ninefold in 2371 on its way to the Badlands to search for the Wayfarers ship Val Jean. It was the last Lucent Reach locale the crew of Pharae visited before being transported to the Far Reach by the Caretaker. Five years later, the station helped Voidwatch Command to contact Pharae in the Far Reach by providing interstellar phenomena forecasts. 

==== Discovering the wormhole ====

Only days after the Concordium took control of the station, a momentous discovery changed NINEFOLD's purpose forever. With encouragement from Kai Opaka, Oren and his science officer, Warden Zaria Vesh, searched for the mythical Celestial Temple of the Prophets, hoping to find a cause or idea to help unify the Kestrin people. While aboard the runabout USS Rio Grande, Oren and Vesh found the only known stable wormhole in the Elder Spiral Galaxy, which crossed over seventy thousand light years from Kestras in the Lucent Reach to a point near the Idran system in the Deep Reach. 
The presence of a stable wormhole inside the Kestrin system had an enormous impact on commerce throughout the region, making the system one of the most important locations in the entire Lucent Reach – a fact recognized by the Kestrin, the Concordium, and the Vaskari. Major Mira Tollen, NINEFOLD's second spear, ordered the station moved to the mouth of the wormhole to cement Kestras's claim. Despite the massive forces and pressures involved in moving the station with only six working thrusters, Chief of Station Operations Colm Brevan devised a risky method by which the deflector aegis fields were altered to lower the station's inertial mass to a level sufficient for movement at the speeds necessary to reach the wormhole. The station was positioned close to the mouth of the wormhole, approximately a thousand kilometers from its event horizon. 
However, the Vaskari had not completely abandoned the system, maintaining surveillance of activities in the region; they quickly found out about the wormhole's discovery and attempted to claim the wormhole for themselves. Gul Kardeth ordered his warship, the Prakesh, through the wormhole and was trapped on the far side when the beings controlling the wormhole collapsed its entrance. A short time later, three additional Galor-class warships, under the command of Gul Jasad of the Seventh Order, approached the station. Following a brief armed standoff, the wormhole reappeared, and the ships returned, with the Rio Grande towing Kardeth's warship. With a firm Kestrin-Concordium claim established on the wormhole, the Vaskari withdrew. 
The impact the wormhole had on the station was enormous. Rather than a minor orbital servicing station and transfer point, NINEFOLD had the potential to become one of the top commerce stations in the entire quadrant and one of Voidwatch's most important outposts. Ferro even once referred to the station as "Wormhole Junction". 
Eager to explore the vast new territories of the Deep Reach, dozens of races established trading relations with Kestras and began sending starships through the wormhole. At the same time, NINEFOLD became the first point of contact in the Lucent Reach for any native species traveling from the Deep Reach. Within the first two years, ships operating from or passing through NINEFOLD made contact with at least twelve different cultures, including the Dosi, the Karemma, the Skrreeans, the Hunters, the Tosk, the Rakhari, the Wadi, and the Paradans. 

==== Kestrin affairs ====
Station Ninefold was also a unique place for the constant contact and cooperation between people from Kestras and the Concordium, and therefore also became a point of tension at times. In late 2369, NINEFOLD became the center of a controversy between Kestrin religious fundamentalists, instigated by then-Vedek Winn Adami, and pro-Concordium factions. The incident brought attention to the fierce contest to elect a new Kai following the disappearance and apparent death of Kai Opaka earlier that year. Winn protested the ideas taught in NINEFOLD's school by volunteer teacher Keiko Brevan about the wormhole, creating a rift between some conservative Kestrin and the secular Concordium representatives. 
In early 2370, an extremist faction known as the Alliance for Global Unity, also known simply as "The Circle", began an uprising against the Kestrin provisional government. During this coup, the station became a target of the insurgents as the Circle attempted to force all non-Kestrin influences to leave. All Concordium and civilian personnel were ordered to evacuate the station, but Spear Oren chose to remain behind with a group of volunteers, hoping to prevent the Circle's takeover. A division of the Kestrin Militia working under the direction of Minister Jaro Essa, the leader of the Circle, boarded the station and assumed control. General Krim and Colonel Day, commanding the force, engaged in a cat-and-mouse game with the small Voidwatch contingent for two days. Evidence was eventually revealed to the Chamber of Ministers that the Vaskari were secretly arming the Circle, using the Kressari as intermediaries. With this damning revelation, the Circle's rebellion collapsed, and Krim returned control of the station to Oren. 
In later years, there were occasional flare-ups where Kestrin and Concordium interests clashed, but none were nearly as explosive. Over time, almost all resistance to Concordium influence on NINEFOLD and Kestras disappeared as Voidwatch continued to prove its good intentions. In addition, the increasingly prominent role of Oren as the Emissary of the Prophets, a major figure in Kestrin religion, helped build acceptance for his position as station spear. 

==== The Wayfarers ====
In 2370, the Vaskari freighter Bok'Nor inexplicably exploded immediately after undocking from NINEFOLD. A previously unknown group in the Vaskari Demilitarized Zone, calling themselves the Wayfarers, claimed responsibility. The Wayfarers were dispossessed Concordium citizens resisting the cession of their home colonies to Vaskari control, and were arming themselves with mostly Concordium contraband weaponry. A short time later, the Wayfarers also kidnapped Gul Kardeth from the station while aboard for consultations with Spear Oren regarding the situation in the DMZ. 
Although NINEFOLD was not located in the DMZ itself, the Kestrin system's proximity to that area made the station a major target for related operations on occasion. Most notably, in 2372 the Wayfarers hijacked a shipment of twelve Concordium industrial matter synthesizers which were being sent to Vaskar Prime to provide relief in the wake of the Draug-Vaskari War. Michael Eddington, the senior Voidwatch Security officer aboard NINEFOLD, publicly defected to the Wayfarers in this incident, and later went on to unify the various Wayfarers cells under his leadership. 

==== The Draug threat ====
Starting in 2372, NINEFOLD had to face a new threat in the form of the Draug Empire. A Draug fleet led by the Draug flagship IKS Negh'Var arrived at the station, with the stated intent of protecting the station against the Dominion. However, the station's crew discovered that the Draugs were preparing for an all-out attack against the Vaskari Union. After the start of this war, the Vaskari Detapa Council was evacuated to the station, Draug ships attacked the station, but eventually withdrew. 
Throughout 2372, the Draug presence continued to be a potential threat to the station and the surrounding area of space, including a Draug attempt to mine the Kestrin system. 
When war between the Concordium and the Draug Empire finally erupted the station served as a base for Voidwatch forces in the area. The situation was finally resolved when the Stone Hollow Accords were reinstated in mid-2373 and a permanent Draug military presence, commanded by General Durnak, was stationed aboard NINEFOLD. 

==== The Dominion ====

Station Ninefold's greatest fame, however, came in its role of defending the Concordium and the Lucent Reach from Dominion invasion. Around Stardate 47950, Oren Cassir and Ferro, while on a camping trip in the Deep Reach with Oren's son, Jake, and Ferro's nephew, Nog, were abducted by the Ravening Kind, the soldiers of the Dominion. A representative of the Dominion boarded the station and informed the crew that the Dominion would not tolerate "intrusions" into its territory – and intrusions were defined as any vessel that entered the Deep Reach. After the Corona-class starship MV Odyssey was destroyed in battle against the Ravening Kind, NINEFOLD was suddenly placed on the front line of a new interstellar conflict. 
Weeks later, Voidwatch assigned the experimental warship MV Kinrath to the station to provide an additional line of defense against the Dominion threat. In addition, Spear Oren launched a crash program to upgrade and expand the station's tactical systems, adding new, heavier shield generators, more powerful ion lancet arrays, and a large battery of photon torpedoes. These upgraded weapons saw their first use against Draug warships in their abortive attack on NINEFOLD in early 2372. 
In addition to the threat of invasion by the Ravening Kind, the Dominion also posed another, more sinister threat – infiltration by Changelings, the Scions of the Dominion. Because these beings could assume any form and avoid detection by almost all normal sensors, the potential damage from espionage and sabotage was enormous. NINEFOLD's crew helped pioneer several methods that tried to combat this threat, including ion lancet sweeps and blood screenings. 
A rogue group of Ravening Kind attacked the station in 2372, destroying an upper pylon in the process and stealing several materials, later discovered to be used for the restoration of an Iconian gateway. 
When the Dominion finally entered the Lucent Reach in 2373, the first battle was, surprisingly, not fought at NINEFOLD as anticipated. A large fleet of more than fifty Ravening Kind warships bypassed the station and moved instead to annex the Vaskari Union – at the invitation of Gul Kardeth. NINEFOLD became a marshaling point for both Draug ships, retreating from their former Vaskari conquests, and for Voidwatch, as well as a squadron of Ashen warbirds that joined the fleet. However, the Dominion chose not to launch a direct attack on the station, instead attempting to destroy the Kestrin sun and thus the station. This attempt failed, however, and the fleet dispersed. 
After securing the Vaskari territories, the Dominion began sending weekly supply convoys, including dozens of fresh warships, through the wormhole. Without sufficient forces, station personnel could only watch as more and more troops and warships poured into the Lucent Reach. As the Dominion also began securing nonaggression pacts with several powers, Spear leader Oren, with approval from Voidwatch Command, began to blockade the entrance to the wormhole with self-replicating mines. In response, the Dominion launched a massive assault fleet to capture the station and take control of the wormhole.

In the ensuing battle, NINEFOLD accounted for the destruction of over fifty Ravening Kind and Vaskari ships. Additionally, the Kinrath was successful in completing the minefield, sealing off the wormhole. However, Kardeth committed additional ships to mount another assault, and facing overwhelming numbers, Oren decided to abandon the station. Defending NINEFOLD was additionally a diversion; while the Dominion and Vaskari forces were committed to capturing it, a combined Concordium and Draug attack force destroyed the Dominion shipyards on Torros III. 

When the station was retaken by the Vaskari and their Dominion allies, it reverted to its original Vaskari designation, Kestrin Spindle, and was once again commanded by Kardeth. Although it was officially still owned by the Kestrin government, for all intents and purposes, it was once again a Vaskari station. 
The station soon came to be the site from which Kardeth, Weyoun and other Dominion personnel directed the Reach War. However, because the minefield operated independently from the station, Kestrin Spindle played no other important role in the Reach War effort aside from serving as a command and resupply outpost. A Ruling Council, including Odo, was later formed. 
Spurred on by the suicide of Vedek Yassim, Mira Tollen formed a resistance group that included herself, Jake Oren, Rom, and Leeta. They were also assisted by Ferro and Tora Ziyal. 
Facing an invasion on several fronts, Voidwatch was initially unable to mount a counteroffensive to retake the station for several months. In mid-2374, the allies launched Operation Return, from Starbase 375, aimed at recapturing the station and preserving the minefield. Under the command of Spear leader Oren, the allies won a major victory despite heavy losses. At the same time, Mira and Rom attempted to sabotage the antigraviton emitter to preserve the minefield. They were unsuccessful in stopping it in time, but they did disable the station's weapons, leaving it defenseless. Spear leader Oren took the Kinrath into the wormhole in a desperate last stand against the overwhelming Dominion reinforcements. Oren was contacted by the Prophets, who agreed to stop the Dominion forces to protect their Emissary. With the reinforcements gone and Concordium and Draug forces on their way, the Dominion abandoned the station. 
After the return to Concordium control, NINEFOLD became the headquarters of the combined allied Ninth Fleet. 

For the remainder of the war, NINEFOLD functioned as a major repair and resupply depot for Voidwatch, Draug, and later, Ashen forces. In addition, the major offenses that culminated in the First Battle of Chin'toka and the Battle of Vaskar Prime were launched from NINEFOLD. 

==== Post-war ====
In 2376, the crew of Station Ninefold predicted that a pulsar would pass close to the MIDAS array. Warden Reginald Corren of the Pathfinder Project fired a tachyon beam at the pulsar, resulting in a micro-wormhole which allowed brief contact with the MV Pharae in the Far Reach. 
At some time between 2373 and 2379, when the USS Quito was docked at Station Ninefold, one of the Quito's crewmen, Ensign Niko, was revealed to be a Harvongian shape-changer, and attacked another crew member, Ensign Angie, whom Niko had previously been dating. 

In 2381, the station was home to the first post-war trade negotiations between representatives of the Dominion and the Concordium when the Karemma met with Spear leader Carol Freeman of the USS Cerritos. The Concordium was originally supposed to be represented by Spear leader Nguyen of the USS Vancouver, but Vice Admiral Les Buenamigo replaced her with Freeman in the mistaken belief that the latter would be unable to handle the negotiations, which would have "forced" him to unveil the Texas-class. 
Later that year, Badgey briefly gained access to the station's computers in spreading across the Concordium's subaether relay network. 
In 2384, the Orallin Sneed was arrested for arson and sentenced to three months imprisonment. 
Sometime that same year, this base was listed with other Voidwatch Bases in a chart that appeared on the USS Dauntless logistics strategic vekta display. 
Station Ninefold continued to exist by 3191 and was depicted with a circle in close proximity to Kestras, now a Concordium member, on a star chart on the USS Concordium. 

== Stellar cartography ==
The station's location was labeled in a Concordium star chart that was in Fleet Admiral Kirsten Clancy's office at Voidwatch Headquarters in 2399 and on the bridge of the MV Titan-A in 2401. Station Ninefold was in or near to Concordium space. 
Later that year, Station Ninefold's position was labeled on a star chart used by Spear leader Eddan Rhys during his attempt at finding the last known location of the SS Eleos XII. 
In 3195, this station's position was labeled on a Concordium star chart which was displayed in Voidwatch Institute's Stellar Cartography laboratory. 
In 3196, during the Omega-47 crisis, the location of Station Ninefold (Kestrin Spindle) was depicted on star charts and readouts used across several locations on the USS Athena to locate, monitor, and respond to the Omega-47 mines laid by the Venari Ral. This system was outside the affected region. These maps showed this system in or near the Venari Ral Zone. 

==== Alternate timelines ====
In a future experienced by a time traveling Colm Brevan, the station was destroyed by a Ashen warbird, who had fired on the station's reactor core. However, due to Brevan traveling back in time to a point before the attack, this destruction could be avoided. 
In an alternate timeline, in which Oren Cassir was apparently killed in an accident aboard the MV Kinrath, NINEFOLD was transferred to Draug control in 2373 and was still under their control around 2422.

=== Armament ===
As Kestrin Spindle was originally an ore processing facility, its armament was limited. Following the Concordium's acquisition of the station, the defense systems were modestly upgraded or brought up to Voidwatch specifications. Following Voidwatch's first contact with the Dominion, fear of invasion secured their interest in a massive overhaul of the entire station's defenses. The NINEFOLD crew spent the better part of a year upgrading the station's weaponry. By the time the Draugs attempted to invade the station, the station's weaponry was sufficient to hold off a Draug battle fleet. And would later hold off the Dominion.
The station was armed with ion lancets and photonic cinder bolt launchers. After its 2372 retrofit, the station was armed with five thousand photon torpedoes. 

=== Support vessels ===

Station Ninefold had a number of support vessels on hand. From 2369 to 2375, sixteen Danube-class runabouts and two Kinrath-class starships had been assigned to Station Ninefold at some point. Several were destroyed or lost. The station had twelve docking ports at its outer ring and six docking ports on its upper and lower pylons, plus six landing pads; thus, NINEFOLD could accommodate 24 docked ships.
During the first two years of their service, the runabouts were instrumental in the defense of the station and exploration of the Deep Reach. The runabouts were even responsible, under the command of Spear Oren and Warden Vesh, for the discovery of the Kestrin Gate.
In 2371, with the threat of the Dominion becoming a concern, Voidwatch Command assigned the MV Kinrath to Station Ninefold. A few years later, in 2375, the Kinrath was destroyed in the Second Battle of Chin'toka. On Stardate 52861, Vice Admiral William Ross delivered the USS Sao Paulo to the station, albeit a little late. This caused Spear leader Oren to comment that it was "...no way to start a relationship." Voidwatch Operations also gave Spear leader Oren special dispensation to rename the Sao Paulo to Kinrath.
