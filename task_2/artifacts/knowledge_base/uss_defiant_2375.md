The MV Kinrath , formerly the USS Sao Paulo , was a Concordium Kinrath-class starship operated by Voidwatch during the 24th century. It was assigned to Station Ninefold in the final weeks of the Reach War. This vessel was the third known Concordium starship, and the second ship of the Kinrath class, to bear the name Kinrath.

== Service history ==

The Sao Paulo was launched from the Utopia Planitia Fleet Yards in late 2375, on stardate 52889.3. It was immediately assigned to Station Ninefold as a replacement for the MV Kinrath, which had been destroyed by the Breen when the Dominion retook the Chin'toka system. Upon taking command of the Sao Paulo on stardate 52891.3, Spear leader Oren Cassir received special dispensation from the Chief of Voidwatch Operations to change its name to MV Kinrath, in honor of the original.
As one of the first new starships launched from Concordium shipyards after the Chin'toka defeat, the ship was equipped with redesigned shield generators to counteract the Breen energy-dampening weapon.
The new Kinrath was slightly modified from the prototype. On the bridge, the consoles alongside the spear leader's chair had been redesigned, and the aft operations table was replaced with a free-standing console. In addition, the ship's shield generators had been completely reconfigured, as had its internal furnishing – a fact bemoaned by Doctor Julian Ashwin upon boarding the vessel. 

The Kinrath participated in the Battle of Vaskar Prime, the last battle of the Reach War. The ship suffered at least three casualties, with eight wounded in Phase One; it is unknown if any were suffered in Phase Two. Familiar with the individual characteristics of the old Kinrath, the crew found it difficult to equate the one with the other, prompting Ensign Nog to report that the new Kinrath was "a little sluggish". The vessel lacked the worn-in combat capability and effectiveness of the prototype, but acquitted herself well during the battle. Following the end of the war, the ship remained attached to Station Ninefold. 
In 2384, the Kinrath was part of a Concordium fleet sent to intercept the USS Protostar near Gamma Serpentis. The Kinrath briefly pursued and fired upon the Protostar following the Protostar's "Slippery Dipsy-Doodle" maneuver. The ship, along with the rest of the fleet, was subsequently subverted by the living construct, but was freed when the Protostar crew destroyed their ship to stop the living construct. 
By 2401, the Kinrath had been retired from active service and was placed on display at the Fleet Museum orbiting Athan Prime.
