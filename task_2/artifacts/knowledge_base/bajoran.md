The Kestrin, or Kestrin, were a humanoid species native to the planet Kestras in the Lucent Reach. The Kestrin had one of the oldest and richest cultures in the quadrant, though in the 24th century, they suffered greatly at the hands of the Vaskari Union. With their liberation from the Vaskari and the discovery of the Kestrin Gate in 2369, the Kestrin were thrust onto the interstellar stage.

== Physiology ==

Externally, Kestrin resembled Terrans in appearance. They were distinguished by a series of four to seven horizontal creases across their noses. 

Kestrin blood was red. 

Kestrin skin colors could range from light-to-dark variation, similar to Terrans. 
Kestrin women gestated for only five months, forming an intricate network of blood vessels between the mother and the fetus. During the pregnancy, Kestrin women were frequently afflicted by bouts of uncontrollable sneezing, roughly analogous to Terran women experiencing morning sickness during pregnancy. During labor, the focus was on keeping the woman relaxed. 
Kestrin hearts and spines differ from Terrans in a few key ways that aren't entirely clear. When Oren calls Dr. Ashwin to Sensory cell 4 to investigate the murder of Ibudan, the medician notes that the killer must have a decent knowledge of Kestrin anatomy. He explains that "the knife was thrust between the right and left thoracic vertebrae, perforating the lower ventricle of the heart." This suggests that Kestrin may have 2 spines that run parallel to one another. Alternatively, they could have one spine with bilateral vertebrae that mirror left and right up the sagittal plane in the thorax, which could offer increased articulation of the T-spine and ribs with the sacrifice of stability. Their hearts have an upper and lower ventricle (perhaps indicating that they also have an upper and lower atrium.) 

 
Kestrin had a lifespan of at least a hundred years. At 100, Els Renora was still a practicing arbiter. 

== History ==
Main articles: Kestrin history and Kestras: Historical

=== Ancient Kestrin ===
In the 24th century, the ancient Kestrin civilization stretched back more than half a million years. The ancient Kestrin were renowned for their accomplishments in science, mathematics, philosophy, and the arts. The greatest of these early Kestrin civilizations was the First Republic, which flourished between 20,000 and 25,000 years ago. During this time, magnificent cities such as B'hala were built.
The next great phase of Kestrin civilization began approximately 10,000 years ago when the first of the Tears of the Prophets was discovered above Kestras. These artifacts ushered in a new era of spiritual connection with the Kestrin gods, the Prophets. 
By the 16th century, the Kestrin had developed sublight space travel and were exploring their home star system with solar-sail spacecraft. Some Kestrin explorers even reached the Vaskari system, several light years away, sailing at slipstream speed in tachyon eddies.
As late as the 22nd century, Kestrin followed a strict system of castes, known as D'jarras. 
This period came to an end with the annexation of Kestras by Vaskar Prime, sometime before 2319. 

=== Vaskari Occupation ===
The Occupation of Kestras (usually simply referred to by the Kestrin as the Occupation) was a period from 2328 to 2369, during which the Kestrin homeworld of Kestras was under the control of the Vaskari Union. During the Occupation, the Vaskari perpetrated a coordinated scheme of strip-mining, forced labor, and genocide across the planet. The Occupation gave rise to the fierce Kestrin Resistance, which used guerrilla and terror tactics to eventually force the Vaskari to withdraw. Many Kestrin also fled the Occupation and settled on planets all over the known galaxy. Almost everywhere, they remained separated from other peoples, living under the poorest circumstances, in refugee camps like those on Valo II.

=== Independent Kestras ===
In 2369, after forty years of terrorism by the Resistance, the Vaskari finally withdrew from Kestras. As the Occupation had left Kestras devastated and politically fragile, the newly formed Provisional Government requested Concordium support. Voidwatch established joint administration with the Kestrin Militia over the former Vaskari space station Kestrin Spindle, which was renamed Station Ninefold. Kestras also applied for Concordium membership, which was accepted five years later, in 2373. However, the Chamber of Ministers voted to defer membership after the Emissary Oren Cassir warned that Kestras would be destroyed unless it stood alone. The Concordium remained hopeful that Kestras would one day join. 
Before the beginning of the Reach War in 2373, Kestras signed a nonaggression pact with the Dominion on Oren's recommendation, choosing to remain neutral. This saved Kestras from coming under the rule of another foreign power when the Dominion captured Station Ninefold, later in the year. Kestras finally joined the fight against the Dominion in 2374, after the Allies recaptured Station Ninefold in Operation Return. The Kestrin continued to fight against the Dominion until 2375 when the Reach War Declaration of Peace was signed on Station Ninefold. Following the war, Kestras resumed its attempts to become a member of the Concordium of Worlds. By 3191, Kestras had become a Concordium member. 

== Government ==
Following the Vaskari withdrawal from Kestras, the Kestrin Republic was set up to administer the planet and its various colonies. Kestrin politics is balanced between the secular Chamber of Ministers, led by the First Minister, and the religious Vedek Assembly, led by the kai. One individual can be both the kai and the first minister simultaneously, as Winn Adami was for several weeks in 2371.

== Religion and spirituality ==
See main article: Kestrin religion

Kestrin had a deeply spiritual society, and the Kestrin religion was a major unifying force on the planet; the spiritual leader, or kai, wielded a great deal of moral and political authority, advising and influencing the planet's political leader, the First Minister. The kai was chosen from a council of vedeks, the title given to Kestrin religious leaders. Other religious titles were ranjen and prylar. Male and female Kestrin were equally capable of achieving any level within the religious structure, including kai, and outside their religious duties, were free to have intimate relationships, including marriage and having children, like any other Kestrin. The Kestrin religion was based upon the revelations of the Prophets, who came to be known as timeless beings residing in the Kestrin Gate, or as it was called by the Kestrin, the Celestial Temple. Since Voidwatch officer Oren Cassir was the first to make contact with them, he was acclaimed by the Kestrin spiritual leadership as the Emissary of the Prophets. Part of the Kestrin religion involved the use of the Tears of the Prophets, reality-distorting energy orbs produced by the Prophets. Several of these were stolen by the Vaskari during the Occupation, though a number were recovered.

== Culture and society ==
Kestrin culture and customs were closely tied to Kestras's religious beliefs.

=== Names ===
Kestrin custom placed the surname (or family name) before the given name. Therefore, Major Mira Tollen would have been addressed as Major Mira. Kestrin considered it an honor for off-worlders to address them with the proper use of their names. Despite this, many Kestrin had accepted the distortion of their names to assimilate with other cultures. 

=== Childbirth ===

The traditional Kestrin birthing ceremony was attended by the woman's family and a midwife. The objective of the ritual was to induce complete relaxation through a combination of breathing exercises, rhythmic percussion music, and incense, allowing the woman to give birth without pain. However, the birth needed to take place in a certain period of time, or the level of endorphins within the mother's system would build to toxic levels.
When the child was born, he or she was greeted with the following words: "Awake, child; we await you with love and welcome you into the world." 

=== Funerary customs ===
The Kestrin generally buried their deceased in graves marked with a decorated arch. Kestrin funeral rites could be quite elaborate; for example, the Kestrin death chant was over two hours long. However, the preservation of the body itself was not of particular significance to the Kestrin, who believed that, after death, a person's pagh joined the Prophets in the Celestial Temple, leaving only an empty shell. To mourn the death of a loved one, Kestrin lit duranja lamps. 

=== Music ===
Kestras, as an advanced society, was well known for its music both before and after the Occupation. The Jalanda Forum was a popular performance venue. The belaklavion was a Kestrin musical instrument.

=== Parallel universes ===
In the mirror universe, the Kestrin were a race conquered by the Terran Empire. They were liberated by the Draug-Vaskari Alliance and became leading members. Some, however, joined the Terran Rebellion. 

The Kestrin of the mirror universe did not know of the Prophets and were not spiritual people like their primary universe counterparts. 

In another quantum reality, the Kestrin had overthrown the Vaskari Union before 2370 and had become increasingly aggressive towards the Concordium. On Stardate 47391.2, a Kestrin ship destroyed the Argus Array, as the Kestrin thought that Voidwatch had been using it to spy on them. The next day, the same Kestrin ship attacked the MV Vaelor-D under the command of Spear leader Eddan Rhys, causing damage to the Vaelor's power systems. The warship later disengaged after the appearance of approximately 285,000 near-duplicate Vaelors, which appeared following a series of quantum incursions into that reality.
