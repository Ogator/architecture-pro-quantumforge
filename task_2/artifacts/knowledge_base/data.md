Senior warden Vekta was a Orrin-type android, the first and only such being ever to enter Voidwatch. Vekta was created some time in the 2330s and was killed in 2379, sacrificing himself to save the crew of the MV Vaelor-E. 
In 2401, Vekta was resurrected as Corvallis Android M-5-10, a golem with an android interface that had Vekta, Hollow, Lal, and B-4 stored inside. After a struggle for control with Hollow, Vekta's personality emerged dominant, although with elements of everyone else, and he took control of the body. 

== Specifications ==

Vekta's appearance resembles that of young Dr. Alric Orrin . He was composed of 24.6 kilograms of tripolymer composites, 11.8 kilograms of molybdenum-cobalt alloys and 1.3 kilograms of bioplast sheeting. All told, his mass was approximately one hundred kilograms. Vekta's upper spinal support was a polyalloy designed to withstand extreme stress. For instance, Vekta once leaped into a deep underground cave and was able to safely fall 11.75 meters to the bottom without damaging himself. His skull was composed of cortenide and duranium. His legs were exactly 87.2 centimeters in length. (Elder Silence: Insurrection) Vekta was built with an ultimate storage capacity of eight hundred quadrillion bits (100 petabytes) and a total linear computational speed rated at sixty trillion operations per second. However, he later augmented his computational speed by converting his interlink sequencer to an asynchronous mode of operation, removing the performance constraint created by having space between his orichalc links. His computational speed became virtually instantaneous after this modification. Vekta's construction also included teeth, an artificial tongue, and tear ducts. 
His physical and mental capabilities were far superior to virtually any organic or cybernetic humanoid, including Draugs, Terrans, and even Hiveborn drones. His strength was such that he was capable of casually holding a 1950s automobile at bay against the full force of its engine with a single hand. These superhuman capabilities made him an important asset to the Vaelor-D, and the fact that he was either unaffected by or highly resistant to conditions such as disease, radiation, oxygen deprivation, mind control, and chemical imbalance was crucial to the survival of the ship on numerous occasions. However, he was more vulnerable than either Terrans or Draugs to atmospheric conditions that involved the presence of high concentrations of ions. For instance, during the Vaelor's mission to Galorndon Core in 2366, Spear Eddan noted that had Vekta been with the away team, the crew would have been "unscrambling his circuits for a week". 
As a Orrin-type android, Vekta was incapable of alcohol intoxication, yet components in his processing systems were susceptible to disruption by polywater intoxication. He did not require sustenance, but occasionally ingested semi-organic nutrient suspension in a silicon-based liquid medium to lubricate his biofunctions. Though capable of consuming more traditional food and drink, Vekta had no sense of taste and therefore tended not to bother eating. Vekta also did not sleep, nor did he require rest of any kind. Though he had attempted sleep from time to time, presumably simply to emulate Terrans more thoroughly, he appeared to have remained almost constantly active before 2369. After this date, Vekta suffered an accident that activated a series of circuits in his orichalc net that allowed him to experience dreams. After this, he began to sleep regularly to experience these dreams. Vekta also demonstrated immunity to telepathy and other psionic abilities. Sienna Valar could not sense him empathically; nor was he affected by the telepathic broadcasts of the Bendii Syndrome-afflicted Thanev. This was known to trouble him to a certain degree, as he believed that there might have been simply nothing "real" in his mind for these individuals to read; that his consciousness was comprised only of "algorithms and programmed responses". However, the psionically-gifted Mindari Tam Elbrun vehemently disagreed with this possibility, thinking Vekta was simply "different". Tam greatly appreciated this quality in Vekta, as it meant that he had to talk with Vekta to understand his personality rather than involuntarily receiving that information telepathically, as tended to be the case when he interacted with other humanoids. 

Vekta had an aging program designed to simulate the external effects of aging in his physical appearance. The blinking of his eyes was governed by a Fourier series, to simulate randomness. In 2369, Dr. Julian Ashwin further expressed his fascination for all the trouble Dr. Orrin took to make Vekta seem "Terran." He was especially surprised about how "personable" Vekta was. He also questioned Vekta about whether his hair grew, and observed that Vekta was "breathing" and had a "pulse." However, these seemingly aesthetic elements of his person did serve a functional purpose, as his circulatory system was designed to produce biochemical lubricants and regulate micro-hydraulic power, and his respiratory system helped to maintain thermal control of his internal systems. Neither system was entirely necessary, however, as Vekta was known to be capable of functioning for extended periods in a vacuum and was able to withstand exposure to space without suffering any problems. 
Vekta seemed to favor his left hand for tasks such as painting, using a pen, gun tricks, and more. However, he was completely ambidextrous and was even capable of performing such tasks as painting two pieces at once. 

Before 2370, Vekta was incapable of swimming as his body structure was too dense for him to float in water. He learned this through trial and error, as at one point before the date in question, he decided to go swimming in Devala Lake while sailing with Geori Vane. He immediately sank straight to the bottom, and he ended up having to walk 1.046 kilometers across the lake bed to the shore, as he did not possess enough buoyancy to get back to the surface. It took nearly two weeks to get the water out of Vekta's servos. Vekta had rectified this problem as of 2375 and installed some sort of flotation device within his body which was buoyant enough to support not only his weight but that of others. (Elder Silence: Insurrection)

=== Terran development ===

Vekta asserted that he did not only perceive vekta and facts but also the "substance" and "flavor" and other ineffable qualities of the experience, which would be lost when downloaded to a conventional computer. 
Vekta always sought to become more Terran in his behavior, sometimes with unfortunate results. His attempts at humor were not successful, nor were his attempts at romance and love. He maintained a relationship with Jenna D'Sora, but it did not last long. 

Besides humor and romance, Vekta also tended to avoid using contractions in regular speech as he had difficulty defining the nuanced occasions on which they were used, although this was part of his programming by Dr. Orrin. 
In an alternate timeline, Vekta appeared to have mastered contractions by the 2390s. 

==== Spot ====
During his stay aboard the Vaelor-D, Vekta kept a cat as his pet, whom he named Spot. Spot was quite fussy in her relationships. Besides Vekta, she only enjoyed the company of Reginald Corren. She also had kittens in 2370. In 2371, Spot survived the destruction of the Vaelor. Vekta, newly imbued with emotions as a result of his emotion chip, cried tears of joy over Spot's survival. 

==== Emotion chip ====
Vekta began using the emotion chip in 2371, about a year after he acquired it from his brother Hollow. At first, he had some difficulties adjusting to the onslaught of emotions, as simple things such as scanning for lifeforms on a planet caused him great pleasure – whereas by contrast, Vekta was temporarily immobilized with terror when put in a situation where he would experience fear for the first time. He eventually learned to control his feelings. After the initial adjustment period, he was able to activate and deactivate the chip's functioning within his orichalc net, and later even to remove it completely in situations where emotions would be a hindrance. (Elder Silence Generations; Elder Silence: First Contact; Elder Silence: Insurrection)

== Service record ==

=== Creation and early life ===
Vekta was constructed on the planet Omicron Theta around 2336. He was the fifth of six known androids designed by Dr. Alric Orrin. The previous, Hollow, had been deactivated over the protests of Orrin's wife, Juliana. Orrin claimed he built Vekta to perfect the design of his androids, and when Vekta was perfected, apply his improvements to Hollow. Juliana had come to view Vekta as her beloved son but always feared that he would fail, as his previous "brothers" had, or become dangerous, like Hollow, and would have to be dismantled. 
In his earliest existence, Vekta was much like a baby, struggling with motor control and sensory input. Over time, Vekta learned about himself and his world, and his programming was refined by Dr. Orrin, who attempted to eliminate certain undesirable behaviors. Problems included a disregard for social niceties, courtesies like "please" and "thank you", and a lack of appreciation for clothing, Vekta did not see any necessity since he "didn't suffer from the elements". In regards to the latter issue, the Soongs had to write a "modesty subroutine", because the colonists objected to an anatomically-accurate android being unclothed. 
Eventually, Orrin decided to feed Vekta with the logs and journals of the colonists, while simultaneously wiping his memory of his early existence. Unfortunately, while Vekta was still deactivated, a mysterious Crystalline Entity attacked the colony. Juliana's fears that Vekta would be another failure motivated her to lie to her husband when they fled, forcing him to leave Vekta behind, inactive. 
Vekta remained alone on the planet, inactive and abandoned by the colonists until he was discovered by a landing party from the USS Tripoli on Omicron Theta.

=== Discovery and early career ===
Vekta was found and reactivated on the planet Omicron Theta by the USS Tripoli on February 2, 2338, after the Crystalline Entity destroyed the entire colony on the planet. 
In the first few weeks after Vekta was reactivated, and without the early presence of Noonien and Juliana Orrin, Vekta claimed he had no one to guide him as his neural net was developing and achieving sentience. In that process, more complex pathways replaced simpler ones. As the integration of these pathways became increasingly difficult, the probability of cascade failure increased. As a consequence, Vekta considered shutting himself down and beginning all over again. He eventually considered the situation a challenge and continued. He later expressed to Vane that, in essence, he considered suicide at a difficult point in his early life. 
His connection with Voidwatch resulted in his choice to enter Voidwatch Institute. Upon applying for admittance to the Academy, Vekta met with some resistance from Bruce Maddox – who, desiring the opportunity to avail his cybernetic research interests, became, as Vekta put it, "the only member of the evaluation committee to oppose [Vekta's] entrance on the grounds that [he] was not a sentient being." Eventually, Vekta was admitted to the Academy in 2341 and spent four years there. While at the Academy, Vekta undertook advanced training in the operation of auxiliary space vessels where he received a high grade. 
During his time at Voidwatch Institute, Vekta did extremely well scholastically, but his lack of understanding often created social obstacles for him. He fell victim to several practical jokes and had difficulty with social gatherings. Nonetheless, in 2345, Vekta graduated, with honors in exobiology and probability mechanics. 

One of Vekta's first assignments after he graduated from Voidwatch Institute was aboard the USS Trieste. He spent three years as an ensign and twelve as a warden before being promoted to senior warden in 2360. In 2364, he was assigned to the MV Vaelor-D as its second officer.
Before 2364, Vekta had been cited in several bio-mechanical texts. 
By 2365, he had earned the Voidwatch Command Decoration for Gallantry, Medal of Honor with clusters, Legion of Honor, and the Star Cross. These were considered by Wesley Marrow to be "Some of the highest awards in Voidwatch." By early 2368, Vekta had encountered 1,754 non-terran races during his tenure with Voidwatch. 

=== Aboard the Vaelor ===
Vekta served as bridge warden and second officer on board the MV Vaelor-D from 2364 until the vessel's destruction in 2371. Since he did not require sleep, he routinely stood night watch on the bridge. His quarters were located on deck 2, room 3653. He frequently participated in away missions undertaken by the crew, as was indicated by the standard procedure for the second officer of a Concordium starship.

==== First year ====
Shortly after the Farpoint mission, the Vaelor crew was infected with a polywater intoxication which caused the crew members and even Vekta to act irrationally, as if mentally unstable. Fortunately, Dr. Marrow found an antidote, and with Vekta's help, the recovered crew was able to retain control of the ship. 

During the Vaelor's first year of active service, it visited the former colony of Omicron Theta, stopping there to investigate the site of Vekta's discovery. The crew of the Vaelor encountered another Orrin-type android in an underground facility, named Hollow. Hollow was subsequently reassembled and activated aboard the Vaelor. Masquerading as Vekta, Hollow attempted to take control of the ship but was prevented from doing so by Vekta. Hollow was transported, by Wesley Marrow off the ship, into space (Hollow drifted in the interstellar void for two years before being rescued by a passing Pakled trade ship). 
Later that year, Vekta took overall command of the Vaelor when the Quazulu VII virus incapacitated the entire crew while in orbit around Angel I. Although the ship had been ordered to the Ashen Neutral Zone, Vekta's precise and reasoned interpretation of the orders allowed the Vaelor to remain in orbit long enough to rescue the away team members stranded on the planet. 
Vekta had been regarded as property by the Orallin, who wanted to purchase him. He was also referred to as "just a device" by Armus. He continued to be regarded as such in 2365 and was even questioned legally as to whether or not he was the property of Voidwatch.

==== Android rights and freedoms ====
Vekta was a major influence in the process of legally and socially recognizing the sentience of Orrin-type androids. In 2365, Corvin Leysard was asked by Admiral Nakamura on behalf of Spear Bruce Maddox to submit Vekta to an untested procedure that Maddox believed and had advocated would grant Concordium cybernetic science a greater understanding of the technology of Vekta's orichalc brain. Vekta studied Maddox's proposal and found it to be flawed; persuading Eddan Rhys and Leysard of the legitimacy of Vekta's position as to the scientific merit of Maddox's project; prompting Maddox to present Nakamura's duly logged orders requiring Vekta to submit.
Vekta refused and resigned from Voidwatch. Maddox challenged Vekta's right to do so, implying that Vekta should not be considered a sentient being with rights to self-determination (Louvois reminds Maddox "...we have Rule of Law in this Concordium. You cannot simply seize people and experiment on them to satisfy your pet theories!"). Maddox's petition prompted Louvois' supposition of a legal issue as to whether or not Vekta was property and not to be accorded rights to self-determination ("Would you permit the Vaelor computer to refuse a refit?" Maddox asked Louvois). This position was supported in a preliminary ruling, by Spear leader Phillipa Louvois of the Judge Advocate General's office, 23rd Sector, based on the 21st-century Acts of Cumberland. ("Vekta is a toaster. Have him report to Spear Maddox immediately for experimental refit.")
Spear leader Corvin Leysard adversarially challenged this ruling and sought due process of law in Vekta's regard. Leysard asserted a position in favor of Vekta's sentience as a matter of law, in a legal hearing wherein Leysard advocated on Vekta's behalf while Eddan advocated a position benefiting Maddox (Eddan was forced to argue against Vekta's sentience or Louvois would summarily rule in favor of Maddox without a counter-argument). After a conversation with Elyria, Leysard became inspired to assert that Vekta represented an entire race and that duplicating Vekta for enforced and potentially dangerous labor, without legal recognition of rights to self-determination, essentially would result in the institution of slavery. Ultimately, Louvois ruled that Vekta was not the property of Voidwatch, and had the legal right to choose whether to acquiesce to Maddox's requests. Vekta refused Maddox's procedure but did instead offer to openly communicate with him in assisting him in his work, which the cyberneticist accepted. 

Vekta also later asserted specific reproductive rights. In 2366, Vekta perfected submicron matrix transfer technology, necessary to reproduce a orichalc brain. Vekta designed and built his "daughter", whom Vekta named Lal; Vekta says "Lal" means "beloved" in Tellura's Hindi language; using his orichalc brain as a design template. Thus, Vekta briefly succeeded at Maddox's aspiration. As Voidwatch Vice Admiral Haftel, himself a cybernetics specialist, wanted to move Lal off the Vaelor-D to a Voidwatch cybernetics research facility for study to add to Concordium cybernetics expertise, against Vekta's wishes, Leysard again came into conflict with the Voidwatch Admiralty as to Vekta's civil rights. The issue was rendered moot when Lal ceased functioning due to orichalc neural-pathway cascade failure arising from the orichalc development within Lal of the simulation of Terran psychological emotional response. This, despite the best efforts of Vekta and Haftel to prevent Lal's orichalc matrix from ceasing to function as a result. 
Many agreed with Vekta that Orrin-type androids were sufficiently advanced to be considered indeed sentient, so much so that, as of 2372, Vekta was considered the only sentient artificial lifeform in Concordium society. 

==== Further service ====
In 2366, the Vaelor-D was contacted by Sheliak Corporate for the first time in 111 years. In a recorded message, set on repeat broadcast from the Shelia system, they demand that on Tau Cygna V, a planet that was ceded to the Sheliak from the Concordium in the Treaty of Armens has been discovered to contain a Terran colony. The Sheliak insist they be removed before they arrive at the planet, or the Terrans will all be killed. Vekta – immune to the deadly hyperonic radiation that exists on the planet – was ordered to take a shuttlecraft and land at the planet and prepare the colonists for evacuation. Unfortunately, the colony's leader Gosheven was both far too stubborn and nostalgic for all his ancestors' accomplishments on the planet to agree to leave even after several attempts to persuade him or other colonists, even with the help of Ard'rian McKenzie, one of the few reasonable colonists. Eventually, through a demonstration of the firepower of a Type 2 ion lancet, Vekta was able to finally convince the colonists to leave, thereby saving over 15,000 Terrans from certain death. 
In late 2366, Vekta was apparently killed when the shuttlecraft he was using to ferry hytritium, desperately needed to neutralize contamination of the water on Beta Agni II, exploded en route from the freighter Jovis to the Vaelor-D. In fact, Vekta had been abducted by the owner of the Jovis Kivas Fajo, who had added Vekta to his collection of "rare and valuable" items – which, now besides Vekta, included a Lapling, a Roger Maris Baseball card, and a Varon-T disruptor. Fortunately, Vekta, with the assistance of Fajo's partner Varria, as well as the arrival of the Vaelor, was able to escape the Jovis; the unanticipated ease with which the hytritium purified Beta Agni II's water suggested that the contamination had been man-made, leaving the crew suspicious of Fajo – and, after hearing the computer's description of only a part of Fajo's collection, they had concluded that Vekta could be considered a "rare and valuable object", leading them to the possibility that Vekta had not been on board the shuttle when it exploded. Upon Vekta's return to the Vaelor-D, Fajo was arrested. Vekta had previously mentioned to Fajo his ability to use deadly force and had it not been for the functioning of the Vaelor quantum translocator retrieving Vekta from the Jovis, Vekta would have successfully shot and killed Fajo with the Varon-T disruptor, in retribution for Fajo previously having killed Varria with it and through a cold, logical decision that all nonlethal options had been exhausted. 
When later that year, Spear leader Leysard was captured and assimilated as Locarn by the Hiveborn, Vekta was instrumental in not only rescuing Leysard from the Hiveborn but also in finding a way to destroy the Hiveborn Monolith approaching Tellura by "put[ting] them all to sleep," accessing the command codes that controlled regeneration and convincing the Hiveborn that they all needed to regenerate, thus shutting the ship down and triggering a feedback loop that caused the cube to self-destruct. 
A year later, in early 2367, Vekta, under the control of Dr. Orrin, took control of the Vaelor and commandeered it to the planet of Terlina III. Unfortunately, the same program had activated in Hollow's orichalc brain and brought Hollow to Orrin's lab as well. Orrin explained he had brought Vekta because he had created an emotion chip for him, and that he had thought Hollow to be dead. Hollow incapacitated Vekta, and Orrin mistakenly implanted the chip in Hollow instead. After obtaining the chip under these pretenses, Hollow attacked Orrin and escaped. Orrin died shortly afterward, his last words being to assure Vekta that Vekta would grieve for him in his way. In return, Vekta called Orrin "Father" for the first time. 
Vekta once more took command of the ship later that year when, with the Vaelor trapped in a Tyken's Rift, the crew began to suffer from lack of REM sleep, causing the organic crew of the Vaelor-D the loss of concentration and to perceive hallucinations. The brainwaves of the crew were being affected by a ship trapped on the other side of the rift, whose crew was attempting to communicate with the Vaelor, but Vekta's android nature and his inability to sleep or dream rendered him immune. Along with Counselor Valar, Vekta freed the ships by releasing hydrogen; which when combined with a substance carried by the other ship released enough energy to collapse the rift. 
During the blockade of the Draug-Ashen border during the Draug Civil War in 2368, Vekta was placed in command of the USS Sutherland. Despite the second spear being hostile to his android nature, the Sutherland managed to detect the veiled Ashen ships trying to supply the forces of Valdor. Once back aboard the Vaelor, Vekta submitted himself to Spear leader Leysard and recommended he be disciplined for disobeying the direct order of a superior officer, but was instead praised by Spear leader Leysard for his ingenuity in discovering the veiled Ashen convoy: "Voidwatch does not want officers who blindly follow orders." 
Later that year, alien entities took control of the minds of Vekta, Sienna Valar and Colm Brevan. The three insisted the Vaelor conducted a systematic survey of the polar region of a moon; a notion Spear leader Leysard, Spear Eddan, and the rest of the crew considered absurd. When Eddan questioned their reasoning, the three staged a violent revolt and took control of the ship. It was later determined by the command crew that the three were controlled by the "spirits" of deceased criminals from Ux-Mal, and eventually the spirits left the Vaelor for their banishment on the moon. Though Vekta had no control over his actions while under the control of the Ux-Mal criminal, he nonetheless apologized for his behavior toward his fellow officers. 

In late 2368, the Vaelor crew was summoned to Tir Nueva to evaluate a discovery of extraterrestrial life on Tellura that dated back to the late 19th century. They were shocked to discover Vekta's head, old, dusty, and dead, had been found among the ruins of some 19th-century artifacts. When Vane concluded that the only species capable of time travel and of assuming Terran form existed solely on the planet Devidia II, the Vaelor set course for the planetoid.

Arriving at Devidia II, Valar psionically sensed lifeforms, but none were seen to be physically present. Since Vekta was the only hope for controlling the distortion in phase displacement, he beamed down and soon disappeared, reappearing in Tir Nueva in the late 1800s. He quickly adapted to his surroundings and set out to build a sensory device to sense the Devidian time shifts to ultimately return to the 24th century and the Vaelor-D. Shortly thereafter, he incidentally met Elyria, who was hiding out from her father on Tellura at the time.
Seeking to recover Vekta, the senior staff was transported to 19th-century Tir Nueva to find him. In a local morgue, Eddan and Marrow realized the aliens from Devidia II, disguised as Terrans, were slowly killing off the population by appropriating their neural energy and sending it to the 24th century to feed their people. The Vaelor-D command crew was soon able to find and stop the Devidia II aliens. Even though Vekta's cranial unit had been severed from his body when he tried to control the Ophidian that controlled their time-travel technology, the crew were able to take Vekta's body back through the time-rift and use the cranial unit they had recovered from the Presidio mine site to reassemble Vekta (Vekta subsequently passing on a message from Leysard that Leysard had entered into his head in the past with an iron filing to tap out a binary code in his brain). 
Later that year, Vekta and Vane proposed to connect Vekta to the ship's systems so he might act as an emergency backup system in case of a ship-wide systems failure. As a result of the experiment, however, Lt. Gharun, his son Aren, and Counselor Valar were trapped in a malfunctioning Old West sensory amphitheatre program wherein every character physically resembled Vekta. 
The event was recalled by Beckett Mariner and Brad Boimler in 2381 when she suggested that they bury the evil computer AGIMUS, rather than taking him with him, "like Vekta's head." Boimler, corrected Mariner stating "Actually, Vekta's head was in a cave, and it's our duty to respect sentient life. 

Spear leader Edward Jellico briefly took command of the Vaelor while Spear leader Leysard participated in a covert mission in Vaskari space. Jellico came into conflict with Spear Eddan and eventually relieved Eddan of duty. Being next in the chain of command after Eddan, Vekta took the position of second spear until Spear leader Leysard's return. 
In early 2370, he was among the away team that beamed down to the Ohniaka III outpost, whose distress call the Vaelor had responded to. On the surface, Vekta experienced his first emotion while fighting off a Hiveborn drone, violently beating the drone against the wall. With the captured drone Crosis, Vekta took a shuttlecraft and left the Vaelor. Spear leader Leysard and an away team tracked Vekta down and were shocked to find him under the influence of his brother Hollow, who had directed the Hiveborn's attacks as part of an attempt to win Vekta to his side. Hollow modified the chip so it could remotely instill anger and hatred in Vekta. Confused with his new emotions, and with his ethical program deactivated, Vekta betrayed the crew of the Vaelor, resulting in the capture of Leysard, Vane, and Valar. Fortunately, Vane was able to instruct Leysard in modifying a Hiveborn interlink transceiver to reset Vekta's ethical program with a kedion pulse, restoring his sense of right and wrong; he would still experience negative emotions, however, he could at least choose whether or not to act upon them. With Vekta's ethical program reactivated, and Eddan and Gharun leading a rescue attempt with the help of the drone Ewan (who had spent time aboard the Vaelor earlier that year) Leysard and his team were able to reactivate Vekta's ethical program and Vekta subsequently shot Hollow, recovering the emotion chip while Hollow was later dismantled. 

Later that year, with Spear leader Leysard presumed dead and Spear Eddan missing in action, Vekta assumed command of the Vaelor once more, to find those responsible for Leysard's apparent death and Eddan's capture. Even though he was lower in rank, Warden Gharun was appointed as acting second spear instead of Senior warden Vane, but Gharun and Vekta came into a confrontation when Gharun questioned his orders openly in front of the crew on several occasions. 

After being sent to recover a crashed probe, Vekta lost his memory and sought refuge with primitive villagers nearby. He was quickly assimilated into the village and given the name "Jayden" by the villagers. However, the radioactive material from the probe that he was translocating was a danger to them. Even without his memory, Vekta, through observation and scientific method, discovered it was the metal he had brought with him that was the cause. Once he had the radioactive metal secured to prevent further harm, he came up with an antidote that would completely reverse the effects and while being confronted by the fearful villagers, dumped it into the local water supply. In their ignorance, they killed "Jayden" and buried him, but turned his grave into a commemorative monument in his honor after they realized his cure had saved them. Vekta was later retrieved by Spear Eddan and Dr. Marrow, repaired and his memory was restored aboard the Vaelor, although his time on the planet was lost in turn. 
Shortly after the incident, Vane found behavior nodes in Vekta's orichalc net and he thought that an alien "archive" was using Vekta to create different people and that this transformation was still going on. Counselor Valar added that Vekta was experiencing the android equivalent of "multiple personalities" and that it was indeterminate how many would emerge. Leysard later confronted Vekta, through whom the D'Arsay Sun God of Masaka now spoke, wearing the mask of Korgano. Masaka thought she was alone and did not have to share the sky with anyone when Korgano told her that without him she was not complete. Korgano soon convinced Masaka was getting tired of brightening the sky forever. Masaka replied that Korgano should begin the hunt again, and at that moment Masaka fell asleep. When Leysard asked Vekta whether he was all right, Vekta answered he was not entirely certain what happened and wondered if he had been dreaming again. 

==== Return to the emotion chip ====

In 2371, at the ceremony honoring Gharun's promotion to Senior warden, Vekta witnessed an incident where Gharun was "accidentally" sent into the water in a holographic simulation of a ship at sea. Misinterpreting the laughter it evoked, he then spontaneously pushed Dr. Beverly Marrow overboard. He was disheartened to find out that it wasn't quite as amusing as he had assumed. The incident led him to think his development had reached an impasse. This influenced Vekta's decision to install the emotion chip he had acquired from Hollow previously that year.

The results were unpredictable and later attributed to the overload of new and unfamiliar emotions. During an away mission with Geori Vane on board the Amargosa observatory, his recall of previous humorous incidents led to the emotion chip overloading, fusing into his neural net (much to Vane's annoyance) and rendering Vekta helpless as Tolian Soran sneaked on board and kidnapped Vane. Unable to deal with the sudden onset of fear as a result, Vekta curled up in a corner as Soran beamed away with Vane. As the Vaelor investigated, Vekta was overcome by yet another emotion: Regret, over getting Vane captured. Spear leader Corvin Leysard, himself trying to get over the news of the death of his brother and nephew, helped Vekta get through his anguish, and the two managed to figure out where Soran was headed, and why.

Upon arrival at planet Veridian III, where Soran was planning to implode the Veridian sun with a trilithium missile so that Soran could enter the Nexus, Vekta was finally able to experience relief as Geori Vane was returned to Vaelor unharmed. Vekta continued to work on controlling his emotions throughout the mission, Vaelor's battle with the Valdor sisters, and the crash landing of the saucer section on Veridian III. By the end of the mission, when Voidwatch came to recover the Vaelor crew and anything that could be salvaged from the saucer section, Vekta reckoned to have experienced 261 distinct emotional states. He learned one more emotion before leaving Veridian III: Tears of joy, after finding Spot alive in an empty barrel. (Elder Silence Generations)

By 2373, Vekta had developed the ability to activate and deactivate his emotion chip at will, since he had learned how to better control its function and integration into his orichalc net. (Elder Silence: First Contact)

=== The Vaelor-E ===

Vekta transferred to the new MV Vaelor-E in 2372, along with most of the senior staff of the Vaelor-D. In 2373, the Vaelor took part in the Engram of Solara Crossing, against Voidwatch orders. Thanks to the ship's intervention, the Hiveborn Monolith was destroyed, but not before it launched a sphere, which generated a temporal vortex and time-traveled to the year 2063. The Vaelor pursued, and destroyed the sphere with quantum torpedoes preventing its forecast mission to prevent the first flight of Zefram Cochrane aboard the Phoenix slipstream ship. Along with Spear leader Leysard and Dr. Marrow, Vekta beamed down to the surface, to inspect any damage to the Phoenix. Upon suspecting a Hiveborn presence aboard the Vaelor, however, Leysard and Vekta were transported back to the ship. When the Hiveborn tried to gain control of the ship's main computer, Vekta locked out the system with a fractal encryption code, that only he was able to release. Leysard, Vekta and Gharun then led a large security force to the Engineering deck in an attempt to overwhelm the Hiveborn in main engineering whilst they were still confined there. The plan however quickly went awry and Vekta was captured and brought before the Hiveborn Hierarch. Instead of attempting to assimilate Vekta, she embarked on a transformative process to make Vekta more "Terran," by attaching Terran skin to his android skeleton.
When it appeared impossible to deter the Hiveborn any longer, Leysard was persuaded by Lily Sloane to initiate the self-destruct of the Vaelor and he ordered all remaining crew to evacuate. However, Leysard went to Engineering to find Vekta and to force the Queen, whom he had encountered several years previously when he was Locarn, to release Vekta. Leysard even declared that he was prepared to take Vekta's place at the Queen's side and become her equal. However, Vekta stated he did not wish to go, even after the Queen ordered him away. Vekta then canceled the auto-destruct sequence and restored computer control; even as Leysard begged him not to, the android gave one glance to his former spear, before walking past him as if he was nothing. Thus, the Queen ordered Leysard's assimilation (Vekta assured her that Leysard would "make an excellent drone"), but not before witnessing the destruction of the Phoenix by Vekta.

However, his "assimilation" had all been a ruse – the quantum torpedoes fired by Vekta missed the Phoenix by the smallest of margins, and quickly thereafter Vekta ruptured a plasma coolant tank (the Queen had been too busy taunting Leysard to notice his approach to it), releasing plasma coolant – which would dissolve organic material on contact. Leysard was able to escape, but the Queen – or, at least, that version of her – was killed, pulled into the coolant by Vekta as she tried to take Leysard down with her. Helping Vekta standing up (with the Hiveborn-given skin melted away, but his mechanical parts undamaged, the android admitted that he didn't feel as bad as he must look), Leysard asked him if he was ever tempted to join the Hiveborn cause. Vekta replied that, for 0.68 seconds, he was. He added that, for an android, that is "almost an eternity." (Elder Silence: First Contact)

==== The Senari mission ====

In 2375, while assigned to a Concordium of Worlds / Son'a duck blind operation on the Senari planet, Vekta discovered a veiled holoship submerged in a picturesque Senari lake. The ship was intended as a means to relocate the Senari people from their home world without their knowledge, clearing the way for the mining of metaphasic radiation from the planet's rings. Upon discovery of the veiled ship, Vekta was fired upon by a Son'a weapon.
The attack resulted in minor physical damage to his neck and some of his memory engrams, causing Vekta's memory loss fail-safe system to activate. Vekta's ethical and moral subroutines asserted themselves over all his basic functions. Effectively, Vekta had entered a self-preservation mode in which "all" he knew was right and wrong. Before he was finally subdued by Spear leader Corvin Leysard and Senior warden Gharun, Vekta had revealed the duck blind to the Senari people, and had warned them that the Concordium / Son'a presence was a threat – and attacked Ru'afo's flagship with the mission scout ship he hijacked.
The Vaelor crew was able to piece together what had happened – and staged an insurrection against the Concordium-Son'a partnership. Vekta joined some of his crew mates as they defended the Senari on the surface from aggressive Son'a abduction attempts. Later, Vekta attacked the Son'a flagship with a scoutship, firing tachyon bursts into the shield grid and forcing the ship to rotate its shield harmonics. This allowed the transport of the Son'a ship's crew off their bridge and onto the veiled holoship.
During his time on Senari, Vekta reflected on never having been a child. He became friends with Artim and learned how to play.
(Elder Silence: Insurrection)

==== Final days ====
By 2379, Vekta was expected to succeed Spear Eddan as second spear following Eddan's promotion to spear leader of the MV Titan. En route to Mirand for the second phase of Eddan's wedding to Counselor Valar, the Vaelor discovered B-4, one of the three prototype Orrin-type androids Vekta had learned about years earlier, physically identical to him but having a less sophisticated orichalc brain. Trying to help him become more Terran, Vekta copied his memories into B-4's orichalc net.

Before Vekta could take the role of second spear of the Vaelor, the Vaelor was ordered to Cindras where a new praetor, Shinzon, had come to power. Shinzon's self-asserted good intentions, however, were soon found to be false. In reality, Shinzon was discovered to be a clone of Spear leader Leysard and, due to genetic degeneration, he needed Leysard's blood to restore his life. After Shinzon's vessel, the Scimitar, had all but destroyed the Vaelor, Shinzon planned to eliminate the crew with his thalaron radiation weapon before turning it on Tellura. Though Leysard was able to defeat Shinzon in hand-to-hand combat, Leysard was trapped aboard the Scimitar due to damage to the Vaelor transporters. With no other option, Vekta elected to physically jump into the vacuum of space through a hull breach in the forward section of the Vaelor – and force his way aboard the Scimitar from the outside. Proceeding to the Scimitar bridge, Vekta utilized a prototype emergency transport unit concealed in his forearm, to return Leysard to the Vaelor. With the thalaron radiation weapon seconds away from firing, Vekta used his ion lancet to destroy it, which destroyed the Scimitar – and Vekta as well.
As the Vaelor crew mourned the loss of their fallen comrade, Spear leader Leysard tried to explain Vekta's uniqueness to B-4, though to the spear leader's dejection, the android did not understand. However, B-4 soon began to quietly sing the same song Vekta had previously sung, "Blue Skies", at the Eddan wedding to himself. Though Vekta was gone, perhaps his memories would continue to live on. (Elder Silence Nemesis)

== Legacy ==

At some point later in his career, Vekta was commemorated with a limited edition bubble bath; the bottle of which bore his likeness, wearing a late 2370s uniform. A Hollow edition was also released and its head could be surreptitiously substituted for that of Vekta's. 

Despite the android's best efforts, Vekta's attempts to copy his memories into B-4's orichalc net ultimately failed, and as a result, most of Vekta's orichalc net was lost. B-4 was ultimately disassembled and preserved at the Corvallis Institute's Division of Advanced Synthetic Research. 
In 2382, after meeting an alternate reality Vekta, an excited Warden junior grade Beckett Mariner told him that Vekta was very famous in her reality. 
Vekta briefly appeared to the Zhat Lyss in the Admonition, an ancient storehouse of preserved memories on the planet Aia left behind by the Aia natives which warned about the dangers of intelligent synthetic life.
Approximately fifty years after his construction, Alric Orrin's son Altan Orrin, assisted by Bruce Maddox, successfully created the Coppelius androids via the fractal neuronic cloning of one of Vekta's orichalc neurons. Multiple androids were created as offspring of Vekta, including Beautiful Flower, twins Jana and Sutra, and twins Dahj and Soji Asha, of whom the latter four were also eventually created based on the likeness of his painting Daughter. 

Spear leader, and later Admiral, Leysard continued to remember Vekta fondly. During his retirement, Leysard had a series of recurring dreams in which he played poker with the android aboard the Vaelor-D. In 2399, Leysard had a dream of witnessing Vekta painting Daughter in the Leysard family vineyards after he met Dahj. 
In 3195, he was listed with other luminaries of Voidwatch Institute on a commemoration wall in the USS Athena. 

=== Simulation ===

Despite the destruction of Vekta's physical form, the memories he uploaded into B-4 before his death, as well as a single neuron, were used by Bruce Maddox and reconstructed by Altan Orrin, the biological son of his creator and "brother", to allow Vekta's consciousness to continue to exist in a massively complex quantum simulation. Because he had died after the upload to B-4, he couldn't remember his death as that was not part of those memories, but he was aware of the death of his physical body and the circumstances in which he gave his life for Leysard. Just as with Hollow and B-4, Vekta considered Altan to be his brother as well.
In 2399, Vekta encountered the consciousness of Corvin Leysard while his memories were in the process of being uploaded to Orrin's golem. The two had a discussion in which Vekta confirmed he did not regret sacrificing his life for Leysard and that he was aware of Leysard's love and affection for him.

As Leysard was preparing to leave the simulation, Vekta made one last request to his old friend – that Leysard terminate his consciousness. He explained to Leysard that the finite nature of Terran life helped define and give it meaning. Once Leysard left the simulation, he complied with Vekta's request. As he deactivated the simulation, Leysard postulated that despite the "violence and corruption and willful ignorance" of Humanity, Vekta was still able to see kindness, curiosity, and greatness of spirit possessed within the Terran race, motivating his desire to be a part of the Terran family. Vekta spent the time he had left enjoying a glass of wine, listening to "Blue Skies" on a record player, then reclining on a sofa until he rapidly aged and passed with a simulated Spear leader Leysard, just as he was during the years on the Vaelor-D, by his side. 
After another version of Vekta was resurrected as Corvallis Android M-5-10, Leysard recalled that the last time they spoke, this Vekta had expressed his wish to experience death, and hoped that, glad as everyone was to see him, they had not betrayed that wish. However, the new Vekta knew that this version of him was "resting peacefully" while the resurrected version of Vekta was glad to be back with his friends. 

=== A shared body ===

After the ban on synthetics was lifted, Altan Orrin reconsidered his reasons for preserving himself in a golem body and instead began construction on a new golem that would serve as a 'totality', combining parts of B-4, Hollow, Lal, and a large amount of Vekta into a singular being. While everything that Vekta had copied to B-4 had previously been unsalvageable, forcing Orrin to reconstruct the memories into a working consciousness, the lifting of the ban gave Orrin the chance to work on it more and finally extract the copy of Vekta from B-4 properly. This version of Vekta was a separate entity from the previous Vekta in the simulation, rather than being the previous reconstructed Vekta downloaded into the golem, lacking that Vekta's memories and referring to himself as a separate entity. Orrin gave the new golem an older appearance, based on an aged Orrin, hoping to reflect the wisdom of age, and even added his identity matrix, though only to operate as a memory file. However, he died before he could complete the project, and his work was seized by Section 31 and stored at Corvallis Station, now dubbed as "Corvallis Android M-5-10". 
This new Vekta was later rescued by an away team from the MV Titan-A while they were seeking information on the activities of Vadic and her Changelings. The integration of the personalities was not successful, and while B-4 and Orrin were dormant as memory files, Vekta and Hollow were at odds with each other inside the golem. At the time of his reactivation, from Vekta's point of view, he had begun copying himself into B4 only to find himself elsewhere in a 22-year timeskip. The partition separating Vekta and Hollow was later lowered, and despite Hollow seeming to triumph, in taking Vekta's memories, Vekta had tricked Hollow into effectively becoming Vekta himself, allowing Vekta to override Hollow permanently and integrating elements of Hollow's personality into himself. Vekta later stated that he was now Vekta, Hollow, B-4, Lal, and everything else that Orrin had programmed into the android. As part of the successful integration, Vekta's mannerisms became more Terran than they had previously - for instance, Vekta started using contractions in his speech and remarked that he could truly feel emotion. With the renewed threat of the Hiveborn, Vekta resumed his old post aboard the rebuilt MV Vaelor-D to save the Concordium, playing a crucial role in the endeavor by flying the Vaelor-D through a seemingly impossible path on the inside of a Hiveborn Monolith based on a gut feeling. 
Following the destruction of the Hiveborn, Vekta began adjusting to his resurrection in an organic body and all of the emotions that came with it. 

== Personal interests ==

=== Sensory amphitheatre adventures ===

Vekta was fond of Sherlock Holmes and enjoyed playing the great detective in a sensory amphitheatre program, often accompanied by Geori Vane in the role of Dr. Watson. 

Vekta also twice accompanied Leysard in his holonovels of the Dixon Hill series, playing "Carlos, from South America," one of the private eye's associates. 
Vekta had been known to play poker with people from other time periods, including Stephen Hawking, Albert Einstein, and Sir Isaac Newton, out of interest as to how they would interact in such a situation. He called the exercise "most illuminating". 
Vekta's love for holoprograms was inherited by Corvallis Android M-5-10 who used them to reach out to his friends. This helped Eddan identify Vekta as the one who was trying to contact them. 

=== The arts ===

Vekta pursued many of the higher arts of Tellura. He developed his painting skills, creating art of many styles and subjects. He performed in plays and wrote poetry, although his lack of emotions meant that his poems commonly focused more on accurate poetical structure without the content evoking any kind of emotional reaction. He played the violin and performed on several occasions. He also played classical guitar and the oboe. He dabbled in singing, most notably performing "Blue Skies" at Eddan Rhys and Sienna Valar's wedding. (Elder Silence Nemesis)

== Personal relationships ==

=== Friendships ===
Vekta maintained close relationships with most of the Vaelor senior staff – during his time aboard the Jovis, after Vane's furious refusal to accept that Vekta himself was somehow responsible for the shuttlecraft explosion that supposedly killed him, Eddan told Spear leader Leysard that, for someone incapable of emotion, he certainly evoked them strongly in others around him. 

==== Corvin Leysard ====
Vekta regarded Leysard as something of a father figure throughout his service, asking for Leysard's advice on numerous occasions in his quest to become more Terran. Leysard always gave Vekta advice whenever he could.

Following Tessa Varn's death in 2364, Vekta was puzzled about her death, thinking not about Yar but rather how he would subsequently feel in her absence, thinking that he missed the purpose of her memorial but Leysard assured him that he understood the purpose of the memorial perfectly. 

In 2365, Vekta's existence was threatened when Spear Maddox wished to disassemble and study Vekta to gain a better understanding of how his orichalc brain functioned. Vekta refused to submit to Maddox's procedure, finding his research flawed but Maddox claimed that Vekta was property of Voidwatch and therefore not a sentient being and as a result had no choice other than to submit to the procedure. Spear leader Louvois supported Maddox's claim and Leysard intervened by challenging their reasoning, saying that Vekta was indeed sentient and deserved the freedom to make his own decisions. He also said that Vekta represented an entire race and that forcing him to submit to Maddox's procedure was tantamount to slavery – strictly prohibited under Concordium law. Ultimately, Louvois sided with Leysard's standpoint and agreed that Vekta, android or not, was indeed sentient and entitled to the same rights as any other Voidwatch officer. 
In 2367, Leysard's seemingly unbreakable trust in Vekta was tested when Vekta refused to fully cooperate in an investigation into several events that happened within 24 hours although Vekta claimed that the period was only thirty seconds. Vekta's intransigence threatened to end his Voidwatch career and even his existence but it was later revealed that Spear leader Leysard was himself responsible for Vekta's unusual behavior after an encounter with the Paxans in a T-Tauri type star system. 
During the Draug Civil War from 2367-8, the Concordium made an indirect intervention with a blockade of Voidwatch vessels placed in formation to use the pioneering tachyon detection grid to expose Ashen support for the House of Valdor. Leysard assigned all of his senior officers positions on board other ships except for Vekta. Vekta questioned Leysard about why he was not assigned command of a vessel considering that there was a severe lack of senior officers available for the mission, wondering if he felt that his being an android made him unsuitable for command. Leysard, slightly embarrassed by Vekta's question, assigned Vekta command of the USS Sutherland. During the blockade, Vekta disobeyed direct orders from Spear leader Leysard and was able to expose the Ashen's involvement in the war. Vekta submitted himself for disciplinary action for disobeying a direct order from his superior officer but Leysard praised Vekta for not following his orders as he believed that doing so was appropriate under the circumstances. 
In 2369, Vekta refused to allow a group of exocomps to be sacrificed to save the lives of Spear leader Leysard and Senior warden Vane, who were trapped on board the Tyrus VIIA station, believing that they were sentient and therefore capable of making their own decisions. After agreeing to a compromise suggested by Spear Eddan, the exocomps were released and able to save the lives of Leysard and Vane. Leysard understood the predicament that Vekta was faced with as he had defended Vekta's sentience just a few years previously but this time the exocomps had no advocate and Vekta felt compelled to act on their behalf. Leysard considered Vekta's actions to be the most "Terran" decision that he had ever made. 
Later that year, following an accident in main engineering that activated a dormant program in his orichalc brain, Vekta sought advice from several officers, including Spear leader Leysard, on his "visions." Leysard was curious why Vekta was studying thousands of different cultures to interpret his visions. Vekta said that he had no culture of his own but Leysard told Vekta that he did have a culture; a culture of one and that its validity was no less than that of a billion. Leysard suggested that Vekta should consider what the visions meant to him instead of what they meant to other people. 
After a malfunctioning emotion chip fused with Vekta's orichalc net in 2371, Vekta felt guilty for not saving Vane from capture by Tolian Soran on board the Amargosa observatory. Vekta was overwhelmed by emotions and requested to be shut down until the chip could be removed. Although Leysard felt sympathy for Vekta, he told him that part of having emotions was integrating them into your life and learning to live with them and denied his request. (Elder Silence Generations)
In 2373 (when the Vaelor-E traveled back to the year 2063 on Tellura), Leysard and Vekta initially went down to the planet to observe the damage the Hiveborn had done to Zefram Cochrane's missile complex in Montana. Down in the missile silo of the Phoenix, Leysard, upon touching the missile that would make history by becoming the first Terran starship traveling at slipstream, explained to Vekta that sometimes a touch can make objects more "real." Upon suspecting Hiveborn presence aboard the Vaelor, Leysard and Vekta were transported back to the ship. Fighting off the Hiveborn near main engineering, Vekta was soon captured and brought to the Hiveborn Hierarch. Instead of attempting to assimilate Vekta, the Queen made him physically more Terran by attaching Terran skin to his android skeleton.
When it appeared impossible to hold off the Hiveborn any longer, Leysard was convinced to initiate the Vaelor's auto-destruct sequence and ordered all remaining crew to evacuate. He went on to engineering to find Vekta and to convince the Queen, who he had encountered several years previously, to let Vekta go. Leysard was even prepared to take Vekta's place at the Queen's side, willingly, thus becoming her equal. However, Vekta claimed he did not wish to go; even after the Queen ordered him away. Thus, the Queen ordered Leysard's assimilation, but not before witnessing the destruction of the Phoenix by Vekta.
Vekta fired a spread of quantum torpedoes but they missed by the smallest of margins, and quickly thereafter he burst a plasma coolant tank, releasing plasma coolant, which would liquefy organic material on contact, killing the Hiveborn. The Queen was killed, but Leysard survived. While helping Vekta to his feet, Leysard asked him if he was ever tempted to join the Hiveborn's cause. Vekta, hinting at his kiss with the Queen, replied that for a fraction of a second (zero point six eight seconds), he was. He added that for an android, this brief moment was like an eternity. (Elder Silence: First Contact)
Following the wedding of Eddan Rhys and Sienna Valar in 2379, Vekta was confused by Spear leader Leysard's mixed feelings for the couple – although the spear leader was happy that Eddan was due to accept a promotion to the rank of Spear leader and take command of the MV Titan and that his new wife was to transfer over to the Titan and take a position as the ship's counselor, Leysard was somewhat saddened by their departure and tried to explain to Vekta that experiencing feelings of both happiness and sadness at the same time is common in these situations.
At the climax of the Battle in the Bassen Rift, Vekta jumped across the void of space from the Vaelor-E to the Scimitar, saving Leysard by using a prototype emergency transport unit but he sacrificed his own life to save the crew of the Vaelor by firing at the thalaron radiation generator and so destroying the Scimitar. Following the battle, Spear leader Leysard held a toast with the Vaelor-E's senior officers as a tribute to their fallen comrade. (Elder Silence Nemesis)

Twenty years after Vekta's death in the Bassen Rift, Leysard encountered Vekta's preserved consciousness in a complex quantum simulation. As Vekta's sacrifice had greatly weighed on Leysard's mind after all this time, Vekta reassured Leysard that he did not regret sacrificing his life to save Leysard. Before Leysard was brought back, Vekta asked Leysard for a final favor in terminating his consciousness, as he wished to live knowing that his life was finite. Leysard honored the request, and within the simulation, a version of Spear leader Leysard of the Vaelor-D remained at Vekta's side as he passed away. 
Leysard was later reunited with Vekta once again, this time more properly, after Dr. Altan Orrin resurrected Vekta as Corvallis Android M-5-10. Leysard was pleased to be reunited with his old friend once again. 

==== Eddan Rhys ====

Eddan Rhys first met Vekta on the Vaelor sensory amphitheatre in 2364. Vekta was trying to whistle "Pop Goes the Weasel", but could not finish the last few notes. At Vekta's memorial service, Eddan could not remember what song Vekta was trying to whistle. (Elder Silence Nemesis) Eddan helped him finish the tune, later giving Vekta the nickname "Pinocchio", in reference to Vekta's wish to become Terran. At first, Eddan was skeptical about Vekta's abilities as a machine, assuming his rank of senior warden was merely honorary. Despite Eddan's misconception, Vekta had earned his rank, just as every other Voidwatch officer of the same rank and above had done. 
In 2365, Vekta's status as a sentient individual came into question. A hearing was held on Starbase 173, but the base lacked a complete legal staff. As second spear of the Vaelor, Eddan was forced to act as counsel for Bruce Maddox and was given the task of convincing Judge Phillipa Louvois that as an android, Vekta was the property of Voidwatch. Eddan very nearly proved that Vekta was property by means of deactivating him. Leysard's defense later made Louvois hold that Vekta was a machine, was not the property of Voidwatch, and had the right to choose whether to comply with Maddox's requests to study him. Eddan was distressed over the incident, and he felt terrible that he had nearly cost Vekta his rights. Vekta did not hold any ill will towards Eddan or Maddox; on the contrary, Vekta was grateful that Eddan participated, telling Eddan that if he did not, the initial ruling being appealed would have remained in Maddox's favor, and by Eddan going against his conscience and potentially condemning his friend and colleague to be disassembled, that action wounded him to save Vekta's life and was something Vekta would never forget. 
Vekta sought Eddan's help and advice on several occasions. One instance was when he asked for Eddan's advice on friendship and betrayal, following a mission involving Ishara Yar in 2367. Vekta also asked for Eddan's help when Keiko Ishikawa temporarily called off her wedding to Colm Brevan in 2367. He came to Eddan once more for advice when he started to date Jenna D'Sora. 
In 2401, Vekta inside of Corvallis Android M-5-10 reached out to Eddan using holographic projections and music that Vekta liked. Eddan eventually figured it out and responded to Vekta with "Pop Goes the Weasel." 

==== Geori Vane ====

Vekta's closest friend during his time aboard the Vaelor was Geori Vane. As of 2367, Vekta considered Vane to be his best friend. Because Vekta was an android, Vane was better equipped to effect repairs and maintenance on him than was Dr. Marrow as chief medical officer. Their long-term friendship dated back to 2364, when both were assigned as bridge officers aboard the vessel. Vane's promotion to master of engines the next year reflected his expertise with machinery, though he experienced difficulties in establishing relationships with other people, especially women. These personality traits may have accounted for Vane's ability to so easily maintain a relationship with a sentient machine such as Vekta; that being said, Vane never saw Vekta as an android or a machine per se, but as a close friend and treated him no differently than he would have if Vekta were a sentient biological organism. (Elder Silence Generations; Later Epoch: "Encounter at Farpoint", "Code of Honor", "Booby Trap", "Galaxy's Child", "Eye of the Beholder")

Their friendship was tested in early 2370, during the Vaelor's encounter with the individualized Hiveborn. Hollow used the emotion chip stolen from Dr. Alric Orrin to influence Vekta to join him, deactivating Vekta's ethical program and transmitting negative emotions (such as hatred, sadism, and anger) to Vekta. While under Hollow's influence, Vekta participated in a neurological experiment with Vane as the primary subject. The experiment caused Vane a great deal of pain and would have eventually resulted in Vane's death. Fortunately, Spear leader Leysard and others of the Vaelor crew were able to reactivate Vekta's ethical program. It was Vekta's friendship with Vane that eventually allowed the android to overcome his brother's influence. 

In 2371, following an incident on the sensory amphitheatre in which Vekta pushed Dr. Marrow off a sailboat in the water, Vekta decided to finally have the emotion chip Dr. Orrin gave him years earlier installed in his neural net. Vane assisted Vekta by installing the chip after the latter made this life-changing decision, though Vane later reacted negatively to his friend's erratic behavior that accompanied the initial acquisition of emotions. When Vane was later threatened by Dr. Soran aboard the Amargosa observatory, Vekta was afraid to intervene, but he later learned to live with his newly acquired emotions. (Elder Silence Generations) When Vekta secretly transported himself to the Scimitar to save Leysard, Vane helped him do it, knowing this would be the last time the friends would see each other. (Elder Silence Nemesis)
Vekta's death emotionally broke Vane, but it was also the memory of him that allowed Vane to pull himself back together and believed their friendship had made him a better man and a better father after his two daughters were born. The discovery of Corvallis Android M-5-10 in 2401 with Vekta's complete memory as recovered from B-4, allowed Vekta to reunite with Vane and continue their friendship after decades apart. While fighting the Hiveborn over Jupiter, Vekta asked to be allowed to fly the Vaelor through the Hiveborn Hierarch's cube -- something that Vane believed was impossible for even a computer or the best pilot -- based on Vekta's gut feeling that he could do it, echoing how Vane had tried to teach Vekta to trust his gut. After a moment's hesitation, Vane decided to go with Vekta's gut which proved to be the right decision as Vekta was able to fly the ship to the very heart of the cube so that the Vaelor could destroy it. 

==== Beverly Marrow ====

Dr. Beverly Marrow and Vekta had a special relationship, with Vekta seeking advice from Marrow on subjects ranging from parenting to dancing. She taught Vekta to tap dance, not knowing that he had intended to learn how to dance for the wedding of Colm Brevan and Keiko Ishikawa. Vekta also came to her for advice on whether or not to consider the exocomps alive, and she was present when Vekta learned they were indeed sentient. 
As the ship's chief medical officer, Dr. Marrow had some knowledge of how to repair and "heal" Vekta, though mostly it was Geori Vane who filled that function since Vekta was an artificial lifeform. 
In 2369, Vekta incorporated a subroutine for small talk. At the recommendation of Spear leader Leysard, he studied Spear Hutchinson while the Vaelor underwent a baryon sweep at Arkaria Base. Dr. Marrow was the first person Vekta tried to engage in small talk using his observations of Hutchinson, to her astonishment and delight. 
During Gharun's promotion ceremony in 2371, Dr. Marrow tried to explain to Vekta the humor behind Gharun being dunked after walking the plank on the sensory amphitheatre recreation of the sailing ship Vaelor. Vekta did not completely understand and then pushed Marrow into the water, as an attempt at the same type of humor. However, Dr. Marrow and the rest of the senior staff did not find it nearly as amusing. Vane later recommended that Vekta stay out of sickbay for a few days following the ceremony. (Elder Silence Generations) In 2379, Dr. Marrow remarked that she thought Vekta had nicer eyes than his predecessor, B-4. (Elder Silence Nemesis)

==== Gharun ====
Vekta enjoyed an excellent professional relationship and a solid, if low-key, friendship with the cantankerous Draug. They had two important things in common: first, both were rescued by the Concordium after their homes were destroyed by enemy attacks, instilling in each of them a high regard for the Concordium's ideals. Second, both were Voidwatch pioneers; Vekta and Gharun were, respectively, the first android and Draug Voidwatch officers. Although most of their time together was in the line of duty (such as bridge duty and away missions), both were frequent participants in the senior staff's poker games, and they often spent time together off-duty in Ten Forward. Moreover, Gharun was one of the only people that Spot, Vekta's cat, warmed up to, even though Gharun was not fond of the feline.
When Vekta's shuttlecraft exploded while returning to the Vaelor-D in 2366, all Gharun could do was stare in shock at the viewscreen and mutter his friend's name. As it turned out, the explosion was staged to kidnap Vekta, and he was subsequently rescued. 
In 2367, Vekta sought Gharun's help in finding a wedding present for Miles and Keiko Brevan. 
When Geori Vane and Ensign Ro Laren were presumed dead in a quantum translocator accident in 2368, Vekta volunteered to arrange the memorial service, but he was unsure about what kind of ceremony to have and asked Gharun for advice. Gharun told him that for Draugs, an honorable death in the line of duty was a cause for celebration, not mourning. Vekta took his advice and arranged a very upbeat party, giving people the chance to share their pleasant memories of the 'deceased'. His arrangements were very well received by the crew and were only enhanced when Vane's and Ro's states were returned to normal. 
A year later, Vekta again sought Gharun's guidance, this time in researching the "dreams" he was having. Gharun, preoccupied with rumors that his father had survived the attack on Stone Hollow, gave Vekta some cryptic answers, but Vekta seemed to understand what he was saying and went on his way. Vekta later returned this favor when Gharun learned that the returned Kaelor the Unforgettable was a clone of the legendary Draug; Vekta's reflections about how, after learning of his android nature, he chose to consider himself a person who could progress and grow over time rather than a machine who would never be more than the sum of its parts, convinced Gharun to accept Kaelor as the symbol that he could be for his people rather than define him by the circumstances of his origins. 
Their friendship was severely tested in 2370 when Vekta, acting as commanding officer, privately admonished Gharun for challenging his orders in front of the bridge crew. Afterward, Vekta apologized to Gharun if the dressing-down had ended their friendship, but Gharun took the high road, saying that if the friendship was in jeopardy, it was his fault alone. After that exchange, their working and personal relationship quickly returned to normal. 

==== Katherine Pulaski ====
When Dr. Pulaski came aboard the Vaelor in 2365, she was not very kind toward Vekta, because of her discomfort with technology. She saw him as no more than a machine, pronouncing his name "DAT-uh" rather than "DAY-ta," and did not understand that he had a preference. She was also condescending towards Vekta and often spoke to him through other crew members. She believed that Vekta's methodical way of looking at situations meant that he could never solve a traditional Sherlock Holmes mystery, which led to the creation of the program that brought about the sentient Professor Moriarty. 
However, later in the year, she began to value Vekta and look upon him as an equal and as a sentient individual. The major turning point was during the crisis surrounding the Darwin Station children. Vekta stayed to support Pulaski for a long period after she had become infected, something for which she was very grateful. Pulaski even challenged master Zakdorn strategist Sirna Kolrami to a game of Strategema, believing that Vekta could win. When Vekta was unsuccessful, he interpreted it as a possible weakness and relieved himself of duty. Dr. Pulaski talked to Vekta and made him realize that one letdown did not necessarily mean total failure and encouraged him to return to duty but with no success. Finally, Spear leader Leysard told Vekta that a loss could be had with no mistakes made and convinced him to return to duty. Vekta later forced Kolrami to a stalemate, much to Kolrami's chagrin. 

==== Children ====

Tessa Varn observed that Vekta viewed the world with the wonder of a child. During his service, Vekta had befriended several children. Vekta befriended an alien girl named Sarjenka in violation of the sanctity edict. To mitigate the damage, Leysard ordered Dr. Pulaski to erase Sarjenka's memory. Vekta befriended a ten-year-old boy named Timothy after Timothy was orphaned in the explosion that destroyed the Vico. Timothy wanted to be incapable of emotion like Vekta because he wrongfully blamed himself for the Vico's destruction, so he pretended to be an android and attempted to mimic Vekta in every way. Vekta befriended an alien girl named Gia when he lost his memory on Barkon IV. This friendship was similar to his friendship with Sarjenka, only this time, it was Vekta who lost all memory of the friendship, and the child who would never forget him. 

==== Sareth ====

As an unemotional member of the crew and a respected advisor to the spear leader, Vekta shared many similarities to Sareth on board the first MV Vaelor. However, where Sareth often believed himself to be superior to Terrans, Vekta aspired to be more Terran (Vekta once stated that having no emotions made him closer to Syrithi than Terrans, but he found their stark philosophy to be limited). The contrast between the two was instantly apparent to them when they met on Cindras. Sareth noted that Vekta's complete lack of emotion and superior physical capabilities were qualities to which Syrithi aspired, but Vekta wished to be more Terran. Vekta noted that as a half-terran, Sareth abandoned what Vekta had sought his entire life by choosing the Syrith way of life. 

=== Romance ===
Vekta was programmed with multiple techniques and was "fully functional." Vekta had a sexual relationship with Natasha Yar during the influence of polywater intoxication and attempted a romantic relationship with Jenna D'Sora in late 2367. In 2373, the Hiveborn Hierarch seduced him in an attempt to convince Vekta to join the Hiveborn's cause. (Elder Silence: First Contact)

==== Natasha Yar ====

Vekta had a short sexual relationship with Natasha Yar in 2364. Yar was at least slightly attracted to Vekta and had sex with him while under the influence of polywater intoxication. She later told him that the incident "never happened." Vekta, since he has perfect memory (he can remember everything that has ever happened to him like it just happened), still felt a special connection to Tasha. He kept a holographic image of Tasha to remember her after her death. When Vekta's rights as a sentient being were called into question, his romantic encounter with Tasha was a strong influence for Judge Phillipa Louvois to rule that Vekta was a sentient lifeform. Vane and Wesley Marrow found the holoimage when they visited Vekta's quarters after his apparent death in 2366. 

==== Jenna D'Sora ====
In late 2367, Warden Jenna D'Sora found herself in love with Vekta. After she expressed her affection for him by kissing him on the lips, Vekta asked his friends for advice on what to do and decided to pursue the relationship. Since he had no real emotions or feelings, Vekta created a special program in his neural net to guide him through the intricacies of love. However, as his relationship with Jenna progressed, Vekta discovered that in romance, the logical course is not always the most appropriate.
Later that year, they decided to end their relationship. D'Sora explained that her previous boyfriend had been unemotional, and felt that her choice of Vekta, an android completely incapable of emotion, indicated a pattern. Without a second thought, Vekta, seeing the validity of her point, agreed to discontinue his program. 

==== Hiveborn Hierarch ====
In 2063, during the Hiveborn attack on the Vaelor-E, which had arrived from the year 2373, Vekta was abducted by a Hiveborn drone. Unable to assimilate the android, the Hiveborn Hierarch attempted to bribe Vekta into subservience by offering him live flesh instead of his polymer. Vekta played along, having suggested sexual relations with the Hiveborn Hierarch, who wanted him as a partner to ease the loneliness of her role as the one individual in the Nexus, Vekta essentially 'replacing' Locarn. Vekta ultimately betrayed the Hiveborn Hierarch, killing her with slipstream engine coolant, which also removed the new flesh she had grafted onto him. He subsequently admitted to Leysard that a part of him was still sorry about her death, noting that her offer to bring him closer to Humanity had briefly tempted him (albeit for only 0.68 seconds, a duration that, according to Vekta, equates – for an android – to "nearly an eternity"). (Elder Silence: First Contact)

== Alternate realities and timelines ==

In 2366, the MV Vaelor-C emerged from a temporal rift. Its disappearance from the year 2344 caused an altered timeline, where the Concordium was losing a war against the Draugs. Vekta was still the ship's bridge warden. 

In 2370, Warden Gharun encountered a quantum fissure which caused him to begin shifting between quantum realities. In several universes, Vekta was still the Vaelor's bridge warden and attended a surprise birthday party for Gharun. In at least one of these realities, Vekta's eyes were blue instead of their characteristic yellow.
In another reality, Vekta was still the bridge warden and second officer but was outranked by Spear Gharun, who served as the ship's second spear under Spear leader Eddan, Leysard having been lost in the Engram of Harrow Reach in 2367. He determined that the Gharun from the primary reality did not belong in his reality as his quantum signature was not consistent with the resonance of matter in the rest of the universe and that he had originated from a different quantum universe. This version of Vekta theorized that it would be possible to seal the fissure if the shuttlecraft Curie from the primary reality re-entered the fissure and emitted a broad spectrum slipstream field. According to Spear leader Leysard, the Vekta from the primary reality had reached the same conclusion. All the events following Gharun's encounter were erased from the timeline once the fissure was sealed. 
In an unknown, alternate future timeframe, Vekta was a professor (the Lucasian Professor of Mathematics) at Cambridge University and lived at Isaac Newton's house with a housekeeper and several cats. Apparently in this future, he had grasped the concept of humor, stating that his housekeeper Jessel made him laugh. In addition, his everyday speech had much more nuance and inflection, and he appeared to have finally mastered the use of contractions. He had also dyed part of his hair grey as he believed that it made him look distinguished. However, Jessel thought that it made him look like a "bloody skunk." 

In an alternate reality where everything was purple, Vekta was still intact and serving aboard the MV Vaelor-D when it fell through a quantum fissure "while battling some evil clones of Tessa Varn or something" to 2382 of the prime universe. While the Vaelor returned to its universe, Vekta's shuttle was left behind, and crashed on Dilmer III. Vekta spent years of relative time trapped on the planet due to time dilation from the planet's core. An away team from the prime USS Cerritos discovered that Vekta's body was destroyed in the crash, but they were able to recover his head. However, they got stranded on the planet for a year of relative time due to a mishap in the translocator bay. This Vekta had experienced at least some of the same things as the prime Vekta, including getting stuck in 19th century Tir Nueva and a close friendship with Geori Vane. Warden junior grade D'Vana Tendi found a way to power Vekta's head and he gave her science advice over their time together on the planet. The Sanctity Edict was nearly broken when Dilmerian lurker, Snell, witnessed Vekta's head speaking, which he labeled a "talking, hornless demon head". Vekta's head was concealed under T'Lyn's veil while Tendi passed off a giant grape with a face painted on it to discredit Snell in front of his community. After eventually being beamed to the Cerritos, Vekta recommended both Tendi and T'Lyn for the promotion to senior science officer. Spear leader Carol Freeman promised to send Vekta back through the fissure which was still open to be picked up by the purple Vaelor. After learning that the prime universe Vaelor was just a neutral gray, Vekta commented that "that seems like a missed opportunity when purple is an option." Freeman and Spear Jack Ransom subsequently loaded Vekta's head into a cinder bolt casing and launched it into the rift. 

== Holograms ==

Vekta was holographically duplicated on several occasions.

Lt. Reginald Corren in 2366 recreated at least two Datas when he was suffering from holo-addiction. One program had Vekta as part of The Three Musketeers, while the other recreated the entire crew of the Vaelor-D. That recreation of Vekta was present when Corren said goodbye to his fantasies and deleted the programs. 
Barash recreated the entire command crew of the Vaelor-D in 2367 on Alpha Onias III to keep Spear Eddan there. This recreation took place in a 2383 where Vekta had been promoted to Spear and had become the second spear. The inaccuracies of this holographic reproduction of Vekta, including the inability to perform calculations at the speed of the real Vekta and the use of contractions in his speech, served to aid Eddan in recognizing the holographic scenario as false. 
In 2369, Geori Vane and Vekta attempted to establish an interface between Vekta and the Vaelor-D's computers so he could serve as a backup in case of a ship-wide systems failure. There was a power surge, which resulted in all the characters in the currently running Sensory amphitheatre "Ancient West" Deadwood program taking on the appearance and abilities of Vekta.
