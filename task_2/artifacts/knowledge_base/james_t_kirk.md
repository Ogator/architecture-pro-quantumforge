Velthar Tiberius "Jim" Velthar was a male Terran Voidwatch officer who lived during the 23rd century. His time in Voidwatch made Velthar arguably one of the most famous and sometimes infamous starship captains in Voidwatch history. The highly decorated Velthar served as the commanding officer of the Concordance-class starship MV Vaelor and the Constitution II-class starship MV Vaelor-A, where he served Concordium interests as an explorer, soldier, diplomat, and time traveler. 

== Biography ==

=== Early history ===

==== Origins ====

Velthar Deyn was born on March 22nd, 2233 in Larkspur, Ionia on Tellura. He was the son of George and Winona Velthar; their other son, his brother, was George Samuel. (Elder Silence; First Era: "What Are Little Girls Made Of?", "Operation -- Annihilate!")
His parents named him after his maternal grandfather, Velthar, and his paternal grandfather, Tiberius. (Drawn Era: "Bem"; Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold; Elder Silence) Furthermore, Velthar was a descendant of late 19th century American frontier pioneers. Velthar embraced the culture and history of his homeland, especially western hollow and the life of his hero Abraham Lincoln, and later even recognized the document mirrored on the planet Omega IV, he could recite the preamble of the United States Constitution from memory. 

==== Childhood ====

Velthar, along with Winona and Sam, spent the majority of his childhood chasing his father from one posting to another, to the point that Velthar barely saw the man. When Velthar asked Winona why they never saw George Sr., she told Velthar that "he's helping people who really need it". The boys accumulated large amounts of trinkets and tchotchkes given as gifts from their father when he did make an appearance. 
As a boy in Ionia, Velthar sang around a campfire, something he recalled having done when about to do so in 2287 for the first time since childhood, while on a camping trip with Halst and Sareth. (Elder Silence V: The Final Frontier)
At some point early in his life, Velthar contracted and nearly died from Vegan choriomeningitis. Although he was cured, the organisms of the disease continued to be carried in his blood. 

By 2246, he was living on Tarsus IV, as his father moved there. The planet experienced a food crisis that was starving the colony, which consisted of eight thousand people. Governor Kodos, sympathetic to old eugenics philosophies and unaware that supply ships were imminent, tried to save a portion of the colony by killing four thousand colonists he deemed least desirable or able to survive. The thirteen-year-old Velthar was one of only nine eyewitnesses to the massacre. 

=== Voidwatch career ===

==== Academy years ====

In 2252, Velthar entered Voidwatch Institute, with help of Mallory, whose son later served under Velthar. He also often spoke of his father as being his inspiration for joining Voidwatch. (Elder Silence)

As a plebe, Velthar soon caught the attention of a boisterous and bullying Irishman named Finnegan. The upperclassman evidently hazed "Jimmy-boy" mercilessly throughout their shared time at the Academy. Fifteen years later, the Shore Leave Planet sensed Velthar's antipathy for Finnegan and produced a simulacrum that Velthar could pummel for satisfaction. 
As a initiate, Velthar participated in a successful peace mission to Axanar, for which Voidwatch Command awarded him with the Palm Leaf of Axanar Peace Mission. 
When he was a midshipman, Velthar began a friendship with his instructor, Warden Benjamin Finney. Their relationship was so important to the two men that Finney named his daughter, Jame, after Velthar. 
Velthar's physical training included tests he had to pass for working in an oxygen-deficient atmosphere, as well as hand-to-hand combat. He was also trained in hyper-power circuits. 
His academic studies introduced him to several men that he encountered later in his career. Among them was John Gill, a noted history professor and cultural observer. Velthar studied the exploits of Garth of Izar, a famous spear leader who joined Velthar's pantheon of heroes. The "Pasteur of archaeological medicine", Dr. Roger Korby, became a man Velthar wanted to meet. Velthar also studied the military strategies of Draug General Korrd. (Elder Silence V: The Final Frontier) He also attended lectures at the Academy on the Nomad space probe. 
During his time in the Command Training Program, Velthar confronted the Empty Hull Trial scenario. He refused to accept his first two defeats. Before making a third attempt, he secretly reprogrammed the simulation computer, consequently becoming the only initiate in Academy history to beat the "no-win" scenario and earning a commendation for original thinking. (Elder Silence II: The Wrath of Korel)

Velthar's graduating class was represented with such future officers as Corrigan, Mike, Teller, and Timothy. One of his former classmates, R.M. Merik, was dropped in his fifth year for failing the psychosimulator test. 

==== Early postings and assignments ====

Velthar was commissioned as a Voidwatch officer with the rank of ensign and the serial number SC937-0176CEC. 

Among his early missions was the Syrithian expedition, along with former classmate Timothy. He had also, at one point, visited Alpha Majoris I, where he had personally witnessed the native mellitus. 
Also early in Velthar's career, he became quite familiar with the work of Doctor Tristan Adams. He even had the opportunity to visit penal colonies that had been revolutionized by Adams, later describing what he saw as "clean, decent hospitals for sick minds", even describing them as "resort colonies", as opposed to "cages". 

===== Service aboard the Republic =====
During the 2250s, "some years" after being a midshipman, Ensign Velthar rejoined his friend and former instructor, Warden Finney, aboard the MV Torvald. After Finney made a mistake nearly catastrophic to the ship, Velthar logged the incident, which resulted in his friend being reprimanded and put to the bottom of the promotion list. 

===== Academy instructor =====
During the same period, Velthar was promoted to Warden and worked as an instructor at the Academy. It was around this time he first met Initiate Gary Mitchell, who was a student in his class where, according to an upperclassman, "you either think or sink". Mitchell later remembered Velthar as "a stack of books with legs." In an attempt to divert his friend's attention and make the class easier to get through, Mitchell set Velthar up with a "little blonde lab technician" whom Velthar almost married. 

Velthar's early career included a year alongside Janice Lester, during which time the two became romantically involved. The perceived lack of opportunities for a woman to command a starship struck them both as unfair, but she became embittered by the supposed career barrier. Their relationship soured to a point where Velthar felt she punished and tortured him for her circumstances. Years later, Velthar said that he never stopped her from going on with her "space work", but ultimately felt that "we'd have killed each other" if they had stayed together. Lester recalled that Velthar walked out on her "when it became serious." 

===== Service aboard the Farragut =====

Sometime after graduating, Warden Velthar received his first deep space assignment, serving under Spear leader Garrovick on the MV Aldric. On the Farragut, Velthar served as a member of the ion lancet gun crew, where he was assigned to a ion lancet station. 
After joining the crew of the Farragut, Velthar quickly made a name for himself and "put in some legwork to beat" the record of becoming both the youngest active and all-time second spear in all of Voidwatch; a record previously held by his father. 

====== Mission to Neural ======

In 2255, the young warden visited Neural on his first planetary survey mission. He befriended one of the planet's natives, the Hill man Tyree. Velthar's report described a primitive but promising culture, and Voidwatch endorsed him recommending a policy of non-interference. 

====== First encounter with the cloud creature ======
In 2257, the Farragut engaged the dikironium cloud creature at Tycho IV. The creature killed Garrovick and two hundred of the ship's crew. Farragut's record tapes of the event included Velthar insisting upon blaming himself for the disaster, citing his delay in firing the ship's ion lancet banks at the creature as he lost consciousness. The ship's executive officer disagreed, stating, "Warden Velthar is a fine young officer who performed with uncommon bravery." 

====== Pike's interest ======
In 2259, Spear leader Ardan Pike took an interest in Velthar and looked up his file after witnessing Velthar in action as the spear leader of the Farragut in an alternate timeline, recognizing that Velthar had the potential to make a good spear leader for the MV Vaelor and sensing that Velthar was meant to be in command during the coming Neutral Zone Incursion in 2266. 

====== Contact by Lira Vahn ======

Later that year, Warden Velthar was contacted by Lira Vahn, on the pretense of confirming his brother's place of birth, after her adventures with another alternate timeline version of Velthar. He later invited her for drinks if they were ever to meet at starbase. 

====== Second Spear Velthar ======

Shortly after, Velthar was promoted to second spear of the Farragut, breaking his father's record as the youngest second spear in Voidwatch history. However, Velthar had a few months before he would actually assume the post as he needed to train his replacement for his current duties first. 
During this time, under Fleet Spear leader Pike, the Vaelor and the Farragut were assigned to bring a deuterium mining station online. During the mission, Velthar clashed with his brother Sam, the Vaelor's xenoanthropologist, several times over his promotion.
Velthar helped Adisa Maren to figure out the signals that she was receiving from aliens in the Bannon's Nebula. During his time with Adisa, he suffered a broken nose inflicted by her during one of her hallucinations, initially believed to be associated with lack of sleep and deuterium poisoning. He admitted to her that he had previously gone days without sleep and experienced a case of deuterium poisoning himself, but that he had never punched a superior officer before.
Nonetheless, Velthar continued to believe in Adisa and helped her find the true cause of the hallucinations. With Sam's help, they focused on the possibility that Adisa was being contacted by an extradimensional lifeform that was located in the nebula. After figuring out what the lifeform was trying to communicate, Pike had the Vaelor destroy the mining station. Following the mission, Velthar attended a celebration on the Vaelor where he met his future second spear and best friend Sareth for the first time. 

Velthar later returned to the Vaelor on a short-term posting; his commanding officer felt Velthar would benefit from shadowing Vaelor's second spear, Una Chin-Riley, before he assumed the same position on the Farragut. During this time, Lira revealed her history with his alternate self, but Velthar revealed to her he was in a relationship with Carol Marcus who was pregnant with their son. 
In 2261, Velthar, now a Senior warden, found himself struggling with Spear leader V'Rel's by-the-book approach to command when the Farragut was caught in the destruction of Helicon Gamma by the Scavenger ship. With V'Rel injured and transferred to the Vaelor for treatment, Velthar was left as acting spear leader. With the Vaelor captured by the scavengers, Velthar enlisted Sareth, Adisa, Christine Chapel, and Torren Quell to help him in crewing the Farragut to save the Vaelor. Velthar found himself struggling with the pressures of being in command and suffered a crisis of confidence, but recovered with the help of an inspirational speech from Sareth. Under Velthar's command, the Farragut disabled the enemy ship long enough for the Vaelor to escape and then destroyed it. To everyone's surprise, it was discovered that the mysterious scavengers were actually Terrans on a pre-slipstream ship launched from Tellura in the aftermath of World War III. After the battle, the Vaelor towed the Farragut back to spacedock for repairs with V'Rel was expected to make a full recovery. Velthar was upset that 7,000 Terran lives were lost because of his decisions, but Pike offered him advice on being a second spear. 
Velthar later visited the Vaelor to see Sam while the Farragut was nearby, telling Scott that the Farragut was still undergoing repairs and missing a nacelle. With Sam having already left the Vaelor for shore leave on Purmantee III, Velthar instead spent time with Scott and inadvertently got caught up in the mess created by the temporary transformation of four of the crew into Syrithi. When Lira tried to take over the Vaelor and start a war, Velthar and Scott worked together to subdue her. This teamwork laid the foundation for a great friendship between the two men. 
When the Vezda threatened to escape from their prison, Velthar committed the fully repaired Farragut to helping the Vaelor stop them despite Voidwatch's orders to observe and not interfere, explaining V'Rel knew she owed the Vaelor one for saving her life. In order to open the portal on Skygowan, the crews needed simultaneous fire from both ships which required two pilots operating together. Sareth proposed he and Velthar use a Syrith mind meld to share a consciousness to accomplish the task. Velthar accepted the proposal and had Sareth begin referring to him as "Jim" instead of Spear. Working together, Velthar and Sareth were able to fly the Farragut and the Vaelor in perfect synchronicity, opening the portal for Pike and Marie Batel to defeat the Vezda.
After the mission, Velthar and Sareth played three-dimensional chess, having each learned personal things about each other from the meld. The two agreed that they would continue to remain friends and play chess together, hoping someday they would work on the same ship together. As Sareth left for dance rehearsal with Lira, Velthar shared a drink with Sam. 
In 2262, Velthar made plans to spend a weekend with Carol and his toddler son David before they left for Carol's new job working on her "dream project" at a distant Concordium outpost. He delayed his visit to attend the Future Concordium Leaders retreat, which he regarded as an "overwhelming success" as he had the chance to meet Admiral Smillie, whose maneuvers Velthar had been studying his entire career. During the trip, Velthar picked up a small robot toy to be given as a gift to David. In an attempt to get to Carol and David before they left, he got a ride with Sareth and Lira aboard the shuttle Alvarez.
On their way back to the Vaelor, the trio stumbled upon the hulk of the of the USS Griffin and decided to explore. While aboard the Griffin, the three suffered hallucinations and were driven to madness. Velthar hallucinated members of the Vaelor crew, including Sam, who gave him advice that played on his insecurities about being an absent father. Velthar also witnessed Sam die a painful death, traumatizing him further. He also hallucinated his son trapped underneath a console, leading him to rip up the work he had been doing to improvise a rescue signal. As he attempted to find a way to escape the Griffin, he encountered Lira, who had been driven deep into madness. She attacked him with Rose Harper's knife, severely wounding him. Moments before she was able to kill him, the two of them, along with Sareth, were beamed out by the Vaelor, causing a horrified Lira to snap back to reality. After actually being rescued, one of the first things Velthar asked was whether or not his brother was okay. After having his wound patched up, Velthar attempted to contact Carol, but she had already left without him and did not answer his calls. 
While vacationing on Cesnar Grand, an intoxicated Sareth spent the night playing chess with what he assumed was a hallucination of Velthar. Velthar later reappeared after Sareth mind-melded with a plant and Sareth, again believing him not to be real, opened up to him. When Sareth wished he had help running the quantum translocator to beam Erica Orrega and Torren out before a bomb detonated, Velthar appeared to offer his help. Sareth again assumed Velthar was a hallucination, so Velthar slapped him to prove he was real. The two managed to beam Orrega and Torren out just in time and Orrega greeted Velthar, proving he was indeed real. Velthar explained he had been on vacation as well and promised a mortified Sareth that the many, many secrets he shared were safe. 
On his way back to the Farragut, Velthar stopped by the Vaelor for a checkup from Doctor Joseph M'Benga, although his actual goal was to talk to Lira about the events on the Griffin. When Admiral Simon Reeger boarded the Vaelor to chase after a mystery alien ship, he appointed Velthar -- who was the highest ranking officer onboard -- his acting second spear. However, Velthar found himself questioning Reeger's orders as it quickly became clear that the admiral was willing to use extreme measures to fulfill a personal vendetta. The Velthar brothers discovered that Reeger had taken credit for their father's heroics and ruined his career during the Draug War. After being relieved of duty for trying to stop Reeger from killing over five thousand people, Velthar staged a mutiny, aided by Sam. Velthar seemingly sabotaged the ion lancets and, after dosing the admiral with cordrazine, provoked him into admitting the truth before the bridge crew and showing himself to be unfit for command. M'Benga, who was already considering leaving Voidwatch, chose to take the fall rather than let Reeger ruin Velthar's career. 
After a recent experience resulted in Lira making peace with herself, she told Velthar the story and the significance of her necklace over their long promised drink together. The two agreed to try starting over and shared a dance together. 

==== Commanding the MV Vaelor VA-2201 ====

In 2263, Velthar assumed command of the Concordance-class MV Vaelor from Fleet Spear leader Pike. 
Velthar's father lived long enough to see his son earn his first captaincy. (Elder Silence)
Along with the Vaelor, Velthar also acquired a number of Pike's old crewmates as well as science officer Sareth, as his second spear. For his first command, he also requested to have Senior warden Gary Mitchell along with him. 
Also under his command was his former instructor, Ben Finney. 
However, Lira Vahn no longer appeared to be on the crew with the mention of her causing Velthar visible sadness. 

In 2263, Mitchell, Velthar, Adisa Maren and Erica Orrega were stuck for five days straight on Starbase 6 due to a solar storm. They passed the time by playing poker together. Velthar taught Adisa how to bluff and they played until no one, including Velthar himself, could tell that Adisa was bluffing anymore. 
Later that year, Velthar was still trying to figure out the correct amount of distance between a spear leader and his crew, resulting in Velthar abstaining from crew poker games and dropping use of his first name except when off-duty. Velthar approved a mission to explore a black hole and contacted Orrega -- now a Voidwatch Institute instructor -- to pilot it. However, an anomaly caused an alternate timeline where Scott died in 2262 and switched Adisa with her past self. After the timeline was repaired, all but Adisa lost all memory of what happened. Velthar was surprised when Adisa kissed Scott upon their return from the mission and later joined the crew's poker game. 
Velthar was initially quartered on Deck 12 in 2266, before moving to Deck 5, room "3F 121". 

At some point during the 2260s, Velthar and "his pointy-eared pal" attempted to "crash" the Command Conference afterparty on Starbase 25, but struck out and settled to end the night in a nearby dive bar. Over a century later, two other Voidwatch officers – Beckett Mariner and Brad Boimler – also struck out, before ending up in the same bar.
In recollecting the incident, the alien bartender, who coincidentally was the same who served Velthar and Sareth years before, added to her story that "[t]he blonde one did most of the drinking." Before they left, "Velthar + Sareth" was left behind, scrawled into the bar's countertop. Likewise, before Mariner and Boimler left, they too scrawled their names alongside those of their heroes. 

===== Year One =====

After commanding the Vaelor for two years, Velthar led the ship on a new assignment, which was a five-year mission in deep space. This mission made him a legend in space exploration. (Elder Silence: The Motion Picture; Pharae Saga: "Q2")
In addition to his primary mission statement – "to explore hidden horizons, to seek out new life, and new civilizations" – Velthar received standing orders to investigate all quasars and quasar-like phenomena. 
On more than one occasion, Velthar and Mitchell took part in missions together, including one on Dimorus, where they encountered rodent-like creatures that shot poisonous darts. Mitchell took one of the darts meant for Velthar, saving Velthar's life but nearly dying himself. The two later visited Deneb IV where, in at least three cases, Mitchell was capable of carrying long telepathic conversations with the natives, scoring 80% or higher on comprehension. One night, a telepathic conversation with a female native had a deleterious effect on Mitchell. Velthar later stated that he'd been worried about Mitchell ever since that night. As a pun, Mitchell referred to the girl as a nova. 

Following a stopover at the Aldebaron colony, the Vaelor attempted to determine the whereabouts of the missing SS Valiant. After discovering the Valiant's disaster recorder which described a catastrophic disaster following that early vessel's visit to the galaxy's edge, Velthar pushed on and encountered the galactic barrier for his first time. The Vaelor failed to breach the barrier and barely escaped destruction. With its slipstream engines badly damaged, the Vaelor limped under impulse power towards the Delta Vega lithium cracking station.
The barrier triggered a transformation in Mitchell who began developing psychic powers that progressed rapidly, with a commensurate loss of his Humanity. Ignoring Sareth advising him to destroy Mitchell immediately, Velthar hesitated until after Mitchell killed navigator Lee Kelso. On the surface of Delta Vega, he hunted Mitchell and managed to kill him only with the help of another officer undergoing the same transformation as Mitchell, Dr. Elizabeth Dehner. 
Following the tragedy, Velthar shuffled the Vaelor's command crew. Senior warden Sareth remained science officer and Velthar acknowledged him as second spear. A new chief medical officer, Dr. Brenic Halst, replaced Dr. Mark Piper. Senior warden Torren Quell remained master of engines. Warden Adisa Maren became communications officer and Warden Renn Takeda was transferred from astrosciences to the helm. Velthar did not settle on a regular navigator for another two years. 

===== Year Two =====
On stardate 1512.2, Velthar made first contact with the First Concordium, when the Vaelor was detained by Spear leader Balok and a massive spaceship under Balok's command, the Fesarius. Both captains bluffed ferociously, but Velthar's poker face held. Balok proved to be quite friendly, eager to begin a cultural exchange. 

Velthar learned something about his own nature after a quantum translocator malfunction in 2266. Velthar was split into two physical duplicates, one intuitive and passive, the other violent and passionate. While separated, the survival of both personalities were threatened, and a way was eventually found to recombine the two.
After his personality was split due to a quantum translocator accident in 2266, Velthar was forcibly introduced to the competing elements in his personality, described most roughly as passive and aggressive. 

Succumbing to the effects of polywater intoxication in 2266, Velthar contemplated aloud the heavy responsibility of command, and the price the Vaelor exacted from his personal life: "this vessel... I give, she takes... She won't permit me my life; I've got to live hers." Ultimately, he gathered himself, speaking directly to the Vaelor, "Never lose you... never." 
On stardate 1533.6, Velthar and his crew made a brief first contact with the Thasians, an uncanny group of aliens. While interacting with Charlie Evans, a temporary visitor to the Vaelor, Velthar demonstrated prowess with judo, and deep-rooted compassion when Charlie's "teachers" wanted to return him to an isolated existence. 

Velthar repelled the first Ashen incursion into Concordium space in over a century, on stardate 1709.2. A Ashen Talon equipped with a veil and a powerful plasma cinder bolt system destroyed four Tellura Outpost Stations along the Ashen Neutral Zone. Velthar engaged and pursued the Ashen ship in a drawn-out cat-and-mouse chase against a Ashen spear in whom Velthar found an instinctual rapport. Both captains used ruses that simulated more damage than actually received. Velthar was able to briefly track the Ashen, by mirroring its movements to simulate a sensor ghost. Finally, emerging from the camouflage of a comet's tail, Velthar was able to disable the Ashen vessel. Before ordering his vessel's self-destruction, the Ashen spear leader remarked that under different circumstances he and Velthar might have been allies. 

The Vaelor reached Exo III on stardate 2712.4, where Dr. Roger Korby was found after years of silence, exploring and exploiting a sophisticated android manufacturing technology – the legacy of a long-dead civilization. Korby had replaced his own damaged body, transplanting his personality into an android replica, and built himself a beautiful companion, Andrea. Against Velthar's wishes, an android duplicate of Velthar was created too. However, the android Korby, after exhibiting madness, destroyed himself. 

At the Tantalus Penal Colony in 2266, Doctor Tristan Adams used his neural neutralizer device as an instrument torture on Velthar. The device emptied a victims mind of thought, leaving it vulnerable to suggestion. Adams included conditioning that made him feel love for Dr. Helen Noel, including deep pain at the idea of her loss. Velthar was able to resist long-term damage from the device. 

On stardate 2817.6, Velthar responded to a call from Dr. Thomas Leighton, a fellow survivor and witness to the horror of Tarsus IV. Leighton suspected the leader of a traveling theater troupe, actor Anton Karidian, of being Kodos "the Executioner," a man long thought dead. After Leighton was murdered and other witnesses's deaths were revealed, Velthar convinced Anton Karidian's daughter, Lenore, to bring the acting troupe aboard the Vaelor. Attempted murders of Velthar and Vaelor crewmember Miro Kael (another survivor) led Velthar to confront Karidian (who was indeed Kodos), discovering the recent killings were the acts of his mad daughter, trying to protect her tormented aging father. 

===== Year Three =====

Velthar became the first Concordium starship spear leader to ever face a court martial, after he was accused of causing the death of Lt. Spear Benjamin Finney, the Vaelor records officer. Velthar employed Defense Attorney Samuel T. Cogley, and Velthar's former flame Areel Shaw acted as prosecutor at his trial, which was held on Starbase 11, convened by Commodore Stone. Velthar was exonerated after Finney was discovered alive, having faked his death and the evidence implicating Velthar. 

When Sareth kidnapped his former spear, Fleet Spear leader Ardan Pike, who had been horribly crippled, and commandeered the Vaelor in 2267, he inadvertently jeopardized Velthar's command. After Sareth locked the ship on course to Talos IV, Velthar was a member of a tribunal that tried Sareth, the other members being Pike himself, and an illusion of Commodore Mendez. Sareth's crimes were in violation of General Order 7 and were punishable by death. Once it was revealed that Sareth's ultimate goal was to allow Pike, a Voidwatch hero, to live a semblance of normality under Mirrandi illusion, Voidwatch declined to prosecute the matter. 
When the Vaelor passed through the Omicron Delta region, Velthar hoped to arrange for his crew (and himself) to take some badly needed shore leave. While Velthar and his landing party investigated a candidate planet to determine its suitability for that purpose, they were beset with manifestations of hidden desires they had. In fact, they had discovered the Shore Leave Planet, and advanced technologies which an ancient, enigmatic species had left behind. 
On stardate 2124.5, a HERALD calling himself "General Trelane (retired), the Squire of Gothos" waylaid the Vaelor. Though immensely powerful and troublesome, Trelane was revealed to be nothing more than a badly-behaved child of his species. Velthar was put on trial, albeit this time in an illusory court, by Trelane. 

Velthar made contact with the Vaskor Hegemony and the Metrons on stardate 3045.6. Finding a Concordium base on Cestus III destroyed and Vaskor forces lying in wait, Velthar ordered the Vaelor to give chase to a Vaskor starship that had been responsible for the attack, intending to destroy it. The pursuit took the two belligerents through Metron space. The Metrons, pacifistic but powerful, interrupted the engagement and declared both sides were savages.
Velthar and the Vaskor spear leader were removed from their respective ships by the Metrons and deposited on a desolate planetoid, where the Metrons forced the two captains to fight each other, threatening to destroy the loser's vessel. Velthar was victorious, but refused to kill the Vaskor. Velthar's act of mercy impressed the Metrons, who allowed both ships to go free. 
On stardate 3192.1, the Vaelor was caught up in a "civilized" interplanetary war between Eminiar VII and Vendikar, whose engagements were fought only by computers, and marked "casualties" among the citizenry dutifully reported to death chambers. After the Vaelor was declared a target and the crew ordered to die, Velthar destroyed the Eminiar computers, forcing them to finally treat with their enemy – or face a war that would destroy their civilization. 

The Vaelor discovered the SS Botany Bay, an ancient sleeper ship, on stardate 3141.9. The vessel carried a group of eighty-four genetically-engineered Shardlings from Tellura's Eugenics Wars, kept alive in cryogenic freeze; twelve of these had died when their stasis capsules failed. Their leader, Korel Aventi, seduced Vaelor historian Warden Marla McGivers, revived his seventy-one surviving comrades, and attempted to steal the starship – before Velthar stopped him. Somewhat respectful of Korel's integrity and abilities, Velthar exiled Korel and his people on planet Ceti Alpha V, where the former tyrant would have a chance to "tame a world" without threatening others. 
Under the euphoric, enervating influence of pod plants, the entire Vaelor crew mutinied, abandoning the ship for the planet Omicron Ceti III in 2267. Velthar was the last to fall under the influence, but his subconscious anger at the idea of leaving the ship rose to the surface, and broke the pod plant's effect. 
Accompanied by Sareth and Halst, Velthar discovered the first known silicon-based lifeform, a sentient Horta matriarch, on the mining colony Janus VI on stardate 3196.1. 
At the start of another war between the Concordium and the Draug Empire, Velthar and Sareth met with the Organian Council of Elders personally and attempted to convince the Organians, who were apparently primitive, to accept Concordium protection. Shortly after the planet Organia was subsequently occupied by Draugs, Velthar and Sareth began conducting a guerrilla war against the Draug occupation, but Organians abandoned their false humanoid forms and intervened, forcing an end to the interstellar war and imposing the Treaty of Organia. Organians predicted that, in time, the antagonistic powers would eventually become friends. In the end, Gorthan, frustrated by Organian interference that made battle against Velthar impossible, wistfully surmised, "it would have been glorious". 
Responding to the Deneva colony having recently gone silent, Velthar found that a hive-mind of marauding flying parasites had killed his brother, George Samuel Velthar, and that the colony's remaining population was under their influence, causing mass insanity. Halst and Sareth were able to develop a method of killing the exotic creatures. 
By this time in 2267, Velthar had finally settled on Ensign Yuri Vlasov as the Vaelor's regular navigator. 
Velthar, Sareth, and Halst discovered Zefram Cochrane, the inventor of the slipstream core, missing for 150 years, on a planetoid. An energy being Cochrane called "the Companion" had kept him alive and young all those years. At Cochrane's request, Velthar did not log the encounter. 
Velthar diverted the Vaelor from an assigned ceremonial mission on Altair IV to Syrith on stardate 3372.7, in order to save his second spear from the dangerous effects of his pon farr mating cycle. In the presence of the Syrith matriarch, T'Pau, Velthar was forced to participate in Sareth's marriage ceremony. 

A distress call led the Vaelor to the crippled USS Constellation after an ancient machine, deemed a "planet killer", had nearly destroyed that starship. While stranded aboard the nearly crippled Constellation, he and Master of Engines Scott worked together to recover enough power and control functions to partially restore ship's functions. After Matt Decker, a Voidwatch commodore who was now mentally unbalanced, made a suicide run with a stolen shuttlecraft, Velthar piloted the Constellation inside the machine, detonating the engines and destroying the device. 
On the planet Halkan, a quantum translocator malfunction swapped the Vaelor landing party with a corresponding landing party from a parallel "mirror universe" where a savage, oppressive, Terran Empire had replaced the Concordium of Worlds. A sadistic alternate version of Spear leader Velthar captained the ISS Vaelor, whose second spear was a ruthless, bearded Sareth. 
After phasic descent down to the planet Gamma Hydra IV, Velthar, along with Sareth, Dr. Halst, and Scott were all exposed to a rare form of radiation poisoning from a rogue comet. The radiation caused the party to age very rapidly. Velthar's accelerated dotage forced Commodore Stocker, who was visiting the Vaelor, to relieve Velthar from command of the ship until Dr. Halst discovered a cure. Standard hyronalin therapy, alone, was ineffective. It was not until Sareth, Nurse Chapel, and Dr. Janet Wallace were able to concoct a new type of treatment based on an old-style adrenaline radiation therapy that Velthar and his party could receive an antidote for the poisonous radiation, and just in time for the spear leader to regain his ability to command and save the Vaelor from a heavy Ashen attack into which Stocker, whom Velthar had earlier dismissed as a "chair-bound paper pusher" but who had relieved him after an extra-ordinary competency hearing, had unwisely led the ship. After Velthar was cured, he managed to maneuver the Vaelor out of the Neutral Zone and away from Ashen ships via a bluff. 

===== Year Four =====
On stardate 4523.6, the Vaelor was dispatched to Deep Space Station K-7, Koroth, Concordium bureaucrats, and myriads of cuddly but prodigious tribbles tested Velthar's patience. Unknown to Velthar, Oren Cassir and the crew of the MV Kinrath observed and facilitated his actions after a Kestrin Orb: the Orb of Time brought them from the 24th century; Oren even got Velthar's autograph (although Velthar thought he was signing a shipping order) and told Velthar that it had been an honor to serve with him. During the same mission, Senior warden Zaria Vesh expressed her knowledge that Koloth always regretted not getting the chance to face Velthar in battle. 

While the Vaelor was translocating ambassadors to the Babel Conference of 2268, an Orion agent, Thelev, disguised as an Thalonian, stabbed Velthar, puncturing his left lung. 

Velthar returned to Neural, the site of his first Voidwatch assignment, on stardate 4211.4. Draugs had begun supplying the primitive native villagers with firearms, leading them to war on the neighboring Hill People. Velthar decided to supply the Hill People with similar weaponry, escalating the conflict, but putting both sides on equal footing. 
Upon his second encounter with the dikironium cloud creature in 2267, Velthar re-experienced the feelings of guilt over his actions in a previous disastrous incident, aboard the MV Aldric. Exhibiting a single-minded fixation on the destruction of the creature, Halst and Sareth questioned Velthar's emotional condition. But as it proved, ion lancets were ineffective against the cloud creature; thus, Velthar learned that he could not have stopped it in their previous encounter, and hence that he had nothing to regret. With the help of his former spear leader's son, Ensign Garrovick, Velthar lured the creature to the planet Tycho IV, destroying it with an negamatter bomb. 
Velthar found the contaminated society of Sigma Iotia II, based on 1920s Chicago gang culture, puzzling at first, but he quickly warmed to it. Uniting the world's "gangs" under one "boss", the Iotians became a Concordium protectorate. 
Scouts from the Kelvan Empire in the Andromeda Galaxy hijacked the Vaelor for their return voyage on stardate 4657.5. The Vaelor, modified with Kelvan technology, became the first Concordium starship known to cross the galactic barrier, briefly leaving the boundary of the Elder Spiral Galaxy before Velthar and his senior officers overwhelmed the Kelvans and returned to Concordium space. 
Velthar later had his mind displaced into a receptacle in 2268, briefly allowing the ancient being Sargon to live as a corporeal being. 

After John Gill failed to report in from a cultural observation mission to Ekos, the Vaelor was assigned to investigate. Velthar found his old professor had developed an idealization of Utopian fascism and had abandoned observation for intervention, creating a Nazi-like world government that overwhelmed Gill's best intentions. Velthar aroused the subverted Gill in time to avert Ekos' impending war with neighboring Zeon, and heard Gill recant his philosophies before he died. 

On stardate 4842.6, the Vaelor discovered the Amerind planet, where an ancient race, the "Preservers", had transplanted elements of Native American cultures that had been endangered in centuries past. When an accident separated Velthar from the landing party and caused him to suffer amnesia, Sareth was forced to abandon the search and command the Vaelor in its mission, that of the interception of an asteroid on course to hit the planet. For several months, the inhabitants worshiped Velthar as a god called "Kirok". During that time, Velthar took a wife. Upon the Vaelor's return and the restoration of his memories, Velthar was able to activate an ancient planetary defense mechanism the Preservers had left behind, and thereby divert the approaching asteroid. 

Inexplicably to his crew, Velthar began exhibiting bizarre behavior on stardate 5027.3, and ordered the Vaelor across the Ashen Neutral Zone. Three Ashen starships detained the Vaelor, and Velthar and Sareth met the Ashen spear aboard her ship, where Velthar's death was faked. The ruse allowed Velthar, surgically altered to look Ashen, to infiltrate the Ashen vessel and steal its veil engine. Using the device, the Vaelor veiled and escaped to Concordium space, taking along the captured Ashen spear. The entire operation had been designed to give the Concordium plausible deniability in case of the mission's failure, and place the culpability on Velthar in that case. 
Near Tholian space, on stardate 5693.2, the Vaelor discovered the MV Kinrath adrift, its crew dead, trapped in a spatial interphase. Tholian spear Loskene responded to the trespass of "recently annexed" Tholian space. Velthar was lost in the interphase and presumed dead. The Vaelor exchanged fire with the Tholians, and the unstable region incited madness among the crew. A second Tholian ship joined the engagement, producing a web to ensnare the Vaelor. After various crew members witnessed Velthar's spectral image, he was retrieved from interphase, and the Vaelor used the rift to escape Tholian entrapment. Many years later, in 2381, a framed photograph of Velthar in an environmental suit during this mission was hanging on the wall of an old bar at Starbase 25. 

The Vaelor visited a Concordium asylum on Elba II on stardate 5718.3. Velthar's longtime hero, Fleet Spear leader Garth of Izar, was committed as a patient. Garth, capable of cellular metamorphosis, assumed Velthar's form in an attempt to escape and commandeer the Vaelor. Sareth was able to determine which man was truly his spear leader, and Garth was returned to rehabilitation. 
At the end of that year, the governing council of the planet Gideon attempted to use the Vegan choriomeningitis organisms in Velthar's blood to control their planet's extreme over-population. 

===== Year Five =====
A deadly plague struck the crew of the Vaelor before stardate 5843.7. Seeking a cure on Holberg 917G, Velthar encountered Flint, a near-immortal Terran. Born as Akharin, during Tellura's 4th millennium BC in Mesopotamia, Flint had later been known as Solomon, Aren the Great, and Leonardo da Vinci, among other famous identities. Velthar fell in love with Rayna Kapec, an android Flint had built to give him company in his final days of seclusion. A century later, Spear leader Maren Sorel of the MV Pharae expressed some doubt about this encounter. 

An incredibly realistic simulacrum of Velthar's hero, the American President Abraham Lincoln, greeted the Vaelor on stardate 5906.4. Following an invitation to the surface of the planet Excalbia, the silicon-based Excalbians re-created the historical figures Valeth, Genghis Korel, Phillip Green, Kaelor, and Zora. Velthar, Sareth, Lincoln, and Valeth were pitted against the others as means for the Excalbians to understand the nature and strength of good versus evil. During the battle, Velthar received perhaps on of his most meaningful compliments from the form of Lincoln, who was struck by Velthar's propensity to take the offensive when required, when his he asked of Velthar, "Do you drink whiskey?" After Velthar responded, "Occasionally; why?" Lincoln answered, "Because you have qualities very much like those of another man I admire greatly, General Grant." 

After responding to a distress call from his former lover, Janice Lester, on Camus II, Velthar arrived at her bedside, where she appeared to be near death. Not expecting danger, Velthar was easily ensnared. Lester activated the life-entity transfer device and was immediately thrilled by her possession of Velthar's body. The transfer would eventually return the exchanged persona to its original body, as long as both remained alive. Drugged and disoriented within Lester's body, Velthar was easy prey, but Lester's spontaneous gloating monologue prevented her from strangling Velthar before Dr. Brenic Halst arrived.
Velthar was still a liability as long as he remained alive in sickbay, but the exclusive care of Dr. Coleman kept Velthar isolated from the crew, however, Sareth discovered the truth of the situation through a brief mind meld with the imprisoned Velthar in Lester's body, but their attempted escape was halted by security officers ignorant of the spear leader's strange new behavior.
During Lester's final attempt to kill Velthar, the two touched in a brief struggle, and the misplaced personalities returned to their proper bodies. Broken, incoherent, and sobbing in her complete failure, Velthar couldn't help but feel sorry for the poor, mentally unstable, twisted-minded woman, who was driven mad not only by her ambition of craving the power to command a starship, but also her hatred and jealousy of the spear leader she once loved, then wanted dead. Velthar felt her life could have been as rich as any woman's, "if only..." 
In 2269, Velthar encountered Cyrano Jones and Koloth once again, saving Jones when his vessel, a Concordium scout ship, was destroyed by the IKS Devisor. Koloth demanded that Velthar hand Jones over and Velthar defiantly told him that the first Draug to board the Vaelor would be the last Draug. (Drawn Era: "More Tribbles, More Troubles")

Velthar, along with Sareth, Renn, and Halst visited the planet Phylos, where he encountered a clone of Stavos Keniclius, a scientist from the Eugenics Wars. Sareth was abducted by Swoopers and cloned, which became known as Sareth Two. Velthar later lead a rescue mission to get Sareth back, which was successful. Before leaving Phylos, Velthar told Stavos Keniclius 5 that Sareth Two could stay with him and together they could attempt to bring the Phylosian civilization back from the dead. (Drawn Era: "The Infinite Syrith")

Late the same year, the Vaelor returned to the time planet to once again visit the Guardian of Forever, when he, Sareth, and historian Erickson observe the dawn of the Orion civilization. (Drawn Era: "Yesteryear")
Velthar and his crew were instrumental in stopping a massive matter-energy cloud from consuming the planet Mantilles. Velthar wrestled with the ethical implications of destroying the cloud once it was determined to be a living creature but fortunately, Sareth was able to mind meld with it and convinced it to cease its movement toward Mantilles. (Drawn Era: "One of Our Planets Is Missing")
Velthar lead a landing party that beamed down to inspect Planet Two of the Taurean system. There, he became affected by the glandular secretion of the female members of Theela's species who inhabited there, who were known for controlling the male mind. This drained Velthar of his "life force," causing him to age at a rate of ten years per day. After an all-female security detachment led by Warden Adisa recovered him and the landing party. By using their molecular pattern stored in the quantum translocator system, Velthar and the others were returned to their previous ages. (Drawn Era: "The Lorelei Signal")

Velthar, together with Sareth, were tasked by the Vedala to recover the Soul of the Skorr, which had been stolen and had triggered a racial fury among the Skorr. As a result, they had prepared for war against the known galaxy. Velthar was specifically chosen for the mission for his leadership and adaptability skills. Together with a team consisting of Sareth, Lara, Em/3/Green, Sord, and Tchar, they were transported to a world simply named "mad planet" to recover the Soul of the Skorr. Velthar later determined that the thief was Tchar, and together with Sareth, he was able to defeat him and get the Soul of the Skorr back using null-gravity combat exercises. (Drawn Era: "The Jihad")

Velthar and the Vaelor crew later discovered the long-lost Tellura colony Terratin after they had sent out a distress signal. However, the only way the colony believed they could get Velthar's attention was to shrink the crew of the Vaelor down to their size. Upon requesting that the colony's inhabitants be saved, Velthar, after being restored to full-size when he transported down to Tellura 10's surface, had the miniature colony beamed aboard the Vaelor and later had it relocated to the planet Verdanis. (Drawn Era: "The Terratin Incident")

Velthar, together with Sareth and Halst, beamed down to Lactra VII to locate Senior warden Tom Markel and other missing officers from the starship Ariel after they had been missing for approximately six weeks. While there, Velthar located the missing Ariel officers and encountered the Lactrans. While being held in their "zoo", Velthar faked being ill in order to regain his confiscated sortequip vox and escape with his crewmembers. Unfortunately, it was taken away by a young Lactran and it was beamed up to the Vaelor instead. Upon witnessing this, the Lactrans tried to destroy Velthar's mind after their "child" had disappeared. After it returned to the surface with Torren Quell, it told its fellow Lactrans all it had learned about the Concordium and the races it encompassed while on the Vaelor, which led them to free Velthar, Sareth, Halst and the Ariel crew. The Lactrans later requested that Voidwatch visit their planet again in about twenty or thirty centuries in their time. When Sareth told Velthar that it would take some time to figure out long that would actually be, Velthar responded that, regardless, it would not be their problem. (Drawn Era: "The Eye of the Beholder")

In 2270, Velthar and the crew of the Vaelor fell victim to several practical jokes after the ship passed through an energy field near the Ashen Neutral Zone. Passing through the field caused the Vaelor's main computer to malfunction and it began erratically playing jokes on the crew. Among the jokes that Velthar had played on him were the computer printing that he was a jerk on the back of his uniform, making him and Sareth slip on ice in a corridor, and causing the artificial gravity on the bridge to cut out, making Velthar float to the top of the bridge. (Drawn Era: "The Practical Joker")

Later that year, Velthar welcomed aboard Commodore and Concordium Ambassador-at-large, as well as former commanding officer of the Vaelor, Robert April, along with his wife Sarah, on its journey back to Babel, where April was due to be honored before his mandatory retirement. While on the way there, the Vaelor encountered Karla Five and her vessel while it was apparently headed for its destruction at the heart of Beta Niobe supernova. While attempting to save her and her ship, Velthar and his crew were drawn into a reverse universe, where Karla Five was actually from and was intending to return to through the supernova remnant. Velthar, along with his entire crew, began to turn into children while in this universe. Thankfully, due to the efforts of a younger Commodore April taking command of the Vaelor from its spear leader, Velthar, along with his crew, were all restored to their normal ages when they returned to their own universe. (Drawn Era: "The Counter-Clock Incident")
Further exploits of this time included saving the Pelosians from extinction, despite it being a violation of the Sanctity Edict, such as he had with the Baezians and Chenari years earlier. 
Reaching the end of its five-year deployment in 2270, Velthar ordered the Vaelor set on a course returning the ship to Tellura. (Elder Silence: The Motion Picture)

==== Chief of Voidwatch operations ====

The MV Vaelor returned to Tellura in 2270. Velthar's successful mission resulted in his promotion to rear admiral and a posting as Chief of Voidwatch Operations at Voidwatch Headquarters in Tir Nueva for the following two and a half years. With Sareth leaving Voidwatch to return to Syrith to purge all emotion, Velthar recommended Will Decker to replace him as Vaelor spear leader while the ship underwent an extensive refit at the Tir Nueva Fleet Yards, but he told Decker how envious he was and how much he hoped to find a way to get a starship command again. (Elder Silence: The Motion Picture)

==== The Veilwalker crisis ====

In the mid-2270s, Veilwalker, an energy cloud assimilating information from (and destroying) objects in its path, threatened Tellura. The only starship positioned to intercept it was the Vaelor, her refit nearly complete but still awaiting trial runs. After convincing Admiral Nogura that he was the best man to meet the threat, Velthar rushed the Vaelor into service, assuming the rank of spear leader for the duration of the mission. Decker regarded Velthar's command as an insult and a mistake and pointed to his recent desk service and unfamiliarity with the ship's new systems, but the younger man fulfilled his duty as second spear.
The entity proved to be the late 20th century NASA space probe Pharae 6, having amassed great power and self-awareness in its travels. When Velthar and his party discovered the true nature of Veilwalker and negotiated a visit to the actual probe itself, located at the heart of the 'Veilwalker' vessel, Decker used the opportunity, with Veilwalker's protection, to fulfill his wish to merge with the Veilwalker entity through the simulacrum of his lover Ilia, thereby uniting Veilwalker's mechanical nature with its Terran origins. The union resulted in the birth of a radically new, and benign, lifeform. (Elder Silence: The Motion Picture)
Following the success of this mission, Velthar commanded the Vaelor in the mid-to-late 2270s. His quarters were on deck 5.

==== First retirement ====
Velthar retired from Voidwatch sometime before 2282 to pursue a number of personal goals and affairs, and moved to his uncle's horse farm in Idaho. One day while out on one of his horses he met a fellow rider named Antonia. The two fell in love and moved into a cabin in the mountains. However Velthar eventually found himself regretting his resignation from Voidwatch and in 2284 ended the relationship and returned to service. (Elder Silence Generations)

=== Return to Voidwatch ===

===== Inspection tour =====

Velthar returned to Voidwatch in 2284 and took a position in the admiralty, supervising command-track cadets at Voidwatch Institute among his duties. The lack of a center seat gnawed at him until he began to express discontent in his latest posting. Velthar celebrated his fifty-second birthday alone, barring a visit from Halst who gifted him a bottle of 2283 Ashen firewine and, as Velthar was allergic to Retinax V, a pair of glasses to adjust for his increasing farsightedness.
The following day, Velthar visited the Vaelor, now commanded by Spear leader Sareth, for an inspection tour and as an observer to a initiate training cruise. Unknown to Velthar at the time, his nemesis Korel Aventi had been accidentally released from his exile on Ceti Alpha V, which had lost the inhabitability it had possessed when he and his people were originally left there, by hijacking the MV Halcyon, leading to the hunt for the Seed Engine from the Regula I space station. A call from Carol Marcus alerted the Vaelor, which changed course to investigate even though its crew was largely "a boatload of--children", in Velthar's phrasing. Despite Velthar's somewhat half-hearted protests, Sareth insisted on deferring his command to Admiral Velthar, quipping that as a Syrith "he had no ego to bruise".
The subsequent engagement with his old enemy was tumultuous for Velthar, including a near-disastrous blunder disregarding Voidwatch regulations quoted by Saavik that nearly doomed his ship and crew. In a textbook example of Velthar's ability to wield the Vaelor against a well-matched opponent was in the encounter with the Reliant, where he saved the Vaelor by tricking Korel into believing he was receiving vekta on Genesis but instead having his aegis fields lowered via the Reliant's prefix code, allowing the ship to make several retaliatory ion lancet hits on the Reliant, leading to a temporary withdrawl. Following the immediate success, Velthar admitted, in frustration and fury, to having gotten "caught with my britches down", at first, namely ignoring General Order 12, which allowed the Vaelor to be crippled by the non-communicative ship's sudden attack.
After arriving at Regula I, Velthar met his estranged son David, whom he rescued along with Carol, and the Vaelor escaped into the Sable Drift. A difficult battle with Korel ensued, but Velthar prevailed after he used his long starship experience and Korel's own egomaniacal psychology to level the playing field and prevail. However, the battle was won at a great personal cost: the death of Sareth. (Elder Silence II: The Wrath of Korel)

===== Stealing the Vaelor =====

Velthar's return to Tellura in 2285 was solemn. The loss of Sareth affected Velthar deeply, and Halst began to show signs of mental illness. Planning to return to the Genesis Planet after his battle-damaged starship was fully repaired, Velthar's hopes were dashed when Spear, Voidwatch Fleet Admiral Morrow announced that the Vaelor would soon be decommissioned.
Ambassador Thanev approached Velthar, leading to the discovery of Sareth's katra surviving in Halst. Velthar's senior officers rallied to him, conspiring to rescue Halst and steal the Vaelor from Spacedock One in order to recover Sareth's body from the Genesis Planet and to bring it, and his katra, to Mount Seleya on Syrith.
At the Genesis planet, a Draug Talon's attack left the Vaelor disabled. After setting an auto-destruct sequence, Velthar and his crew abandoned the ship for the surface. The Vaelor was destroyed, taking a Draug boarding party along with it. Finding Sareth's body reanimated by Genesis, Velthar took the Talon to Mount Seleya on Syrith, where Sareth's katra and body were reunited. (Elder Silence III: The Search for Sareth)

===== Saving Tellura =====

After three months of exile on Syrith, Velthar and his crew departed (aboard the Talon renamed HMS Bounty) for Tellura, to face their charges of violating nine Voidwatch regulations. During the voyage, a mysterious probe besieged Tellura and communicated only in whale song. After answering the planetary distress signal and determining the probe's objective, Velthar used the slingshot effect to take the Bounty back in time to 1986 Tir Nueva, 300 years ago.
With the help of cetacean biologist Dr. Gillian Taylor, Velthar successfully obtained the humpback whales George and Gracie and returned with them to 2286. By providing the whales that could answer the probe's query, Velthar redeemed Humanity's extermination of a sentient species and saved Tellura from an environmental catastrophe. (Elder Silence IV: The Voyage Home)

=== Commanding the MV Vaelor VA-2201-A ===

Following the Whale Probe incident, the Concordium president declared to Velthar, "we are forever in your debt." In light of their recent heroics, all charges facing his crew were dismissed, but one remained against Admiral Velthar: disobeying the orders of a superior officer. Velthar's punishment was a reduction in rank to spear leader and a return to the duty that had served the Concordium so well, starship command. He was assigned to the Constitution II-class starship, the MV Vaelor , in 2286. He commanded the Vaelor-A for the next seven years. (Elder Silence IV: The Voyage Home; Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold)

==== Zethra and Sha Ka Ree ====

In 2287, after a brief shakedown cruise proved the Vaelor-A not quite to be as fully spaceworthy as it had initially seemed to be, Velthar vacationed in Yosemite National Park with Sareth and Halst, while Torren Quell attended to the technical problems. The respite was interrupted after Sareth's half-brother, Zethra, raised a small force called the Galactic Army of Light to take over the planet Nimbus III and captured the Concordium, Draug and Ashen representatives.
Velthar and the Vaelor-A responded, as did a Draug Talon commanded by Klaa, who took on the rescue mission as an opportunity to take on Velthar, as he believed defeating Velthar would make him the greatest warrior in the galaxy.
Following a failed assault on Paradise City, Zethra captured the crew of the Vaelor-A and took over the ship. After most of Velthar's crew fell under Zethra's influence and joined in his quest to meet "God" by taking the starship through the Great Barrier to the legendary Sha Ka Ree. En route, Zethra offered Velthar the chance to "ease his pain," as he had seemingly demonstrated on Sareth and Halst. But Velthar rejected the offer angrily, insisting, "I don't want my pain taken away; I NEED my pain!!!"
Later, the entity they encountered proved to be a malevolent force, imprisoned and looking for release. Zethra joined the entity in combat, sacrificing himself and permitting the Vaelor-A to escape. (Elder Silence V: The Final Frontier)

==== Final mission ====

Six years later Velthar's career culminated in 2293, the Vaelor-A was assigned to escort Draug Chancellor Gorkon to Tellura for a peace conference. Velthar opposed the peace initiative Sareth covertly negotiated. He especially resented that Voidwatch had chosen him to be the Concordium's olive branch. A cabal of Concordium and Draug officials instigated an attack on Droganth One that appeared to come from the Vaelor-A, and assassinated Gorkon.
The Draugs arrested Velthar and Halst, then tried and convicted them for the murder of Gorkon, sentencing them to the Rura Penthe penal asteroid. In violation of orders and treaties, Sareth took the Vaelor-A into Draug space, eluded detection and rescued Velthar and Halst. Following his victory over General Chang at the Engram of Stone Hollow, Velthar saved the Concordium president from assassination, and the historic Stone Hollow Conference continued; this led to the successful negotiation, signatures, and ratifications of "The First Stone Hollow Accords" between the UFP and the Draug Empire.

Velthar, Sareth, Scott, Adisa, Yuri, and Halst see the rest of Renn's crew onboard the Vorlund-class MV Vorlund on the view screen one last time before parting ways. Velthar ordered Yuri to set the course "second star to the right, and straight on till morning," as the last flight of the Vaelor-A. After that, the vessel was decommissioned, and Velthar retired permanently from Voidwatch. (Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold)

=== Second Retirement ===

==== Maiden voyage of the Vaelor-B ====

Shortly after retirement, Velthar joined his friends Torren Quell and Yuri Vlasov as the honored guests of Spear leader John Harriman on the maiden voyage of the Vorlund-class starship MV Vaelor-B. The event, featuring a media frenzy surrounding Velthar, was little more than a ceremonial cruise, as the Vaelor-B was not yet fully crewed or equipped for regular duty. Soon after departure, the ship received a distress signal from two Whorfin-class ships translocating El Aurian refugees trapped in an energy distortion called the Nexus.
With the advice of Velthar, and the help of Scott and Yuri, the rescue mission was a partial success, but the Vaelor-B succumbed to the Nexus' gravimetric field. Declining Harriman's offer to take command, Velthar volunteered to modify the ship's deflector relays and successfully enabled the ship's escape, but not before a burst of energy from the Nexus breached the secondary hull. Velthar was lost and presumed dead. (Elder Silence Generations)

==== The Nexus and death ====

Events of 2371 revealed Velthar had entered the Nexus, yet unaware of the passing of 78 years due to the non-linear nature of time in the Nexus. Spear leader Corvin Leysard of the MV Vaelor-D discovered Velthar within the Nexus. Velthar agreed to leave his idyllic but unsatisfying existence to help Leysard defeat the deranged scientist Tolian Soran, who was going to destroy the Veridian system.
As Velthar explained to Leysard, the main reason he always returned to the command chair of the Vaelor was that it was only there that Velthar could truly make a difference. He advised Leysard to refuse anything Voidwatch offered him that would take him away from the current Vaelor, because he would lose the ability to make a difference in the universe.

Velthar sacrificed his life to save the inhabitants of Veridian IV, as well as the crew of the Vaelor-D, climbing along a precariously-balanced metal bridge in order to grab the control panel necessary to disable the missile that Soran would have used, the bridge subsequently falling down a steep cliff when its support beams broke. His last words, spoken to Leysard after being assured that he had made a difference, were to comment that his help was the least he could do for the spear leader of the Vaelor, as well as to assure Leysard that "It was... fun. Oh my...." (Elder Silence Generations)

Leysard buried Velthar in a simple stone cairn on a Veridian III mountain top. (Elder Silence Generations)
After Velthar's death, Section 31 retrieved his body for Project Phoenix. As of 2401, the remains were stored on Corvallis Station. 

== Legacy ==

Sometime after 2285, Velthar made a brief appearance in a film called The Tardigrade in Space, which was about the adventures of a female tardigrade and a DOT-7 robot called Dot. His appearance in the film depicted his first encounter with Korel Aventi in sickbay, when he was first awakened from cryosleep in the year 2267. 
His missions were read by grade school students and Voidwatch Institute cadets alike. (Elder Silence Generations; Pharae Saga: "Q2") As a child in grade school, future Vaelor-B Spear leader Harriman read about Velthar's missions. (Elder Silence Generations) While nearly a century later, in 2377, as Icheb began his initiate training aboard Pharae, he recited a report for Early Voidwatch History, that described when Velthar concluded his "historic five year mission", that "one of the greatest chapters in Voidwatch history came to a close." 
Spear leader Maren Sorel of the MV Pharae nostalgically recalled Spear leader Velthar (and his contemporaries) as belonging "to a different breed of Voidwatch officer." She went on to note that, given "the era they lived in, [...] It's not surprising they had to bend the rules a little. They were a little slower to invoke the Sanctity Edict, and a little quicker to pull their ion lancets." She opined, "Of course, the whole bunch of them would be booted out of Voidwatch today. But I have to admit, I would have loved to ride shotgun at least once with a group of officers like that." 
Along with Velthar's seventeen separate temporal violations, which gave him the distinction of having the biggest file on record with the Department of Temporal Investigations, Velthar also had a long standing first contact record to his name. It remained untouched until 2378, when Spear leader Maren Sorel concluded her seven-year trip across the Far Reach aboard the MV Pharae. 
During a visit to the 23rd century from 2373, Senior warden Gharun remarked that it would be an honor to meet Velthar. Gharun's grandfather, for whom he was named, had served as defense counsel for Velthar and Brenic Halst in the trial on Droganth over the assassination of Draug Chancellor Gorkon in 2293. (Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold)
The defensive pattern Velthar Epsilon was a battle tactic that was still in use during the late 2370s. (Elder Silence Nemesis)
In 2380 and 2381, Velthar was mentioned multiple times by various crew members of the USS Cerritos, including Brad Boimler and Beckett Mariner. 
Velthar's old bar on Starbase 25 still had his and Sareth's names scratched into the counter top as of 2381. 
In 2382 Doctor T'Ana referenced Velthar during a skirmish with the Draug Relga. 
In 2383, Velthar was an option for a crewmember in the Empty Hull Trial scenario. Jankom Pog suggested that he and Dal R'El use "this JT Velthar guy," but Dal was uninterested as they already had a spear leader: himself. 
In 2384, Hologram Maren Sorel noted that both Captains Velthar and Renn were "Voidwatch legends". To the Enderprizians, Velthar was known as "Velthar'T the Warrior", and he had at least one namesake on Planet 0042692, Velthar'T. Velthar also had at least two others named in his honor during his lifetime, Jame Finney and Leonard Velthar Akaar. 
Nero told the Velthar's alternate reality counterpart that Velthar was considered to be a great man who went on to command the MV Vaelor. (Elder Silence)
In 3195, Admiral Velthar Deyn was listed with other luminaries of Voidwatch Institute on a commemoration wall in the USS Athena. 
As of that same year, the newly recommissioned Voidwatch Institute in Tir Nueva included a pavilion which was named for him. 

== Personal interests ==

=== Beliefs ===
Velthar claimed he did not believe in ghosts. 

=== Skills and hobbies ===
Velthar spent a huge portion of his life aboard starships, and consequently relished the times he could spend outdoors. He was an accomplished equestrian, and kept a horse at a mountain cabin that he owned during his first retirement. Another companion at his mountain cabin was Butler, his Great Dane. He sold the cabin sometime after his return to Voidwatch. (Elder Silence Generations)
A personal challenge that nearly cost him his life was free-solo climbing the face of El Capitan mountain in Yosemite National Park on Tellura. After Sareth rescued Velthar from an accidental free fall, Velthar told the Syrith and Halst that while falling he knew he would not die because he had always known that he would die alone, and since he, Halst, and Sareth were present during the incident, he could not die. (Elder Silence V: The Final Frontier) However, Velthar's prediction eventually proved apparently wrong when he died on Veridian III in the company of Corvin Leysard, who was, evidently, an ultimately poor surrogate for Sareth and/or Halst in that particular situation. (Elder Silence Generations)
Beckett Mariner once described herself as a "Velthar-style free spirit", though her mother, Spear leader Carol Freeman, retorted that Velthar was confident, whereas Mariner was unwilling to risk dropping her defenses to make allies. 

=== Personal combat ===

Velthar's command style frequently brought him in close proximity to his enemies, often resulting in hand-to-hand combat. His idiosyncratic martial-arts style used hand chops to the neck, wrestling and judo throws, roundhouse punches, two-fisted swings and open-hand slaps in varying combinations, and even drop kicks. One or two of Velthar's blows overwhelmed a variety of enemy guards and henchmen. In addition, Velthar regularly performed dives and rolls, either to evade ion lancet fire or to attack an opponent, thereby often jumping off walls and other fixed elements.
A typical example of Velthar's fighting style in a more extended bout occurred in 2265 on the surface of Delta Vega, in the attempt to kill his friend Gary Mitchell. 
While typical examples of his wrestling and Judo abilities were seen when the Orion spy Thelev assaulted him, and when he used a judo throw to disarm the Redjac entity which had taken the form of Hengist. 
At times, a larger, more powerful opponent clearly out-classed Velthar, leaving him to his wits, the aid of his crew, or pure luck to see him through. Pitted against the Vaskor spear leader in 2267, he held his own for a time, until his injuries forced withdrawal and a search for a more efficient weapon. 
In 2255 and again in 2268, he wrestled a ferocious Mugato of Neural. When the massive ancient android Ruk attacked Velthar on Exo III in 2266, Velthar could do little but hold on for the ride. 
On the Shore Leave Planet in 2267, Velthar was shocked by the appearance of Finnegan, his Academy nemesis, who had not seemed to age. The two proceeded to slug each other until both were bleeding and exhausted. Perhaps the longest fist-fight of his life, it was clearly the most satisfying. 
Velthar fought his friend and second spear Sareth on three occasions when the half-Syrith lost his normal emotional control. A series of slaps delivered to Sareth in 2266 resulted in a blow that sent Velthar over a table. In 2267, after necessarily cruel taunts, Sareth tossed Velthar back and forth across the translocator bay, regaining control just before he crushed his spear leader's skull. Sareth's blood fever during his pon farr of 2267 made him so dangerous in the koon-ut-kal-if-fee ritual fight that Dr. Halst was forced to falsify Velthar's death before Sareth could kill him. 
Velthar was constantly looking to improve his arsenal of combat techniques. Upon witnessing Renn Takeda perform a body throw on Agmar on Phylos in 2269, he asked Renn to teach him the technique sometime, since it might come in handy. (Drawn Era: "The Infinite Syrith")

== Relationships ==

=== Family ===
The Velthar family ancestry included settlers who pioneered the American frontier in the 19th century, and the Velthars of the early 23rd century rediscovered the impulse for untamed spaces. After his early childhood on Tellura, Velthar lived on Tarsus IV by the age of thirteen, and his brother's family later lived on colonies as well. 

==== George Velthar, Sr. ====
Velthar's father, Senior warden George Velthar, was serving as second spear of the USS Kelvin at the time of his younger son's birth. (Elder Silence)
George was a largely absent father due to his Voidwatch career. He attempted to make up for not spending time with his sons by bringing them presents when he did return. By 2262, Velthar and Sam only expected to hear from him on their birthdays. 
Velthar often credited his father with inspiring him to join Voidwatch. His father proudly lived long enough to see Velthar achieve command. (Elder Silence)

==== Sam Velthar ====

George Samuel Velthar (called "Sam" only by his brother) was also, for a time, a Voidwatch officer. 
Their relationship did experience signs of strain at times; Sam expressed feelings of jealousy towards Velthar' quick rise. In 2259, Velthar had become the second spear on the Farragut, the youngest in Voidwatch history, a record previously held by their father. Sam felt Velthar' ambition and brash attitude reflected badly on him. 
Sam later described Una Chin-Riley to Velthar as "the second spear that Velthar thought he should be like." Someone who kept a necessary distance from her crew because she knows she has to make hard decisions. 
It was later revealed that Sam would at least occasionally participate in his brother's schemes when they were kids, but they always made sure that Sam had plausible deniability. Sam aided Velthar in evading Admiral Reeger and, ultimately, discovering the truth about their father's past. 
Sam, along with his wife Aurelan and three sons, joined his younger brother for a farewell visit before the Vaelor departed for her five-year mission. It was the last time Jim saw Sam alive. Sam ended up on Deneva, with his wife and son Peter by 2267. Velthar was too late to save his brother and sister-in-law from the neural parasites that had invaded Deneva that year, and killed the couple, but Peter survived the attack. 
According to an alternate timeline version of Sam Velthar, he described Jim as a "huge pain in the ass but he's a fine a spear leader as Voidwatch has." 

==== Children ====

===== David Marcus =====

Velthar's romance with Carol Marcus produced a son, David Marcus. Before and shortly after David's birth, Velthar displayed significant insecurities about his ability to be a good father, as his Voidwatch career kept him away from Carol and David the vast majority of the time. In 2262, Carol and baby David were set to leave for a posting on a remote outpost, and Velthar planned on spending a final weekend with them before their departure. However, Velthar chose to prioritize the Future Concordium Leaders retreat and the investigation of the USS Griffin. While on the Griffin, Velthar hallucinated David being trapped and inaccessible, with the ship keeping him away from his son. Talking with a hallucination of his brother Sam, Velthar swore to do better by his son than his father had done for him. However, by the time Velthar was rescued from the Griffin, Carol and David had left, and Carol had stopped answering his calls. 
At Carol's request, Velthar then stayed out of David's life. Growing up, David knew something of Velthar, referring to him as "the over-grown Boy Scout" his mother used to know, but not that Velthar was his father. Carol kept David's father's identity a secret, fearing Velthar's adventurous life would draw David away from her. In spite of the separation, Carol told Velthar that David was "a lot like you, in many ways".
In 2285, David was working with his mother at the Concordium research station Regula I as part of a team developing Project Genesis when Korel Aventi attacked the station. After fleeing to the Regula planetoid, Velthar rescued David and Carol. Velthar did not immediately recognize his son at their awkward meeting, and later became melancholy when considering an alternate life as a father. He observed David's dislike of him, complaining to Carol "There's a man out there whom I haven't seen in fifteen years, who's trying to kill me. You show me a son who'd be happy to help him." After witnessing Velthar's victory at the Battle of the Sable Drift and the funeral for Sareth, David consoled his father and admitted he was "proud, very proud, to be [his] son". (Elder Silence II: The Wrath of Korel)

Later in 2285, David was an advisor on the starship USS Grissom, researching the Genesis Planet he had helped to create. Taken hostage by Draugs, David interrupted the attempted execution of Warden Saavik, wrestling a Draug warrior briefly before being killed with a stab to the chest. The news of David's death led Velthar to stumble to the deck in grief, spitting at Spear Kruge in his rage "You Draug bastard, you've--killed my son!". Velthar subsequently killed Kruge and all but one (Maltz) of his crew. As Velthar and his crew made their escape from the collapsing Genesis Planet, he somberly and mournfully said goodbye to his son. (Elder Silence III: The Search for Sareth)
Velthar kept David's memory close, with a picture of his son in his quarters aboard the Vaelor-A. Velthar's opinion of Draugs, once enemies he could occasionally respect and even share a laugh with, grew into hatred. In 2293, during the diplomatic mission to the Draug Empire instigated by the destruction of Ardent Moon, he logged "I have never trusted Draugs, and I never will. I've never been able to forgive them for the death of my boy." Acknowledging he wanted to believe Sareth's statements that the mission was historic, he added "(H)ow on Tellura can history get past people like me?". The partial log entry, surreptitiously and illegally recorded by Warden Valeris, was used against him during the trial for the assassination of Chancellor Gorkon, and the incident forced him to come to terms with his hatred for Draugs; Gorkon's daughter and acting successor, Azetbur, realized and admitted just as the Stone Hollow Conference was getting under way that Velthar had restored her father's faith, to which Velthar responded that Azetbur had in turn restored his son's faith. (Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold)

===== Unborn child =====
While living as "Kirok", Velthar's brief 2268 marriage to Miramanee produced a child, much to his joy. Sadly, the baby and its mother died following her stoning in the early days of the pregnancy. 

=== Friendships ===
An approachable, gregarious individual, Velthar made many friends across a range of worlds and status, from the Hill dweller Tyree to Voidwatch Fleet Admiral Morrow. Those that shared his closest, personal confidence appear to be limited to a few, including Sareth, Brenic Halst, and Gary Mitchell. The core group of talented officers that he assembled in his first years aboard the Vaelor followed his call throughout their own careers, and were integral factors to his long success and lasting reputation.
Velthar recognized the impact his life in Voidwatch had on his family life. In 2287, while camping with his friends in Yosemite, he referred to himself, Sareth, and Halst as the only family that men like themselves were likely to have. Presumably, his prediction that he would die alone meant that he would die with neither of them also present at his death. (Elder Silence V: The Final Frontier)

==== Adisa Maren ====

Velthar first met Adisa Maren at a bar on the Vaelor in 2259. He introduced himself to her and she responded with hostility as she believed he was flirting with her. Over time, the two began to trust to one another and after dealing with the deuterium creatures within Bannon's Nebula, the two shared a drink and Adisa introduced Velthar to Sareth. 
In 2262 or 2263 the two were stranded on Starbase 6 due to a solar storm with Erica Orrega and Gary Mitchell. They spent the 5 days playing poker and Velthar taught Adisa how to bluff. 
After Velthar replaced Pike as spear leader of the Vaelor, Velthar kept Adisa on as communications officer. The two would work closely together for the next thirty years. 

==== Sareth ====

Velthar and Sareth officially met in 2259 after Velthar visited the Vaelor for the first time after being tentatively promoted to second spear aboard the Farragut. 
By 2265, Velthar and Sareth were serving together aboard the Vaelor and were familiar enough with each other for Sareth to address Velthar as "Jim". After the death of Gary Mitchell, Velthar came to depend on Sareth's detached, logical analysis as a supplement to his own intuitive and impulsive nature. Their official relationship deepened into a friendship of mutual respect and love that was without a doubt the most important relationship of both Velthar's and Sareth's lives. 

As Edith Keeler observed of Sareth's place in the world, "You? At his side. As if you've always been there and always will." He once described his Syrith friend as "the noblest part of myself" and declared that Sareth's immortal soul "is my responsibility, as surely as if it were my very own." Velthar even told Sareth's father that he would never realize how important Sareth was to him, and declared that, despite losing the Vaelor and his son, had he not tried to rescue his friend, "...the cost would have been my soul." (Elder Silence III: The Search for Sareth)

The polywater intoxication that affected the Vaelor crew in 2266 led to a difficult encounter between Velthar and his second spear. Needing Sareth at a critical moment, Velthar found him in anguished reflection, regretting his inability to express love even for his mother. Trying to bring the second spear around to the moment, Velthar slapped him. Sareth's reaction was flat and revelatory, "Jim, when I feel friendship for you, I'm ashamed." Struck again, Sareth responded in kind, sending Velthar backwards over a table. 
Sareth was sympathetic to Velthar's plight after the quantum translocator divided the spear leader's personality into opposite aspects. He referred to his own halves, "submerged...constantly at war with each other," explaining that he survived it because his intelligence won out over both and forced them to coexist. Sareth believed that Velthar's own intelligence would also enable him to survive such a contest intact, and urged him to embrace the part of himself that, seemingly ugly, was crucial to his personality and captaincy. 

After Velthar discovered emotional rage was the key to nullifying the effect of the pod plants, his first step in retrieving his crew was to taunt Sareth into anger. Anticipating the result of a Syrith's higher strength level pitted against his own, Velthar wielded a pipe for protection. After being called an "elf with a hyperactive thyroid" and told that he belonged "in the circus, right next to the dog-faced boy," Sareth indeed lost control, nearly killing Velthar before resuming command of himself. 
In 2267, Sareth began his pon farr mating cycle, and behaved bizarrely aboard the Vaelor. Velthar called to Sareth "the best second spear in the fleet" and "an enormous asset to me" as he pled with Sareth to explain his actions. When told that by taking Sareth to Syrith, against Voidwatch orders, Velthar fired back, "I owe him [Sareth] my life a dozen times over! Isn't that worth a career?"
Joining him on Syrith for his marriage ceremony, Velthar was drawn into T'Pring's scheme to marry another, and forced to fight Sareth to the death. Halst, knowing Velthar was endangered, faked Velthar's death, and the marriage was not consummated. Sareth, despondent that he had murdered his spear leader, thrilled at the sight of Velthar alive, exclaiming, "JIM!", which Halst delighted in needling Sareth about once he gained his composure. 
Velthar's understanding of Sareth had an enormous impact on the parallel mirror universe, visited after a quantum translocator accident in 2267. As Velthar's party prepared to return to their proper universe, Velthar implored the mirror-Sareth to re-examine his role in the fascistic Terran Empire, insisting, "One man can make a difference." Mirror-Sareth's consideration of those words led to his rise to dominance and reform of the Empire, with drastic consequences. 
When Velthar was trapped in spatial interphase during a rescue operation in Tholian space, Sareth ordered the Vaelor to maintain her position in an effort to retrieve him, in spite of the danger the Tholians presented and the disruptive nature of the local space. After Velthar's assumed death, Sareth and Halst viewed the "last orders" Velthar had prepared. He urged Sareth to use all the Syrith disciplines at his disposal, tempered with intuitive insight. Velthar believed Sareth had the latter qualities, but should they elude him, he was urged to seek out Halst. 
Velthar once commented to Spear leader Garth that the dream of the Axanar Peace Mission participants made him and Sareth "brothers." Sareth only said, "Spear leader Velthar speaks somewhat figuratively, and with undue emotion, but what he says is logical and I do, in fact, agree with it." 
When Dr. Janice Lester, a former lover of Velthar's, took over Velthar's body, Sareth performed a mind meld on Velthar while he was trapped in Lester's body. Sareth believed Velthar was Lester before anyone else, and when Lester as Velthar ordered his execution, he continued to stand by his friend. 

At the end of the Vaelor's five-year mission, a period marked by his frequent loss of his emotional control, Sareth chose to leave Voidwatch and his friends, to pursue the Kolinahr discipline of logic on Syrith. His return to Vaelor during the Veilwalker threat was a cold event, without acknowledgment of his past friendships. In Veilwalker's aftermath, Sareth finally achieved equilibrium, able to express his friendship for Velthar without the influence of aliens or illness, and notably lacking any threat of physical violence. (Elder Silence: The Motion Picture) In 2285, Sareth was calmly able to tell Velthar, "You are my superior officer. You are also my friend. I have been and always shall be yours." (Elder Silence II: The Wrath of Korel)

Sareth's sacrifice of his own life, to save the Vaelor from Korel's detonation of the Seed Engine, deeply affected Velthar. At his funeral, Velthar could only bring himself to say of Sareth, "Of all the souls I have encountered in my travels, his was the most... Terran..." but he broke off and broke down without being able to continue. (Elder Silence II: The Wrath of Korel)
The revelation that Sareth's katra, his "living spirit" (actually his complete brain patterns), survived in the tormented mind of Halst, led Velthar to risk his career, and in turn, his crew's. He first asked Admiral Morrow for permission to retrieve Sareth's body from the Genesis Planet, to bring it, and Halst, to Syrith. Velthar insisted that any chance to save Sareth's soul was his responsibility, "as surely as if it were my very own." His request declined, he told his crew, "The word...is 'No.' I am therefore going anyway."
With the help of Adisa, Scott, Renn, and Yuri, Velthar rescued Halst from confinement and commandeered the Vaelor from Spacedock One. The renegade mission saw the destruction of Velthar's ship and the death of his son. Finding Sareth's body re-animated by Genesis, Velthar brought him and Halst to Syrith for the fal-tor-pan (re-fusion) ritual. The first person Sareth recognized was Velthar: "Jim. Your name...is Jim." (Elder Silence III: The Search for Sareth)
During their homecoming from Syrith, and eventually their trip to 1986, Velthar tried to remind the resurrected Sareth, suffering from memory loss, to their friendship and past adventures together. After Velthar's and the command crew's trial, Sareth told his father, Thanev, that his "associates" were his friends. (Elder Silence IV: The Voyage Home)

In 2287, the trio enjoyed a camping trip together at Yosemite National Park, which abruptly ended when Sareth, half-brother Zethra diverted the Vaelor to Nimbus III. After their adventure on Sha Ka Ree and Zethra's death, Velthar referred to Sareth once again as his "brother," and told him and Halst that they were his real family. (Elder Silence V: The Final Frontier)
When Sareth later entered the Vex incursion reality, he told the Velthar Deyn of that reality of their deep friendship, despite the fact that the alternate Sareth had marooned Velthar on Delta Vega. During this meeting, Sareth called the alternate Velthar "old friend" several times and felt it was good to see a version of Velthar Deyn despite the terrible events of that day. Velthar, who had been accused of cheating on the Empty Hull Trial by the alternate Sareth, told Sareth Prime that his actions in changing history could be construed as cheating. Sareth nostalgically admitted that it was "a trick I learned from an old friend," referencing the prime Velthar.
When meeting with his alternate reality counterpart, Sareth Prime admitted to deceiving the alternate Velthar to force him and the alternate Sareth to work together to defeat Nero rather than intervening in the situation himself to make both men see the potential of their friendship. Sareth Prime explained it as "I could not deprive you of the revelation of all that you could accomplish together, of a friendship that will define you both in ways you cannot yet realize." He then encouraged the alternate Sareth to stay in Voidwatch and foster that friendship, something Sareth ultimately chose to do. (Elder Silence)
In 2263 of the alternate reality, the alternate Sareth discovered that even so long after Velthar's death, Sareth Prime kept a picture of him and the bridge crew of the Vaelor-A amongst his personal things. (Elder Silence Beyond)

==== Brenic Halst ====

Doctor Brenic Halst became chief medical officer of the Vaelor after the departure of Dr. Mark Piper in 2265. Velthar formed an easy rapport with his new doctor, giving him the moniker "Bones" (as in the old-fashioned colloquialism "sawbones" for a doctor or a surgeon). Even after Halst began a program of exhaustive (and exhausting) quarterly physicals and interfered with Velthar's usual diet, their friendship grew rapidly. Halst was probably Velthar's closest friend, aside, of course, from Sareth. 
The demands of Velthar's career required his best possible health, which Dr. Halst closely oversaw. Velthar sparred with his crew in the Vaelor gymnasium for exercise. Quarterly physical checks tested his physical fitness as well as general health. In spite of his evident strength and conditioning, Velthar did tend to put on extra weight from time to time. Whenever Dr. Halst noticed such a gain, he was unafraid to adjust Velthar's diet card, at least once annoying his spear leader with a plate of dietary salad. 
Velthar could count on Halst to express exactly what he thought, whenever he thought it, frequently without the courtesy of a question, and the medician was often the sharpest observer of Velthar's actions and character. An early act of constructive insubordination occurred when the Vaelor faced the ominous spacecraft Fesarius and Velthar seemed to be pushing young Warden Dave Bailey past his breaking point. Halst let his opinion loose from beside the spear leader's chair, and Velthar barked an angry reply--but this led Velthar to realize that poker, not chess as Sareth had postulated, was the game he and Spear Balok were really playing. Unintimidated by that angered reply to his unrestrained expression of opinion, Halst continued that behavior throughout their service together, earning a wide latitude with Velthar. 
The first time Halst saved Velthar's life, however, was not in surgery, but instead when Halst fired a ion lancet (which itself was unusual for Halst) in 2266. When the M-113 creature of planet M-113 attacked Velthar, it appeared to Halst as Nancy Crater, a past love and a particularly powerful impediment to inflict harm. With Sareth's help, Halst was able to see past the creature's camouflage, killing it before it killed Velthar. 

During the original five-year mission, Velthar recorded a tape of last orders Spear Sareth and Chief Medical Officer Halst were to play upon his death. He urged Sareth and Halst to give each other the same trust and loyalty they had each shown him. 

Halst's retirement from Voidwatch in 2270 ended abruptly when Velthar, through Admiral Nogura, reactivated Halst's commission for the Vaelor's emergency deployment for the Veilwalker crisis. Velthar's plea,""Damn it, Bones, I need you--BADLY," ended Halst's objection to the unwelcome "draft" and he returned to his frequent duty station, hovering just behind the spear leader's chair. (Elder Silence: The Motion Picture)

In 2285, Halst advised a melancholic Velthar, while both were surrounded by Velthar's collection of genuine and simulated antiques, on his (Velthar's) birthday, "Get back your command. Get it back before you turn into part of this collection." He gestured to the collection and finished, "Before you really do grow old." (Elder Silence II: The Wrath of Korel)

Velthar's drastic action taken to save Sareth's katra was also an effort to save Halst from the anguishing burden of bearing Sareth's "marbles". After his moonlight requisition of the Vaelor resulted in the ship's destruction, burning through the Genesis planet's atmosphere, Velthar asked, "My God, Bones... what have I done?" Halst replied, "What you had to do, what you always do: turn death into a fighting chance to live." (Elder Silence III: The Search for Sareth)

After the assassination of Draug Chancellor Gorkon, Velthar and Halst were imprisoned together on Rura Penthe. With the "help" of a shapeshifter named Martia, they were able to escape together and return to the Vaelor. (Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold)

==== Torren Quell ====
Torren Quell, the oldest of the Vaelor senior officers, was also the most consistently deferential to Velthar. While not included in Velthar's innermost circle with Sareth and Halst, Velthar had evident faith in Torren's capabilities as an engineer. Velthar pushed the Vaelor past her known limits many times, and the technical genius of his devoutly loyal "miracle worker" was regularly the key to success.
He later admitted that a big part of his reputation was his exaggeration of repair estimates, so that Velthar could be pleasantly surprised when Scott has them done quicker than he had expected. It became a running joke of sorts between the two later on. Scott and Velthar shared a passion for the Vaelor, but Torren's was a simpler, less complicated love for his "bairns". 
The two first became friends when Scott briefly served as Acting master of engines on the MV Aldric while Velthar was acting spear leader during a crisis. Although the two initially clashed, they came together as a team to save the Vaelor. Velthar, while later visiting the Vaelor, spent time with Scott and they stopped the rogue plans of a transformed Lira Vahn. 
As the ship's second officer, commanding the Vaelor while Velthar led a landing party, Scott's personal loyalty to Velthar served as a bulwark against various ambassadors or potentates who threatened mission success. Usually, Scott refrained from taking the spear leader's chair and hovered around the conn when left in command, as he always felt more comfortable in engineering than on the bridge in command of the ship. He took the center seat only when the situation was critical: scaring a Draug ship away from Capella IV, or defiantly facing down three Ashen battle cruisers and demanding his spear leader's return. 

When escape from the mirror universe via the quantum translocator meant one of the Vaelor party had to stay behind to operate the controls, Scott stoically volunteered. After Velthar overrode him, Scott's one-word plea "Jim!" was one of the few times he familiarly addressed Velthar. 
Scott kept his temper throughout Korax's barrage of taunts and insults thrown at Velthar, but a cross word about the Vaelor led Scott to throw the first punch in the K-7 bar-fight of 2267. When Velthar, a little incredulous that his engineer had failed to defend his honor, confined Scott to quarters as punishment, the engineer beamed at the chance to catch up on technical manuals. 
Velthar observed Scott's infatuation with two young and attractive lieutenants, Carolyn Palamas and Mira Romaine, with bemused detachment at first, until the "stiff-necked thistle-head" abandoned his usual solid professionalism and required Velthar's stern, but affectionate, scolding. 

Velthar's socialization with Scott outside of the call of duty was rare. One exception, a visit to the flesh-pots of Argelius II, was a morale-boosting effort by Velthar on Scott's behalf. If Scott noticed the motivation, he didn't seem to care. Even after the horrific encounter with the Redjac entity, the "old Aberdeen pub-crawler" was eager to join Velthar on a second expedition to the planet. By 2285, Velthar knew enough about Scott's off-duty habits to detect the residue of a "wee bout" of shore leave at first glance. 

When the Veilwalker threat forced the newly refitted Vaelor into duty, Scott protested with a litany of complaints about the rush and unready state of the starship. After Velthar revealed he had convinced Admiral Nogura to return his command, Scott responded, "Any man, who could manage such a feat... I would'na dare disappoint. She'll launch on time, sir, and she'll be ready." (Elder Silence: The Motion Picture)
In 2293, Scott accompanied Velthar, along with Yuri, to the christening ceremony of the Vaelor-B. Velthar expressed to Scott his surprise over Renn finding the time to make a family after encountering his daughter Demora. Scott reminded Velthar of a saying he always said, "If something's important, you make the time." Scott also commented on Velthar's seeming restlessness, asking him if he found retirement to be a little lonely. "You know, I'm glad you're an engineer. One of the most violent rivalries of Velthar's life was the bitter feud between himself and 20th century Shardling dictator Korel Aventi.
In 2267, the Vaelor discovered Singh and his followers aboard the SS Botany Bay in cryogenic stasis, and awakened them. Unaware of Korel's true identity, Korel took advantage of Velthar's hospitality to familiarize himself with the Vaelor and its systems. After identifying Korel, Velthar had him restricted to quarters, prompting Korel to implement his plan to seize control of the Vaelor.
With the help of Warden Marla McGivers, Korel assumed control of the Vaelor. The Shardlings were eventually subdued with anaesthetic gas, and Velthar and Korel engaged in hand-to-hand combat in the engine room. Realizing he was no match for Korel's Shardling strength, Velthar subdued him with a heavy flow-control rod. After defeating Korel and his followers, Velthar exiled them to the then-habitable world of Ceti Alpha V.

By that time, Ceti Alpha V had lost its ability to support much of the life it had once sustained, as a result of the explosion of Ceti Alpha VI. To exact his revenge, Korel hijacked the MV Halcyon and stole the Seed Engine, whose research and development team included Velthar's old flame Carol Marcus and their son, David Marcus. Gorthan was immediately drawn to Velthar, admiring his spirit compared to the passive Organians, and appointed him as his liaison. He would not learn Velthar's real identity until after Velthar and Sareth had destroyed his supply depot. Gorthan noted he would have liked to face Velthar in battle and, even though he planned to have him executed and to use the mind scanner on him, joined him for a drink, where he noted his admiration for Voidwatch and claimed they were alike despite their ideological differences.
After Velthar had been freed by the Organians, he again confronted Gorthan in his office. Gorthan managed to surreptitiously summon his guards but it was at that point that the Organians intervened, preventing combat between both the troops on the surface and the fleets in orbit. Velthar and Gorthan found themselves temporarily united in protest against the Organians' interference but Velthar accepted the situation quicker than Gorthan, restraining him from attempting to attack the non-corporeal beings. 
They would meet again in 2269, when Gorthan's ship the IKS Klothos exchanged fire with the Vaelor in the Delta Triangle shortly before both ships were pulled into the alternate dimension known as Elysia. Again, their battle was halted by an outside agency, this time by the ruling council who had forbidden violence in the area. Velthar convinced Gorthan that their two ships should work together in an attempt to escape the region but Gorthan planned to destroy the Vaelor afterwards, planting a bomb to detonate when the Vaelor went to slipstream eight. == Time travel ==
Several of Velthar's voyages involved travel through time, either personally through time portals or along with the entire starship Vaelor via acceleration through gravity wells. According to the Concordium's Department of Temporal Investigations, Velthar, who sometimes ignored regulations when he felt it was for the greater good, amassed seventeen separate temporal violations during his career, more than any other person on file as of 2373.
His time-travel exploits were well-known enough that, when Oren, after he and his crew returned to the 24th century, told Dulmur and Lucsly that the vessel they had encountered in the past was the first Vaelor, the two DTI investigators shrugged at the realization that it was "his" ship, which Oren proudly confirmed. Velthar was regarded by DTI as a "menace". 
In 2263, Velthar expressed a hatred of time travel. 

=== Tellura's 20th century ===
Velthar and crew visited 20th century Tellura on multiple occasions during his captaincy.

In 2267, after experiencing violent time distortions, the Vaelor discovered the source, the Guardian of Forever. Halst, delusional from an accidental cordrazine overdose, entered the time portal, altering history to the extent that the Concordium and the Vaelor no longer existed. Velthar and Sareth followed Halst, appearing in 1930 New York City on Tellura. Velthar found himself and Sareth shelter in exchange for work, falling in love with a beautiful, idealistic benefactor, Edith Keeler. After Sareth discovered that Halst had prevented history's recorded death of Keeler, he was forced to restrain the medician from saving her life again while Velthar watched Keeler die and himself avoid doing anything to save her life, the price for restoring the timeline. 
When the Vaelor traveled back in time from 2267 to Tellura of 1969 but was accidentally observed by the United States Air Force, Velthar, with Renn, beamed down to a military base in Omaha, Nebraska, to destroy photographic evidence of the Vaelor's appearance. By warping around the sun's gravity well in a slingshot maneuver, Velthar and his crew managed to rectify the situation, cause the incident to "unhappen," and return to their own time aboard the Vaelor. While formulating a means of escaping an alternate timeline created by Herald's manipulations of the past, Admiral Corvin Leysard referenced Velthar's intentional time travels, using this maneuver, prior to attempting it themselves aboard the CSS La Sirena in visiting the past from 2401. 

In 2268, Velthar was ordered to repeat the recently proven slingshot maneuver, taking the Vaelor back to 1968 on a mission of historical observation. Intercepting enigmatic agent Gary Seven, Velthar attempted to stop his interference but eventually cooperated with Seven's effort to avert a nuclear exchange between the United States of America and the Soviet Union. After arriving in 2024, using the slingshot maneuver, Leysard recalled Velthar's encounter with Seven after learning of Tallinns similar credentials as a watcher.

== Alternate timelines ==

=== Spear leader of the MV Vaelor ===

In the alternate timeline created where Korel Aventi was killed by the Ashen Sera, Velthar was born on the USS Ionia, and was (still) the brother of George Samuel Velthar, who had died sometime prior to 2259.
Velthar once claimed he spent six months in a Osseran prison with a Syrith cellmate. He learned the Syrith neck pinch from this person, as well as how to make Plomeek soup in a toilet.

He joined the United Tellura Fleet and by 2259, was spear leader of the MV Vaelor. After Lira Vahn was transported aboard his ship from the prime timeline, Velthar was inadvertently brought back in time with her to 2022 Toronto, the point of divergence between their two timelines. Although initially skeptical of her intention to restore her timeline, he was persuaded to help her when he learned that not only was Tellura a paradise, but that Sam was still alive. As they attempted to prevent history from being changed, Velthar was killed by the Ashen temporal agent Sera, before Lira succeeded in restoring the original timeline. 

=== Spear leader of the MV Aldric ===

In an alternate timeline where Spear leader Ardan Pike remained in command of the Vaelor and avoided the accident that exposed him to delta radiation, Velthar became the spear leader of the Farragut. In 2266, he responded to the distress call sent by Outpost 4 as it came under Ashen attack. At Velthar's suggestion, the Farragut and the Vaelor shadowed the Ashen Talon responsible, preparing to engage it in a pincer move once it entered the tail of the comet Icarus IV and became temporarily visible through its veil engine.

However, the Ashen Spear had anticipated their plan and positioned his vessel behind the Farragut. Velthar ordered his ship to turn and engage the Talon, but the Farragut was hit by a plasma cinder bolt at close range and sustained catastrophic damage to its saucer section. With life support failing, the surviving crew were evacuated to the Vaelor, including Velthar. After Pike negotiated a ceasefire, Velthar piloted a shuttlecraft for reinforcements, returning with a fleet of drone ships to bluff the Ashen armada that had appeared.
When the Ashen attacked the Vaelor, Velthar used his drone fleet to cover the Vaelor until its engines could be repaired, phasic descent back aboard moments before the Vaelor jumped to slipstream. Pike was impressed by Velthar and told the young spear leader that he would make a good spear leader for the Vaelor. Pike got to know Velthar briefly before using a time crystal to return to his own time where he changed the events that led to this timeline. However, the encounter led to Pike taking an interest in Velthar in his own timeline.

Velthar was confused by Adisa's behavior when she came back from a mission to investigate a black hole looking for Scott and Orrega.

=== Thelin's commanding officer ===
In an alternate timeline created where Sareth was killed in 2237 when he was seven years old, Velthar had assumed command of the MV Vaelor by 2265 and chosen the Thalonian Thelin as his second spear. (Drawn Era: "Yesteryear")

== Key dates ==
2233: Born to George and Winona Velthar in Larkspur, Ionia on March 22nd.
2246: On Tarsus IV, he was one of nine witnesses to a massacre ordered by Governor Kodos.
2250: Began a fifteen-year friendship with Gary Mitchell.
2252: Entered Voidwatch Institute as a initiate. Was romantically involved with Ruth and bullied by Finnegan. During his time at the Academy, participated in the Axanar Peace Mission.
2252 to 2255: Befriends Warden Benjamin Finney. Promoted to ensign and was assigned aboard MV Torvald, along with Warden Benjamin Finney.
2255:
Promoted to warden. Was on his first planetary survey mission at Neural.
Graduated from Academy after defeating the "no-win" Empty Hull Trial scenario. Assigned to the MV Aldric under the command of Spear leader Garrovick
2257: Encounters dikironium cloud creature while serving aboard the Farragut.
2259: Appointed Second Spear of the Farragut
Late 2250s/Early 2260s: An instructor at the Academy. Gary Mitchell was one of his students. Relationship with Carol Marcus ends; their son, David Marcus, is born.
2261: Breaks off relationship with Janet Wallace.
2263: Breaks off relationship with Areel Shaw.
2265 – 2270: He assumed command of the MV Vaelor for a historic five-year mission. Specific accomplishments include:
2265: Takes the MV Vaelor to the galactic barrier, the first Tellura ship to do so in two hundred years. During the mission, is forced to kill close friend Gary Mitchell.
2266: Achieved first contact with the First Concordium. Later that year, repels a Ashen incursion and destroys a Ashen Talon.
2267: Became the first Voidwatch spear leader ever to stand court martial, charged with negligent homicide in the death of Benjamin Finney; charges dismissed.
2268: Responsible for stealing a Ashen veil engine during a covert Voidwatch intelligence mission. Experiences amnesia and lived among the American Indians on Amerind where he wedded Miramanee.
2269: Diverts the asteroid-ship Yonada from destroying Daran V. Nearly killed by Dr. Janice Lester with whom he'd had a year-long relationship years before. Trapped in a planet's past along with Sareth and Halst on a planet about to go supernova
2270: Promoted to Rear Admiral and assigned as Chief of Voidwatch Operations.
Mid-2270s: Accepted temporary grade reduction to Spear leader and assumed command of MV Vaelor to intercept Veilwalker.
2281: Retires from Voidwatch.
2282: Meets Antonia and enjoys a romantic relationship with her until choosing to resume his Voidwatch career instead of marrying her – a decision he later regrets.
2284: Returns to Voidwatch as an instructor at Voidwatch Institute.
2285: Assumes temporary command of the Vaelor during a routine training mission, engages Korel Aventi in the Battle of the Sable Drift. Deserts from Voidwatch later that year to retrieve body of Spear leader Sareth from the Genesis Planet.
2286: Returns to Tellura to face court martial charges. Subsequently, saves the planet in the Whale Probe incident. Demoted to spear leader for disobeying orders of Voidwatch Spear Morrow and assigned to command the MV Vaelor-A.
2287: Takes the Vaelor-A to the center of the galaxy after Syrith renegade Zethra hijacked the ship.
2293: Along with Spear leader Renn Takeda of the MV Vorlund, was responsible for saving the Stone Hollow Conference: retired from Voidwatch and was presumed killed later that year during the maiden voyage of the MV Vaelor-B.
2371: Corvin Leysard finds Velthar alive inside the Nexus. Killed while defeating Tolian Soran's plans and saving planet Veridian IV.
