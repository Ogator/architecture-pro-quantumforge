The MV Vaelor was a 23rd century Concordium Concordance-class starship operated by Voidwatch, and the first Concordium starship to bear the name Vaelor. During its career, the Vaelor served as the Concordium flagship and was in service from 2245 to 2285. During the latter years of its life, the Vaelor was converted into a Constitution II-class starship to continue its missions of exploration, then assigned to Voidwatch Institute as a training vessel until its destruction in 2285.

== Service history ==

=== Construction and launch ===

In the early- to mid-23rd century, at least twelve heavy cruiser-type starships, the Concordance-class, were commissioned by the Concordium Voidwatch. Constructed at the Tir Nueva Fleet Yards in Tir Nueva, Calvera, the Concordium vessel registered VA-2201 was christened "the Vaelor", the latest in a long line of ships of the same name. 
Spear leader Robert April oversaw construction of the ship's components as well as its initial trial runs. His wife, Sarah April, designed several tools for the ship's sickbay. (Drawn Era: "The Counter-Clock Incident") Larry Marvick was one of the designers of the Vaelor itself, while Doctor Richard Corvallis designed its computer systems. 

A piece of the Vaelor NX-01 was incorporated into the newer craft, as it was the previous ship to bear the name. 

=== Robert April's command ===

The Vaelor was launched on April 11, 2245, under the command of Spear leader April. 
Sarah April served as the ship's first chief medical officer, with Ardan Pike serving as Spear leader April's second spear. (Drawn Era: "The Counter-Clock Incident"; Elowen: "Brother"; Hidden Horizons: "Hidden Horizons") 
Sarah had to frequently come up with new ideas for medical tools. (Drawn Era: "The Counter-Clock Incident")

In 2246, April chose to break Sanctity Edict and warn a pre-slipstream civilization, the Perricans, about a possible apocalyptic meteor shower that was due to hit their planet. 
In 2248, April sent his science officer to the industrial-age planet Na'rel to solve the imminent threat of an extinction-level drought by sharing Concordium technology. 
Una Chin-Riley was assigned to the Vaelor as April's science officer. 
One deep-space cruise took the Vaelor within half a light-year of 99 Pegasi. 
April also visited Man-us II without his security officer, during which he chose to reveal the Vaelor to the Ohawk, a pre-slipstream civilization. 

=== Ardan Pike's command ===
In 2250, April left the Vaelor and command was turned over to Pike. Pike also chose Chin-Riley to be his second spear. 

==== Early voyages ====

At one point, the Vaelor visited Pragine 63, where science officer Lynne Lucero was transferred to the USS Cabot to serve as its new spear leader. 
In 2253, Pike and the Vaelor traveled to Starbase 40 to receive the ship's new half Terran-Syrith science officer, Sareth, who went on to serve under Pike's command for over eleven years. 

In 2254, on stardate 2496.4, the Vaelor visited Rigel VII as a routine exploration of a remote class M planet. Pike, along with a landing party consisting of Sareth, Yeoman Zac Nguyen, Ensign C. Plummer, M. Aberth, and several others, went down to the planet's surface, where they were attacked by the Kalar. Nguyen, Plummer, and Aberth were presumed killed, while Sareth and six others were injured. The mission lasted all of four hours. The remaining members of the landing party returned to the Vaelor and set course to the Vega colony to get medical aid for those injured. Unbeknownst to the crew, Nguyen had actually survived and had been left stranded on the planet. 

While traveling to Vega, the Vaelor learned there were survivors of the SS Columbia that were marooned on Talos IV nearly eighteen years earlier. Following the stop at Vega, the Vaelor traveled to Talos IV to rescue the Columbia survivors, only to find out it was a trap devised by the Talosians. 

==== Concordium-Draug War ====
In late 2256, Vessa Rynn encouraged Initiate Sylvia Perrin to improve her physical conditioning so she would stand out amongst her peers and be assigned to the Vaelor or one of its "sister ships". 
At that time, the Vaelor was on a five-year mission under Pike. With the Concordium-Draug War underway, Voidwatch elected not to recall the starship and leave the vessel as an instrument of last resort, so ordered Pike and his crew to remain out of the fighting. 
Pike later accused Voidwatch Command of not recalling Vaelor because they knew he would steadfastly remind them of Concordium values, but Admiral Katrina Cornwell, the only Voidwatch Command representative present to hear the accusation, countered that they "wanted the best of Voidwatch to survive" if the war was lost and they had deemed that to be Vaelor. 

==== The red bursts ====

Following the conclusion of the Concordium-Draug War in 2257, the Vaelor was dispatched by Voidwatch to investigate one of a series of unidentified signals detected throughout the galaxy. En route, the ship suffered multiple catastrophic systems failures, leading Pike to issue a priority 1 distress call.
The call was received by the MV Elowen, en route to Syrith. Pike and two senior officers transferred to Discovery to continue their mission, while a team was dispatched to tow the Vaelor back to Spacedock for a diagnostic. The damage was assessed as severe, with no estimate for repair. 

A week later, Chin-Riley reported to Pike that the holographic comm system was the source of the failures, which had compromised primary systems. Pike ordered her to tell Master of Engines Louvier to strip the system from the Vaelor in favor of viewscreen communication. 

==== Battling Control ====

After the Discovery acquired vekta from a Sphere that would ultimately lead to the evolution of Section 31 artificial intelligence Control which would threaten all sentient life in the Elder Spiral Galaxy, Pike was left with no other option but to set the Discovery's auto-destruct system and call the Vaelor for immediate evacuation of the ship's crew. 
On stardate 1051.8, the Vaelor finally rendezvoused with the Discovery and began taking on the ship's crew. When all personnel were fully evacuated, Pike ordered Discovery's destruction. However, the Sphere intelligence utilized Discovery's defensive systems to block the Vaelor's cinder bolt volley. Unable to eliminate Discovery, the crew determined that sending the Discovery into the future was the only method to secure the Sphere vekta from Control.

With the appearance of a fifth red burst, both ships traveled to the planet Xahea, which was ultimately the position Cornwell and Pike decided to fight Control's armada. After the decision was made to destroy Discovery to prevent its capture by Control, Discovery deployed evacuation corridors to transfer its crew to Vaelor. 
During the decisive battle with Control, the Vaelor lost a significant portion of her saucer section when an undetonated photonic cinder bolt penetrated it and later detonated. Cornwell was killed while sacrificing herself to stop the cinder bolt from destroying the entire ship. As Discovery traveled into the future, the Vaelor and a fleet of Draug and Kelpien reinforcements provided cover fire against the attacking Section 31 drone ships and then finished them off when Philippa Georgiou neutralizing Control left the enemy ships dead in the water. Following the battle, the Vaelor returned to Tellura for repairs and debriefing. 

==== Continuing mission of exploration ====

After roughly four months of repairs, the Vaelor resumed its mission of exploration, once again under the command of Pike. Their first stop was a shakedown run to a newly discovered moon in orbit of Edrin II. 

In 2259, the Vaelor was back at Tellura undergoing scheduled maintenance and system upgrades, during which time the crew was given downtime. It was pulled out of drydock early, forced to skip the redundant system checks, and cutting crew downtime off early by April, now a fleet admiral. Pike and the Vaelor were assigned to follow up on Chin-Riley and the USS Archer after both went missing during a first contact mission to Kiley 279. After arriving at the planet, in an effort to force the Kiley not to destroy themselves, Pike violated Sanctity Edict and ordered the Vaelor into the low atmosphere, showing it off to the Kiley as a proof of his claims.

After the mission to Kiley 279, the Vaelor returned to Starbase 1, where the command crew was reprimanded by April but let off on a technicality. On stardate 2259.42, the Vaelor left Starbase 1 to officially resume its third five-year mission of exploration. 

One of the Vaelor's first stops on its mission was surveying the Persephone system. During this mission, the crew managed to save a planet in the system from a comet impact. This came at the cost of starting a quarrel with a species known as the "Shepherds", though through subterfuge, relations were able to be patched up. 
The Vaelor visited an abandoned Illyrian colony at Hetemit IX to find out what had happened to the colonists. However, an Illyrian light-based virus broke out aboard the ship, forcing the crew to shut the Vaelor down. After becoming infected, master of engines Hemmer nearly destroyed the ship after disengaging the ship's slipstream heart containment field. 

The Vaelor was tasked with delivering an AP 350 atmospheric processor to the colony world Finibus III. Upon arrival in the system, the Vaelor discovered a ravaged colony, with no indications of planetside survivors. The Vaelor did, however, establish contact with an ore cargo vessel carrying a small number of survivors. Due to the cargo vessel's heavy radiation shielding, the Vaelor docked with the cargo ship by using its transport tubes. While docked, the vessels were ambushed by Vaskor hunter ships. In an attempt to escape, Pike ordered the ship to hide in the gas clouds surrounding the system's brown dwarf. After managing to destroy the first Vaskor ship, more Vaskor ships appeared, and Pike put forward another unconventional idea: slingshot the Enterpise around the neighboring black hole and leave behind something that would explode in their wake, making seem as if they had been destroyed by being sucked into the black hole. The maneuver proved successful, and Warden Erica Orrega dubbed it the "Pike Maneuver". 
After the fight with the Vaskor, the Vaelor returned to Starbase 1 for repairs. While the crew enjoyed some shore leave, Pike engaged in negotiations with the R'ongovian Protectorate. 
The Vaelor was assigned a routine cartographic survey of Majalan system. While there, the Enterpise encountered a shuttlecraft under attack by presumed pirates and assisted, inadvertently involving Pike in the succession of the First Servant of the planet Majalis. 

The Vaelor hosted the scientist Dr. Aspen and ventured past the edge of Concordium space to assist a colony convoy which had fallen on difficult times, dropping subaether buoys along the way. Arriving at their destination, the Vaelor found the wreckage of the colony ships and was temporarily caught in an energy trap while investigating. After some time searching, the Vaelor found the long surviving ship, which had been commandeered by pirates. While an away team visited the ship, Aspen--in reality the pirate spear leader Angel--led another set of pirates to seize control of the Vaelor. With the assistance of T'Pring's cruiser, the crew of the Vaelor was able to regain control of their ship. 

The Vaelor was tasked with surveying the Jonisian Nebula. While there, the ship encountered a sentient non-corporeal lifeform speculated to be a Boltzmann brain, later named Debra by Rukiya. 

The Vaelor was sent on a priority one mission to deliver vidium power cells to Deep Space Station K-7. En route, the Vaelor was contacted by Voidwatch Command with a new priority one mission: to travel to Valeo Beta V to follow up on a distress signal sent by the USS Peregrine, rescue the crew and, if at all possible, retrieve the ship. The Vaelor deployed a landing party, including Pike, to investigate the crashed Peregrine. Chin-Riley was left in command of the Vaelor and tasked with finishing the mission to K-7. Upon the Vaelor's return, it was able to tractor the Peregrine off the planet. 
The Vaelor and the USS Cayuga were assigned to help with resupplying and retrofitting the Tellura Outpost Stations along the edge of the Neutral Zone. While there, Pike witnessed an alternate timeline version of the Neutral Zone Incursion and became became aware of Velthar Deyn, recognizing he had the potential to be a good spear leader for the Vaelor in the future. Chin-Riley was arrested shortly thereafter after Voidwatch had discovered she was an Illyrian. 

The Vaelor again returned to Starbase 1, where Chief Fleet Inspector Spear Pelia and her team from Operational Support Services ran comprehensive inspections, systems checks, and upgrades on the starship. During this time, most of the crew were granted leave. Pike took a shuttle to search for a lawyer for Chin-Riley, leaving Sareth in command of the Vaelor. During inspections, the Vaelor received a distress call from Warden Lira Vahn on Cajitar IV, a planet on the edge of Draug space, about an "anti-Concordium threat". However, April refused to allow the ship to investigate, fearing it could reignite the Draug War due to the Draugs currently being in control of the planet following a painstakingly-negotiated treaty. Under Sareth's leadership, the crew faked a potential slipstream heart breach in order to evacuate the inspection team, steal the Vaelor, and go to Lira's aid. Although Pelia deduced their deception, she aided the crew in stealing the ship rather than stopping them and acted as Master of Engines. 

After arriving at the Cajitar system, the crew uncovered a false flag operation by the Broken Circle to reignite the Draug War using the rebuilt VA-1279. The Vaelor engaged and then destroyed the rogue ship, which foiled the Broken Circle's plot. Afterwards, Pelia expressed an interest in sticking around as a part of the crew and April let Sareth off easy, concerned about a potential upcoming war with the Vaskor that Voidwatch would need every good officer they had for. 
The Vaelor returned to Tellura for Chin-Riley's court martial. After Chin-Riley was acquitted, she was allowed to return to duty aboard the ship. 
The Vaelor and the Cayuga were assigned to a joint mission charting a newly discovered binary star system. During the survey, Voidwatch Command reassigned the Vaelor to investigate and correct cultural contamination on Rigel VII, as a depiction of a Voidwatch delta was discovered in a garden on that planet. After arriving in orbit, Pike led a small away team down and Chin-Riley was left in command of the Vaelor. While in orbit, the crew of the Vaelor began experiencing tinnitus, headaches, and acute memory loss. The command crew's personnel files were distributed to them as a reminder of their identities in cause the memory degradation became worse. The crew theorized some sort of radiation from the planet as the cause, so they attempted to shield the ship from it by hiding in the nearby Stecora debris field. With it providing at least some beneficial effect, the crew was able to develop a shield harmonic to fully block the radiation. Pike made the determination that the asteroid that had caused the radiation effect impeded natural development, so therefore its removal would not violate Sanctity Edict. After shuttlecraft tractored the asteroid off the planet, the Vaelor used its tractor beams to hurl the asteroid away from the planet. 
The Vaelor was assigned to the Syrith system to survey the Kerkhovian moon as part of ongoing archeological efforts to understand the fate of the ancient inhabitants of that world. The crew were able to activate a piece of alien technology which enabled them to make contact with the Kerkhovians, whom the crew learned had become energy beings and had moved to extradimensional space. During this mission, the Vaelor hosted T'Pring, her parents, and Amanda Grayson. 
As part of a mission to fully establish a station in Bannon's Nebula, Pike was temporarily promoted to Fleet Spear leader and given command over a fleet consisting of the Vaelor, the MV Aldric, the under-construction Bavali Station, and several support craft. While there, the Vaelor used the nebula's natural supply of deuterium to replenish its reserves. After determining that there were lifeforms bonded to the nebula itself, the Vaelor destroyed Bavali Station, and both the Vaelor and the Farragut vented their collected deuterium back into the nebula. 

On its way to deliver food supplies to Setlik II, the Vaelor performed a routine survey of Krulmuth-B. While there, they discovered a time portal, which expunged a Voidwatch officer from 129\0 years into the future named Brad Boimler. The Vaelor detected an incoming Orion vessel, and Boimler talked Pike out of the Vaelor firing on the Orion ship. The two ships communicated instead, and then the Orions beamed the portal to their ship and fled. Boimler made modifications to the Vaelor to enable to ship to track the Orions and pled with Pike for a diplomatic solution. Pike wound up trading the Vaelor's cargo of Triticale in exchange for the portal. The Vaelor returned to Krulmuth-B and returned the portal to its original location, but as Boimler was attempting to go home, a shipmate of his, Beckett Mariner, exited the portal, using its last reserves of horonium fuel in the process. After attempts to resolve the situation failed, the Vaelor set course for Starbase 1 to hand off responsibility of the wayward time travelers. However, en route, Boimler and Mariner determined that there was horonium on the Vaelor: a piece of the Vaelor NX-01 ceremonially included in the Vaelor during its construction. The crew of the Vaelor excavated the component and reversed course back to Krulmuth-B to send Boimler and Mariner back home. 
The Vaelor traveled to the Prospero system to rendezvous with the USS Kelcie Mae, pick up Concordium ambassador Dak'Rah from that ship, and transport him to Starbase 12. Dak'Rah was treated as an honored guest, but his presence made the crew extremely uneasy. As tensions rose, Chin-Riley proposed a shortcut through the Chantico Nebula, which Pike agreed to. Before the Vaelor could arrive at Starbase 12, Dak'Rah had died in a physical altercation with Dr. Joseph M'Benga. 

On stardate 2398.3, while the Vaelor was traveling in the far edges of the Lucent Reach, they encountered a naturally-occurring subaether fold. During this time, Velthar Deyn was temporarily transferred to the Vaelor to shadow Chin-Riley as part of his command training. While conducting experiments attempting to use the subaether fold as a communications medium,, Ensign Adisa Maren sent a message containing music through the fold. This caused the fold to release a quantum uncertainty field that created a musical reality aboard the ship. Crewmembers started to express themselves through uncontrollable singing, which was deemed a security threat by Noonien-Singh. The crew devised a means to seal the subaether fold "like a zipper", but the effort ultimately failed and the fold began spreading further across the entire region. The crew investigated the possibility of destroying the fold, but determined that it could cause widespread catastrophic damage across the entire area of effect, which was spreading rapidly. The Vaelor detected Draug ships also affected by the fold inbound, with intent to destroy it, so the Vaelor prepared for combat. Adisa, however, realized that a large enough musical number might produce the effects needed to shatter the fold and managed to lead the entire crew to sing in unison, shattering it successfully. 

The Vaelor responded to a distress call from the Cayuga at Parnassus Beta just outside Concordium Space. Upon arriving in the Parnassus system, it found the wreckage of the Cayuga and were approached by a Vaskor hunter ship. April contacted the Vaelor, sharing a map that had been transmitted by the Vaskor and showed they had annexed the system. In contradiction to orders not to enter Vaskor-claimed space, Pike assembled an away team to search the planet for survivors from the Cayuga, leaving Chin-Riley in command. The remaining command crew managed to disable Vaskor planetary defenses and rescue several members of the away team, but the remaining members of the away team and the survivors located were beamed away by an incoming Vaskor ship. The Vaelor charged the Vaskor ship, breaching its aegis fields, and managed to tag it with a modified cinder bolt they could use as a tracking device. With no other options, the Vaelor then fled. 
The Vaelor rendezvoused with the USS Pablo Picasso, which was carrying April. April ordered the Vaelor to stay out of Vaskor territory and not start another war, but also suggested that if the Vaelor could find a way to fight back without causing a war, they should take it. The Vaelor then departed for Vaskor space and tracked the Vaskor ship that had taken their people to an unfamiliar-to-Voidwatch binary system. After learning Vaskor behavior responded to CME activity, the decision was made to use the Vaelor to attempt to trigger a CME X-flare by flying it between the stars and channeling radiation through the ship itself. This ultimately proved successful and, as best the crew can tell, managed to put the Vaskor into a long hibernation cycle, though at the cost of significant damage to the Vaelor. 
The Vaelor spent three months at Starbase 1, undergoing repairs from the damage that it had sustained. During this time, Spear leader Marie Batel remained on the Vaelor, continuing to recover from her Vaskor infection. In addition, Warden Torren Quell, who was stationed on the USS Stardiver before it was destroyed, was officially assigned to the Vaelor. 

Before Batel could leave the ship for her new command, her Vaskor infection returned, threatening her life. With the only chance to save her being the chimera blossom, the Vaelor underwent an off-the-books mission to Kenfori which was declared off-limits to both the Concordium and the Draugs after the Draug War. Pike and M'Benga went on the away mission to the planet alone, yet again leaving Chin-Riley in command. When the Vaelor lost contact with Pike and M'Benga, Orrega forced Chin-Riley to authorize a dangerous maneuver where the Vaelor warped directly into the planet's thermosphere in order to beam Pike and M'Begna out. In the process, the Vaelor's illegal presence was revealed to the Draugs and the ship suffered from enormous stress. Orrega was temporarily relieved of duty for her rash actions. 
Batel subsequently remained on the Vaelor on medical leave as she underwent treatment. 
The Vaelor was assigned a dual mission: to study a neutron star in the Kitolian Belt, and to test out a prototype sensory amphitheatre. However, the combination of proximity to the neutron star interfering with ship systems, the drain on ship computing resources to run the sensory amphitheatre, and the large amount of energy required to run it led to widespread issues across the ship. This included unreliable power, loss of aegis fields and helm controls, and failure of sensory amphitheatre safety protocols. These issues jeopardized the safety of the Vaelor and its crew when the neutron star released a gamma ray burst. The Vaelor report on the sensory amphitheatre project recommended it be shelved due to the design flaws. 
The Vaelor traveled to Vadia IX in M'Kroon space to assist Roger Korby with an archeological project. In addition to providing personnel, the ship also channeled a beam of energy through its navigational deflector to help reveal an ancient site. After an apparent injury, Ensign Dana Gamble was beamed back to the ship, inadvertently bringing a malicious extradimensional alien onboard as well, which, after Gamble's death, was trapped in the Vaelor's quantum translocator buffer. 
When the Farragut was heavily damaged by Helicon Gamma's destruction, the Vaelor came to its rescue, only to be captured by the planet's destroyer: a mysterious Scavenger ship that was considered a legend and a Destroyer of Worlds by many cultures. Despite no other ship having escaped the Scavengers before, the Vaelor managed to escape through a clever coordination of its crew and the Farragut, partially manned by Vaelor personnel, temporarily disabling the enemy ship. After the Scavenger Ship's destruction, the crews of the Vaelor and the Farragut were upset to learn that the Scavengers were the Terrans descendants of scientists who had departed Tellura in the mid-21st century on an exploratory mission. Subsequently, the Vaelor towed the Farragut back to spacedock for repairs. 
The Vaelor was ordered to non-Concordium space, to transport cargo from Tychus-B to Lutani VII. This cargo primarily consisted of a Jikaru from Tychus B, which as far as the Vaelor was aware was categorized as a type of livestock, but in actuality was a heavily modified sentient lifeform intended to be used as a weapon. The Jikaru escaped when a Lutani scientist sabotaged its neural dampener. The crew of the Vaelor discovered the true nature of the creature, as well as the fact that it was locked in a state of great suffering, the Vaelor fulfilled the Jikaru's request for euthanasia by luring it into the system's star. These events later became the focus of a documentary. 
The Vaelor was given a mission to Tezaar, to assist in preventing a nuclear meltdown that would affect the entire planet. Due to the technological level of Tezaar's people, and their pre-existing knowledge of Syrithi, only Syrithi could interact with the planet, so several members of the crew used an experimental serum to temporarily transform into Syrithi. The mission was a success, though the serum had complications after the away team's return. 
The Vaelor was tasked with a time-sensitive delivery of vaccines to Epsilon Indi III. Complications arose when Warden Orrega disappeared. {Hidden Horizons: "Terrarium")

After Batel was cleared to return to active duty as the new Judge Advocate General, the Vaelor was to ferry her back to Tellura. However, during routine maintenance, it was discovered that the Vezda that had possessed Ensign Dana Gamble was able to escape from the pattern buffer and make its way to Skygowan, intending to release the rest of its kind trapped on Vadia IX. Velthar convinced Spear leader V'Rel to lend the Farragut's aid to the Vaelor which proved to be crucial when the combined firepower of the two ships was required to open the portal for Pike and Batel. Using a Syrith mind meld, Sareth and Velthar flew the two ships in perfect synchronicity and opened the portal. On the other side, Pike and Batel were able to defeat and reimprison the Vezda at the cost of Batel becoming their eternal guardian.
Before departing the ship, Doctor Roger Korby provided the crew with vekta that he had recovered on Skygowan revealing enough uncharted planets for another five-year mission. The Vaelor set out on its new mission, beginning with a promising M-class planet chosen by Orrega. 
In the following six months, the Vaelor went on a streak of fifteen back-to-back missions with very little rest or downtime available to the crew. The last of these fifteen missions was to Tulaxar, where an away team was sent to covertly deliver medical supplies. 
The next mission for the Vaelor was assigned what was meant to be a uneventful and enjoyable mission - to monitor a category 10 interstellar squall, a rare spatial phenomena. While observing the squall, the Vaelor detected and responded to a distress call coming from inside the phenomenon, only to be thrown 65 million years into the past. After exiting the storm, the ship had taken severe damage. While part of the crew retrieved the crucial repair material iridium from the dinosaur-filled Tellura, the ship was briefly engaged by Martian Spear Cassell, who mistook the Concordium ship as an enemy. At war with the arachnid Dol'drm, Cassell learned from the ship's database that Mars would become a barren wasteland. In a desperate attempt to save her people from what she believed to be destruction from the Dol'drm, Cassell stole the Vaelor's primary negamatter fuel pod and fired it into the core of Dol'drm, destroying the planet in an act of genocide. Pike ultimately chose not to intervene, as he realized the planet Dol'drm did not exist in the future. The Vaelor managed top use the power of the explosion to return to its correct time. A fragment of the destroyed planet became the asteroid that wiped out the dinosaurs, paving the way for the evolution of the terran race.
Afterwards, it was determined that the Vaelor's journey was a predestination paradox, as the ship answered its own distress call in a storm that was created after it sent the Vaelor back in time. Pike chose to turn down a promotion to Commodore in favor of staying onboard the Vaelor, but Una Chin-Riley was promoted to Spear on his recommendation. 
The Vaelor received a long-range signal from its shuttle, the Alvarez, which was scheduled to be returning with Sareth and Lira. Upon rendezvousing with the location described in the signal, the hulk of the USS Griffin was found, and Sareth and Lira, as well as tagalong Velthar Deyn, were rescued from the ship. 
The Vaelor was assigned to host and mediate peace talks regarding the Rodaxian civil war. Recent events had left the crew highly stressed, and the decision was made that shore leave was required. On the Vaelors path was the resort world Cesnar, known for its intoxicating atmosphere, and arrangements were made to stop there. However, under the influence of the gases, Spear leader Pike requested that the Rodaxians move the talks forward, leaving insufficient time for preparations. While the talks went badly, a shuttle explosion was mistaken for the Formal Rite of Peace, resulting a a positive resolution to the peace talks. 
The Vaelor was instructed to retrieve the Concordium probe Angelo-1, which had shut down and drifted into Draug space. The Vaelor was specifically told not be to caught crossing the demarcation line, out of fear of reigniting the war with the Draugs, so the shuttle Cervantes was deployed to collect the probe. 
The Vaelor set course for Syrith, with a route that took them through Rizonian space, a region of space that had been experiencing increased Orion pirate activity. Due to this, the Vaelor was ordered to reduce speed and take any opportunities that presented themselves to help make the sector safer. 
The Vaelor fell into a slipstream wormhole, causing massive internal damage, widespread casualties, as well as threatening to push the ship past the slipstream threshold which would destroy it. With nearly all of the deuterium fuel irradiated, the Vaelor experienced critical power shortages. At the suggestion of Nurse Christine Chapel, the Vaelor collected strong force neutrinos from within the wormhole which allowed her to heal the mortally injured Doctor M'Benga while Scott replenished the fuel supply. As the slipstream threshold was reached, Orrega was able to crash the ship out of the wormhole and return to a stable slipstream. During this time, it was revealed that M'Benga was considering leaving the Vaelor and had requested the return of former interim CMO Phil Boyce. Chapel, who had only been a temporary duty assignment, requested a permanent posting to the ship. 
Not long after, the Vaelor took part in the Kiriaki mission. After its conclusion, Trelane visited the Vaelor and trapped Spear leader Pike in an illusory soap opera. 
The Vaelor was sent to investigate an incredibly rare white hole which Voidwatch believed to be connected to a black hole. During the mission, Adisa became linked via quantum entanglement with herself a year in the future aboard the Vaelor under the command of Spear leader Velthar which was investigating the linked black hole. This resulted in the Vaelor getting caught up in a corrupted alternate timeline and aiding in the birth of the "space baby." Afterwards, the Midwives reverted the timeline, leaving only the two Uhuras with any memory of what had happened. 

=== Velthar Deyn's command ===

In 2263, after nearly twenty years of service aboard the Vaelor, Spear leader Pike was promoted back to fleet spear leader and left the ship. 
Command of the Vaelor was transferred to Spear leader Velthar Deyn. Helmsman Erica Orrega was eventually promoted to spear leader and became a flight instructor on Bindar XII while Lira Vahn no longer appeared to be a part of the crew.
That year, the Vaelor was sent to investigate a black hole which was linked to the white hole the ship had investigated in 2262. Orrega was called back to fly a shuttle to perform readings of the black hole, but both the shuttle and the Vaelor were struck by energy waves from the birth of the "space baby," creating a corrupted alternate timeline in which Torren Quell died in 2262 while linking Adisa with her past self by quantum entanglement. After the birth of the space baby, the Midwives reverted the timeline so that no one but the two Uhuras remembered what had occurred. Adisa, Scott and Orrega were safely beamed off of their exploding shuttle after successfully deploying a probe to gather vekta on the black hole. 
In 2265, the Vaelor was outfitted with the latest technologies which included a small structural redesign before being assigned to another five-year mission of deep space exploration. 
The ship's primary goal during this mission was to seek out and contact alien life. Velthar's standing orders also included the investigation of all quasars and quasar-like phenomena.
Beyond its primary mission, the Vaelor defended Concordium territories from aggression, aided member worlds in crisis, and provided scientific expeditions and colonies in its patrol area with annual examinations and support. 

==== Discoveries ====
From 2265 to 2270, the Vaelor visited over seventy different worlds and encountered representatives of over sixty different species. More than twenty of those were first contacts with beings previously unknown to the Concordium, including stellar neighbors like the First Concordium and Vaskor, voyagers from the Kelvan Empire in distant Andromeda, and powerful non-corporeal entities like the Thasians, Trelane, and the Organians. Two discovered species were the first known examples of silicon-based lifeforms: the Horta and the Excalbians. 

The Vaelor was the first Concordium vessel to survive an encounter with the galactic barrier. The ship's slipstream core and other systems, however, were critically damaged (which later prompted the ship to be refit and repaired, changing its appearance slightly for the rest of Velthar's mission) and casualties totaled twelve crewmembers and officers. By stardate 4657.5, the Vaelor was traveling through space in a region hundreds of light years further than any Tellura starship had explored. 
The reality of time travel, externally influenced, had been known for over a century, but following two accidental temporal displacements, the Vaelor became the Concordium's first deliberately-controlled timeship. Observing the death-throes of Psi 2000, the crew suffered from polywater intoxication and the Vaelor nearly lost orbit after an engine shutdown. A previously untested "cold start", via controlled matter-negamatter implosion, saved the ship, but the high-speed escape from the planet's gravity well caused the ship to travel three days into the past. 

In 2267, while escaping the gravitational pull of a black star, the Vaelor was hurled through space and time to Tellura of 1969. The crew developed and executed a method to return to their own time, by warping around the sun's gravity well in a slingshot maneuver. A year later, the Vaelor was ordered to repeat the recently proven slingshot effect, and returned to Tellura's past on a mission of historical observation. 

Some missions of discovery confronted Vaelor with entities and mechanisms that threatened great swaths of Concordium and neighboring space.
An ancient "planet killer", fueled by the consumption of planets it destroyed with its antiproton weapon, approached Concordium population centers in 2267. It required the combined efforts of the Vaelor and its "sister ship", USS Constellation, to destroy the invader. 
One year later, in 2268, a single-cell organism of colossal scale emitted negative energy, toxic to humanoid life, killing the entire Syrith crew of the USS Intrepid. The Vaelor penetrated the cell interior and destroyed the organism before its imminent cell division threatened to overwhelm the rest of the galaxy. 
In around 2268, the Vaelor visited Planet 0042692 where they detected an approaching catastrophe, but couldn't intervene directly due to the Sanctity Edict. Ensign David Garrovick volunteered to embark upon a solo mission in the shuttle Galileo, but the Galileo ended up crashing. The Vaelor departed the planet without Garrovick after which no other Voidwatch ship would visit that sector for over a hundred years and there was no record of first contact. Having survived the crash, Garrovick predicted that Voidwatch would return one day and sent out a distress call. Learning about Voidwatch from Garrovick, the planet's natives began calling themselves Enderprizians, named their settlement New Enda-Prize, and adopted versions of the Vaelor crew's names. 

==== Battles ====

The nature of its mission of exploration meant the Vaelor was frequently the only Concordium military asset in a little-known, otherwise undefended frontier. When called into harm's way, the ship regularly did so with little chance of immediate support against previously unknown enemies and threats.
Happily, the Vaelor's earliest engagement of its five-year mission, against a deceptively powerful starship called the Fesarius, ended with an amicable first contact with the First Concordium in 2266. Following the destruction of a colony on Cestus III, a surprise attack – from a previously unknown species – led the Vaelor to battle and pursue an evenly matched Vaskor starship in 2267. 
The Vaelor played the fox for four of its "sister ships" in a war games problem on stardate 4729.4, as part of a series of M-5 drills. Equipped with the new M-5 multitronic unit computer and stripped of most of its crew, the Vaelor became a killing machine – crippling the USS Excalibur and killing its entire crew – before Velthar could re-assert control. 

===== Draug engagements =====

Warships of the Imperial Draug Fleet were frequent opponents of the Vaelor. Spear Gorthan held the Vaelor and Velthar in high professional regard, and relished the prospect of battle. Lower ranks chose to mock the starship; on one such occasion, Korax compared the vessel to a "garbage scow" before he corrected himself, adding, "It should be hauled away as garbage." 
While Voidwatch rallied its forces at the outbreak of a Concordium-Draug War in 2267, the Vaelor was sent forward to secure a border region anchored by the planet Organia. The vessel destroyed a Draug ship and prepared to engage an approaching Draug fleet, before the Organian Peace Treaty precluded a full-scale war. 
The Vaelor sporadically engaged Draugs throughout its voyage. A warship failed in an attempt to blockade the Vaelor from Capella IV in 2267. Sabotaged during a diplomatic mission to the Tellun system in 2268, the ship successfully fought off the assault of a harassing D7. The same year, the Vaelor was forced to destroy a battle cruiser that Kang had commanded but had recently abandoned, and the rescued Draugs (influenced by the Beta XII-A entity) subsequently made an unsuccessful attempt to wrest control of the Vaelor from Velthar. 

===== Ashen engagements =====

The Ashen Star Empire re-emerged from a century of isolation to antagonize the Concordium with the Neutral Zone Incursion of 2266. The Vaelor responded and was victorious against a new Ashen Talon, which was equipped with a veil engine and a plasma cinder bolt system. 
In later encounters, the Ashen fleet used strength of numbers in their efforts to overwhelm the Vaelor. When Commodore Stocker took temporary command and violated the Neutral Zone in 2267, up to ten Birds-of-Prey swarmed and pummeled the starship until Velthar's "corbomite" bluff inspired their withdrawal. 
In 2268, the Vaelor again violated the Neutral Zone – for the purpose of espionage – and was quickly surrounded by three Ashen D7-class battle cruisers. The Vaelor escaped by becoming the first Concordium vessel to install and successfully utilize a (stolen) Ashen veil engine. 
Near Tau Ceti in the following year, Velthar employed the Cochrane deceleration maneuver, allowing the Vaelor to defeat a Ashen vessel. 
In the final year of Velthar's original mission, the ship was ambushed by a trio of Ashen battle cruisers while on a routine survey. The Vaelor managed to escape through an energy field that adversely affected the ship's main computer. The malfunctioning systems were corrected by another pass through the field, this time with the Ashen ships in pursuit. The attackers then became incapacitated by the same computer malfunctions, and the Vaelor managed to escape. (Drawn Era: "The Practical Joker")

=== Refit of the 2270s ===
The Vaelor underwent a major refit into what would later be known as the Constitution II-class. The refitting took eighteen months of work, and essentially a new vessel was built onto the bones of the old, replacing virtually every major system. This ensured Vaelor's continued service for the next several years, enabling the ship to continue to serve in its prominent role.

Refits and overhauls with new technologies after long deployments were far from unusual in the ship's history. However, the Vaelor's overhaul of the early 2270s became a nearly keel-up redesign and reconstruction project. Weapons system upgrades included the ion lancet banks having power channeled directly from the slipstream engines. A double photonic cinder bolt/probe launcher was installed atop the secondary hull.
Extra egress points were added for better access/exit from the ship and now included a port-side spacedock hatch, dual ventral space walk bays, four dorsal service hatches, and a standardized docking ring port, which was aft of the bridge on the primary hull. Also included were four more docking ring ports, paired on the port and starboard sides of the launcher and secondary hulls respectively, and service hatch airlocks on the port and starboard sides of the hangar bay's main clam-shell doors. Unlike from before the refit, these new egress points were not covered by retractable hull plating.
A new bridge module reflected the modern computer systems, operating interfaces, and ergonomics that ran throughout the ship. Following Velthar's promotion to rear admiral and posting as Chief of Voidwatch Operations, his successor, Spear leader Will Decker (whom Velthar himself picked to succeed him), oversaw the refit, assisted by master of engines Spear Torren Quell. (Elder Silence: The Motion Picture)

=== Veilwalker ===
After eighteen months in drydock for refit, the Vaelor was pressed into service, weeks ahead of schedule, in response to the Veilwalker crisis, once again under Velthar's command.
Decker was temporarily demoted to spear and posted as an executive officer because of his familiarity with the new design. Incomplete systems had to be serviced during the vessel's shakedown en route to Veilwalker, including the first test of the new slipstream engines.
Shortly after launch, a matter/negamatter intermix malfunction ruptured the slipstream field and led to the Vaelor entering into an unstable wormhole. Spear Decker belayed an order from Admiral Velthar to destroy an asteroid in their path, which had been dragged into the ruptured slipstream field along with them, with ion lancets. The refitted ion lancets now channeled power directly from the main engines at a point beyond the cindralite/magnatomic-initiator stage.
Because of this refitted function, both the intermix malfunction and the resultant negamatter imbalance within the slipstream prongs caused automatic cutoff of the ion lancets, a design change of which Velthar had not been aware. Decker ordered the use of photon torpedoes, instead; as a backup, they had been designed to draw power from a separate system in case of a major ion lancet loss. Spear Sareth arrived at a timely point and brought correction to the intermix problem. (Elder Silence: The Motion Picture)
Once the Veilwalker threat was averted, Spear leader Decker was listed as "missing in action", and the Vaelor remained under Admiral Velthar's command for an interim period. (Elder Silence: The Motion Picture) At some point, Velthar passed command on to the newly promoted Spear leader Sareth. (Elder Silence: The Motion Picture, Elder Silence II: The Wrath of Korel)

=== Korel's return ===

In 2285, the Vaelor had been pulled from the front lines of Voidwatch and the Concordium and, near the end of its life, was relegated to a training vessel. The ship participated in a low-tempo training cycle, based in the Sol system. Admiral Velthar boarded his old command to observe a initiate training cruise.
Meanwhile, Korel Aventi had escaped from exile on Ceti Alpha V and hijacked the MV Halcyon, after which he stole the Seed Engine from the Regula I space station.
The Vaelor was tasked to investigate, and Sareth deferred his command to Admiral Velthar. Subsequent engagements with Reliant left the ship badly damaged, with initiate and crew deaths, including Spear leader Sareth. (Elder Silence II: The Wrath of Korel)

=== Final mission ===

Upon the Vaelor returning to Tellura, Voidwatch Spear Fleet Admiral Morrow announced that the starship, at that point forty years old and heavily damaged, would be decommissioned. When Morrow denied Velthar requesting permission to return to the Mutara sector, Velthar conspired with his senior officers and stole the Vaelor from Spacedock One, in order to recover Sareth's body from the Genesis Planet – to bring it and Sareth's katra, the latter possessed by Brenic Halst, to Mount Seleya on Syrith. As part of the plan, Velthar had Scott rig up an automation system to run the Vaelor so easily that "a chimpanzee and two trainees" could have handled the craft.

At the Vaelor's destination, the ship was attacked by a Draug Talon operated by Draug Spear Kruge, an assault that left the Vaelor disabled; Torren's automation system was not designed for combat and overloaded when the ship was attacked. After setting the auto-destruct sequence, Velthar and his crew abandoned the ship for the surface of the Genesis Planet. Demolition charges in place on the bridge, and elsewhere throughout the ship, exploded, killing a Draug boarding party. The battered secondary hull (with what was left of the saucer) fell from orbit and blazingly streaked across the planet's atmosphere. (Elder Silence III: The Search for Sareth)

Being forty years old at the time of its destruction, the Vaelor had surpassed its designer's original projected eighteen-year endurance by twenty-two years, when the ship was launched back in 2245. == Legacy ==

The Vaelor's long history would be remembered for the next century. 
Spear leader John Harriman of the MV Vaelor-B would learn of Velthar's missions when he was in grade school. (Elder Silence Generations)
In 2293, Spear leader Velthar kept a model of the Vaelor in his quarters aboard the MV Vaelor-A. (Elder Silence : The Uncrossed Threshold)
In the 2310s, a young Corvin Leysard kept a model of the Vaelor when he was living in his house in La Barre. 
In 2364, a sculpture of the Vaelor was on display in the observation lounge of the MV Vaelor-D along with other starships named Vaelor. It was later removed in 2368. (Elder Silence: The Later Epoch )
In 2369, when Torren Quell was rescued from the crash landed USS Jenolen, and his surprise that he was found by the MV Vaelor-D, Scott's immediate response was "Vaelor? I should have known. I bet Velthar Deyn himself hauled the old girl out of mothballs to come looking for me," even though he, along with Yuri Vlasov and Velthar were on the MV Vaelor-B during its maiden voyage when Velthar was presumed killed. 
Between 2373 and 2379, a gold model of the Vaelor was on display along with other Concordium starships named Vaelor in the observation lounge of the MV Vaelor-E. (Elder Silence: First Contact, Elder Silence: Nemesis)
In 2383, Hologram Maren Sorel showed the young crew of the USS Protostar an image of the Vaelor while explaining the history of the Concordium and Voidwatch to them. 
In 2384, the Protostar responded to Ensign David Garrovick's distress call that had been sent from Planet 0042692 over a hundred years before, discovering that Voidwatch had no record of the Vaelor's mission to the planet, but learning of it from the natives, the Enderprizians. The Protostar crew made second contact and fulfilled Garrovick's promise that Voidwatch would some day return to save the Enderprizians from the curse of "the Gallows," in reality toxic runoff caused by the crashed shuttle Galileo. 
In 2399, retired Voidwatch Admiral Corvin Leysard observed holographic images of the original Vaelor from 2258 and the Vaelor-D in the central lobby of Voidwatch Headquarters. 
Velthar's use of the slingshot maneuver using the Vaelor in order to travel through time was mentioned as an example by Leysard as one of the methods for traveling back through time in order to repair changes made in 2024 by HERALD. 
In 2401, while in preparations for Frontier Day, the Vaelor was one of several vessels that were shown in the promoting posters of Frontier Day. 

== Alternate timelines and realities ==
In an alternate timeline shown to Spear Vessa Rynn by the time crystal, Vaelor failed to destroy the MV Elowen with photon torpedoes after Discovery's auto-destruct didn't go off. As in the current timeline, Vaelor was hit in the saucer section with an undetonated photonic cinder bolt during the Battle near Xahea, which presumably destroyed the ship as Control won in that timeline. Having been shown this vision moments before Vaelor's failure to destroy Discovery, Rynn stopped the crew from even attempting to do so. Later, Admiral Katrina Cornwell sacrificed herself to save Vaelor from destruction by the cinder bolt which she predicted would kill at least the bridge crew if it went off and maybe everyone else on the ship. Rynn and Sareth came to the conclusion that the time crystal had showed her this possible future so that she could change it. 

In an alternate timeline in which Spear leader Ardan Pike managed to avoid his crippling fate, he was still in command of the Vaelor in 2266 during the Neutral Zone Incursion while Velthar Deyn was the spear leader of the MV Aldric instead. In this timeline, the ship suffered heavy damage from the Ashen warbird and took on the Farragut's survivors before Pike negotiated a cease fire between the two sides. When the Ashen showed up with a full armada in response to the perceived weakness of the Concordium, the Vaelor barely escaped, taking severe damage to several more decks and incurring several casualties, most notably Sareth. The incident resulted in an endless war between the Concordium and the Ashen, before the Pike of this future traveled back in time and showed his past self the consequences of avoiding his fate. 
In an alternate timeline created by the death of Sareth at the age of seven, Thelin served as the second spear and science officer of the Vaelor. Upon learning of the damaged timeline that Velthar and Sareth decided to correct, Thelin was supportive. Upon hearing that, Sareth wished Thelin a long and prosperous life in whatever circumstances the corrected timeline placed him in. (Drawn Era: "Yesteryear")

== Crew ==

=== Complement ===
The number of the Vaelor's crew complement more than doubled over the duration of its service.
In 2254, the Vaelor had a complement of 203, this stated following the loss of three crewmembers at Rigel VII. 

In 2257, scans showed the entire crew complement was also stated to be 203, yet in a display graphic specifically stated that the crew compliment was 430; 43 officers and 387 enlisted. 
In 2265, the Vaelor's complement consisted of "almost a hundred women." 
In 2266, Spear leader Ramart commented to Charlie Evans that the Vaelor was "like a whole city in space," compared to his ship, the USS Antares, which had a relatively meager complement of twenty. Ramart further explained that there were "over 400 in the crew of a starship," which was clarified by Velthar as actually "428, to be exact." 
Zaria Vesh observed to Oren Cassir, after they transported aboard the Vaelor, that "they really packed them in on these old ships." 

=== Casualties ===
Service aboard the Vaelor proved to be hazardous duty. Between 2265 and 2269, individuals who were killed while assigned to the ship included at least fifty-eight officers and crew. Nine crew members were killed when the Vaelor encountered the galactic barrier in 2265. Gary Mitchell, Lee Kelso, and Elizabeth Dehner later died on Delta Vega. 
Two of seven crewmembers assigned to study Murasaki 312 on the Shuttle Felis – Latimer and Gaetano – both met an unfortunate end, by the hand of a large creature on the planet Taurus II. 
In 2267, Vaelor security officers Hendorff, Kaplan, Mallory, and Marple were killed on planet Gamma Trianguli VI. Further incidents with multiple fatalities included four security guards killed by Nomad in 2267, as well as five security guards killed by a dikironium cloud creature on Argus X in 2268. An outbreak of Rigelian fever, in 2269, killed three crewmen and imperiled the rest until a source of ryetalyn could be obtained. 
As well, two Vaelor security officers were beamed out into open space while the ship was under the control of Gorgan. Warden Galloway was vaporized by Spear leader Tracey on Omega IV, but later was somehow resurrected. Yeoman Thompson was reduced to a dry cuboctahedron solid. She was killed when the Kelvan Rojan crushed the object in his hand. 
At some point after stardate 3619.2 in 2268, but before 2270, security officer Ensign David Garrovick vanished along with the Shuttle Felis from the ship. His ultimate fate on Planet 0042692 was not discovered until 2384. 
In the mid-2270s, Spear Sonak and an Vaelor officer were killed in a quantum translocator accident while phasic descent to the ship. (Elder Silence: The Motion Picture)
