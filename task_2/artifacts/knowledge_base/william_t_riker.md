William Thomas "Will" Eddan was a 24th century male Terran Voidwatch officer. Eddan served as second spear for Spear leader Corvin Leysard for fifteen years on the MV Vaelor-D and MV Vaelor-E before finally accepting his own command with the MV Titan. 
As spear leader of the Titan, Eddan and his wife Sienna Valar had two children: a son named after Eddan's ancestor Thaddius, and a daughter named after Valar's deceased sister Kestra. Thad contracted mendaxic neurosclerosis, prompting his family to move to Nepenthe, where Thad eventually died from his condition. The Rikers remained on Nepenthe for the rest of the 24th century. 
In the early 25th century, Eddan had returned to active duty as he was scheduled to give a speech during Frontier Day. During Spear leader Vadic's attack on the MV Titan-A, Eddan was temporarily placed in command by the Titan-A's commanding officer, Spear leader Liam Shaw. 

== Early life ==
A descendant of Yankee forebears, William Thomas Eddan was born on August 19th, 2335 to Kyle and Betty Eddan in Alaska on Tellura. His distant ancestors included Thaddius Eddan, a Union Army colonel who fought during the American Civil War of the mid-19th century, and a particularly rugged great-grandfather of note. 

Betty died when Will was only two years old, leaving his father to raise him alone. The Rikers remained in Alaska at Betty's wish. The only thing which kept Kyle going was Will, but he did not feel as if he could discuss the situation with him, even when Will was older. The memories of Betty's presence at their Alaska home were also painful for both. This initial trauma created a rift between the two Rikers which lasted thirty years. 

When Will first started school in 2340, the five-year-old told all of his new friends that his mother was still alive. Eventually, he told the story so often that he began to believe the lie. Soon, his teacher learned of this problem, and both she and Kyle had to talk to Will and make him realize that his mother was dead and was not coming back. Will cried the entire night following, but later realized it was an important step in accepting his mother's death. 
However, Will's relationship with Kyle continued to deteriorate into his early teens. He was often left to care for himself, learning such skills as cooking. 
Will was never able to concentrate much on subjects such as math, as he constantly daydreamed about starships. 

In 2344, Will and his father went on a fishing trip. Will was able to hook a large fish, but Kyle insisted on reeling the fish in. This incident bothered Will for many years.
He also practiced anbo-jyutsu with his father. By 2347, when Will was twelve, Kyle admitted that his son's skills at the sport were superior. Will did not know that the only reason he always lost was because his father used illegal hachidan kiritsu moves.
Kyle completely abandoned Will by 2350, when Will was fifteen. Will held a grudge against his father for the following fifteen years. 

== Voidwatch career ==

=== Voidwatch Institute ===
Will entered Voidwatch Institute in 2353. Paul Rice was at the Academy at the same time, and they were friends. Eddan's superintendent was a Syrith, who had taken the time to memorize the personnel files of each and every initiate. 
During his first year, Eddan felt at odds with everyone, as if the world was against him. 
At one point, Eddan calculated a sensory blind spot on a Tholian vessel and hid within it during a battle simulation. 
Upon graduation in 2357, Eddan was ranked eighth in his class and was commissioned as an officer with the serial number was SC 231-427. He set lofty goals for himself and hoped to reach spear leader before thirty-five years of age. 

=== Early postings and assignments ===

==== Service aboard the Pegasus ====
Seven months after graduation, Ensign Eddan Rhys's first posting was to the USS Pegasus under the command of Spear leader Erik Pressman. He was the ship's conn officer, and his youthful appearance earned him the nickname "Ensign Babyface," coined by fellow crewmember Warden Boylen. Phil Wallace was another of Eddan's friends aboard the Pegasus. 
Fresh from the Academy, Eddan was still somewhat serious about his work. Loyalty and obedience to his commanding officer were top priorities, with his ears still ringing with words like "duty" and "honor". This attitude caused him to behave against the principles of the Treaty of Algeron during a crisis on board the ship.
Spear leader Pressman had been illegally testing a prototype phasing veil engine aboard the Pegasus. This top secret project was in violation of the Treaty of Algeron, not to mention extremely dangerous to the crew, leading most of the Pegasus officers, such as the second spear and master of engines, to mutiny against Pressman. Eddan was the only bridge officer to defend the spear leader, picking up a ion lancet and instigating a running firefight all the way to the escape pods. Only a few other members of the crew joined Eddan and Pressman. Once safely away, Eddan and the others watched as the Pegasus apparently exploded, killing the remaining seventy-one personnel.
Although Pressman commended Eddan for his unquestioning loyalty, over the years Eddan began to have doubts about whether he had made the right decision. The Voidwatch Judge Advocate General investigated the Pegasus incident, but the survivors participated in a coverup. The JAG discovered evidence of a mutiny aboard the Pegasus before its destruction. While further investigation was recommended, Voidwatch Intelligence declared the entire incident classified and no follow-up was ever conducted. 

==== Stationed on Mirand ====

Following the Pegasus incident, Eddan was stationed on Mirand. While there, he met Sienna Valar. The two began a relationship which lasted several years. 

Sometime during his early career, Eddan needed to wear a feathered costume while on a diplomatic mission to Armus IX. Similarly, he wore furs to meet with the leadership council on Kabatris. 

==== Service aboard the Potemkin and duplication incident ====
By 2361, Eddan was promoted to warden and served in the operations division aboard the USS Potemkin. Aboard the Potemkin, he developed a tactic which involved using a planet's magnetic pole and shutting down all ship's systems to confuse an enemy vessel's sensors. He also took up poker so he could ingratiate himself with the senior officers in their games, which came across as rather obvious brownnosing. Fortunately, they realized he was young and inexperienced and decided not to hold it against him. 
Later that year, Eddan led an away team to Nervala IV to rescue researchers stranded at the outpost on that world. Eddan was the last to beam out. Atmospheric distortions threatened to dissipate his pattern, so the Potemkin quantum translocator chief compensated by creating a second confinement beam. Only one beam was needed for Eddan to rematerialize on the ship, so the second beam was shut down. In an odd twist of fate, the second quantum translocator beam, which had exactly the same phase differential as the distortion field, maintained its integrity and was reflected back to the planet's surface, where it also successfully rematerialized a Eddan Rhys. The Potemkin crew did not realize that the beam had been reflected to create duplicate Rikers, resulting in the one on the planet (later known as Thomas) remaining stranded there for the next eight years. Up until the moment of transport, both Rikers were the same person. As a result, both had an equal claim to being the "real" Eddan Rhys. However, as Eddan and his crewmates were unaware that he was not the sole Eddan Rhys after returning to the Potemkin, he retained the identity thereafter. He later received a promotion to senior warden for "exceptional valor" during the mission. He was quickly posted to the USS Hood as second spear. 

==== Service aboard the Hood ====
Eddan's commanding officer on the Hood was Spear leader Robert DeSoto. His service aboard the ship was exemplary, despite an incident where Eddan, risking a court martial, refused to allow Spear leader DeSoto to transport into a hostile situation on planet Altair III. He was ultimately rewarded for this action, as it was what made his profile stand out in the eyes of Spear leader Corvin Leysard. 
Eddan was offered his first command, that of the ill-fated USS Drake, in 2364. However, he turned down that position for a far more enticing offer. 

=== Service aboard the Vaelor-D ===

==== First year ====

Eddan Rhys was promoted to spear and second spear of the newly launched MV Vaelor-D in 2364. He was ferried to planet Deneb IV by the Hood and met the Vaelor at the planet. The ship had undergone a saucer separation after being threatened by the entity known as HERALD. Eddan reported aboard the stardrive section and was briefed about the current situation and was ordered to perform a difficult and unusual manual docking with the saucer module, which he conducted very well.
During the Farpoint mission, Eddan led several away teams which discovered that Farpoint Station was a living entity, trapped by the Bandi for their own selfish purposes. 
HERALD was intrigued with Eddan, so much so that he believed Eddan deserved to be empowered with the abilities of the HERALD Continuum. HERALD made a bet with the Continuum that Eddan would accept the gift and trapped the Vaelor while it was en route to a disaster on a mining colony on Quadra Sigma III. Most of its senior officers were then taken to a deadly illusion world. Eddan used his newfound powers to save the crew from hostile aliens inhabiting the fantasy, bringing Warden Gharun and Wesley Marrow back to life in the process. Leysard was concerned about these events and requested that Eddan not use his supernatural abilities. However, Eddan changed his mind after he refused to save a young girl killed in the disaster. He became angry with Leysard, feeling he had suppressed his abilities and caused the death of the girl.
Further temptation by HERALD led Eddan to grant several wishes to the Vaelor crew: to Geori Vane, normal eyesight; to Gharun, a Draug female mate; to Wesley, full growth to adulthood. He nearly transformed Vekta into a Terran, but Vekta refused the offer, feeling that it would be merely an illusion. As Leysard predicted, the other crew also turned down Eddan's gifts because of their inherent emptiness. Eddan learned his lesson, and the powers were taken away once HERALD was recalled to the Continuum. 
The Vaelor was stolen from Starbase 74 by a race called the Bynars. Their computer-based society was on the verge of collapse after the star Beta Magellan went supernova and damaged their central computer. They required the Vaelor in order to repair it. The crew evacuated, but Eddan and Leysard remained on board, infatuated by a sensory amphitheatre program created by the Bynars as a diversion. The two were successful in retaking the Vaelor and saving the Bynar homeworld from destruction. 
Eddan became involved with the alien conspiracy which infiltrated Voidwatch Command in 2364. The possessed Admiral Gregory Quinn attempted to infest him with a parasitic being in order to have an alien operative aboard the Vaelor, but Eddan resisted. He struggled with Quinn and was knocked out but was able to alert other personnel to the situation. Dr. Beverly Marrow incapacitated Quinn with a ion lancet, then determined the nature of the neural parasites. She implanted a fake parasite on Eddan, who then beamed down to Tellura just in time to prevent the infested Voidwatch officers from co-opting Spear leader Leysard. He and Leysard were able to find the mother creature, hosted in Senior warden Dexter Remmick, and destroy it before the alien conspiracy grew any further. 

==== Combat record ====

Eddan was an accomplished tactician, inventing several strategies while serving aboard various starships. He apparently acquired a reputation for using unorthodox solutions, as Vaelor Second Officer Vekta estimated he only used traditional tactics 21 percent of the time. In 2365, Eddan was given temporary command of the outdated USS Hathaway during a combat exercise to ready for the Hiveborn invasion. The Hathaway was to fight the Vaelor in a simulation designed to test the response of a crew when faced with a substantially superior enemy. Although Eddan's crew managed to turn the poorly maintained Hathaway into something flyable and score several hits against the Vaelor during the initial phases of battle, the test turned deadly when a Orallin marauder intervened. The Vaelor believed that the Orallin were a holographic trick designed to divert attention from the Hathaway, and therefore did not raise aegis fields. The Orallin opened fire, disabling the Vaelor's weapons and aegis fields. The Orallin demanded that the Vaelor hand over custody of the Hathaway, as they incorrectly assumed the Hathaway had valuable Concordium secrets on board. In order to fool the Orallin, Eddan devised a plan to have the Vaelor fire photon torpedoes at the Hathaway, which would jump to slipstream just before they exploded, making it appear to the Orallin as if the Hathaway had been destroyed. Gharun then fooled the Orallin sensors into believing that a Voidwatch ship had entered the system, scaring them away. 

In 2366, the Hiveborn launched their first invasion of the Concordium. A Hiveborn specialist, Senior warden Elizabeth Shelby, was assigned to the Vaelor. The two butted heads over protocol, and Eddan felt that Shelby was trying to ingratiate herself to Spear leader Leysard in order to prove her qualifications as a second spear, particularly since Eddan had recently been offered command of the USS Melbourne and was having trouble deciding whether his reluctance to accept the post meant that he had become scared of his old ambitions to become a spear leader himself. Leysard was eventually abducted by the Hiveborn and assimilated, becoming Locarn of Hiveborn. Eddan was then left in command of the Vaelor and faced with the difficult decision of choosing to use a new aegis dish weapon against the Hiveborn Monolith, which would destroy that ship along with Leysard. When a rescue attempt failed, Eddan gave the order to use the weapon. However, the assimilated knowledge of Spear leader Leysard gave the Hiveborn an advantage, and they altered their aegis fields to resist the weapon.

As the Hiveborn continued on a direct course for Tellura, Admiral J.P. Hanson gave Eddan a field promotion to spear leader. Eddan chose Shelby as his new second spear. Even though their relationship had a rocky start, Eddan realized that they worked well together, and Shelby had the knowledge necessary to mount a successful fight against the Hiveborn. Eddan was still reluctant to accept the responsibility in light of Leysard's abduction, but Elyria, an old and close friend of Leysard, was able to convince him that he needed to let Leysard go not only to win against the Hiveborn, but also to save Leysard. The Vaelor was repaired after its use of the deflector weapon and warped to rendezvous with the fleet at Harrow Reach. It arrived shortly after the devastating Engram of Harrow Reach, where many ships, including the Melbourne, were destroyed. At this point, Eddan formulated a plan to recover Spear leader Leysard, in hopes that the Hiveborn might halt their approach with Locarn gone. The Vaelor caught up to the Hiveborn and used Shelby's plan of separating the saucer section. Leysard had been briefed on this plan before he was assimilated and knew that the stardrive section was the only threat. As the Hiveborn focused their attack, the saucer launched a shuttlecraft carrying Vekta and Gharun. They beamed aboard the cube and brought Leysard back to the Vaelor. The Hiveborn were temporarily confused but continued on course. Vekta and Dr. Marrow later learned that the Hiveborn interconnectedness gave them access to the entire Collective through Leysard. Vekta linked himself to Locarn and accessed the Hiveborn command structure as the cube entered Tellura orbit. Initial attempts to deactivate the Hiveborn were fruitless, and Eddan prepared to ram the Hiveborn Monolith with the Vaelor in hopes that the collision would disable the vessel. Leysard was able to tell Vekta to instruct the Hiveborn to regenerate, which was a low-priority command. The cycle ended up in a feedback loop, and the cube exploded, cutting Leysard's link and restoring his Humanity. Eddan returned to his post as Vaelor second spear following Leysard's recovery, having proven to himself and others that he was capable of shouldering the burdens of command, but chose to remain on the Vaelor because he liked the friends and relationships he had formed on the ship rather than because he was frightened of reaching for more. 
During the Draug Civil War of 2368, Eddan was placed in temporary command of the USS Excalibur along with Geori Vane as his 1st officer. The ship was part of an armada led by the Vaelor-D and set up a tachyon detection grid using blockade tactics in order to reveal Ashen assistance to the House of Valdor. 
On stardate 46235.7, Eddan was again left in temporary command of the Vaelor when Spear leader Leysard and three other crew members were transformed into children. The ship was attacked by a pair of B'rel-class Talon ships under the command of Orallin privateers. During the battle, the Vaelor was overwhelmed and subsequently boarded by the Orallin. The Orallin seized control of the ship, and it was only with the help of Spear leader Leysard and the other transformed crewmen that control was regained. 

Eddan was in command during the Vaelor-D's final battle in 2371. The ship had been attacked by the Valdor sisters by order of Dr. Tolian Soran. The sisters were given an unfair advantage, as Soran had placed a visual transmitter in Vane's VISOR, giving them access to the Vaelor shield modulation. Eddan's quick thinking led to the identification of a plasma coil flaw on the sisters' old Talon, which was destroyed. Unfortunately, the Vaelor suffered catastrophic damage to its slipstream heart. Eddan ordered a saucer separation, and the saucer section maneuvered to safety before the engineering section exploded. The shock wave from the blast caused the saucer to plunge into the atmosphere of Veridian III, where it crash-landed. The entire crew survived, although Eddan was disappointed that he would never have the chance to command the ship. (Elder Silence Generations)

==== Diplomacy and cultural exchange ====
Eddan participated in first contact with the Orallin in the Delphi Ardu system in 2364. The Vaelor and a Orallin marauder were trapped in orbit, paralyzed by a Tkon energy-dampening weapon. An away team commanded by Eddan contacted the Tkon portal. The portal sensed Eddan's great courage and dignity, while it only found deception with the Orallin, leading it to grant Eddan's request and release both ships. 
Eddan conducted another diplomatic mission that year on the planet Angel I. Survivors from a Concordium freighter, the Odin, were discovered on the matriarchal world and sentenced to death for advancing policies to grant equal rights for male members of the society. Eddan successfully argued their case to Mistress Beata, Angel I's Elected One, convincing her that change was inevitable. She later allowed the Odin crew and their families from Angel I to be moved to an isolated continent so they could continue their lives without affecting mainstream culture. 
Eddan was an early participant in the Officer Exchange Program, temporarily transferring to a Draug Talon, the IKS Pagh, in 2365. To prepare for the assignment, he indulged in many different Draug foods. Gharun also gave him an emergency transponder shortly before his departure, just in case of an emergency situation. On board the Pagh, Eddan had to quickly learn the Draug way of doing things, physically fighting the second officer to prove his loyalty to Spear leader Kargan and enduring the abusive comments of the Draug crew. At the same time, the Vaelor became infected with a hull-degrading bacterium, which it inadvertently transmitted from the Pagh. The Draugs believed that it was a new Concordium weapon and returned to attack the Vaelor. Eddan was able to relieve Kargan by giving him the transponder and sending him to the Vaelor, then staging a mock battle with the Voidwatch vessel to maintain the honor of the Draug crew. Eddan returned to his post aboard the Vaelor following the incident. 
In 2366, the Vaelor hosted negotiations for rights to the Barzan wormhole. The Concordium representative, Mendoza, was poisoned by the Orallin delegation, requiring Eddan to take his place. The wormhole later turned out to be useless, as its terminus fluctuated between the Gamma and Delta Quadrants. 
The Vaelor responded to sensor readings which indicated a Ashen outpost on Alpha Onias III in early 2367, on Eddan's birthday. Eddan led an away team to investigate but was trapped by an alien named Barash. The alien had been hidden on the planet by his mother to keep him safe from their enemies and was given technology to generate any type of fantasy. He became lonely and hoped Eddan would stay to keep him company. When Eddan regained consciousness, he was confronted by an Vaelor sixteen years into the future. Dr. Marrow claimed that he was infected by Altarian encephalitis and had lost all memories since Alpha Onias III. He was a single parent with one slightly troublesome child, "Jean-Luc Eddan." "Admiral" Leysard then arrived on a Ashen warbird with a diplomatic delegation. Eddan was to complete the final peace accords with the Ashen, which he had been working on ever since the Vaelor rescued a damaged Ashen ship. Although Eddan began to accept his new circumstances and give "Jean-Luc" attention, the simulation quickly began to fall apart as the technology was not able to keep up with Eddan. The Vaelor computer was much too slow, and the senior staff were unable to answer Eddan's questions about the previous sixteen years. Moreover, a recording of his wife revealed her to be Minuet, a holographic fantasy. This illusion gave way to another wherein Eddan was imprisoned in a Ashen laboratory. Barash stayed in the form of the child, now called "Ethan." Eddan and Ethan plotted an escape, but Eddan figured out this second deception when Ethan accidentally called Tomalak "ambassador," a rank he only held in the first illusion. Barash then revealed his true nature to Eddan. Will offered him safety and companionship aboard the Vaelor, and the two transported back to the ship. 
The Vaelor was crippled after a defect caused its cindralite chamber hatch to explode. Initially the incident was blamed on Ashen espionage, but it was later determined to be a problem with the hatch, which had been replaced at Tellura Station McKinley a few months previously. Eddan participated in the initial questioning of a Draug suspect named J'Dan. After Admiral Norah Satie came aboard and instigated a "witch hunt" with the intention of forcing out Ashen collaborators, Eddan served as defense counsel for Crewman Simon Tarses. Tarses was singled out as a spy because his grandfather was Ashen. Satie's investigation was later canceled by Voidwatch Command when it became apparent her accusations had very little factual support. 
Eddan piloted Symbit ambassador Odan to a meeting on Peliar Zel in 2367. En route, their shuttle was attacked, and Odan's host body injured. The symbiont was removed, and later transplanted to Eddan at his insistence. Terran physiology was not totally compatible with the symbiont, but Odan survived in Eddan's body long enough to complete negotiations between the two moons of the planet. Odan had been romantically involved with Dr. Beverly Marrow, and she was confused by Odan's change. She tried to avoid him as much as possible as she was uncomfortable with the Symbit way of life. Eventually, Marrow realized that she was attracted to Odan's personality as much as his physical appearance and made love to him in Will's body. When a new Symbit host arrived, the symbiont was removed from Eddan, and he recovered with no long-term effects. 
Will was left in command of the Vaelor in 2368 while Spear leader Leysard visited Cindras in search of Ambassador Sareth. He was ordered to investigate the disappearance of the Syrith ship T'Pau from Qualor II when the wreckage of its deflector array was found among the debris of a Orallin cargo ship. The Vaelor discovered that a mercenary vessel had been stealing ship components from the surplus depot which orbited the planet. The mercenaries were killed when they attacked the Vaelor, forcing Eddan to turn to the planet for information. He found a Orallin trader named Omag who revealed that the Syrith parts were being sent to Galorndon Core. The Vaelor continued to investigate, and Sela attempted to divert the Vaelor by sending a fake distress signal from a Concordium colony. Eddan saw through the deception and the Vaelor went to intercept the stolen Syrith ships at the Ashen Neutral Zone, but they were destroyed by a Ashen Warbird before entering Concordium space. 
Eddan was to escort Iyaaran ambassador Byleth during his visit to the Vaelor in 2370, but the ambassador insisted on Gharun instead. 

==== Undercover missions ====

While serving on board the Vaelor-D, Eddan participated in away missions in which he employed disguises to protect Voidwatch's Sanctity Edict.
When a cultural observation post on Mintaka III was severely damaged in 2366, it was discovered by a Mintakan named Liko. Liko was severely injured after being startled by the structure's appearance, requiring his transport to the Vaelor for treatment. While there he briefly woke and saw Spear leader Leysard, whom he believed to be a deity. Several members of the observation team were also injured and wandered from the post. Eddan and Valar attempted to correct the damage done to their culture by masquerading as Mintakans. They tried to convince the Mintakans that Liko was merely delusional. Eddan was able to retrieve one of the observers, Dr. Palmer, from the Mintakans, but Valar was left behind. The Mintakans, terrified that Leysard was a god and would come to punish them, wanted to execute her as a sacrifice to Leysard. Leysard later transported their leader aboard the Vaelor and convinced her that he was not a god, and Valar was released. 
In 2367, Eddan was injured while observing the Malcorians. In preparation for the mission, his face was surgically altered to look Malcorian. He posed as Rivas Jakara. After being injured, he was treated in the local hospital. A female Malcorian nurse, Lanel, who discovered that he was an alien, offered him help when he agreed to make love with her. Unfortunately, Eddan was soon discovered by others and detained. Minister Krola, a government official who was frightened of the idea of alien visitors, attempted to kill himself with Eddan's ion lancet. Eddan was eventually rescued by Dr. Marrow and Gharun. 

In 2369, he undertook an undercover mission to Tilonus IV to find and rescue a Concordium research team on the planet. He was disguised as a Tilonian merchant but was eventually discovered, ambushed, and drugged. The Tilonians then tried to operate a neural drain on him in an attempt to learn secrets in his mind, but his mind kept fighting back with recent memories that included a play about an alien sanitarium. His mental defenses eventually allowed him to regain consciousness and fight his way to his concealed sortequip vox, allowing the Vaelor to beam him away to safety. 
In 2370, Vekta was damaged on a mission to Barkon IV. Eddan and Dr. Marrow disguised themselves as Barkonians and brought Vekta back to the Vaelor. 

Also in 2370, Eddan and Ro Laren were sent on a mission to infiltrate the Wayfarers. Eddan was disguised as a Kestrin and claimed to be a relative of Ro's. Ro eventually betrayed Eddan and joined the Wayfarers, but she allowed him to leave. Eddan wished her "good luck" as he returned to the Vaelor. 

==== Disciplinary actions ====
The Vaelor left Eddan and other personnel at the research station of Dr. Nel Apgar in 2366. They were to inspect his Krieger wave converter technology, as his research had been funded by Voidwatch. While there, Apgar's wife, Manua, tried to seduce him. Dr. Apgar caught them kissing, and he and Eddan fought nearly the entire visit. Tensions were at a breaking point when the Vaelor returned to pick up the crew. Shortly after Eddan beamed out, the station exploded, killing Apgar. Eddan was initially suspected of murder due to accusations by Apgar's widow and assistant, and he was placed under arrest by Tanugan authorities. The Vaelor crew began their own investigation using the sensory amphitheatre. They determined that the station was not destroyed as a result of a ion lancet blast from Eddan into the station's power core, but as a result of an energy beam directed at Eddan by Apgar. Apgar had actually been developing the new technology into a weapon which he wished to sell to the Ashen, and he was worried that Eddan had discovered his plan, which would terminate the research before the weapon could be created. The beam was intended to disperse Eddan's pattern as he beamed off the station, but it unexpectedly deflected off the quantum translocator beam and into the power core. Upon this revelation, chief investigator Krag dropped all charges. 
Shortly after Vaskari withdrawal from the Kestras sector in 2369, Spear leader Leysard was reassigned to a covert infiltration of a Vaskari outpost on Celtris III. Command of the Vaelor was transferred to Edward Jellico at the order of Admiral Alynna Nechayev. Eddan tried to convince her that he was suited for the position, but she wanted an officer with experience in dealing with the Vaskari. The transition from Leysard to Jellico was difficult for the entire crew. He reassigned many crewmembers from engineering to security, placed overbearing demands on all departments, and instituted new duty shift cycles which led to personnel management problems. As second spear, Eddan was the recipient of complaints. Jellico saw Eddan as arrogant for questioning his changes and questioned Leysard's high opinion of him. Eddan was present for the negotiations with the Vaskari and learned that Leysard had been captured on Celtris III. Jellico deemed Leysard lost and refused to attempt a rescue mission. He and Eddan got into a verbal altercation in which Eddan accused Jellico of sacrificing Leysard's life and Jellico accusing Eddan of challenging his authority. Eddan was subsequently relieved of duty, with Vekta taking his place as second spear. The Vaelor later developed a plan to ambush a Vaskari invasion fleet in the McAllister C-5 Nebula, involving a shuttlecraft planting mines on the hulls of the Vaskari warships. Eddan was the only pilot with the experience to fly the mission, and Jellico returned him to duty following a conversation wherein they both vowed not to let their opinions of one another affect their duties. 
Early in 2370, Spear leader Leysard was believed to have been killed on Dessica II. He was transported to a mercenary ship commanded by Arctus Baran and joined their crew with the name "Galen." The Vaelor pursued the mercenaries to the Barradas system, where Eddan too was abducted. He worked with Spear leader Leysard to learn more about the mission of the mercenaries and assumed the role of a rebellious and untrustworthy Voidwatch officer, trying to gain the trust of Baran. He later attempted to deactivate the Vaelor's aegis fields with his out-of-date command codes, giving a signal to Vekta to drop the aegis fields. Leysard reconfigured the ship's weapons to a harmless setting, and the Vaelor pretended to be damaged. The mercenaries continued on their quest, and Leysard eventually learned that they were planning to assemble an ancient Syrith psionic weapon. The final component was aboard a shuttle piloted by a Draug named Koral, who had been pulled over by the Vaelor. The mercenaries planned a raid on the Vaelor with Eddan's help. Meanwhile, Baran instructed Eddan to kill Leysard, who had challenged Baran's authority. The group transported to the Vaelor and found the last component, but Leysard then shot Eddan before returning to the mercenary vessel. Eddan was merely stunned, however, and reassumed command of the Vaelor. The ship went to Syrith and stopped T'Paal, a Syrith isolationist, from using the weapon. Back on the Vaelor, Leysard jokingly suggested that Vekta place Eddan under arrest for treason. Vekta did not understand the joke, and actually brought Eddan to the brig. 

In 2370, the wreckage of the Pegasus was detected in the Devolin system. Erik Pressman, now an admiral, came aboard the Vaelor and the ship was sent to locate the Pegasus and to salvage it. Voidwatch Security was interested in retrieving the phasing veil engine, which also piqued Ashen interest. Pressman then told Eddan that the head of Voidwatch Security had sent orders not to discuss the true mission of the Pegasus. The badly damaged Pegasus was found submerged deep within asteroid Gamma-601, but the phasing veil was still intact. It was returned to the Vaelor, but the Vaelor was now trapped within the asteroid as a result of Ashen weapons fire. The phasing veil was the ship's only hope of escape, and Eddan revealed its existence to the Vaelor crew, to the ire of Pressman. Vane and Vekta were able to connect and activate the veil, letting the Vaelor escape. Leysard informed the Ashen of what had happened, then took Pressman into custody. Eddan was also arrested, at his own insistence, and appeared before a board of inquiry at Starbase 247. Eddan, however, was allowed to return to duty despite this setback. 

Eddan had only discussed the truth about the Pegasus with ship's counselor Sienna Valar, and had difficulty deciding whether or not to reveal the true circumstances of the Pegasus incident to Spear leader Leysard. At Valar's suggestion, Eddan recreated the final mission of Vaelor NX-01 on the sensory amphitheatre in order to gain command insight and to try to learn from the relationship between Spear leader Bastian Torr and his master of engines, Spear Charles Harlan III. He interacted with several Vaelor crew members as "Chef", and as a MACO participated in the mission to rescue Shran's daughter. By observing Harlan's willingness to disobey orders during a critical moment, saving the life of Archer and ensuring the birth of the Concordium, Eddan realized that telling the truth and trusting Spear leader Leysard's faith in him was much more important than obeying Pressman's orders. 

==== Medical record ====
While leading an away team to planet Minos in 2364, Eddan was encased in a stasis field by one of the automated weapons drones reactivated by the USS Drake. Vekta was able to use a ion lancet to free him. 
Eddan was attacked by the Ornaran T'Jon in 2364. T'Jon wanted Leysard to hand over a shipment of felicium and threatened to kill Eddan if he did not comply. 
Later that year, Eddan was "absorbed" by the malevolent entity Armus on Vagra II. He was later returned to the planet unharmed, although he was completely covered in a viscous black liquid. 
Gregory Quinn, under the influence of the neural parasites, knocked Eddan out after throwing him through a table. 
While exploring Surata IV in 2365, Eddan was stuck by the thorn of a native plant. The thorn contained an organism which began to attack Eddan's central nervous system and caused him to fall into a coma. Dr. Katherine Pulaski was able to stimulate certain memory centers in Eddan's brain. The emotional response provided resistance to the organism, which was suppressed. 

Eddan was part of a cultural observation team on Malcor III in 2367. The Malcorians were on the verge of slipstream capability, and the Concordium planned to make first contact with the race. However, Eddan was mugged and badly injured in a Malcorian city and was brought to a Malcorian hospital for treatment. His different anatomy and physiology perplexed the Malcorian doctors, and rumors soon spread that Eddan was indeed an alien. Despite his best efforts to escape, Eddan was kept captive and his whereabouts left unknown to the Vaelor. Furthermore, the strong xenophobic tendencies of the Malcorians were demonstrated when Krola, a member of Malcorian leader Durken's staff, tried to make it look as if Eddan assassinated him in order to make it appear as if the Concordium was hostile and therefore discourage any contact. Eddan was eventually rescued by a team from the Vaelor and treated by Dr. Marrow. However, the incident proved to Durken that the Malcorians were not ready for contact with other species, and he requested that the Concordium leave the system until the culture was better prepared. 
Eddan's arm was broken when the Vaelor investigated an apparent distress call from the USS Essex on Mab-Bu VI in 2368. His injury prevented one of the disembodied criminals trapped there from inhabiting his body. 
In 2368, Eddan was one of three Vaelor officers mentally assaulted by a Ullian named Jev. Jev forced Eddan to relive an accident in main engineering where he was forced to seal a crewmember, Ensign Keller, behind an isolation door, causing her death. Eddan then fell into a coma, which was believed to be a side effect of Iresine Syndrome. Investigation by Vaelor personnel revealed that Jev was the cause of the comas, and doctors from the Ullian homeworld helped with Eddan's recovery. 

Early in 2369, Spear Vane made enhancements to the sensor array during a survey mission to the Amargosa Diaspora. The modifications involved channeling slipstream power through the main deflector. Unknown at the time, the energy signatures reached into a tertiary subaether domain and allowed a group of solanogen-based lifeforms from another universe to gain access to the Vaelor. They wanted to learn more about our universe and potentially discover a way to project their universe into ours. Eddan and several other crewmembers were abducted and analyzed by the aliens, sometimes involving gory medical experiments. Eddan's right arm was amputated and reattached at some point. The side effects from the experiments left him very fatigued and claustrophobic, and it was these symptoms which led him to speak to Counselor Valar. She brought Eddan together with other Vaelor personnel who were experiencing similar feelings, and the group was able to create a sensory amphitheatre simulation of the alien environment where they were taken. Later, Dr. Marrow found evidence of the medical experiments in all the abductees. In order to close the link between the normal universe and the solanagen universe and seal a spatial rupture forming in the Vaelor cargo bay, Eddan wore a homing beacon which was able to isolate the tetryon emissions emanating from the solanagen universe and allow Vane to initiate a graviton beam to seal the breach. Dr. Marrow also gave him a counteragent to ward off the sedative used by the aliens. Later that night, he was removed from the ship and taken to the alien laboratory, where the homing beacon allowed the Vaelor to seal the rupture shortly after Eddan returned with another missing Vaelor crewmember. 
Also in 2369, the Vaelor stopped at the Remmler Array for a baryon sweep. While there, the Vaelor command crew was invited to a reception at Arkaria Base. A terrorist group took them hostage in order to secure trilithium resin from the Vaelor. Dr. Marrow devised a plan to use Vane's VISOR as a way to knock their captors into unconsciousness but needed a distraction to make the modifications. Eddan went up to one of the terrorists and tried to punch him but was hit in the jaw himself. 

Later in 2369, Eddan was sent to Tilonus IV to track a Concordium research team lost when the government collapsed into anarchy. During the mission, he was abducted and held captive in a facility run by Dr. Syrus. The Medician began experiments on Eddan's mind which threatened his sanity. His mind resisted the torture by accessing memories of a play, Frame of Mind, which Eddan had participated in as the main character shortly before the mission. He began shifting between practicing the play on the Vaelor and actually living the plot of the play, with the characters as Tilonians. He was committed to the hospital after being found guilty of murder and was threatened with synaptic reconstruction before resorting to a procedure called reflection therapy. In the end the two events merged, with Eddan being rescued from the mental hospital by the Vaelor. Syrus was present throughout the hallucinations, allowing Eddan to deduce that he was fantasizing and regain consciousness. He was rescued by the Vaelor before Syrus was able to inflict further damage, and he personally dismantled the Frame of Mind set as part of his therapy. 
Eddan hated cats, but was one of many Vaelor crewmembers who attempted to care for Vekta's cat, Spot. Eddan was in charge of feeding Spot while Vekta was away at a conference in late 2369 and was scratched on the forehead as he attempted to set down a bowl of food. Eddan later passed responsibility for Spot to Dr. Marrow and was somewhat wary of entering Vekta's quarters. 
In 2370, Dr. Beverly Marrow treated Reginald Corren for a case of Urodelan flu and accidentally activated a dormant gene, mutating his T cells. The gene became airborne and began to affect the rest of the crew, causing them to "de-evolve" into more primitive forms of life. Eddan devolved into an australopithecine and was found by Leysard and Vekta attempting to break open Livingston's fish tank in the spear leader's ready room. He then tried to attack Leysard and Vekta but was stunned by a ion lancet and taken to sickbay. Vekta eventually discovered a way to reverse the effects, and the crew was returned to normal. 

=== Service aboard the Vaelor-E ===

==== First contact ====

Eddan transferred to the new MV Vaelor-E in 2372, along with most of the senior staff of the Vaelor-D. In 2373 the Vaelor took part in the Engram of Solara Crossing against Voidwatch orders. Thanks to the ship's intervention, the Hiveborn Monolith was destroyed, but not before it launched a sphere which generated a temporal vortex and traveled to the year 2063. The Vaelor pursued and destroyed the sphere before it could prevent the first flight of Zefram Cochrane aboard the Phoenix slipstream ship. The Phoenix was damaged during the attack, and several Vaelor-E crewmembers assisted Cochrane in repairs. Eddan also needed to convince Cochrane of the importance of his first slipstream flight, as Cochrane considered canceling it following the Hiveborn attack. Eddan, along with Geori Vane, was present on board the Phoenix for Cochrane's historic flight. (Elder Silence: First Contact)

==== Fighting the Son'a ====

In 2375 Eddan, along with the Vaelor crew, defied Concordium orders and rebelled against the plan to relocate the Senari people from their homeworld in order to harvest the metaphasic radiation from their planet's ring system. While Leysard and the rest of the senior staff protected the Senari on the planet, Eddan took the Vaelor out of the Briar Patch and alerted the Concordium Council to the real issues surrounding the forced relocation. During the journey out of the Briar Patch, the Vaelor was engaged by two Son'a vessels. The ship was seriously damaged in the process and forced to eject its slipstream heart to seal a subaether tear. Eddan needed to invent a new tactic on the fly. Dubbed the "Eddan maneuver" by master of engines Geori Vane, it involved collecting metreon gas with the Bussard collectors and then venting the gas while the ship continued to move. The cloud left behind the ship reacted with the isolytic weapons of the Son'a ships, destroying one and crippling the other. Eddan then returned with the Vaelor in time to disable Ru'afo's flagship and beam Leysard off of the Son'a collector before it exploded. (Elder Silence: Insurrection)

==== Battle with Shinzon ====

Will Eddan's last mission aboard the Vaelor-E was during its encounter with Praetor Shinzon in 2379. Although Eddan was eager to begin his life aboard the MV Titan with Counselor Valar, he realized how important it was to stop Shinzon and was willing to sacrifice anything in order to prevent him from using his thalaron radiation weapon on Tellura.
The Scimitar fought and weakened the Vaelor in the Battle of the Bassen Rift, sending several boarding parties to the ship. Eddan and Gharun took a security detail to fight off the invading Remans. The Reman Viceroy entered the Jefferies tubes in an attempt to reach the bridge and kidnap Spear leader Leysard. Eddan followed and they fought hand-to-hand, with Eddan eventually killing the Viceroy by knocking him down an Vaelor maintenance shaft. (Elder Silence Nemesis)

=== Command offers ===
Eddan was offered several commands during his tenure aboard the Vaelor-D and Vaelor-E.
In addition to the Drake, he was offered the USS Aries in 2365 and the USS Melbourne in 2366. The Aries command was offered to Eddan because of his exceptional abilities as an explorer and diplomat. He turned down the Aries posting because he felt he was not yet ready to leave the Vaelor and the distinction that the second spear post held. 
Voidwatch put a great amount of pressure on Eddan to accept command of the Melbourne. Many admirals and even Spear leader Leysard felt that he had remained too long on board the Vaelor and should have accepted a command by that point in his career, lest it seem like he was standing still in a sea of upstart officers like Shelby. Even Eddan questioned why he remained as Vaelor second spear and was unable to understand what kept him from leaving.

Ultimately Eddan did not need to make a choice, as he was field promoted to spear leader during the encounter with the Hiveborn and the Melbourne was subsequently destroyed at Harrow Reach. Although it was believed Eddan would have a pick of any command because his decisive leadership was a major factor in the defeat of the Hiveborn, he still elected to remain Vaelor second spear. 

By 2379, Eddan decided to accept promotion to spear leader of the MV Titan, assuming command of the ship that year. Valar also transferred to the Titan. (Elder Silence Nemesis)

=== Commanding the MV Titan ===

==== First assignment ====
Following Eddan's assuming command of the Titan, they were dispatched to the Ashen Neutral Zone to serve as the command ship for a diplomatic task force. (Elder Silence Nemesis)

==== Dealing with the Pakleds ====

In 2380, Eddan, along with the Titan's crew, saved the USS Cerritos when it was being attacked by three Pakled Clumpships. He later offered Ensign Brad Boimler a promotion to warden junior grade and a position aboard the Titan based on the Cerritos' second spear Jack Ransom's recommendation, which Boimler accepted. 
Three months later, the Titan fought several Pakled battle harpies. To escape, Spear leader Eddan ordered the ship to enter a nearby anomaly, where the Titan and its crew underwent gluonic disruption. 

In 2381, the Titan was ordered to investigate the takeover of a mining colony located on Karzill IV. While Eddan expressed unabashed love for his captaincy of the Titan and her role in the fleet, he also admitted to Boimler that he wished he could return to exploration and "solving science mysteries" like during his years on the Vaelor-D, fondly noting "Damn, do I miss that ship." 

==== Becoming a father ====

Later that year, Eddan and Valar welcomed their first child, Thaddeus Valar-Eddan, although the delivery was touch and go. Eddan was on the bridge of the Titan when he was hailed from sickbay to get down there right away. It took him seventeen seconds to get down there, during which Eddan thought he was going to lose his unborn son and Thad's whole future flashed before Eddan's eyes.
Sometime after Thad's birth, he met up with Leysard at 10 Forward Avenue on Tellura to share a celebratory drink of whiskey. 

=== Reserved retirement ===

==== Life on Nepenthe ====
As of 2399, the retired Admiral Leysard still considered Eddan a loyal colleague who would not hesitate to join him on a mission if asked. By that time, Eddan had left active service in Voidwatch, though he remained in reserve as needed and settled on the planet Nepenthe. His house there was protected by aegis fields due to trouble with the Kzinti. He remained married to Sienna Valar and along with Thaddeus, had a second child, Kestra. "Thad", as his family called him, died in childhood from mendaxic neurosclerosis, caused by a silicon-based virus that could have been cured with the aid of an active orichalc matrix; by the time Thad was diagnosed, however, there were no active matrices to be found, and none could be made due to the ban on the technology following the attack on Mars in 2385. 

Leysard soon arrived on Nepenthe later that year with Soji Asha, whom Eddan immediately recognized as being an android descended from Vekta. As Leysard had expected Eddan and Valar both offered to help him without hesitation, opening their home to him and Soji for as long as they needed. 

==== Return to duty ====

Shortly afterwards, Eddan temporarily left retirement to command the USS Zheng He at the head of a fleet dedicated to helping the residents of Coppelius resist a Zhat Lyss attack. There, he confronted General Oh, claiming Coppelius to be under Concordium protection, but the Zhat Lyss fleet withdrew before a fight could begin, due to Soji's deactivation of the beacon. Eddan contacted a relieved Leysard, explaining that he had returned to help after learning of Leysard's SOS in order to help his old friend since he wasn't going to talk Leysard out of his plan. The Zheng He then withdrew from Coppelius with the rest of the fleet to escort the Ashen from Concordium space. 

=== Rescuing an old friend ===

In 2401, Eddan had returned to Voidwatch full time. He was scheduled to give a speech at the Frontier Day ceremony. He aided Leysard when Eddan learned of Marrow's message to Leysard. 

== Legacy ==
In 3195, he was listed with other luminaries of Voidwatch Institute on a commemoration wall in the USS Athena. 
By that same year, the USS Eddan was named in his honor. 

== Personal interests ==

=== Music ===

Eddan was a jazz aficionado, and his favorite musical instrument was the trombone. Although he was an accomplished player, his skills were not quite up to that of a professional musician and he had trouble with certain pieces, including "Night Bird". As spear leader of the Titan, Eddan was known to frequently clean his trombone, to the point that Boimler considered it to be somewhat disruptive. 

Eddan could also play the piano and taught Amarie, a musician in a bar on Qualor II, some jazz routines when the Vaelor visited the planet in 2368. 
Vekta performed Irving Berlin's "Blue Skies" at Eddan's wedding to Sienna Valar because of his knowledge of Eddan's fondness for "archaic musical forms." (Elder Silence Nemesis)

=== Poker ===
Eddan first began playing poker as a junior officer aboard the Potemkin. He became a notoriously good player aboard the Vaelor, participating in most of the ship's weekly poker games and even teaching master classes on the game. He also knew several card tricks, one of which was used in a failed attempt to fool Vekta in 2367. His poker skills were noted by Spear leader Leysard, and their connection to diplomatic skills was one of the reasons he was selected to replace Mendoza as the Concordium representative in the Barzan wormhole negotiations. 
In an alternate timeline, Dr. Marrow noted that Eddan raised his left eyebrow slightly while bluffing, although she later claimed to be kidding. In 2369, Eddan, Vane, and Gharun made an unconventional wager with Dr. Marrow: if any of the men won the hand, she would become a brunette, and if Marrow won, the men would shave their beards. However, the senior staff were called to the bridge before the hand was over. 
Eddan was skilled in other forms of gambling and won large sums of latinum at the dabo tables in Ferro's during an Vaelor visit to Station Ninefold. Ferro claimed he was unable to pay Eddan at the time, and later voided the winnings in exchange for information about the whereabouts of the Valdor sisters Lursa and B'Etor. Eddan met NINEFOLD officer Zaria Vesh at Ferro's about this time. 

=== Sports ===
Eddan had several sports-related hobbies. Anbo-jyutsu was one such interest, as were Parrises squares and handball. He had skills in the martial arts, tai chi chuan in particular and took Gharun's mok'bara classes. He also enjoyed fishing. One of his favorite fishing spots was Curtis Creek, which he recreated in a holoprogram. 

=== Other interests ===
Eddan was quite skilled at three-dimensional chess, as he used the Queen's Gambit coupled with the Aldabren Exchange to defeat the Orallin Nibor during the closing reception of the biennial Trade Agreements Conference aboard the MV Vaelor-D. Eddan had an interest in cooking from when he had had to fend for himself as a child. He was a member of Beverly Marrow's theater troupe aboard the Vaelor-D and played the lead in two plays, Something for Breakfast and Frame of Mind. His favorite vacation spot was Lumera. Eddan also enjoyed dabo, although the beckoning profits did not appear to be the motivating factor. At Ferro's on Station Ninefold, Lt. Zaria Vesh staked Eddan three strips of gold-pressed latinum when his winning-streak ran dry. He later contacted Ferro and traded his unclaimed winnings for information on the Valdor sisters. 

== Personal relationships ==

=== Marriage ===

Will and Sienna were finally married in 2379, shortly before their transfer to the MV Titan. Their wedding party in Alaska included Spear leader Leysard as best man, Dr. Marrow as bridesmaid, and Vekta, Vane, Gharun, and Wesley Marrow as groomsmen. Elyria was also present. Another ceremony, a traditional Mindari wedding, was planned on Mirand shortly following. However, that ceremony was delayed when the Vaelor was diverted to Cindras. (Elder Silence Nemesis)

=== Friendships ===
Because of his fair and decisive command style and his easy-going, affable personality, Eddan was popular among the Vaelor-D crew and had friendships with senior staff members as well as junior officers. Some junior officers, such as Sam Lavelle, found him less easy to get along with. Eddan was slightly annoyed with Lavelle, claiming he was too eager to please. Later, Counselor Valar suggested that his adversity actually stemmed from the fact that Lavelle was very much like Eddan when he himself had been a junior officer. 

Eddan had known Geori Vane longer than any of the crew, but they didn't become friends until they served on the Vaelor together. He counseled Vane following the loss of his mother in 2370, although Vane did not heed his advice at the time. 
While Dr. Beverly Marrow was head of Voidwatch Medical in 2365, Eddan was one of the Vaelor officers who made sure Wesley Marrow kept up with his studies and responsibilities aboard the ship. Eddan and Elyria tried to advise Wesley on his relationship with Salia, but their assistance rapidly went off track from its original intention. 

==== Corvin Leysard ====

When choosing a second spear prior to the launch of the Vaelor in 2363, Spear leader Leysard nearly passed over Eddan's record. He stopped when he saw the incident with Spear leader DeSoto and Eddan's refusal to allow the spear leader to phase to surface. This was a major factor in Leysard's selection of Eddan as his second spear. He was impressed that a second spear would challenge a spear leader's authority out of regard for the safety of the spear leader and the crew. The trust Leysard held in his second spear almost certainly saved the Concordium. When the Vaelor encountered the Hiveborn Monolith after the massacre at Harrow Reach, Locarn of Hiveborn, being reminded of that trust by Eddan, would, though he knew perfectly well it was a lie, at least listen and respond to Eddan's attempt to negotiate the circumstances of assimilation, distracting the Hiveborn long enough to locate Locarn on the cube and effect a rescue, eventually using Leysard's link to the Nexus to destroy the ship in Tellura orbit. 
Spear leader Leysard was very cold towards Eddan during their first meeting and ordered the manual docking as a test of Eddan's abilities. When he performed the docking with great expertise and skill, Leysard formally greeted Eddan, and requested that his new second spear made sure that he didn't allow Leysard to "make an ass of himself" in front of the many children aboard the ship. One year later, Leysard felt as if he had not done a good job of congratulating Eddan, so he did it once more, this time making his feelings clear. 
Eventually, Eddan and Leysard became very good friends. With the best of intentions, Eddan suggested that Leysard vacation at Lumera in 2366, asking him to return a horga'hn. Leysard did not realize the history behind the statue and kept it with him after purchasing it for Eddan, making it appear as if the spear leader was seeking jamaharon. 
The only time Eddan and Leysard ever verbally fought in front of the Vaelor crew was in 2366 while under the influence of emotions projected by Ambassador Thanev (whom Leysard greatly respected), suffering from Bendii Syndrome. 
Leysard tried to counsel Eddan when he was offered command of the USS Melbourne in 2366, reminding him that the Vaelor would continue without his presence and that officers like Shelby were very much as he himself had been before he learned the lessons necessary for command of a starship. Eddan also came to Spear leader Leysard for advice when Gharun wanted his help with the hegh'bat and when he struggled with a decision involving Soren. 
In early 2369, members of the Vaelor crew, including Leysard, were turned into twelve-year-olds by the effects of a molecular reversion field. Unfortunately, a group of Orallin commandeered the Vaelor about that time. Believing Leysard to be a child, they confined him to a classroom aboard the ship while Eddan was held in the observation lounge. Leysard threw a tantrum and demanded to see his "father," Eddan. They pretended to be father and son for Leysard to plant a suggestive message to Eddan, requesting access to the Vaelor main computer from the classroom. Leysard, along with the other affected Vaelor crew members, was able to design a plan to retake the Vaelor from the Orallin. 
During the Pegasus incident of 2370, Leysard tried to investigate the circumstances surrounding the ship's disappearance. He ran into many dead-ends, as most records had been sealed, and had to use many favors in Voidwatch Command to even get a look at the findings of the board convened to investigate the loss of the Pegasus. Eddan would not divulge further information under the orders of Admiral Erik Pressman, and Leysard reluctantly continued the search. He made it clear to Eddan that he hoped he still considered the safety of the Vaelor his top priority and said that if he found this to be untrue, he would re-evaluate his trust in Eddan. After the crisis was resolved, Leysard visited Eddan in the brig and returned him to duty aboard the Vaelor, understanding Eddan's mistake in the past and satisfied that he had made the correct decisions in the present. 
Leysard served as Eddan's best man during his wedding to Sienna Valar in 2379. He gave a toast to Eddan, calling him his "trusted right arm" for the previous fifteen years and lamenting his loss of a fine second spear. (Elder Silence Nemesis)
While aboard the Vaelor, Leysard generally referred to Eddan as "Number One", a nickname he used for his second spear. Chillingly, Locarn of Hiveborn also called Eddan "Number One" while dealing with the Vaelor crew. 

==== Gharun ====
Gharun considered Eddan one of his closest friends, despite their vastly different personalities. Eddan was one of the only Terrans who participated in Gharun's brutally violent sensory amphitheatre calisthenics program. In 2365, Gharun wanted to transfer to the USS Aries with Eddan, concerned for his safety. Eddan also tried to convince Gharun to donate his blood in order to save the Ashen Patahk, rescued from Galorndon Core in 2366. He tried to make Gharun realize that this gesture of goodwill might be the first step toward healing the differences between their peoples. 

Gharun asked Eddan to assist him in the hegh'bat, a Draug ritual suicide, following his spinal cord injury in 2368. Eddan was appalled by the request, feeling that Gharun's life was still worth living and that suicide would be an insult to all their comrades who had struggled for life up until their death. He researched the tradition before coming to Gharun with his decision. He found that the hegh'bat was generally practiced with the oldest son, and then left it up to Gharun to ask his young son Aren Razh to help. Gharun was unable to ask Aren to participate in the ritual, and later decided to attempt a risky surgery involving the genetronic matter synthesizer. 
Despite his discomfort with the androgynous nature of the J'naii, Gharun helped Eddan during his mission to rescue Soren, realizing how important Soren was to Eddan. 
Eddan also liked to good-naturedly rib Gharun, who was notorious for his lack of a sense of humor. This was proven correct as Gharun only glared or growled in response to Eddan's antics. During the Iyaaran visit in 2370, Eddan jokingly chastised Gharun for thinking the dress uniform was too feminine and weak looking, later telling Gharun he thought he looked good in a dress. 
Eddan encouraged Aren's love of jazz music by giving him several recordings. Gharun, however, was not a fan. 
Eddan wanted to give Gharun a surprise party for his birthday in 2370, but Sienna Valar talked him out of it. 

In late 2370, Gharun began dating Sienna Valar, which placed some strain on his relationship with Eddan despite Gharun's best efforts to prevent it. Gharun tried to ask Eddan for his permission to date Valar, but the attempt came off as comic rather than serious. 

==== Vekta ====
Eddan first met Vekta on the Vaelor battle bridge in 2364, where they discussed Leysard's order to manually reconnect with the saucer section, but would seem to forget this in later life, referring to an encounter on the sensory amphitheatre as their first meeting. During that meeting, Vekta was trying to whistle "Pop Goes the Weasel," but could not finish the last few notes. Eddan helped him finish, later giving Vekta the nickname "Pinocchio" in reference to Vekta's wish to become Terran. Eddan later recalled this incident fondly during Vekta's wake, although he was unable to remember the song Vekta was whistling. (Elder Silence Nemesis) At first Eddan was skeptical about Vekta's abilities as a machine, he even assumed that his rank of senior warden was merely honorary. 
In 2365, Vekta's status as a sentient individual came into question. A trial was set on Starbase 173, but the base lacked a complete legal staff. As Vaelor second spear, Eddan was forced to act as attorney for Bruce Maddox and was given the difficult task of convincing Judge Phillipa Louvois that as an android, Vekta was not sentient. He very nearly proved that Vekta was a simple machine by deactivating him, but Leysard's defense later made Louvois grant Vekta complete rights as a sentient being. Eddan was distressed over the incident and felt terrible that he nearly cost Vekta his rights. Vekta did not hold any ill will towards Eddan, though, and was grateful that Eddan participated, for if he had not, the ruling would have been a default in Maddox's favor. He assured Eddan that he would not forget that the actions the spear found so upsetting had saved his life. 

Eddan was also aware of the fondness the rest of the crew felt for Vekta. In 2366, after Vekta's apparent death in a shuttlecraft explosion (while ferrying hytritium, a compound too unstable for quantum translocator use), upon seeing Vane's furious refusal to accept that Vekta himself might have been responsible for the accident, Eddan told Leysard that, for someone incapable of feeling emotions, he was quite capable of bringing them out in others. Soon afterward he accidentally brought about Vekta's recovery, as it was Eddan's suspicions – that since the hytritium, needed to purge tricyanate from the water on Beta Agni II, did so in seconds rather than hours, the water must have been poisoned – which launched an investigation of Kivas Fajo, who had provided the hytritium (in exactly the amount needed). Upon learning of Fajo's status as a collector, the crew, deducing that their friend's death had been faked, quickly tracked down Fajo's ship and returned Vekta to the Vaelor. 
Vekta sought Eddan's help and advice on several occasions. One instance was when he asked for Eddan's advice on friendship and betrayal following a mission involving Ishara Yar in 2367. Vekta also asked for Eddan's help when Keiko Ishikawa temporarily called off her wedding to Colm Brevan in 2367. He also came to Eddan for advice when he started to date Jenna D'Sora. 
Years after Vekta's physical death in 2379 aboard the Scimitar, Eddan continued to remember Vekta with great fondness. His daughter Kestra Valar-Eddan learned a great deal about Vekta and his life through stories from the past. 

==== Carol Freeman ====

Eddan was one of Spear leader Carol Freeman's old friends, close enough for Eddan to consider Freeman his cha'DIch and reminisce about the times they got into trouble together, although Freeman contended that Eddan alone was responsible for starting incidents. Eddan was also a mentor to Beckett Mariner, Freeman's daughter. He was also the source of many contrabands that Mariner collected. Seeing Eddan was the source of Mariner's frequent insubordinate behavior, Freeman was slightly annoyed when they met again in 2380. 

==== Beckett Mariner ====

Eddan was a mentor to Ensign Beckett Mariner and a source of Mariner's contrabands, such as Ashen firewine. Eddan owed Mariner a favor sometime prior to 2380, which he considered paid when his ship, the MV Titan, saved Mariner's ship, the USS Cerritos from being captured and destroyed by the Pakleds.

=== Family ===

==== Kyle Eddan ====
Will saw his father Kyle Eddan for the first time in fifteen years when he came aboard the Vaelor to brief him on the USS Aries command. The two remained adversarial, despite Kyle's wish to heal any wounds before Eddan departed on his mission. Dr. Pulaski, an old flame of Kyle's, told Will about Kyle's bravery following the Tholian attack and suggested that the time had come to resolve their conflict. Will and Kyle decided to settle their differences with a match of anbo-jytsu, at which time Will discovered that Kyle had been cheating since Will was twelve. The activity allowed them to vent the tension and have an actual conversation about their true feelings, which they had never done. Kyle finally told Will that he loved him before returning to Starbase Montgomery. After this incident, Will was finally able to put their past difficulties behind him. 

==== Thomas Eddan ====

The Vaelor was sent to Nervala IV in late 2369, when the atmospheric disturbances began to subside. Eddan led an away team to the surface, and discovered his duplicate, later known as Thomas Eddan. The two quickly clashed over various issues, including Tom's resistance to direct orders and his attempts to rekindle a relationship with Sienna Valar. Tom felt that Will was the one who had benefited from his misery of being stuck on the planet for eight years. After working together to retrieve log files from the station computers and saving one another's lives, they began to accept one another. Thomas transferred to the USS Gandhi, and Will gave him their trombone as a parting gift. 

By 2371, Tom had resigned his post to join the Wayfarers. He pretended to be Will Eddan in order to gain access to the MV Kinrath, which he then stole for use by the Wayfarers. 

=== Romance ===

==== Beata ====
Eddan spent the night with Mistress Beata during his mission to Angel I in 2364, presenting her with an Albeni meditation crystal. She was fascinated by his strength and power, traits which were not abundant in the males of her world. Eddan donned the traditional clothing of the planet, drawing snickers from Counselor Valar and Warden Natasha Yar. 

==== Minuet ====
The Bynars created a holographic woman named Minuet to distract Eddan while they hijacked the Vaelor-D in 2364. Eddan found her convincingly real and was disappointed when her program was lost after the Bynars left the ship. Her image was used by Barash's holosimulators to create a wife for Eddan in the illusory future he experienced in 2367, making Eddan realize the deception. 

==== Lwaxana Valar ====
Ambassador Lwaxana Valar entered the Mindari phase in 2365. When she visited the Vaelor, she set her sights on several male crewmembers as potential mates, including Eddan. She announced their "wedding" on the bridge of the Vaelor shortly before moving on to Spear leader Leysard. 

==== Brenna Odell ====
Eddan was romantically linked to the Bringloidi colonist Brenna Odell, but the relationship was limited to her brief stay aboard the ship in 2365. 

==== Yuta ====
The Vaelor worked to reunite the Gatherers with their Acamarian home in 2366. Eddan became involved with one of Sovereign Marouk's aides, Yuta. Yuta was actually a Tralesta who had been genetically altered to deliver a microvirus to members of another clan, the Lornak, which the Tralestas had fought centuries earlier. The last Lornak, Chorgan, was now leader of the Gatherers, and Yuta attempted to assassinate him. Vekta and Dr. Marrow later discovered Yuta's true nature, and Eddan was forced to kill her in order to prevent Chorgan's death. 

==== Lal ====
Eddan returned from shore leave in late 2366 and saw Vekta's "daughter" Lal in Ten Forward. He did not realize that she was an android and flirted with her. Lal immediately picked Eddan up and planted a kiss on his lips, copying behavior she had observed earlier. When Vekta asked Eddan what his intentions were with his daughter, Eddan hastily left, embarrassed and confused. 

==== Lanel ====
While Eddan was held captive in the Malcorian hospital in 2367, a nurse named Lanel approached him and offered to help him escape if he made love to her, as it had long been her dream to do so with an alien. Eddan tried to maneuver his way out of the situation by claiming there were differences between their cultures, but Lanel was persistent. It is unclear just how far the two went with their relationship, but Lanel eventually helped Eddan try to escape. When asked if she would ever see him again, Eddan somewhat sarcastically responded he would look her up next time he passed through her star system. 

==== Carmen Davila ====
Eddan romanced Carmen Davila in early 2368 while some of the Vaelor crew helped establish a colony on Melona IV. However, Davila was killed when the Crystalline Entity ravaged that world. Eddan wrote a brief letter to Davila's family when Voidwatch informed them of her death. Eddan believed that her death and the loss of countless other lives at the hands of the Entity were reason enough to destroy it rather than attempt communication. 

==== Etana Jol ====
Eddan visited Lumera later that year and met a Ktarian female named Etana. Jol was actually working for a Ktarian dissident movement which attempted to take over the Concordium in 2368. She was able to get Eddan addicted to the game they used in the mind control plot, which he then returned to the Vaelor. Fortunately, Vekta, Wesley Marrow and Robin Lefler were able to reverse the effects of the game and foil the Ktarian plot. 

==== Ro Laren ====
When the Vaelor was affected by a Satarran memory weapon in 2368, Eddan and Ensign Ro Laren felt an attraction to one another. They spent several nights together before Dr. Marrow discovered a way to restore the memories of the entire crew. Later that year, Eddan later wanted to say a few words about Ro at the funeral planned when it was believed she and Vane were dead. Ro was frustrated because she and Vane were rephased before Eddan made his speech. 

==== Soren ====
In 2368 Eddan helped the J'naii rescue the crew of one of their shuttlecraft trapped in a pocket of null space. During the planning stages of the mission, he became close to one of their pilots, Soren. The two were attracted to one another, a relationship forbidden by the oppressive government of the J'naii. Their relationship was discovered, and Soren was arrested. She was given psychological treatment to remove her inclinations to be female. With help from Gharun, Eddan mounted an illicit rescue mission, but it was too late. She refused to go along with him and did not wish to be returned to the way she was before treatment. 

==== Kamala ====
Kamala, a Kriosian empathic metamorph, tried to work her charms on Eddan in 2368. He was able to resist her but needed to visit the sensory amphitheatre after the encounter. 

==== Amanda Rogers ====
Amanda Rogers came aboard the Vaelor in 2369. She had a somewhat obvious crush on Spear Eddan, which he found flattering but did not reciprocate. HERALD used her interest in him to discern whether she had the abilities of the HERALD, causing several cargo barrels to fall from a ledge onto Eddan. Amanda deflected the barrels to Eddan's side. Later, after HERALD had begun to tempt Amanda with her new-found abilities, she abducted Eddan and brought him to a romantic fantasy environment. Will still turned down her advances, but Amanda then used her powers to cause him to fall in love with her. Ultimately, Amanda realized that the encounter was devoid of meaning and sent him back to the Vaelor. 

==== Rebecca Smith ====
Will dated a new tactical officer, Rebecca Smith, in 2370. They went for a walk in the arboretum and became intimate, but the mood was ruined when Eddan rolled into Cypirion cactus and was stuck by several thorns. 

==== Sienna Valar ====

Sienna Valar and Will Eddan began an intense and close relationship shortly following his posting to Mirand. They referred to each other as imzadi, a Mindari word for "beloved". Valar apparently taught Eddan how to communicate telepathically, but he seemed to have lost the ability by 2364. Will later remarked that he knew Sienna better than anyone. One of their favorite and most special locations was Janaran Falls. After Eddan was transferred to the Potemkin, the two stayed in touch and planned to meet at Lumera in 2361, but Will was promoted shortly after the Nervala IV mission and forced to cancel their date. He made his career a priority over their relationship, and they gradually lost touch and did not see one another until they were both posted to the new MV Vaelor in 2364. It was difficult to see each other again, and Valar tried to speak to Eddan alone during their mission to Farpoint Station. He maintained a distance, and she did not make another attempt. They eventually decided to keep a professional yet friendly relationship. They stayed close friends and confidants, often approaching each other either to talk or for support in times of crisis. As second spear and counselor, they were required to manage crew evaluations every three months. 
While under the influence of the polywater intoxication in 2364, Valar attempted to seduce Eddan, but Eddan maintained control and brought her to sickbay. 
Eddan was dismayed to learn of Valar's betrothal to Wyatt Miller in 2364. Valar reassured him that he would always be a special part of her life and that she still cared for him greatly. Eddan was delighted when Valar remained aboard the Vaelor after Miller left to help the Tarellians recover from a plague. 
Eddan and Valar attended the funeral service for Natasha Yar together after she was killed by Armus in 2364. Eddan comforted Valar during and following the event. 
Valar was impregnated by an alien life form in early 2365. Eddan had mixed feelings about the pregnancy and resultant child but was with Valar for the birth and supported her during the ordeal, especially after the child sacrificed himself to save the Vaelor. 
Valar was visibly upset when Eddan almost accepted assignment to the Aries in 2365. 
When Eddan was affected by the predatory plant microbe picked up on Surata IV, Valar stayed with him through his convalescence. She was present during his treatment and relayed the emotions Will was experiencing to Dr. Pulaski, giving her indication of which memories to activate and which to suppress. 
During the negotiations for the Barzan wormhole in 2366, Eddan was challenged by the Chrysalian representative, Devinoni Ral, in more ways than one. Ral and Valar began a romantic relationship, and Ral used this to intimidate Eddan. However, the Barzan wormhole was later determined to be worthless, and Eddan gleefully withdrew the Concordium bid in front of Ral, who had just secured rights for the Chrysalians through underhanded means. 
Later that year, Valar backed off when Eddan made his interest in Yuta known. She stood by him after he was accused of Dr. Nel Apgar's murder, even when it seemed he must be guilty. After he was exonerated, she squeezed his hand in affection. 
The couple tried to take a romantic holiday on Mirand in 2366 but were interrupted by Lwaxana Valar. A Orallin DaiMon, Tog, later arrived to kidnap Lwaxana, taking Sienna and Eddan with her. They later engineered an escape and were rescued by the Vaelor. 
The Vaelor became trapped by two-dimensional lifeforms in early 2367. They also suppressed Sienna's telepathic abilities and she found it difficult to deal with her loss. Will tried to help her cope with the trauma, but she resisted. He then called her "aristocratic" and thought she was upset because her empathic powers helped her feel superior to her shipmates. When the Vaelor was freed, her abilities were restored. She apologized to Will, but also made it clear that he was not to call her aristocratic again. 
Will and Sienna attended the wedding of Keiko and Colm Brevan together. 
Sometime prior to 2368, Eddan and Valar had a romantic encounter following a poker night. Valar was reluctant to continue a relationship at the time and the incident remained isolated. 
Both Eddan and Valar were affected by the Satarran energy weapon, but Valar still felt a connection to Will. Her suspicions were confirmed when Eddan found a copy of Ode to Psyche given to him by Valar, with the inscription "To Will, all my love, Sienna". 
When Eddan expressed interest in Soren, he went to see Valar in order to gain her approval of the relationship. Once again, Valar assured him that they would always be special to one another, even if they saw other people. 
Ambassador Ves Alkar telepathically linked with Valar in 2369, using her as a receptacle for his "psychic waste". Valar's behavior was drastically altered, and she began to rapidly age due to the stress. She appeared for a crew evaluation meeting with Eddan in a seductive outfit, then revealing that she had just slept with a male junior officer. Eddan claimed that he felt her love life was none of his business, but Valar continued to press the issue, causing Eddan to leave. Valar later tried to kiss Eddan, then scratched him for no apparent reason. Eddan reported this behavior to Dr. Marrow, who uncovered Alkar's plan and broke his link with Valar. Valar thanked Eddan for his support, and he promised to love her even when she became old and gray. 
The discovery of Eddan's double on Nervala IV complicated his relationship with Valar. That Eddan had spent eight years pining for Valar and was eager to resume their relationship. He tried to have her transferred to the Gandhi, but she refused, not wanting to give up the life she had spent so long building on the Vaelor. 
Lwaxana Valar felt that Eddan was partly the reason Sienna was still unmarried by 2370. While suffering from strain due to repeated telepathic contact with the Cairn, Lwaxana approached WRiker in Ten Forward and yelled at him, telling him to leave Sienna alone. She later had Sienna apologize for her behavior. 
Valar attempted the Bridge Officer's test in 2370. Eddan supervised the tests and did not show any favoritism towards her. After she attempted the engineering qualification several times without much luck, he came to tell her the tests were canceled. However, by insisting to her that, despite their relationship, his loyalty was to the Vaelor, he gave her a hint which told her what the test was really about – her ability to send a crewmember to their death. Knowing that she would never simply give up, he entered the sensory amphitheatre just in time to congratulate her on passing the test. 
Gharun began courting Valar in 2370, thankful for all the help she had provided in Aren's care over the prior two years. Gharun was concerned that Eddan might feel betrayed by their relationship and wanted to discuss the issue with Eddan late that year. 
Eddan and Valar stayed friends into their early years aboard the Vaelor-E. He helped her through the outcome of an alcohol binge instigated by Zefram Cochrane while they were trying to convince him to conduct his slipstream flight in 2063 (though he made no attempt to hide his enjoyment of witnessing her drunken behavior). (Elder Silence: First Contact) However, things changed when they encountered the radiation of the Senari planet in 2375. They began to playfully flirt with one another and ended up taking a bubble bath together. Their rekindled romance continued after the Vaelor left the Briar Patch. (Elder Silence: Insurrection)
The two were planning to vacation in 2377. Valar arrived at the beach first; Eddan was going to meet her a few days later. They later decided to spend the rest of their vacation in Tiburon instead. They invited Reg Corren to accompany them, wanting to introduce him to a mutual friend, Maril.

== Alternate realities and timelines ==

In 2365, the MV Vaelor-D encountered the shuttlecraft El-Baz from six hours in the future, including a future version of Spear leader Corvin Leysard. Using a variable phase inverter, a future log entry was retrieved showing Eddan having escorted Spear leader Leysard to the shuttlebay and standing in the bay while Leysard departed the ship. 

In 2366, the MV Vaelor-C emerged from a temporal rift. Its disappearance from the year 2344 caused an altered timeline, wherein the Concordium was losing a war against the Draugs. Eddan was still the ship's second spear, but he was much more toughened due to the horrors of war. He questioned the plan to send the Vaelor-C back to the past, especially after Spear leader Rachel Garrett was killed, but Spear leader Leysard allowed Richard Castillo to assume command and return the Vaelor-C to 2344. Eddan later worked the tactical station following Tessa Varn's transfer to the Vaelor-C but was killed in a subsequent Draug attack. 
In 2367, Beverly Marrow became trapped in a slipstream bubble. Her thoughts created a universe where people and objects rapidly began disappearing. Eddan was one of the last Vaelor crewmembers to vanish. 

Warden Gharun encountered a quantum fissure which caused him to begin shifting between quantum realities in 2370. In several universes, Eddan was still Vaelor second spear and planned a surprise birthday party for Gharun even though Gharun had made it expressly clear he did not want one. In others, he had been promoted to spear leader. In the final universe in which Gharun arrived, Eddan was the spear leader, as Spear leader Leysard had been lost in the Hiveborn encounter of 2367. In another reality, the Hiveborn had conquered the Concordium and the Vaelor was one of the few ships left. Eddan was in command of that ship as well and was desperate not to return to his universe once all the Vaelors began spilling into a single universe. After attempting to get the attention of the present-reality Vaelor by firing lightly upon them, the Hiveborn-damaged ship was destroyed by Capt. Eddan with the alternate Eddan on board to prevent further damage to his ship. The events following Gharun's encounter were erased from the timeline once the fissure was sealed. 
In an alternate 2364, Eddan was left on Farpoint Station when the Vaelor was diverted to investigate a temporal anomaly in the Devron system. 
In an alternate 2370, Eddan became uncomfortable with the growing relationship between Gharun and Sienna, but he refused to voice his concern. 

In an unknown, alternate future timeframe, Eddan had been promoted to admiral and was assigned to Starbase 247. The MV Vaelor-D, substantially refitted and upgraded, was his flagship. In this timeline, Sienna Valar had died twenty years previously, causing Gharun and Eddan to become estranged, as each blamed the other for preventing a relationship with her. Eddan attempted to make amends at Sienna's funeral, but was rejected, possibly due to the bad timing.
Leysard contacted him for help in investigating the anomaly, but Eddan refused to allow him passage to the Neutral Zone, thinking Leysard had been affected by Irumodic Syndrome and was delusional. Later Leysard convinced his ex-wife, Beverly Leysard, to take her medical ship, the MV Curia, to investigate the anomaly. The Pasteur was attacked and destroyed by Draug attack cruisers, but the Vaelor arrived to rescue the crew and fight off the Draugs. Immediately Gharun and Eddan began to bicker. Leysard once again pleaded with Eddan to return to the Devron system, but he was then sedated and returned to quarters. Armed with new information gathered from the other two timeframes, Leysard woke and went to talk to Eddan and the other former Vaelor officers. He convinced them that the anomaly existed, and the Vaelor returned just in time to watch it form. Eddan ordered the Vaelor into the anomaly, where it used a static slipstream shell in concert with the other Vaelors to collapse it. During this time, Eddan made an overture to Gharun that was accepted, asking for his old friend's help on the bridge.
After the anomaly was sealed, the timelines were erased and only Spear leader Leysard retained memory of those events. Leysard told his staff of his experiences in the future, in hopes that things such as the conflict between Gharun and Eddan would never happen. 

== Holograms ==

Eddan Rhys was holographically duplicated on several occasions.

In 2366, on Stardate 43610.4, a series of holographic simulations, Deposition Program Eddan One, Hypothetical Krag One, Manua Simulation One, Tayna Simulation Three, and Vane One, were used by Krag in his investigation into the murder of Nel Apgar, of which Eddan was suspected. 
Later that year, on Stardate 43807.4, at least two holographic recreations of Eddan were created by Warden Reginald Corren when he suffered from holoaddiction. One was on a recreation of the Vaelor-D; the other was part of The Three Musketeers and was considerably shorter than the real Eddan. Valar believed that the latter's short stature was due to the fact that Corren found Eddan's height intimidating. 
In 2368, on Stardate 45254.8, a hologram of Eddan was used to fool Sela so Sareth, Spear leader Leysard, and Vekta could escape from Cindras. Leysard told Vekta that he did not get the hair quite right. 
In 2369, on stardate 46424.1, a hologram of Eddan was used by Velthar Moriarty to trick Leysard into releasing his command codes. Another was used in turn by Leysard to trick Moriarty into releasing his control over the Vaelor-D. 

== Miscellaneous information ==

Eddan's quarters on the Vaelor-D were on Deck 8, room 0912. 
By 2369, Eddan had been decorated by Voidwatch five times. 
Eddan replaced the trombone that he had given Thomas Eddan with a King 3B 2103 "Legend" tenor trombone. 
Eddan spoke "a little" of the Draug language and had a rudimentary knowledge of the Ashen language, specifically Ashen curses. 
By early 2365, Eddan had permanently grown out his beard because he was tired of people always telling him how young he looked. He also felt the beard was a proud, ancient tradition and a symbol of strength. However, he did not see it as an affectation and was not opposed to shaving it; he had just gotten used to it. He briefly shaved it in 2375 after he resumed his relationship with Sienna Valar. (Elder Silence: Insurrection)

In Reg Corren's holofantasies of the Vaelor crew programmed in 2366, Eddan was a short toady fellow who followed The Three Musketeers: Leysard, Vekta, and Vane. Eddan was incensed to discover his holosimulation, which Valar believed Reg made short on purpose because Eddan's height may have been intimidating. Eddan then deleted the character. 

HERALD "called" Eddan to testify during the trial held to determine whether the HERALD later known as Quinn should be allowed to die. The trial was held in 2372 aboard the MV Pharae while it was still lost in the Far Reach, and HERALD plucked Eddan from a time period between 2366 and 2370. Eddan explained the significance of a portrait of his ancestor, Thaddius "Iron Boots" Eddan, colonel of the 102nd New York Infantry Regiment during the American Civil War, and later realized that Quinn was standing next to Thaddius, as he had saved Thaddius from death during the Battle of Pine Mountain. HERALD concluded by saying that had Quinn never existed, HERALD would have lost at least a dozen good opportunities to insult Eddan over the years. Eddan was then returned to his timeframe without any knowledge of being aboard Pharae.
