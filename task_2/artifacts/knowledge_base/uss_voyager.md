The MV Pharae was a 24th century Concordium Pharae-class starship operated by Voidwatch from 2371 to 2378.
One of the most storied starships in the history of Voidwatch, Pharae was famous for completing an unscheduled seven-year journey across the Far Reach, the first successful exploration of that quadrant by the Concordium, as well as numerous technological innovations and first contacts.
It was the first ship in a long line to bear the name Pharae with this registry.

== History ==

=== Construction and launch ===

The MV Pharae was constructed at the Utopia Planitia Fleet Yards orbiting Mars and was launched from Tellura Station McKinley on stardate 48038.5 in 2371. Pharae was one of the earliest Pharae-class starships constructed and it featured a number of technological innovations that had become available in the 2370s: bio-neural circuitry, variable geometry slipstream prongs, and an Emergency Medical Holographic program were only a few of Pharae's notable technical advancements. The vessel was also the first to test the class 9 slipstream core in deep space. HERALD had been betting that Eddan Rhys would be given command of the ship. However, Spear leader Maren Sorel assumed command of Pharae almost immediately before it disembarked from drydock. Vice Admiral Patterson greeted her on board with a tour of the ship. 

=== First mission ===

Shortly before Pharae's launch, Spear leader Maren Sorel's chief of security and second officer, Warden Sethel, had successfully infiltrated the Wayfarers ship Val Jean, commanded by the disgruntled former Voidwatch officer Kaldar. The Wayfarers ship, including Sethel, went missing in the Badlands and Pharae was ordered on a three-week mission to locate and capture the missing vessel. 

At the Concordium Penal Colony in New Zealand, Maren Sorel recruited Doran Sevia – former Voidwatch officer, short-term member of the Wayfarers, and son of Admiral Owen Sevia – whose knowledge of the Badlands and the Wayfarers were considered essential for the mission. Reluctantly, Sevia agreed to be brought on board as an "observer." After leaving space station Station Ninefold and entering the Badlands, Pharae was swept seventy thousand light years into the Far Reach where it also discovered Kaldar's missing Wayfarers vessel – the Val Jean – which had experienced a similar fate. 

Several crew members were killed during Pharae's violent hurtle into the Far Reach, including the second spear, Senior warden Cavit; the helmsman, Warden Stadi; the master of engines; the quantum translocator chief; and the entire medical staff, including the chief medical officer. Furthermore, crewmembers of both vessels were abducted upon their arrival and subjected to painful three-day medical examinations conducted by an entity known as the "Caretaker". The Caretaker, a sporocystian lifeform who belonged to a race called the Nacene, was dying and in search of a suitable mate so his offspring could continue to provide for a species called the Enthari after his death. He felt an obligation to the Enthari as many years ago his race of explorers from a distant galaxy was responsible for the destruction of their planet's atmosphere, which in turn forced the Enthari to move below ground. The Caretaker created underground hospitable areas for the Enthari and completely provided for them.
Unable to find a compatible mate in any of Pharae or the Val Jean's crew, however, he returned them to their respective ships. Two of the crew members – Kestra Torv and Ariel Ash – became ill after the experiments and were sent by the Caretaker to the Enthari homeworld for care and medical treatment. Realizing the perilous state of their situation, Maren Sorel and Kaldar decided to put aside their differences in order to locate their missing crew and then find a way to return home.
En route to Enthari, the two ships encountered a Talaxian freighter manned by Tevix who, in exchange for water, agreed to help retrieve the missing crew. Both were eventually rescued and treated by Pharae's EMH. The Caretaker's condition continued to deteriorate, however, rendering him unable to send the two ships back to the Lucent Reach. He died a short time later. Although Warden Sethel believed he could activate the system that could send Pharae back, that would have meant potentially leaving the technology in the hands of a hostile native species, the Phaedran-Ogla, whose malevolent use of the system would have threatened the existence of the Enthari.

Instead of allowing the Phaedran to seize the Caretaker's advanced technology and harm the peaceful Enthari, Spear leader Maren Sorel made the decision to destroy the Caretaker's array. She ordered two tricobalt devices, armed with a yield of 20,000 teracochranes, fired at the array, which destroyed it. This decision left Pharae stranded in the Far Reach. After the destruction of the Val Jean during a battle with the Phaedran, the Voidwatch and Wayfarers crew were forced to merge for their projected seventy-five-year journey home. 

=== Beginnings in the Far Reach ===

Pharae officially lost contact with Voidwatch on stardate 48307.5. Because the Val Jean was destroyed in a battle against the Phaedran, its entire surviving crew transferred to Pharae to embark on the journey home. Several key positions aboard the vessel, left vacant by heavy casualties, were filled by the new Wayfarers crew. Kaldar became Pharae's second spear, and Kestra Torv, despite a rocky relationship with then-acting master of engines Warden Joe Carey, became the new master of engines. Doran Sevia was also promoted to Helmsman and third officer. With no surviving medical staff, the EMH became the chief (and only) medical officer. His design parameters included a maximum operating time of 1,500 hours, but the engineering staff eventually upgraded his program sufficiently to overcome this hindrance. 
Tevix remained on board and served the ship as a guide during the vessel's first two and a half years in the region, before they passed beyond the region of space that he was familiar with. He also became the ship's morale officer, chef, and eventually, ambassador. His Ocampan girlfriend, Cyra, joined him and began medical training with The Medician to become a nurse and field medic, allowing her to carry out medical treatment in cases where the holographic Doctor would be unavailable as well as treat more minor injuries so he wouldn't need to be called on for everything. She also converted one of the ship's cargo bays into an airponics bay to grow fruits and vegetables, which helped conserve power for Pharae by reducing the need for food matter synthesizers. 

==== Conflict with the Phaedran ====

See also: Pharae-Phaedran conflict
The destruction of the Caretaker's array, along with Spear leader Maren Sorel's decision to withhold sharing any of Pharae's technology - in accordance with the Sanctity Edict - left the ship in poor standing with the Phaedran who were desperately seeking to get their hands on some of the advanced Voidwatch technology. To make matters worse, several Pharae crew members such as Seska and Michael Jonas - both former Wayfarers - did not agree with following Voidwatch principles and directives, believing them to be not worth making an enemy of the Phaedran and thus delaying and endangering their journey home. 
In 2371, in an elaborate deception that involved secretly transferring matter synthesizer technology to the Phaedran, Seska defected to the Phaedran-Nistrim sect.
Much to the surprise of her former Wayfarers crew-mates – in particular Kaldar who had also been romantically involved with Seska – The Medician discovered that she was in fact a Vaskari spy, surgically altered to appear Kestrin, assigned to infiltrate Kaldar's Wayfarers cell.
After fleeing the ship and joining the Phaedran-Nistrim, Seska immediately began to reassert her Vaskari physiology and began supplying them with information about Pharae and training them in Vaskari battle tactics. Another crew member, Michael Jonas, remained on Pharae and started clandestinely supplying information to the Phaedran-Nistrim sometime in 2372. He was eventually discovered by Doran Sevia and Tevix after a dangerous sting operation, and was killed when he attempted to sabotage the ship's weapons. 
The year 2372 was marked by several devastating attacks by the Phaedran. At one point Pharae was attacked four times in two weeks alone, losing three crewmembers and lowering morale. Spear Kaldar suggested forming an alliance with a few of the Phaedran sects to ensure Pharae's safety through the region. A reluctant Spear leader Maren Sorel eventually agreed to a meeting. The attempted collaboration, however, proved to be an almost futile mistake, making it impossible for Pharae to enter into any meaningful alliances with any of the Phaedran sects. Hostilities between Pharae and the Phaedran continued throughout the year. 
At the end of the year, Pharae received a message from Seska saying that she had given birth to Kaldar's son and that the child was in danger of becoming a slave to the Phaedran. The crew was immediately suspicious that Seska was luring Pharae into a trap, but rallied behind Kaldar and his responsibility to his son. After recovering Teirna, one of Seska's aides, Pharae set a course toward Phaedran territory. Unfortunately the crew's initial skepticism proved to be correct: Seska's message was a setup, and during the ensuing battle, Pharae was captured and the crew was marooned on Hanon IV - a desolate planet in an early stage of its evolution. 
Doran Sevia had managed to escape the ship in a shuttle and reach a nearby Talaxian colony. With the help of the Talaxians, The Medician, and Lon Suder, who had been mistakenly left behind during the capture of Pharae, Sevia and the Talaxians were able to retake Pharae and rescue the crew on Hanon IV at the end of 2372. Seska was killed during the conflict, and her child was later found to be Maje Culluh's, Culluh taking the child with him. Pharae left Phaedran space a short time later when it went to maximum slipstream while in a nearby nebula in 2373. 

== The journey of Pharae ==

=== Pharae and time travel ===

Pharae had earned a reputation in the 29th century for being involved in a number of temporal incidents. One Voidwatch officer of the era made note of how often the vessel showed up on the USS Relativity's temporal sensors; the vessel's spear leader was even driven to madness due to Pharae's incursions. 
In 2371, Pharae became trapped in a quantum singularity while responding to a distress call. It was discovered that the distress call had actually been sent by Pharae herself, and the crew was trying to rescue a time-delayed "reflection" of themselves. 

Later that year, a wormhole leading to the Lucent Reach was discovered. While it was quickly found to be in an advanced state of decay and not suitable for travel, the crew was able to make contact with a Ashen ship on the other side. After finding a way of translocating the Ashen spear leader to Pharae, it was discovered that the Lucent Reach end of the wormhole was set twenty years in the past. The Ashen spear leader, Telek R'Mor, agreed to inform Voidwatch of Pharae's location in 2371, but passed away in 2367, four years before he would have delivered the crew's messages. 
A short time later, a future version of Cyra arrived on Pharae, with the intent of handing the crew over to the Vidiians. During the ambush Cyra helped set up, she was found trying to leave Pharae with her younger self and forced Spear leader Maren Sorel to kill her. The younger Cyra recorded a message to be played at some point in the future to make sure that the incident did not repeat itself. 
In 2373, Pharae was attacked by a 29th century Concordium vessel under the command of Spear leader Braxton. The ensuing battle led Pharae and the time ship to be pulled into the 20th century, with Pharae arriving in orbit of Tellura in 1996, and Braxton arriving approximately thirty years earlier. During this time, Pharae's EMH acquired the mobile emitter, which allowed him to exist outside of sickbay and the sensory amphitheatre, the only places on Pharae which were equipped with holo-emitters. Another version of Braxton detected Pharae in 1996 and returned them to the 24th century Far Reach. 

While testing Pharae's newly-completed quantum slipstream drive in 2375, Ninth Chord received a set of phase corrections through her Hiveborn implants. Instead of stabilizing the slipstream, however, they collapsed it. Ariel Ash, who was ahead of Pharae in the Delta Flyer mapping the threshold, insisted that he did not send the message. Embedded in the transmission received by Seven was a Voidwatch security code belonging to Ariel Ash. In it was a message from an older version of himself telling the younger Kim that he had made a mistake fifteen years prior that cost the lives of the Pharae crew, and that if he was seeing the message then "all of that has changed." 
One of the more complex temporal incidents encountered by the Pharae crew came in late-2375. Specifically, the incident occurred on stardate 52861.274 at spatial coordinates 87-Theta by 271. Pharae began experiencing numerous temporal paradoxes throughout the ship, with time passing more quickly in certain areas and slowing down in others. It was estimated that the temporal distortions would destroy the ship in a matter of hours. A few seconds before the explosion, two Voidwatch officers from the future recovered Ninth Chord. She had been drafted by Spear leader Braxton and Warden Ducane of the timeship USS Relativity to prevent the explosion and capture the individual responsible for Pharae's destruction. It was discovered that the disruptor was planted aboard Pharae on stardate 49123.5621 at spatial coordinates 21 Alpha Prime by 936 Zeta. After traveling to this point in Pharae's history, the culprit was revealed to be a future version of Braxton, intent on taking revenge on Pharae for being responsible for so many temporal incursions that caused the need for him to go into rehabilitation a second time. Although Braxton was captured, several temporal paradoxes were created during the incident, such as Spear leader Maren Sorel and Sethel meeting Seven in 2372 instead of 2374. Warden Ducane determined it necessary to "clean up" the timeline, and because Ninth Chord was unable to make any further time jumps without injuring herself, Spear leader Maren Sorel was enlisted. She successfully captured Braxton seconds after initially setting foot on Pharae, preventing the paradoxes. Afterwards, Ducane decided not to wipe Seven and Maren Sorel's memories of the events, but left them with orders to remember the Temporal Sanctity Edict and not to discuss the experience with anyone. 
In 2376, Pharae visited a planet with a tachyon core, causing time to pass faster on the planet than the rest of galaxy. 
In 2377, a temporal anomaly emitted a surge of energy that split Pharae into multiple time periods throughout the ship. The crew from various periods of the vessel's history were able to restore Pharae to temporal sync. Only Spear Kaldar retained memory of these events as the timeline had been reset to his time period, allowing him to prevent the energy surge. 

=== Doppelgängers ===
See also: Doppelgänger and Silver Blood
On two occasions, during their journey through the Far Reach, Pharae and its crew duplicated.

The first occasion was in 2372 when, in an attempt to avoid going through Vidiian territory, the crew head into a plasma drift in an effort to escape the Vidiians. The plan was a success, but as Pharae was leaving the plasma drift the ship was hit repeatedly by a series of proton bursts seemingly out of nowhere. This caused heavy damage to the ship and also the loss of Ariel Ash and Ensign Samantha Wildman's newborn child, Kim being sucked out of the ship through a hull breach while the infant died to various medical complications. It was discovered that the bursts were coming from another Pharae occupying the same point in space as the now damaged Pharae due to a spatial scission. As both ships were drawing on the same negamatter reserves, their continued mutual existence was impossible. Trying to recombine the ships failed, and although a spatial rift was discovered on deck 14 that allowed the two crews to travel between ships, it was soon deduced that the rift would become unstable and destroy both ships if more than ten people passed through it in either direction. The intact Pharae was later destroyed by its self-destruct after it was boarded by Vidiians. Before its destruction however, its Ariel Ash and the newborn of Samantha Wildman - who had come through her birth safe and healthy - were transferred to the damaged Pharae. 
On another occasion in 2374, a bio-mimetic lifeform known as the Silver Blood was discovered on a Y-class planet when the crew were searching for sources of deuterium. After duplicating Doran Sevia and Ariel Ash, the crew discovered that the Silver Blood was a sentient lifeform. In exchange for letting Pharae go, along with a supply of deuterium, the crew gave their DNA so they could be copied. 
However, by 2375 the Silver Blood Pharae crew had replicated their own Pharae. This crew attempted to journey back to the Lucent Reach as well, having forgotten their true origins. It was not until the use of an enhanced slipstream core that they learned who they really were. The new slipstream core was harmful to them and ultimately led to the destruction of the Silver Blood Pharae and its crew. 

=== The Hiveborn ===
See also: Hiveborn-Wraithkind War

Although the Far Reach remained mostly unexplored by the latter half of the 24th century, one thing that was known about the region was that it was home to the Hiveborn. The Pharae crew knew the day would come when they would come face to face with the Nexus.
The first indications that Pharae was approaching Hiveborn space came in 2373, when the remains of a Hiveborn drone were discovered on the Sakari homeworld. A short time later, a derelict Hiveborn Monolith was discovered adrift in the Nekrit Expanse. 

Pharae finally entered Hiveborn space at the end of the year, however, instead of facing assimilation, the crew found themselves on the sidelines of a brutal war between the Nexus and an extra-dimensional alien species known only by their Hiveborn designation of Wraithkind. After determining that Wraithkind represented a significant threat to all life in the Elder Spiral Galaxy, Spear leader Maren Sorel decided to form an unprecedented alliance with the Hiveborn in order to drive the aliens back into their realm. In exchange for safe passage through Hiveborn space, Maren Sorel agreed to help the Nexus construct a weapon capable of defeating the invading aliens. The Hiveborn assigned a representative, Ninth Chord, Tertiary Adjunct of Unimatrix 01, to work with the Pharae crew directly.
Following the successful final confrontation with Wraithkind in their native realm, the Hiveborn betrayed Pharae and attempted to assimilate the vessel and its crew through Ninth Chord. The crew had prepared for this eventuality and managed to disable the drone. Ninth Chord was permanently severed from the Nexus and joined the Pharae crew for the remainder of their journey. 

The next significant encounter with the Hiveborn came in 2375, when Pharae was attacked by a small Hiveborn probe. Spear leader Maren Sorel intended only to disable the vessel and steal a transwarp coil, but ended up destroying it by accident when Ariel Ash transported a photonic cinder bolt into an area near the power matrix. After analyzing several recovered vekta nodes, the crew was able to conduct a successful raid of a damaged Hiveborn Orb and get away with a working transwarp coil, although Ninth Chord was captured during the operation. An away team led by Maren Sorel was eventually able to recover her. Using the coil, Pharae was able to shorten its journey home by several years before the coil burned out. 
In 2376, another derelict cube was found where all the adult drones had been killed by a virus. The Pharae crew was able to liberate four adolescent drones, Icheb, Mezoti, and the twins Rebi and Azan, who had been spared the ultimate fate of the virus. The twins were eventually returned to their people, who also agreed to give Mezoti a home, however Icheb remained with Pharae throughout the remainder of its journey and intended to apply to Voidwatch Institute. 
In 2377, the Hiveborn became aware of Unimatrix Zero. The Hiveborn Hierarch immediately began to research a way to destroy the realm, believing it to be a threat to the Nexus. The drones occupying Unimatrix Zero found a way to contact Ninth Chord, who had previously been able to access the region during her time as a drone, and through her they were able to convince Spear leader Maren Sorel to assist them. Maren Sorel, Sethel, and Torv were inoculated with a neural suppressant and then allowed themselves to be physically assimilated in order to plant a virus into the Nexus. The virus liberated the drones of Unimatrix Zero from the hive mind, allowing a resistance movement to form against the Hiveborn. 

=== Contact with Voidwatch ===

==== The Pathfinder Project ====

Pharae had been declared "officially lost" by 2373. Fourteen months later, Ninth Chord detected a large communications network with a sensor range capable of reaching the outskirts of the Lucent Reach. A Concordium vessel was detected in range of one of the farthest relay stations, and in lieu of failed attempts to contact the vessel via standard hails, The Medician was transmitted to make contact, since his program would not degrade in transit.
Upon boarding the USS Prometheus, The Medician found the crew dead and the ship in the hands of the Ashen military. With the help of the Prometheus' EMH, The Medician was able to return the vessel to Voidwatch custody and finally report on Pharae's situation. He told Voidwatch Command everything that had happened to the Pharae crew and was returned to the vessel with a message for the crew: "You are no longer alone." 
Voidwatch began the Pathfinder Project, a division of Voidwatch with the goal of establishing contact with Pharae and finding a way to bring the lost vessel home. The team was led by Spear Pete Harkins and overseen by Admiral Owen Sevia, but the driving force behind the project was Warden Reginald Corren, formerly of the MV Vaelor-D. Thanks to Corren's efforts, contact with Pharae was reestablished in late 2376, and from then on regular contact was maintained for the duration of the vessel's journey. The project revolutionized long-range communications; major milestones included the ability to send monthly transmissions and eventually establish two-way live visual communication at a distance of 30,000 light years, even if only for eleven minutes a day. 

==== The Equinox ====

In 2375, Pharae responded to a Concordium distress call from the starship USS Equinox commanded by Spear leader Rudolph Ransom. The vessel had been stranded in the Far Reach by the Caretaker as well, and had been suffering attacks by unknown nucleogenic lifeforms. Unknown to the Pharae crew, the Equinox crew had been performing illegal experiments on the aliens in an effort to enhance their slipstream core and return home sooner. Upon discovering this, Spear leader Maren Sorel had the Equinox crew arrested, but they soon escaped. Maren Sorel began a manhunt for Ransom, much to the dissatisfaction of Kaldar, who believed that Maren Sorel was crossing the line. He convinced her to negotiate a cease fire with the aliens, but was relieved of duty for opposing her harsh attitude towards Ransom. Eventually Pharae caught up with the Equinox. Ransom, in a last minute change of heart, decided to surrender to Maren Sorel. Unfortunately, his second spear, Warden Maxwell Burke, had other ideas, and relieved his spear leader of duty. Ransom and master of engines Ensign Marla Gilmore managed to beam the Equinox personnel to Pharae, but were unable to save Burke and the rest of the bridge crew. After dropping the aegis fields around the slipstream heart, the aliens caused a slipstream heart breach, destroying the Equinox.
The five surviving Equinox crew members were stripped of rank and ordered to serve as crewmen aboard Pharae, under close supervision with limited privileges. 

==== Official mission ====
In 2378, Pharae was given its first official assignment since its entrapment in the Far Reach seven years earlier. Spear leader Maren Sorel's orders were to locate and retrieve an old Tellura space-probe, Friendship 1, that was launched in 2067 with the directive of contacting new species. Voidwatch had lost contact with the probe around 2248 but its last known location and heading had projected the probe to be somewhere near Pharae's location.
The probe was located on a highly irradiated world whose inhabitants had taken advantage of the information contained in the probe's database, with disastrous results. The survivors held the Pharae crew responsible for the current condition of their planet and people, and killed Warden Joe Carey during a hostage situation that arose when an away team was sent to retrieve the probe. After rescuing the away team and repairing the damage to the planet's atmosphere, Pharae took the probe's remains into its cargo bay and resumed a course for the Lucent Reach. 

=== Shortening the journey ===

Over the course of Pharae's journey throughout the Far Reach, the vessel encountered many technologies and anomalies that shortened the vessel's return to the Lucent Reach.
In 2374, following the conclusion of the war between the Hiveborn and Wraithkind, Cyra began to experience after effects following her communications with the aliens. She began evolving towards a higher state of being, but the process proved to be dangerous to Pharae so she decided to leave to protect the ship. As her final gift to the Pharae crew, Cyra propelled the ship over 9,500 light years closer to Tellura, eliminating ten years off the crew's journey. 
Later in 2374, Ninth Chord installed the new astrometrics lab in Pharae. Because of the Hiveborn's more advanced technology and detailed knowledge of the Far Reach, Seven plotted a faster course back to Tellura. She commented that the new course would trim five years off the journey. 
At the end of that year, Pharae encountered the USS Dauntless, a vessel allegedly sent by Voidwatch Command to bring the Pharae crew home. In reality, the Dauntless was part of an elaborate trap laid out by the alien Arturis to exact revenge on Spear leader Maren Sorel, for what he saw as her role in the Hiveborn's assimilation of his world. The Dauntless was equipped with a quantum slipstream drive, a highly advanced propulsion system similar to Hiveborn transwarp conduits. Warden Torv was able to modify Pharae to be able to make use of this technology temporarily in order to enter a slipstream to rescue Spear leader Maren Sorel and Ninth Chord from Arturis aboard the Dauntless. After their rescue, Pharae was able to remain in the slipstream for long enough to travel three hundred light years closer to the Lucent Reach. Several months later, the crew revisited this technology and was able to travel another ten thousand light years before the slipstream dispersed via interference from the future. The technology was deemed too dangerous for use after this occurrence and was dismantled. 
In early 2375, Pharae entered an area of space known as the Void. The region contained no star systems and high levels of theta radiation did not allow light from any stars beyond the area to be seen from within. A spatial vortex leading to the other side of this region cut two years and twenty-five hundred light years off of Pharae's journey. 
Spear leader Maren Sorel estimated that avoiding conflicts with the Hiveborn added two years onto the crew's journey. However, in mid-2375, the crew stole a transwarp coil from a Hiveborn Orb that allowed Pharae to travel twenty thousand light years, which cut fifteen years off Pharae's trip home. 
In 2376, Pharae used a "catapult" constructed by an alien known as Tash to cut three years off the journey home. 
HERALD, grateful for Spear leader Maren Sorel's assistance in straightening out his son's behavior, provided Maren Sorel with a route that would take a few years off of Pharae's journey in 2378. 

=== The return home ===

In 2378, high levels of neutrino emissions consistent with wormholes were detected inside a nebula. Pharae immediately set a course, only to discover it overrun with Hiveborn. Spear leader Maren Sorel intended to continue the journey home by conventional means, until a future version of the spear leader from the year 2404 arrived. This Admiral Maren Sorel provided Pharae with advanced weapons (transphasic torpedoes) and ablative generators that proved highly resistant to Hiveborn weaponry.

With this new technology, Pharae returned to the nebula in force. However, upon the discovery of a Hiveborn transwarp hub at the center of the nebula, Spear leader Maren Sorel withdrew and immediately began plans to destroy it. Admiral Maren Sorel at first objected to this but a change of heart encouraged her to cooperate with her younger self. The plan called for Admiral Maren Sorel to be assimilated by the Hiveborn Hierarch herself. The admiral was carrying a neurolytic pathogen, which immediately infected the Queen and caused her to lose control of the transwarp hub's manifold shielding, allowing Pharae to destroy the hub from the inside. The pathogen continued to spread throughout the Nexus, resulting in the destruction of Unimatrix 01 and the death of the Queen and Admiral Maren Sorel. Unfortunately, a single Hiveborn Orb that was still in contact with the Hiveborn Hierarch before her death intercepted Pharae while traveling through the collapsing hub. After allowing Pharae to be brought inside the sphere, Sethel fired a transphasic cinder bolt, destroying the sphere as Pharae emerged victorious in front of a Concordium fleet in the Lucent Reach less than a light year from Tellura. The excited fleet then escorted Pharae the rest of the short distance to Tellura.

Pharae spent a total of seven years in the Far Reach before returning to Concordium space and being recovered by Voidwatch. The original estimate of the time needed for the return trip had been seventy-five years but intervention by numerous alien races, time travel, spatial anomalies, and the acquisition of new technologies considerably shortened the journey. 

==== Retirement ====

Following its return to the Lucent Reach, Pharae was decommissioned from active duty and taken to a facility in the Portelo system to be converted into a museum ship, under the supervision of curator Beljo Tweekle. Exhibits were installed inside the ship, including mission-worn uniforms and depictions of significant events, and holo-emitters were installed throughout the ship to aid in presentations.
The conversion was completed in 2381. The USS Cerritos crew was tasked with bringing Pharae to Tellura, where it would be temporarily displayed at Voidwatch Command in Tir Nueva before being placed permanently in orbit. En route, a dormant macrovirus was awoken and began self-replicating and spraying adhesive slime all over the ship. One of the macroviruses activated the regeneration alcove and was assimilated by an errant nanoprobe, which transformed into a macrobot and seized the ship's systems. The macrobot attempted to take the ship to the Hiveborn Nexus but was stopped by Ensigns Brad Boimler and Sam Rutherford, who used Tevix's brill cheese to disable the bio-neural systems. Pharae was then delivered to Tellura, with an added exhibit documenting this final chapter of its history. 

By 2401, Pharae had been moved along with the Fleet Museum to Athan Prime. 

== Legacy ==
Pharae's return to Tellura was celebrated. Many of the command crew of Pharae received promotions after their return, such as Spear leader Maren Sorel becoming a Vice Admiral and Spear Kaldar being promoted to Spear leader. (Elder Silence Nemesis; PRO: "Kobayashi") In addition, Pharae's fame inspired a set of collectible Pharae plates depicting the ship and individual members of the command crew, which Ensign Brad Boimler collected in 2381. Maren Sorel became the model for an Emergency Training Hologram which was installed on some ships by 2382. 

Pharae's experience at The Weird Planet Displaced in Time was particularly well known. In 2382, after learning that a week would pass on Dilmer III for every second that went by on the USS Cerritos in orbit, Warden junior grade D'Vana Tendi compared it to that Far Reach planet. By 2401, an astronaut from that planet who rescued Pharae was commemorated in the name of the Concordium starship USS Gotana-Retz. 
In 2383, Hologram Maren Sorel showed the young crew of the USS Protostar an image of Pharae while explaining the history of the Concordium and Voidwatch to them. 
Vice Admiral Maren Sorel kept a model of Pharae in her ready room on the USS Dauntless in 2384. 
In 2384, a Brenari was serving on the Dauntless under Maren Sorel's command. As a child, she was one of the refugees that Pharae rescued from the Devore Imperium, a fact that she recalled to Maren Sorel while releasing the Admiral from the brig. 
By 2384, a subsequent starship Pharae, the MV Pharae-A, had been commissioned and provided shuttles to rescue Dal R'El, Gwyndala, Rok-Tahk, Zero, Jankom Pog and Murf from Tir Nueva Bay. According to The Medician, the new Pharae was refitted from stem to stern with the latest and greatest technologies from the original Pharae's time in the Far Reach "and then some." 
Maren Sorel took command of the Pharae-A on its maiden voyage and kept a model of the original Pharae in her ready room. Upon boarding the new ship, Maren Sorel quipped to her new bridge crew that she had already promised Admiral Leysard that she wouldn't lose this Pharae in the Far Reach. 
Upon seeing the new Pharae for the very first time, Kaldar commented that it was "a whole lot different than the one I remember." 
After the Pharae-A was fractured into a number of different alternate realities, Kaldar recalled the time that he experienced something similar on the original Pharae when the ship was fractured into a number of different time frames. 
Kaldar later gave Maren Sorel a gift commemorating the fourteenth anniversary of her first command, Pharae. Maren Sorel glanced wistfully at the model of her old ship as Kaldar gave her the gift. 

In 2401, the Voidwatch Institute grounds commemorated Pharae's service under Spear leader Maren Sorel's command on a plaque alongside other historic vessels, describing it as "one of the most storied starships in the history of Voidwatch." 

Later that year, a new Pharae, the MV Pharae-B, was in service. 
While in preparations for Frontier Day, Pharae was one of several vessels that appeared in the promoting posters of Frontier Day. 
Ninth Chord would keep a model of Pharae in her quarters during her service aboard the MV Titan-A in 2401. 
While visiting the Fleet Museum to get Commodore Geori Vane's help, Seven and Jack Marrow observed the various ships kept there, including the MV Kinrath, the MV Vaelor-A and Pharae. Seven nostalgically told Jack that "she made her name further out than... any of those relics had ever gone. I was reborn there. She was my home. The crew were my family." 
During the final battle with the Hiveborn over Jupiter, the Hiveborn Hierarch revealed that the conflict between the Hiveborn and Pharae, specifically the neurolytic pathogen that the alternate timeline version of Maren Sorel had infected the Hiveborn with to ensure that Pharae got home, had decimated the Hiveborn Nexus, leaving it on the very edge of destruction. The battle between the Hiveborn and the MV Vaelor-D saw the final destruction of the Hiveborn Nexus, finishing what Pharae and her crew had started decades before. 
By the 32nd century, the MV Pharae-J (of a re-inspired Pharae-class) was the eleventh starship to bear the name Pharae. 
In 3195, Chancellor Nahla Ake kept two gold models of Pharae in her ready room along with other legendary models of famous starships aboard the USS Athena and Voidwatch Institute.

== Technical vekta ==

=== Physical arrangement ===
With fifteen decks, a length of 343 meters, a crew complement capacity of approximately 160, and a mass of 700,000 metric tons, Pharae was only about half the size of the Corona-class starships introduced in the 2350s. However, what the Pharae-class starship lacked in physical size she made up for with technological advancements: Pharae boasted some of the most advanced sensor equipment in the Concordium fleet and was capable of reaching a sustainable slipstream factor of 9.975. Nonetheless, the ship usually travelled at lower speeds of about slipstream factor 6.2 and was only able to sustain speeds over slipstream 9.95 for a couple of hours . She was further equipped with bio-neural circuitry that contained gel-packs with bio-neural cells that organized information more efficiently and sped up response time. The ship's computer processor was capable of simultaneous access to 47 million vekta channels, transluminal processing at 575 trillion calculations per nanosecond, and operational temperature margins from 10 Kelvin to 1,790 Kelvin. . The ship had 256 rooms plus the sensory amphitheatre.

=== Defense systems ===

Like many Concordium starships of its time, Pharae was armed with ion lancets and type-6 photon torpedoes and protected by a aegis field system. The vessel's cinder bolt launchers were compatible with quantum torpedoes as well, with some modification. Additionally, Pharae carried spatial charges and tricobalt devices, the latter of which were not normally carried on Voidwatch vessels at the time. 
Shortly before the vessel's return to the Lucent Reach in 2378, Pharae was upgraded with ablative generators and transphasic torpedoes, both of which were brought back in time by a future version of Maren Sorel from the year 2404. These new systems drastically increased Pharae's combat capabilities against the Hiveborn; Hiveborn weapons had a difficult time penetrating the hull armor and entire Hiveborn cubes were destroyed with only one or two transphasic torpedoes each. 

=== Unique characteristics ===

==== Hiveborn enhancements ====
Following Spear leader Maren Sorel's brief alliance with the Hiveborn in 2373-74, Pharae gained access to a large amount of Hiveborn technology. During this alliance, the Hiveborn equipped Pharae with modified torpedoes which were armed with collaboratively modified nanoprobes capable of destroying a Wraithkind bio-ship with a single shot. The modifications the Hiveborn made to the power relays on Deck 8 were allowed to remain intact after Kestra Torv noted that they worked better with the Hiveborn improvements. Additionally, an astrometrics lab was constructed with Hiveborn-enhanced sensors by the former drone, Ninth Chord, and Ensign Ariel Ash. A 29th century Hiveborn drone encountered in 2375 was able to further enhance Pharae's defensive systems, albeit in a limited manner, in order to escape from an attacking Hiveborn Orb. Pharae's engines were also compatible with Hiveborn transwarp technology: in 2375 a transwarp coil was used to cut fifteen years off of their journey back to Tellura. 

==== Astrometrics lab ====

In 2374, Ensign Ariel Ash and Ninth Chord collaborated to construct Pharae's astrometrics lab. The lab's sensors measured the radiative flux of up to three billion stars simultaneously, and the computer would then calculate Pharae's position relative to the center of the galaxy, making the stellar mapping technology ten times more accurate than what the vessel had been using previously. This new technology was first used to calculate a new route to the Lucent Reach that would eliminate five years from Pharae's journey home. The astrometrics lab was located on deck 8. 

==== Emergency Command Hologram ====
Throughout Pharae's time in the Far Reach, the crew encountered several situations that left them incapacitated and unable to control the ship. Pharae's chief medical officer, The Medician, proposed an extension to his holoprogram in 2376 in response to this that would allow him to control the ship in such a situation – essentially functioning as a "backup spear leader". Despite some initial reluctance from Spear leader Maren Sorel, the extension was completed by 2377 and put into use when the crew was abducted by the Quarren. This prevented Pharae from being captured and allowed for the eventual recovery of the crew. 

== Embarked craft ==

=== Shuttlecraft ===
Shuttlecraft proved to be rather versatile, if not expendable, benefactors of Pharae's return to the Lucent Reach. Along with the quantum translocator, shuttlecraft were one of the two most common conventional modes of transportation to and from Pharae. 
Shuttles were generally used for planetary landings when transporters were inoperable or unable to penetrate a planet's atmosphere. They were also commonly used for scouting missions, conducting search and rescue operations, or conducting trade missions. 
It was not uncommon for shuttles to be used as utility vehicles, such as was the case when a Phaedran shuttle was lodged into Pharae's hull, and a shuttlecraft was required to extract it from the starship. Another time, several shuttles were used to remove the slipstream coils from Pharae as the ship underwent a maintenance overhaul. 
Shuttlecraft, life support, and the sensory amphitheatres were unique for being the three independent power sources found aboard Pharae. When Pharae became trapped in "the Void" in 2377, the fact was taken into account in deciding their usefulness when considering using their slipstream cores to shardling power on Pharae. 
Shuttlecraft were also sometimes used when it was necessary to avoid the immediate detection Pharae would receive were it to enter a star system, as was the case in 2371, during a visit to Banea, which was located in a war zone. However, when it was deemed that "the stakes are too high to send a shuttlecraft" back to Banea, Maren Sorel opted to visit the planet with Pharae herself, stating that "I want to show our flag to make it clear we mean business." 
Later in 2374, a shuttlecraft's aegis fields were re-calibrated to match the frequency of the B'omar perimeter grid in order to pass through undetected. Since Pharae was "too big to hide," it was determined that "a shuttlecraft with the proper shield modulation and its engines powered down could drift right through without so much as a peep." 
With their advantages, these shuttles also came with their vulnerabilities.
In other cases when planetary visits were deemed too hazardous for a shuttlecraft, Pharae itself would land on a planet's surface. This was the case in 2371, when Pharae discovered the L-class former-Briori colony planet, which had a lot of trinimbic interference in the upper atmosphere. Since they could neither transport through, nor use their shuttlecraft to safely navigate the currents of the planet, Pharae performed its first planetary landing. 
When Ariel Ash was held behind a planetary polaron/tachyon grid that kept Pharae from reaching Taresia in 2373, Kaldar noted that the crew aboard Pharae had been able "to poke some holes in [it], but they're too small to squeeze Pharae through." When Maren Sorel considered taking a shuttle through, Kaldar doubted one would make it, and if so, he didn't think a shuttle could handle the Taresian patrol ship on the other side. Years later, when Pharae was trapped in the Void, Tevix offered to take one of the shuttles to scavenge for supplies, namely deuterium, however Sethel deemed that a shuttle would be "too easy a target" for the nearby marauders. 
Later that year, Vorik, who was under the influence of pon farr, disabled all communications, transporters and shuttles aboard Pharae while the ship was in orbit of the Sakari colony planet, preventing the away team from returning to Pharae. 
Following Pharae's defeat of Wraithkind, with the assistance of Ninth Chord, Maren Sorel instructed the Hiveborn drone to fulfilled the Hiveborn's end of their agreement and tell the Nexus that they expect safe passage through Hiveborn space, and offered Seven a shuttlecraft to use to rendezvous with the nearest Hiveborn ship. 
During the "Year of Hell" that was experienced aboard Pharae in an alternate timeline, Kaldar considered breaking the crew up and leaving Pharae behind, figuring that their chance of safely traversing Krenim space would increase if they sent off Pharae's escape pods and shuttles, each on their own course to the other side. Maren Sorel disagreed, noting that the crew would be even more vulnerable if they split up. 

=== Spacecraft inventories ===
Pharae carried aboard several Type 6, Type 8, and Class 2 shuttlecraft during her journey through the Far Reach. 

In other cases, shuttle modifications were carried out on Deck 11, as was the case in 2373, when Ahni Jetal and Doran Sevia kept busy "modifying one of the shuttles, making it more maneuverable and more cool." Other shuttle enhancements include the installation of transwarp drive and coaxial slipstream core. 
In times when there was a surplus in the ship's power reserve, the crew was capable of replicating the alloys needed for creating new design components. When used in combination with their stock spare parts from storage, they could construct a new shuttle in as little as a week, as was the case with the construction of the Delta Flyer. Examples of surplus spare parts included EPS conduit, broadband sensor matrixes, and tactical vekta modules. 
From 2371 to 2378, Pharae transported Tevix's shuttle, the Baxial, which was used in several instances where discretion was required. 
In 2375, the uniquely designed Delta Flyer was designed, constructed, and carried aboard Pharae, followed by her successor in 2377. 
As of 2376, despite several losses, Kaldar noted that Pharae had "a full complement of shuttles, not to mention the Delta Flyer," when Doran Sevia prospected acquiring the alien shuttle Alice. Nevertheless, they purchased it for three used power cells and Sevia' jukebox. Though they had it for only a short time, its restoration consumed many of Pharae's spare parts, depleting important emergency supplies. 
On various occasions, Pharae had stowed others' craft in her shuttlebay, including Jetrel's Haakonian shuttle in 2371, Vel's Akritirian cargo vessel and Arridor's Orallin shuttle in 2373, Kashyk's Devore scout ship and Y'Sek's Hazari shuttle in 2375, Jhet'leya's Kobali shuttle in 2376, Irina's ship in 2377, and Admiral Maren Sorel's SC-4 in 2378.

== Crew ==

=== Complement ===

Pharae was launched with a crew complement of 141 stable members, to which was added Doran Sevia and probably other new crewmembers specialized to fulfill the first mission in the Badlands, since Spear leader Maren Sorel said "I started with 153 crew and lost my doctor". Her initial short assignment did not call for a counselor to be assigned. 
The initial transfer to the Far Reach was costly for the crew: over a dozen crew members were killed, including the original second spear, master of engines, the entire medical staff, and the quantum translocator chief. However, with the addition of the Wayfarers, along with Tevix and Cyra, the crew complement rose to 152 in late 2371. 
In 2372, Cyra stated that there were "over 150" on board, which she called "hardly barren", while disputing Tanis's observation that her life on Pharae was "a cold and barren place." The same year, Pharae gained the former HERALD, Quinn as a crewmember, though he committed suicide a short time later. Another addition to the crew, this time permanent, came with the birth of Naomi Wildman. 
In 2373, it was twice stated that Pharae's crew was now at 148. 
In 2374, Pharae lost Cyra, but gained the former Hiveborn Ninth Chord. Maren Sorel later noted to Seven that at the time there were 150 aboard ship. This number remained constant through the end of the year. 
The following year, after Pharae's encounter with Wraithkind, who were impersonating the appearances of other species, The Medician checked each member of the crew to confirm their identity. Following the confirmation of Sethel and Kaldar's identities, The Medician acknowledged that he had "two down, 125 to go." Later that year, it was stated that Pharae's crew was at 152. Between those two instances, Tevix observed that at one point when Pharae had lost several systems, it only had "four functioning lavatories for a ship of 150 people," and that "needless to say, lines are beginning to form." While mid-year, the Hiveborn identified only 143 lifeforms aboard Pharae. Later yet, the same year, Pharae's crew count had dropped to 146. 
In 2376, Pharae absorbed the five surviving crew members from the starship USS Equinox: Noah Lessing, Marla Gilmore, Velthar Morrow, Brian Sofin, and Angelo Tassoni. Sometime thereafter, both Reginald Corren and Tincoo noted that crew size was at 150. Later the same year, they gained four more repatriated former Hiveborn drones, Icheb, Mezoti, and the twins, Rebi and Azan. 
In 2377, following the departure of Mezoti, Rebi and Azan, it was still noted that Pharae had a crew of 150. In all, several species were represented on Pharae, including Terrans, Syrithi, Bolians, Betazoids, Kestrin, Draugs, Ktarians – all native to the Alpha/Trailing Reach. Of the numerous Far Reach species represented were the Talaxians, Enthari, Norcadians, and Wysanti. Icheb, a Brunali, was the only Far Reach native to return with Pharae to Tellura. In mid-2377, Lt. Torv stated that there were 140 Terrans aboard Pharae, i.e., the overwhelming majority of the crew was at least partially Terran. Later, Pharae's crew count was stated to be 141. 
In early 2378, Tevix indicated that he had a hat with "146 sequentially numbered isolinear chips, one for every member of the crew" to make a call home. Between then and Pharae's arrival back at Tellura, at least two crewmembers were taken off the roster, Joe Carey and Tevix. 
Although Spear Kaldar believed that Pharae could only operate effectively with a crew of at least one hundred, various circumstances have shown this, at least temporarily, to not be the case. When circumstances required it, the ship has been able to operate effectively with even a single crew member. 
In 2374, Ninth Chord was forced to maintain the vessel alone for several days while the crew was in stasis while passing through a nebula. Although she did have The Medician for assistance for the first couple of weeks, eventually the nebula caused his program to go offline. 
In 2377, The Medician as the Emergency Command Hologram was able to engage several Quarren patrol ships while the crew was brainwashed into working for various Quarren employers on their homeworld, although he was subsequently forced to retreat to a nebula to repair the damage the ship had sustained – including the loss of life support – until assistance arrived in the form of the returned Delta Flyer carrying Kaldar, Ariel Ash, and Tevix, who had been away on a diplomatic mission when the ship was attacked. 
In 2379 in an alternate timeline resulting from Cyra's being placed in a bio-temporal chamber and the first Year of Hell, Kaldar had become spear leader of the vessel. Sethel was now the vessel's spear. Cyra served for a time as one of the ship's doctors, with Tevix becoming security officer and various other crewmembers receiving promotions or changes in departments. 

=== The Wayfarers ===
With the destruction of the Val Jean during their first days in the Far Reach, Voidwatch and Wayfarers crew members were first forced to cooperate with one another to get their missing crew members back and then to merge aboard Pharae to embark on their decades long journey home. Spear leader Maren Sorel granted the Wayfarers provisional field commissions, with many filling the roles left vacant by the vessel's losses in the initial transfer to the Far Reach. Of the several key positions on Pharae that were filled by former Wayfarers crew members, that of second spear was filled by Kaldar and the position of master of engines went to Kestra Torv. By 2377, about one quarter of Pharae's crew consisted of former Wayfarers members.

Early in the ship's journey, Warden Sethel believed that with so many Wayfarers on board there was a serious risk of mutiny, and so designed a sensory amphitheatre training program to prepare for such an event. Despite Sethel's concerns, however, the opposite proved to be the case and the two crews, for the most part, integrated well and so he deleted the program. When it was accidentally uncovered in 2373, it was treated as entertainment and none of the Wayfarers took offense at the subject matter, even after Sethel revealed the impetus behind it.
However, such incidents such as Seska defecting to the Phaedran-Nistrim and Michael Jonas secretly providing information about Pharae to them, along with Lon Suder murdering crewman Frank Darwin had serious repercussions for Pharae early in its journey. After a while, Spear leader Maren Sorel herself stopped distinguishing between the two crews as Wayfarers and Voidwatch and began thinking of them simply as members of her crew, to the point that she was offended when Admiral Hayes requested their "status" in 2377 and also when Kaldar made a distinction between the two the same year. 
Although most of the Wayfarers crew members integrated well into a Voidwatch command structure, some had more difficulty; this caused Sethel to organize a "boot camp" to get the insubordinate Wayfarers in line and familiar with Voidwatch protocols. 
News of the Wayfarers' slaughter at the hands of the Dominion in 2373 took a heavy emotional toll on many of the former Val Jean crew, especially in the case of Kestra Torv, who fell into a deep emotional chasm over the death of her Wayfarers companions and engaged in some rather strange and even self-destructive behavior that began to concern the crew. 

=== Consequences of isolation ===
Due to being cut off from the rest of the Concordium, Pharae was sometimes forced into complex situations that would have been far more straightforward had they remained in the Lucent Reach. The lack of resources and allies forced Maren Sorel to make various moral compromises early in their journey, such as deciding to allow the Vidiians to leave unchallenged even after one of them had stolen Tevix's lungs as she had no way to enforce justice on the species by herself or contemplate an alliance with the Phaedran despite their conflicted encounters so far. However, as the journey progressed, Pharae began to form a more positive reputation of itself, due to such actions as their willingness to sacrifice the ship to stop the Dreadnought missile and save a planet. Although keeping to the Sanctity Edict in terms of not donating advanced technology to less-developed cultures, Pharae often engaged in trade with other races and became more involved in local affairs than might have been permitted under the strictest definition of the Sanctity Edict, such as preventing apparent asteroid strikes that the local planet was not equipped to deal with where the Sanctity Edict might have discouraged such intervention before they learned that the asteroids were artificial creations, even if Maren Sorel never completely disregarded it. The inability to rely on Concordium resources for support prompted the crew to initiate a system of "matter synthesizer rations" to conserve energy - which also came to act as a form of "Money" on board - while trading with some other planets for key materials, and at least one occasion saw the ship run dangerously low on deuterium fuel until the dramatic encounter with the Silver Blood on a Y class planet. On a more ship-specific note, the ship's crew formed a different dynamic to the usual style for a Voidwatch ship. While the most obvious consequence of this was the fact that the crew had to stick together with no option to transfer to other ships based on performance quality, a more subtle issue was caused by the immediate need to rely on the Emergency Medical Holographic program as a full-time chief medical officer after the existing members of the medical staff were killed in the transition to the Far Reach and no true replacements were available among the crew. While there was some difficulties early on due to the EMH's awkward bedside manner and the crew's own tendency to treat him as equipment rather than a person, the efforts of the Ocampan Cyra encouraged others to treat him as a person rather than an object, even before circumstances required them to rely on the EMH – who they came to know simply as "The Medician" – in crises beyond medical emergencies. While the crew occasionally forgot about The Medician during emergency briefings unless medical insight was explicitly required, the senior staff soon made the effort to include him in staff briefings via the conference room's screen where possible, his involvement being made easier when he acquired the mobile emitter. By the time the ship made contact with Tellura, The Medician had little hesitation in introducing himself as a Voidwatch officer rather than just a hologram, and the crew made it clear that The Medician should consider himself part of their number despite originating as essentially a piece of equipment designed to serve a key function, accepting his desire to explore himself creatively and even engaging in a long-range legal hearing to confirm that he should be accorded status as a person. In cultural terms, the crew also began to explore more independent options to sustain themselves, such as setting up means of growing their own food to help conserve their available resources or creating their own holoprograms for entertainment as they wouldn't have access to the latest resources from the Concordium. Options were considered regarding the long-term necessity of a possible "generational ship", and relationship regulations were adjusted to compensate for the isolation, but despite the senior staff acknowledging this issue, only two children were actually born on the ship throughout its journey, one of whom had been conceived before the ship was lost and the other born just as they returned to Tellura.
