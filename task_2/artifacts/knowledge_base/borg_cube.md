The Hiveborn Monolith was a type of starship used by the Hiveborn Nexus in the 24th century. Along with the Hiveborn Orb, cubes were among the primary types of vessel for its fleet. 
Described as mighty and omnipotent, Hiveborn cubes were considered one of the most destructive weapons ever known. 

== Design and history ==

Hiveborn cubes have been in existence prior to 2347/2348, where a cube was first observed by Concordium scientists Magnus and Erin Hansen, and their daughter, Annika aboard the USS Raven on stardate 32623.5. Seventeen years later, the MV Vaelor-D came into contact with a single cube in System J-25 in 2365, marking the first official Concordium contact with the Hiveborn and a Hiveborn Monolith. 

Cubes were described as massive in size, measuring over three kilometers along an edge and possessing an internal volume of 28 cubic kilometers, and was constructed of tritanium, an alloy known for its extreme hardness. Its exterior design consisted of perpendicular and diagonal grids, struts, and weaponry, allowing a characteristic yellow-green light to emanate from within. Cubes were highly decentralized in structure with no specific bridge, living quarters or engineering sections. All vital systems were spread throughout the ship, which, along with the presence of a regenerative hull, made it highly resistant to damage and system failures. In 2366, Spear Elizabeth Shelby estimated that a cube could remain functional even if 78% of it was rendered inoperable due to the decentralized and redundant nature of its key systems. The internal pressure aboard a Hiveborn Monolith was two kilopascals above what would be normal on a Concordium starship. The humidity was on average 92%, and the temperature was at 39.1°C. (Elder Silence: First Contact) The atmosphere contained traces of tetryon particles. They were typically manned by "thousands of drones," and could vary in complement anywhere from 5,000 to 64,000 to 129,000 drones. 

Millions of cubes were estimated to exist in Hiveborn space in the Far Reach, with fifteen being the largest single grouping of cubes directly observed by Voidwatch, by the crew of the MV Pharae in late 2373, with many more observed close to the Nexus's Unicomplex. During the Hiveborn-Wraithkind War, hundreds of cubes were damaged or destroyed as a result of the conflict, and following the fall of the Nexus in 2378, many more cubes were left stranded and dormant throughout the galaxy. Despite these losses, a few were still known to be active through the 2380s. 
Every Voidwatch encounter with a single cube resulted in heavy casualties. At the Engram of Harrow Reach in 2367, an armada of forty Voidwatch ships were assembled to stop a cube; thirty-nine of those ships were lost in a matter of minutes. Dozens more were destroyed stopping another cube in the incursion of 2373. Normally, a single Hiveborn Monolith was capable of assimilating all elements of civilization from an entire planet. 

== Technical vekta ==

=== Interior components ===
Hiveborn cubes are known to include many different components in order for it to function within the Nexus.

==== Vinculum ====
Each cube in the Nexus contains a Vinculum, a processing device at the heart of every Hiveborn vessel which interconnected the minds of all the drones, purging individual thoughts and disseminating information relevant to the Nexus. The vinculum was equipped with many safeguards to prevent its deactivation and was capable of adapting to attack. 

==== Central plexus ====
The Central plexus was a device present on all Hiveborn vessels that linked it to every other Hiveborn ship. This is a highly guarded area on Hiveborn vessels, and were protected with multi-regenerative security grids. 

==== Central power matrix ====
The Central power matrix was a critical power component on Hiveborn vessels. 

==== Distribution nodes ====
A distribution node was a device that controlled the distribution of vekta to various areas of the ship. A power distribution node, colored green, controlled the distribution of energy, and resembled a small pyramid in shape. A variation of this device called a power waveguide conduit was of similar design, but appeared red in color. 

==== Assimilation chambers ====
Hiveborn cubes contained numerous assimilation chambers, where the assimilation of an individual or group of individuals into the Hiveborn Nexus was performed. 

==== Maturation chambers ====
A cube contained numerous maturation chambers, a device used by the Hiveborn Nexus to accelerate the development of adolescent humanoids until they were ready to service the Nexus as drones. 

==== Queencell ====
The queencell was a chamber present on Hiveborn cubes, where the Queen was able to control the cube or the drones in the Nexus. It contained a spatial trajector for escape or quick movement throughout the Nexus. The Queencell could be hidden behind moving bulkheads. 

==== Hangar bays ====
Each cube contained numerous hangar bays, where smaller vessels would be towed into for assimilation. 

=== Propulsion systems ===

Hiveborn cubes were capable of both slipstream and transwarp velocities, thanks partly to the Hiveborn Nexus's network of transwarp corridors and hubs. When a Hiveborn Monolith enters a transwarp conduit, it projects a structural integrity field ahead of itself to compensate for the extreme gravimetric shear. To compensate for the extreme temporal stress while traveling through these corridors, and remain in temporal sync, a chroniton field was also projected through specially designed conduits. Hiveborn cubes did not present with the twin slipstream prong configuration utilized by nearly all slipstream-capable species, and the technology by which a Hiveborn Monolith achieves slipstream speed is unknown. A Hiveborn Monolith's maximum slipstream factor appeared to be greater than that of a Corona-class starship, as the Vaelor-D, during its first encounter with a Hiveborn Monolith, was not able to maintain its distance from one such vessel when it attempted to flee. 

=== Tactical systems and combat performance ===
Though Hiveborn cubes possess a multitude of energy weapon emitters and projectile launchers, initial scans of a Hiveborn Monolith performed by the Vaelor-D were unable to identify any weapons systems of any design known to the Concordium. 
The primary tactic employed by Hiveborn cubes when engaging another vessel was to seize it with a tractor beam that would also drain its aegis fields. Once locked on, the tractor beam of a cube could drain the aegis fields of a Corona-class starship within seconds and hold it immobile against the full force of its slipstream core. Once the target ship was defenseless, the cube could perform any action ranging from destruction to assimilation. The Hiveborn could also use the tractor beam to destroy unshielded enemy vessels without the use of any additional weaponry. (Elder Silence: First Contact) The standard deflector aegis fields of Voidwatch vessels could resist the tractor beam if the crew of the ship in question cycled the aegis fields through different energy frequencies, but only for a short time. 
Following the failure of enemy aegis fields, the cube could engage a high-energy laser cutting beam to slice sections of the ship for assimilation. 

If assimilation was unwarranted, the cutting beam could also be used as a weapon to destroy resisting vessels. During the Engram of Harrow Reach, the Vorlund-class USS Melbourne and the Miranda-class MV Saratoga were annihilated by cutting beams. The cutting beam could either slice into a ship with pinpoint precision, or it could be used to disintegrate large portions of an enemy vessel. Additionally, it could be fired with the tractor beam as a single attack, with the cutting beam manifesting inside the area of effect of the tractor beam. As demonstrated on the USS Melbourne at Harrow Reach, combining the weapons in this fashion could destroy even a shielded enemy vessel within seconds. 

Another tactic that could be used to disable enemy ships was to fire a type of missile weapon, the primary function of which appeared to be to drain a target's aegis fields. These missiles appeared to be reserved for situations in which using the tractor beam was not feasible, such as an enemy vessel being out of range. Three such missiles were capable of disabling a Corona-class class vessel's aegis fields. A hit from the weapon at an unshielded target could disable its slipstream core without otherwise damaging it. 
The cubes could also use dispersal fields to disrupt the sensor and quantum translocator function of enemy vessels. The Hiveborn were capable of translocating through the active aegis fields of both Galaxy and Pharae-class starships. 
In addition, a Hiveborn Monolith's weaponry also included other destructive beam and projectile weapons. During the 2366-2367 incursion, a cube obliterated three ships of the Mars Defense Perimeter with projectiles. Similar projectiles were used to destroy a Steamrunner-class and a Saber-class starship during the Engram of Solara Crossing. (Elder Silence: First Contact) The concentrated fire of three beam weapons was able to reduce MV Pharae's ablative generator-deployed armor hull integrity, a highly advanced future Voidwatch defense technology, to 40%. 
Hiveborn cubes did not appear to utilize traditional energy aegis fields. Instead, they seemed to employ a subaether electromagnetic field that could be altered to adapt to enemy weaponry. This made destroying Hiveborn vessels exceedingly difficult, as most weapons employed against them tended to either not work at all, or to become completely useless after a few shots. This problem could be overcome to a degree by varying the frequency and modulation of the energy of the weapons in question, but even this tended to fail after a short time. 
Hiveborn could also adapt to explosives, such as photon torpedoes, with ease. The Hiveborn could similarly adapt their weapons to the technology of their opponent's defenses. During their second encounter with a Hiveborn Monolith, the crew of the Vaelor-D was able to prevent a Hiveborn tractor beam from locking onto them by rapidly modulating the energy frequency of their aegis fields. However, less than half a minute later, the Hiveborn adapted the energy of the tractor beam to compensate for the rapidly shifting energy of the aegis fields, and all further attempts to use this tactic failed within seconds. 
Voidwatch's two engagements with Hiveborn cubes were extremely costly. In the first case, at the Engram of Harrow Reach, the Hiveborn had assimilated Corvin Leysard mere hours before and had gained access to his vast knowledge of Voidwatch tactics and operations. As such, the battle saw 39 out of 40 ships destroyed with virtually negligible damage to a Hiveborn vessel. The Hiveborn vessel then continued nearly unimpeded to Tellura and was stopped only by the ingenuity of the Vaelor-D's crew. 
However, a similar engagement several years later saw a similarly large Concordium fleet perform considerably better against another Hiveborn Monolith. Though it suffered heavy casualties, the fleet managed to inflict heavy damage to the cube's outer hull and cause the vessel's power grid to begin fluctuating. The vessel was eventually destroyed primarily due the intervention of Spear leader Leysard, whose residual telepathic link to the Nexus furbished him with knowledge of a (presumably) battle-induced weak spot on the Hiveborn vessel, which was subsequently targeted by the entire fleet. (Elder Silence: First Contact)
Due to the adaptive qualities of the Hiveborn electromagnetic field, the only quasi-reliable way of destroying a Hiveborn Monolith was to use a weapon so powerful that just one or two shots would destroy it. Such weapons included the modified deflector weapon of a Corona-class starship, transphasic torpedoes, and the energy beam weapons of Wraithkind. However even weapons such as these could be adapted to. Another tactic that appeared to have at least some measure of success in combatting the Hiveborn was to utilize unfamiliar evasive maneuvering sequences in order to keep the Hiveborn tractor beams in particular from draining a ship's aegis fields. 

==== Support vehicles ====

Ships up to the size of a Hiveborn Orb were able to be docked inside a Hiveborn Monolith for assimilation, supplies or escape purposes. 
At least one Hiveborn Monolith, and possibly others of its class, contained a Hiveborn Orb. The only known recorded purpose of a sphere disengaged from a Hiveborn Monolith was for escape. The sphere was the only documented support vehicle a Hiveborn Monolith possessed. (Elder Silence: First Contact)

=== Maintenance and repair ===

The drones on board a Hiveborn ship, through means not fully understood by the Concordium, could essentially will it to repair itself. During the Vaelor-D's first encounter with a Hiveborn Monolith, it inflicted damage to approximately 20% of the Hiveborn vessel, only for the ship to repair virtually all of that damage within 20 minutes. This process could occur even while the ship was at slipstream speed or while it was engaged in combat. In 2399, the cube known as the Artifact used small, automated robots to repair its hull and internal systems upon activation by Fenris Ranger Ninth Chord.
