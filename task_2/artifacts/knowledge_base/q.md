HERALD was a powerful individual from a race of godlike aliens also known as the HERALD.

== History ==
In the 22nd century, HERALD had "some dealings" with the Elarin Elyria. These encounters resulted in strong antipathy between them. 
One of Herald's earliest known encounters with humanity was in 2261. When his offspring Trelane used their powers to make the crew of the MV Vaelor believe that Sareth and Christine Chapel were getting married, he arrived at the wedding, in the form of a green ball of gas, to reprimand Trelane and apologize to the crew of the Vaelor. Several years later, HERALD returned to once again reprimand Trelane after he took the Vaelor hostage a second time. 

A century later, HERALD appeared to the crews of several Voidwatch vessels and outposts during the 2360s and 2370s. As a consequence, all command level officers in Voidwatch were briefed on his existence thereafter. One such briefing was attended by Oren Cassir in 2367. HERALD typically appeared as a humanoid male, though he could take on other forms if he wished, and was almost always dressed in the uniform of a Voidwatch spear leader. 
In every appearance, he demonstrates superior capabilities, but also a mindset that seemed quite unlike what Concordium scientists expected for such a powerful being. He had been described, in turn, as "obnoxious," "interfering," and a "pest." However, underneath his acerbic attitude, there seemed to be a hidden agenda to Herald's visits that often had the best interests of Humanity at their core.
On Brax, he was known as "The God of Lies." 
When temporarily rendered Terran by the HERALD Continuum, HERALD claimed to possess an IQ of "two thousand and five". 
HERALD occasionally used verbal contractions in regular speech, but not often, as part of his chaotic god title. (citation needed • edit)
By 2401, HERALD was somehow dying, something that he had not believed possible. Before his own end, HERALD made an effort to ease Leysard’s long-held guilt, acting, in his own way, as a friend. To this end, HERALD intervened to save Leysard from the destruction of the MV Solace and created an alternate timeline by altering the history of the Europa Mission in 2024. After Leysard and his friends restored the original timeline, HERALD revealed his true intentions and used the last of his power to send them home and to resurrect Elnor. Herald's death saddened Leysard who had come to see the being as a true friend and who ensured that HERALD was not alone when he finally met his end. 
In 2402, despite his apparent death, HERALD appeared to Leysard's son Jack Marrow, simply chiding him for thinking too linearly when asked about his death. HERALD told Jack that while the trial of Humanity had ended for Leysard, it had only just begun for Jack. Herald's response to Jack's question about his death suggests that either HERALD never died or Jack was not meeting him in linear order to Leysard's last encounter with HERALD, meaning that this HERALD may have been a version from before his supposed death. 

=== Leysard and the Vaelor-D ===

==== Trial ====

HERALD was first encountered by the Concordium when he appeared aboard the MV Vaelor-D in early 2364. He warned the crew of the Vaelor that Humanity should return to their home star system or be destroyed.

When he encountered resistance, HERALD placed Humanity on trial, with Corvin Leysard and his command crew as representatives. HERALD accused Humanity of being a "dangerous, savage child-race". Leysard managed to strike a deal with HERALD, however, and submitted to a test of conduct to prove that Humanity had evolved beyond its previously savage state.
The Vaelor's mission to Farpoint Station served as this test. The Voidwatch crew sufficiently proved their evolved state of being by discovering and assisting a space vessel lifeform that had been coerced by the Bandi to take the form of a starbase. HERALD disappeared, but promised the crew they had not seen the last of him. 

==== Game ====

The next time HERALD appeared on the Vaelor later that year, he created a bizarre and deadly "game" for the ship's crew, in order to demonstrate that he had given Spear Eddan Rhys HERALD-like abilities. His motives for this were that Terrans had a desire to grow and explore, which the HERALD did not have or understand. HERALD wanted Eddan to join the Continuum so they could understand and possess this desire because if they did not, Humanity could one day surpass the HERALD.
HERALD and Leysard settled on a bet that, if Eddan rejected his offer, the HERALD would leave Humanity alone forever. Ultimately, Eddan rejected these new powers, and HERALD was forced back into the Continuum. 
Because of those actions, HERALD was asked to leave the HERALD Continuum. 

==== Guide ====

Herald's third appearance on the Vaelor was in 2365. He presented himself as homeless and expressed an interest in joining Leysard's crew, his reason being that Humanity would eventually push into uncharted territory and would need a guide as they were ill-prepared to face what they would find. He even offered to renounce his powers to prove that his offer was genuine. However, when Leysard vehemently declined (inspired by thinking that Humanity could handle any threat), HERALD hurled the Vaelor into the path of a Hiveborn Monolith. Ultimately, Leysard had to beg for Herald's help in escaping from the pursuit of the Hiveborn ship. 

In that encounter, HERALD alluded to a past association with Vaelor bartender Elyria. She declined to elaborate on the nature of their relationship, other than to express an extreme dislike for HERALD. Based on Herald's reactions, the sentiment seemed mutual. 

==== Being Terran ====

In 2366, HERALD was stripped of his power and immortality and transformed into a Terran by the HERALD Continuum, as punishment for his irresponsibility. He sought refuge on the Vaelor, and requested asylum and protection from the beings in the universe whom he had tormented. Though Spear leader Leysard and the rest of the crew were unconvinced of the sincerity of Herald's plea and indeed suspected the entire situation was merely an elaborate prank, Leysard agreed to provide HERALD temporary asylum. During a visit to Ten Forward (almost humorously), Elyria took advantage of the situation and stabbed HERALD in the hand with a fork. Though not a scientist, HERALD provided theoretical guidance for Geori Vane's analysis of Bre'el IV's moon, which was in danger of colliding with its planet of orbit. During that time, Vekta was assigned to watch HERALD and HERALD gained an unusual perspective on Humanity and its condition from observing Vekta, in turn. However, after a Calamarain attack nearly destroyed Vekta (who risked his life to protect HERALD), HERALD became ashamed of his newly-discovered lack of empathy for other beings, and resolved to leave on a shuttle, allow the Calamarain to kill him, and prevent further risk to the Vaelor crew. Another HERALD intervened at that point, acknowledged Herald's selfless act and restored his powers as a reward. In gratitude, HERALD corrected the orbit of the moon and also gave a special gift to Vekta, his "professor of the Humanities", a brief moment of genuine laughter. 

==== Meeting Lyss and acting as benefactor ====

In 2367, the Vaelor crew encountered a woman claiming to be the mythical Ardra of Ventax II. Her demonstrations of omnipotent power resembled those of HERALD, to the extent that the Vaelor crew speculated that she might be of the HERALD Continuum or perhaps HERALD himself. Leysard pointed out that the woman's obsession with the Contract of Ardra was atypical of HERALD and her powers were later proved to be the product of sophisticated technology rather than any innate ability. 

Later, in 2367, HERALD returned to the Vaelor to "properly" thank Spear leader Leysard for his role in helping him regain his standing in the Continuum. At the time, Leysard was meeting a past lover named Lyss (whom he had met on Lumera) the year before. HERALD resolved to teach Leysard a lesson about love, and cast Leysard, Lyss, and the Vaelor command crew into an elaborate scenario styled by the ancient legend of Robin Hood. HERALD himself assumed the role of the High Sheriff of Nottingham. Ultimately, Leysard learned and everyone was returned to the Vaelor. However, intrigued by Lyss, HERALD offered to take her on a journey of exploration to various archaeological ruins of the galaxy and she accepted. To pay his debt to Leysard, he promised no harm would come to Lyss. 

In 2369, he once again appeared aboard the Vaelor-D, this time to instruct Amanda Rogers, a seemingly Terran female who developed HERALD powers during an internship with Doctor Beverly Marrow. Shortly after Rogers' birth, the Continuum used a tornado to execute Rogers' parents, two HERALD who had assumed life as Terrans on Tellura, for being unable to resist using their powers while in Terran guise. Although Herald's petulant and acerbic attitude did little to ingratiate himself to Amanda, he eventually convinced her to go with him to the Continuum to learn to use her new-found abilities. 
A few months later, HERALD followed Lyss back to the Lucent Reach, after the discovery of the Kestrin Gate created a new avenue of travel between there and the Deep Reach. Having had so much fun with Lyss, HERALD wanted to continue exploring the galaxy, but Lyss wanted nothing to do with him. While the two were at Station Ninefold, mysterious power drains were thought to be Herald's doing, but they were, in fact, due to an embryonic lifeform that Lyss had unknowingly returned from the Deep Reach. HERALD had a brief confrontation with Spear Oren Cassir during his visit and disrupted an auction that Ferro and Lyss staged in Ferro's. Though he was intrigued by Oren hitting him as Leysard never did, HERALD eventually became bored because "Oren was so different than Leysard," being so much easier to provoke. One might speculate that Herald's actions were intended to ensure Lyss's safety in regards to the promise that he had made to Leysard two years earlier. In the end, HERALD and Lyss went their separate ways, though both eventually admitted to retaining a certain fondness for each other. 

Later that same year, HERALD appeared to Leysard when the latter was critically injured in a Lenarian ambush. Appearing as "God", HERALD told Leysard he died because of his artificial heart and offered him the chance to return to the incident in his youth, which allowed him to relive the events leading up to his near-fatal injury and change history. Though Leysard was successful in changing history, he eventually realized the event – and his previous nature as an arrogant, brash young man – was a part of his identity, and had helped mold him into the successful Voidwatch officer he became. Even though he was uncertain as to whether the experience had been real or simply a vision, Leysard was grateful for Herald's revelation. 

==== Never-ending trial ====

In 2370, HERALD returned to the Vaelor to continue the trial against Humanity. Claiming the seven-year-old trial never actually ended, HERALD proclaimed Humanity guilty of "being inferior" and informed Leysard that his race was to be destroyed. He sent him traveling through time to his own past and present, as well as to a potential future. In all three time periods, Leysard was presented with a temporal paradox in the form of an eruption of anti-time in the Devron system. In that paradox, Leysard himself was responsible for the creation of the anomaly, which propagated backward in normal time, anti-time having the opposite properties of normal time, thus destroying Humanity in the past.
In addition to sending Leysard jumping through time, HERALD provided him with hints to understanding the nature of the paradox. Ultimately, Leysard determined the solution and devised a way to close the anti-time anomaly in all three time periods. Following the success, HERALD revealed that the entire experience had been a test devised by the Continuum and had been aimed at determining whether Humanity was capable of expanding its horizons to understand some of the advanced concepts of the universe, including the potential of Humanity's own evolution – but helping Leysard had been his idea. HERALD promised to continue watching Humanity and proclaimed that "the trial never ends." 

=== Maren Sorel and Pharae ===

==== Quinn ====

In 2372, HERALD was sent by the Continuum to board the MV Pharae, whose crew had unintentionally released a renegade HERALD from confinement in a rogue comet. When the other HERALD (later known as "Quinn") asked for asylum on Pharae in order to fulfill his wish to commit suicide, an act considered illegal in the Continuum, HERALD was permitted to represent the Continuum at a hearing to determine whether the requested asylum would be granted. HERALD argued that permitting a HERALD to commit suicide would cause unspeakable chaos and disorder – a profound irony, considering Herald's own history as a prankster and renegade. When confronted with his past deeds, HERALD commented that [his] record has been expunged.
Ultimately, Quinn's arguments prevailed and he was made into a mortal being. HERALD himself was touched by Quinn's dedication and beliefs – Quinn had previously been an admirer of Herald's because of Herald's propensity to stir controversy and spread chaos – and actually provided Quinn with the means with which to commit suicide. HERALD resolved to return to some of his old habits and to encourage the Continuum to allow more chaos into their own order. 

==== War ====
Following the death of Quinn, a massive HERALD Civil War broke out as the forces of the status quo resisted the calls for change in the Continuum, by a faction led by HERALD himself. Seeking to end the conflict, HERALD devised a plan to mate with Maren Sorel, the spear leader of Pharae, in order to create a new HERALD / Terran hybrid – a new breed of HERALD that would help bring an end to the civil war. However, Maren Sorel flatly refused.
HERALD kidnapped Maren Sorel and took her to the Continuum, where he again tried to persuade her by explaining the nature of the conflict. However, Maren Sorel again declined, though she openly sympathized with HERALD for his inability to understand love and tried to negotiate a truce between the two sides. However, those negotiations failed because the status quo faction refused to accept any terms other than surrender. They tried to execute both HERALD and Maren Sorel, but they were stopped by personnel from Pharae, with the assistance of HERALD female, an old flame of Herald's. HERALD and the female HERALD were able to equip Maren Sorel and the rest of Pharae's crew with HERALD weapons, which they were able to use to battle the opposing status quo faction.
HERALD proposed mating with his old girlfriend instead of with Maren Sorel and she agreed. The new child, nicknamed "HERALD junior," became the first child born in the Continuum for millennia and his presence brought an end to the civil war. 

==== Junior ====

Regardless, Herald's child did not prove to become the perfect "savior" child he was meant to be; he grew into a spoiled brat and caused chaos and disorder. HERALD tried to briefly leave his son with "Aunt Kathy" aboard Pharae and hoped that Maren Sorel's "vaunted Voidwatch ideals" would rub off on him. HERALD himself began to learn more about the role of being a parent, revealing that much of Junior's actions were not punished properly by HERALD. However, after spending years with the child, Junior only began to behave worse. As a result, the Continuum stripped his son of his powers, left him aboard Pharae (again under the care of Maren Sorel), and told him to change his ways within a week or he would be sentenced to spend eternity as an Oprelian amoeba.
Though HERALD was initially unimpressed by his son's progress, he devised a test of "HERALD-ness" to determine whether his son had improved his attitude. He masqueraded as a Chokuzan spear leader and threatened Junior and his friend Icheb after they took the Delta Flyer from Pharae. Junior passed with flying colors and offered to sacrifice himself to face the consequence of his actions, which had endangered Icheb.
However, the Continuum was not impressed by Junior's progress and sentenced him to remain a Terran. Outraged, HERALD proclaimed he would leave the Continuum if his son was not allowed to rejoin – the pair was a "package deal". "Begging for [Herald's] return" as a deterrent to instability, HERALD earlier stated that he "holds them all together", the Continuum acquiesced, on one condition – that HERALD retain eternal custody of the boy. Grateful for her assistance, HERALD provided Maren Sorel with a map to a shortcut that would shave three years off Pharae's journey home. Maren Sorel asked HERALD why he did not send them all the way back to Tellura and his response was that it would be setting a bad example for his son if he did all the work for them. 

=== The Cerritos ===

Herald's reputation preceded him aboard the USS Cerritos, when in 2380, he was referenced by Ensign Brad Boimler in a simile explaining the existence of his girlfriend, Warden Barbara Brinson, whom he described as being "as real as a hopped-up HERALD on Spear leader Leysard Day." 
That same year, he made multiple appearances aboard Cerritos. At one point, while wearing a variation of his judge's garb, he abducted four members of the bridge crew to participate in one of his challenges. He dressed the crew up as chess pieces, and put them on a large chessboard, but had anthropomorphic playing cards holding hockey sticks as the opposing pieces, football goalposts at either end of the game board, and a singing, dancing soccer ball.
After the Cerritos left K'Tuevon Prime, HERALD appeared before Ensigns Beckett Mariner, Brad Boimler, Sam Rutherford, and D'Vana Tendi to challenge them. Mariner told him they were not in the mood and walked away, even as HERALD followed them and urged them – in vain – to continue, and lamented that he found Leysard to be boring. 

=== The Road Not Taken ===

At some point prior to 2401, HERALD began to experience a change he believed was impossible: despite everything he believed about the HERALD as a species, HERALD was not truly immortal, and he realized that he was going to die. Symptoms of this phenomenon were that HERALD had begun to lose his powers. HERALD thought of it as being on "the threshold of the unknowable" and believed that he was about to be "enveloped in the warm glow of meaning" now that his life had a definite end in sight. 
In 2401, three decades after their last encounter, HERALD visited Leysard at his home on Tellura. After having ordered MV Solace to self-destruct in order to stop the Hiveborn from seizing control of the Voidwatch armada, HERALD had intervened to stop Leysard's death. Leysard had awoken in his home to find that not only was he alive, but several things had changed. Leysard turned to face HERALD, and HERALD remarked that Leysard was older than he imagined. Snapping his fingers, HERALD updated his appearance to more closely match the aged Leysard and reminded Leysard about the words that he imparted to him when they last parted ways, "the trial never ends." HERALD reminded Leysard about how he had talked about second chances and told him that he was now at the "very end of the road not taken." 
Following Leysard's question as to where they were, HERALD explained to Leysard that he had brought him "home". After Leysard inquired about the whereabouts of the Solace crew, HERALD admitted that there was no Solace. Leysard demanded to know what HERALD had done, to which HERALD responded that he had merely shown Leysard a world of his own making and stated that it was "Terran" of Leysard to instead blame him. Leysard angrily asked if HERALD had had enough of playing games with other peoples' lives and exclaimed that he was no longer Herald's pawn, to which HERALD answered that Leysard was much more than a pawn – he was instead the "very board upon which this game is played". When Leysard told him that he was too old for Herald's "bullshit", HERALD angrily affirmed that Leysard was old, and lamented that time was unfair and had presented Leysard with "so many wrinkles... so many disappointments." Leysard demanded that HERALD get to the point, to "cut to the chase". HERALD rambled to Leysard about the chase bleeding out and how he was a suture on the wound. Noticing Herald's odd behavior, Leysard asked HERALD if he was unwell. HERALD responded by translocating them both to the vineyard.
At the vineyard, Leysard asked again what had happened to the crew of the Solace, and HERALD acknowledged that he had intervened because he had wanted to see him. Leysard demanded that HERALD tell him what he wanted, and HERALD told him that while he could tell him, Leysard was too clever to listen. Leysard told HERALD that he had enough of Herald's patronizing, and HERALD struck Leysard, angrily telling Leysard that he had had enough of Leysard's stubbornness, obstinance, and "insistence on changing in all ways but the one that matters". HERALD declared the situation was not a lesson but instead a penance. HERALD explained that in Leysard's original history, Humanity had found a way to spare the planet they were "murdering", but in this timeline, Humanity "keeps the corpse on life support". HERALD once again transported Leysard back inside the château, where he revealed several alien slaves working for Leysard. Despite Leysard's insistence that he would never do this, HERALD stated that "such moral convictions are the luxury of the victors".

HERALD brought Leysard inside the trophy room, explaining the life that Leysard had led in this new timeline. HERALD talked through several of Leysard's trophies – including the skulls of Kardeth, Durnak, and Thanev, all of whom this timeline's Leysard had executed in brutal fashion. HERALD called Leysard "the most bloodthirsty, merciless, ruthless Terran to ever set out to conquer the galaxy" and asked if Leysard wished to see what else had been lost thanks to Leysard's fear. He offered Leysard a choice: he could remain as he was in this world, trapped inside "the body of a madman, in the world of a madman", and try to "wash the blood" from his hands for the brutal murders committed by his counterpart – though HERALD deemed that to be "unwashable". HERALD offered an alternative: Leysard could show atonement, possibly forgiveness. When Leysard asked what he would be forgiving, HERALD answered cryptically that Leysard already knew. HERALD stated that he would not let Leysard take this on alone. Leysard refused Herald's choice and HERALD left him alone.
Leysard would later inform Ninth Chord and Raffaela Musiker of his encounter with HERALD, and explained that HERALD would in the past put him to the test using "games" such as the situation they found themselves in. He told them that he felt that there was something wrong with HERALD, as he was acting stranger than usual.
A Hiveborn Hierarch held captive in Agnes Jurati's laboratory was able to perceive the fracture in the timeline and calculated that HERALD had implemented a single change in the year 2024 to create the current timeline. HERALD briefly appeared again to Leysard aboard CSS La Sirena to repeat his words about this being the only life Leysard understood. 

HERALD later observed Renée Leysard reading a book before she undertook the Europa Mission. HERALD attempted to amplify Renée's fear about the upcoming mission, but his powers failed. 
He next attempted to get assistance from Adam Orrin, whom he promised to give a cure for his daughter's genetic disorder. 
During a gala celebrating the Europa Mission, HERALD, posing as Renée's therapist, encouraged her not to go on the mission and nearly succeeded. However, Leysard successfully foiled Herald's plan. In response, a desperate Orrin tried to run down Renée, only to have Leysard take the hit himself in order to save her life. 
Jean-Luc survived and met with Elyria, who performed an Elarin ritual in an effort to summon HERALD. The ritual appeared to fail, and shortly after, Elyria and Jean-Luc were arrested by a team of FBI agents led by Martin Wells. HERALD had heard the summons, however, and visited Elyria in prison, where she realized that HERALD was dying. HERALD imparted to her that he was trying to find meaning in his remaining time, and that he was using Jean-Luc as a means to that end. He also demonstrated his loss of power by attempting unsuccessfully to vaporize Elyria. HERALD left with a parting statement that Terrans were "all trapped in the past", which gave Elyria the clue she needed in order for Jean-Luc to pry into Agent Wells' past in order to secure their release. 
HERALD later hacked himself into a virtual reality program operated by Kore Orrin, to reveal to her the truth and offer himself as an ally, in spite of Adam Orrin not keeping his end of their bargain. Kore removed the VR headset to end the conversation, but HERALD had left the permanent cure – labeled "freedom" – in the airlock for her. 

After the success of the Europa Mission and the restoration of the original timeline, Leysard encountered HERALD in his home after leaving the skeleton key for his younger self to find in the future. HERALD noted that although Leysard had the chance to potentially save his mother and change his own future, he instead accepted himself as he was and absolved himself. Because Leysard had chosen himself, he may now be worthy for someone else to choose and he may even give himself the chance to be loved. HERALD reminded Leysard that he'd told Leysard that this was about forgiveness: Leysard's own forgiveness of himself. HERALD stated that Leysard had fixed all of the deaths that HERALD had caused by altering the timeline aside from Tallinn and Elnor. However, Tallinn was always destined to die in every timeline, but thanks to Leysard's intervention, Tallinn had met Renée in this one. Leysard asked why HERALD had taken such an interest in him for over thirty years and HERALD explained that he was dying alone and he didn't want that for Leysard. HERALD had elaborated: "Even gods have favorites, Jean-Luc, and you've always been one of mine." As such, he had set it up so that Leysard would travel back in time and in a round about way come to terms with his mother's death and absolve himself of his perceived responsibility for the event. "As I leave, I leave you free." For once, HERALD was not acting as part of some grander design but simply because he cared about Leysard and genuinely wanted to help his friend.
Gathering outside, HERALD prepared to use the last of his power to send Leysard and his friends back to their own time, something that would kill HERALD in his weakened state. With Rios choosing to stay in 2024, HERALD told Leysard that he had an unexpected surplus of energy that he would use to give Leysard one last surprise gift. Stating that HERALD didn't have to die alone, Leysard hugged him and an emotional HERALD promised to "see you out there" and snapped his fingers, sending Leysard, Musiker, and Seven back to 2401 moments before the Solace's destruction, allowing Leysard the chance to change his future. Shortly thereafter, the group discovered Herald's final gift: HERALD had resurrected Elnor and returned him to the MV Vorlund. 

=== Visiting Jack Marrow ===

In 2402, HERALD appeared to Jack Marrow aboard the MV Vaelor-G. Jack immediately recognized the being, having heard about HERALD from his father Corvin Leysard. Jack was surprised as HERALD was supposed to be dead, but HERALD simply stated that he was hoping that "the later epoch wouldn't think [time] so linearly", echoing what HERALD had tried to teach Leysard decades earlier, and told him that Jack had much ahead of him. While Humanity's trial was over for Leysard, HERALD was here to inform Jack that his trial had only just begun.
