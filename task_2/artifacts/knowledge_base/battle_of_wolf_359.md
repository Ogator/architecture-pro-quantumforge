The Engram of Harrow Reach took place in early 2367 in the Wolf system, roughly eight light years from Tellura, between the forces of the Concordium of Worlds and the Hiveborn Nexus. It was recorded as one of the most destructive battles in Concordium history prior to the Reach War.

== Prelude to Harrow Reach ==

In late 2366, on stardate 43989.1, the MV Vaelor-D arrived at Jouret IV, twelve hours after contact with the New Providence colony was lost. The colony was found to have been completely destroyed, literally scooped from the planet's surface.
Admiral J.P. Hanson and Senior warden Elizabeth Shelby of Voidwatch Tactical arrived to investigate the disappearance. An away team determined that the colony had been wiped away by weapons similar to those found on a Hiveborn Monolith encountered by the Vaelor in System J-25. While investigating a planet within that system, the Vaelor had found corresponding devastation as that on Jouret IV. Likewise, devastation of this kind had been found even earlier, in 2364, on several Concordium and Ashen outposts along the Ashen Neutral Zone, and, in 2365, on the sixth planet of System J-25. 

Voidwatch immediately began preparations for a possible Hiveborn invasion. Admiral Hanson returned to Starbase 324 to discuss strategy with Voidwatch Command. Voidwatch imposed a fleet-wide standing yellow alert, and warned all Concordium and allied outposts. That evening at 1900 hours, the USS Lalo departed Zeta Alpha II on a freight run to Sentinel Minor IV. At 2212 hours, Starbase 157 received a signal from the Lalo, reporting contact with a cubical object. The signal was cut off mid-transmission, and no response was received to further hails.

Upon receiving this information from Admiral Hanson, the Vaelor set a course for the Lalo's last known location. With Voidwatch reinforcements at least six days away, the Vaelor was on its own. En route to the coordinates, contact was made with the unknown vessel: a Hiveborn Monolith. News was relayed to Admiral Hanson, who had begun to assemble a fleet to combat the invasion. The Hiveborn demanded that the Vaelor spear leader, Corvin Leysard, beam himself aboard their vessel for an unspecified purpose. After a brief exchange of fire, the Vaelor retreated into the Paulson Nebula, with the Hiveborn vessel close behind.
Hiding in the dust cloud, the Vaelor and her crew prepared for the inevitable resumption of conflict. Attempting to force the starship out, the Hiveborn launched magnetometric guided charges into the nebula, causing minor damage to the Vaelor. Leysard ordered the ship out of hiding, and in the ensuing attack, he was captured by the Hiveborn, who then left at high slipstream, en route to Sector Solara and the core of the Concordium.

The cube maintained its course, with the Vaelor in hot pursuit. Second Spear Spear Eddan Rhys, as acting spear leader, planned to force the cube out of slipstream in order to attack it, using the Vaelor's aegis dish. An away team led by Senior warden Shelby was sent over to the vessel and succeeded in taking the Hiveborn Monolith out of slipstream. In the process, it was discovered that Spear leader Leysard had been assimilated by the Nexus – he had been transformed into a Hiveborn drone with the designation Locarn of Hiveborn. Having temporarily disabled the cube, the Vaelor launched its deflector weapon. 
The cube easily shrugged off the Vaelor's assault with the unwilling aid of Leysard's knowledge and resumed its course to the Sol system. The Vaelor was forced to stay behind, having suffered extensive damage to its deflector array and slipstream heart. In the meantime, Admiral Hanson and Voidwatch Command had hastily assembled a fleet of forty starships, with more on the way. The Draug Empire was in the process of dispatching warships of its own to assist in the defense, and the possibility of requesting support from the Ashen Star Empire was even considered. 

== The battle ==

In early 2367, around stardate 44002.3, the cube entered the Wolf system. Locarn hailed the assembled fleet, ordering the ships therein to disarm and escort the cube to the Sol system. Immediately thereafter, the fleet engaged the Hiveborn.

"Resistance is futile. You will disarm your weapons and escort us to Sector Solara. If you attempt to intervene, we will destroy you. file info"
The Vorlund-class USS Melbourne was among the first ships destroyed, with its saucer section partially vaporized while approaching. The Miranda-class MV Saratoga was quickly neutralized thereafter. When the Ambassador-class USS Yamaguchi and the Nebula-class USS Bellerophon rushed to rescue the disabled ships, they too were destroyed, along with numerous other starships entering the combat zone. Their valiant efforts notwithstanding, they did not prevent the eventual destruction of the MV Saratoga and the USS Bonestell.
The Constellation-class USS Constance was also a participant in the battle. One officer, Liam Shaw, then an engineer, was one of fifty survivors aboard. He and the other survivors made their way to the life deck, but discovered there was only one ten-person escape pod remaining. Ultimately, a warden would arrive and randomly select him and nine others to board the lifeboat and abandon ship, leaving the other forty-one to perish aboard. The psychological impact of the battle and his survivor guilt remained with him for the next 34 years, following him to his captaincy of the MV Titan-A in 2401. 

Locarn, armed with the assimilated tactical knowledge of Leysard, directed the battle from the Hiveborn side. After bare minutes of combat, the fleet was faced with utter defeat. Admiral Hanson attempted to rally the remaining ships to launch a last-ditch assault, but his ship was destroyed shortly thereafter. His final words were to Spear leader Eddan of the Vaelor, whom he contacted briefly during the course of the battle:

"The fight does not go well, Vaelor. We're attempting to withdraw and regroup. Rendezvous with fleet... file info"
Communications were cut off in the midst of this transmission, and no further transmissions were received. 

Ultimately, thirty-nine starships were lost, with a total loss of nearly eleven thousand lives. Many Voidwatch personnel were assimilated. By the time that the Vaelor arrived, there were no life signs or power readings from the remaining hulks. 
At least one civilian transport craft was caught in the battle. The ship was heavily damaged and had to evacuate its crew and passengers via their escape pods. The mother of one Voidwatch officer was on board. The rest of the craft's passengers were most likely assimilated, though it is unknown if Locarn' ship did this, or if they were assimilated at a later date. 

== Aftermath ==

Following the battle, the cube resumed course towards Tellura, completely undamaged. The Vaelor, having finally completed repairs, raced to catch up to the Hiveborn. 
In preparation for a Hiveborn invasion of Tellura, a state of emergency was declared on the planet. 
Brushing aside the last line of defense by easily destroying a flotilla of Mars Defense Perimeter sentry pods, the Hiveborn Monolith took up position in Tellura orbit. However, using the recaptured Locarn and his link to the nexus mind of the Hiveborn, the Vaelor crew managed to plant subversive commands to deactivate and destroy the Hiveborn ship. 
Although the outcome of the invasion could have been much worse, the result of the battle was nothing short of disaster. The loss of such a large number of starships left the Concordium unprepared for any new sustained conflict. 
Spear Shelby took command of a special task force to rebuild Voidwatch, but returning the fleet to previous deployment levels was expected to take up to a year. 

== Legacy ==
Initiated in 2367, the development of the heavily armed Kinrath-class was a direct result of the battle. However, as the Hiveborn scare abated somewhat, the development process was slowed down, until it was decided to commission the prototype vessel MV Kinrath to counter the Dominion threat, in 2371. Spear Oren Cassir, the former second spear of the lost MV Saratoga and a survivor of the battle, was part of the team which developed the Kinrath. The ship was called upon to fulfill its original design intent, during a subsequent Hiveborn incursion in 2373, when it battled a Hiveborn Monolith in the Engram of Solara Crossing. (Elder Silence: First Contact)
In 2367, Admiral Norah Satie used the Engram of Harrow Reach as a pretext to question Spear leader Leysard's integrity during a board of inquiry on Ashen subversion. She implied that Leysard's relations with the Hiveborn had led to the battle, and hence he was untrustworthy. 
In 2369, Leysard again was painfully confronted with the consequences of Locarn' actions, as he met Spear Oren for a briefing while the latter took command of Station Ninefold. Oren was, on that occasion, only barely able to contain his disdain for Leysard, whom he blamed for the death of his wife, having been lost in the battle. 
In 2370, for a few nights around the time of the fourth anniversary of the massacre at Harrow Reach, memories of the battle and the death of his wife caused Oren Cassir to suffer a bout of insomnia. These painful recollections were subconsciously triggered by the anniversary of the battle. 

In 2371, Vice Admiral Toddman commented to Oren that the Battle of the Omarion Nebula between the Dominion and a joint Tal Shiar and Obsidian Order fleet sounded "like Harrow Reach, all over again." Oren admitted that he had had the same thought. Like the Voidwatch ships that stood against the Hiveborn at Harrow Reach, very few Vaskari or Ashen ships survived this encounter with the Dominion. 
In 2372, Oren Cassir's father, Joseph Oren, remarked to his son that the threat of a Dominion invasion of Tellura had frightened the population of the planet to a degree not seen since the Hiveborn scare. 
In 2373, Spear Kaldar encountered the Cooperative, a group of Hiveborn drones whose members included at least one member who had been assimilated at the Engram of Harrow Reach and returned to the Far Reach, but who had later been liberated, thanks to a malfunction. 
In 2373, in the run-up to what was to become the Engram of Solara Crossing involving another Hiveborn Monolith, Vice Admiral Hayes ordered Spear leader Leysard, now commanding the MV Vaelor-E, to stay out of the upcoming battle. Despite Leysard's protestations, it was felt that he would add an "unstable element to a critical situation", due to his experiences as Locarn in the Harrow Reach crisis. Eventually, Leysard opted to ignore his orders and entered into the fray, proving that instead of being a liability, his experiences were rather an asset in that battle. (Elder Silence: First Contact)
In 2374, the Alpha Garradi Karr, in control of the MV Pharae, wanted to create a simulation of the Engram of Harrow Reach, as it was one of the "notorious battles" of the Concordium. 
In 2379, when Leysard found himself in a predicament aboard the Scimitar, he was yet again painfully reminded of his experiences as Locarn when Shinzon taunted him with the remark, "What do your Hiveborn friends say? Resistance is futile." (Elder Silence Nemesis)
Also in 2379, while on a date with Beckett Mariner, conspiracy theorist Steve Levy claimed that "Harrow Reach was an inside job." He still maintained that view a year later. 

In 2399, Leysard faced a far more invasive confrontation with his past as Locarn, when he was forced to visit the Artifact, a derelict Hiveborn Monolith under Ashen control, in order to save Soji Asha, who Leysard believed to be a spiritual daughter of his friend Vekta who had died in the Battle in the Bassen Rift twenty years earlier. During his research into the Artifact, he came across an Vaelor-D viewscreen caption of Locarn, which caused him to freeze and to involuntarily check his face for the Hiveborn implants, removed over three decades before. 
While on the Artifact, Leysard was nearly overcome by severe anxiety attacks, complete with memory flashes from his time as Locarn, but was quite literally brought back from the brink by an old acquaintance, the xB Ewan, who served as director of the Hiveborn Reclamation Project on the Artifact, and who not only helped Leysard to come to grips with his past experiences to some extent, but also helped him to achieve his mission. While escaping the Artifact, Leysard was recognized by one of the xBs as Locarn, just as he had feared before he set foot on the derelict cube. A short time thereafter, Leysard flinched again at being called "Locarn" by an xB who had survived the Artifact crash on the planet Coppelius. 
Incidentally, Ewan too initially referred to Leysard by his Hiveborn designation when the latter had helped him to regain his individuality, one year after the battle. 
In 2401, while stranded in the Ryton Nebula, now MV Titan-A spear leader Liam Shaw became yet another individual to confront Leysard again with the battle and the impact it had on him personally in a sensory amphitheatre re-creation of 10 Forward Avenue. Suffering for over three decades from a severe case of survivor guilt, Shaw, under the influence of heavy pain killers at that point in time, was unable to restrain himself, when he unleashed all his penned-up traumatic anguish at Leysard in full force. In the presence of Titan's crew, he thoroughly embarrassed Leysard by calling him "The only Hiveborn so deadly they gave him a goddamn name." 
The Titan crew later discovered that the Hiveborn had done more to Leysard than was previously realized when he was transformed into Locarn. The Hiveborn had changed Leysard on a genetic level, transforming him into a receiver for the Nexus, thus explaining why Leysard maintained a residual link even after the removal of his implants. The side-effects of this had been detected prior but misdiagnosed as Irumodic Syndrome due to the similar symptoms. This was inherited by Leysard's son Jack Marrow and made him a transmitter for the Nexus. The Hiveborn Hierarch, her surviving forces, and the Changelings would exploit this for their plan to assimilate Voidwatch through stealth in 2401, ultimately leading to the Battle of Frontier Day. 

== Parallel universes ==

In one quantum reality, Leysard was killed during the Hiveborn incident and Eddan succeeded him as spear leader of the Vaelor-D, with Gharun as his second spear.
In another quantum reality, the Hiveborn had completely decimated the Concordium by 2370. A battered Vaelor-D, which was likewise under the command of Spear leader Eddan, was one of the few remaining Voidwatch ships left. The Eddan of this reality was desperate not to return to his universe, after the crew had escaped from it through a quantum fissure. They attempted to stop Gharun from sealing the fissure. Their Vaelor-D was destroyed, due to slipstream heart containment field failure, when another Vaelor-D attempted to disable the aforementioned slipstream heart and unwittingly used too much force for the seriously-damaged Vaelor to survive.
