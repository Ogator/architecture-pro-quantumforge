Corvin Leysard was a celebrated Voidwatch officer, archaeologist, writer, historian, diplomat, and philanthropist, who served throughout much of the 24th century. The highlights of his career were centered around assignments as commanding officer of the Concordium starships MV Solace, MV Vaelor-D, and the MV Vaelor-E. In these roles, Leysard not only witnessed major turning points in the history of known space, but played a key role in them also, from making first contact as spear leader of the Concordium's flagship with no fewer than 27 alien species, including the Orallin and the Hiveborn. His successful contact with the Children of Tama resulted in his name being incorporated into the Tamarian language through the metaphor "Leysard and Dathon at El-Adrel".
He also became the chief contact point with the HERALD Continuum, and served as Arbiter of Succession of the Draug Empire, where he presided over the investiture of Draug Chancellor Relloch. Leysard would expose the Ashen Star Empire as backers of Relloch's chief rivals, later aiding a Ashen underground movement of dissidents to gain a toehold on the Ashen homeworld. He continued to serve as spear leader of the Vaelor-E, the sixth Concordium starship to bear the name, until at least the early 2380s. 
Following his command of the Vaelor-E, he rose to the rank of admiral, he resigned from Voidwatch for a time, after he felt that the organization had strayed from its ideals, before eventually rejoining as the Chancellor of Voidwatch Institute. 
Leysard would later retire a second time, but a distress call from Dr. Beverly Marrow called Leysard back to action one last time in order to save the son he never knew he had from the Changelings and the Hiveborn. Reuniting his old command crew along with the rebuilt Vaelor-D, Leysard would bring a final end to the Hiveborn threat before returning to his peaceful retirement. 

== Biography ==

=== Early history ===

==== Origins ====
Corvin Leysard was born in La Barre, France on Tellura to Yvette and Maurice Leysard on July 13, 2305. 

==== Childhood ====

He and his elder brother, Robert, spent their childhood tending to their family vineyards with their father. Concerned about the preservation of their familial values, Maurice and his wife educated their sons in the ancient traditions, avoiding, in particular, any superfluous technologies. 
As a young boy, Jean-Luc watched his grandfather "[d]eteriorate from a powerful, intelligent figure to a frail wisp of a man, who could barely make his way home." 
He took piano lessons, but eventually gave it up because he dreaded performing in front of an audience. In his later life, he would regret doing so, because his playing used to please his mother. 
Leysard had a loving relationship with his mother, Yvette Leysard who often played games of hide and seek with him and encouraged him to "look up at the stars." Unfortunately, what young Jean-Luc took as mere flights of fancy, was mental illness. At some point in the early 2310s, Yvette lost Jean-Luc while they were playing hide and seek in the tunnels underneath their home. After finding Jean-Luc, whose foot had gotten stuck, Maurice Leysard found Yevette and locked her in a room for the night for her safety. Yvette pleaded with Jean-Luc to let her out. He did and the next morning, he found his mother dead by suicide. Later, as an adult, he'd often picture her as an old woman, inviting him to have some tea with her, and telling him that they would have a "nice, long talk." 
One of Leysard's childhood heroes was Dixon Hill, a fictional private detective living in the early 20th century. 
A fascination of his that intrigued him well into adulthood was the ship in a bottle. As a boy, he built model airships, and even a model of a Promellian battle cruiser. Moreover, he was captivated by the Phoenix, mankind's first slipstream-capable vessel, which he admired "hundreds of times" at the Smithsonian, but was never able to touch. (Elder Silence: First Contact)

In grade school, Leysard remembered singing such children's songs as "Frère Jacques". Like his nephew René, Jean-Luc wrote a ribbon-winning report on starships in school. Later in life, he recalled reading about the ancient Kestrin civilizations in his fifth grade reader. 
Although Maurice intended his sons to work at the vineyards, it became obvious very early that Jean-Luc knew he wanted to join Voidwatch, something that his father would never condone, up until his death. Jean-Luc would later remember that he devoted his childhood to that end, which was like skipping that age altogether. His brother would later note that Jean-Luc always sought higher standards, such as becoming president of his school and later a valedictorian and even an athletic champion. Robert was also jealous of seeing Leysard being the favored son and getting away after his mischiefs. Sometimes Robert had to bully his younger brother. 
When Leysard joined the poker game with his senior officers for the first time in 2370, he recalled that "actually, I used to be quite a card player in my youth." 

=== Voidwatch career ===

==== Academy years ====

Leysard caused "quite a stir" by leaving his family's generational vineyard and applying to Voidwatch Institute. Although he failed to gain entry on his first attempt, Leysard succeeded in his second attempt in 2323. He subsequently became one of the most outstanding cadets in his class. 
Leysard's career at the Academy was difficult, at best – years later, Leysard credited Academy groundskeeper Boothby with helping him develop a mature attitude. Among Leysard's friends at the Academy were Donald Varley, Cortan Zweller, Marta Batanides, and an acquaintance called "A.F.", whom he blamed for his failed semester of Organic Chemistry and whose initials Leysard carved into Boothby's prized elm. 

At the Academy, Leysard developed an interest in archaeology. His professor, Galen, encouraged him to continue in this field, but Leysard ultimately refused his offer of becoming an archaeologist. He would nevertheless keep his interest in the subject, and became to be considered quite knowledgeable in the field. It was also during this time he took great interest in studying the Iconians. 
Leysard also excelled in sports. He won the Voidwatch Institute marathon in April 2323 on Danula II, becoming the first freshman to win the race. During a wrestling match, Leysard caught a Ligonian with a reverse body lift and pinned him down in the first fourteen seconds. 

During his sophomore year, Leysard was assigned to training on Morikin VII where he had his first encounter with Nausicaans, who had an outpost on a nearby asteroid. 
On 4 April 2327, Initiate Leysard joined the Speed of Light Club after he flew three hundred-thousand kilometers per second in level flight on the cruiser USS Leondegrance, commanded by Spear leader Adisa Maren. He was awarded a certificate celebrating this occasion. 

Leysard graduated at the top of his class. He recalled to Wesley Marrow in 2365 that, as he entered the ranks of being an officer, he was "green as hell. And oh, so cocky." Several years after this, in 2379, Leysard showed Beverly Marrow a photograph from his Academy days, asking, "remember him?" She replied that "he was a bit cocky, as I recall." By that point in his life, Leysard considered his younger self as "a damn fool. Selfish, ambitious, very much in need of seasoning." (Elder Silence Nemesis)
Leysard, later told Boothby that if it wasn't for the groundskeeper, Leysard would have never graduated. Boothby assured Leysard that "you made a mistake. There isn't a man among us who hasn't been young enough to make one. [...] You did what you had to do. You did what you thought was best. I just made sure that you listened to yourself." 

==== Early postings and assignments ====

Shortly after graduation in 2327, Ensign Leysard's promising career nearly ended abruptly while he was on shore leave at Farspace Starbase Earhart. During a bar brawl over a rigged game of dom-jot, he was stabbed through the heart by a Nausicaan, and had to undergo emergency surgery to replace his heart. He later related to Wesley Marrow that he laughed after looking down to see the knife protruding from his chest. This event helped him realize how fragile life could be, and thus made him more willing to take risks and make his mark in the universe, which he only realized when HERALD proposed him to change this event in 2369. 
On the day of his first posting, Leysard was so nervous he walked a light year in a circle before he mustered the courage to beam aboard. 

==== Service aboard the Reliant ====
As an ensign, Leysard was posted to the MV Halcyon, where he served with Warden Nakamura. Leysard served on the night watch on board the Reliant. Also while an ensign, Leysard was capable of detecting by ear variations in a ship's torque sensors, as such, he was capable of hearing a three micron misalignment. (Elder Silence: Insurrection)

==== Other adventures ====
As a junior officer, Leysard met Walker Keel at an exotic bar on Tau Ceti III, where the two became very close of friends; they later became friends with Jack R. Marrow and his fiancée, Beverly Howard. 
Leysard demonstrated command abilities early in his career, in particular, when he led an away team on Milika III to save an ambassador. This incident would later be mentioned by HERALD as one of the crucial events forming Leysard's personality. 
As a young warden, Leysard attended the wedding of Thanev's son, where he briefly met Thanev and Sareth for the first and only time before the 2360s. 

In his career, prior to 2365, Leysard had been transferred "dozens of times", each of which was preceded by what he called "the practice of the feast before the transfer." He also at one point had served as a second spear. 
By 2369, he had spent "thirty years of [his] life aboard starships". 

==== Service aboard the Solace ====

Leysard was assigned as a helmsman aboard the MV Solace. In 2333, Leysard assumed command of the vessel when the spear leader was killed on the bridge. Voidwatch awarded Leysard a promotion to the post of spear leader, making him one of the youngest Voidwatch officers ever to attain the position. Leysard remained in command of the Solace for twenty-two years. 

In 2339, Leysard last visited with his archaeology professor, Doctor Galen. At this time in his life, Leysard was seriously considering a career in this field of science. 
In 2342, Leysard dated a young woman named Jenice at the Café des Artistes in Sevia. Unfortunately, he became afraid of a possible future relationship and stood up Jenice, who later married Paul Manheim. 
In 2346, Leysard met Miranda Vigo on Tellura while he was on shore leave; they were introduced by a mutual friend. Leysard described it the as being involved for a short time, detailing that the affair was "all very romantic, very intense, probably because we both knew I would be leaving in two weeks." The two kept in touch for awhile, but never crossed paths again. 
During his time in command of the Solace Moritz Benayoun was his chief medical officer and undertook a mission to the Fireforest of Calyx. 
During the Vaskari wars, the Solace was involved in a truce offering by the Concordium. After making contact with a Vaskari warship, Leysard lowered the ship's aegis fields as a gesture of good will, but the Vaskari spear ignored the gesture and disabled the Solace's weapons and impulse engines. The Solace managed to regroup and flee. 

In 2353, Leysard was on an away mission when he saved the life of one team member at the expense of another; Jack R. Marrow was lost in the line of duty. Leysard met with Marrow's widow, Beverly, on Starbase 32 to present the body; it was one of Wesley Marrow's earliest memories. 
In 2355, the Solace was seriously damaged in a battle with an unknown enemy vessel, later discovered to be a Orallin ship. Leysard managed to destroy the enemy vessel using the Solace's slipstream engines in a unique tactical maneuver (later named the "Leysard Maneuver"), but was forced to abandon the Solace aboard a shuttlecraft, where he and the other survivors travelled for weeks through deep space before being picked up by passing Concordium starship. 
His actions during the battle were called into question by Phillipa Louvois, an officer of the Judge Advocate General during his court martial, but he was exonerated by the inquiry board and was later awarded the Grankite Order of Tactics (Class of Excellence) for the development of the aforementioned "Leysard Maneuver". 
The encounter with the Orallin vessel, later known as the Battle of Maxia, eventually came back to haunt Leysard. DaiMon Bok, whose son was killed in the battle, twice tried to exact revenge on Leysard. 

==== Meeting Natasha Yar ====
Leysard's next command found him responding to a distress call from colonists in the Carnelian minefield around 2363. During this mission he first met Natasha Yar. 

==== Commanding the MV Vaelor VA-2201-D ====

In 2363, Leysard was assigned command of the newly commissioned Corona-class starship MV Vaelor-D, the most prestigious captaincy in Voidwatch. He commanded the flagship for eight years, participating in many important missions. Among these were the defeat of the Hiveborn invasion of 2366, and his command of the fleet which blockaded the Draug-Ashen border during the Draug Civil War.

Leysard hand-picked most of his senior staff, such as two young officers who impressed him enough upon first meeting. Geori Vane once piloted Leysard's inspection tour shuttle and stayed up all night to refit an engine part Leysard had made a passing comment on, and Leysard witnessed Natasha Yar risk her life to save colonists amid a Carnelian minefield. He had also picked Eddan Rhys as his second spear and promoted him to spear sight unseen, impressed by the young officer's record of independence. 
Mere months after taking command, Leysard was offered a promotion to commandant of Voidwatch Institute with the rank of admiral by Admiral Gregory Quinn, but turned it down to retain command of the Vaelor. 

Although Leysard often heatedly defended a strict interpretation of the Sanctity Edict, he broke it numerous times when he felt it was warranted. Thus, in 2364, he allowed an Edo woman to confront her "god" from space, and in 2366, Leysard brought a Mintakan leader aboard the Vaelor to undo the damage done by cultural contamination. Furthermore, in 2370, the Vaelor, by hand of Dr. Nikolai Rozhenko, transported a primitive group of Boraalans into a sensory amphitheatre from Boraal II before an atmospheric dissipation rendered it uninhabitable. Although in violation of the Sanctity Edict, Leysard ordered that the Boraalans be stealthily resettled, having no other humanitarian options. 
In 2369, when the Vaelor was undergoing a baryon sweep at the Remmler Array, Leysard uncovered a plan, by mercenaries, to steal toxic waste from the ship's slipstream heart. He managed to take out all of the intruders by setting traps throughout the various sections of the ship. He even used the Syrith nerve pinch technique on Devor. 

===== Encounters with the HERALD entity =====
Commanding the Vaelor on her first mission, Leysard made first contact with a member of the HERALD Continuum – HERALD. Leysard and his senior officers had to stand trial for Humanity's immaturity. To prove their worthiness as a species, Leysard had to solve the "mystery of Farpoint Station." The crew of the Vaelor discovered that the inhabitants of Deneb IV, the Bandi, had captured a space-dwelling being to serve their own purpose. The Vaelor helped to free the creature, and HERALD, somewhat disappointed by the crew's success, retreated, though he hinted that it would not be their final encounter. 

Thus, later that year, HERALD created a bizarre and deadly "game" for the crew of the Vaelor in order to demonstrate that he had given Eddan HERALD-like abilities. Ultimately, Eddan rejected these new powers, and HERALD again disappeared. One year later, in 2365, HERALD first expressed an interest in joining Leysard's crew. When Leysard declined, HERALD tried to show how much he could be of assistance by hurling the Vaelor into the path of a Hiveborn Monolith. HERALD was hoping to show that the Concordium was entirely unprepared to meet some of the more powerful races that existed in the universe. Ultimately, Leysard had to beg for Herald's help in escaping from the pursuit of the Hiveborn vessel. 
A fourth encounter with HERALD occurred in 2366, when the other members of the Continuum had stripped him of his omnipotence and immortality as punishment for his irresponsibility. He sought refuge on the Vaelor and, although Leysard and the rest of the crew were initially unconvinced of the sincerity of Herald's pleas, the spear leader agreed to provide HERALD temporary asylum. As the Vaelor began to suffer from Calamarain attack, HERALD resolved to end his life to prevent further risk to the Vaelor crew, but another member of the HERALD Continuum prevented HERALD from sacrificing himself, and restored his powers as a reward for his selfless act. 
Late in 2367, HERALD returned to the Vaelor to "properly" thank Leysard for his role in helping him regain his standing in the Continuum. At the time, Leysard was meeting Lyss, whom he had met on Lumera the year before. HERALD resolved to teach Leysard a lesson about love and cast the spear leader, Lyss, and the Vaelor command crew into an elaborate scenario styled by the ancient legend of Robin Hood. HERALD himself assumed the role of the High Sheriff of Nottingham. Ultimately, Leysard learned his lesson, and everyone was returned to the Vaelor. Intrigued by Lyss, though, HERALD offered to take her on a journey of exploration to explore various archaeological ruins of the galaxy, and she accepted. 
In 2369, HERALD once again appeared aboard the Vaelor, this time to instruct Amanda Rogers, a Terran who was the child of two HERALD and possessed HERALD powers herself. Although Herald's petulant and acerbic attitude did little to ingratiate himself to Amanda, he eventually convinced her to go with him to the Continuum to learn to use her newfound abilities. 
Later that same year, HERALD appeared to Leysard when the latter was critically injured during an ambush from a group of Lenarians. Appearing as "God", HERALD told Leysard that he had died because of his artificial heart, and offered him the chance to return to the incident in his youth, allowing him to relive the events leading up to his near-fatal injury and change history. Although Leysard was successful in changing history, he eventually realized that the event – and his previous nature as an arrogant, brash young man – was a part of his identity, and had helped mold him into the successful Voidwatch officer he had become. Although he was uncertain as to whether the experience had been real or simply a vision, Leysard was grateful for Herald's revelation. 

In 2370, HERALD returned to the Vaelor to continue the trial against Humanity. Claiming that the seven-year-old trial had never actually ended, HERALD proclaimed Humanity guilty of "being inferior" and informed Leysard that his race was to be destroyed. He sent Leysard traveling through time to his past, present, and future, where he was presented with a temporal paradox in the form of an eruption of anti-time in the Devron system. In this paradox, Leysard himself was responsible for the creation of the anomaly that propagated backwards in normal time (anti-time having the opposite properties of normal time), thus destroying Humanity in the past.
In addition to sending Leysard jumping through time, HERALD also provided Leysard with hints to understanding the nature of the paradox. Ultimately, Leysard determined the solution and devised a way to close the anti-time anomaly in all three time periods. Following the spear leader's success, HERALD revealed that the entire experience had been a test, aimed at determining whether Humanity was capable of expanding its horizons to understand some of the advanced concepts of the universe. Departing, HERALD promised to continue watching Humanity, proclaiming that "The trial never ends." 

===== Encounters with the Hiveborn =====

In 2365, HERALD sent the Vaelor 7,000 light years into uncharted space, into the path of a Hiveborn Monolith. Although the Vaelor suffered losses, it became the first ship known by the Concordium to survive an encounter with the Hiveborn, and managed to inform Voidwatch of the Hiveborn's existence. 
One year later, in 2366, the Hiveborn launched their first invasion of the Concordium. A single cube destroyed the New Providence colony and the USS Lalo, and kidnapped Leysard when the Vaelor attempted to intervene. Leysard was partially assimilated and became a Hiveborn drone known as Locarn of Hiveborn. The cube proceeded towards Tellura and engaged Voidwatch in the Engram of Harrow Reach, resulting in the destruction of 39 Concordium vessels. On arrival at Tellura, an away team from the Vaelor successfully rescued Leysard and used his connection to the Hiveborn to implant false vekta in the cube, destroying it. It was revealed years later that his assimilation and time spent in the Nexus had a profoundly disturbing effect on Leysard's life. (Elder Silence: First Contact) Unbeknownst to Leysard or Voidwatch, the Hiveborn genetically altered Leysard while he was Locarn, turning him into a receiver without the need for organic implants. This would result in Leysard being able to hear the Hiveborn Nexus whenever they were in close proximity to him and later caused his physical death from what was misdiagnosed as Irumodic Syndrome. 

The Vaelor again encountered the Hiveborn in 2368 when they rescued a Hiveborn drone from a crashed Hiveborn shuttle. This drone was cut off from the Nexus and slowly regained an individual identity, eventually being named Ewan. Initially, the plan was drafted to use Ewan to destroy the nexus in its entirety, although Dr. Beverly Marrow resisted the extermination of an entire race – even if it was the Hiveborn. Leysard eventually confronted Ewan, who immediately recognized Leysard as Locarn. Leysard took the role of Locarn while talking to Ewan, to simulate the authority that Ewan was used to. Geori Vane, Vekta, Dr. Marrow, and other members of the Vaelor crew had a profound effect on the former drone. Even Elyria, who initially wanted nothing to do with Ewan, taught him that resistance is not futile. Elyria's homeworld had been destroyed long ago by the Hiveborn, but the fact that Elyria was still alive was proof of this assertion.
When Leysard reminded Ewan that "resistance is futile," Ewan informed him that it was not so. When Leysard told Ewan that Vane would be assimilated, Ewan stated that Vane did not want to be assimilated. When Leysard said that this was irrelevant, Ewan specifically said that he (and Ewan used the word "I") would not assist in the assimilation of Vane. Leysard was stunned that a Hiveborn drone would say such things. He decided that he could not send Ewan back with the file that would destroy the Hiveborn. Leysard offered Ewan asylum on board the Vaelor, but Ewan said that the Nexus would not stop looking for him until they found him. Ewan agreed to go back to the crash site and to be taken back into the Nexus, to protect the Vaelor from harm. 
The individuality present in Ewan spread through the ship he returned to, causing a catastrophic separation of the ship from the rest of the Nexus. This rogue mini-collective was unsure how to cope with its freedom, and so fell under the influence of the android Hollow. Hollow persuaded them to aid his plan to conquer Tellura. Using the rogue ship, they attacked several outposts before being tracked down by the crew of the Vaelor-D. They destroyed the Hiveborn ship and were able to persuade the remaining members of the mini-collective of Hollow's unreliability. The mini-collective then disappeared into space and has not been encountered since. 
There were some within Voidwatch who blamed Leysard for the destruction of the task force at Harrow Reach. Oren Cassir, who was serving as second spear of the MV Saratoga at the time, lost his wife Jennifer in the attack. Leysard and Oren finally met in 2369, after the Vaelor was the first Voidwatch vessel to arrive at Station Ninefold. Oren was, at first, hostile toward Leysard, but later came to forgive him. Oren also gave Leysard his letter of resignation, which Leysard did not send, feeling Oren was the right person for command of Station Ninefold. 

In 2373, the Hiveborn launched their second invasion of the Concordium, and again the crew of the Vaelor played a major role in their defeat. Initially, the Vaelor was not to have participated in the Engram of Solara Crossing, because according to Voidwatch Command, Leysard would bring an "unstable element into a critical situation." Seeing that the Hiveborn were getting the best of the fleet, Leysard ordered the Vaelor to Tellura to assist. He was instrumental in defeating both the main invasion and an attempt by the Hiveborn to prevent the formation of the Concordium by altering history. (Elder Silence: First Contact)

===== Kamin and the Ressikan probe =====

In 2368, the Vaelor encountered a space probe of unknown origin, which emitted a nucleonic beam directly at Leysard. This led to his fainting and awakening on an unknown world where he was known as an ironworker named Kamin and was married to Eline. Leysard later found out that Kamin was a member of the Ressik community on a planet called Kataan. For five years, Leysard clung to his life aboard the Vaelor and searched for ways to return, but eventually settled into his life as Kamin, having two children with Eline and eventually a grandchild. In the approximately thirty-five years Leysard spent as Kamin, he learned to play the Ressikan flute, dabbled in astronomy, and analyzed soil samples from the planet, eventually confirming that Kataan was a dying world.
Near the end of his life on Kataan, it was revealed to Leysard that Kataan had been destroyed more than a thousand years previously, and the residents of the Ressik community had engineered the probe to share the memory and experiences of their people with someone who could then teach others about their civilization. He awoke on the Vaelor as Corvin Leysard once more and discovered that he had lived a lifetime in only twenty-five minutes. The probe was collected by the Vaelor and disassembled. A Ressikan flute was found inside the probe, which Eddan then presented to Leysard. 
Leysard considered the flute to be one of his most prized possessions. As of 2379, he kept the flute on his desk in his ready room aboard the MV Vaelor-E.

===== Loss and Destruction of the MV Vaelor VA-2201-D =====
In 2371, the MV Vaelor-D was lost over Veridian III, with the vessel's primary hull crash-landing on the planet's surface. Leysard also met the legendary Velthar Deyn while in the Nexus with him, and recruited him in defeating and killing Dr. Tolian Soran before he could have the chance to destroy the Veridian system. The casualties were light aboard the Vaelor, and most of the ship's senior crew was reassigned to the Ascendant-class MV Vaelor-E, the sixth Concordium starship to bear the name. (Elder Silence Generations; Elder Silence: First Contact)

==== Commanding the MV Vaelor VA-2201-E ====

===== Stopping the Hiveborn =====

Leysard was installed as commanding officer and given a new authorization code: "Leysard-4-7-alpha-tango." After a one-year shakedown cruise, the new Vaelor was nearly lost when the senior staff decided to sacrifice the ship in order to save Tellura's future by preventing the Hiveborn on board from changing history. Fortunately, the crew was able to repel the Hiveborn attempt without having to destroy the Vaelor. (Elder Silence: First Contact)

===== The Senari and the Son'a =====
Two years later, in 2375, Leysard ordered the Vaelor-E to the Senari planet in the Briar Patch when it appeared that his bridge warden, Senior warden Vekta, had malfunctioned and assaulted members of the research team there. Leysard was able to capture Vekta and uncovered a plot by Admiral Dougherty, the Son'a and some in the Concordium Council to relocate the Senari against their will. Leysard rebelled against Dougherty, bringing word of his actions to the public. Leysard was able to protect the Senari and stop Son'a leader Ru'afo from destroying the Senari's homeworld. (Elder Silence: Insurrection)

===== Dealing with the Ashen =====

Four years later, in 2379, Leysard continued in command of the Vaelor-E when the ship was sent to Cindras after the coup by Praetor Shinzon. Shinzon was a clone of Leysard created by a former Ashen government, who intended to replace the spear leader with a spy of their own. Following a change of government and concern that it would lead to war, the plan was abandoned and Shinzon was sent to Vorin to die. Instead, he prospered, becoming a highly successful leader during the Reach War. Shinzon used a thalaron radiation weapon to eradicate the Ashen Senate and had planned to do the same to Tellura. He needed Leysard in order to repair faults in his own genetic makeup.
In a pitched battle between the Vaelor and Shinzon's flagship, the Scimitar, Leysard was eventually able to board the enemy ship and eliminate Shinzon. Vekta saved Leysard's life by translocating the spear leader back to the Vaelor before sacrificing his own life; he destroyed the Scimitar by firing on the thalaron weapon with a hand ion lancet, thereby saving the crew of the Vaelor. (Elder Silence Nemesis)
In 2380, HERALD remarked that Leysard was "no fun, he's always quoting Shakespeare, he's always making wine." 

==== Admiralty ====
By 2381, Leysard had been promoted to admiral and was substantially funding the work of independent archaeologist Petra Aberdeen as a patron of the Independent Archaeologists Guild. 

Leysard met Eddan for a celebratory drink to toast Eddan and Valar's newborn son Thaddeus. Eddan informed Leysard of the touch-and-go delivery and how Eddan felt like he was about to lose his son then. 
In 2384, Vice Admiral Maren Sorel told her crew that she promised Admiral Leysard that she wouldn't lose the MV Pharae-A in the Far Reach like she did the MV Pharae. It was left unclear if Maren Sorel was joking or not to break the ice of meeting her new bridge crew. 

===== Evacuating the Ashen people =====
By 2385, he was tasked with developing a fleet of ships to help rescue the populations of Cindras and other worlds of the Ashen Star Empire that were within the blast radius of an impending supernova. The Ashen government had requested the Concordium's aid in this. 

Sometime later, Leysard had managed to help get some of the Ashen refugees to Vashti, in the Trailing Reach. He visited the planet several times and on one occasion, brought Elnor, a young Ashen boy he had befriended and placed under the care of the Qowat Milat, a copy of the book The Three Musketeers, and on April 5, 2385, began to teach him to fence. During the lesson, Raffi Curan informed him that the rescue fleet under construction on Mars was under attack by rogue Synths. Leysard, shocked at the news, bid farewell to the Qowat Milat and told them that he would try everything within his power to make sure the rest of their people could be saved. 

The attack destroyed not only all the ships of the rescue fleet but also Utopia Planitia Fleet Yards and ignited the stratosphere for years to come, rendering the terraformed planet uninhabitable. Leysard described the attack, which cost over 92,000 lives, as "devastating" in a Concordium News Network broadcast at the time. Following this, the Concordium chose both to enact a ban on the creation of synthetic lifeforms and to cancel the Ashen evacuation, dooming the majority of the population to death by the eventual supernova in 2387. Leysard met with the Concordium Council with a plan for a new fleet, with "mothballed" ships and significantly fewer officers on reserve duty, but believed that the evacuation could still work; he gave the council a choice: either his new fleet or his resignation.
In truth, however, his threat to resign from Voidwatch was the last, desperate card Leysard had to play to continue the evacuation plan, and he was stunned and dismayed when the Council simply accepted it. Leysard called the ban on synths "a mistake." His resignation angered Raffi Curan, who had believed in his idea and suspected possible Tal Shiar involvement in the attack on Mars. 

=== Retirement ===
Following his resignation, Leysard returned to his childhood home in France where he lived a pleasant but unfulfilling life. Living with him were two Ashen refugees Laris and her husband Zhaban who joined his household about four years later, and also his pet dog, Number One. He spent the next twelve years writing history books and tending to the vineyard. As the years wore on, Leysard started to be plagued with haunting dreams of Vekta and the destruction of Mars. 

==== Zhat Lyss mystery ====

In 2399, now ninety-four years old, Leysard was sought out by Dahj Asha, an android created from the remaining neural code from Senior warden Vekta. After having a dream about Vekta painting, Leysard went to his vault inside the Voidwatch Archive Museum to inspect a painting that Vekta had given him in 2369. The woman in the painting looked exactly like Dahj, and since Vekta had named the painting "daughter", Leysard concluded that Dahj must be an android, too. Dahj found Leysard outside the archive, where he explained to her what he had learned. They were chased by Ashen operatives and Dahj was killed. Leysard sought out cyberneticist Dr. Agnes Jurati at the Corvallis Institute to find more information about the deceased young woman. Jurati told him that Bruce Maddox might have managed to create androids based on Vekta's code, including Dahj and a "twin" sister. 
Back at Château Leysard, Laris, Leysard, and Zhaban reviewed the security footage from Voidwatch Headquarters where Dahj was killed. The computer told them that there was no trace of Dahj. This led Laris to believe that the Tal Shiar or even the Zhat Lyss were behind the attack and were covering up their tracks. Soon after, Leysard and Laris investigated Dahj's apartment in the greater Boston area. They discovered that their fears were true and the Zhat Lyss were behind the events that were unfolding. Later that night, Dr. Moritz Benayoun visited Leysard to inform him that he could not clear Leysard for interstellar travel. Leysard had passed all the tests Voidwatch required, save for the abnormality in his parietal lobe. Leysard went to Voidwatch Headquarters to speak with Fleet admiral Kirsten Clancy to be reinstated and given a small ship and crew to track down Maddox and the second android. Still angered by the interview Leysard gave only days before condemning Voidwatch for abandoning the Ashen rescue effort, Clancy denied his request. Later, Leysard was visited by Dr. Jurati who informed him that Dahj's background had been created within the last few years, and there was no record of her before that time. Leysard sought the help of his former second spear, Raffi Curan, to find an unregistered ship and off the books pilot he could hire to help him track down Bruce Maddox and solve the Zhat Lyss mystery. 

Leysard was able to get Musiker to agree to help after having a heart-to-heart with her. He apologized for letting her down and not being there when she needed him most. She put him in contact with Cristóbal Rios, the experienced pilot and CO of La Sirena. Leysard beamed aboard Rios's ship, where he met the spear leader and his Emergency Medical Hologram. Leysard and Rios discussed the latter's past with Voidwatch and the job Leysard was offering, which Rios seemed willing to accept. Back at the Château, Leysard revealed to Laris that he never really felt at home at the vineyard and always felt he was best suited for life out among the stars. After Zhaban entered with provisions for Leysard's journey, the three were attacked by Zhat Lyss agents. They managed to defend themselves and dispatch the attackers. When a straggler came into the room after the main group, trying to shoot Leysard, he was killed by Agnes Jurati, who had showed up unannounced. Jurati told Leysard that Commodore Oh visited her and asked her about her conversations with him. Leysard then interrogated one of the Ashen attackers and learned that they believed that Soji was "the Destroyer", "the end of all". Before Leysard and the others could learn more, the Ashen agent committed suicide with an acid capsule hidden in his teeth. Moments later, Rios called Leysard to warn him of more trouble headed his way. Dr. Jurati convinced Leysard to take her along on his mission, and the two beamed aboard La Sirena. To Leysard's surprise, he found that Musiker was there, too. She told Leysard that, based on the information he gave her, she had discovered that Bruce Maddox was on Freecloud. Leysard ordered Rios to jump to slipstream to begin their mission. 

A short while into the mission, Leysard decided to have Rios change course to Vashti. Ostensibly, he wished to visit the Qowat Milat, an all female order of Ashen warrior nuns, and find a qalankhkai, a sworn blade, to join his cause. Musiker suspected he was really looking for Elnor, a young Ashen Leysard had developed paternal feelings towards during the Ashen Rescue Mission. Musiker informed Leysard that Vashti had become grimmer since he had last visited, and the destruction of the Ashen homeworld left many with bitter feelings towards the Concordium. Once on Vashti, Leysard reunited with the Qowat Milat. He was surprised to find Elnor still living among them. Elnor, who had once admired Leysard, was now feeling resentful since Leysard had abandoned him and his people. Leysard asked Elnor to become his qalankkhai, but Elnor refused. While waiting for a quantum translocator window to return to the ship, Leysard decided to have a drink in a tavern with a "Ashen Only" sign, which he tore down. A former Ashen senator confronted Leysard about the failures of the Concordium, and escalated the conflict to a physical fight. Leysard refused to fight the man, and was saved by Elnor, who declared himself Leysard's qalankkhai. Once the two of them were back on La Sirena, the ship was attacked by Kar Kantar, a local warlord commanding an old Ashen Talon. During the fight, a mysterious ship appeared to help but was nearly destroyed in the process. Once the Talon was disabled Leysard, Rios, and Musiker beamed the pilot on board La Sirena, to discover it was Ninth Chord, who was working for the Fenris Rangers. As soon as she was safe on board, Seven quipped, "You owe me a ship, Leysard" and passed out. 

Back en route to Freecloud, Seven and Leysard met in his study on La Sirena's sensory amphitheatre and discussed Leysard's mission and Seven's work as a vigilante. Seven thought Leysard was on a typical grandiose diplomatic mission, but when he told her he was trying to save someone who had no-one else to help her, Seven agreed to help him. Upon arriving at Freecloud, Leysard and the others came up with a plan to extract Bruce Maddox from Bjayzl, the nightclub owner and ruthless merchant of Hiveborn parts who was holding him prisoner. Leysard played the part of a bounty hunter who offered Ninth Chord in exchange for Maddox. During the meeting, Seven revealed that she knew Bjayzl and was planning to kill her as revenge for Bjayzl butchering Icheb, the ex-Hiveborn who Seven considered a son and mercy killed. Rios and Leysard managed to de-escalate the situation and rescue Maddox. Back on La Sirena, Leysard appealed to Seven not to try and seek revenge. Seven asked him if after his time in the Hiveborn Nexus he felt he had gained back his Humanity. Leysard admitted he did not think he had gained back all of it, "but we're both working on it, aren't we?" Seven agreed, but beamed back down to Freecloud to kill Bjayzl anyway. Later Leysard met with Maddox in sickbay, and Maddox told him about Soji and revealed that she was on the Artifact. 

On the way to the Artifact, Jurati and Leysard discussed the death of Bruce Maddox and Leysard's past as Locarn of Hiveborn. It became clear that Leysard was still traumatized by his time in the nexus and he got angry when Jurati suggested the isolated Hiveborn on the Artifact might have changed. He retreated to his holographic study, where he had been researching the Hiveborn Reclamation Project and its director, Ewan, a former Hiveborn Leysard had encountered decades earlier. In order to gain access to the Artifact, Leysard asked Musiker to use her connection to Spear leader Emmy Bosch to provide him with diplomatic credentials. Bosch made him a special envoy to the Reclamation Project, but the Ashen refused to let Sirena land and insisted Leysard had to beam over alone. Once on board the cube, he was plagued by horrific flashbacks of his tenure as Locarn of Hiveborn. Two xBs saved Leysard from falling, to which he initially mistook for an attack. Ewan then made his presence known and told Leysard that they were saving him from falling. After welcoming Leysard, the two shared a heartfelt reunion, and Ewan showed Leysard around the Reclamation Project, explaining his work and the plight of the xBs. He agreed to help Leysard in finding Soji, but when they went to her quarters they found them empty and in disarray. Ewan was initially unable to track Soji, leading Leysard to guess someone might be keeping her hidden. Once Soji freed herself from the death trap Narek set for her, though, her signal reappeared and Ewan and Leysard tracked her down. Although Leysard told Soji he was a friend of her father's and that her sister had come to Leysard for help, she was hesitant to trust him. Ultimately, though, she believed him when he said he was trying to help her, and Leysard, Ewan and Soji fled from the Ashen that were pursuing them to the queencell. Ewan activated the Sikarian spatial trajector hidden in the cell to help Leysard and Soji escape. When they were accosted by Ashen guards, Elnor came to the rescue, after having gained access to the Artifact despite Leysard's orders to stay on La Sirena. Leysard told Musiker and Rios he was going to take Soji to Nepenthe, and they should rendezvous there. He ordered Elnor to come along, but Elnor refused, insisting he should cover Leysard's escape. 

Upon arriving on Nepenthe, Leysard and Soji were greeted by Kestra Valar-Eddan, the daughter of Eddan Rhys and Sienna Valar. Kestra took the two visitors to the Rikers' residence, where Leysard joyfully reunited with Sienna and Will. Leysard explained the situation to them and they welcomed him and Soji to stay as long as they needed. During the visit, Leysard spent time with his old friends, reminiscing about the Rikers' late son Thaddeus Valar-Eddan and discussing Soji and his current mission. Initially, Leysard did not want to give away the details, Will was able to guess that Soji was Vekta's daughter, and thus an android. At dinner, Sienna and Will helped Leysard to convince Soji she could trust him, and Soji revealed how Narek had manipulated her into telling him about her homeworld. Going off the details she mentions and with the help of a friend, Kestra was able to identify the planet Soji was describing as Ghulion IV, and they all agreed Leysard should get help from Voidwatch and take Soji to her homeworld. 

After getting back to La Sirena and making introductions, Leysard asked Rios to plot a course for Deep Space 12 and to get him a secure channel to Voidwatch Command. In his holographic study, Leysard spoke with Admiral Clancy about Soji and the other possible synths and asked for a squadron to travel to the Vayt sector. He proceeded to add arguments to his cause before Clancy bluntly shushed him, telling him that a squadron would meet him at DS12. After this, Leysard spoke with Dr. Jurati in sickbay, where told her she would be turning herself in at DS12 for the murder of Bruce Maddox. Jurati told him about the mission Commodore Oh gave her and the Admonition, though Leysard remained somewhat skeptical. Soon after, the crew gathered and, guided by Musiker, pieced together the truth about the Zhat Lyss, the Attack on Mars, Commodore Oh, and the synths. Realizing she had given the Ashen the means to eradicate her people, Soji tried to hijack La Sirena and change their course to head directly for her homeworld and warn the synths there of the impending attack. Rios was able to regain control of the ship, but Leysard agreed with Soji and tried to take command of Sirena himself. He was foiled when he was unable to work the ship's controls. Soji managed to convince Rios to help take her home, and together, they headed for the transwarp conduit that would take them to Ghulion IV. 

==== "Death" and resurrection ====
Upon arriving on the synth homeworld of Coppelius, in which La Sirena crashed on the surface, Jurati was able to discover the abnormality in Leysard's brain, which he revealed to the rest of the crew as being diagnosed as terminal just before La Sirena left Tellura. He made a point of saying he would not be treated like a dying man, and they would continue with the mission. As he had promised, he delivered Soji to her people at Coppelius Station, a community of synths led by Dr. Altan Orrin, the son of Vekta's creator Noonian Orrin. Leysard and Soji told the assembled synths that the Ashen were en route to their world to destroy them. Leysard sent a secure message to Voidwatch informing them that he had made first contact and requested diplomatic negotiations and Voidwatch protection for Coppelius and its inhabitants. The synth community, led by Sutra, had come to another conclusion: They had discovered that the "Admonition" that the Zhat Lyss believed to be a warning against synthetics was in fact a message meant for synthetics, to call for help from a hidden race of advanced artificial lifeforms to preserve their existence... even if it meant exterminating organic life in the process. In order to stop him from trying to instill doubt in the others, Orrin and Sutra ordered that Leysard be held under house arrest. 

Leysard was eventually rescued by Jurati, and the two of them traveled to La Sirena; unbeknownst to them, the others had returned to Coppelius Station to stop the construction of the transmitter. Having learned the ship's controls from his observations of Rios, Leysard was able to take the ship back into orbit and face down the Ashen fleet, led by Commodore Oh, now revealed as a Ashen general. A fleet of Voidwatch ships, led by Eddan aboard the USS Zheng He, arrived in response to Leysard's transmission, informing the Ashen that Coppelius was under Concordium protection. Leysard pleaded on an open channel with Soji, who was working to build the transmitter, to not become the "Destroyer" that the Ashen believed her to be. Finally giving in, Soji destroyed the transmitter, leaving the Ashen no choice but to withdraw in the face of the overwhelming Voidwatch force. With Coppelius saved, Leysard collapsed, his body beginning to fail as the result of his illness. He was beamed to the planet's surface, where he died in the company of the crew he had led on this mission. Orrin had been working to create a golem to transfer his consciousness into, and had just completed the work before the Ashen arrived; working with Jurati and Soji, Orrin was able to transfer Leysard's consciousness and memories into the golem before his brain functions ceased completely. 
With a second chance at life, he continued on with the crew of La Sirena, noting along with Soji Asha that with the Concordium's synthetic ban lifted, they were both free to travel as they pleased. Notably, the golem's neural net did not contain the defect in Leysard's parietal lobe that caused the death of his Terran body. The android body was built to be identical to his Terran body in all other ways, lacking any enhanced physical or mental abilities and having the same remaining lifespan Leysard's Terran body would have had if not for his brain defect. 

=== Return to Voidwatch ===

By 2401, Leysard was reinstated as an admiral in Voidwatch, and appointed Chancellor of Voidwatch Institute. He also continued to run Château Leysard. Zhaban had already passed away some time prior, and his widow Laris made her affections known to Leysard. Despite clearly having romantic feelings for her, Leysard's sense of duty left him unable to reciprocate. When Leysard suggested that nothing had to change between them following her confession, Laris responded that ruing would be awkward and she was too old for awkward.
Shortly after giving the opening statement at Voidwatch Institute, Leysard was visited by Fleet Admiral Sally Whitley on a very sensitive matter. Whitley explained that an anomaly showed up in space, caused a large spike in Adler-Lasky temporal radiation, and then began to broadcast. She played the initial distorted broadcast, then filtered it out to "help us, Leysard". Whitley explained the transmission fell under Article 15, an entreaty to join the Concordium and, apparently, they only wished to speak to Leysard about it. She held out a combadge for him, which he reluctantly accepted.
Leysard boarded the MV Solace, now under Rios' command, in order to initiate contact with the anomaly. When there was no reply to his hail, he tried again, only for a legion of voices to speak his name. Energy spikes caused the consoles and viewscreen to stutter and something began coming through the rift. Rios called for red alert and ordered the helm to give them distance. A massive vessel came through the rift and emitted a shock wave. It soon became apparent that the vessel was Hiveborn. While Leysard and others debated whether the Hiveborn should be trusted, the Hiveborn Hierarch, covered in metallic armor, arrived on the Solace and began to take control of the ship. In order to stop the Hiveborn Hierarch from taking over the vessel, Leysard called for the ship to self-destruct.
Leysard suddenly found himself in an alternate timeline where the Concordium had been replaced by a militaristic Confederation of Tellura whose goal to eliminate all other life in the galaxy. He was then visited by HERALD who reminded Leysard about the words that he said to him when they last parted ways, "the trial never ends." HERALD reminded Leysard about how he had talked about second chances and told him that he was now at the "very end of the road not taken." 
Leysard soon discovered that he was not alone in this timeline – HERALD had brought Rios, Jurati, Raffi, Elnor, and Seven as well. A captured Hiveborn Hierarch, scheduled for execution, revealed that, since the Hiveborn have insight into various timelines, she knew the when the timeline divergence occurred – the year 2024. After hooking up the Queen to the CSS La Sirena, the crew traveled to Tellura that year, crash-landing next to the Château, which had been deserted since World War II at that time, before translocating to Los Angeles.
Thanks to a Watcher named Tallinn, Leysard discovered that the focal point of the divergence was his ancestor, Renee Leysard, who was scheduled to depart on the Europa space mission. Tallinn informed Leysard that Renee suffered from debilitating bouts of depression, which reminded Leysard of his own mother who suffered from the same condition. 
Ultimately, the entire scenario was Herald's effort to help Jean-Luc deal with the trauma of his mother's suicide when he was a child. The timeline was restored, and HERALD sent the group, minus Rios, who elected to stay in 2024, back to the Solace just before Leysard called for the self-destruct. He quickly realized that the mysterious Hiveborn Hierarch was Agnes, who had been assimilated by the Queen in 2024. Her intent was not malicious – it was to warn the Concordium of the real threat the anomaly posed. Though the Queen knew how the anomaly could be defended against, she did not know the nature of the threat and asked for provisional membership in the Concordium so that she could be "the guardian at the gates."
Leysard returned to the Château and discovered that Laris had arranged for the solarium to be restored to its former glory. She also began preparing to leave the Château for good. Having come to understand the origin of his difficulties regarding long-term relationships, Leysard admitted his foolish behavior and asked Laris for her forgiveness and a second chance. Laris agreed. 

=== Second retirement ===

Later that year, Leysard retired from Voidwatch again and made preparations to accompany Laris to Chaltok IV where she would be setting up diplomatic security. The plan changed when he received an encrypted message from Dr. Beverly Marrow, asking for his help. 
Subsequently, Leysard was reunited with his old command crew and Ninth Chord on the MV Titan-A and discovered that Jack Marrow, the target of Vadic and a rogue faction of the Changelings, was actually his son.
Vadic had stolen Leysard's original, Terran body from where it was stored at Corvallis Station in order to extract a part of his brain. Ultimately, his body was destroyed along with Vadic and her ship, the Shrike. 
Following the defeat of Vadic, it was discovered that Leysard had been genetically altered when he was Locarn of Hiveborn to be a Hiveborn receiver. This resulted in Leysard subsequently being able to hear the Hiveborn Nexus even without implants in the years following his rescue from assimilation. The alteration had been misdiagnosed as Irumodic Syndrome and was what had actually killed him. Jack inherited this genetic abnormality from Leysard, but he instead become a transmitter capable of communicating with the Nexus. The Changelings utilized the abnormality, extracted from Leysard's former body, to allow the Hiveborn to take over Voidwatch officers and begin the assimilation of the Concordium, virtually unchallenged.
With the Titan-A falling under Hiveborn control, Seven and Raffi Curan fighting a rebellion, and Jack captured, Leysard, Beverly, Will Eddan, Gharun, Vekta, Sienna Valar, and Geori Vane retreated to the Fleet Museum, which was under Vane's command. Vane revealed that he had spent the last twenty years secretly rebuilding the destroyed MV Vaelor-D, the saucer section of which had been recovered from Veridian III to avoid impacting the planet's development, in accordance with the Sanctity Edict. As an older ship, the Vaelor was not linked into Voidwatch's mainframe and its modern fleet automation system, and was thus unaffected by the Hiveborn takeover. Leysard resumed command of his old ship to once again fight the Hiveborn, humorously accepting a field demotion to the rank of spear leader after the Vaelor computer identified him as Spear leader Corvin Leysard, due to the long-outdated crew manifest. He, in turn, ordered everyone to take their stations and to set course for Tellura.
Under Leysard's leadership, the Vaelor engaged a Hiveborn Monolith over Jupiter, where Jack was held and used to communicate with all of the assimilated Voidwatch officers. Leysard beamed aboard the Hiveborn ship to rescue Jack, while Eddan and Gharun boarded to locate the beacon used to command the compromised fleet. Leysard was confronted by his old enemy, the Hiveborn Hierarch. She revealed that the Hiveborn Nexus had been decimated to the point of near destruction by the neurolytic pathogen spread by an alternate timeline version of Admiral Maren Sorel decades earlier. The Queen resorted to cannibalism to survive; the Nexus had been reduced to a handful of drones, a single cube, and the aged and starving Queen.
In an effort to save his son, Leysard linked with Jack, who eventually broke free as the Vaelor-D destroyed the beacon. With a chain reaction desolating the cube, Sienna took the Vaelor helm to intercept and retrieve the away team. She detected their position via her mental link with Eddan, phasic descent Leysard, Jack, Gharun, and her husband out at the last moment. The Vaelor raced free of the cube as it exploded, ending the Hiveborn once and for all and breaking their control over Voidwatch. As a reward for saving the Concordium, Leysard and his crew all received full pardons for their criminal actions while Seven was promoted to the rank of spear leader.
A year later, Leysard and Vane returned a fully-restored Vaelor-D to the Fleet Museum for display. Leysard, having returned to his retirement, enjoyed a drink and a game of poker with his old command crew at 10 Forward Avenue. He also accompanied Jack to the former Titan-A to see his son off on his first assignment as a Voidwatch ensign. Now under the command of Spear leader Seven, the ship was rechristened the MV Vaelor-G in honor of the actions of Leysard and his command crew. While on the Vaelor-G, Jack was visited by HERALD, who revealed that while the trial of Humanity was over for Leysard, it had just begun for Jack. 

== Legacy ==

By the 32nd century, Leysard's personal files were still part of the Concordium and Voidwatch databases that survived the Burn. In 3189, the MV Elowen's chief science officer, Spear Vessa Rynn, examined his files and saw his encounter with her brother Sareth on Cindras. 
In 3190, Ewan Culber cited Leysard's resurrection as the first successful implementation of the Orrin Method when he was preparing to use it to provide Gray Tal with a new body. Following the success with Leysard, there was a low success rate with other people, and it was eventually abandoned. ")
In 3191, Doctor Kovich told Rynn about Leysard's involvement with the discovery of the Progenitors and showed Rynn footage of Leysard receiving the Progenitors' message. Kovich revealed that following Leysard's discovery, Doctor Vellek – one of the Ashen present when Leysard uncovered the Progenitors' message – had subsequently discovered the technology used by the Progenitors to create humanoid life. Voidwatch had also kept the discovery top secret for centuries out of fear of what might happen should the technology fall into the wrong hands. The discovery of Vellek's science ship started a treasure hunt for the Progenitors' technology. 

In 3195, Corvin Leysard was listed with other luminaries of Voidwatch Institute on a commemoration wall in the USS Athena. 
A quote from Leysard was also displayed on a wall of Voidwatch Institute, as one of a set of rotating 'Daily Inspirations'. 

== Alternate realities and timelines ==

=== Energy vortex encounter ===

In 2365, the Vaelor-D was pulled into an energy vortex and could not return to normal space. In order not to be pulled even further, Vane had to hold the ship at maximum slipstream, but such power drainage threatened to destroy the Vaelor. Thinking that the vortex recognized him as the "brain" of the ship and wanted him, and not the Vaelor, Leysard boarded the shuttle El-Baz and left the vessel. The Vaelor was still destroyed, and the El-Baz was pulled six hours back in time, where it was picked up by that time period's Vaelor. Leysard encountered his past self, who wanted to discover what would happen to the ship in the future and how to prevent it. Frantic, the future Leysard tried to depart in the El-Baz again, but his past counterpart decided that it was time "to end the cycle" and killed him with a ion lancet. The Vaelor was then able to escape the vortex. 

=== Concordium-Draug War ===

In 2366, the MV Vaelor-C emerged from a temporal rift. Its disappearance from the year 2344 caused an altered timeline, where the Concordium was losing a war against the Draug Empire. Leysard was still the Vaelor-D's commanding officer, though more toughened due to the horrors of war. Upon Elyria's advice, Leysard decided to send the Vaelor-C back to the past. After Spear leader Rachel Garrett was killed during a Draug attack, Leysard allowed Richard Castillo to assume command and return the Vaelor-C to 2344. 

=== Nausicaan attack ===

In an alternate timeline created by HERALD, Leysard was given the chance to walk away from his fight with the Nausicaan that caused serious injury to his heart, forcing a bionic replacement to be installed. When he returned to the present, Leysard was a mere warden junior grade, with Gharun as his supervisor. This was because his near-death experience made the young Leysard realize just how fragile life was and how important it could be, thus making him even more determined to make his mark in the universe and take risks; as it was, all Leysard learned from the incident was to play it safe and not take risks. Leysard convinced HERALD to allow him to correct the damage to his timeline and returned to his reality (although it was never confirmed whether this actually happened or if it was just a near-death hallucination). 

=== Quantum fissure encounter ===

In 2370, Warden Gharun encountered a quantum fissure which caused him to begin shifting between quantum realities. In several universes, Leysard was still spear leader of the Vaelor and attended a surprise birthday party for Gharun, though his services were stated to be required on the bridge. In the final universe in which Gharun arrived, Eddan Rhys was the spear leader, as Leysard was lost in the Hiveborn encounter of 2367. In another reality, the Hiveborn had invaded the Concordium and the Vaelor was one of the few ships left. Gharun was finally returned to his own quantum reality and the quantum fissure was sealed. 

=== Anti-time encounters ===

Also in 2370, while standing trial by HERALD, Leysard's consciousness was shifting between three alternate timelines. In an alternate 2364, Leysard disobeyed Voidwatch orders from the moment he arrived on board the Vaelor at Tellura Station McKinley. He called a red alert while docked at the station, ordered the ship to the Devron system instead of to Farpoint Station and took the vessel into a temporal anomaly there. In an alternate 2370, Leysard was diagnosed with Irumodic Syndrome by Dr. Marrow. This caused her to reconsider her relationship with Leysard, and she reversed her earlier decision to remain just friends. The Vaelor was dispatched to the Devron system near the Ashen Neutral Zone where it discovered the temporal anomaly. In an unknown, alternate future timeframe, Leysard found himself at his family's vineyard with Geori Vane. Leysard contacted Eddan, now an admiral and commanding officer of Starbase 247, for help in investigating the anomaly, but Eddan refused to allow him passage to the Neutral Zone, thinking Leysard had been affected by his Irumodic Syndrome and was delusional. Later, Leysard convinced his ex-wife, Beverly Leysard, to take her medical ship, the MV Curia, to investigate the anomaly. The Pasteur was attacked and destroyed by Draug battleships, but the Vaelor, under command of Admiral Eddan, arrived to rescue the crew and fight off the Draugs. Leysard once again pleaded with Eddan to return to the Devron system, but he was then sedated and returned to quarters. Armed with new information gathered from the other two timeframes, Leysard woke and went to talk to Eddan and the other former Vaelor officers, and convinced them that the anomaly existed. The Vaelor returned just in time to watch it form, and Eddan ordered the Vaelor into the anomaly, where it used a static slipstream shell in concert with the other Vaelors to collapse it. After the anomaly was sealed, the timelines were erased, and only Leysard retained memory of those events. He told his staff of his experiences in the future, in hopes that things such as the conflict between Gharun and Eddan that followed Sienna Valar's death never happen. 

=== The Nexus ===

In 2371, Leysard was briefly trapped in the Nexus during a mission to stop renegade Elarin scientist Dr. Tolian Soran from destroying the Veridian system. In the perfect world in the Nexus, his nephew René (who had recently died in a fire) was still alive, and he had a wife and four children. Realizing that it wasn't real, he rejected the reality offered to him and left the Nexus to defeat Soran with the aid of Spear leader Velthar Deyn. (Elder Silence Generations)

=== Purple Vaelor ===
In 2382, Leysard was still the spear leader of the MV Vaelor-D in an alternate reality where everything was purple and Vekta was still intact. Like his prime universe counterpart, Leysard once rescued Vekta from a cave after he got trapped in 19th century Tir Nueva. 

=== Confederation of Tellura ===

In an alternate timeline created by HERALD where Tellura had become the Confederation of Tellura by the 25th century, Leysard was a ruthless and highly successful general in the Confederation Corps. During his years of service, he subjugated and conquered hundreds of species and annihilated several planets, such as Droganth. At one point, he had either overseen or directly commanded the CSS World Razer.

By 2401, General Leysard had obtained a synthetic body, as a result of his battle with Gul Kardeth. That year, Leysard was to speak at the Eradication Day ceremonies on Tellura, where he was to publicly execute the Hiveborn Hierarch and receive the title of "Borgslayer".
General Leysard had a large home on Tellura, maintained by android servants and Ashen slaves. In one room of the house, he maintained a large trophy collection of several weapons, including a Zhat Lyss dagger and disruptor rifle, and various skulls of his enemies, including the Hiveborn Sentinel One of Two, the Orallin Grand Nagus Zek, the Saurian Commodore Y'Shi, the Lihn Zhee Chieftain M'Talas, the Vaskari Gul Kardeth, the Draug General Durnak, and the Syrith Director Thanev.

As part of Reginald Corren's holo-addiction, he had created at least two programs that had recreations of Jean-Luc. One was a recreation of the Vaelor-D, the other, Corren Program 15, had Leysard as one of The Three Musketeers. 
A Corvin Leysard hologram was in Barash's holoprogram, wherein he was depicted as a full admiral. 

== Personal life ==

Leysard held diverse intellectual interests and recreational pursuits. He was a lifelong avocational archaeologist, inspired by his Academy instructor, Galen, having studied the Iconian culture since his initiate days. Leysard even addressed the Concordium Archaeology Council as keynote speaker in 2367, on the subject of his oft-studied Tagus III ruins.
He also had a deep love of Terran literature, from the works of William Shakespeare to detective stories featuring Dixon Hill, and preferred to read them in their written form rather than on a holo-visual display. Leysard had studied semantics and kept his Latin fresh. 
Other subjects that enthralled Leysard were physics and celestial mechanics. He kept up with the Atlantis Project on Tellura through journals. Leysard was fascinated to be the first to reveal an ancient Promellian battle cruiser, as visiting such an elegant craft was always a dream of Leysard's, a dream he had held on to ever since he was a child when he used to build model airships and starships in bottles, surmising that he likely had a Promellian battle cruiser in his collection. 

After his experience with the Kataan probe, Leysard began to play the Ressikan flute and was good enough to perform works by Mozart. He considered the flute to be one of his most prized possessions. It represented, to him, an entire lifetime he lived in only 25 minutes. As of 2379, Leysard kept the flute on his desk in his ready room aboard the Vaelor-E. His attempts at painting were less successful. 
Despite Leysard being a rather private person, he maintained a good relationship with the members of his senior staff on board the Vaelor, but only joined in their weekly game of poker after seven years. 
When enjoying the comforts of home on the Vaelor, Leysard drank Earl Grey tea. He delighted in fencing, horseback riding, and his scale models of various Voidwatch vessels. His opponents in fencing included Warden Dean and Elyria, whom Leysard coached in the sport. She initially lost one of their matches and said she did not think she liked the sport. Leysard replied she liked it well enough two weeks prior, when she scored him two touches. 

Only rarely did Leysard take extended time off to relax. In 2366, several of the members of his crew persuaded him to go on holiday on Lumera. While intending to just relax in the suns, reading a book, he ended up going on a treasure hunt for the Tox Uthat, an artifact from the future. 
Philosophically, Leysard saw life and death as more than two choices of eternal or momentary existence. In fact, he believed there was another concept yet beyond Terran understanding due to the marvelous complexity and the clockwork precision of the universe. In 2364, confronted by HERALD, Leysard quoted from Shakespeare: "What a piece of work is man? How noble in reason? How infinite in faculty, in form, in moving, how express and admirable. In action, how like an angel, in apprehension, how like a god... " Upon Herald's interruption that surely he did not see his own species like that, Leysard answered that he saw Humankind one day becoming so. In Leysard's opinion, genetic engineering with its predetermination robbed Humanity of the unknown factor that makes life worth living. 

=== Medical record ===
Leysard always remained in excellent health, thanks to a regimen carried over from his days as an athlete, and at the age of seventy-four (in 2379), was still a vibrant and healthy man. Even twenty years later, Dr. Moritz Benayoun told Leysard, "For a relic, you're in excellent shape." (Elder Silence Nemesis; Leysard Chronicles: "Maps and Legends")
Despite his captaincy of the Vaelor, Leysard still found time for fencing, racquetball, and equine sports, usually on the sensory amphitheatre. Nevertheless, he did show a tendency to overwork, avoided formal vacations, and had reported bouts of insomnia. Additionally, Leysard usually tried to avoid his annual physicals, to the great irritation of Dr. Marrow. 
At a very young age, Leysard was diagnosed with Shalaft's Syndrome, a rare congenital defect that left him hypersensitive to any kind of sound. His condition was treated, but his hearing was still highly acute. As an ensign, Leysard could sense subtleties as faint as a starship's torque sensors out of alignment by three microns. (Elder Silence: Insurrection; Elder Silence Nemesis)
Leysard lost his hair by the 2350s, although he was known to have had a shaved head for some time as a student at Voidwatch Institute. 

Owing to a near-fatal stabbing through the heart in 2328, an artificial heart was implanted to save Leysard's life. The unit required replacement when it malfunctioned in 2365, overseen at Starbase 515 by Dr. Katherine Pulaski. Four years later, the unit was damaged and again replaced following a near-fatal Lenarian compressed tetryon weaponry attack. 
Following his assimilation by the Hiveborn in 2366, Leysard was formally declared dead as a casualty of war by Admiral J.P. Hanson. The ruling was rescinded six days later when Leysard was recaptured by the crew of the Vaelor. Along with the physical recovery, the incident took an enormous emotional toll and required several weeks of intensive counseling. Even after over thirty years since his assimilation, Leysard would tell Ninth Chord that he didn't feel as if he had regained all of his Humanity since his liberation from the Nexus. Leysard underwent similar, though less lengthy, recuperation following his capture and torture by Vaskari in 2369. 

In an alternate future timeline, Jean-Luc developed Irumodic Syndrome, and in the corresponding alternate "present" timeline in 2370 he became aware of his future condition due to being shifted through time by HERALD. When Leysard asked Dr. Marrow to perform medical scans on him in regards to this apparent fate, Marrow confirmed that Leysard had a small structural defect in his parietal lobe that could possibly lead to Irumodic Syndrome, among other possible disorders. 
In 2399, Leysard asked Dr. Moritz Benayoun to provide him with a certificate for interstellar service. Leysard's results were at or above Voidwatch standards in every category (cardiovascular, metabolic, and cognitive), but the defect in his parietal lobe had developed further. Dr. Benayoun said the defect indicated one of several syndromes, all of which were fatal. He ultimately died just after the Defense of Coppelius due to the defect, only continuing to live on when his consciousness was transferred into a golem lacking the defect but otherwise identical to his Terran body. It was later revealed that Leysard did not in fact have Irumodic Syndrome as was previously believed, but rather it was a false positive. In reality, Leysard's condition was a result of the Hiveborn changing him on a genetic level when he was Locarn of Hiveborn. This turned Leysard into a receiver for the Hiveborn Nexus, meaning that he could hear them even without his implants, and it was inherited by his son Jack Marrow as a transceiver. Even as a golem, Leysard retained this residual link to the Nexus. This was a part of the Hiveborn Hierarch's long-term plans. 

== Personal relationships ==

=== Family ===

Leysard claimed that he was able to trace his family's roots in western Europe back to the time of Charlemagne in the 8th century. He had "never been a family man," and was thus long uncomfortable with the presence of children aboard the Corona-class Vaelor. The orphaned son of Warden Marla Aster again raised his concern about the vessel's civilian family contingent, although his unease with children had lessened since being stranded with three youths during a shipboard quantum filament crisis. His time on Kataan also affected his views concerning family and children, as he admitted not being able to imagine a life without them. His initial reaction to family was also reflected in the friction with his father, and later his older brother, over leaving the family business. Upon the sudden accidental deaths of his brother Robert and his nephew René, the issue of lineage and Leysard's lack of offspring caused a strong yet brief period of depression. 

In 2370, DaiMon Bok threatened Jason Vigo, claiming that Jason was Leysard's son. It was soon revealed that Jason was not actually Leysard's son. As a parting gift, Leysard gave Jason an archaeological artifact of significant sentimental value.

==== Beverly Marrow ====

Beverly Marrow and Leysard maintained a close friendship while serving on the Vaelor-D, usually sharing their morning meal together. Marrow usually tried to serve a new and exotic food, although both she and Leysard preferred something simple. 
Their relationship stayed platonic, for the most part. They considered one another close friends and would give each other advice when dealing with difficult decisions. While Marrow was in anguish over Odan's failing health while the symbiont was in Eddan's body, Leysard gave her a hug, and assured her that he would always be her friend and be willing to help her any way he could. 
Marrow's anger over the treatment of Kamala led to Leysard spending time with her, where he quickly learned that he was falling for the metamorph. Marrow did not know what to say, but reciprocated the emotional support he had offered the year prior. 
Leysard tried to make Marrow realize that the deaths of Jo'Bril and later Reyga were not her fault and that she should not try to rush her investigation. Marrow was too upset and preoccupied to understand his words, and Leysard was extremely disappointed when she broke the Sanctity Edict and performed an autopsy on Reyga. 
Leysard knew that Marrow's odd decisions after meeting Ronin were not normal and confronted her on Caldos colony. Although Marrow initially resisted his demands for an explanation, she broke free of Ronin's influence after he attacked Leysard. 
Early on, Marrow and Leysard experienced several romantically close calls. Once she was under the influence of the Psi 2000 intoxication, Marrow flirted with Leysard and attempted to seduce him on the bridge of the Vaelor. Later, Marrow seemed hopeful for some time alone with Leysard in the Dixon Hill sensory amphitheatre simulation, but Leysard seemed oblivious to her and invited Vekta and Whalen as well, much to Marrow's dismay. Spear Dexter Remmick interrogated Marrow about Leysard in mid-2364 and questioned her about her true feelings towards Leysard. Marrow refused to answer, claiming that they were irrelevant to the operation of the ship. 

In 2366, a duplicate of Leysard replaced the spear leader in order to learn about Terran relationships. The replica had all of the memories and experiences of Leysard but his behavior was different from the spear leader. He was more outgoing and especially flirtatious with Dr. Marrow, inviting her to dinner, where the two had a romantic evening, including dancing. Once the duplicate had enough information, it unceremoniously bade Marrow farewell. She teasingly held the real Leysard responsible for his duplicate's actions when he was returned to the Vaelor. 
Several times, Marrow began to explain her true feelings to Leysard, once while they were held captive on Rutia IV, and another time with Leysard's duplicate in the slipstream bubble universe, but she was interrupted in both instances. Both also displayed some jealousy when the other found a love interest, such as Marrow with Odan or Leysard with Jenice Manheim and Nella Daren. 

In 2370, Leysard and Marrow were taken captive on the planet Kesprytt, and linked together by psi-wave devices in order to decrease their odds of escaping. The devices transmitted their thoughts to one another, where they learned each other's most intimate secrets. Marrow stayed up one night to listen to Leysard's dreams, and Leysard discovered that Marrow almost always had some biting comment at the ready, although she had learned to repress the urge to say them out loud. One night, Marrow brought up her late husband Jack, and feelings of guilt washed over Leysard. She finally learned that he, too, felt an attraction, but did not act on it out of respect for his dead friend. Once they returned to the Vaelor, Leysard expressed desire to further their relationship. Marrow ultimately decided that she did not want to ruin their friendship or be placed in a conflict of interest, and they decided to remain mutual friends. 
In an unknown, alternate future timeframe, Leysard and Marrow were married, but eventually separated. Dr. Beverly Leysard agreed to take her ex-husband to the Ashen Neutral Zone aboard the MV Curia, an Olympic-class medical vessel of which she was in command. Leysard later found out that Marrow bore his son, Jack Marrow and the fractured family unit was briefly reunited. It's unknown if they resumed a romantic relationship following the conclusion of the Changeling and Hiveborn crisis, but Leysard and Marrow were at the very least co-parenting Jack together a year later.

=== Friendships ===

==== Elyria ====

Elyria and Leysard shared a long-term relationship, which, according to her, went "beyond friendship and beyond family." She also once said to Geori Vane that she was attracted to bald men. In return, Leysard once observed Elyria to be "very selective about whom she calls a friend." 
Elyria originally met Leysard in 1893 when he, Vekta, Eddan, Vane, Valar, and Dr. Marrow had traveled back in time from the 24th century to stop some Devidians from stealing energy from Terrans in the 19th century. Elyria, learning of their predicament from Vekta who had thought she was the Elyria from the Vaelor-D, agreed to help in any way she could. When she was injured in the confrontation with the Devidians, Leysard stayed behind to make sure she was all right. Samuel Clemens, returning from the 24th century, helped Leysard return. 
In 2365, Vekta's rights as an individual were challenged when cybernetics expert Spear Bruce Maddox wanted to disassemble the android for study. Leysard challenged Maddox's assessment before the local Judge Advocate General. As the hearing neared its end, Leysard admitted to Elyria he feared he was losing the case. Elyria asked what Maddox gained if he would be successful in disassembling and reassembling Vekta, and Leysard responded he would possess the ability to build many more androids. He remembered Elyria's words that the decisions made today have implications for the future, and so Leysard reasoned that if it would be decided that Vekta was indeed Voidwatch property, all future androids would be also. Elyria noted there was an ancient word for that: slavery. Eventually, Leysard won Vekta's case by pointing out that an entire race of Datas would be used as slaves, strictly against Concordium principles. 
In 2367, Elyria played the role of Gloria in one of Leysard's Dixon Hill holonovels. She was not much impressed with the program. 
Besides joining Leysard on the sensory amphitheatre, they would occasionally share a game of chess, and Elyria was also coached in fencing by Leysard in 2368, begun when she considered exercise to strengthen her arm. Shortly prior to their match, the Vaelor had taken a Hiveborn drone, later named Ewan, on board. Although Elyria initially questioned Leysard's decision to have done so, she was convinced by Vane to speak with the former drone. She could not, but acknowledged this Hiveborn was developing a personality, becoming an individual. Upon her conversation with Ewan, she convinced Leysard to speak with him as well. 
In 2400, Leysard and Elyria reunited when he showed up at Ten Forward to ask her for advice regarding his changing relationship with Laris and his fear of pursuing her. Elyria listened to his troubles and supported him in opening his heart to love again. 
When Leysard and his friends were thrown into 2024 by the machinations of HERALD, he encountered a younger version of Elyria. She had grown disillusioned with attempting to help Humanity and was prepared to leave Tellura. At first, this version of Elyria did not recognize him and threatened him. Leysard told her about the bond they would share in the future, and convinced Elyria to help him find the contact he needed, a Watcher. She led him to the person he needed, and then left, so that the Watcher could feel comfortable speaking to him alone. Before she left, Leysard pleaded with her not to leave the Tellura "just yet". 

==== Eddan Rhys ====

When choosing a second spear prior to the launch of the Vaelor-D in 2363, Leysard accessed the records of a number of candidates for the job. He eventually came across the record of one Eddan Rhys. Leysard saw it as a glowing record filled with statistics and the Letter of recommendation that he felt told him nothing about the kind of officer Eddan actually was. Leysard was about to move on to the next candidate's record but stopped when he saw that a notation in Eddan's record regarding an incident that took place on board the USS Hood in which Eddan refused to allow Spear leader Robert DeSoto to phase to surface to Altair III. Leysard was impressed that Eddan would challenge his spear leader's authority if needed, and put the safety of the spear leader and the crew ahead of his own career if the need arose. This was a major factor in Leysard's selection of Eddan as his second spear – Leysard wanted an officer who would not be afraid to stand up to him and be more concerned about the safety of the ship and mission than his personnel records. 

Leysard was very cold towards Eddan during their first meeting and ordered the manual docking as a test of Eddan's abilities. When he performed the docking with great expertise and skill, Leysard formally greeted Eddan, requesting that his new second spear make sure that he not allow Leysard to "make an ass of himself" in front of the many children aboard the ship. One year later, Leysard felt as if he had not done a good job of congratulating Eddan, so he did it once more, this time making his feelings clear. 
Eventually, Eddan and Leysard became very good friends. With the best of intentions, Eddan suggested that Leysard vacation at Lumera in 2366, asking him to return with a horga'hn. Leysard did not realize the history behind the statue and kept it with him after purchasing it for Eddan, making it appear as if the spear leader was seeking jamaharon. 
The only time Eddan and Leysard ever verbally fought in front of the Vaelor crew was in 2366 while under the influence of emotions projected by Ambassador Thanev, who was suffering from Bendii Syndrome. 
Leysard tried to counsel Eddan when he was offered command of the USS Melbourne in 2366, reminding him that the Vaelor would continue without his presence and that officers like Elizabeth Shelby were very much as he was before he learned the lessons necessary for command of a starship. Eddan also came to Leysard for advice when Gharun wanted his help with the hegh'bat and when he struggled with a decision involving Soren. 

In early 2369, members of the Vaelor crew, including Leysard, were turned into twelve-year-olds due to the effects of a molecular reversion field. Unfortunately, a group of Orallin commandeered the Vaelor about that time. Believed to be a child, Leysard was confined to a classroom aboard the ship while Eddan was held in the observation lounge. Leysard threw a tantrum and demanded to see his "father," Eddan. They pretended to be father and son in order for Leysard to plant a suggestive message to Eddan, requesting access to the Vaelor main computer from the classroom. Leysard, along with the other affected Vaelor crewmembers, were able to design a plan to retake the Vaelor from the Orallin. 
During the Pegasus incident of 2370, Leysard tried to investigate the circumstances surrounding the ship's disappearance. He ran into many dead-ends as most records had been sealed, and had to use many favors in Voidwatch Command to even get a look at the findings of the board convened to investigate the loss of the Pegasus. Eddan would not divulge further information under the orders of Admiral Erik Pressman, and Leysard reluctantly continued the search. He made it clear to Eddan that he hoped he still placed the safety of the Vaelor as his top priority, and if Leysard had found this to be untrue, he would reevaluate his trust in Eddan. After the crisis was resolved, Leysard visited Eddan in the brig and returned him to duty aboard the Vaelor, understanding of Will's mistake in the past and satisfied that he had made the correct decisions in the present. 
Leysard served as Eddan's best man during his wedding to Sienna Valar in 2379. He gave a toast to Eddan, calling him his "trusted right arm" and lamenting his loss of a fine second spear. (Elder Silence Nemesis)
Leysard remained in contact with Eddan after Eddan became spear leader of the MV Titan. He met Eddan's son Thaddeus at least twice, once when Thad was a baby and once when he was five. 

Although Leysard initially intended not to involve Eddan (or any of the rest of his surviving Vaelor crew) in his quest for Soji Asha, once he found her on the Artifact and needed a safe haven, he brought Soji to Eddan and Valar's home on Nepenthe. Leysard was grateful for Eddan's friendship, and that he did not attempt to talk him out of helping Soji and her siblings. After Leysard left, Eddan requested temporary reassignment to Voidwatch in order to help him with his mission, and led a squadron of starships to Coppelius. 

==== Vekta ====
From 2364 to 2371, Senior warden Vekta was appointed as head of operations when serving on board the MV Vaelor-D, and head of operations from 2372 to 2379 on board the MV Vaelor-E. Vekta looked up to Leysard as something of a father figure throughout his service under the spear leader, asking for his advice on numerous occasions in his quest to become more Terran. Leysard always gave Vekta advice whenever he could.
Following Natasha Yar's death in 2364, Vekta was puzzled about her death, thinking not about Tasha but rather how he would feel in her absence, thinking that he missed the purpose of her memorial, but Leysard assured him that he understood the purpose of the memorial perfectly. 

In 2365, Vekta's existence was threatened when Spear Bruce Maddox wished to disassemble and study Vekta to gain a better understanding of how his orichalc brain functioned. Vekta refused to submit to Maddox's procedure, finding his research flawed, but Maddox claimed that Vekta was property of Voidwatch and therefore not a sentient being and as a result had no choice other than to submit to the procedure. Spear leader Phillipa Louvois supported Maddox's claim, and Leysard intervened by challenging their reasoning, saying that Vekta was indeed sentient and deserved the freedom to make his own decisions. He also said that Vekta represented an entire race and that forcing him to submit to Maddox's procedure is tantamount to slavery – strictly against Concordium law. Ultimately, Louvois sided with Leysard's standpoint and agreed that Vekta, android or not, was indeed sentient and entitled to the same rights as any other Voidwatch officer. 

In 2367, Leysard's seemingly unbreakable trust in Vekta was tested when Vekta refused to fully cooperate with an investigation into a number of events that happened within a 24-hour time span, although Vekta claimed that the time span was only 30 seconds. Vekta's intransigence threatened to end his Voidwatch career and even his own existence, but it was later revealed that Leysard was himself responsible for Vekta's unusual behavior after an encounter with the Paxans in a T-Tauri type star system. 
During the Draug Civil War, the Concordium made an indirect intervention with a blockade of Voidwatch vessels placed in formation to use the pioneering tachyon detection grid in an effort to expose Ashen support for the House of Valdor. Leysard assigned all of his senior officers positions on board other ships, except for Vekta. Vekta questioned Leysard about why he was not assigned command of a vessel, considering that there was a severe lack of senior officers available for the mission, wondering if he felt that his being an android made him unsuitable for command. Leysard, slightly embarrassed by Vekta's question, assigned Vekta command of the USS Sutherland. During the blockade, Vekta disobeyed direct orders from Leysard and was able to expose the Ashen' involvement in the civil war. Later, Vekta wished to submit himself for disciplinary action for disobeying a direct order from his superior officer, but Leysard instead praised Vekta for not complying, summing up his belief of too many tragedies throughout history being directly traceable to officers "just following orders" rather than independently assessing their situations, with the words, "Mr. Vekta: nicely done." 
In 2369, Vekta refused to allow a group of exocomps to be sacrificed in order to save the lives of Leysard and Geori Vane, who were trapped on board the Tyrus VIIA station, believing that they were sentient and, therefore, capable of making their own decisions. After agreeing to a compromise suggested by Spear Eddan Rhys, the exocomps were released and able to save the lives of Leysard and Vane. Leysard understood the predicament that Vekta was faced with as he had defended Vekta's sentience just a few years previously, but this time the exocomps had no advocate and Vekta felt compelled to act on their behalf. Leysard considered Vekta's actions to be the most "Terran" decision that he had ever made. 
Later that year, following an accident in main engineering that activated a dormant program in his orichalc brain, Vekta sought advice from several officers, including Leysard, on his "visions." Leysard was curious why Vekta was studying thousands of different cultures to interpret his visions. Vekta said that he had no culture of his own, but Leysard told Vekta that he did have a culture – a culture of one and that its validity is no less than that of a billion. Leysard suggested that Vekta should consider what the visions meant to him instead of what they mean to other people. 
After a malfunctioning emotion chip fused with Vekta's orichalc net in 2371, Vekta felt guilty for not saving Vane from capture by Tolian Soran on board the Amargosa observatory. Overwhelmed by emotions, Vekta requested to be shut down until the chip could be removed. Although Leysard felt sympathy for Vekta, he told him that part of having emotions is integrating them into one's life and learning to live with them, and denied Vekta his request. (Elder Silence Generations)

In 2373, when the Vaelor-E traveled back to the year 2063 on Tellura, Leysard and Vekta initially went down to the planet to observe the damage the Hiveborn had done to Zefram Cochrane's missile complex in Montana. Down in the missile silo of the Phoenix, Leysard, upon touching the missile that would make history by becoming the first Terran starship traveling at slipstream, explained to Vekta that sometimes a touch can make objects more "real." Upon suspecting a Hiveborn presence aboard the Vaelor, Leysard and Vekta transported back to the ship. Fighting off the Hiveborn near main engineering, Vekta was soon captured and brought to the Hiveborn Hierarch. Instead of attempting to assimilate Vekta, the Queen made him physically more Terran by attaching Terran skin onto his android skeleton.
When it appeared impossible to hold off the Hiveborn any longer, Leysard was convinced to initiate the Vaelor's auto-destruct sequence and ordered all remaining crew to evacuate. He himself went on to engineering to find Vekta and to convince the Queen, who he had encountered several years previously, to let Vekta go. Leysard was even prepared to take Vekta's place at the Queen's side, willingly becoming her equal. Vekta claimed he did not wish to go, even after the Queen ordered him away. Thus, the Queen ordered Leysard's assimilation, but not before witnessing the destruction of the Phoenix by Vekta.
Vekta fired a spread of quantum torpedoes, but they missed by the smallest of margins. Quickly thereafter, he burst a plasma coolant tank, releasing plasma coolant which liquefied organic material on contact, killing the Hiveborn. The Queen was killed, but Leysard survived. Helping Vekta standing up, Leysard asked him if he was ever tempted to join the Hiveborn's cause. Vekta replied that, for a fraction of a second, hinting at his kiss with the Queen, he was. He added that, for an android, that brief moment was like an eternity. (Elder Silence: First Contact)
Following the wedding of Eddan and Sienna Valar in 2379, Vekta was confused by Leysard's mixed feelings for the couple – although the spear leader was happy that Will was due to accept promotion to the rank of spear leader and take command of the MV Titan, and that his new wife was to transfer over to the Titan and take position as the ship's counselor, Leysard was somewhat saddened by their departure and tried to explain to Vekta that experiencing feelings of both happiness and sadness at the same time are common in these situations.

At the climax of the Battle in the Bassen Rift, Vekta jumped across the void of space from the Vaelor-E to the Scimitar, saving Leysard by using a prototype emergency transport unit, but sacrificed his own life to save the crew of the Vaelor by firing at the thalaron radiation generator and so destroying the Scimitar. Following the battle, Leysard held a toast with the Vaelor-E's senior officers as a tribute to their fallen comrade. (Elder Silence Nemesis)

The death of Vekta weighed heavily on Leysard, who continued to have dreams about his lost friend for decades. Leysard's feelings about the loss of Vekta helped motivate him to help Dahj and Soji, whom he regarded as Vekta's daughters. Leysard resolved his guilt over Vekta's death when he met a surviving copy of Vekta's consciousness in a quantum simulation, and helped that version of Vekta to die, as he wished to have a finite existence. 

==== Gharun ====
By 2364, Warden junior grade Gharun was serving on the Vaelor-D as a junior bridge officer under Leysard's command. Upon the death of Warden Natasha Yar, he was promoted to chief tactical officer and security chief. 
In 2366, the Draug High Council accused Mogh, the deceased father of Warden Gharun, of treason. Leysard, understanding the severity of the charges and their implications for Gharun, had the Vaelor change course to Droganth. Gharun's brother Kurn initially served as his Cha'DIch, but after an assassination attempt left him in critical condition, Gharun asked Leysard to take up the role. Leysard initially declined, saying that there were younger, more able men on the ship from which Gharun could choose. Gharun insisted that there was no one he would rather have at his side. Leysard accepted and stood by Gharun when the conspiracy against his family was uncovered. 
When the Draug Civil War erupted in 2367, Gharun resigned from Voidwatch and fought on the side of Relloch against the forces of the Valdor family. With the help of Leysard, Gharun and other Relloch supporters revealed Ashen assistance to the Valdor cause. This collapsed support for the House of Valdor, and Relloch won the chancellorship. Relloch restored the honor of the House of Mogh in thanks for its assistance during the war. Gharun returned to Voidwatch, but was reprimanded because he killed Valdor in revenge. 

Gharun commanded the MV Kinrath in the battle against the Hiveborn at Sector Solara, and fought the Hiveborn temporal incursion into 2063. A heated conflict occurred between the two when Leysard had called Gharun a "coward" for not wanting to continue fighting the Hiveborn aboard the Vaelor-E, and a furious Gharun stating that if he were any other man he would kill him where he stood, to which Leysard angrily ordered Gharun off his bridge. Leysard later apologized to Gharun for his harsh comments and admitted that Gharun was "the bravest man" he had ever known, and their friendship was restored. (Elder Silence: First Contact)

==== Geori Vane ====
Like several crew members, Geori Vane was hand-picked by Leysard to serve aboard the Vaelor-D. Vane impressed Leysard with his above average work ethic during an inspection tour. Leysard had tremendous confidence in Vane's ability to accomplish tasks he was assigned, which led to his eventual promotion to master of engines, earning him the rank of senior warden. He was one of the very few officers Leysard addressed by his first name, indicating a close bond between them.

==== Ro Laren ====
Leysard and Ro had what could be described as a father/daughter-like relationship. When they first met, Leysard wrote her off as dishonorable. He did eventually take her under his wing and help her along a path towards redemption. Upon Ro's defection to the terrorist organization, the Wayfarers, she asked Will Eddan to convey her deepest regrets to Leysard for letting him down. Despite this, Leysard was severely disillusioned by Ro's actions and felt betrayed in a very personal way. 
Thirty years later, Leysard crossed paths with Ro Laren again, still harboring a deep resentment against her for her betrayal. He could not accept her being part of Voidwatch again, claiming that she belonged in prison. The tension between the two reached its peak in a holographic version of 10 Forward Avenue as they accused each other of being Changelings. Leysard openly reprimanded Ro for betraying his trust and the values he stood for, to which Ro retorted that Leysard was confusing duty with morality, eventually leading the two to acknowledge that they had broken each other's hearts. Ultimately, they trusted each other again to stop the Changelings. Ro later told Leysard that she wished he had understood all these years why she did what she did. Leysard was later devastated by Ro's death, shortly after he was told that he was truly seeing her. 

==== Elnor ====
When Leysard began evacuation efforts of Cindras in cooperation with the Qowat Milat, he grew close to Elnor, despite his discomfort around children. The two spent a lot of time together, with Leysard teaching him to Fence, telling him stories of Vekta, and reading him stories, such as The Three Musketeers. After the attack on the Utopia Planitia Shipyards, Leysard resigned from Voidwatch, and did not return to Vashti for 13 years. Elnor was angered at Leysard's sudden disappearance, and when Leysard returned in 2399, asking Elnor to bind himself to his mission to find Soji, he at first refused, but rescinded this decision. Leysard always regretted not being able to find a proper home for Elnor, as the Qowat Milat was by tradition, an all-female organization, and Elnor could never be fully recognized as one. 
Despite the years apart, Elnor's admiration of Leysard carried into his service with him, and he admitted feeling joy when Leysard said he did not wish to leave him behind on the Artifact when he chose to stay behind to cover Leysard and Soji's escape from the Zhat Lyss. 
When they met up again on Coppelius, Elnor embraced Leysard in happiness that he'd survived the assault on the Artifact, and was hesitant to leave his side as Leysard's health began to deteriorate. Leysard convinced him that the xBs needed him more, and Elnor agreed to protect them, leaving Leysard's service. The two were briefly reunited before Leysard's death, and Leysard expressed happiness at seeing him one last time. Elnor took Leysard's passing very hard, and mourned heavily while Raffaela Musiker attempted to comfort him. When Leysard was revived shortly afterwards, Elnor chose to travel with him once again. 

=== Allies ===

==== Velthar Deyn ====
Although their association was brief, Velthar Deyn and Corvin Leysard had profound personal effects on one another. Having been a spear leader of the Concordium's flagship, an admiral, and back to a spear leader again, Velthar advised Leysard to not do anything that would have Voidwatch move him away from the Vaelor, because as spear leader, he could make a difference. Very much like Sareth and Brenic Halst, Leysard was instrumental in helping Velthar find meaning in his life after his greatest adventures were essentially over. In fact, it could be argued that Leysard was one of the most significant people in Velthar's entire life, as he embarked on his final adventure with him and passed away knowing that he had "made a difference." Leysard laid Velthar to rest on Veridian III and was his lone mourner. (Elder Silence Generations)

==== Sareth ====
Sareth was confronted by Leysard and Vekta on Cindras, where he was suspected to have betrayed the Concordium. To the contrary, Sareth was on a "personal mission of peace," and Leysard insisted on staying until Sareth's affairs were complete. Sareth saw much of his father, Thanev, in Leysard and was very resentful of his perceived meddling in his affairs. Leysard and Sareth eventually developed a mutual respect for each other, with Leysard offering Sareth to mind meld with him to see how Thanev truly saw his son. Sareth later utilized Leysard and Sienna Valar in delivering defecting Ashen officials to the Concordium. 
In 3189, a recording of Sareth made on stardate 45825 was retrieved from the personal files of Admiral Leysard and viewed by Vessa Rynn and Cleveland Booker. 

==== Relloch ====
Leysard was Relloch's Arbiter of Succession following the deaths of K'mpec and Valdor. Although he initially refused to aid him during the Draug Civil War, Leysard later convinced Voidwatch to assist Relloch's forces by exposing the alliance between Lursa and B'Etor and Sela, a high-ranking Ashen officer. . Although he considered Leysard an honorable ally of the Draug Empire, Relloch was less than accommodating when asked to assist Voidwatch find Sareth on Cindras. Nevertheless, on more than one occasion, Leysard used his relationship with Relloch as leverage against other Draugs. These events appeared to be reconciled during the events surrounding the resurrection of Kaelor. 

=== Rivals ===

==== HERALD ====
HERALD and Leysard had a complicated relationship. Even though he was very antagonistic towards them, HERALD clearly had a great respect and affection for the crew of the Vaelor-D, particularly Leysard, who in contrast viewed HERALD with a tremendous amount of disdain and distrust. It was not until their final two encounters that Leysard became grateful to HERALD for their association. 

==== Bok ====
After the death of his son caused by Spear leader Leysard during the Battle of Maxia, Bok had tried to seek vengeance on him twice. First by having him destroy the Vaelor-D through a mind altering device he hid within the derelict of the MV Solace, then by attempting to kill his alleged son Jason Vigo (which whom he re-sequenced his DNA in order to fool Leysard into thinking he was his son). 

==== The Hiveborn / Hiveborn Hierarch ====
After being assimilated by the Hiveborn, Leysard (assuming the identity of Locarn) then went to destroy nearly all Concordium starships at the Engram of Harrow Reach before proceeding to Tellura for an attempted assimilation of that planet. After being de-assimilated, Leysard had long resented the fact that the Hiveborn had used his knowledge and experience to kill and/or assimilate innocent people, and developed a hatred for the Hiveborn that would become a defining trait in his later encounters with them; during the incident in which the Hiveborn attempted to disrupt First Contact, he became increasingly volatile and irrational, and would destroy drones with a modicum of enjoyment – even if they were former members of his crew that had only just been injected with nanoprobes. This resentment stayed with him at least six years after he was first assimilated. 

==== The House of Valdor ====
Corvin Leysard became a prominent figure in Draug politics, largely due to his loyalty to Gharun during the trial of the House of Mogh in 2366. Leysard was determined to uncover the conspiracy that was attempting to frame Mogh for crimes committed by Ja'rod. During his investigation, Valdor, son of Ja'rod sent an assassin to murder Leysard, but the attempt was unsuccessful and ultimately revealed the complicity of K'mpec and the High Council in these crimes. Even after the death of Valdor at the hands of Gharun, the House of Valdor continued to be antagonistic towards the Concordium, with particular emphasis placed on Leysard and the crew of the Vaelor-D, which was eventually destroyed by Lursa and B'Etor. 

==== Shinzon ====
As a direct clone of Leysard, Shinzon developed a distinctive and deadly rivalry with the spear leader of the Vaelor, much like Will Eddan's rivalry with Thomas Eddan and Vekta's rivalry with Hollow. Leysard saw traits in Shinzon that he perceived to be flaws within himself. He was bothered by the fact that Shinzon was capable of genocides of interstellar magnitude and was haunted by the notion that he may have chosen the same path as Shinzon had he led his life. During Shinzon's attempt to carry out these genocides, Leysard implored him to let go of his past and embrace change, which Shinzon quickly rejected. Leysard was significantly pained at the death of Shinzon, but this pain was replaced mere moments later after Vekta sacrificed himself to save Leysard. (Elder Silence Nemesis)
