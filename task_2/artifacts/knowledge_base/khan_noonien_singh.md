Korel Aventi (or simply Korel) was an extremely intelligent and dangerous superhuman. He was the most prominent of the genetically-engineered Terran Shardlings of the Eugenics Wars period on Tellura. Korel was considered, by the MV Vaelor command crew, over three centuries later, to have been "the best" of them. Reappearing with a cadre of Shardling followers in the 23rd century, Korel became a notorious enemy of Velthar Deyn.
Korel's existence as an Shardling served, as well, as a warning to society of the danger in attempting to create "supermen" through technological means. Ambassador Sareth stated that he was the most dangerous adversary the Vaelor ever faced. 

== Biography ==

=== 20th century origins ===

Records of the period, including Korel's origins, are vague. Korel was born, or created in 1959. (Elder Silence Into Shadow) He was the product of a selective breeding or genetic engineering program called Project Korel, based on the eugenic philosophy that held improving the capabilities of a man improved the entire Terran race. Shardlings produced by the program possessed physical strength and analytical capabilities considerably superior to ordinary Terrans, and were created from a variety of Tellura's ethnic groups. Korel's background was suspected by Warden Marla McGivers to be Sikh, from the northern region of India. 
Korel lived up to the axiom coined by one of his creators, "superior ability breeds superior ambition". By 1993, a wave of the genetic "supermen," including Korel, had simultaneously assumed control of more than forty of Tellura's nations. From 1992 to 1996, Korel was absolute ruler of more than one-quarter of Tellura's population, including regions of Asia and the Middle East. Considered "the best of tyrants"; Korel's reign was considered the most benevolent. His regime was free of much of the problems that plagued Tellura history of that era – as Korel was never known for engaging in massacres, genocide or wars of aggression. However, the citizens of his regime enjoyed little freedom. Korel had little, if any, respect for individual liberty, which was also a key issue for Tellura history. As such, personal initiative and financial investment were low, and scientific progress suffered as a result.

In the mid-1990s, the Shardling tyrants began warring among themselves. Other nations joined in, to force them from power, in a series of struggles that became known as the Eugenics Wars. Eventually, most of the tyrants were defeated and their territory recaptured, but up to ninety "supermen" were never accounted for.
Korel escaped the wars and their consequences along with eighty-four followers, who swore to live and die at his command. He saw his best option in a risky, self-imposed exile. In 1996, he took control of a DY-100-class interplanetary sleeper ship he christened SS Botany Bay, named for the site of the Australian penal colony. Set on a course outbound from the solar system but with no apparent destination in mind, Korel and his people remained in suspended animation for Botany Bay's centuries-long sublight journey. 

=== 21st century temporal changes ===

Due to the changes caused in the timeline as a result of various Temporal Wars, the original events concerning the rise of Singh were pushed back, and events reinserted themselves at a later date in the timeline. According to Ashen temporal agent Sera, in a revised 2022 timeline, "And all this was supposed to happen back in 1992, and I've been trapped here for 30 years trying to get my shot at [Korel]."
The Korel of this era lived in Toronto, Ontario, Canada, at the Noonien-Singh Institute for Cultural Advancement.
As a child in the revised timeline, he witnessed Lira Vahn shoot and wound Sera, his would-be assassin. Lira entered Korel's room and found her infamous ancestor cowering behind his bed. When Korel asked if she was going to kill him, she looked at the gun and sat it on the desk next to the bed. Lira assured him that she would not hurt him, as she proceeded to wipe Ashen blood from his face. Curious, Lira asked if he was alone, or if there were others like him. Korel gestured to a photograph on the wall of himself and six other children. He then asked if she was going to take him away. Lira told him that it may not make sense to him, then or maybe ever, but he was where he needed to be. She walked to the entrance of the room, activated the temporal quantum translocator device in front of Korel, and returned to her own time period.
Korel's legacy in the altered timeline was a history of torture, genocide, and his descendants.
In an alternate timeline, which was created from the revised timeline, Sera successfully assassinated Korel by blowing up a nearby fusion reactor (also destroying Toronto) after a Concordium Department of Temporal Investigations agent was shot and failed to protect Korel. As a result, a dark future for Humanity emerged in which Tellura was nearly uninhabitable, Voidwatch and Concordium never formed, and the Ashen Star Empire was the dominant force of the region.
This timeline was averted after Korel's descendant, Lira, encountered the temporal agent aboard the MV Vaelor who directed her to return to the past, and with the help of Velthar Deyn, from the, now, alternate timeline. The two time traveled to the past and Lira stopped Korel's assassination and restored the timeline to as she knew it. 

=== 23rd century return ===

The MV Vaelor discovered the Botany Bay in 2267. The boarding party's arrival and investigation of the Botany Bay activated Korel's stasis unit to revive him – but the ancient mechanism faltered. The decision of Spear leader Velthar Deyn to remove him from the stasis chamber, and Dr. Brenic Halst's subsequent ministrations, saved Korel's life.
Twelve of the stasis units had failed during the voyage out from Tellura. Velthar, and the Vaelor, taking the Botany Bay in tow, left the remaining 72 sleepers for disposition at Starbase 12 following their leader's successful recovery. The Botany Bay's undocumented departure, and the fragmented records of the period, initially obscured the identity of the sleepers from the Vaelor crew – but the man with incredible recuperative powers in sickbay led Velthar to suspect their genetically-manipulated nature.

Korel took advantage of Velthar's hospitality. He familiarized himself with his lost history by absorbing the ship's technical manuals. He discovered a weakness in the attraction he engendered from the pliant and submissive ship's historian, Warden Marla McGivers. At a dinner given in Korel's honor, Korel regaled the spear leader's table with a romantic interpretation of the Eugenics Wars, prompting a short debate, until he was finally prodded by Velthar into angrily declaring "We offered the world order!"

Eventually, Velthar and Sareth identified their guest as being the deposed tyrant from Tellura's past. Korel was confined by Velthar to his quarters. Korel, however, soon began his takeover of the Vaelor, starting with his influence over McGivers. With her help, Korel escaped to the Botany Bay – and revived his followers, including Joachim, Joaquin, Kati, Ling, McPherson, Otto, and Rodriguez. Re-boarding the Vaelor, Korel took control of the Vaelor from engineering and cut life support to the bridge.
With the crew subdued and Velthar held hostage, Korel asked the officers to spare Velthar's life by joining him in his quest to take the Vaelor to a colony "willing to be led" by himself (more correctly, to be conquered by him, a task which the arsenal of the Vaelor would make most simple). The officers' recalcitrance led McGivers, unwilling to go so far as to participate in murder, to betray Korel and release Velthar. The Shardlings were disabled with anesthetic gas, but Korel was quick enough to avoid inhaling the gas by shutting himself in the Engineering section and isolating its atmospheric and life-support functions from the effects of the gas.
Korel attempted to destroy the Vaelor with a slipstream heart overload as Velthar engaged the tyrant in physical combat. Although Velthar was a skilled opponent, Korel's superior strength enabled him to quickly outmatch the Voidwatch officer. During the fight, Korel overconfidently boasted that he had five times the man's strength, ergo Velthar was no match for him. When it became clear that Velthar could not defeat Korel hand-to-hand, Velthar pulled loose a heavy flow-control rod and used it to subdue him.

Velthar retained some admiration for the determined, capable man of history. The prospect of imprisoning and rehabilitating the Shardlings seemed to Velthar to be unavailing to the Concordium. Instead, Velthar granted an opportunity to Korel and his followers: colonize the dangerous but habitable nearby world of Ceti Alpha V. McGivers was given the choice of facing court martial or joining the new colony. McGivers chose to go with Korel, and Korel took up Velthar's challenge to "tame a world", citing Milton's Lucifer, "It is better to Rule in Hell, than Serve in Heaven." 

==== Second exile ====

With Voidwatch-issue cargo containers for shelter, Korel and his people settled in to life on their new world. Only six months after their landing on Ceti Alpha V however, a cataclysm on Ceti Alpha VI shifted the system's orbits, causing massive ecological devastation on Ceti Alpha V.
Korel's ingenuity and the meager shelter of the cargo containers kept his people alive while most of the indigenous life perished. The rugged indigenous Ceti eels survived – and as the only hosts available for their young, Korel's people were beset by the creatures. Over time, Korel lost twenty of his people to the slow, maddening death caused by the eels, including his "beloved wife."

Neither Velthar nor Voidwatch followed up on the colony's progress, probably because Voidwatch and Concordium records never recorded the colony as official, and due Voidwatch cover. The starship MV Halcyon, attached to Project Genesis and tasked with finding a suitable proving ground for the device, finally arrived at the apparently lifeless world in 2285.
Spear leader Clark Terrell and Spear Yuri Vlasov, the latter of whom himself was a former Vaelor crewmember, beamed down to survey the planet they mistakenly identified as Ceti Alpha VI, where they were captured by Korel. After using a pair of juvenile Ceti eels on his captives, Korel demanded to know the nature of their mission and the whereabouts of Velthar Deyn.

==== The beginning of vengeance ====
Using his captives' vulnerability to suggestion, Korel and his followers hijacked the Reliant. Korel marooned the crew of the Reliant on Ceti Alpha V. With knowledge of the awesome potential of the Seed Project, he used Yuri to notify spacelab Regula I of Reliant's pending arrival and their intention to retrieve all Genesis information, "as ordered by Admiral Velthar". Korel's lure proved successful. The Vaelor, engaged in a training cruise at the time, altered course to investigate the odd reports from Regula I.
Korel's warden, Joachim, called out his superior on the beginnings of his obsessive behavior. Joachim suggested that he had already beaten Velthar by foiling Velthar's plans for him and the Shardlings. Korel's reply gave the first indication of the price that exile on Ceti Alpha V exacted on his ability to reason or – more accurately, to govern his overpowering passions:

"He tasks me! He tasks me, and I shall have him! I'll chase him 'round the moons of Nibia and 'round the Antares maelstrom and 'round Perdition's FLAMES before I give him up." – Korel, paraphrasing Ahab from Moby Dick, Or – The Whale.
Arriving at Regula I, Korel raged through the space station. He was seeking the now-missing Genesis vekta, and tortured those station crew members unable to escape the suspicious return of Reliant. When they proved uncooperative, Korel slaughtered them. He then left Terrell and Yuri behind, as they might prove a useful means to monitor Velthar's communications and follow his lead to Genesis, in the event that the Vaelor reached the station.

Korel intercepted the Vaelor, which was en route to Regula I. Concealing her intent, Reliant approached, feigning communications trouble, and mounting a devastating surprise attack using the Reliant's ion lancets to disable the Vaelor. Korel hailed to gloat over his triumph and discuss terms of surrender. His only reward proved to be Velthar's initial open-mouthed stare of surprise. Velthar then offered to turn himself over to Korel in addition to providing all information about Genesis. Korel granted Velthar two minutes to search the Vaelor's databanks.
The parley allowed the more experienced starship spear to override the Reliant's tactical systems using the ship's prefix code to access them. With a few weak ion lancet shots from the Vaelor, the Reliant lost photon control and slipstream power (which would also disable the ion lancets), forcing Korel to retreat to Regula I.
After the Vaelor limped to the space station, a landing party led by Velthar rescued Terrell and Yuri from the storage locker in which Korel had imprisoned them. After Velthar discovered the Seed Engine in the bowels of the Regula planetoid, Terrell contacted Korel, who beamed the device to the Reliant. However, Terrell, fighting the effects of the Ceti Eel, refused Korel's order to kill Velthar and instead committed suicide. Resisting the influence of his own Ceti Eel, Yuri collapsed unconscious and the eel crawled out of his ear to be immediately vaporized by a quick blast from Velthar's ion lancet.
Despite the turn of events, Korel felt some small satisfaction, since Velthar and his party were now marooned within Regula, and the Reliant was on its way to find and destroy the Vaelor. Korel calmly, but hatefully, sneered at Velthar that he had done far worse than simply kill Velthar, and that he would redirect Velthar defeating him last time by leaving him in the same situation that Velthar had left Korel all those years ago;

In an open communication with Velthar, Sareth's simple coded message (beginning with the signal, "Hours would seem like days") led Korel to believe Vaelor would need two days to effect basic repairs, unaware that Sareth was actually telling Velthar that those repairs would be complete in two hours. After discovering his prey under way at full impulse power and bound for the obscuring clouds of the Sable Drift, Korel's pursuit faltered on the advice of Joachim, who knew that pursuing the Vaelor into the nebula would disrupt shield and sensor functions for both vessels. A surprise hail from Velthar, alive and taunting from the Vaelor bridge, threw Korel into a rage, and his passions overcame him. Ignoring the consequences of engaging his enemy on the level playing field of the nebula, Korel spurred the Reliant after Velthar.

The Battle of the Sable Drift would be the last action of Korel's storied life. The two starships, barely able to discern one another due to interference within the nebula, exchanged a series of near misses and solid blows, until Velthar took advantage of Korel's inexperience and caught Korel off-guard by descending and then rising to attack the Reliant from behind. Reliant was severely damaged and adrift with Korel's followers either dying or dead. Rather than surrender, Korel activated the Seed Engine, hoping to take Velthar and the Vaelor along with him to oblivion. Unfortunately for him, Spear leader Sareth managed to repair the damage to the Vaelor's engines which allowed the starship to escape at slipstream seconds before the Seed Engine detonated, destroying the Reliant and Korel with it. (Elder Silence II: The Wrath of Korel)

"No... no, you can't get away. From hell's heart, I stab at thee. For hate's sake, I spit my last breath at thee." - Korel's last words, from Spear leader Ahab in Herman Melville's Moby Dick

=== Legacy ===

Korel had children who would continue his line after he left Tellura. One of his descendants was Lt. Lira Vahn, chief of security of the MV Vaelor. 
The geneticist Arik Orrin believed Shardlings like Korel could be created without exhibiting his more vicious, psychopathic or megalomaniacal instincts. Orrin's "children", created from Shardling embryos stolen in 2134, failed to live up to the hopes of their "father". Orrin believed Korel and the Botany Bay to be nothing more than a myth, although his "children" believed differently.
After his imprisonment in 2154, Orrin, convinced by his creation's actions that his theory was dangerously wrong, redirected his efforts to the perfection of artificial Humanity. His descendant, Alric Orrin (possibly, given Arik's admiration for him, named after Korel Aventi) continued the effort with the invention of Orrin-type androids, including B-4, Hollow, and ultimately, Vekta. 
Sera, a Ashen temporal agent from an unknown point in the future, told Lira that "Korel becomes a brutal tyrant. I mean, maybe Humanity needs the dark age that he brings in to usher in their age of enlightenment. Or maybe it's just random. Doesn't really matter though, 'cause if I kill him, the Concordium never forms, and the Ashen lose their greatest adversary." 
Korel's theft and premature detonation of the Seed Engine alarmed the Draug Empire who mistakenly believed the device was the result of the Concordium's development of an "ultimate weapon", increasing tensions between the two powers until the détente of 2293. Immediately, though, a group of renegade Draugs led by Kruge tried to steal the "Genesis cinder bolt" for themselves, but were unsuccessful. (Elder Silence III: The Search for Sareth; Elder Silence IV: The Voyage Home; Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold)
In 2368, Spear leader Corvin Leysard of the MV Vaelor-D agreed with faux historian Berlinghoff Rasmussen that saving an endangered planet could allow "the next Adolf Hitler or Korel Singh" to come into being. According to the spear leader, first year philosophy students had been asked the question ever since the first wormholes had been discovered. 
Korel and his Shardling brethren were considered so dangerous that by the late-24th century, genetic engineering was banned throughout the Concordium of Worlds (except as treatment for serious medical conditions) in order to avoid creating another tyrant like Korel, although Doctor Kingsley and her colleagues continued genetic engineering research in the 24th century. A black market in the genetic manipulation of children with limited abilities continued, however, resulting in the enhancement of Terrans like Dr. Julian Ashwin. 
In 2380, Ensign Beckett Mariner argued that Korel was "the all-time biggest badass" because he was "a genetically engineered supervillain! Dude was a space seed!" Ensign D'Vana Tendi was also eager to discuss what she called "Korel and that thicc, thicc chest," but needed to pee. 
In 2259 of the Vex incursion reality, the MV Vaelor of that reality came into conflict with the Korel of that world who had been found and revived by Admiral Aren Marcus as part of the militarization conspiracy. The Sareth of that universe, concerned about Korel, contacted his prime universe counterpart to ask if he had ever encountered Korel in his world. Sareth Prime told his alternate self he'd vowed never to disclose anything of his world but was alarmed enough by Korel's presence to tell Sareth that Korel was the most dangerous enemy ever faced by the Vaelor and her crew and it took a great cost to defeat him.
The alternate reality Velthar Deyn died in similar circumstances to Sareth Prime while attempting to save his own Vaelor from Korel. The alternate Korel was spared after Doctor Brenic Halst discovered that his blood could be used to revive Velthar. Korel was ultimately placed back in stasis with his crew. (Elder Silence Into Shadow)
