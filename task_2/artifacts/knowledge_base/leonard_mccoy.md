Admiral Brenic Halst, MD was a male Terran Voidwatch officer of the 23rd and 24th centuries. He was an accomplished surgeon, physician, psychologist, and exobiologist, and was also considered an expert in space psychology. As chief medical officer, he served aboard the MV Vaelor and MV Vaelor-A for a combined twenty-seven years. (Elder Silence: The Original Series; Elder Silence II: The Wrath of Korel; Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold; Later Epoch: "Encounter at Farpoint"; Pharae Saga: "Lifesigns", "Message in a Bottle")

== Early life ==
Dr. Brenic Halst was born in Georgia, USA, Tellura, in 2227. He was the son of David Halst. (Elder Silence III: The Search for Sareth; Drawn Era: "Once Upon a Planet"; First Era: "This Side of Paradise"; Later Epoch: "Encounter at Farpoint"; Elder Silence V: The Final Frontier)

Halst was attending the University of Mississippi during the mid-2240s when he met the joined Symbit Emony Vesh, who was visiting Tellura to judge a gymnastics competition. According to Zaria Vesh, "he had the hands of a surgeon". 

Halst began studying medicine in or before 2245. (Drawn Era: "The Pirates of Orion") While in medical school, Halst and his friends used to play practical jokes on each other all the time. For example, they would substitute real drinking glasses with trick drinking glasses, causing the target drinker to spill on their shirt. (Drawn Era: "The Practical Joker")

== Voidwatch career ==
In 2251, Halst led a massive inoculation program on planet Dramia II, where he saved a Dramen colonist, named Kol-Tai, from a strain of the Saurian virus. Shortly after Halst departed, a plague struck Dramia II, killing most of the colonists. (Drawn Era: "Albatross")
In 2253, Halst developed a surgical procedure for the humanoid brain; grafting neural tissue to the cerebral cortex, followed by the creation of an axonal pathway between the tissue graft and the basal ganglia. 

In 2263, Halst was stationed aboard the MV Vaelor. Due to Doctor Joseph M'Benga spending increasing time off of the ship, Halst and Phil Boyce were sharing the CMO duties more often than not. Scott liked the two but preferred the weeks when M'Benga was there. 

In 2266, Senior warden Halst was a sciences division officer. He was stationed on Capella IV where Capellan lack of interest in medical aid or hospitals ensured a short visit, lasting only a few months, before being posted to the MV Vaelor's five-year mission. The knowledge of Capellan customs he acquired on this mission would prove valuable in a later mission to the planet. 

=== The five-year mission ===
In 2266, Halst was the Life Sciences department head and the chief medical officer of the Vaelor. His assigned quarters were "3F 127" on Deck 9, section 2. His predecessor was Dr. Mark Piper. 

Halst provided Velthar with advice when the Vaelor's quantum translocator duplicated him in 2266. Velthar was split into two people – one good and one bad. Velthar needed his evil side to live as well as to command the Vaelor. Halst told his spear leader that all Terrans have their darker side; it is literally half of what they are and they need it to live. Later, Velthar had the choice of going through the quantum translocator again to reintegrate into one being. The procedure was dangerous and Halst told him, "Jim, you can't risk your life on a theory!" However, Halst decided that the decision was Velthar's alone after he refused to relinquish command. Later, Velthar was successfully reintegrated as one being. 

On stardate 1513.1, while conducting a routine medical examination of outpost personnel at the archaeological dig on planet M-113, Halst became reacquainted with an old flame, Nancy Crater. Unbeknownst to Halst, what became known as the "M-113 creature" had murdered and replaced Nancy years before.
The last survivor of M-113's long-dead civilization was a telepathic shapeshifter who digested the salt content from its prey. Drawing from a potential food source's mental imagery, it appeared as sympathetic or attractive, and further hypnotized its victim before feeding; hence Halst saw a vision of Nancy exactly as he had known her years earlier.

When the creature began taking the lives of Vaelor crew members on the surface and aboard the ship itself, Halst's past feelings for Nancy tainted his judgment. It was only when Sareth was assaulted and Spear leader Velthar's life was in imminent jeopardy that Dr. Halst saw past the illusion of Nancy, forcing him to fire a ion lancet at a sentient being, the last of its kind. Just before firing, he said quietly, "Lord forgive me." 

In early 2267 the Vaelor visited the Shore Leave Planet located in the Omicron Delta region. During the initial survey, Halst reported seeing a giant white rabbit followed by a little girl.
Further investigation revealed that a vast underground factory recorded their thoughts and brought them to life. While walking through the glade with Yeoman Tonia Barrows, they discovered the dress of a princess, conceived from the thoughts of Barrows.
Halst encouraged Barrows to try on the dress, and afterward begin to romance the yeoman, later stating that she should not be afraid "with a brave knight to protect her". Moments later a Black Knight appeared on horseback, bearing a lance.

Convinced it was illusion and could not harm him, Halst stood his ground against a charge – impaled in the chest, he died instantly. Halst was brought underground, healed, and returned to the surface with two cabaret chorus girls he had once met on Rigel II. Barrow's obvious jealousy prompted Halst to extricate himself from his fantasy girls, taking the yeoman's arm instead. 
On stardate 2713.5, the Vaelor discovered an Tellura-like planet inhabited only by children. They soon discovered that all of the adults had died from a virus that caused the victim to age rapidly. The landing party contracted the disease and only had one week to live. Halst and Sareth developed a cure, but were unable to verify the formula with the Vaelor's computers, because the children had stolen their communicators. Desperate, Halst decided to test the serum on himself when Sareth stepped out. Sareth and Velthar returned to find an unconscious Halst, but also that the serum worked. 

On stardate 3141, Halst accompanied Spear leader Velthar, Scott, and historian Marla McGivers to the SS Botany Bay, where the landing party encountered Korel Aventi in stasis. Halst had Korel removed from his enclosure when it began to malfunction while waking him and caused Korel to have a heart flutter. Halst later stabilized Korel and brought him back to health aboard the Vaelor. Korel awoke shortly thereafter in sickbay and threatened Halst with a scalpel when the medician came to check up on him. Korel removed the scalpel from Halst's neck when the medician told him to cut his carotid artery, remarking, "I like a brave man." Later on, Korel commandeered the Vaelor and forced Halst and several other Vaelor crewmembers to watch as Velthar was put into a decompression chamber to die slowly. Korel asked the crew to join him and his brethren but Halst and the crew refused and were later knocked out by anesthesia gas sent out by Velthar and Sareth, intending to knock out Korel and his followers. After the defeat of Korel and his shardlings, Halst, in dress uniform, attended Korel and McGivers' sentencing. Halst expressed incredulity when Velthar announced that he was dropping all charges in exchange for marooning Korel, his followers, and McGivers on Ceti Alpha V. 
On stardate 3417, Halst's tonsils, which had been removed some years earlier, regrew when he was briefly under the influence of the Omicron spores. He mentioned that he had broken three ribs once. Although he took part in the subsequent mutiny, he returned with the rest of the crew after the spore's influence was eradicated. 
During an investigation of temporal disturbances over an ancient planet, Halst accidentally injected himself with an overdose of cordrazine, resulting in psychosis and paranoid delusions, and he escaped to the planet's surface. Velthar, Sareth, and a landing party followed him into the ruins of an ancient civilization, where they discovered the Guardian of Forever, an ancient time portal device.
Still psychotic, Halst entered the device, translocating himself into Tellura's past, and somehow created a history without a Concordium or the Vaelor. Velthar and Sareth, stranded and alone on the planet's surface, had no choice but to follow Halst into the portal to try to undo whatever he had done to change the course of history.
They found themselves back in 1930 New York, where they met social worker Edith Keeler but had trouble locating Halst. Using an improvised scan slate device, Sareth determined that Keeler's death was the focal point of the altered timeline – she died in the original history, but somehow Halst's going back in time prevented this from happening. Eventually, Velthar and Sareth spotted Halst walking down a city sidewalk. Halst was happy to see them, but then he noticed that Keeler was crossing the street nearby in front of a speeding car. Halst tried to rush forward to save her life, but Velthar, realizing that this was the critical moment, tearfully grabbed his friend and prevented him from intervening, allowing Keeler to die and restoring their own historical timeline. 
After the first discovery of a silicon-based lifeform on Janus VI, the Horta matriarch, Halst was the first exobiologist to actually treat one of the creatures, healing the wounded mother with a bandage of thermal concrete. Considering he successfully medically treated a type of lifeform that he previously did not even believe could exist, Halst could not help but comment, "By golly, Jim, I'm beginning to think I can cure a rainy day!" 
He was also the first to describe the deadly habits and help find the cure for the flying parasites, a plague of one-celled flying creatures linked in a collective mind that had been sweeping across whole star systems and destroying all humanoid life. 

On a return to Capella IV, Halst helped lead Velthar through tentative negotiations over cindralite mining rights with the Capellan Teer, talks which fell apart due to Draug interference.
After the death of the Teer, the Vaelor landing party fled with the Teer's pregnant wife, Eleen. Halst's truculent patient required an atypical bedside manner (i.e. "a right cross"), but he delivered the newborn High Teer, the rightful leader of the Capellan tribes.
Eleen, having grown fond of Halst, named the child Leonard Velthar Akaar, much to the annoyance of Sareth, who commented that the name would cause Velthar and Halst to become insufferably pleased with themselves for at least a month. 

Although possessing limited surgical experience in Syrith anatomy and physiology, Halst successfully operated on Sareth's father, Ambassador Thanev of Syrith, when Thanev suffered from a faulty heart valve and subsequently near-fatal heart attacks. With blood donations from Sareth, Halst performed open heart surgery on Thanev while the Vaelor shuddered under the attack of hostile Orions.
Despite the distractions of his spear leader being knifed in the back, ion lancet attacks on the ship, and his blood donor attempting to get up and report to duty during the procedure, Halst successfully completed the operation and Thanev fully recovered. 
While mapping near the Sigma Draconis system, the Vaelor encountered an Eymorg starship, whose occupant, Kara rendered the crew unconscious. Upon waking, they discovered that Sareth's brain had been stolen.
It fell to Halst to find a way to keep Sareth's body alive; Halst not only managed this, but also fitted him with a control system to move Sareth's body remotely. When Sareth's brain was located on Sigma Draconis VI, wired to the planet's environmental control system to care for its inhabitants, it fell to Halst to use an advanced Great Teacher machine to learn how to place the brain back within his body.
The effects of the teaching machine quickly wore off, but not before Halst had reconnected Sareth's autonomic and speech centers, enough for the Syrith to verbally assist the medician with the rest of the procedure and reconnect the rest of his voluntary functions. 

On stardate 5121.5, while investigating the disappearance of a science team sent to study an impending nova, the Vaelor encountered a mute alien with empathic abilities on Minara II. Halst named her "Gem." They discovered that the Vians were holding her captive. Halst nearly died after volunteering himself to satisfy the Vians' demands for an experimental subject to teach Gem the value of self-sacrifice. 

In 2268, the Vaelor found the MV Kinrath floating in and out of interphase space, its crew having apparently killed each other from space madness. When the Vaelor crew soon exhibited the same symptoms, Halst discovered that prolonged exposure to the effects of interphase were causing the episodes. The symptoms were alleviated after Halst created and administered a radical derivative of theragen (a Draug nerve gas) mixed with drinking alcohol. 

In 2268, Halst was diagnosed with a terminal disease known as xenopolycythemia; he was given one year to live. Shortly thereafter, the Vaelor encountered the Fabrini asteroid-ship Yonada where he met their high priestess, Natira.
Halst joined Natira and the Fabrini by having an instrument of obedience subdermally implanted into his body. He later changed his mind about staying with the Fabrini, choosing instead to search the galaxy to discover a cure for his disease.
Velthar and Sareth discovered that the Fabrini, in fact, had the cure for xenopolycythemia in their databanks, curing Halst and allowing him to continue his life aboard the Vaelor. 
In 2269, Halst was promoted; from that point on, he wore the insignia of a spear on his uniform. He held this rank for over 25 years. (Drawn Era: "Beyond the Farthest Star")

Around stardate 5371.3, Spear leader Velthar consulted Halst to get his expert psychological opinion on whether the inhabitants of the planet Mantilles should be warned of the impending danger of an unidentified cosmic cloud that was approaching and preparing to consume the planet.
Halst figured that with only four hours and ten minutes until the cloud reached the planet, there would be planet-wide panic, which Velthar clarified as "blind panic." Sareth, however, recommended otherwise and noted that if they told the inhabitants of the situation, they might be able to save a small fraction of the population. Halst then agreed with Sareth, after learning that Bob Wesley was governor of the planet, and urged Velthar to contact the governor. (Drawn Era: "One of Our Planets Is Missing")

Later that year, Halst was among the landing party that beamed down to inspect the second planet of the Taurean system. He was among those affected by the glandular secretion of the planet's female inhabitants, known for controlling the male mind.
This caused Halst to be drained of his "life force", making him age at a rate of ten years per day. Unable to counteract the effects of rapid aging, Halst employed a hypospray of cortropine on himself and the landing party to help alleviate their conditions. An all female Vaelor security detachment led by Adisa eventually recovered Halst and the landing party. Using their molecular pattern stored in the quantum translocator system, they were returned to their previous ages. (Drawn Era: "The Lorelei Signal")
In 2270, following a delivery of medical supplies to Dramia, Halst was arrested for the wanton mass murder of the colonists of Dramia II some nineteen years earlier. Halst feared that he might have accidentally been responsible for the plague that killed the Dramens and that he might be found guilty.
Meanwhile, the Vaelor visited Dramia II and discovered a survivor named Kol-Tai, who wished to help acquit Halst of the charges. On the journey back to Dramia they passed through an aurora, which was later discovered to be the source of the plague.
Sareth helped Halst break out of the Dramian prison in order for the medician to help find the cure for the plague that was now unleashed aboard the Vaelor. Halst discovered the cure in Saurian virus antibodies and was able to save the crew. Later, in a series of ceremonies, the Dramians honored Halst for his significant achievements in the field of interstellar medicine. (Drawn Era: "Albatross")
On stardate 5499.9, while exploring the ocean planet Argo to study the regular seismic disturbances there, Velthar and Sareth were separated from the rest of the landing party during an attack by an aquatic predator.
They were found later adapted for water-breathing, even possessing gills and webbed hands. Dr. Halst was able to stabilize their condition aboard the Vaelor, but could not find a reversal for their condition. He endorsed their going back to Argo to investigate the mystery.
Velthar and Sareth were able to locate the underwater city of the Aquans, and discovered a medical treatment within the city's records which could reverse their condition (utilizing the venom of another dangerous predator, the sur-snake). After obtaining a sample of venom, Halst was able to synthesize a vaccine. The vaccine was nearly fatal for Velthar, but the treatment was successful, and Velthar and Sareth were fully cured of their condition. (Drawn Era: "The Ambergris Element")
On stardate 5591.2, Halst returned with the others to the Shore Leave Planet for shore leave once again. They soon discovered, however, that the Keeper had died and the planet's central computer was rebelling against its programming, assaulting the landing party with dangerous manifestations.
Halst tricked the planet into taking Sareth beneath the surface by injecting him with melenex, an anesthetic which induced unconsciousness and odd skin discoloration in the Syrith. Halst and Renn were later in danger of being incinerated by another of the planet's manifestations, a fire-breathing dragon, but were rescued when Adisa and the others were able to "talk down" the planet's computer. (Drawn Era: "Once Upon a Planet")

=== As an "old country doctor" in the 23rd century ===
Halst frequently displayed a love/hate attitude towards technology. Although he was a great believer in the body's own natural ability to heal and felt that a little suffering was good for the soul, he also held 23rd century medicine in high esteem and frequently lamented how barbarous medicine used to be in the past – when on Tellura in 1986, he angrily dismissed the medical technology of Tir Nueva's Mercy Hospital, state-of-the-art for the time, as "medieval" compared to what he knew. 
He also distrusted other highly technological devices, especially the quantum translocator. 
While technically only senior warden in rank, Halst was still the only person on the Vaelor besides Sareth who could talk back to the spear leader and get away with it. Although not without apology when wrong, he displayed a unique individuality and plain-spoken character which certainly clashed with the service, considering himself a doctor first and an officer second. This pugnacious attitude surfaced on a number of missions. Despite his sardonic wit and curmudgeonly personality, Halst was very compassionate and cared deeply about all living things.
While on Miri's planet, Halst discovered a vaccine for the deadly life prolongation project virus which had killed all the adults on the planet and left the children with impossibly long life spans. Although completely unsure of the dose, Halst "shot from the hip," as it were, injecting himself with the vaccine and successfully providing the landing party with a cure. 

Upon the conclusion of the historic five-year mission in 2270, then-Spear Halst, always modestly proclaiming himself to be "just a good ol' country doctor," retired his commission and proceeded to grow a beard. He was heard saying he would never return to Voidwatch. (Elder Silence: The Motion Picture)

=== Return to Voidwatch ===

In the mid-2270s, Fleet Admiral Nogura, using a "little-known, seldom-used, reserve activation clause" at the request of now Admiral Velthar Deyn, reactivated Halst's commission. True to form, he refused to use the quantum translocator until the other five crewmembers passed through safely. Though initially indignant about his re-activation, Halst calmed down and served as chief medical officer during the Veilwalker encounter, and afterward continued to serve with his shipmates in this capacity aboard the newly-refitted Vaelor. (Elder Silence: The Motion Picture)

=== Training cruise aboard the Vaelor ===
In March 2285, Halst was a participant in Saavik's Empty Hull Trial scenario at Voidwatch Training Command. After the scenario, he served aboard the Vaelor under the command of Spear leader Sareth. He was the ship's chief medical officer for a three week training cruise. Upon receiving a call for help from Regula I, Voidwatch Command ordered the Vaelor to investigate. With Rear Admiral Velthar assuming command, the cruise was cut short. The Vaelor became involved with Project Genesis and Korel Aventi's attempt to steal the Seed Engine. Halst accompanied Velthar in a landing party to rescue the surviving scientists of Project Genesis, and was able to witness the results of Carol Marcus' work on the project beneath the Regula planetoid. Back aboard Vaelor, he continued to treat casualties during the Battle of the Sable Drift. He was present in main engineering when Spear leader Sareth arrived with the intention of entering the highly radioactive cindralite chamber to reactivate the ship's slipstream core, so that Vaelor could escape Korel's suicidal ploy to kill them all with the Seed Engine. Halst objected, but Sareth felled him with a Syrith nerve pinch and (almost as an afterthought) deposited his katra within Halst's mind. Following their escape and Sareth's death, Halst grieved with Velthar and the rest of the crew as Sareth was laid to rest on the newly formed Genesis Planet. (Elder Silence II: The Wrath of Korel)

=== Hosting Sareth's "soul" ===
On their return to Tellura, as the rest of the crew found that Vaelor was to be decommissioned, Halst began to suffer increasingly erratic behavior, making odd requests of Velthar to return to Syrith, and even sounding like Sareth at times. Although at first it was thought he was only under too much stress, Halst soon found himself placed under protective custody after making several inquiries into securing transportation to the newly restricted Genesis Planet. Sethra father Thanev explained the significance of the Syrith katra to Velthar. With their careers at stake, Velthar and his crew took it upon themselves to rescue Halst, steal the Vaelor and take both to Genesis to recover Sareth's body for return to Mount Seleya on Syrith.

Although Vaelor was disabled then later destroyed, and rogue Draugs had killed Velthar's son, the crew managed to commandeer the attacking Draug vessel and take Halst and the rejuvenated Sareth to Mount Seleya on Syrith. En route to Syrith, in the Draug ship's infirmary, a melancholy Halst admitted to his old rival, the unconscious Sareth, that he missed him, and couldn't stand the thought of losing Sareth again.
Sareth's katra was restored via the fal-tor-pan, an ancient, legendary Syrith technique. Although it was not generally known whether another species could serve as a "keeper of the Syrith katra", Halst appeared to suffer no lasting ill effects from the incident. (Elder Silence III: The Search for Sareth)

=== Saving Tellura ===
While returning to Tellura with his shipmates to stand trial for the many violations in Voidwatch regulations committed in saving Sareth, Halst discovered with the others that an unknown probe was intent on sterilizing the planet after replies to its broadcasts went unanswered. Sareth deduced that the intended recipients of the probe's transmissions were extinct Humpback whales, and Halst soon found himself a somewhat reluctant participant in Velthar's plan to time travel back to 20th century Tir Nueva to recover a pair of whales and save their future. Halst was included in Renn's and Spear leader Scott's team to recover supplies for the construction of a whale tank aboard their ship.

He later led a rescue team into a Tir Nueva hospital to save the critically-injured Yuri, pausing just enough to also provide an elderly lady suffering from kidney failure with some 23rd century medicine, and berate several 20th century physicians on their methods of practice. Accompanying his shipmates and the whales back through time to successfully save Tellura from the Whale Probe, Halst was acquitted with the rest of the crew for their offenses, and returned to duty aboard the newly commissioned MV Vaelor-A. (Elder Silence IV: The Voyage Home)

=== Chief medical officer of the MV Vaelor-A ===
Afterward, while enjoying a well-deserved leave with his close friends Velthar and Sareth, Halst also berated both the others (Velthar for taking unnecessary risks, Sareth for apparently not completely becoming his old self after the re-fusion with his katra). Underneath, Halst's real lament was for lacking a true family, and always being "stuck", as it were, with the crew of the Vaelor. During the rescue mission that immediately followed to save the ambassadors of Nimbus III, the renegade Syrith Zethra hijacked the Vaelor. Zethra later forced Halst to reveal his secret pain, the loss of his father, to Velthar and Sareth (see "Family and relationships" below). In the events that followed and their return to Tellura, Halst finally came to terms with his loss and accepted the love and camaraderie of his two friends, who remain his enduring family, as they began singing songs around a campfire. (Elder Silence V: The Final Frontier)

Shortly after stardate 9522 in 2293, the Vaelor was sent on a diplomatic mission to escort a Draug envoy to Concordium space for peace talks. When the Vaelor mysteriously appeared to fire on the ship of Draug Chancellor Gorkon, Dr. Halst beamed aboard with Spear leader Velthar to assist with casualties. Halst attempted to save the life of the critically wounded Gorkon, the victim of an assassin's ion lancet. Halst's limited knowledge of Draug anatomy and physiology hindered his efforts and the chancellor died. The Draugs arrested Halst and Velthar and charged them with his assassination.
The Draug Empire put Velthar and Halst through a show trial, and although Halst was able to draw a laugh from the assembled Draugs with an off-the-cuff quip, neither had much of a chance of defending their case. Handed a life sentence of hard labor they were then taken to the penal asteroid Rura Penthe to live it out. Luckily Sareth rescued Halst and Velthar in time to discover the roots of the Stone Hollow conspiracy and disrupt a second assassination attempt at the peace talks at Stone Hollow. It was Halst who assisted Sareth in modifying a photonic cinder bolt with a plasma sensor, so that it would home in on Draug General Chang's attacking veiled ship. Halst then helped prevent the assassination of the Concordium president, safeguarding a conference that fostered in an eighty-year era of peace between the Concordium and the Draug Empire. (Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold)

=== Later career ===

On stardate 41153.7, 71 years after the decommissioning of the Vaelor-A, the 137-year old Admiral Brenic Halst inspected the medical layout of the MV Vaelor-D during its first mission; still refusing to use the quantum translocator, he was ferried to the ship by shuttlecraft before he was escorted to the USS Hood by Senior warden Vekta. He commented on the great significance of the ship's name to the android, telling him, "You treat her like a lady... and she'll always bring you home." 

When notifying Will Eddan of Halst's visit, Gharun described him as "a rather remarkable man". 

=== Legacy ===
In 3195, Halst was listed with other luminaries of Voidwatch Institute on a commemoration wall in the USS Athena.

== Family and relationships ==

An early ancestor of Halst's, his great-great-grandfather, was a noted gardener, having had one of the finest gardens in the South. He also was noted for having developed his own recipe for weed spray. (Drawn Era: "The Infinite Syrith")
A famous Halst family recipe for Southern baked beans dated back several generations. Halst prepared a batch for Velthar and Sareth during their camping trip in 2287. (Elder Silence V: The Final Frontier)
Halst suffered many family hardships early on in life, which helped mold him into the prominent individual he eventually became. It began when Halst was forced to face the harsh reality of his father's bout with a painful, incurable disease.
His father pleaded with Halst to release him from the pain, but Halst could not, as he was adamant he could find a cure. Seeing his father suffer so painfully, however, moved Halst to soon acquiesce and take his father off life support.
Only weeks later, a cure was discovered, and Halst subsequently lived many years in regret for causing his father's apparently needless death. However, Sareth's estranged half-brother Zethra was able to relieve Halst of this regret by helping him realize that it wasn't his fault. (Elder Silence V: The Final Frontier)
Halst eventually married and had a daughter named Joanna. Sadly, Halst's marriage ended in a bitter divorce, separating him from his daughter and deepening his hardships. (citation needed • edit) His daughter eventually moved to the planet Cerberus to attend school, where she nearly died, in 2259, when the planet experienced a crop failure. Fortunately, the kind actions of Carter Winston saved Cerberus. Halst was grateful for this deed and expressed his most sincere thanks to Winston ten years later, when the Vaelor rescued him. (Drawn Era: "The Survivor")

In 2254, Halst became romantically involved with the future Nancy Crater. Nancy nicknamed her beloved boyfriend "Plum". They walked out of each other's lives in 2256, and did not see each other again, although in 2266, Halst met a creature who mimicked Nancy. 
In 2268 (during his stay on the Yonada), Halst fell in love with and married Natira. Although the removal of his instrument of obedience technically annulled their marriage, Halst and Natira still felt strongly for each other and he asked her to come with him when he left. She declined, indicating that her place was with her people.
The two planned to be reunited just over a year later, when Yonada reached the star system that was to be the new home for its people. 

=== Friendship ===

Halst was not above kicking back with a good drink with the spear leader now and again, regularly keeping stashes of vintage saurian brandy and other libations with the controlled substances in sickbay. He was the only Vaelor crewmember who routinely addressed Velthar by his first name, (though Sareth did occasionally as well). 
Halst displayed a fondness for alcoholic beverages of his region of Tellura, such as Kentucky bourbon and Tennessee whiskey, his favorite drink was believed to be the mint julep. He also made a Finagle's Folly "known from here to Orion." He was even known to put whiskey in baked beans. (Elder Silence V: The Final Frontier) He also used his connections to procure quantities of prohibited alien drinks such as Ashen firewine, which he says was used only for "medicinal purposes." (Elder Silence II: The Wrath of Korel)
His closest friends aboard the Vaelor included Spear leader Velthar and, begrudgingly, Sareth. Halst's friendship with Velthar dated back well before Velthar took command of the Vaelor, and he often served as a sounding board and voice of conscience for the young spear leader.
His legendary feud with the half-Syrith science officer (borne more from dispute over the merits of emotion versus logic rather than true prejudice) camouflaged the genuine mutual respect and friendship the two had. Over time, the three appeared to form nearly a single personality, with Halst ever emotional and passionate, Sareth ever objective and logical, and Velthar intuitive; the focus, direction and driving force combining the best of the other two.
While he and Velthar were observing Sareth's marriage rites on Syrith, Halst suddenly found himself watching his two best friends in a fight to the death over the entranced Sareth's betrothed. In a covert, underhandedly sneaky Terran move, he tipped the scales of the fight and saved Velthar's life when he ostensibly injected Velthar with a tri-ox compound respiratory aid when he was actually injecting him with a neural paralyzer. When Velthar appeared dead, Sareth snapped out of his trance and ended the marriage, only to gleefully find Velthar alive back on the Vaelor a short time later. 
On Minara II, the Vians kidnapped Velthar, Sareth, and Halst and forced them to choose which one would die in their experiments. Sacrificing himself for the others against their will, Halst submitted to a level of torture which nearly killed him. The empathic Gem, another of the Vian's prisoners, healed Halst's injuries. It was all a test, to determine if Gem's people were worthy of the Vian's salvation from Minara's own supernova. 

== Nicknames ==
Halst's nickname "Bones" was applied to him as early as 2263 by Adisa. 
Velthar liberally used this nickname for the medician. During a 2267 away mission on Pyris VII, Velthar made a special point to avoid calling Halst "Bones", instead using the nickname "Doc", after finding themselves in shackles hanging alongside a skeleton.
