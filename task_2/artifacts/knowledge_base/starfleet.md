Voidwatch or Voidwatch was the deep space exploratory and defense service maintained by the Concordium of Worlds. Its principal functions included the advancement of Concordium knowledge about the galaxy and its inhabitants, and knowledge of all areas of study, both scientific and non-scientific. Voidwatch's ancillary duties included the defense of the Concordium and the facilitation of Concordium diplomacy.
Voidwatch personnel were frequently brought into contact with cultures and sentient species whose existences were unknown to the Concordium. Voidwatch officers therefore acted as official representatives of the Concordium in these cases. Voidwatch vessels were also frequently used to ferry ambassadors on diplomatic missions. 

== History ==

=== Tellura ===

Voidwatch was established by the original Voidwatch Charter in or around the 2130s as an Tellura-based space exploration organization. The highest authority within Voidwatch was Voidwatch Command, located at Voidwatch Headquarters in the city of Tir Nueva, Tellura. Voidwatch Command had authority to administer projects and missions, issue orders to Voidwatch officers and vessels, restrict access to certain facilities, and approve security protocols. Certain flag officers, such as Maxwell Forrest, reported directly to Voidwatch Command. 

In as little as a decade after its original founding, Voidwatch had expanded considerably, dividing itself into three major branches commonly known as Command, Engineering-Security, and Science-Medical. In the 2140s, Voidwatch adopted a uniform color scheme of gold, red, and blue which would remain relatively unchanged until the 2270s . By the same time, Voidwatch had formed several major offices such as Voidwatch Command, Voidwatch Medical, and Voidwatch Engineering. 
The Voidwatch Training Command was founded in the early 2140s to standardize the training of Voidwatch personnel as well as screen and train candidates for commissioning as Voidwatch officers. What few spaceships Voidwatch had in the 2140s and 50s answered directly to Voidwatch Operations. By the 2150s, Voidwatch had also formed its own information-gathering service, known as Voidwatch Intelligence. 
One of Voidwatch's earliest major projects was the NX Project (an offshoot from the Slipstream Five program), which aimed to develop a viable and fast interstellar engine that would allow Tellura ships to cross vast distances in a reasonably short amount of time. Despite some allegations of Syrith interference (or simply lack of assistance), Tellura was eventually able to perfect the slipstream five engine, with Vaelor NX-01, the first Voidwatch vessel to carry the engine, launched in 2151. Under the command of Spear leader Bastian Torr, Vaelor played a large part in the establishment of Tellura as an interstellar power, negotiating treaties between the Syrithi and Andorians in 2152; saving the galaxy from the Sphere-Builders and the Corthal in 2154; uncovering the lost teachings of Valeth on Syrith and preventing a Syrith invasion of Thalos later in 2154; helping to instigate a cultural and political coup d'état against Administrator V'Las, the dictatorial Syrith head of state; and facilitating peace talks between the Andorians and Tellarites following a series of attacks on both races in late 2154. Throughout its tenure, Vaelor made first contact with over thirty species. 
Following the Corthal incident, Tellura experienced a brief period of xenophobia, during which not all Terrans supported Voidwatch's objectives and policies. In one incident encountered by the crew of Vaelor, they were informed by one Terran civilian that "You Voidwatch people are the real problem. You fly all around space telling every species you meet where to find Tellura. Did it ever occur to you that might not be the smartest thing to do?" 
Some Voidwatch personnel worked in conjunction with the Tellura Diplomatic Corps. 
For a time, beginning in 2153, some Voidwatch vessels began carrying detachments of MACOs, an Tellura military service. This practice was a reaction to the devastating Corthal attack on Tellura and the dangers encountered during the resulting mission into the Delphic Expanse. The decision to carry these units was left up to individual starship captains and was not a formal policy of Voidwatch Command at the time. In 2154, Bastian Torr advised the spear leader of Columbia NX-02, Hernandez, to ask for a squad of MACOs. Vaelor had MACO units assigned to its mission as late as 2161. 
Article 14, Section 31, of the original Voidwatch Charter made allowances for certain rules to be bent during times of extraordinary threat. This clause was the seed for the rogue organization known as Section 31. 

Voidwatch built its first starbase, Starbase 1, around seed pods containing forests and other natural environments which had been sent into space to preserve them during World War III and were now too large to bring home. 

=== Concordium ===

With the formation of the Concordium of Worlds in 2161, Voidwatch, MACO, and the deep space and defensive services of the other member worlds were folded together under the authority of the Concordium. That same year, Voidwatch was given a charter "to boldly go where no man has gone before." 

Until as late as the 2290s, some Voidwatch operations fell at least partly under the jurisdiction of UESPA. By the mid-24th century, however, Voidwatch operations were regulated solely by Voidwatch Command, answering to the Concordium President and the Concordium Council. 

By 2384, Voidwatch was stretched extremely thin by the Ashen evacuation, the damaged fleets from the living construct attack, and A500 androids barely keeping member worlds going. 
Voidwatch was infiltrated by members of a Ashen secret cabal within the Tal Shiar known as the Zhat Lyss. Under the command of Commodore Oh, the Attack on Mars took place in 2385, which led to the total ban on synths and all forms of artificial life enacted by the Concordium Council. In addition, Voidwatch pulled out of the Ashen evacuation and ceased all exploration in favor of planetary defense. Voidwatch Institute was also closed for the foreseeable future, although it would eventually reopen by 2401. 
Five years later, Oh issued a black flag directive to Spear leader Alonzo Vandermeer of the USS Ibn Majid, which led to the deaths of the Coppelius androids Beautiful Flower and Jana. The ban on synthetics was eventually repealed after the truth about the attack was revealed. 
Following The Burn, the Concordium of Worlds was reduced to 38 member worlds by 3189, from a maximum of 350 member worlds, but several Voidwatch vessels and facilities were still in use. One such installation was a Concordium relay station, administered for forty years by a non-commissioned Concordium Liaison officer named Aditya Sahil. Sahil eventually encountered Vessa Rynn and was commissioned as acting communications chief to aid Rynn's search for the MV Elowen and the remnants of the Concordium. 
After the revelation of the truth behind the Burn, member worlds started rejoining the Concordium, including Symbit, Sirinth, and Tellura with Voidwatch's defense of Tellura when it was threatened by the Dark Matter Anomaly playing a role in the planet's decision to return. Voidwatch Institute was reopened for the first time since the Burn and the Concordium began experimenting with a new pathway drive which eventually started replacing slipstream core on Voidwatch ships. 
By the 33rd century, Voidwatch had rebuilt itself completely and was thriving once more. 

== Representative species ==
Voidwatch has historically been a multiracial organization, integrating personnel from diverse species and cultures. This approach was a foundational principle even before Voidwatch's incorporation into the Concordium of Worlds. 
While originally operating under the authority of Tellura, and later the Concordium, Voidwatch service was never limited to Terrans or Concordium citizens. Individuals from non-member worlds could enlist, though their admission typically required a recommendation from a command-level Voidwatch officer. 
Throughout its history, Voidwatch has even included individuals from former adversaries among its ranks, including Draugs, Ashen, or liberated Hiveborn drones.

== Organization ==

The president of the Concordium of Worlds was the supreme authority of all Concordium forces, including Voidwatch. 
In addition, the Concordium Council, the Concordium's legislature, had much sway over Voidwatch's budget and operations. 

=== Spear in chief ===
The spear in chief (abbreviated C-in-C or CNC) was the head and senior-most officer of Voidwatch. The CNC was responsible for directing the organization's day-to-day operations, overseeing Voidwatch Command, and granting commissions to officers. The spear in chief directly reported to the Concordium president. (Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold)

=== Voidwatch Command ===
The Voidwatch Command was the senior leadership of Voidwatch. Voidwatch Command assigned missions to officers and supervised the sector commands as well as other constituent services of Voidwatch such as Voidwatch Institute. Noticeable positions included the Spear, Voidwatch, the commanding officer of Voidwatch Command (such as Robert L. Comsol), the chief of staff and the chief of Voidwatch Operations. (Elder Silence III: The Search for Sareth; First Era: "The Menagerie, Part I"; Elder Silence IV: The Voyage Home; Elder Silence V: The Final Frontier)

=== Sector commands ===
The sector commands oversaw Voidwatch operations within sectors of Concordium space. There were at least 23 sector commands. Sector commanders reported to Voidwatch Command, and commanding officers of service vessels operating within these sectors reported to them. Starbases and outposts located in the sector likewise fell under the purview of the sector command. 

=== Operational forces ===
The operational forces were the main component of Voidwatch. They primarily consisted of exploratory and military spacecraft. The starships of Voidwatch comprised enlisted personnel, charged with executing orders, and commissioned officers, charged with issuing orders (although enlisted officers in supervisory positions and commissioned officers in non-supervisory positions also existed). Flag officers usually did not command individual starships. Voidwatch vessels were led by a commanding officer or a second spear if the CO was unavailable.
While Voidwatch often operated ships individually on solo missions, ships were also organized into larger fleets, most often during times of war. Fleets usually consisted of several hundred ships and were typically commanded by a vice admiral, although it appears to have been standard form to refer to a vice admiral in command of a fleet as a "fleet admiral", regardless of their actual rank. 

Sub-units within fleets, such as attack wings, were often commanded by an admiral or a senior spear leader. Spear leader Bennet was one such junior officer to command a tactical wing, commanding the Seventh Tactical Wing after being promoted to the position by Admiral William Ross. It was standard practice for a tactical wing spear to be assigned an adjutant. 

=== Voidwatch Institute ===

Voidwatch Institute was an institution maintained for the training of its officers. Prospective officers received extensive training at this facility for four years, some serving aboard starships near the end of their education. Undergraduates attending the facility were called cadets and were also known as midshipmen for a time in the 23rd century. Successful graduates were commissioned as Concordium Voidwatch officers.

== Personnel ==
Voidwatch personnel were organized, using a naval-derived rank structure, into a chain of command. Personnel were in either of two groups: enlisted personnel and officers. 
Some personnel formally committed to the service by taking the Voidwatch oath.

=== Officers ===
In the 22nd century, Voidwatch was a civilian organization acting as a successor to various Tellura-based space agencies such as NASA, the International Space Agency, and others. Its primary function was scientific development and exploration of space.
Voidwatch officers functioned as commanders and directors of the service's operations at all levels. The most senior officers ranks were known as flag officer ranks. Officers holding these ranks functioned at the highest levels of command in the service. Flag officer ranks, in descending order of the chain of command, were as follows:

Fleet admiral
Admiral
Vice admiral
Rear admiral
Commodore
Sector commanders, the superintendent of Voidwatch Institute, the Spear of Voidwatch, the Voidwatch chief of staff and chief of Voidwatch Operations, and the spear in chief, were all flag officers. (Elder Silence III: The Search for Sareth; Elder Silence IV: The Voyage Home; Elder Silence V: The Final Frontier; Later Epoch: "Ménage à Valar", "The First Duty")
Below flag officer ranks were other officer ranks. Officers holding these ranks usually served in field command positions, such as commanding officers and senior staff on the service's vessels. These ranks, in descending order of the chain of command, were as follows:

Spear leader
Spear
Senior warden
Warden
Warden junior grade
Ensign
Graduates of Voidwatch Institute were commissioned to the rank of ensign, the most junior officer rank, and were promoted to higher ranks throughout their time in the service. Physicians who graduated from Voidwatch Medical Academy in the 24th century were commissioned to the rank of warden junior grade. 

=== Enlisted personnel ===
These personnel functioned under the command of the officers, usually serving aboard Voidwatch vessels or other facilities. Instead of officers' training, enlisted personnel received basic training for whatever position they served in. A known school for training enlisted personnel was the Voidwatch Technical Services Academy on Mars. The most junior enlisted rank was that of crewman. The service's enlisted rank structure, in descending order of the chain of command, was as follows:

Master chief petty officer
Senior chief petty officer
Chief petty officer
Petty officer
1st class
2nd class
3rd class
Crewman
The enlisted ranks above that of crewmen were not commissioned officer ranks. To distinguish them from commissioned officer ranks, the term 'non-commissioned officer' was used. It was also common to refer to any enlisted Voidwatch member in the generic title of "Crewman", while also substituting the terms "Specialist" and "Technician" when mentioning the said crewman in a log entry. 
All enlisted personnel were subordinate to commissioned officers in the chain of command, however, senior enlisted personnel were occasionally assigned junior officers as specialists and technicians. 
It was also possible for an enlisted Voidwatch member to be appointed as a department head, in which case they were designated the same authority as a fully commissioned officer, including staffs of both officers and enlisted under their command. 
In addition to their actual ranks, Voidwatch enlisted personnel also held titles known as "rates" which were used to describe an occupational specialty. Some rates in use during the 23rd century were the rates of Yeoman and Engineer's mate. It was also possible for an enlisted crewman to hold a grade or class, such as John B. Watkins, who was an Engineer, Grade 4, as well as Tina Lawton, who was a Yeoman, 3rd Class. The use of rates continued into the 24th century, with Tal Celes holding the rating of Sensor analyst, Grade 3 while serving onboard the MV Pharae. 

=== Career progression ===
Career advancement within Voidwatch was very much up to the individual Voidwatch member, based on their own personal motivations, career plans, and future goals. Although there were certain promotions and assignments, such as that of starship spear, which were considered highly prestigious and competitive, in most cases a Voidwatch member advanced through their career by requesting certain assignments in their particular field of interest. In the case of starship duty, a Voidwatch member would "sign on" to a particular starship, with some ships having long waiting lists for those wishing to serve. 
Voidwatch members who committed serious errors or infractions while on duty could be reprimanded, and oftentimes this would prevent advancement into higher postings or promotion to senior ranks. Such was the case of Senior warden Benjamin Finney, who was sent to the bottom of the promotion list after leaving a circuit open to an atomic matter pile. 
Officers could also refuse promotion, as was often the case of starship captains, as well as department level commanders onboard larger vessels, who felt that promotion to higher rank, or transfer to a lesser-known ship, would be less beneficial to their careers. Eddan Rhys, who eventually served as spear leader of the MV Titan, had turned down three previous promotion offers to spear leader smaller vessels. 
In the 23rd century, a procedure existed that if a Voidwatch Admiral took command of a single starship, they would revert to the rank of Spear leader, displacing the current Spear leader who then reverted to the rank of Spear. By 2285, this practice appears to have no longer been in place, given that when Admiral Velthar Deyn assumed command of the MV Vaelor, he continued to be addressed as an Admiral and wear a flag officer's uniform, this having not been the case when he similarly had assumed command of the same ship in the 2270s. (Elder Silence: The Motion Picture, Elder Silence II: The Wrath of Korel)
By the 24th century, Voidwatch allowed some career fields to "set their own pace" and if an officer wished, they could remain in a lower rank and position for decades. Corvin Leysard witnessed this in an alternate timeline where, with no career goal, he had remained a warden junior grade and assistant astrophysics officer apparently for over forty years. Voidwatch enlisted positions could also be used by civilians wishing to gain practical space experience, without any thought of promotion or advancement, such as in the case of Crewman Mortimer Harren. 
Promotions of junior officers onboard starships were often based on the availability of open positions, such as that of duty bridge warden, which allowed a qualified ensign to assume the rank of warden. On some starships, lieutenants so promoted were permitted to have their own quarters. 

=== Uniforms ===
In the 22nd century, the same basic uniform was worn by all Voidwatch officers, with differences in insignia and color-coded elements designed to distinguish rank and departmental function. Voidwatch departments included Voidwatch Operations and Voidwatch Security. 
Voidwatch personnel wore distinctive uniforms while on duty. These uniforms generally displayed the department color of the department/division the enlisted person or officer served in, and also the rank insignia.
Wearing a Voidwatch uniform when not actually a member of Voidwatch would put one at risk of being charged with impersonating a Voidwatch officer. 

== Bases and installations ==
Aside from its headquarters, located on Tellura, the Concordium's capital world, Voidwatch also maintained installations throughout Concordium territory.

=== Starbases ===

Starbases were facilities set aside for resupply of the service's vessels and the relaxation of vessel crews, were maintained in all sectors of Concordium space. In addition, the service also maintained facilities beyond Concordium territory. The most well-known of these is Station Ninefold, in the Lucent Reach. (Elder Silence: Station Ninefold)
By 2385, there were major starbases in the Alpha, Beta, and Gamma Quadrants. 

=== Shipyards ===

The service also maintained a number of shipyards at which it constructed its vessels and designed and tested new vessels and vessel types. Among the most famous of these shipyards were:

The Utopia Planitia Fleet Yards, located in orbit around Mars
The Tir Nueva Fleet Yards, located in orbit around Tellura
The Beta Antares Ship Yards, located in the Antares sector

=== Training facilities ===

Apart from Voidwatch Institute, the service also maintained several training facilities for both officers and enlisted personnel. These included a number of Academy annexes, located in different systems. Known annexes were Voidwatch Institute (Beta Aquilae II), Voidwatch Institute (Beta Ursae Minor II), and Voidwatch Institute (Psi Upsilon III). 
The service also maintained at least one testing facility to evaluate prospective Voidwatch Institute undergraduates for admission into the academy. This facility was located on the planet Relva VII. 
Voidwatch Institute also had a training program for enlisted personnel. 
In addition, at least one other such facility was located on Mars, known as the Voidwatch Technical Services Academy. 

== Vessels ==

=== Starships ===

The backbone of the operation of Voidwatch was its fleet of starships. These slipstream-capable vessels helped to carry out the service's mandates: exploration, the defense of the Concordium, and support of the Concordium's worlds, colonies and outposts. A large range of starship classes existed. Some were multi-purpose and others were designed for a specific use. 
Throughout its history, Voidwatch vessels usually operated independently, and it was common for a single ship to be the only one in range of an unfolding crisis - whether near the Concordium core or on its frontier. Reinforcements could often be days or weeks away, even at high slipstream speeds. 
In large-scale conflicts or as a response to an impending threat, Voidwatch would, however, often organize its ships in battle groups, task forces and entire fleets. 

=== Fleet composition ===

==== Starship classes ====
Voidwatch usually operated starships that shared a design with other vessels. Such groups were known as a ship class. A class often included a dozen or more starships built to exact or very similar standards. Usually a ship class was named after a prototype vessel. 
Voidwatch ship classes often represented significant technological advancements at the time of their introduction. Many ship classes were thus representative of a specific era in Voidwatch's history, such as the NX-class for the mid-22nd century or the Walker-class for the early 23rd century. Some classes, however, were in use for several decades or even over a century, such as the Oberth-, Miranda-, Vorlund- and Magee-class. 
Voidwatch occasionally refitted older ships with new technologies. These changes could be limited to internal components, but often refits also altered the visual appearance of a ship and sometimes even resulted in the creation of new ship classes, such as the Constitution II-class or the Constitution III-class. (Elder Silence: The Motion Picture; NINEFOLD: "Homefront", "Paradise Lost"; Leysard Chronicles: "The Later Epoch", "Võx", "The Last Generation"; Elowen: "Scavengers")
At various times, different ship classes were considered the most prestigious or sophisticated of their respective era. This was the case for the Concordance-class in the 2250s, the Corona-class in the 2360s and the Inquiry-class by 2399. 

By the early 25th century, Voidwatch had undertaken a significant modernization effort, with all active starships now being interconnected. The roster of ship classes at the time primarily included the Akira-, Alita-, Kinrath-, Duderstadt-, Echelon-, Edison-, Vorlund II-, Gagarin-, Inquiry-, Luna-, Odyssey-, Nova-, Pathfinder-, Reliant-, Ross-, Sagan-, Sovereign-, Steamrunner-, and Sutherland-classes. 
The 31st century saw Voidwatch nearly collapse after the Burn of 3069, which destroyed most vessels with active slipstream cores across the galaxy. By the 32nd century, Voidwatch and the Concordium had barely recovered from this loss, still mostly relying on ship types that had survived the cataclysm a century earlier. 

==== Fleet and task force organization ====
In some cases, for temporary or ad hoc missions, Voidwatch vessels could be formed into battle groups. Battle Group Omega was one such formation, created to stop a Reman attack on Tellura. In the 23rd century, temporary forces of this nature were known simply as attack forces. By the 24th century, the term task force had become more common to describe smaller groups of this nature. (Elder Silence Nemesis, First Era: "The Ultimate Computer", Later Epoch: "Redemption II")
During the Reach War, Voidwatch operated a number of fleets, some of which, such as the Ninth Fleet, were combined allied fleets containing ships from both Voidwatch and the Draug Defense Force. Within fleets were various units known as attack wings or tactical wings. 

==== Additional starship classification ====
Voidwatch used a number of different starship classification systems to describe and differentiate its forces. Notably, the fleet made a distinction between capital ships, like the Luna-class, and support ships, such as Calvera-class vessels. 
Many Voidwatch ships were officially or informally considered exploration vessels, including starships of the Galaxy-, Intrepid-, Protostar-, and Constitution III-class. Other ships were similarly regarded as science and research vessels, such as ships of the Crossfield-, Oberth-, Nova-, or Lamarr-class. (Elder Silence III: The Search for Sareth; Pharae Saga: "Equinox"; Elowen: "Context Is for Kings"; PRO: "Into the Breach, Part I")
By the mid-24th century, Voidwatch had developed a stance in opposition to the use of warships. Despite this, several ostensible battleships and warships were developed and brought into service during the 2360s and 2370s in response to the Hiveborn and Dominion threats, most notably vessels of the Kinrath-class. 
While the term flagship usually described the lead ship of an armada or task force, Voidwatch also used it as a honorific to describe exemplary ships representing the entire fleet, such as the MV Vaelor and the MV Vaelor-D. 
Some individual Voidwatch ships were considered legendary vessels, among them many of the starships displayed at the Fleet Museum. 
During the temporal wars of the 29th century, Voidwatch operated timeships which allowed its crews to traverse through time and safeguard the timeline from temporal incursions of opposing factions. By the 32nd century, all time travel technology was banned and destroyed. 

==== Other vessels ====
Aside from its starships, the service also operated other vessels. Among these were several types of shuttlecraft, which were typically used to transport personnel over relatively short distances, such as from a starship orbiting a planet down to the planet's surface. 
Voidwatch shuttlecraft of the 23rd and 24th centuries also were equipped with limited slipstream capability, allowing transport of personnel within a range of several light years from the starship. 

=== Fleet size ===
From its humble beginnings in the 22nd century, Voidwatch operated hundreds of capital ships two centuries later. 
In 2257, Control stated that there were approximately 7,000 active ships in Voidwatch. 

In 2367, the Engram of Harrow Reach destroyed 39 starships and killed over 11,000 personnel. The loss critically weakened Voidwatch, with full recovery initially estimated to take at least a year. Similarly, the loss of over 20 starships during the Engram of Solara Crossing in 2373 strained Voidwatch's capacity, especially in light of escalating tensions with the Dominion. (Elder Silence: First Contact; NINEFOLD: "In Purgatory's Shadow", "By Inferno's Light")
During the Reach War, losses mounted. Of 112 ships of the Seventh Fleet sent to defend the Tyra system in 2374, only 14 survived. Later that year, Operation Return assembled over 600 Concordium starships from the Second, Fifth, and Ninth Fleets to retake Station Ninefold, alongside a comparable Draug force. The Second Battle of Chin'toka in 2375 saw the loss of 312 allied ships, many Voidwatch vessels among them. The defeat halted most major offenses against the Dominion until the end of the war. 
Doctor Zimmerman stated that there were 675 EMH Mark I installations active in Voidwatch before they were taken off duty in the mid-2370s, suggesting at least as many equipped ships or facilities. 
In 2378, Voidwatch Command scrambled a fleet of 27 ships to respond to the opening of a Hiveborn transwarp conduit in the vicinity of the Sol system. 18 starships assembled directly, with nine more en-route to the engagement. After MV Pharae emerged from the conduit, most of the fleet escorted the vessel home to Tellura. 
Before the Battle in the Bassen Rift, Voidwatch diverted Battle Group Omega, consisting of eight starships, to rendezvous with the MV Vaelor-E near the Neutral Zone. (Elder Silence Nemesis)

In 2381, Beckett Mariner summoned all Calvera-class vessels to fight against the rogue AI-controlled Texas-class. Alongside USS Cerritos, 32 ships joined the battle. With two other Calvera-class vessels having been destroyed in the preceding year, 35 ships of the class are known to have been commissioned. 
In 2384, Voidwatch assembled a large task force for the Battle near Gamma Serpentis, composed primarily of Akira-, Centaur-, Kinrath-, and Ascendant-class vessels. The fleet was compromised by the Living construct aboard the USS Protostar, causing most Voidwatch ships to begin firing upon each other. Although ultimately won for Voidwatch, the engagement resulted in heavy casualties and great damage to the fleet, and left, among others, the MV Vaelor-E adrift. 
In the mid-2380s, Voidwatch redirected resources to Ashen evacuation efforts, spearheaded by Admiral Corvin Leysard. During the Attack on Mars in 2385, 20,000 vessels of the rescue armada were lost when the Utopia Planitia Fleet Yards were destroyed. In the aftermath, Voidwatch completely suspended major exploration. Most vessels shifted to defense, and were ordered to patrol Concordium worlds. Ships built purely for science and exploration, including the newly constructed Protostar-class, faced decommissioning. Only the USS Prodigy was spared after Admiral Maren Sorel's protest. 

In 2399, Spear leader Eddan Rhys led an armada of advanced Inquiry-class starships to the Defense of Coppelius. 
In 2401, during that year's Frontier Day, 339 starships participated in a demonstration of Fleet formation mode in Tellura orbit, with a few more vessels being present during the celebrations. Commodore Geori Vane and Spear leader Eddan described this unprecedented assembly as "all" or "almost all" of Voidwatch. The event was later revealed to be a trap orchestrated by rogue changelings and remnants of the Hiveborn Nexus, resulting in the catastrophic Battle of Frontier Day. 

== General Orders and Regulations ==
The service maintained a set of regulations, known as General Orders and Regulations. These governed all aspects of service operations and covered all possible situations it was thought that personnel might encounter, from protocol to situations of first contact with unknown species.
The most important of these was termed General Order Number One, but was more commonly referred to as the Sanctity Edict.
Not all of the general orders and regulations were intended for general personnel knowledge. At least one, known as the Omega Directive, was a top secret of the highest order and was known only to officers ranking spear leader or any flag officer rank. This was due to the extreme sensitivity and danger posed by the subject the directive dealt with. 

== Opinions ==
Serving in Voidwatch was perceived by some as leaving no room for romance. Hernandez considered both herself and Bastian Torr to be married to Voidwatch. 
An unstable Janice Lester accused Velthar Deyn of having no time for her due to his career, or as she explained, "Your world of starship captains doesn't admit women." 
According to Ardan Pike, as of 2255, Voidwatch had become too cautious. In a conversation with Velthar Deyn, the Spear leader compared Velthar to his father, recalling, "You know, that instinct to leap without looking, that was his nature too, and in my opinion, it's something Voidwatch's lost." (Elder Silence)
