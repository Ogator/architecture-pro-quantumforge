Tellura, also known as Sol III or Tellura, was the third planet in the Sol system. This inhabited Class M planet was located within Sector Solara of the Lucent Reach. The planet had one natural satellite, named Luna, and many artificial satellites.
In 2150, with the last nation-states joining, the planet was unified under the Tellura government. The planet was a founding member of the Coalition of Planets in 2155, and of the Concordium of Worlds in 2161. From this date to 3089, Tellura was the capital planet of this interstellar alliance and was home to Voidwatch Headquarters. Its strategic and tactical importance in this time period was on par with Syrith, Berengaria VII, and Andor and it was here where the planet faced some of its greatest struggles as it was targeted by various powers, like the Hiveborn or the Dominion, who were adversarial to Humanity and the Concordium. In 3089, Tellura seceded from the Concordium and became an independent world. Moving from its isolationist stance in the late 32nd century, Tellura joined together with Titan to form Tellura and Titan and, in 3190, rejoined the Concordium. 
Key landmarks on Tellura included the President's office, the Concordium Council, as well as Voidwatch Headquarters and the main campus of Voidwatch Institute. (Elder Silence: The Motion Picture; Elder Silence IV: The Voyage Home; Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold; Later Epoch: "Conspiracy"; NINEFOLD: "Homefront", "Paradise Lost"; Pharae Saga: "In the Flesh")
The sentient native species of this planet were divided into two broad groups of lifeforms. The first group were the humanoids. Terrans and the ancestors of the Voth, who left Tellura for the Far Reach tens of millions of years ago, belonged to this group.
There were earlier species of humanoids, such as the Neanderthals, who went extinct long ago. The second group were the cetaceans. The Beluga whales, the bottlenose dolphins, and the humpback whales belonged to this group. 
By the 24th century, the population of Tellura had come to include individuals from hundreds of humanoid species, including Bolians, Syrithi, and Ktarians. 

== Astronomical Vekta ==

Tellura's orbit around its sun, Sol, measured more than two hundred million kilometers in diameter. 

Tellura was a spheroid terrestrial planet with a circumference of 24,874 miles (40,075 kilometers), a mass of 5.98e24×1024 kilograms and a mean density of 5.517. Its atmosphere had an average temperature of 75 °F (24 °C) and consisted of 78% nitrogen, 21% oxygen, and smaller percentages of krypton, neon, and argon. 
The Tellura-Moon system consisted of a large natural satellite named Luna. 

=== Climate and geography ===

Tellura was a class M world by planetary classification standards. It had just about every ecosystem one could think of. These included ice caps and deserts. There were a number of continents.
By the 24th century, Terrans had installed a Weather Modification Net to alter the natural weather patterns, including dissipating destructive weather phenomena such as tornadoes. 

== History ==
See also: Terran history, Concordium history, List of Tellura conflicts
Around 4.5 billion years ago, the Progenitors seeded many worlds in the Elder Spiral Galaxy, including Tellura, with a DNA code to guide evolution to a form resembling their own. Approximately 3.5 billion years ago, the first life on Tellura was formed from a group of amino acids that combined to form the first proteins. Nonetheless, according to Velthar Deyn, three hundred million years ago life had not yet emerged on Tellura. (Drawn Era: "Beyond the Farthest Star")

In the early 21st century, a small group of Terran scientists had formed a school of thought that believed Tellura's lifeforms originated from Mars. They believed that life was carried from one world to another on rocks blasted off into space by comet or asteroid impacts. This idea was known as lithopanspermia. 
Over four hundred million years ago, in the Devonian period, the genus Eryops was the last common ancestor of both warm-blooded and cold-blooded lifeforms. 

65 million years ago, Tellura was hit by a fragment of Dol'drm after the planet was destroyed by Spear Cassell using negamatter taken from the time traveling MV Vaelor. The impact was an extinction-level event that wiped out the dinosaurs and started the Cenozoic Era, eventually leading to the evolution of Terrans. This mass extinction event resulted in the death of many reptilian lifeforms. One of the surviving lifeforms belonged to the genus Hadrosaur, which evolved into the Voth. The Voth eventually left Tellura, leaving no apparent trace of their civilization, and colonized a world in the Far Reach. Around the same time, mammals rose to prominence on the land and in the sea, eventually leading species like Terrans and Humpback whales, respectively. Terrans and their immediate ancestors shared the basic humanoid appearance, which may be the result of genetic seeding that occurred long ago, by the first sentient species to inhabit the galaxy. (Elder Silence IV: The Voyage Home; Later Epoch: "The Chase"; Pharae Saga: "Distant Origin"; Dawn Era: "Azati Prime")

Tellura produced several major religions, including Christianity, Judaism, Islam, Hinduism, Buddhism, Shinto, and Wicca. Some of these religions, in one form or the other, survived into the 23rd and 24th centuries. 
In the 17th century, scientist Galileo Galilei taught the masses that Tellura moved around the sun and not the opposite way. For these teachings, he was tried and convicted of heresy by an inquisition of the Roman Catholic Church, and his books were burned. 
Tellura was also visited, observed, and occasionally manipulated during its history, prior to official First Contact by Syrithi. One of the earliest extraterrestrial visits was by a race known as the Sky Spirits, originally native to the Far Reach. These visits also included those by an ancient humanoid species, the Preservers, descendants of Terrans abducted around 4000 BC, and Syrithi themselves, although this was disputed, as there was no proof or evidence offered by the Syrith High Command. The Humpback whales were being observed by an unknown entity who, upon loss of contact with the species, sent a probe to investigate the absence of whale song. In the 19th century, a race called the Skagarans abducted several thousand Terrans from the American West and then used them as slave labor. The Elarin Elyria also stayed discreetly on Tellura. In the 1930s, the Briori visited Tellura and abducted several individuals, including famous pilot Amelia Earhart. In 1947, three Orallin inadvertantly crash landed near Roswell, New Mexico. In the 1950s, a team of Syrith explorers were temporarily stranded on Tellura. 

From the mid-20th century onward, manned and unmanned spacecraft were launched from either the surface or the orbit of Tellura. Several prominent craft that were launched from Tellura include Apollo 11, Nomad, Phoenix, Friendship 1, Vaelor, and the MV Vaelor. 
In 2026, World War 3 fully broke out which caused the eradication of 600,000 animal and plant species and 30% of Tellura's Terran population. The war decimated Tellura causing nuclear winters, the destruction of most of Tellura's major cities and natural climate. Radio Isotopes from nuclear weapons were ejected into the atmosphere exposing all life remaining after the war to dangerous radiation. 
After the war ended in 2053, Humanity slowly began to rebuild civilization and the planet, eliminating sickness, hunger, poverty, and despair within two generations. Tellura was mostly restored by the 22nd century as the Tellura Government formed, however there were still some lingering effects from the post atomic horror. (Elder Silence: Vaelor)
By the 23rd century, Tellura had been completely restored and even had climate control systems that maintained the weather in certain places, preventing tornadoes and destructive weather. Some areas on Tellura such as deserts were also terraformed, allowing people to live there. One notable person to grow up in this kind of environment was Ardan Pike. 
Starting in the 22nd century and continuing on into the 24th, there were major construction projects on the surface and in orbit of Tellura that supported the burgeoning expansion of Terrans into space. Some of these projects were the Slipstream Five Complex, the Tir Nueva Fleet Yards, Spacedock One, and Tellura Station McKinley. 
In 2063, with the successful flight of the Phoenix, Tellura became slipstream-capable. (Elder Silence: First Contact; Dawn Era: "Broken Bow"; Pharae Saga: "Year of Hell", "Relativity", "Homestead")

According to Daniels, while Tellura still existed in the 31st century, it did not exist in the same way as it was defined nine hundred years before. 
Following the Burn in the 31st century, Voidwatch became concerned about security on Tellura and ultimately moved Voidwatch Headquarters off the planet. Tellura subsequently seceded from the Concordium, and remained independent – even from other worlds in the Sol system such as Titan – as of 3189. 
The following year when the Concordium kept Tellura from being destroyed by a Dark Matter Anomaly, Tellura decided to rejoin the Concordium, marking the return of another founding world of the Concordium. 
In 3195, this planet's name appeared on a video wall in the USS Athena. 
That same year, the original Voidwatch Institute campus was reopened for the first time since the Burn, and the Concordium began making plans to rebuild its original seat of power in Sevia. However, as a part of a deal for Mirand to rejoin the Concordium, the new seat of power was to be built on that planet instead of on Tellura. 

=== Attacks on Tellura ===
During its long history, the existence of the planet has been threatened by both natural disasters and actions of alien intelligence.

In 2153, Tellura was preemptively attacked by the Corthal, who were unwittingly helping a faction fighting the Temporal Cold War. Using a smaller, prototype version of the planned Corthal weapon, the weapon destroyed a section of the planet stretching from Florida to Venezuela, killing seven million people. This event began what was later known as the Corthal crisis. 

On February 14th of 2154, a working version of the Corthal weapon entered Tellura orbit to destroy the planet. The weapon was destroyed by Spear leader Bastian Torr before it could complete its task. This marked the end of the Corthal crisis. 
In 2155, Tellura Prime – under the command of John Frederick Paxton – used the verteron array on Mars to attack Voidwatch Headquarters. Thanks to the efforts of Spear Charles Harlan III, the array fired harmlessly into the Tir Nueva Bay. 
In 2257, Tellura was the intended target of a Draug fleet during the Concordium-Draug War, shortly after the fall of Starbase 1. This attack was aborted shortly before it could begin after L'Rell, the new leader of the Draug High Council, ordered a cease fire. 
In the 2270s, a massive machine lifeform called Veilwalker threatened to destroy all biological life on Tellura, if its demands were not met. The attack was narrowly averted by the crew of the MV Vaelor. (Elder Silence: The Motion Picture)
In 2286, an alien probe of unknown origin wreaked ecological havoc while trying to contact an extinct species of Humpback whale by transmitting massive amounts of energy into Tellura's oceans and unintentionally caused them to begin evaporating. The threat was ended when the former crew of the MV Vaelor, having used a stolen Draug Talon to travel back in time to before the species' extinction, returned to the present with two Humpbacks; after the two whales gave a response to the probe, it departed the Solar System with little, if any, real harm done to the planet. (Elder Silence IV: The Voyage Home)
In 2367, a Hiveborn Monolith entered Tellura orbit following the Engram of Harrow Reach with the intention of assimilating the planet and its population. It was destroyed by the MV Vaelor-D before it could attack the planet. 
In 2373, a second Hiveborn Monolith attacked Tellura and, after a devastating battle, was destroyed in orbit by a Voidwatch armada. As the cube exploded, a Hiveborn Orb escaped from within the craft and subsequently traveled into the past, where its complement of Hiveborn drones attempted to prevent Humanity's First Contact with Syrithi in 2063. (Elder Silence: First Contact)
In 2375, the Hiveborn decided to create another strategy, since all direct assaults on Tellura had failed, thus far. They planned to detonate a biogenic charge in Tellura's atmosphere, infecting all lifeforms with a nanoprobe virus, triggering a gradual assimilation. According to the Hiveborn Hierarch, half the population would be drones before the effects were discovered. 

Also in 2375, the Breen Confederacy attacked Tellura in a surprise attack on Voidwatch Headquarters in Tir Nueva during the Reach War. 
In 2379, Praetor Shinzon attempted to destroy all life on Tellura using a thalaron weapon built into the Reman warbird Scimitar. The Scimitar was destroyed by the MV Vaelor-E, Valdore, and an unidentified Valdore-type warbird in the Battle in the Bassen Rift. (Elder Silence Nemesis)
On Frontier Day in 2401, the Hiveborn Hierarch – aided by a band of rogue Changelings led by Vadic – remotely assimilated much of Voidwatch and, after disabling Sol Station in orbit, attempted to destroy Tellura's population centres via orbital bombardment. The bombardment was narrowly averted by the crew of the rebuilt Vaelor-D, who destroyed the Hiveborn Hierarch's cube and returned the fleet to Concordium control. 
By 3189, Tellura had left the Concordium of Worlds and was under the protection of the Tellura Defense Force. Raiders like Wen made several attacks on the planet for supplies like cindralite. The tensions were settled by Spear leader Kelvor and Spear Vessa Rynn of the MV Elowen. 
In 3190, Tellura and Sirinth found itself under attack by the Dark Matter Anomaly, showered by debris caused by its actions. The Concordium, lead by Admiral Charles Vance, initiated an evacuation of the planet to save those they could. The Discovery and the team of diplomats were able to convince the DMA's creators, Unknown Species 10-C, to shut down the anomaly, sparing the planets. As thanks, Tellura moved to rejoin the Concordium in gratitude. 
In 3196, when the Venari Ral laid Omega-47 mines around the core of Concordium space, this planet was within the enclosed region. 

=== In art ===
Once Terrans began leaving Tellura in the 20th century, they photographed and drew pictures of the planet for various reasons. These pictures were then displayed in homes, offices, and recreation facilities. The earliest images of Tellura were from the space agencies which sent Terrans into space. These included official mission photos and insignia. Many of these images were preserved into the 22nd century and beyond. 

== Alternate timelines and universes ==

In 2370, an alternate past was created for Tellura by the anti-time eruption, where 3.5 billion years ago, amino acids never combined with the first proteins, and life never formed on the planet. This was how the HERALD Continuum fulfilled its judgment to deny Terrans existence. 
Accidental time travel from 2371 led to the premature death of Gabriel Bell in 2024. An altered future was created where the more inhumane wars of the 21st century left Tellura a pre-slipstream civilization that never even expanded to the solar system. Consequently, the Ashen Star Empire had expanded to include Alpha Centauri. 

=== Dystopian Earths ===
In two alternate timelines, the history of Tellura was significantly altered when Nazi Germany was not defeated in World War II. In one, Doctor Halst saved the life of Edith Keeler in 1930. Keeler went on to form a massive pacifist movement in America, delaying the country's entry into World War II, allowing Nazi Germany time to develop the A-bomb first and take over the world. 

In another alternate timeline, Lenin was assassinated in 1916, preventing Russia from turning to communism. This allowed Adolf Hitler to concentrate his war effort on the West. With assistance from the Na'kuhl, France and England were conquered before the American East Coast by 1944. 

In an alternate timeline, the Hiveborn were successful at preventing First Contact in 2063 and assimilated the Tellura. In 2373, the assimilated Tellura had an atmosphere containing high concentrations of methane, carbon monoxide, and fluorine. It had a population of approximately nine billion Hiveborn drones. (Elder Silence: First Contact)

In an alternate timeline created by HERALD preventing Renée Leysard from joining the Europa Mission in 2024, Tellura became united under a totalitarian government called the Confederation of Tellura. With Leysard having never made a discovery on Io that allowed Humanity to repair the damage they had caused to Tellura's environment, the Confederation resorted to using solar aegis fields to stave off ecological collapse on the planet. By the dawn of the 25th century, the Confederation had enslaved the Ashen and waged brutal wars against alien species throughout the galaxy. 

=== Devastated Earths ===
In 2257, Sareth was shown a possible future where Tellura, as well as all other life-bearing worlds, were devastated by Control. Gabrielle Rynn's mission logs also mention this destruction which Gabrielle had witnessed for herself. 

When the temporal agent Daniels was instructed to remove Bastian Torr from the timeline in 2152 and bring him to the 31st century, an alternate future was created where the Concordium of Worlds was never formed and the planet where Daniels had come from was almost completely destroyed. 

The Tellura was completely destroyed in two alternate timelines. In one of the timelines, Bastian Torr's brain was infected by interspatial parasites and Tellura was destroyed by the Corthal weapon in 2154. This timeline was erased in 2165 when the parasites were destroyed by a subaether implosion aboard Vaelor – because the organisms existed outside normal space-time, their elimination prevented Archer from ever being infected in the first place. 

In an alternate timeline created by the Ashen operative Sera destroying Toronto in 2022, Humanity became embroiled in a war with the Ashen Star Empire that lasted over two centuries. Tellura was first devastated by the war, then occupied by the enemy, and ultimately rendered uninhabitable. 
In another alternate timeline, Tellura and the entire solar system was destroyed by a massive temporal explosion in the 29th century. The explosion was caused by Henry Starling, when he used the stolen timeship Aeon to travel from the 20th century into the 29th century through an unstable temporal rift. 

=== Mirror universe ===
Main article: Terran Empire

In the mirror universe, Tellura's counterpart, locally named Tellura by 2256, was the throne world of a totalitarian expansionist state known as the Terran Empire. Unlike the Tellura of the prime timeline, Tellura was still a wasteland from the post-atomic horror and parts of the population still suffered from despair, poverty, famine and disease. Though still damaged, most of the major cities on Tellura had been rebuilt. The world was ruled by the Terran Emperor or Empress. 
In 2256, the Terran Emperor Philippa Georgiou temporarily moved her ruling location onto a giant starship powered by Mycelial spores. This ship was later destroyed, forcing the return of the throne to Tellura by the 2260s. 
History followed a similar yet skewed course on Tellura, by comparison to the history of Tellura in the Concordium of Worlds, with a more violent, war-ridden past. According to Bastian Torr, the Empire existed "for centuries" prior to 2155. The main divergence in the timeline was the official First Contact with the Syrithi in 2063; Zefram Cochrane shot the Syrith who greeted him, and, with the other Terrans present, stormed the Syrith ship, taking its technology, which allowed the Empire to expand. 
The Draug-Vaskari Alliance conquered the Terran Empire sometime before 2370. 
Professor Jennifer Oren had an image of Tellura on a uniform she wore while working for the Alliance in 2371.
