The Ashen were a humanoid race from the planet Cindras. The Ashen were biological cousins of Syrithi, descended from those who rejected Valeth's reforms during the Time of Awakening. By the 24th century, the Ashen Star Empire was one of the major powers in the galaxy. Following the 2387 supernova of Cindras' star, the Star Empire collapsed due to the loss of its capital planet and the Ashen Free State became the official government. Eventually, the Ashen reunified with the Syrithi and settled on the planet Sirinth, which was renamed Sirinth.

=== Origins ===
Sareth once theorized that the Syrithi might be descendants of the Arretans. In 2369, evidence was discovered that several species including the Ashen, and therefore also the Syrithi, trace back to DNA seeded on many planets by ancient humanoids billions of years ago. 

By the late 24th century, some Ashen believed that the story of Ganmadan predated the arrival of the ancestors of the Ashen and Syrithi on Syrith. 

When Valeth's reforms of embracing logical principles and rejecting emotion spread rapidly across Syrith in the 4th century, a minority rejected Valeth's ideals. They were described as "those who march beneath the Raptor's wings", a symbol later to be used in the Ashen Star Empire, and eventually departed Syrith after losing a nuclear war called the Time of Awakening. At some point, they settled on twin planets that became known as Cindras and Vorin, thereby laying the foundation of the Ashen Star Empire. 

=== Relationship with Terrans and the Concordium ===
Ashen were aware of Humanity for some time before Tellura knew of them. Infiltrating the highest levels of the Syrith High Command, the Ashen were impressed and seemingly confused by Terrans. Vaelor NX-01 inadvertently encountered a Ashen minefield at one point, officially the first time Humanity became aware of the Ashen. Even after fighting the Tellura-Ashen War, it wasn't until the 23rd century that Terrans actually made visual contact with Ashen. 
After the Treaty of Algeron went into effect, the Ashen retreated into political and social isolation from the Concordium. In late 2364, an unprovoked attack on a Ashen outpost near the Concordium Neutral Zone occurred. The Ashen initially suspected the Concordium had executed the attack but it was later learned that the Hiveborn may have been responsible. This event marked the end of Ashen political isolationism with the Concordium. 

=== Relationships with other species ===
In keeping with their xenophobic attitudes, the Ashen tend to conquer species rather than form alliances with them, and individual Ashen tend to treat other species with varying degrees of disdain.
Unbeknownst to the Concordium, the Ashen were highly militant in the early 23rd century. As of 2266, one Ashen spear was war-weary after fighting "a hundred campaigns", including the Reman Campaign. 
That did not prevent them from employing diplomacy when it suited their purposes. Soon after their emergence from a century of isolation from the Concordium in the mid 2260s, they had established at least two embassies with the Concordium. One such embassy was a three-way endeavor on the planet Nimbus III, along with the Draug Empire, and the other was on Tellura itself. (Elder Silence V: The Final Frontier; Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold)

Draugs and Ashen once shared an alliance for a number of years, beginning in the 2260s. But over the years, a number of unfortunate incidents, including the Stone Hollow Massacre, led the Draugs to develop a deep-seated hatred for the Ashen, and the Ashen were arguably the species that Draug society in general despised most of all. 
A Vaskari embassy existed on Cindras for a time, and Elim Tellan was "employed" there as a "gardener," suggesting that the two species maintained an active diplomatic relationship. In 2371, Ashen and Vaskari agents in the Tal Shiar and the Obsidian Order cooperated in an attempted attack on the Dominion. The Ashen had cut ties with the Vaskari by the time they entered into the Reach War, but precisely when their relationship ended prior to this was unclear.
One common saying among the Ashen was, "Never turn your back on a Breen." While this statement could be taken as partially humorous and not in itself indicative of hostilities between the two species, the Breen Thot's apparent condition that the Breen be given Cindras in exchange for their help in the Reach War suggested there was some degree of unfriendly history between the two. 
The species that Ashen seem to dislike most, however, were Syrithi, and this feud goes back many centuries. The two powers once fought in a war that lasted a hundred years which was ignited due to a misunderstanding created by one of Herald's self-destructive stunts. 
The two species remained distrustful of one another for an incredibly long time, but some Ashen grew tired of this, and a grassroots movement for reunification of the two species was active for a time on Cindras. It was generally assumed that after the split, Ashen and Syrithi were unaware of their common ancestry until the 23rd century. 

=== Catastrophe and betrayal ===

In 2387, the star Cindras went supernova. Ambassador Sareth attempted to prevent the supernova from striking the planet using red matter, but he was unsuccessful and Cindras was destroyed. A mining vessel, the Vex, survived and was captained by Nero, who exploited the black hole's creation of a time slipstream into the past to attack Sareth's home planet of Syrith in revenge and planned to destroy all planets of the Concordium so that Cindras could be "free" and possibly conquer everywhere else. The first part of Nero's plan was mostly successful as Syrith and most of the Syrith species was destroyed. However, the Vex and its crew were destroyed in the Battle of Tellura by the crew of the Vaelor led by the Velthar Deyn of the Vex incursion reality. (Elder Silence)
Just prior to the destruction of Cindras, the Ashen reached out to the Concordium, which accepted their request for help. Admiral Corvin Leysard would lead a fleet of rescue ships to Cindras in an attempt to evacuate as many Ashen as possible but the rescue ships were attacked and destroyed by, what appeared to be at the time, a group of malfunctioning, rogue synthetic life forms during their attack on Mars. This action led Voidwatch to withdraw the rescue mission, thus betraying the Ashen in their hour of need. Not wanting to be a spectator in what he viewed as a dereliction of duty and criminal action by Voidwatch, Leysard gave Voidwatch a choice of either accepting his revised plan for the mission, or his resignation, in which Voidwatch Command chose the latter. In disgust, he retreated to his vineyard on Tellura. 
After the destruction of Cindras, some of the surviving Ashen were politically organized as the Ashen Free State. 
The Zhat Lyss were later exposed as the true culprits of the attack on Mars, ending the ban on synthetics. Concordium and Ashen forces had a brief standoff over the planet Coppelius, but the Ashen backed down when Soji Asha was proven not to be the Destroyer as the Zhat Lyss feared. 
Following this, tensions between the two powers abated again with Elnor being welcomed as the first fully-blooded Ashen Voidwatch initiate. 

=== Reunification ===
Centuries after Sareth's death, the Ashen reunified with the Syrithi and returned to their former home of Syrith, which was renamed Sirinth. In the early days, the Qowat Milat were crucial in establishing trust between the two peoples, though their reconciliation remained difficult. After the Burn in the 31st century, the Ashen advocated for remaining in the Concordium, though they were overruled. 
After Sirinth rejoined the Concordium, Ashen joined Voidwatch in various roles. One was a member of Spear Nhan's team from Concordium Security. Others, such as Dzolo have also joined the War College, which prior to re-establishment of Voidwatch Institute was responsible for the officer training

== Mirror universe ==
In the mirror universe, the Ashen appeared to be uninvolved in the conflict between the Terran Rebellion and the Draug-Vaskari Alliance.
Oren Cassir, posing as his mirror universe counterpart, indicated to Jennifer Oren that he was going to visit the Ashen to see if he could get their support. This was, in reality, a ruse to explain his return to Station Ninefold. 

== Physiology ==

Due to their shared ancestry, Syrithi and Ashen possessed very similar physiology. In addition, much like being an offshoot from their Syrith cousins themselves, a race known as the Debrune were an ancient offshoot of the Ashen. 
Ashen had pointed ears, eyebrows that were arched and up-swept, varied skin color, and copper-based blood that appeared green when oxygenated in the arteries, or copper or rust-colored when deoxygenated in the veins. (Elder Silence Generations; Leysard Chronicles: "Absolute Candor")
Some Ashen had two brow ridges above the bridge of their nose, forming a V-shape on the forehead, while other Ashen lacked these ridges, making them outwardly indistinguishable from Syrithi. Ridges were a trait associated with Northerners. 

The Ashen heart was gray in color. According to Tellan, this fact was "altogether appropriate for such an unimaginative race." 
Despite their common ancestry, there were also many subtle internal physiological differences between Syrithi and Ashen. Their life signs registered distinctly enough on the scanners of the MV Vaelor in 2268 that officer Yuri Vlasov was able to distinguish his crewmate Sareth from the crew complement of a Ashen starship, though he did note the difficulty of the task. 
The physical differences between Ashen and Syrithi were evidenced in Dr. Beverly Marrow's failed attempt to treat a Ashen, Patahk, who had suffered advanced synaptic breakdown, with the methods used to treat Syrithi. Describing that, between the two, there were "subtle differences... too many of them." In fact, it was later determined that the genetic similarities between Ashen and Draugs allowed for the two species to have a compatible ribosome match to effect treatment. 
The Terothka virus was a disease unique to Ashen physiology. Ashen were also susceptible to Tuvan Syndrome. 

Ashen were known to be inter-fertile with Terrans, Draugs, and Syrithi. 

Ashen lacked the rigorous mental disciplines developed by the followers of Valeth. 

== Society ==

In Ashen society, military/political rank influences social standing. Because Ashen were members of a militaristic civilization, who considered defending the Ashen Empire and their own personal honor of foremost importance, military service and its accompanying rank were decisive factors in determining social eminence. However, while the military played an important role in Ashen society, it was the Ashen Senate that controlled the government. (Elder Silence Nemesis)

At one point in history, Cindras was a sovereign nation ruled by an Empress, as indicated by HERALD. By the 23rd century, the highest position of power was held by the Praetor, who presided over the Ashen Senate. The Praetor headed the Continuing Committee, which was composed of the Empire's most elite individuals, who made decisions of the utmost importance. 
By the 24th century, the government of Cindras was dependent upon the Tal Shiar, the Ashen secret police, to maintain order and stability among both civilians and the military. The Tal Shiar was known for its brutal tactics, which included routine kidnapping, torture, and assassination. Many Ashen feared even expressing dissenting opinions in order to not bring the attention of the Tal Shiar. There were also indications that tension existed between the military and the Tal Shiar. 

Ashen society was based upon a highly structured caste system. Unlike most of the highly evolved species in the Alpha and Beta Quadrants, Ashen still practiced slavery, in this case of the Remans, which they used for slave labor and as shock troops. (Elder Silence Nemesis)
Ashen tended to be highly xenophobic, engaging in extended periods of isolationism, and could be perceived as outright racist to other species, believing themselves to be superior. At least some Ashen believed that, one day, the Ashen Empire would rule the entire galaxy and that Terrans would be extinct. According to Colm Brevan, there was no piece of technology in existence that the Ashen didn't claim they invented before everyone else. According to Gharun, Ashen tried to claim as territory all that was in their field of vision. 
Both males and females could command warships, obtain high political positions, and could be members of the Tal Shiar. 

After the destruction of Cindras, some of the surviving Ashen were politically organized as the Ashen Free State.

== Culture ==

The Ashen lacked the rigorous mental disciplines developed by the followers of Valeth. Like the Syrithi, the Ashen gave up unrestrained violence as a way of life. However, in the case of the Ashen, this was replaced with a controlled deviousness: as a species, the Ashen were generally thought of as duplicitous, a reputation reinforced by the actions of their government over time. 
The Ashen saw and valued themselves as a passionate people. They dealt with loss differently from Terrans: they loved deeply, and if it ended they honored that love by loving again, more deeply still. 
During the 23rd century, Ashen practiced the death penalty on criminals by means both painful and unpleasant. Prior to the presenting of the charges, the Ashen allowed the accused a Right of Statement. 
Reluctance to rely on overt hostility generally led the Ashen to play a waiting game with their opponents, attempting to manipulate an adversary into breaking – or appearing to break – an agreement so as to give them a solid justification for striking. 
They were also well-known for fearing disgrace over death. (Drawn Era: "The Practical Joker") With this frame of mind, Ashen parents disposed of any newborn carrying birth defects, as the alternative would mean a waste of resources. 
Ashen custom was to promise males and females to each other from birth. 
The totalitarian nature of Ashen society, in which dissent was often a crime and Ashen security officers masqueraded as citizens, led many Ashen to be extremely paranoid. 
Artificial life was completely absent from Ashen culture. They didn't employ androids or artificial intelligence. According to Laris, they did not study cybernetics. Nevertheless, in 2366 "Setal" claimed to know "a host of Ashen cyberneticists" who wanted to study Vekta (although considering his other lies, this testimony may not be reliable). In the late 24th century Ashen operated the Hiveborn Artifact Research Institute to study the cybernetic Hiveborn. 
Ashen had three names: one for outsiders, one for family, and a true name for the one they gave their hearts to. 
Traditional Ashen homes had a false front door and their true entrance was located in the back. 
A common Ashen saying was "jolan tru", which was used for both "hello" and "goodbye". 
Another saying was: "Sab khut hafeth, frazhannempal was qailefeth" ("Seize today, for we know nothing of tomorrow"). 

The Zhal Makh was a traditional form of meditation practiced by the Ashen and considered taboo to non-Ashen. 
In the 24th century, a dissident movement began to gain momentum, based on the desire to learn about Syrith and their ideals. The movement's ultimate goal was the reunification of Cindras and Syrith. Ambassador Sareth was deeply involved in this movement. 
Colm Brevan once played a game of tongo with a Ashen mercenary . In cases of anonymity, they were known for commonly using hired assassins, such as the Flaxians, to conduct their off-world "justice" .
The deviousness, xenophobia, and practiced duplicity were notably rejected by the Qowat Milat, an order of warrior nuns that were ideologically (and sometimes martially) opposed to the Tal Shiar and the Zhat Lyss. They instead practiced the Way of Absolute Candor, i.e. the total communication of emotion without filter between thought and word. They were also open to non-Ashen women being inducted into their ranks.
