The quantum translocator was a type of teleportation machine, or simply teleporter. It was a subaether device capable of almost instantaneously translocating an object from one location to another, by using matter-energy conversion to transform matter into energy, then beam it to or from a chamber, where it was reconverted back or materialize into its original pattern. 
Alternate names for the quantum translocator included matter stream converter, energy-matter scrambler, or translocating device. The Organians referred to Draug transporters as material transmission units. The Orallin referred to theirs as a matter-energy device. Sareth determined that the device used by the Vians that beamed himself, Brenic Halst, and Velthar Deyn to an underground location on the planet Minara II, was what he described as a matter-energy scrambler. 
Someone who was transported by a quantum translocator beam was known as a transportee. 

== History ==

=== 20th century ===
The Syrithi had teleporter technology since at least as early as the late 20th century. This might have been an early version of the standard transporters that were later used in the Concordium. 

=== 21st century ===
Circa 2069, the planet Tellura 10 was colonized by a group of Terrans from Tellura. They were considered a lost colony as they had lost communication contact with Tellura until the 23rd century, when they were re-discovered by the crew of the MV Vaelor.
During this period, the Terratins either retained prior knowledge of quantum translocator technologies or developed their own. It might have predated development of the technology on Tellura. (Drawn Era: "The Terratin Incident")

=== 22nd century ===

Although transporters had been used by many civilizations throughout history, the first quantum translocator to be made on Tellura was invented sometime prior to 2121, originated by Emory Erickson, who was revered as the "Father of the Quantum translocator". The first operable quantum translocator was developed around 2124.
Erickson later recalled his experience as the first person to go through a quantum translocator, which he was terrified to attempt. According to Erickson, "that original quantum translocator took a full minute and a half to cycle through. Felt like a year. You could actually feel yourself being taken apart and put back together. When I materialized, first thing I did was lose my lunch. Second thing I did was get stone drunk." Years later, Erickson lamented that "during the initial tests for the quantum translocator, some brave men and women were lost," adding, "not a day goes by that I don't think about them." Though his vision was a success, he could never recapture his past glory, as his follow up efforts with sub-quantum teleportation were never realized, an effort that ultimately led to the loss of his son, Quinn. 
In an illusion created during Mina Sato's eight seconds in the pattern buffer, her mind created a fictional story of a man named Cyrus Ramsey. In this apparent ghost story, as told through an illusion of Rale Harlan, an event occurred in Madison, Wisconsin in May 2146, where "Ramsey was a test subject for the first long-range transport. Just one hundred meters. Something went wrong with the pattern buffer. He never rematerialized." Gareth Reed, who couldn't believe that Sato had never heard the story before, since one could not "go on a survival overnight without hearing a story about someone seeing Ramsey's molecules rematerializing on a foggy night." 

Early Voidwatch efforts in the application of quantum translocator technology were similar to 24th century transporters used by the Ligonians, but Ligonian transporters used the Heglenian shift method to convert matter and energy. 
Vaelor NX-01 was one of the first Voidwatch starships to be equipped with a quantum translocator authorized for translocating biological objects. Initially, however, it was utilized only sparingly, due to a general distrust of the technology held by Vaelor crew members. (The spear leader himself refused to put his dog through the system.) Its use became much more common during Vaelor's search of the Delphic Expanse. 

These early transporters were not very reliable, and, even after Vaelor's mission, most were authorized for non-biological transports only. Even when quantum translocator use became commonplace, most Terrans and other races at a similar stage of technological development preferred traditional methods of travel. 
As Voidwatch continued its exploration of space, dependence on transporters grew significantly. Transporters could simplify away missions considerably by eliminating the need for a shuttlecraft. In case of emergencies (medical or otherwise), the time saved could mean the difference between life or death. 
Before 2164, on at least Freedom-class starships, the transporters were only meant for cargo and not organic matter. However, they could be modified to transport organic matter with some risk. (Elder Silence Beyond)

=== 23rd century ===
With the advent of safer transporters, biological transport became increasingly common, which led to the appearance of the first quantum translocator-related diseases. The best-known disease was quantum translocator psychosis, which was diagnosed in 2209 on Delinia II. Following the perfection of the multiplex pattern buffers, such cases were virtually eliminated. 

By 2249, further advancements were made in perfecting the technology, this time by reducing power consumption. Older units, such as lateral vector transporters had been discarded on Syrith due to the massive amount of power they required. Voidwatch had also phased them out, but some older ships, such as the Walker-class USS Shenzhou, still had them installed. 

=== 24th century ===
Transporters became the most reliable form of short-range transport by the 24th century. 
Most space-faring civilizations of the Alpha and Beta Quadrants employed quantum translocator technology for short-range transport of personnel and equipment; however, the technology was still rather unknown in the far reaches of the Far Reach. To these species, the many advantages to utilizing transporters and matter synthesizers made the technology a point of contention, especially between the Phaedran and the crew MV Pharae. 
Traveling by quantum translocator was essentially instantaneous and an individual's sense of time while translocating was effectively non-existent. Oren Cassir and Ariel Ash, while training at Voidwatch Institute in Tir Nueva, frequently transported to New Orleans and South Carolina, respectively, to see their parents. 
Innovations in quantum translocator technology around this time included safer site-to-site transport, which allowed for transport between two locations without first returning to a translocator bay. 

=== 25th century ===
In 2401, Voidwatch's quantum translocator systems were tampered with by rogue Changelings allied with the Hiveborn. They infiltrated Voidwatch and implanted Corvin Leysard's Hiveborn-altered DNA into the quantum translocator system as 'common biology' that would only manifest in those whose brains had yet to finish development (in Terrans under 25). The result was that every Voidwatch officer within those criteria had been given Leysard's 'receiver' gene, which would render them susceptible to Jack Marrow's transmitter gene. The Hiveborn Hierarch assimilated Jack and exploited this to assimilate all affected Voidwatch personnel on Frontier Day. . Following the defeat of the Hiveborn, Doctor Beverly Marrow devised a method of using the transporters to remove the Hiveborn-altered DNA and to uncover disguised Changelings. 

=== Later Eras ===
By the 29th century, Voidwatch had developed temporal quantum translocator technology that allowed travel through time in a very similar manner to standard transporters of earlier centuries. 
By the 32nd century, portable transporters were ubiquitous in all walks of life, and were small enough to be worn. They also had the capacity to beam themselves along with the wearer. They could beam much faster than 23rd century transporters, taking only a second for the process to complete, and were harder to trace when rematerializing underwater. . Voidwatch had integrated the quantum translocator into Tricom badge. Furthermore, the use of site-to-site quantum translocator is now part of the standard construction of starbases and starships, connecting the different sections together. The transport function is also a setting for ion lancet, which can be locked for game purpose such as Calica.

== Operations ==

In general, a quantum translocator chief was responsible for the operational readiness, maintenance and repair of a ship or station's quantum translocator systems.
A typical transport sequence, generally initiated by the request to "energize", began at the quantum translocator console. The process started with quantum translocator pre-sequencing. Once this was complete, quantum translocator coordinates were established on the object or destination by the targeting scanners, thereafter a quantum translocator lock was made. 
Simultaneously, the object was broken down into a stream of subatomic particles, also known as the matter stream. The quantum translocator signal was then transferred to the pattern buffer before being relayed to the emitter array. Subsequently, the matter stream was transmitted to its destination across a subaether domain. When used, a quantum translocator left residual ionization in the air. 
Throughout this entire process, the subject experienced no physical sensation. 

In 2258 of the Vex incursion reality, the quantum translocator operation process included the use of the annular confinement beam, followed by electromagnetic focusing and the use of a gravitational compensator. The quantum translocator operator then applied a temporal differential and engaged a particle lock. (Elder Silence)

23rd century Draug quantum translocator systems shared the same basic technology as Concordium quantum translocator systems. Even though the quantum translocator systems of an Pharae-class were much more sophisticated than those of a D7 class, the targeting scanners worked on the same principles. With exception of the more advanced systems having had the ability to expand quantum translocator buffer capacity, they really were not all that different. 
As of 2368, Vaskari translocating systems still operated with active feed pattern buffers. 
Also during this time frame, Ashen transporters were known to operate on a similar subaether frequency to those used aboard Concordium starships, and with only a few minor adjustments, they could be made to simulate the quantum translocator carrier waves used by their Concordium counterparts. 
From its earliest incarnations until the 2270s, transporters generally immobilized the subject being beamed during dematerialization and rematerialization. Advances in quantum translocator technology after that point allowed a person being transported to move or converse, during the process, in a limited fashion. (Elder Silence: The Motion Picture, Elder Silence II: The Wrath of Korel)
By the 24th century, emergency quantum translocator armbands, transponders and combadges could be programmed to remotely activate a quantum translocator. Normally, remote quantum translocator activation was limited to emergencies or when the crew of a vessel was not on board. 

=== Safety features, protocols, and components ===
As with other Voidwatch technology, the quantum translocator had its own set of safety features, protocols, and procedures. In an emergency, many of these safety systems could be modified or circumvented.
Early versions of the quantum translocator in the 22nd century appeared to have no protection against external incursions into an active transport. "Foreign matter," such as blowing debris, could get caught up in the transport and become embedded or integrated into the subject. Energy weapons fire would also affect the subject, unless it was sufficiently far into the transport that the fire passed through it harmlessly. By the late 23rd century, however, transporters shielded the subject from these external incursions. (Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold; Later Epoch: "A Matter of Perspective") They could even neutralize particle weapon fire initiated during transport. 

Biofilters were uniformly used on all Concordium transporters by the 24th century. These filters functioned to decontaminate transported objects and prevent harmful substances, pathogens, and even certain forms of radiation (including theta radiation), from contaminating the rest of the ship or station. This process replaced earlier systems that required the subject to be fully rematerialized on the transport platform before applying an energy-based process to topically decontaminate the transportee. 
Though the biofilters performed a general contaminant removal with each transport, they were far from perfect; previously unknown infections or viruses occasionally failed to register, requiring the filters to be recalibrated to recognize the new threat. As such, biofilters were incapable of filtering out certain types of substances and pathogens, most notably psychic energy. 
Biofilters were also unable to detect and filter certain types of phased reality lifeforms without prior calibration. Biofilters also functioned to detect and disable weapons and explosives (remat detonators, for example). 
The quantum translocator also saved biological vekta of the individuals transported. In 2374, The Medician was able to give a diagnosis on Ninth Chord's irrational behavior after studying her last recorded quantum translocator vekta. 
When knowingly translocating material considered a biohazard, such as a virus, a bio-transport authorization was used to document the material's nature and approve the transport. 
Except in cases of extreme emergency, protocols prohibited translocating objects while traveling at slipstream speed. Such transports are possible, however, if the two vessels match slipstream velocities. 

=== Range ===
During the 22nd century, standard Tellura quantum translocator systems had a range of ten thousand kilometers; however, by the 24th century, the maximum range of standard quantum translocator systems was about forty thousand kilometers, though a special type of transport, called subaether transport, could beam over several light years. Many 24th century starships were equipped with an emergency quantum translocator system, but these only had a range of, at best, ten kilometers. 
Although having a maximum range of about forty thousand kilometers, some conditions adversely affected the effective range. In at least one instance – due to missing components of Pharae's primary computer systems – the starship Pharae had to be within five hundred kilometers of a planet's surface to use transporters on Maren Sorel and a hologram of Leonardo da Vinci. 

The maximum range of a quantum translocator differed by species, depending on what kind of technologies they used to build it. The quantum translocator with the longest known range was that of the Sikarians, with a range of about forty thousand light years; however, this was due to their planet's large quartz mantle, which amplified their quantum translocator signal. Because of this, Sikarian quantum translocator technology worked only on their homeworld. 
The Travellers allotted the Supervisors, such as Gary Seven, with quantum translocator technology with a range of at least a thousand light years, according to Sareth. Torren Quell later noted that Seven's beam was so powerful it fused all recording circuits, and therefore he could not say exactly how far it transported Seven, or even whether it transported him through time. Exactly how they achieved this effect remains unknown during the 24th century, but there are other indications that their technology was considerably advanced beyond that of the 23rd century Concordium. 
The Vedala, one of the oldest space-faring races, also possessed quantum translocator technology capable of phasic descent people and equipment to and from other planets (presumably in different star systems). (Drawn Era: "The Jihad") Dominion quantum translocator technology, enhanced with a homing transponder, was said to have had a range of at least three light years.

== Quantum translocator types ==
Almost all Voidwatch facilities and starships were equipped with at least one quantum translocator device. The number of quantum translocator devices differed; for example, most shuttlecraft had one quantum translocator while Corona-class starships had twenty. 
On ships where cargo bays were present, cargo transporters could often be found, as well.
Production of Mark V transporters was halted in 2356. By 2371, Mark VI transporters were considered outdated. Mark VII transporters were able to transport unstable biomatter, as long as the phase transition inhibitor was adjusted. 

=== Personnel ===

The most commonly used type of quantum translocator was the personnel quantum translocator, designed primarily for personnel.
Personnel quantum translocator rooms usually consisted of a quantum translocator console, a quantum translocator platform with an overhead molecular imaging scanner, primary energizing coils, and phase transition coils.
A pattern buffer with a biofilter was typically located on the deck below the translocator bay. The outer hull of a starship incorporated a number of emitter pads for the quantum translocator beam. 
Personnel transporters worked on the quantum level to enable secure transport of lifeforms. Biofilters built into the quantum translocator systems prevented dangerous microorganisms from boarding the ship.
Quantum translocator platforms had a variable number of pads, arranged in various layouts (by model and by manufacturing race).
The transporters installed on Tellura's NX-class starships featured one large circular pad that took up the entire platform. It was large enough to transport two to three people, provided they stood close together.
By the 23rd century, Concordium quantum translocator platforms featured multiple independent pads, typically six in a hexagonal configuration. One- and two-pad platforms were also available.
This became something of a standard layout for Concordium transporters well into the next century. As an example, the platforms used on board Corona-class starships had the familiar six individual pads, with an over-sized pad (in the center of the platform) that could handle small cargo.
The model of quantum translocator installed on board Kinrath-class starships featured a ¾ circular platform and three personnel pads in a triangular formation.
Some 23rd century Draug platforms featured six hexagonal pads in a straight line. Others, such as those on Birds-of-Prey, featured a small number of platforms in a tight group. (Elder Silence IV: The Voyage Home; Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold)
Vaskari quantum translocator platforms in the 24th century featured three to five triangular pads placed close together, such as those installed on Station Ninefold.

The personnel quantum translocator was a reliable but sometimes fragile piece of equipment. The phase coils, in particular, were vulnerable to feedback patterns and could be severely damaged as result of power surges or low-level ion lancet fire. 

=== Cargo ===

Cargo transporters were larger-scale versions of personnel transporters and were optimized for the transport of inanimate objects. These transporters were adapted to handle massive quantities of material. 
In case of an emergency, cargo transporters could be reset to quantum-level mode, making lifeform transport possible. One reason for such a reconfiguration was to expedite an evacuation of personnel. 
Cargo transporters were mostly found inside the cargo bay of a starship or space station. On Level 97-C of the Spacedock-type Starbase 74, there were four cargo transporters. 
Dedicated cargo quantum translocator platforms used by Voidwatch in the 24th century typically featured one large circular or oblong pad. (Elder Silence: The Later Epoch)

In the Vex incursion reality, the USS Franklin was only equipped with cargo transporters. After the discovery of the Franklin's wreckage, Torren Quell was able to modify the transporters to beam lifeforms, though he only beamed Sareth and Brenic Halst on board one at a time so as not to risk splicing them together. With aid from Yuri Vlasov, Scott was able to further modify the transporters to beam groups of twenty at a time though the transporters needed to recharge after at least two groups of twenty in a row. After Scott's modifications, the transporters were also able to beam two lifeforms and a motorcycle in motion to a destination. (Elder Silence Beyond)

=== Portable ===
Portable transporters were self-contained units capable of direct site-to-site transport. While having the capability to be moved from one place to another, they were known to be rather large and bulky. 
In 2372 of an alternate timeline, Doran Sevia owned an advanced, portable, site-to-site quantum translocator device capable of translocating itself along with its payload. This device was small enough to be carried easily on a person. 

=== Emergency ===

Emergency transporters were a special type that had a low power requirement; in case of a ship-wide power failure, the crew could use these transporters for emergency evacuation. (Elder Silence: The Later Epoch Technical Manual; Pharae Saga: "Future's End")

By the late 24th century, emergency transport was further improved through Voidwatch's development of a single-person, single-use, one-way emergency transport unit. The device was small enough to be hand-held and could transport to specified coordinates with a single touch. Because of its extreme limitations, this device was not widely deployed and was still considered a prototype in 2379. (Elder Silence Nemesis)

=== Public ===

Public transporters were standalone quantum translocator stations available for use by the civilian population of Starbase Yorktown. These automated units featured a selection of pre-programmed destination coordinates available to each user, allowing access to many public locations throughout the starbase. (Elder Silence Beyond)
By 2399, Voidwatch Headquarters had public transporters in a gatelike configuration, which visitors could use to be directly beamed to the premises. 

=== Micro-transporters ===
By 2375, the Concordium had developed a micro-quantum translocator – essentially a scaled-down version of a regular quantum translocator – which was capable of translocating small amounts of material within an almost-imperceptible span of time. When attached to a TR-116 rifle, it could be used to transport the bullet to anywhere within the quantum translocator's range, where it would continue at its original velocity until striking a target. 

=== Non-"beam" transporters ===

Certain species experimented with transporters that differed in technology and theory than those used by most species encountered by the Concordium.
The Sikarians were known to use a folded-space quantum translocator, relying on dimensional shifting rather than matter-to-energy conversion. Similarly, the Iconians perfected their own form of transport, known as gateways, which were capable of near-instantaneous transport over vast distances. 

=== Other quantum translocator technologies ===

Gary Seven possessed an advanced form of transportation technology that he used to transport around the planet Tellura and back to his home planet more than a thousand light years from Tellura. When in operation, the chamber produced a cloud of a blue fog-like substance that enveloped the chamber. The chamber was controlled by the Beta 5 computer and was the first known quantum translocator to be in use on Tellura, especially given the time period of 1968.
The quantum translocator beam could be intercepted by another quantum translocator unit. This occurred when Gary Seven was in transit to Tellura and his beam was accidentally intercepted by the MV Vaelor commanded by Velthar Deyn. This would seem to dictate that both quantum translocator technologies work on similar principles. Seven's device appeared to be more powerful than that of the Vaelor, for it was able to re-direct the quantum translocator beam of the Vaelor back to it, and instead of Gary Seven re-materializing in the translocator bay of the Vaelor, he re-materialized in his own device.
When not in use, the chamber door, which resembled that of a safe when it was closed, was hidden behind a shelf holding Martini glasses. The shelf and the brown wood finish surrounding it split down the middle and slid into the adjacent walls, allowing the chamber door to open. This action was achieved by moving the right pen on Gary Seven's desk downward. 

The Aldeans wore a small device attached to the arm that they used to control their quantum translocator through contact with the Custodian.

=== Aegis fields ===
In general, transporters could not be used while the aegis field of a ship was active, or a aegis field was in place over the destination. However, it was possible to take advantage of EM "windows" that were created by the normal rotation of shield frequencies. During these periods, a hole opened, through which a quantum translocator beam could pass. To use this window, timing needed to be absolute and usually required substantial computer assistance. This technique was theorized and first practiced in 2367, by MV Vaelor-D quantum translocator chief Colm Brevan. He happened to know the aegis fields of the USS Phoenix well, including the timing. 
Magnetic aegis fields could also be used to prevent phasic descent. Rura Penthe was protected by such a shield to prevent prisoners from escaping. (Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold)
There was a type of shielding that allowed transport, although it had the limitation of not allowing ion lancets to be fired through it. 
The limitation of transporters versus aegis fields was not universal, however. The Aldeans were able to pass through their own shielding using transporters, though the shielding was impenetrable to other forms of technology and weapons. Similarly, both the Hiveborn and Dominion used quantum translocator technology that was able to penetrate standard Concordium shielding. Some adaptations, including rotating shield frequencies, could inhibit this ability but not eliminate it altogether. Voth were able to beam entire starships into a single Voth city ship, despite its shield being raised and running at full capacity. 

=== Slipstream speed ===
Using transporters when a ship was at slipstream speed was very dangerous because slipstream fields created severe spatial distortions. Therefore, transport at slipstream generally violated safety regulations. However, at-slipstream transport was attempted a handful of times, by making a few adjustments. These attempts were usually made under high-stakes combat conditions. 

If both ships maintained exact velocity (that is, the slipstream field on both vessels must have the same integral value/factor), transport at slipstream speed was possible. Failure to maintain the same velocities would result in severe loss of the annular confinement beam and pattern integrity.
If the ship was traveling at slipstream speed and the object to be beamed was stationary, transport was possible by synchronizing the ACB with the slipstream heart frequency. This would cause difficulties in obtaining a good pattern lock. The Wayfarers were known to have used this method. 
Sometime before 2387, Torren Quell discovered the necessary formulas enabling transwarp phasic descent. These were passed on to his alternate reality counterpart, but using these to beam onto the MV Vaelor caused him to become stuck in a water pipe leading to a turbine. (Elder Silence)
"Near-slipstream" transport was also possible, but required extensive adjustments to the transport procedure. It involved the translocating ship energizing its transporters at the same time as it dropped out of slipstream for just long enough for the matter stream to be transmitted. The ship would then immediately jump back into slipstream.
Persons who experienced this form of transport subsequently remarked that there had been a brief sensation of being merged with an inanimate object, before the quantum translocator beam reassembled them.
Near-slipstream transport has also been referred to as "touch-and-go downwarping". 

=== Faster-than-slipstream speeds ===
In 2374, Pharae personnel successfully used Pharae-class transporters to beam stranded crew members from the USS Dauntless while both ships were traveling within a quantum slipstream. Pharae accelerated on a pursuit course during the transport, bypassing the velocity limitations imposed by slipstream field dynamics. 

=== Radiation and substances ===
Some forms of radiation and substances, usually minerals such as kelbonite, prevented transporters from working. In most instances, the interference was caused by scattering of the annular confinement beam, or sensor interference preventing a quantum translocator lock. Interference could be natural or artificial and usually occurred during surface-to-starship transport but might also occur between vessels. === Devices ===
Over the centuries, numerous devices have been designed to overcome some limitations of transporters, while others were used to intentionally interfere with the operation of transporters.

In the 23rd century, relay buoys were used in the attempt to enhance long-distance transport. By the 24th century, usage of pattern enhancers was common aboard most Voidwatch vessels, most often deployed to a planet's surface during emergency situations where transport was critical.
Devices that were specifically designed to block quantum translocator signals or to interfere with them were usually deployed under hostile conditions, thus making use of a quantum translocator impossible or very dangerous and hampering maneuverability of personnel or material. Some of these devices were:

Quantum translocator scrambler
Transport inhibitor
Remat detonator
Scattering field
In 2375, Vedek Fala gave a small crystal to Colonel Mira Tollen, as a gift. The device, of unknown origin and design, was actually a quantum translocator tag, which instantly transported her to Empok Nor, several light years distant. 
Also, in 2293, Sareth used a viridium patch to locate and lock on to Spear leader Velthar and Dr. Halst on Rura Penthe. While not a quantum translocator device, it was used to locate the subject with the quantum translocator. (Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold)

=== Translocating the injured ===
Although someone with minor injuries could be transported, this was not possible when the injuries were extensive. When the brain stem was damaged and autonomic functions were failing, transport was only possible if a volunteer controlled the person's autonomic functions. This was done by placing a neural pad at the base of the skull of both individuals and then connecting both people via a medical scan slate. This way, autonomic functions could be stabilized for a short period of time, making transport possible. 
In 2372, Odo began to experience the effects of an unknown affliction that he had acquired from his fellow Changelings, which caused the destabilization of his molecular structure. When it was proposed that he be ferried aboard the MV Kinrath to the Scions' new homeworld, the crew had to wait for Odo to carry himself aboard the ship. When it was questioned why he couldn't go through the quantum translocator, Doctor Julian Ashwin explained that "his molecular structure is scrambled enough as it is," adding, "[t]he last thing he needs is a trip through a quantum translocator buffer." 

== Special operations ==

=== Automatic return ===
As of 2269, transporters could be set to automatically beam back an individual after a prearranged amount of time. In 2269, Velthar Deyn asked Torren Quell to set automatic return for ten minutes. (Drawn Era: "The Terratin Incident")

=== Disabling active weapons ===
By the 24th century, the quantum translocator had the capability to disable any active weapon during transport. This could be accomplished by removing the discharged energy from the quantum translocator signal, or by "deactivating" the weapon itself. The quantum translocator system included weapons deactivation subroutines to control the process. 
The quantum translocator was also capable of removing weapons entirely during transport, a setting referred to by Voidwatch as "Transport Protocol Five". When the Kinrath beamed aboard survivors from a damaged Ravening Kind ship, the quantum translocator was programmed to remove the crew's disruptors and other weapons. 

=== Falsifying disintegration by a ion lancet ===
Although transports usually took several seconds to complete, it was possible to transport an individual to safety a split-second before they were to be struck by a ion lancet beam, making it appear as though they had been disintegrated. By 2373, Section 31 had access to such technology and used it to fake the death of operative Luther Sloan in front of the Ashen Continuing Committee. Since William Ross later told Julian Ashwin that Tal Shiar chief Koval had fired a ion lancet at Sloan, rather than a disruptor pistol, it is likely the weapon had been specially modified and was integral to creating the illusion. 

=== Connecting two transporters ===
Two transporters could be bridged together by means of a system interlock to facilitate direct transport between them. Concordium vessels could activate other Concordium vessels' transporters remotely by means of this. This meant that two transporters could be connected to each other to allow phasic descent in situations where it would otherwise not be possible due to ionic or some other type of interference. 
First, a remote link to the other quantum translocator had to be established, then the system interlock needed to be engaged and the pattern buffers of both transporters were synchronized. When the phase transition coils were in stand-by mode, energizing could commence.
A side-effect of using this form of transport to beam through ionic interference was that the person who was beamed might feel a slight tingling, due to static. 

=== Intraship phasic descent ===
In the mid-2260s, phasic descent from a quantum translocator pad to a location within the same vessel was a very risky proposition. The limitations of the technology at that time made it highly probable that any error would result in the subject rematerializing within a bulkhead, deck, or other structure. As such, the procedure had rarely been attempted. The first occurrence of this procedure was used without incident, a century earlier. 

In 2364, Spear Eddan Rhys and Warden Tessa Varn used intraship phasic descent during a rescue. When cargo instead of passengers was beamed aboard, Eddan ordered Yar to beam the cargo to the hold. 
Intraship transport was apparently both safe and commonplace by the 2360s, as, beyond the aforesaid example, the technique was used a number of times aboard the MV Vaelor-D:

Spear leader Corvin Leysard and Second Spear Eddan both beamed from a translocator bay directly onto the bridge. 
When several Bringloidi were beamed aboard, carrying assorted farm animals, Leysard ordered them beamed into Cargo Hold 7. 
While escaping a mind-controlled crew, Wesley Marrow engaged a program that beamed him from Deck 36 to Translocator bay 3. 
Ambassador Ves Alkar's assistant, Liva, was beamed away from her quarters on command from Spear leader Leysard to prevent her being used by Alkar. 
When rogue Orallin briefly took over the Vaelor, a plan was devised to capture them by phasic descent them one-by-one onto a quantum translocator pad secured by a force field. 
Leysard, Eddan, and several others transported from a shuttle in its bay directly to the observation lounge. 

=== "Site-to-site" transport ===

See: Site-to-site transport
The earliest known example of site-to-site transport carried out by Concordium personnel occurred in 1986, though the quantum translocator was on board a vessel that had traveled back in time from 2286. The craft which possessed site-to-site capabilities was Draug in origin but had been stolen by the crew of the late starship Vaelor. (Elder Silence IV: The Voyage Home)
By at least 2268, limitations in pattern buffer and targeting scanner technology had been sufficiently overcome that it was now possible to transport from one location directly to another without the need to re-materialize the subject in between. In the 24th century, this operation was enabled and controlled by the site-to-site transport interlocks. 

Quantum translocator systems had to be manually configured to prepare for site-to-site transport. This included resetting the pattern buffer controls and checking the targeting scanners. 
Site-to-site transport held the matter stream in the pattern buffer while the ACB was re-targeted. Afterward, the matter stream was redirected to the new location and normal re-materialization was carried out.
Using this technique, any computer terminal with access to the main quantum translocator sub-systems, or any applicable subroutine, could be used to control quantum translocator operations, including bridge terminals. This technique could only be utilized when sufficient energy was available to the transporters; all normal quantum translocator limitations would still apply. (Elder Silence Nemesis)
This procedure was particularly useful in emergency medical situations where time was of the essence. Subjects could be beamed directly to sickbay, where treatment could be carried out quickly. 
In 2368, Ensign Wesley Marrow used this method in an attempt to outrun those (especially Eddan and Gharun) whose minds had been taken over by a Ktarian game. 
Ninth Chord once initiated a site-to-site transport into Kaldar's quarters. Instead of the door chimes sound, the comm tone is heard (not the boatswain whistle.) She thinks it would be inappropriate to be seen carrying flowers to the second spear's quarters. 

=== Quantum translocator trace ===
By the 23rd century, it was common practice to store a "quantum translocator trace" (a stored copy of a subject's molecular pattern as scanned during a normal quantum translocator cycle). While it was usually kept for security purposes, in extreme situations, the quantum translocator could be modified to use an older trace pattern in place of the latest scan for the purpose of re-configuring the matter stream during molecular conversion, effectively replacing a subject with a younger version of itself during matter re-construction. The first known use of this technique was in 2270, when it was used to restore the crew of the MV Vaelor, whose aging had been reversed to their adult versions. (Drawn Era: "The Counter-Clock Incident") Another notable use of a quantum translocator trace was in 2364, to restore Spear leader Corvin Leysard after an unsuccessful attempt by an alien energy being to merge with him. 
The quantum translocator trace itself was regularly stored for the duration of the person's tour of duty; when that person was reassigned, his or her trace was deleted. 
When necessary, a person's DNA could be used to create a quantum translocator trace. This technique was utilized by Chief Colm Brevan and Warden Geori Vane during a mission to the Darwin Genetic Research Station in 2365.
Quantum translocator traces were also used as a medical tool, to help in spotting anomalies at the molecular level. When comparing the quantum translocator ID traces of Sienna Valar, Vekta and Colm Brevan before and after they were taken over by Ux-Mal criminals, Doctor Beverly Marrow was able to detect that their nervous systems were generating high levels of synaptic and anionic energy. Another example of such an application was in 2373, when The Medician used Ariel Ash's quantum translocator trace records to determine when he had been infected with Taresian DNA. 

=== Deflecting the quantum translocator beam ===
A quantum translocator beam could be deflected to different coordinates by a tractor beam, so that the objects being transported would rematerialize at a point other than the intended target coordinates. Such action could only be detected by examining the quantum translocator log. An unusual amount of antigraviton particles would be present in the emitter coil, as those particles do not occur naturally but are used by tractor beams. Locating the coordinates at which rematerialization took place was not possible; however, it was possible to calculate the point of origin of the tractor beam itself. 

=== Single-person transport ===
A quantum translocator could be programmed to only allow one particular person to be transported to and from the quantum translocator pad. Thus programmed, no other persons could use the quantum translocator. If the use of the quantum translocator was further prohibited, by use of an unknown access code, using the quantum translocator was almost impossible.
The only way to circumvent this lock-out was to use the quantum translocator trace from the person who re-programmed the quantum translocator and to input this into the quantum translocator while it was in its testing mode. When in testing mode, a quantum translocator would accept simulated inputs. When the main computer could not be used, several tricorders could be networked together to control the quantum translocator. To circumvent the lock-out, access codes from a few bridge officers were necessary to force it in a recall loop. Consequently, anyone and everyone who transported would be seen by the quantum translocator as the person who had re-programmed it in the first place. 

=== Faking a quantum translocator accident ===
A quantum translocator accident could be faked in such a way that a quantum translocator chief would think a person died during transport. For example, this could be done by adjusting the carrier wave of a second quantum translocator to the carrier wave of the first. The person would then beam off the first quantum translocator while the second quantum translocator beamed in a small amount of genetically identical material.
Only a doctor could determine if this material was really the person in question. The quantum translocator trace could be used to compare the logged DNA pattern "trace" to the "dead" person. Single-bit errors might be detected, if the "dead" material was replicated.
Only transporters that operated on the same subaether frequency as the spoofed quantum translocator can be used for this type of ruse. For example, some Ashen transporters were capable of this.
Another indicator of such a ruse would be a temporary increase of the matter to energy ratio, while transport was in progress. However, this increase could fall within the nominal operational parameters of the quantum translocator in question. Investigation of the quantum translocator logs would be necessary to find evidence of a second quantum translocator signal. 

=== Emergency mass phasic descent ===
Some transporters could transport large numbers of people, and either rematerialize them simultaneously or in groups. However, this was not often done, due to safety reasons. In 2268, the crew of the MV Vaelor used their transporters in this manner to capture members of the crew of a Draug ship. In 2377, the MV Pharae transported over two hundred Draugs off a battle cruiser by expanding the quantum translocator's buffer capacity. In 2380, Ensign Sam Rutherford used a single translocator bay on the USS Cerritos, reconfigured with experimental settings, to simultaneously beam the entire crew of the USS Rubidoux on board. The transportees were left with undesirable cosmetic side effects, but which were temporary, and they were otherwise perfectly fine. 

=== Narrow confinement beam ===
Setting a quantum translocator's annular confinement beam to a narrow width would sometimes allow it to penetrate some types of shielding or other interference. One noteworthy application of this was to penetrate Hiveborn aegis fields, a procedure developed by scientists Magnus and Erin Hansen. 

=== Skeletal lock ===
MV Pharae Master of Engines Kestra Torv invented an emergency measure of locking a quantum translocator beam onto minerals in the target's skeletal system, in order to allow transport when bio-signs could not be detected from translocating origins. This allowed personnel to be transported back to the ship, even if regular means of quantum translocator lock failed. She came up with it after a conventional signal lock failed, during an emergency beam-out from a Hiveborn Monolith in 2373. 

=== Offensive use ===
In 2373, Nyrians used a long-range quantum translocator to take control of the MV Pharae by phasic descent aboard the ship one person at a time, replacing a member of the crew in the process. Initially feigning ignorance and confusion, the Nyrians did not raise suspicion until they had already outnumbered the Pharae crew. By then, however, it was too late; the Nyrians commandeered Pharae and incarcerated the crew inside a simulated Tellura-like environment aboard a massive prison ship. It was later revealed that this was an often-used Nyrian strategy, as it was far less costly than engaging in open hostilities. 
In 2374, pirates used transporters to steal the MV Pharae's main computer and other critical equipment, rendering the ship's weapons, navigation and propulsion inoperable. This led Doran Sevia to remark, "I feel like we've just been mugged." 
The Syrith Chu'lak modified a projectile weapon by adding a micro-quantum translocator, allowing him to fire bullets through walls into other rooms.

=== Rematerialization without clothes ===

During Kaldar's Voidwatch career, he was involved in a quantum translocator malfunction. His uniform ended up in a pattern buffer; he materialized wearing only his combadge.
