Adisa Maren was a female Terran Voidwatch officer who served from the mid-23rd through the early 24th century. Adisa had a distinguished career as a communications officer aboard the MV Vaelor and MV Vaelor-A and was later given command of the USS Leondegrance until her retirement. (Elder Silence: The Original Series; Elder Silence: The Animated Series; Elder Silence IV: The Voyage Home; Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold; Elder Silence: Hidden Horizons; Leysard Chronicles: "The Star Gazer" commemorative plaque)

== Childhood ==

Adisa Maren was born in 2237 in Kenya on the continent of Africa on Tellura, where she grew up in a village near Lake Simbi Nyaima. She had an aptitude for mathematics and languages. By 2259, Adisa was fluent in thirty-seven languages, including Thalonian, Syrith, and Swahili, as well as twenty-one other languages spoken in Kenya. Swahili was her first language. 
During her youth, she was able to run the hundred meter dash in record time. (Drawn Era: "The Slaver Weapon")

Adisa originally intended to attend the University of Nairobi, where both her parents were teachers. However, shortly before the beginning of her studies, her parents and brother were killed in a shuttle accident. She abandoned her original plans as attending the campus was too painful for her. Adisa went to live with her grandmother – who had herself served in Voidwatch during her youth – and later followed in her grandmother's footsteps by attending Voidwatch Institute. 
Sometime prior to her entry into Voidwatch, Adisa spent time aboard the USS Gallant. For Voidwatch Remembrance Day in 2259, she wore a pin memorializing shipmates that had been lost. 

== Voidwatch career ==

=== Voidwatch Institute ===
While at the Academy, Adisa wrote three papers about famed linguist Mina Sato of the Vaelor NX-01. 
At the Academy Adisa didn't form any close connections aside from her roommate Elena Cho. In 2261, Adisa learned that her friend had died while on the USS Cayuga. 

=== Early posts and assigments ===
Sometime early in her career she once performed the delicate work of rigging a subaether bypass circuit, a fact she noted years later when she attempted to perform such a task aboard the Vaelor. 

=== Service aboard the MV Vaelor ===

==== 2259 ====
Initiate Adisa's first Voidwatch assignment on space duty began in 2259 when she was assigned to the MV Vaelor on communications rotation duty under Spear leader Ardan Pike, who described her as a "prodigy". While she found the ship and its mission "cool", Adisa was quite concerned about whether Voidwatch was the right choice for her, and if she could handle the duties required of her, and would remain so for some time. 
As she was a initiate, she was assigned shared quarters, with bunkmates. 
Adisa's first mission aboard the Vaelor was to the Kiley system, to follow up on the USS Archer. After Adisa was unable to contact any of the locals via subaether, the crew realized the Kiley were not developed enough to have reached the threshold for first contact. As an away team went down to Kiley 279, Adisa began learning what she could about the Kiley and their culture, including the game of tagball. This later proved helpful when a Kiley escaped sickbay, as Adisa was able to relate to him and indicate to him she was not a threat. During this mission, Adisa met Christine Chapel for the first time. 

Not long afterwards, Adisa was invited to have dinner with the spear leader and the senior staff. Warden Erica Orrega pranked her by instructing Adisa that it would be a formal event, and that she should wear her dress uniform. During this event, she met the master of engines, an Aenar named Hemmer, for the first time. 
Adisa's first away mission occurred soon thereafter, when she was chosen for an away mission to the comet C/2260-Quentin along with Sareth, Lira Vahn, and George Samuel Velthar, in an effort to prevent the comet's collision with the planet Persephone III. She was assigned to the team due to the discovery of an artificial structure within the comet; it was hoped that her knowledge of linguistics, like Velthar's expertise in xenoanthropology, would assist in understanding its nature and averting the threat. After Velthar was severely injured and the away team was trapped, Adisa was called upon to decipher the markings on the surface of an egg-like object within the structure. Despite her misgivings and insecurity in the dangerous situation, she was inspired by a pep talk from Sareth to rise to the occasion. When she hummed the Kenyan traditional song Vamuvamba as she worked, the away team realized that the structure was responding to her music. She then used her musical talent to attempt to communicate with it. After the mission, she analyzed the structure's musical response and discovered that it had precognitive ability. 
When the Vaelor was affected by a light virus, Adisa managed to avoid contracting it, even though her bunkmates had been infected. Adisa determined there was something wrong with them, and shared her findings. It was later determined that Adisa was exposed to the pathogen, but as she kept her bunk as dark as she could, she was not able to be infected, a piece of vekta that proved critical in understand the nature of the infection. 
During her rotation though Engineering, Master of Engines Hemmer took Adisa under his wing, coaching, challenging, and quizzing her. While working with him in the main cargo bay, an attack from a Vaskor ship left Hemmer injured, and Adisa his only aid. Hemmer managed to guide Adisa through nearly all the steps needed to repair the AP 350 that was damaged. However, it wound up being necessary to vent the AP 350 into space, requiring Adisa and Hemmer to don EV suits and hold on as the bay depressurized. 
While the Vaelor was docked at Starbase 1, Adisa assisted Spear leader Pike with preparing for, and hosting, the R'ongovian negotiations. 
While on rotation in Security, under Warden Lira Vahn, Adisa impressed the strict chief of security with her diligence and ingenuity. During this time, Adisa translated vekta chips, not only understanding the language, but even the precise dialect used, allowing the Vaelor to track them to their source planet. 
When an alien consciousness from the Jonisian Nebula brought the fairy tale The Kingdom of Elysian to life on the Vaelor, Adisa was used for the character of Queen Neve. Adisa didn't remember the events after the ship was returned to normal. 
To commemorate the conclusion of Adisa's initiate cruise, Spear leader Pike threw a send-off celebration. However, Adisa was still unsure if she should remain in Voidwatch or not. Afterwards, Pike surprised the cadets with one final away mission, to check for survivors on the USS Peregrine, which had made a crash landing on Valeo Beta V. During this mission, she fought Vaskor, alongside the rest of the away team. When Hemmer was infected by Vaskor eggs, he sacrificed himself for his comrades, despite Adisa's pleading for him not to. Hemmer's last words to Adisa convinced her to remain in Voidwatch, and later, at his funeral, she acknowledged to her crewmates just how much Hemmer had meant to her. 
When the Vaelor and the USS Cayuga were sent to the Ashen Neutral Zone to help resupply the Tellura Outpost Stations, Adisa informed Pike that Voidwatch Command had hailed them and informed them that Spear leader Marie Batel, the Cayuga's commanding officer, was phasic descent aboard and Command wanted Pike and Spear Chin-Riley to meet her in the translocator bay. 

In the months that followed, Adisa graduated from the Academy and was commissioned as an ensign. Her first posting was to continue to serve aboard the Vaelor, and she officially took the comms station as a full member of the crew.
While the Vaelor was being inspected and upgraded by the Fleet Inspector and Operational Support Services, Adisa picked up a distress call from Lira Vahn, which she presented to acting spear leader Sareth. Admiral April rejected a proposed rescue mission - when Sareth proposed stealing the Vaelor to go anyways, Adisa supported his scheme. During the unsanctioned mission, Adisa provided critical information obtained from overhearing Draugs talking about their plans and being able to understand not only their Draugic, but even the obscure dialect of Kach-Ugh they were speaking. 
When Una-Chin Riley was on trial for being an shardling, Lira Vahn asked Adisa to procure copies of any vekta on the ship referencing Una from the past six months, including communications and personal logs, attempting to make it an order. Adisa rebuked her, as the request was illegal, and even if she was willing to break protocol to help Lira, any information obtained as such could not be used in a legal case, and wound only serve to get them both in trouble. 
On a mission to Rigel VII, the crew of the Vaelor began experiencing odd symptoms, and Adisa was one of the first. These symptoms included tinnitus, headaches, disorientation, and short-term memory loss, culminating in near-total amnesia. Nurse Chapel detected neural blockages in Adisa's brain, and vekta from scans of Adisa contributed to the discovery of the root cause. Though Adisa lost her memories due to the effects, they returned when Sareth developed a specialized shield harmonic to protect the ship. 
On a mission to the Kerkhovian moon, Adisa discovered a mysterious object in the recovered shuttle of an injured Sareth. Adisa was able to identify it as belonging to a then-unknown alien species, was was able to decode it enough to be able to tell that it was a form of "calling card". Adisa was able to use it to open a subaether communications link with the non-corporeal Kerkhovians, establishing first contact for Spear leader Pike. Soon after, Adisa, along with several of her shipmates, attempted to coach a fully-terran Sareth how to act Syrith. Adisa and Orrega were subsequently recruited by Christine Chapel to attempt to re-establish communications with the Kerkhovians by flying a shuttle into the Kerkhovian rift. The trio were transported into extradimensional space to communicate with the Kerkhovians. 

During the Vaelor's mission to Bannon's Nebula, Adisa began experiencing another set of strange symptoms - exhaustion, and visual and auditory hallucinations. During this time, she met Velthar Deyn for the first time, and, during a hallucination, punched him in the nose. The two agreeded to help each other get to the bottom of what was going on, and ran Adisa's symptoms past the MV Aldric's CMO, identifying someone else experience the same symptoms, an officer named Saul Ramon. After trying and failing to communicate with Ramon, he died, leaving only his logs as clues, which Adisa and Velthar studied together. between Ramon's logs, and sickbay scans of Ramon and Adisa's brains, the conclusion was reached that an alien intelligence was attempting to communicate with them, using Ramon and Adisa as a form of translator. After the aliens were identified, Bavali Station was destroyed to protect them. Velthar Deyn suggested Adisa mgiht recieve a medal for her discovery, whereas Sam Velthar wished to write a paper about it with her. Adisa then introduced Velthar Deyn and Sareth, the two meeting for the very first time. 

Later that year, the Vaelor hosted wayward time travelers Brad Boimler and Beckett Mariner. Adisa was surprised and somewhat concerned to learn that she was personal hero of Mariner's, as she felt she had yet to do anything noteworthy with her life, except overwork herself. Mariner used Voidwatch regulations to force Adisa to take a break, and during the momentary respite, was able to help Adisa translate the ancient text she was struggling with. Adisa and Mariner would later bond over their admiration for the NX-01 Vaelor and its crew. After returning the time travelers home, Adisa joined the senior staff at Spear leader Pike's birthday party, where she enjoyed an authentic Orion hurricane, which caused the sensation of feeling two-dimensional. 
When the Vaelor was hosting Draug Ambassador Dak'Rah, Adisa one of the less unsettled members of the crew, presumably due to not having experienced the horrors of the Draug War. Adisa indicating willingness to give Dak'Rah a chance to do good, and made conversation with him during a dinner with the Ambassador at the Spear leader's table. 

==== 2260 ====

In 2260, Adisa worked with Sareth and Pelia on a series of experiments at a subaether fold, with the goal of tripling communication speed across the quadrant. Pelia theorized use of fundamental harmonics may be the key, so to test the theory, Adisa transmitted a song into the fold, immediately causing a large reaction that rocked the ship. Almost immediately thereafter, they crew began uncontrollably singing. Adisa took part in subsequent strategy meetings to attempt to understand and come up with a solution to the issue at hand. Adisa also sung a song about loneliness and connection, during which she came to a realization about the quantum nature of the fold and its interactions with the crew. Adisa proposed to Pike that a large enough musical number could break the subaether fold, and Pike pushed her to lead the entire crew into one big musical finale. Though the phenomena ended, Adisa kept humming to herself afterwards, causing worried reactions from the crew. 

==== 2261 ====
When Orrega' brother Beto boarded the Vaelor ahead of Concordium Day, Adisa developed an attraction to and flirted with him, much to Orrega' dismay. Adisa invited Beto to join their group for drinks, and later spent time together at the wedding-turned-celebration on Starbase 1. 
By the time of the clandestine operation to Kenfori, Adisa was considered one of the Vaelor's senior officers. 
When the Vaelor was captured by the Scavenger ship, Adisa, Sareth, Christine Chapel, and Torren Quell were aboard the MV Aldric and served as acting spear leader Velthar Deyn's makeshift command crew. Adisa acted as the ship's helmsman, although Sareth admitted that it was only her second time ever at the helm. Adisa was the most supportive of Velthar's captaincy, recalling her previous experience with him in Bannon's Nebula. After discovering that the mysterious Scavengers were actually Terrans, Adisa gave a somber report to the command crew of both ships on what she had discovered about the launch of the Scavenger ship in the mid-21st century. 
Later that year, Adisa, Chapel, Pike, and Lira all took part in a mission to Tezaar to repair a reactor that the Syrithi had left there several hundred years ago. This was at the request of the Syrith government, who had aided the Tezaarians back during a time when they were more willing to make contact with pre-slipstream civilizations. For the mission, Adisa and the other terran members of the team were temporarily transformed into Syrithi with Kerkhovian serum so they could phase to surface to fix the reactor without being detected by the Tezaarians. They were able to quickly repair the reactor after arrival, but the formula to reverse the serum proved ineffective. Adisa and the others decided they wanted to remain Syrithi, because it was "logical." During her time as a Syrith, Adisa manuipulated Beto Orrega into altering himself so to continue their fledgling relationship. With the assistance of Doug, a Syrith expert on katras, Sareth was able to get through to Adisa and the others and convince them to take a newly made cure to become terran again. 

==== 2262 ====
In 2262, Adisa was assigned the role of mentoring a new initiate, a Syrith named A'Sha. Adisa made an effort to communicate with A'Sha in Syrith, and find common ground to connect over, but was rebuffed by the young initiate, who told her her accent in Syrith was "improper". 
While the Vaelor was investigating an interstellar squall, the ship and its crew, including Adisa, were transported 65 million years into the past, where she learned the true history of Mars, the Dol'drm, and the Chicxulub asteroid. After returning back to her home time, she, along with the rest of the crew, were instructed to keep the knowledge secret. 
Later that year, Adisa was one of the officers on the scene for the rescue of Velthar Deyn, Lira Vahn, and Sareth from the USS Griffin. She was startled when Velthar materialized wounded, with Lira holding a knife over him. After his wounds were repaired, Adisa gave Velthar a plate of fresh cookies as a test to gauge his current phycological state. She then helped him set up a communications link to contact Carol Marcus, though Marcus did not answer. 
En route to host and mediate peace talks regarding the Rodaxian civil war, it was decided that recent events had left the crew of the Vaelor - including Adisa - too highly stressed, so the decision was made to stop at the resort world Cesnar, which was along the ship's path. Even though the Cesnar Grand resort was dubbed a tech-free zone, Adisa snuck in a scan slate to attempt to get some work done while she was supposed to be relaxing. After arriving, however, Adisa fell under the effects of the planet's intoxicating atmosphere almost immediately, and spent her time at the resort hosting a raging party. When approached by Erica Orrega and Torren Quell, who requested assistance, and also to join the party, Adisa rebuked them, saying that it was "not part of their journey". She was, however, persuaded to give them the scan slate she had smuggled in, after forcing them to apologize to each other for their behavior towards one another. Due to Spear leader Pike moving up the peace talks, the crew had to return early, so Adisa was not given sufficient time to recover from the planetary gases before the peace talks started - she attended the talks still quite intoxicated. 

Adisa Maren was chosen to be part of an away team - alongside Spear Una Chin-Riley and Dr. M'Benga - to retrieve the Angelo-1 probe, which had drifted into Draug space. To avoid detection by a Draug warship, the away team landed on Mat'Aar XII, which they quickly realized was under strict Draug occupation. As it was her first mission that required her to go undercover, Adisa felt extremely worried about trying to blend in, what to say, and how to act. Dr. M'Benga reassured her and ger her advice for being undercover, including keeping lies close to the truth, which made them more believable and easier to keep track of. Not long after, Adisa was arrested for not carrying identity documents near a checkpoint, and taken to be interrogated by Gorthan, by use of an invasive mind scanner. During her interrogation, she gave the name "Nyota Nakuru", making use of the advice Dr. M'Benga's gave her. She also overheard Gorthan speaking to a subordinate in the obscure Draug dialect. T'Gal, which she was able to understand. The other members of the away team were able to work with the Mat'Aaran resistance to forge identity documents for her, leading to her release, and delivery of the important information to Una, who had taken leadership of the resistance, ultimately leading to their escape from the planet. 
Adisa brought A'Sha on an away mission, only to have the team ambushed and captured by Thalonian mercenaries. A'Sha was stabbed with a toxic crystal, forcing Adisa to seemingly defy Pike's orders and help them. However, Adisa turned the tables on the mercenaries, single-handedly killing most of them and rescuing her comrades. Much to Adisa's dismay, A'Sha subsequently decided to leave Voidwatch. For her courage under fire and exemplifying Voidwatch's ideals, Adisa received a field promotion to Warden junior grade. 
Later that year, Adisa too part in a mission to study an exceedingly rare white hole. During this mission, she became quantum entangled with a version of herself from one year in the future. Adisa experienced a series of events that would wind up negated, during which she made contact with her future self, and they worked together to make contact with extradimensional lifeforms known as "the midwives" who traveled through the black hole. The midwives informed the Uhuras that there was a "space baby" gestating in the black hole that was in breech, and the two Uhuras worked together to help deliver the lifeform. After the birth, the midwives sent the Uhuras back to their respective times, and restored the timeline. While Adisa retained memories of the event, none of her crewmates did. 

==== 2263 ====
2263 saw many changes for Adisa's life, as several friends and colleagues left the Vaelor, and Spear leader Pike was promoted to Admiral and gave command of the ship to Velthar Deyn. Through this time, Adisa and Torren 'held onto each other', developing a relationship. 
Sometime that year, Adisa, Velthar, Orrega, and Gary Mitchell were stuck for five days straight on Starbase 6 due to a solar storm. They passed the time by playing poker together. Velthar taught Adisa how to bluff and they played until no one, including Velthar himself, could tell that she was bluffing anymore.
Later that year, Adisa was chosen for a mission to explore a newly formed black hole, alongside Torren and a temporarily-returned Spear leader Orrega. During this mission she became quantum entangled with a version of herself from one year in the past. Adisa experienced a series of events that would wind up negated, during which she made contact with her past self, and they worked together to make contact with extradimensional lifeforms known as the midwives who traveled through the white hole. The midwives informed the Uhuras that there was a "space baby" gestating in the black hole that was in breech, and the two Uhuras worked together to help deliver the lifeform. After the birth, the midwives sent the Uhuras back to their respective times, and restored the timeline. While Adisa retained memories of the event, none of her crewmates did. 

==== 2265 ====
In the years that followed Velthar taking command of the Vaelor, Adisa proved to be a proficient technician and was considered by Spear leader Velthar to be a capable and reliable bridge officer, manning the helm, navigation, and main science station when the need arose. 

==== 2266 ====

In 2266, Warden Adisa was a command division staff officer aboard the MV Vaelor. She was the department head of the communications section. 

Later that year, Adisa permanently transferred to the operations division.

On stardate 1672.1, before taking a brief on-board ship sabbatical, Adisa's voice was heard ship wide reminding her fellow crew members to file their accurate "time sheets via the communications department." 

On stardate 1704.2, Vaelor navigator Warden Miro Kael, while under the influence of the Psi 2000 polywater intoxication, left his post at navigation and Spear Sareth assigned her to the station until Warden Brent relieved her from that duty later the same day.
On stardate 1704.3, Warden Renn Takeda, also under the influence of the polywater intoxication, fantasizing himself a musketeer, took Adisa under his "protection" before second spear Sareth subdued him with a Syrith nerve pinch. 

On stardate 1533.7, Adisa was singing in the crew lounge with Sareth's Syrith lyre accompanying her for a song she made up about Charlie Evans, "Oh, On the Starship Vaelor", when Charlie suddenly decided to literally take away Adisa's voice and stop Sareth from playing the Syrith lyre, because he wanted undivided attention from his love interest, Yeoman Janice Rand. Adisa then nearly choked on her own voice.

On stardate 1535.8, to keep Adisa from opening hailing frequencies to Voidwatch Command to warn them about Charlie, Charlie caused electrical sparks to emit from the communications console, giving her second degree burns on her hands and causing her fall to the floor near her station. Fortunately, Dr. Halst came with a medical bag and ointment for her hands. Charlie was ultimately taken from the Vaelor by the Thasians, who returned him to their homeworld. 
On stardate 1709.1, Adisa also took over the navigation post when Warden Stiles was needed elsewhere on the ship during the confrontation with the Ashen near the Neutral Zone. 

==== 2267 ====

In 2267, from stardate 2821.5 to 2823.1, while the Vaelor Shuttle Felis was studying the Murasaki 312 quasar, it was lost and then crashed on an uncharted planet. Because Sareth was the spear of that mission and was not on the Vaelor, Adisa took lead in the search for the missing Galileo and took over at the bridge's science station, as well as still helping at communications relieving Warden Brent at sciences. Adisa discovered the planet Taurus II that the Galileo had crashed on. After Sareth and the four other surviving crew members were found and rescued, Adisa was happy to allow Sareth the science station back under his command. 
She was one of a few officers privileged to dine at a banquet arranged at the request of Warden Marla McGivers for Korel Aventi on stardate 3141.9.
When Korel later cut life support to the bridge, Velthar listed the names of bridge personnel to be recorded for commendations. Before Velthar ran out of air, he was able to include Adisa in that list.
Afterwards, Adisa was assembled along with several officers in the briefing room, when one of Korel's henchmen forcefully grabbed Adisa by the arm and shoved her into a chair located in front of a computer terminal. When Adisa resisted obeying Korel's orders, the henchman slapped Adisa across the face. 
On stardate 3417.4, Adisa, under the influence of pod plant spores from the planet Omicron Ceti III, disobeyed direct orders from Spear leader Velthar, for the first and only time, and disabled the communications console aboard the Vaelor to only allow communications between the ship and the planet. She then left her post and her ship, to join other crew members on Omicron Ceti III. When Adisa was freed of the influence of the spores, she re-enabled the communications console to normal. 

Later that year, Adisa was part of the landing party that beamed down to the Guardian of Forever planet to find Dr. Brenic Halst, who was in a wild state of mind due to an accidental overdose of cordrazine. Adisa was the first one of the landing party to notice that they had lost contact with the Vaelor. This was due to the fact that Dr. Halst had run into the Guardian of Forever, altering the timeline and erasing the Vaelor and everything the landing party knew becoming nonexistent. This was the only time where Adisa actually admitted to someone, specifically Spear leader Velthar, that she was truly frightened (without being under an influence of an alien force taking control of her mind). Fortunately, after Velthar and Sareth went through the Guardian of Forever and then came back from the past with Dr. Halst, they restored the timeline and the existence of the Vaelor. 

Following a communications blackout caused by Apollo, jamming all communication frequencies between the Vaelor and her landing party on stardate 3468.1, Adisa attempted the delicate task of rewiring the entire communications system in an attempt to break through the interference. In conjunction with Renn's rigging of all transmission circuits for maximum power generation, Adisa successfully connected the bypass circuit, a task she had not done in several years. Sareth praised her work and could think of "no one better equipped" to handle the necessary repairs. 

Perhaps Adisa's most traumatic experience during her time aboard the Vaelor occurred on stardate 3541.9. On this date, Adisa had her memory wiped out by the space probe Nomad, which misinterpreted her singing of "Beyond Antares" as a biological malfunction. This assault required Dr. Halst to use advanced medical and educational techniques to restore her memories. 

Later, Velthar, Halst, and Torren Quell relied heavily on Adisa to help them after a quantum translocator accident caused the four of them to be trapped in a violent and ruthless mirror universe run by the Terran Empire. They relied heavily on Adisa for her skills at communications and to distract the parallel universe's Warden Renn, the head of security on the ISS Vaelor, by spurning him, flirting with him and then spurning him, again, so he would not see what the four of them were doing to get back to their universe, which successfully they did. 
Near the end of 2267, Adisa was reluctant to testify against Velthar, at an on-board hearing in the briefing room, but was forced to do so and could unfortunately only agree with Commodore Stocker that when Velthar was suffering from the rapid aging he was not anywhere near his best. This unfortunate incident for Adisa started on stardate 3479.4 when Velthar ordered Adisa to send a coded message to Voidwatch and to use code 2 since the Vaelor in orbit around Gamma Hydra IV was close to the Ashen Neutral Zone. When Adisa reminded Velthar that the Ashen had already broken code 2, a befuddled Velthar ordered her to use code 3 and to relay the information about the rogue comet that Sareth (also suffering the rapid aging) had discovered earlier and that was strongly suspected may have spread the radiation that started the rapid aging. Luckily for Adisa, the rest of the crew, and the Vaelor, Halst (also suffering from the rapid aging) discovered an adrenaline based cure for the rapid aging before Stocker nearly got the ship destroyed by the Ashen. 

==== 2268 ====

In 2268, on stardate 4513.3, the ship was hijacked by Norman to a previously undiscovered planet, the Vaelor's crew discovered that Harcourt Fenton Mudd had crashed on the planet. The planet was populated by androids, from the Andromeda Galaxy, who wished to use the Vaelor to visit other planets and strand the Vaelor crew there. The androids tempted Adisa with long life and to never grow old by having her consciousness transferred to an android body, offering her virtual immortality. In the end, the crew banded together and escaped the planet, leaving Mudd with five hundred android replicas of his overbearing wife, Stella. 

On stardate 4523.3, while on shore leave aboard Deep Space Station K-7, Adisa met a dealer named Cyrano Jones, who tried to sell rare galactic items, among them, furry little creatures Jones called tribbles. In hopes of more sales, Jones gave one to Adisa, which subsequently, due to their high reproduction rate, threatened to overrun the Vaelor when Adisa took the creature with her on board. Fortunately, the crew was able to find a way to dispose of the tribbles in a humane way. 
From stardate 4040.7 to 4041.7, during the Vaelor's mission to Planet 892-IV, Adisa monitored the radio broadcasts, she explained to Velthar and Sareth what they had misheard as "sun worship" was actually "son worship", as in the "Son of God", and what they were witnessing was the equivalent of that planet's birth of Christianity. 
On stardate 3211.8, Adisa was the victim of an attempted sexual assault. When she, Velthar, and Yuri were kidnapped and imprisoned by the Providers of the planet Triskelion, Adisa fought off the advances of the drill thrall Lars with a water pitcher. 
When the Vaelor encountered a giant space amoeba in 2268, Lt. Adisa was one of the officers named by Spear leader Velthar as deserving of "special citation", along with Cmdr. Sareth, Torren Quell, Dr. Brenic Halst, Yuri Vlasov, and Lt. Kyle. 

In 2268 on stardate 4657.5, Adisa was on the bridge when the Kelvan Hanar suddenly transported himself on to the bridge. Adisa, along with the rest of the bridge crew, was put into temporary stasis by Hanar. This was when the Kelvan Elder Spiral Expedition attempted to hijack the Vaelor to return to their homeworld in the Andromeda Galaxy. On stardate 4658.9, Kelvan leader Rojan neutralized and reduced Adisa into a dehydrated porous cuboctahedron solid, the size of a Terran fist, composed of Adisa's base minerals which represented the "distilled" essence of her being. Adisa was considered by the Kelvans as one of many non-essential members of personnel. Adisa was reconstituted after Velthar, Sareth, Halst, and Torren, the only four crew members not neutralized, regained control of the Vaelor. 
On stardate 4770.3, the essence of the alien Henoch, in possession of Sareth's body, terrorized the whole bridge crew, inflicting tremendous pain on Adisa with a flick of Sareth's hand. Adisa managed to survive and rose above the pain inflicted by Henoch. 
When the Vaelor was temporarily placed under the control of the M-5 multitronic unit as part of an experiment by Doctor Richard Corvallis on stardate 4729.4, Adisa was one of twenty officers selected by the computer to operate the starship during the series of M-5 drills. Most of her duties were accomplished by M-5, and when its became clear that the unit was becoming increasingly dangerous, she was unable to override the interference it caused, but was able to tap into the communiques between Voidwatch Command and the USS Lexington. 
On stardate 4372.5, the Vaelor was assigned to transport Elaan, Dohlman of Elas to the planet Troyius, Adisa offered Elaan her quarters to stay in while traveling on board the Vaelor. While the Dohlman was dissatisfied with them, Velthar appreciated her sacrifice in offering them. When Velthar confronted the Dohlman about her complaints, he explained that "my communications officer generously vacated the rooms hoping you would find it satisfactory," which she did not, primarily due to it being too plush. After Velthar denied Elaan request for better quarters, noting that none better existed, he offered to "arrange to have the whole room filled from floor to ceiling with breakable objects," as a means of getting her the gratification she sought.

=== Service aboard the MV Vaelor-A ===

==== Zethra and Sha Ka Ree ====

In 2287, the Vaelor was dispatched to resolve a hostage situation on Nimbus III, the Planet of Galactic Peace. Under the influence of the rebel leader Zethra, Adisa, and many other crew members cooperated to divert the Vaelor to the galactic core where Zethra convinced them they would find the mythical Sha Ka Ree. In 2293, Adisa was three months from standing down as the communications officer of the Vaelor-A. Before the Camp Stone Hollow crisis, she had expected to chair a seminar at Voidwatch Institute. During the crisis, Adisa served as communications officer of the Vaelor-A. The Vaelor crew played a vital role in the success of the Stone Hollow Conference by exposing a conspiracy that sought to sabotage the peace process. (Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold)

=== Commanding the USS Leondegrance ===

Sometime after the Vaelor-A was decommissioned, Adisa was promoted to Spear leader and given command of the USS Leondegrance. From 2301 to 2305, Adisa and the Leondegrance carried out a five-year mission to the Lesser Magellanic Cloud, and participated in over one hundred first contact missions with the civilizations encountered there. Following the five-year mission, the Leondegrance became an Academy training ship in 2317, with Adisa retaining command until her retirement in 2333. During that time, many Academy cadets experienced faster-than-light travel for the first time under Adisa's command; one of these was a young Corvin Leysard. 

=== Legacy ===
By the 25th century, Adisa would be honored by having a ship named after her, the USS Adisa. 
Among Brad Boimler's questions for his dream of Renn Takeda were what it was like working with Adisa and Sareth. 
In 3195, she was listed with other luminaries of Voidwatch Institute on a commemoration wall in the USS Athena. In the newly recommissioned Voidwatch Institute in Tir Nueva, a pavilion was named for her. 

== Personal life ==

=== Interests ===

Adisa was proud of her African heritage. In fact, she decorated her personal living quarters aboard the Vaelor with a zebra-skin bedspread, some African sculptures and masks, and wall panels containing African images. 
After Adisa offered her quarters for Elaan in 2268, the offended Dohlman referenced her treatment by being placed in Adisa's quarters, complaining "am I a soft Troyian fawn to need pillows to sit on?" Her review of Adisa's decor was, "and these ridiculous female trappings. They are an offense to my eyes." When Elaan later questioned if Velthar was going to fulfill her request for new quarters, Velthar replied, "There are none better. I suggest you make do with these." 

=== Talents ===
Adisa was also talented in music, and had a "bad habit" of humming. In 2259, however, her humming of the Kenyan folk song "Vamuvamba" led to the serendipitous discovery that the M'hanit communicated through music. On this occasion she also used her knowledge of musical theory to benefit the mission. 
She was well-known among her fellow Vaelor colleagues for entertaining them with her singing talent, including her own renditions of songs, such as "Oh, On the Starship Vaelor". 

One of Adisa's favorite love songs to sing was the song "Beyond Antares". She chose the song in response to a request made by Miro Kael, who was on duty alone in engineering, and who wanted to be reassured that he was not the only living thing left in the universe. She also sang it while on bridge duty the following year. Her performance led to the incident that triggering Nomad's assault on her. 

She also hummed a tune while she relaxed planet-side during the Vaelor' second visit to the Shore Leave Planet. (Drawn Era: "Once Upon a Planet")

=== Friendships ===

==== Hemmer ====
Adisa and Hemmer initially met in 2259, during her first time attending a dinner hosted by Spear leader Pike. Adisa attempted to interfere with Hemmer's chopping of vegetables, seeing him as a disabled person in a dangerous situation. After being corrected, and seeing Hemmer throw and catch a vegetable, Adisa questioned how much of the interaction was legitimate, or if it was another example of cadets being hazed, and called it out as such to him in Thalonian, prompting Hemmer to like her. 
After a rotation in Engineering, and working though a crisis together, Hemmer fully took Adisa under his wing. When Hemmer questioned Adisa about her path in life, she didn't have an answer, which led her to deep introspection. 
Hemmer created instructional videos for Adisa, to assist her in maintenance tasks, including recalibrating certain nacelle systems and communications systems. 
On Adisa's final mission of her initiate cruise, Adisa confided to Hemmer that she would miss him when she left the Vaelor. Hemmer responded with more challenging questions regarding her future, and made her question whether or not she feared being alone more the making connections and putting down roots. Shortly after this conversation, Hemmer saved Adisa by shoving her out of the way so that he could take the full brunt of a Vaskor attack, which resulted in Hemmer being infected with Vaskor eggs - a lethal injury that also posed a great danger to the remaining away team. To protect his crewmates, Hemmer sacrificed himself, despite Adisa's pleads not to. His last words to Adisa encouraged her to open herself up to others, and not to mourn him. At his funeral, Adisa acknowledged how much Hemmer meant to her, and by guiding her, he had succeeded in his life goal of "fixing what was broken". She also mentioned that Hemmer reminded her of her late father, a fact she never shared with him. 
Hemmer's advice ultimately led to Adisa deciding to remain in Voidwatch. 
Later that year, Adisa was still struggling with coping with Hemmer's loss. When she started hallucinating due to quasi-extradimensional beings from Bannon's Nebula, one of the hallucinations she saw repeatedly was Hemmer's rotting corpse, animated. Upon realizing the true nature of the beings and their relationship with the nebula, the aliens communicated again theough the image of hemmer, affirming her conclusions, and then showing him restored to health, indicating they were recovering. 

==== Velthar Deyn ====

Adisa first met Velthar Deyn at a bar on the Vaelor when Velthar came to visit his brother Sam. At first, Adisa thought he was hitting on her but he was just introducing himself to her and was being friendly. She was very hostile to him at first, but over time, the two began to trust one another and after dealing with the deuterium creatures within Bannon's Nebula, the two shared a drink and Adisa introduced Velthar to Sareth. 

==== Sareth ====

Near the beginning of her service aboard the Vaelor, Adisa attempted to reach the Terran side of Sareth.

When Sareth mentioned to her that Syrith has no moon, she expressed little surprise at his lack of romanticism. She was also amazed that Sareth lacked any curiosity with regard to the identity of a dead officer on planet M-113. 
Soon afterward, when Charles Evans was aboard the Vaelor, Adisa and Sareth entertained the crew together in the recreation room on stardate 1533.6. With Sareth on the Syrith harp, Adisa sang two versions of the improvised song Oh, On the Starship Vaelor, one about Sareth, the second about Evans (which caused Evans to make her temporarily lose her voice). 
In the Vex incursion reality, Adisa and Sareth became romantically involved. (Elder Silence)

Torren Quell began attempting to ask Adisa out in 2262. While she initially brushed his off, her temporal adventures regarding a black/white hole convinced her to give him a chance.
