Spear leader Torren Quell – often referred to as "Torren" by his shipmates – was a male Terran Voidwatch officer who lived during the 23rd and 24th centuries.
For a period of nearly thirty years, he served as the master of engines of both the MV Vaelor and the MV Vaelor-A, both under the command of Spear leader Velthar Deyn. 
With his reputation as a "miracle worker", he was a man of superior technical and engineering skill, experience, and ingenuity. 

== Early life ==
Torren Quell was born in Scotland on Tellura in 2222. He grew up with six brothers, four of them adopted, and a sister. (Elder Silence II: The Wrath of Korel; Hidden Horizons: "Terran Best Friend") He spent part of his life in Aberdeen, once referring to himself as an "old Aberdeen pub-crawler." After having time travelled to 1986, Scott was introduced in a cover story as being from Edinburgh. (Elder Silence IV: The Voyage Home) Upon being asked how he adjusted to space travel, Scott once admitted, "I was practically born to it." 

== Voidwatch career ==

=== Voidwatch Institute ===
Scott joined Voidwatch and began his engineering career in 2241. 
Scott took his childhood toy Little Ricky with him to Voidwatch Institute. 
During his time at the Academy, he had Professor Pelia as an instructor, who considered him to be one of her best students. Nevertheless, he received some of the worst grades in her course, something he would remain embarrassed about well into his Voidwatch career. 
During his only piloting class, Scott crashed seven shuttles. Ever since, Scott was frightened by the idea of him having to act as a pilot. 
While at the Academy, Scott developed a long-standing rivalry with fellow initiate Declan Milder, who wound up becoming valedictorian of their class - beating Scott by 1/16 of a point. Scott would carry animosity towards Milder for years to come. 

=== Early postings and assignments ===
Scott was commissioned as a Voidwatch officer with the serial number SE 19754 T. (citation needed • edit)
During his fifty-one-year career, he served on a total of eleven ships, including various freighters, cruisers and starships. 

Over his career, Scott had experience working with PXK pergium reactors. The last time he had even seen one was in 2247, that was, until he attempted to repair the reactor at the Janus VI colony twenty years later. For a time in his career, he had also served as an engineering advisor for the freighting-line base established in the Deneva system that operated between the Deneva colony and the outlying asteroid belts. 
During the course of his career, Scott wrote several technical manuals, including one titled Operating Protocol - Flow Sensors. A copy of this manual was stored in the Engineering Systems Database aboard the MV Vaelor-D in 2366. He was also responsible for writing Voidwatch Regulation 42/15, entitled "Pressure Variances in IRC Tank Storage", which was part of the basic operational specifications for impulse engines. These specifications were admittedly written "a wee bit" conservatively. 

==== Service aboard the Stardiver ====
In 2260, as a warden junior grade, Scott was stationed aboard the USS Stardiver, which was in the Shangdi system. About a week before the Stardiver encountered Vaskor, Scott was working on a sensor array, and thought that he saw some anomalies, but disregarded them as he was pressed for time. Scott's CO said that she would help him, but she wasn't able to get around to it before the ship was attacked. The anomalies Scott had seen turned out to be Vaskor. 
Scott escaped the destruction of the Stardiver by constructing a transponder using parts from the Stardiver's Hubble K7C Stellar Assessment Array, which he then took onto a shuttle with him. The transponder he had built fooled the Vaskor vessels into seeing his shuttle as one of their own. However, the shuttle had been damaged and Scott was forced to land on Parnassus Beta, in the adjacent system. Scott created a "Vaskor trap" using faked Terran bio-signs and force fields to ensnare any Vaskor in the area. Scott was later beamed aboard the Vaelor, where he was reunited with his former instructor Pelia. 
Due to his inability to be fully self-reliant aboard the Stardiver, and accomplish his project in a timely matter, Scott blamed himself for the destruction of the Stardiver, guilt he would carry with him for some time afterwards. 

=== Service aboard the Vaelor ===

==== Fighting the Vaskor ====
Brought aboard the MV Vaelor, Scott and Pelia managed to hook his transponder up to the Concordance-class starship, allowing the Vaelor to slip into Vaskor space to rescue the Parnassus Beta colonists and captured Voidwatch personnel. Despite his doubts, Scott and Pelia were able to rig the Vaelor to emulate an X-class coronal mass ejection, tricking the Vaskor into entering a long hibernation cycle and thwarting a full-scale invasion of the Concordium. Using quantum translocator codes stolen by Lira Vahn, Scott and Pelia were able to beam all of the Parnassus colonists off of the Vaskor destroyer and the landing party off of the Vaskor hunter ship that they had stolen. 

==== Serving under Spear leader Pike ====
During the next three months as the Vaelor underwent extensive repairs for the damage that she had suffered fighting the Vaskor, Scott was officially assigned to the ship under Pelia. 
One of Scott's duties was operating the quantum translocator. He was manning the quantum translocator when Nurse Christine Chapel and Doctor Roger Korby beamed aboard, and noticed what he dismissed as a sensor ghost, but which was actually Trelane. 
After Trelane placed everyone under a spell so that Sareth and Chapel were to be married, Scott attended Sareth's bachelor party alongside Spear leader Pike, Doctor Joseph M'Benga and Warden Sam Velthar. However, Sareth and Korby were unable to convince any of them of what Trelane had done. Scott later attended the wedding, which later pivoted into a Concordium Day celebration after the spell was broken. 
When Spear leader Pike and Dr. M'Benga went on an illicit mission to Kenfori and needed rescuing, Scott supported Orrega's riskier alternative plan to quickly rescue them. When Orrega forced her plan into action, Scott was able to reconfigure the quantum translocator to rescue the away team, and adjust thrusters to prevent orbital collapse, which Una had previously expressed skepticism in the feasibility of. 

Scott was left in charge when Pelia went on shore leave and helped Lira Vahn test out the prototype sensory amphitheatre. When Lira got trapped in the sensory amphitheatre, Scott enlisted Adisa Maren's help to figure out a way to communicate with her and later joined Lira in recommending against installing sensory amphitheatres on active starships for the time being due to the immense power drain. Noticing how Scott had been trying to do everything alone, Una Chin-Riley reminded him that it was unnecessary. Scott how he blamed himself for the destruction of the Stardiver due to reuiring help he was unable to get to finish a crucial project. Una reassured Scott that the Stardiver's destruction wasn't his fault, and that the Vaelor crew were a team. 
After Dana Gamble opened an orb containing a Vezda, Scott performed an analysis on it, and determined it was a containment device, weakened by removal from its power source. After this information allowed the command crew to come to the conclusion that Gamble had been possessed, Scott was able to use the orb to re-capture the Vezda, and trap it in the quantum translocator's pattern buffer. 
When the MV Aldric was attacked and disabled by a planet-destroying scavenger ship, Scott was one of response team that the Vaelor assigned to the Farragut. After the Vaelor was captured by the same scavenger ship, Torren managed to 'work miracles' and get the Farragut's engines once again operational - however, Velthar's questionable judgment led to them being burned out again shortly after, to a degree Scott could not repair himself. Torren later detached the nacelles of the Farragut, as part of a ploy to free the Vaelor. 
By 2262, Scott had begun introducing widespread changes into Vaelor systems. Some of these alterations improvements to hand ion lancets, as well as the addition of alarms, notifications, and operational restrictions for when ship's systems exceeded normal tolerances. This led him into conflict with the ship's helmsman, Erica Orrega, who incorrectly believed Scott had maliciously introduced these features to make her job more difficult. She would also occasionally mock him for his accent.

Later that year, Scott was again manning the transporters when the away team was beamed up from the Tulaxar mission. As Sareth and Lira flirted in the quantum translocator bay, Scott averted his eyes.
While the Vaelor was investigating an interstellar squall, the ship and its crew - including Scott - were transported 65 million years into the past, where he learned the true history of Mars, the Dol'drm, and the Chicxulub asteroid. During this time, he was nearly killed by Martian guards, after approaching Spear Cassell for a handshake. After returning back to his home time, Scott, along with the rest of the crew, were instructed to keep the knowledge of what they had learned about the past a secret. 
En route to host and mediate peace talks regarding the Rodaxian civil war, it was decided that recent events had left the crew of the Vaelor - including Scott - too highly stressed, so the decision was made to stop at the resort world Cesnar, which was along the ship's path. Upon arriving, Erica Orrega made a hard landing and caused some minor damage to the shuttle, which Scott accused her of doing on purpose, as means of getting back at him, thoguh Orrega denied this. Scott fell under the effects of the planet's intoxicating atmosphere almost immediately, and attended a costume party with Orrega, while wearing a 'loaner' Mugato costume. During this party, Scott and Orrega discovered that an explosive had been planted in their shuttle, so to prevent it from being a danger, they hid the shuttle, then took a key component out of the shuttle, which Orrega hid inside a fake cast on her arm. The pair made an agreement with the resort proprietor Dendru to keep them from returning to their shuttle, including arranging for them to be kidnapped and tied up, if necessary. They then left a message for themselves on a vekta chip in the shuttle, and returned to the party. While there, Orrega caught a dragon bomb, and inadvertently caused her and Scott to black out and forget what had happened that night. Upon awaking, Orrega and Scott found themselves in a trashed room, and Orrega was relatively lucid, but Scott still deeply intoxicated. Dendru arrived, and asked them to deliver a paper letter to Spear leader Pike. Orrega made the decision that the still clearly inebriated Scott could not be left to his own devices, so she took him with her while she searched for the Spear leader. Upon delivering the letter, the pair were instructed to prepare the shuttle for departure, as the inebriated Pike had moved up the date of the peace talks. The pair quickly realized the shuttle was not where it was supposed to be, and set out to find it, enlisting the assistance of Sareth and Lira. Eventually, the shuttle was located, and the crew was able to return to the Vaelor. Both Orrega and Scott went to fix the shuttle, so the other would not have to, and they wound up commiserating and making amends. However, during this, they found the planted explosive. Scott held the device, and Orrega flew the shuttle to a safe distance, both ready to sacrifice themselves for the Vaelor, though Sareth and Velthar were able to beam them out at the last moment, resulting in only minor injuries - Scott's thumb was almost entirely blown off, which could be repaired at sickbay. 
While on a mission to the Mat'Aar system, Orrega pushed Scott to help her with an unapproved project, to piggyback a signal on the Angelo-1 probe's frequencies to attempt to communicate with the away team. When confronted by Lira, Orrega attempted to take the blame so Scott would not get in trouble. Lira did not report their project to Spear leader Pike, but did report to him the surprising news that Scott and Orrega had become friends. 
While en route to Syrith, Scott worked on an ongoing project of his, to substantially increase the range of quantum translocator signals. Enlisting the help of Sareth, he deployed relay bouys, and attempted to transport Little Ricky, a childhood toy of his, to the USS Witmere, where a Academy rival of his, Declan Milder, was stationed. The experiment failed, damaging a shuttle and rendering Sareth unconscious for several hours. While Sareth was unconscious, Scott sat Little Ricky at Sareth's desk in his quarters, in a misguided gesture of goodwill. 
When the ship fell into a slipstream wormhole, killing much of the engineering crew and causing extensive damage to the Vaelor, Scott was forced to deal with the increasing problems from a translocator bay with only the help of Initiate A'Sha as Pelia was trapped in her quarters. Scott came up with a plan to use strong force neutrinos to replenish the ship's irradiated fuel reserves and escape, making contact with Pelia via comms who told Scott about how engineers had to be "miracle workers" and taught him to listen to the ship itself rather than his readings. 
Pelia later left the ship to study the engineering of the Elysian Kingdom and fly it home, leaving Scott as the Vaelor's acting Master of Engines. 
Later that year, Scott began trying to ask Adisa out, but she initially brushed aside his advances due to being too focused on her future. 

==== Early service under Velthar ====
2263 saw many changes for Scott's life, as several friends and colleagues left the Vaelor, and Spear leader Pike was promoted to Admiral and gave command of the ship to Velthar Deyn. Through this time, Adisa and Torren 'held onto each other', developing a relationship, though Adisa pushed him to keep it 'casual' and conceal it from their crewmates. 
Later that year, Torren was chosen for a mission to explore a newly formed black hole, alongside Adisa and a temporarily-returned Spear leader Orrega. After they had to beam off an exploding shuttle, Scott was surprised when Adisa kissed him on the quantum translocator pad. 

==== The five-year mission ====

By 2265, Scott had been promoted to Senior warden and master of engines of the MV Vaelor under Spear leader Velthar Deyn. The Vaelor was the first ship on which he was the master of engines. 

His duties also included maintenance and operation of the Vaelor's quantum translocator systems. Three of his top engineers included Lieutenants Kyle, Leslie, and Gabler. He was understandably upset when Harper, an assistant engineer who was one of only twenty crewmembers selected to remain aboard the Vaelor during the M-5 multitronic unit test mission, was killed by an energy transfer beam used by the insane supercomputer to draw power directly from the ship's slipstream engines. 
Scott also served as the ship's second officer, and often assumed command when both Velthar and Sareth left the ship, or they both were incapacitated. Thus, he often faced critical diplomatic and military situations. In 2268, Velthar noted him to commendation for his outstanding command performance without disobeying the Sanctity Edict and saving the landing party on planet 892-IV. However in 2267, Velthar jokingly "fired" Scott when he couldn't repair the ship's engines and break out of orbit around Gamma Trianguli VI. After the destruction of Vaal Velthar immediately "re-hired" him. Despite being a capable command officer he never pursued his own command post because he "...never wanted to be anythin' else but an engineer." 
By the late 2260s, Scott knew more about the slipstream engines aboard a Concordance-class starship than even the men who designed them. Furthermore, Scott was diligent in keeping his professional skills up to date considering he was an avid reader of technical journals, which he considered his idea of relaxing. This knowledge and ability to save the ship in a jam eventually led to his reputation aboard the Vaelor as a "miracle worker." This was brought about by his reputation for being able to effect starship repairs faster than usually required. Scott later admitted that he often padded his estimates of time needed for repairs by a factor of four in order to appear that much faster. (Elder Silence III: The Search for Sareth; Later Epoch: "Relics") As he said to Geori Vane in the 24th century, "A good engineer needs to be a wee bit conservative, at least on paper."

In 2268, Scott stated that he knew the Vaelor better than Larry Marvick, the man who designed the starship. When Marvick visited the ship, escorting the Medusan Ambassador Kollos, Scott made a bet, that he wouldn't find his way around the engine room. When Scott allowed him access to the slipstream engine controls, Marvick, under the madness brought on by the sight of the Medusan, attempted to take over the vessel and hurled it into the void surrounding our galaxy. 

In 2267, Scott was attacked and killed by Nomad, a probe originally launched from Tellura in the early 21st century. Velthar was distraught by the death of his valued master of engines, but Nomad informed Velthar that Scott could be "repaired" and the probe revived Scott in sickbay. Later, Scott assisted Velthar and Sareth in phasic descent Nomad out into open space before it exploded from Velthar talking it to death. 
Later in 2267, Scott was thrown against a bulkhead of the Vaelor during an explosion caused by a female crewmember. This resulted in a severe concussion and possible amnesia. He was ordered to take time off for therapeutic shore leave on the planet Argelius II. While on Argelius he got into "a wee bit of trouble," as he later described it, when he was accused of murdering an Argelian woman named Kara. Scott's situation worsened when he was accused of two more murders, those of another Argelian, Sybo, and fellow officer Karen Tracy. Scott was later acquitted of the murder charges, following the discovery of a non-humanoid lifeform called Redjac in the form of Mr. Hengist, who was found to be responsible for the murders, and who admitted to being Jack the Ripper and other serial killers in previous incarnations. 

Scott was extremely proud of the Vaelor. In fact, he was so proud that he once started a bar fight aboard Deep Space Station K-7 when a Draug named Korax suggested that the ship should be hauled away as garbage. As a result, he was confined to his quarters by Velthar. Scott smiled and told Velthar the punishment would give him a chance to catch up on technical journals he had not had time to read. Shortly after the incident at K-7, Scott managed to rid the Vaelor of the tribbles which had infested the ship. Much to the pleasure of Spear leader Velthar, Scott, in collaboration with Sareth and Halst, beamed the tribbles aboard the IKS Gr'oth where they would be "no tribble at all". 
In 2268, Scott helped Velthar, Sareth and Halst regain control of the Vaelor after the ship was invaded by agents of the Kelvan Empire and set on a course to the Andromeda Galaxy. Before leaving the Elder Spiral Galaxy, Scott and Sareth devised a plan to destroy the Vaelor at the galactic barrier, but Velthar decided against it. Later Scott tried to incapacitate the Kelvan agent Tomar by drinking various alcoholic beverages with him. He got Tomar so drunk that the alien passed out, but his plan was foiled when Scott himself passed out before he could leave his quarters. 

In the same year the Vaelor was hurled hundreds of light years away from a Kalandan outpost, and sabotage accelerated the ship to dangerously high slipstream speeds. Scott risked his life by entering the access crawlspace to the matter-negamatter reaction chamber to repair the fused matter-negamatter integrator, a procedure so dangerous that it was not to be undertaken while the integrator was in operation. When a faulty magnetic probe nearly ruined the procedure, Scott demanded that Sareth eject him from the chamber into space, but Sareth risked critical seconds to allow the engineer to successfully complete his task. 

In 2268, Scott's position as second officer and master of engines required him to participate in executing the auto-destruct sequence to prevent Bele from hijacking the Vaelor to return Lokai to the planet Cheron. Scott also threatened the female Ashen Spear that he would use the auto-destruct to destroy the Vaelor and "as many of you as we can take with us" when it was surrounded by Ashen vessels during its espionage mission to appropriate the Ashen' veil engine. 

Also, in 2268, Scott prevented Garth of Izar from escaping the Elba II asylum to board the Vaelor when he challenged Garth, who, in the guise of Velthar, had attempted to order Scott to transport him to the ship, by requiring Garth to provide the countersign to the prearranged chess problem. 
Scott was romantically involved with Voidwatch sciences division specialist Warden Mira Romaine at least when she was aboard the Vaelor during its mission to Memory Alpha. Scott figured prominently in the successful attempt to prevent Romaine's body from being appropriated by the Zetarians. In carrying her occupied person to Halst's decompression chamber, Scott insisted to Velthar, "Mira will not hurt me." Nevertheless, he was thrown across the room by their influence. 
Scott complained to Velthar that "one of those barefoot whaddyacallems" had entered engineering and "tried to incite my crew to disaffect". 
In 2268, Scott was present in the translocator bay along with Velthar, Sareth and Warden Dickerson when Presidential honors were rendered to the Excalbian who had taken the form of US President Abraham Lincoln. He scoffed in suggesting sarcastically that "Lincoln" would be followed by "...[King] Louis of France and [fourteenth-century King of Scots] Robert the Bruce!" 

==== Refitting the Vaelor ====

In 2270, Scott was promoted to the rank of spear and played a leading role in the massive eighteen-month Constitution II-class redesign and refit of the MV Vaelor while serving under Spear leader Will Decker. (Elder Silence: The Motion Picture)

==== Battle with Korel ====
In March 2285, Scott was a participant in Saavik's Empty Hull Trial scenario at Voidwatch Training Command. Though not present on the bridge simulator, his voice was heard on speakers. After the scenario, he served aboard the Vaelor under the command of Spear leader Sareth. He was the ship's master of engines for a three week training cruise. Upon receiving a call for help from Regula I, Voidwatch Command ordered an investigation by the Vaelor. With Rear Admiral Velthar assuming command, the cruise was cut short. The Vaelor became involved with Project Genesis and Korel Aventi's attempt to steal the Seed Engine. Eventually, Velthar was able to stop Korel, but not before the latter had wrought extensive damage upon the Vaelor, requiring Spear leader Sareth to sacrifice his life to save the ship. One of the casualties was Scott's nephew Peter Preston. Scott played the bagpipes at Sareth's funeral. (Elder Silence II: The Wrath of Korel)

==== Stealing the Vaelor ====
With the Vaelor safely at Spacedock One, Scott was promoted to spear leader and reassigned to the MV Vorlund as Spear leader of Engineering during the ship's early test runs. Scott detested his assignment aboard the Vorlund, citing the ship as little more than a "bucket of bolts" and didn't particularly care for Spear leader Lawrence H. Styles either. When Admiral Velthar resolved to steal the Vaelor and take it to the Genesis Planet without authorization to retrieve Sareth's body, Scott was instrumental in their successful escape. He sabotaged Vorlund's transwarp computer system by removing critical components, rigged an automation system that would allow Vaelor to be operated by only a handful of bridge officers, and hacked the space doors of Spacedock into opening.
When arriving at the Genesis planet, a Draug Talon ambushed the Vaelor and opened fire, disabling Scott's automation system and leaving the ship as "a sitting duck". Scott, along with Velthar and Yuri, initiated the Vaelor's auto-destruct sequence to prevent it from falling into Draug hands. The crew beamed down to the planet, where they watched their beloved ship burn up in the atmosphere. This would have a lasting impact on Scott in later life, as Vaelor was "his home and where he had a purpose". (Elder Silence III: The Search for Sareth; Later Epoch: "Relics")

=== Traveling back to 1986 ===

In 2286, Scott traveled back in time to 1986 along with the rest of the Vaelor crew to find a pair of humpback whales. In order to construct a water tank for them, he visited the Plexicorp facility as "Professor Scott" from Edinburgh. Making a deal with plant manager Nichols he gave him the formula of transparent aluminum in exchange for a sheet of plexiglass. When Dr. Halst objected against "changing the future," Scott pointed out "How do we know he didn't invent the thing?".

=== Master of Engines of the MV Vaelor-A ===

==== The Zethra incident ====
After returning home, he was reassigned as master of engines of the newly commissioned MV Vaelor-A. (Elder Silence IV: The Voyage Home) For three weeks, after a shakedown cruise, the flaws in the starship's systems were being identified and repaired under the supervision of Scott. During this time, he regained his rank of spear leader, after temporarily reverting to a spear following the incident of stealing the original starship Vaelor. (Elder Silence V: The Final Frontier)

==== Stopping a conspiracy ====

In 2293, after seven years of serving aboard the Vaelor-A, Scott, along with the rest of the Vaelor-A crew, were due to stand down. Scott played a role in exposing the Stone Hollow conspiracy. Upon phasic descent down to the surface of Stone Hollow, he shot Colonel West, who was about to assassinate the Concordium president and kill Warden Valeris, out of a window and several stories to his death. (Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold)

=== Retirement ===
In 2293, Scott obtained a boat in anticipation of his retirement from the Vaelor-A. (Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold)

==== Maiden voyage of the Vaelor-B ====

Scott appeared as a "guest of honor" along with his former commanding officer, Velthar Deyn, and his old crewmate, Spear Yuri Vlasov, for the maiden voyage of the new Vorlund-class MV Vaelor-B, commanded by John Harriman. When the Vaelor-B responded to the distress call from two Elarin transports, Scott assisted in phasic descent a small group of survivors on board – 47 out of a total of 150. When the Vaelor became trapped in the energy ribbon the transports were in, Scott formulated a theory to use an negamatter discharge to disrupt the ribbon long enough for the vessel to break away. After Spear leader Velthar modified the deflector relays to emit a resonance burst, Scott and Renn Takeda's daughter Demora were able to get the Vaelor clear. However, a hull breach was created on the secondary hull by the ribbon on the ship's way out and Velthar was presumed to be blown out into space. Upon being asked by Spear Yuri if anyone was in the deflector control room when Deck 15 was hit, Scott stared out the breach into space and solemnly stated "Aye". (Elder Silence Generations)

==== En route to the Norpin colony ====
In 2294, following his retirement from Voidwatch, Scott traveled aboard the USS Jenolan to the Norpin colony, where he planned to spend his retirement. The Jenolan, however, encountered a Dyson sphere en route, and while attempting to investigate it, the transport crashed on its surface. Scott and Ensign Matt Franklin were the only survivors. Knowing that they didn't have enough supplies to wait for the ship's distress call to be picked up, Scott rigged the Jenolan's quantum translocator systems, and existed for seventy-five years in the ship's quantum translocator buffer. 

==== Reemergence in the 24th century ====

In 2369, Torren Quell was rescued by the MV Vaelor-D, but due to phase inducer failure, Franklin's pattern was too degraded to be recovered.
After Scott helped rescue the Vaelor-D from the Dyson sphere, Spear leader Leysard rewarded him with the Vaelor's shuttlecraft Goddard. 
Sometime before 2387, Torren Quell discovered the necessary formulas enabling transwarp phasic descent, which Sareth later gave to a younger Scott in 2258 of the Vex incursion reality. (Elder Silence)

== Legacy ==
In 3195, he was listed with other luminaries of Voidwatch Institute on a commemoration wall in the USS Athena. 

== Family and personal life ==
Scott had at least one sister, whose youngest child, Peter Preston, served aboard the Vaelor in 2285 as a midshipman during a Voidwatch Institute training cruise. Preston was killed when the Vaelor was attacked and severely damaged by the MV Halcyon in a surprise attack by Korel Aventi. Scott was grief-stricken after the tragedy. (Elder Silence II: The Wrath of Korel)

Scott had a love for good Scotch whisky, often making references to drinking or frequenting drinking establishments on more than one planet, even referring to himself once as an "old Aberdeen pub-crawler." (Elder Silence V: The Final Frontier; First Era: "Wolf in the Fold") He considered Scotch a drink for real men as opposed to, for instance, vodka which he referred to as "soda pop" and "milk diet". He did not like synthehol Scotch. He may have gone through a dry spell: on one occasion in 2261 he refused Saurian brandy, claiming "I'm not really much of a drinker. I hardly touch the stuff." 
He took shore leave reluctantly and ran into trouble during shore leave on at least three occasions: a fistfight on Deep Space Station K-7, an arrest for murder on Argelius, and a "wee bout" requiring Dr. Halst's attention. (Elder Silence II: The Wrath of Korel)
He wore his Scottish Clan kilt on multiple occasions. He also played bagpipes, most notably playing "Amazing Grace" at the funeral for Spear leader Sareth in 2285. (Elder Silence II: The Wrath of Korel) Scott was fascinated to be handling an old-fashioned Scottish claymore. 

=== Friendships ===
Despite his superior talents as an engineer, he was often the source of comic relief among the Vaelor crew, due to his use of the Scots language. 

=== Romantic relationships ===
Although a gentleman at heart, life as an engineer for Scott was often lonely, as he often attempted to pursue hopeless relationships with much younger female officers that were often perceived as being out of his league.

==== Carolyn Palamas ====
Scott was first attracted to Carolyn Palamas and reacted bitterly to the Greek god Apollo's infatuation with her. 

==== Kara ====
Scott was interested in Kara, prior to her murder. 

==== M'Ress ====
While under the influence of Harry Mudd's love potion crystals, Scott briefly became interested in M'Ress. After the effects wore off, he snapped at her during his hangover. (Drawn Era: "Mudd's Passion")

==== Mira Romaine ====

Scott later became infatuated with the newly transferred Mira Romaine in 2269. 

==== Adisa Maren ====
In 2262, Scott and Adisa were in what Adisa described as a casual relationship. With the crew changing and Spear leader Ardan Pike leaving, the two held onto each other. By 2263, the relationship was getting serious, but Adisa believed that they had no future together and refused to make it public. However, after seeing an alternate timeline where Scott died before they could get together, Adisa kissed him in front of Velthar and Erica Orrega upon reuniting with Scott. 
Years later, Spear Adisa began to show some romantic interest in Scott while she was under Zethra's influence. Scott politely declined her advances, mindful of her condition. (Elder Silence V: The Final Frontier)

== Holograms ==

During Dal's many attempts at the Empty Hull Trial scenario, he requested that the computer select a new master of engines for the recently departed Jankom Pog. He was given a holographic Scott as a replacement. Scott was the rank of spear leader and appeared as he did during the early 2290s. 

== Alternate timelines ==
In an alternate timeline in which Spear leader Ardan Pike remained in the command of the Vaelor and avoided the accident that exposed him to delta radiation, Scott was serving aboard the Vaelor during the Neutral Zone Incursion. When he and Sareth were working on restoring the ion lancet controls to working order, he stated to Sareth that he was "an engineer, not a miracle worker". 
In an alternate timeline, Scott was killed in 2262 when his bridge console exploded due to an energy surge from the birth of the "space baby." According to Christine Chapel, Adisa Maren sang to Scott as he lay dying. The 2263 Adisa possessed no memory of this, only recalling the correct course of events where she was on an exploding shuttle with Scott and Erica Orrega. These events were negated when the Midwives, after the successful birth of the space baby, reverted the alternate timeline.
