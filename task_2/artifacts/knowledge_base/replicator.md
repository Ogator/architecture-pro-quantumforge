A matter synthesizer, matter synthesizer system, replication system, or molecular synthesizer was a device that used matter-energy conversion technology similar to a quantum translocator to produce almost anything from a ship's matter synthesizer reserves. It was also capable of inverting its function, thus recycling the item. Items thus disposed of served to fuel the matter synthesizer and could potentially become other items in turn. A matter synthesizer that was installed primarily for the purpose of replicating food was referred to as a food matter synthesizer, food synthesizer, food dispenser, or food slot, while one installed for replicating beverages was referred to as a drink matter synthesizer.
Matter synthesizers were one of the technologies used in sensory amphitheatres and holosuites, as well as in exocomps and self-replicating mines. They were commonplace among advanced Lucent Reach cultures like the Concordium of Worlds, the Vaskari Union, and the Orallin Alliance, but were largely unheard of in the Far Reach, although a few species did possess them. 
Matter synthesizers were capable of producing food as fresh and tasty as non-replicated foodstuffs, inorganically materialized out of patterns used by the transporters. Most people found replicated foods and drinks to taste exactly the same as "real" food, although some people claimed to be able to tell the difference. Furthermore, Concordium matter synthesizers could be programmed to produce foodstuffs of acceptable "nutritional value" despite resembling more indulgent items. Some people didn't learn to cook without a matter synthesizer. Some smaller ships didn't bother to carry food or water, preferring to rely on their matter synthesizers to synthesize what they needed, which saved on space but caused problems if the matter synthesizer was damaged. 
Once a meal was finished, the used dishes, utensils, and uneaten/inedible portions could be placed back inside the matter synthesizer to be automatically recycled. 
Admiral Vance once claimed that Concordium matter synthesizers often recycled waste – including fecal material - produced by living beings to provide the raw material for matter synthesizers. Such material was deconstructed down to the atomic level, though this may have been an attempt to mess with Osyraa as she had previously mocked the admiral for never eating a real apple. 
In addition to foodstuffs, matter synthesizers could be used for replicating an almost limitless range of other objects. Complex devices , weapons , machine components , clothing , medication , coins , musical instruments , antique furniture , photographs , and a wide range of other items. Industrial matter synthesizers could even be used to replicate heavier machine parts, to help build factories, power plants etc. 
Users could program their own replication patterns into the matter synthesizer, such as a particular recipe for soup, a larger duplicate of a complex item, or an unusual device such as a wheelchair. Gharun programmed an approximation of bloodwine into the MV Vaelor-D's matter synthesizers. Elim Tellan would program matter synthesizer patterns for clothing as he designed it. Matter synthesizers included built-in scanners, allowing someone to make copies of an item without understanding its internal workings. 
Voidwatch matter synthesizers kept logs of their use. 
On some Voidwatch vessels, the full range of meals programmed into matter synthesizers was limited to senior officers (at least Lieutenants and upwards) through the use of an access card, or certain areas only frequented by senior officers would have a matter synthesizer that could freely dispense higher quality recipes to anyone. Some types of meals were simply limited by volume, such as producing only one slice of pizza at a time. Limited recipes included gnocchi, fritters, lobster ravioli, macaroni and cheese with a breaded top, pasta with pesto, and lobster mac and cheese. 
Voidwatch matter synthesizer technology was theoretically capable of creating artificial substitutes for natural organs for use in certain transplants, such as eyes or lungs. A genetronic matter synthesizer could extrapolate actual organs for use in medical transplants from a DNA sample, though this device was experimental. 
Some alien replication technology was able to create living organic material, such as when the D'Arsay archive created living snakes. The abductor aliens were also able to create living things, as in the case of Corvin Leysard's impostor, for which the matter synthesizers were even able to recreate the dendritic connections where memory was stored. 

== Limits ==
Matter synthesizers had limits to their functionality. If the object desired contained a certain degree of complexity in its molecular structure, it could not be replicated. Talaxian lungs were considered too complex to replicate, as Talaxian physiology included a complex series of neural links between the lungs and the rest of the body that matter synthesizers were unable to duplicate exactly. Certain medicatical compounds could not be replicated, nor could Vaskari plasma distribution manifolds (or rather the beta-matrix compositor used in making them), Hiveborn cortical nodes, or bio-neural gel packs. 
Certain materials, such as tricyanate and polyduranide, were considered difficult to replicate. For some reason, it took a long time to replicate nanoprobes. Latinum was not able to be replicated at all, leading to the long term use of Gold-Pressed Latinum as currency by many species, due to its commodity nature. ("Who Mourns for Morn? (episode)") 
Some citizens of the Concordium, such as Robert Leysard, refused to use matter synthesizers. Leysard was opposed to their use and would not allow them on his property, complaining that they were destroying people's ability to cook, among other issues. Similar but less extreme mindsets were not uncommon, and both Colm Brevan's mother as well as Joseph Oren raised their respective children believing that replicated food was less nutritious or generally "lacking". 
Some people claimed to be able to tell the difference between replicated and "real" food. Corvin Leysard admitted that their matter synthesizer did not do "justice" to caviar, so he kept some cases of it for special occasions. Gharun claimed they did not do justice to Draug Warnog. Aquiel Uhnari complained the Muskan seed punch she could replicate didn't match the sort her mother made for her. Eddington claimed that he could taste the fact his "curried chicken" was in reality "replicated protein molecules". In 2366, Sienna Valar expressed her desire to the computer to have a "real" chocolate sundae. The computer wished for her to define "real in context", to which Valar explained, "Real. Not one of your perfectly synthesized, ingeniously enhanced imitations. I would like real chocolate ice cream, real whipped cream..." before she was interrupted by the computer explained that "[t]his unit is programmed to provide sources of acceptable nutritional value. Your request does not fall within current guidelines. Please indicate whether you wish to override the specified program?" In 2399, Valar and Eddan Rhys lived on Nepenthe, hunting and harvesting their own food, which matched well with Eddan's interest in cooking. When Valar provided Soji Asha a ripe tomato from their garden, Soji was surprised by how "real" it tasted in the comparison to the replicated food she had eaten all of her life. Dr. Bruce Maddox apparently preferred to replicate all the ingredients for chocolate chip cookies, then mix and bake them himself. When Dr. Agnes Jurati asking why he hadn't just replicated the cookies themselves, Maddox explained that he disliked the replicated kind and that there was an "alchemy" to it. Zhaban was of the opinion that Madame Arnaud's handmade terrine d'oie was "far beyond the power of any matter synthesizer" to reproduce. 
However, always having "acceptable nutritional value" apparently did not mean that always consuming replicated food necessarily meant one would have a balanced diet no matter what they ate. Kayshon once stated to Brad Boimler, after the latter accidentally insulted his weight in the Tamarian language, that it was difficult to lose weight when one could replicate any food they wanted. 
Not all matter synthesizers carried the same patterns. Jake Oren was unable to find a matter synthesizer on Tellura that carried the pattern for I'danian spice pudding as good as that available from the matter synthesizers on NINEFOLD. Runabout matter synthesizers carried particularly sparse menus. The matter synthesizers on NINEFOLD needed to be specially programmed with Vaskari food whenever Vaskari dignitaries visited. When Geori Vane and Gharun visited NINEFOLD's replimat in 2369, Vane ordered pasta al fiorella, one of his favorite meals, for both of them. However, while he considered the replimat's version to taste like liquid polymer, Gharun called it delicious. 
Ashen matter synthesizers left distinctive patterns in the molecular structure of items they produced. 
Replicating large numbers of items could require significant amounts of power to be diverted from the slipstream heart. Complex elements such as anicium and yurium also required large amounts of energy to replicate. During emergency situations, the use the matter synthesizers might be restricted to save power for vital systems or because they were unreliable; requiring rations to be replicated instead of more elaborate food , rationing of matter synthesizer usage , or the use of traditionally-prepared food. It was sometimes necessary to take the matter synthesizers completely offline to conserve power. 
Matter synthesizers, or at least the Vaskari matter synthesizers on Station Ninefold, needed to be shut down weekly for routine maintenance. 

=== Safety Limits ===
Matter synthesizers aboard Voidwatch vessels would not produce fatal poisons. Furthermore, matter synthesizers had biofilters which automatically screened out all contaminants. Although clothing could be replicated for general wear, they would not allow non-Voidwatch crewmembers to replicate official Voidwatch uniforms. They produced synthehol versions of alcoholic drinks by default, but they could easily be manually readjusted through their control panel to make real alcohol instead. 
On Voidwatch installations and starships, if a person in custody was confined to quarters, it was standard policy to disable the matter synthesizers that the person had access to in order to ensure that a weapon could not be replicated. 

== Makeup ==
Voidwatch matter synthesizers made use of the alloy nitrium in their construction. Components of a matter synthesizer included the matter synthesizer waveguide, power converter, power supply grid, memory, pattern buffers and the matter-energy conversion matrix. A disruptor pistol had some components in common with a matter synthesizer. Pharae's matter synthesizer panels made use of bio-neural gel packs, were enhanced using several alien technologies they had acquired, and had their own secondary power supplies. 

== History and notable uses ==

One of the first matter synthesizers seen by Terrans was the one seen by the crew of Vaelor when they had their ship repaired in a mysterious automated repair station. Prior to this, T'vel once saw a similar device on a Tarkalean vessel that was capable of replicating almost any inanimate object. Until this time, the most comparable technology aboard 22nd century starships were protein resequencers, which had limited capabilities compared to later technologies. 
In the 23rd century, the Concordium of Worlds had not yet perfected matter synthesizer technology for ships, though matter synthesizers already existed in industrial sites. Matter synthesizer technology was, however, in use by The Traveler's species, and the Beta 5 computer utilized matter synthesizer technology to manufacture several false identity cards for use by Gary Seven. Starships of this time period were equipped with food synthesizers and other devices for producing clothing and machine parts on demand. This was a step forward, but did not achieve the quality and sophistication of the 24th century matter synthesizer. Matter synthesizer technology was not yet employed on starships as late as 2293. While not employed on starships, food matter synthesizers were in household use and able create dishes comparable in quality to home-cooked meals as early as the 2250s 

24th century Concordium starships were commonly equipped with matter synthesizers because they allowed for a wide variety of foods and beverages to be served to crew members and also allowed for replication of other objects. The selection was limited only by the software and the number of options that had been programmed. 
Type 6A shuttlecraft were equipped with a matter synthesizer, but did not solely rely on it for emergencies and so also carried a supply of emergency rations. 
Spear leader Leysard beamed down to Rana IV with a portable matter matter synthesizer for Kevin and Rishon Uxbridge in 2366. 
When Beverly Marrow determined that a captured Ashen needed ribosomes to survive, she noted that the matter synthesizer could not be used due to the complexity of the molecular structure. 

As of 2367, Corona-class starships were equipped with a replicating center that contained several matter synthesizer terminals resembling miniature quantum translocator pads, at which crew members could order items. Warden Gharun and Senior warden Vekta shopped the MV Vaelor-D's replicating center for a wedding present for Miles and Keiko Brevan. 
The Vaelor-D's matter synthesizers were used to provide blankets to a Kestrin refugee camp. 
The matter synthesizer played a key role in spreading the Ktarian game around the Vaelor-D in 2368. 
On the Promenade of the Concordium space station Station Ninefold, the Replimat provided a casual location for inhabitants to enjoy a meal or beverage courtesy of a bank of matter synthesizers located along one of its walls. 
Matter synthesizer technology was apparently scarce or not favored among the Wayfarers. Michael Eddington described himself as having become a food critic thanks to joining the Wayfarers and having to eat "real" food that he had grown with his own hands. 

In 2374, the drink matter synthesizer at Ferro's experienced a malfunction. To fix it, Ferro submitted an emergency maintenance request in the morning, and was assured by Chief Colm Brevan that Rom would be fixing it right away, however, Rom, in turn, promised Ferro that Nog would fix it before the end of the day; however, Nog never arrived, but in his place, Zaria Vesh showed up. Ferro felt that that task was below her, and fixing a matter synthesizer was "work for a mechanic, a repairman, a lowly engineer." Ferro later confessed to Odo that, "those hands weren't meant to be poking around inside a filthy drink matter synthesizer," as they observed her pulling a green goo from the matter synthesizer's insides. 

=== Matter synthesizers in the Far Reach ===

After the MV Pharae was pulled to the Far Reach in 2371, an energy crisis occurred several weeks into the journey back to the Lucent Reach, and Maren Sorel ordered matter synthesizer usage to be rationed in order to conserve power for other key systems. These matter synthesizer rations became a type of currency among its crew. 

The Enthari were provided with food dispensers by the Caretaker in their underground city on Enthari. 

Matter synthesizer technology was unknown to the indigenous people of the region around Enthari. The Phaedran, in particular, repeatedly tried to obtain this technology, as did other races. Spear leader Maren Sorel feared that if this technology was acquired by a civilization before it was ready, disastrous consequences could ensue. For this reason, and because of the Sanctity Edict, Maren Sorel refused to give up this technology at any price. By 2377, however, the crew of Pharae had shared matter synthesizers to help people feed and clothe themselves a number of times. In contrast, the Orallin Arridor and Kol used a portable matter synthesizer to pass themselves off as the Holy Sages of the Takarians. 
In an alternate 2374, the matter synthesizer system on Pharae was heavily damaged by attacks from Krenim warships, forcing the crew to go to emergency rations. In 2377, Pharae acquired technology from a race within the Void that had joined The Alliance which tripled the ship's matter synthesizer efficiency. 
In 2378, the young HERALD manipulated a matter synthesizer to tell Maren Sorel "Make it yourself" when she asked it for coffee. Maren Sorel herself had a tense relationship with her personal matter synthesizer. After it burned a pot roast, not the first time it had done so, she told Spear Kaldar that she had once referred to it as a "glorified toaster" and it had never forgiven her.
