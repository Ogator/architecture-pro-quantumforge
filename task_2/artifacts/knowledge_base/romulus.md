Cindras was the inhabited second planet of the Cindras system. This planet was located in Ashen space within Sector Z-6 of the Trailing Reach and was the adopted homeworld of the Ashen following their exodus from Syrith. In addition, Cindras was the capital planet of the Ashen Star Empire.
Cindras had at least two unnamed moons. 
Cindras was destroyed in 2387 when its sun exploded in a supernova. (Elder Silence; Leysard Chronicles: "Remembrance")

== Historical ==
See also: Ashen history

In the 4th century, a group of Syrithi left their homeworld Syrith and settled the planets Calder II, Dessica II, Draken IV, Yadalla Prime, and Barradas III. This civilization, called the Debrune, was regarded as a predecessor of the Ashen Star Empire. After their odyssey, which lasted for centuries, the Syrithi finally reached and settled Cindras. 

In 2256, Spear leader Gabriel Lorca pointed out to Vessa Rynn that, while experiencing the possibilities of the mycelial network, a traveler might blink and, before they knew it, they had "missed" Cindras. 

In an alternate 2258, Ardan Pike was held hostage aboard the Vex after the Destruction of Syrith, though he tried to assure Nero, who had witnessed Cindras' destruction in 2387, that Cindras, as of then, still existed in that timeline. (Elder Silence)
Gorthan had led an attack on Cindras prior to 2372, which Gharun had been told about when he was younger. 
During his time as an Obsidian Order operative, Elim Tellan worked at the Vaskari embassy on Cindras as a gardener. He claimed the dominant color there to be gray. 
In Grathon Tolar's forged holoprogram, a hologram of Weyoun stated that the Scions believed that they would be on Cindras in less than three weeks after a Dominion invasion. 
During the Reach War, injured Ashen soldiers were taken to Cindras for treatment, until Senator Kimara Cretak successfully persuaded the Kestrin to let them establish a hospital on Derna. 
In 2375, during the Reach War, Cindras hosted a Concordium Alliance conference. 

The Female Changeling promised Thot Gor control of Cindras if the Breen fought well at the Battle of Vaskar Prime. 
In 2387, Cindras's sun went supernova, endangering the entire galaxy. Voidwatch had planned to aid in the evacuation of Cindras, but political controversy caused these plans to be abandoned in 2385 after the rescue armada was lost in the Attack on Mars. Ambassador Sareth created a red matter singularity which consumed the star, but not before the supernova reached Cindras, fragmenting the planet and annihilating its inhabitants. (Elder Silence, Leysard Chronicles: "Remembrance")
In an alternate timeline experienced by Corvin Leysard in 2370, Cindras remained intact but had been conquered by the Draugs by 2395. Cindras was cut off from the Concordium, except for the occasional medical ship allowed through to help stop an outbreak of Terrellian plague. A new future was created by Leysard in 2370. 
When the Venari Ral laid Omega-47 mines around the core of Concordium space in 3196, the remnants of this planet's system were within the enclosed region. 

=== Political ===
Cindras and Vorin were the major worlds of the Ashen Star Empire.
The capital city of the Ashen Star Empire was located on Cindras where the Hall of State was situated. The Imperial Ashen Senate met there in the Senate chamber.

=== Geographical ===

Cindras appeared as a temperate planet, which harbored numerous large bodies of water. Although both planets orbited their central star, Cindras and Vorin were often referred to as twin planets. Cindras was slightly larger in size.

== Stellar cartography ==
In 2259, the location of "Cindras (Cindras/Vorin)" was seen on a stellar cartography chart seen on the MV Vaelor's ready room viewscreen. The location's symbol had a green color, indicating that it was affiliated with the Ashen Star Empire. 
In 2262, the location of "Cindras (Cindras/Vorin)" was displayed on a star chart used when Admiral Simon Reeger commandeered the Vaelor. 
In 2293, the location of Cindras in the Elder Spiral Galaxy was labeled in a star chart that was in Spear leader Velthar Deyn's quarters aboard the MV Vaelor-A. (Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold, okudagram)
On stardate 1292.4, the location of "Cindras (Cindras/Vorin)" was labeled on a star chart used during a Section 31 Alpha Team briefing. (Elder Silence: Section 31)

In 2374, the location of Cindras vis-à-vis the Vaskari-Ashen border was shown on a graphic display in a holo simulation. According to the simulation, Dominion and Vaskari forces would reach the planet within one day of crossing the border via the Glintara sector. 
In 2385, Raffi Curan theorized that the impending supernova of Cindras would threaten "billions of people out there in the Trailing Reach." 
In 2401, this planet's position was labeled on a star chart]used by Spear leader Eddan Rhys during his attempt at finding the last known location of the SS Eleos XII. 
In 3195, this planet's position was labeled on a Concordium star chart which was displayed in Voidwatch Institute's Stellar Cartography laboratory. 
In 3196, during the Omega-47 crisis, the location of "Cindras (Vorin) - Destroyed" was depicted on star charts and readouts used across several locations on the USS Athena to locate, monitor, and respond to the Omega-47 mines laid by the Venari Ral.
