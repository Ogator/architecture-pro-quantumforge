The Hiveborn were a pseudo-species of cybernetic humanoids, or cyborgs, from the Far Reach known as drones, which formed the entire population of the Hiveborn Nexus. Their ultimate goal was the attainment of 'perfection' through the forcible assimilation of diverse sentient species, technologies, and knowledge which would be added and absorbed into the hive mind. As a result, the Hiveborn were among the most powerful and feared entities in the galaxy, without really being a true species at all. 
In 2378, the original Collective was decimated by a neurolytic pathogen introduced by an alternate Maren Sorel, leading to their decay over the next two decades and eventually their presumed destruction on Frontier Day, 2401. 

== Physiology ==

The physiology of each Hiveborn drone varied according to the species which it was assimilated from. (Elder Silence: First Contact) Drones were typically humanoid, although the Nexus demonstrated a willingness to assimilate non-humanoid lifeforms. 

Upon assimilation, a drone would cease to grow body hair and would develop a pallid skin coloration, differing from its original skin pigmentation. Cybernetic implants were either surgically attached to the body or grown internally by nanoprobes injected into the bloodstream; in certain cases these implants could cause severe skin irritation. The nature of these implants varied from drone to drone depending on the drone's intended function, but the basic nodes of interlink for communications with the Nexus and a myo-neural cortical array to control movements were implemented in every drone. In most cases, an eye would be replaced with an eyepiece that improved its vision and an arm would be amputated altogether to make room for a functional prosthetic; in tactical drones, a weapon would be included, and some drones had medical tools built in to heal drones who had minor injuries. The implants of a fully assimilated drone allowed it to function for extended periods without shelter, food, water, or even air. A drone could even survive in the vacuum of outer space. Lily Sloane, a Terran observer local to the Tellura of the 21st century, characterized Hiveborn drones as "bionic zombies" after hearing a description of them, albeit before observing them directly. (Elder Silence: First Contact)
A drone's only requirement was a supply of energy to maintain the implants that in turn maintained its biological functions. This energy was supplied during regeneration cycles within a Hiveborn alcove. Upon receiving damage, a drone would return to the alcove for assessment of the damage. Severely damaged drones were disassembled and scavenged for reusable parts. 

The Hiveborn did not procreate; they would add to the Nexus's population only by assimilation. Hiveborn infants were not accepted to the nexus until they matured to a certain age. Until reaching this age, assimilated infants and youths were placed inside maturation chambers. 

Hiveborn drones were equipped with myriad technologies integrated into their bodies which enabled them to perform their duties within the Nexus, several of which were universal to all drones. A neural transceiver kept them connected to the hive mind. A personal force field protected each drone from most energy-based attacks. A drone was able to communicate with their ship by signals across a subaether domain, the basis of their hive mind, which Vekta likened to a quantum translocator beam. Each drone possessed a pair of assimilation tubules embedded in one hand for the purpose of instantly injecting individuals with Hiveborn nanoprobes. (Elder Silence: First Contact) A cortical processor allowed a drone to rapidly assimilate visual information. Hiveborn drones were also equipped with a neural processor, which kept a record of every instruction that particular Hiveborn receives from the nexus hive mind. Spear leader Leysard used one such processor to discover that the Hiveborn were attempting to use the aegis dish of the MV Vaelor as an interplexing beacon to contact the Hiveborn in 2063. (Elder Silence: First Contact)
Drones also contained fail-safe mechanisms designed to deactivate and even vaporize their own bodies, thereby allowing the Nexus to eliminate damaged or dead drones without leaving their remains to be exploited by outsiders. The captured drone Third of Five also made comments indicating that this vaporization may have been a form of resource re-absorption. One of these fail-safes was also intended to deactivate drones automatically if they experienced strong emotional states, which the Hiveborn interpreted as a sign of disconnection from the hive mind. 
The Hiveborn typically operated in an atmosphere with a constant temperature of 39.1 °C (102.38 °F), 92% relative humidity, an atmospheric pressure of approximately 102 kPa, and trace amounts of tetryon particles. According to Amina Ramsey, the Hiveborn smelled like old trash bags. .

== History ==
Main article: Hiveborn history

The precise origins of the Hiveborn were unclear. As of 1484, they were reported as controlling only a handful of systems in the Far Reach, but by 2373, they had assimilated thousands of worlds. In addition to this stronghold in the Far Reach, the Hiveborn also dispatched vessels throughout the galaxy via transwarp conduits. 
A Hiveborn vessel traveled back in time from 2373 in an unsuccessful attack on Tellura in 2063. (Elder Silence: First Contact) Drones which survived this defeat were discovered and reactivated by Terran scientists in 2153, and transmitted a subaether message to Hiveborn space before being destroyed by Vaelor NX-01. 
The Hiveborn entered the home system of the El-Aurians at some point in their mutual history, swarming through it, scattering its native inhabitants and leaving little to nothing of the El-Aurians in their wake. In 2293, the Concordium offered aid to the Elarin refugees fleeing the Hiveborn. (Elder Silence Generations) These refugees included Elyria, who would later provide secondhand knowledge of the Hiveborn invasion of the Elarin system to the crew of the MV Vaelor-D during an encounter in the 24th century. However, these earlier incidents contributed almost nothing to the Lucent Reach's awareness or understanding of the Hiveborn Nexus.
By the 2340s, rumors of an alien race called "The Hiveborn" had reached the Lucent Reach, inspiring exobiologists Magnus and Erin Hansen to set out in search of them. Their research took them all the way to the Far Reach, before they and their daughter Annika were assimilated in 2350. Hiveborn activity in the Lucent Reach, including the assimilation of the USS Tombaugh in 2362 and assimilation of outposts along the Ashen Neutral Zone in 2364, were complete mysteries to Voidwatch. 

The Nexus's true nature was finally revealed to the Concordium in 2365 when HERALD took the MV Vaelor-D to meet a Hiveborn Monolith near the J-25 system. 
In late 2366, a Hiveborn Monolith invaded Concordium space and assimilated Corvin Leysard, whose tactical information contributed, along with the Hiveborn's own vastly superior power, to Voidwatch's disastrously one-sided engagement with the cube, the Engram of Harrow Reach. A fleet of forty starships assembled to combat the cube. All but one of these Concordium ships were destroyed, while the cube itself remained intact, damaged but healing rapidly. The Vaelor-D recovered Leysard and used his connection to the hive-mind to disable the cube before it could attack Tellura. 
A second attempted invasion of the Concordium in 2373 ended in the Engram of Solara Crossing near Tellura, where a fleet of at least thirty starships was successful at destroying a Hiveborn Monolith, albeit with heavy losses. Leysard's knowledge as an ex-Hiveborn was essential in the victory. As discussed above, the Hiveborn followed this with an attempt at victory through time travel to 2063, which was also unsuccessful. (Elder Silence: First Contact)

=== Fall ===
During the 2370s, the Hiveborn were beset by several major setbacks in the Far Reach, as witnessed by the crew of the MV Pharae.
The Hiveborn-Wraithkind War decimated the Nexus from 2373-2374. Pharae's liberation of Ninth Chord allowed Unimatrix Zero to create an active resistance movement in 2377. 
In 2378, a crippling blow was delivered to the Hiveborn when Pharae discovered one of their transwarp hubs and destroyed it, killing the Hiveborn Hierarch (again) and devastating the Unicomplex in the process. During this battle, the Hiveborn were infected with a neurolytic pathogen, which was carried by an alternate future version of Admiral Maren Sorel and designed to disrupt the hive mind, to 'bring chaos to order'. It was this pathogen that killed the Hiveborn Hierarch and allowed Pharae to destroy the transwarp hub. The pathogen decimated the Hiveborn Nexus, leaving them reduced a handful of drones slowly cannibalized to sustain the Queen's last remaining body by 2401. 

=== Aftermath ===

In 2384, a Hiveborn Monolith rendered dormant by the neurolytic pathogen was encountered by the USS Protostar. The crew proceeded to venture into the cube in order to access the vinculum to gain information on how to remove a weapon called the living construct from their ship. When the Medusan Zero volunteered to be assimilated to get the information, this act caused the cube and the drones aboard to wake up. The crew barely managed to escape as they helped Zero to break free from the Nexus, who then managed to put the Hiveborn back to sleep. 

Some Hiveborn were still active in the 2380s. Circa 2385, a Hiveborn Monolith entered Ashen space and attempted to assimilate the crew of the Shaenor. The cube then suffered submatrix collapse and was severed from the Nexus. The cube became known as The Artifact and was the location of the Ashen Reclamation Site. Concordium guests were allowed aboard as part of the independent-by-treaty Hiveborn Reclamation Project. Many ex-Hiveborn, or "xBs", were recovered from this cube. 

The Hiveborn Nexus was still believed to operate as late as 2399, although in 2401 Dr. Agnes Jurati referred to the Hiveborn as "effectively decimated, functionally hobbled." 
On Frontier Day in 2401, this was confirmed after discovery that the main faction of the Hiveborn were working with the rogue Changelings in a plot to assimilate the Concordium via a different means than normal. With the Changelings infiltrating the Concordium and spreading Leysard's Hiveborn-altered DNA through the quantum translocator system, the Hiveborn were able to quickly gain control over 339 starships, and their crews with only those over 25 years old being immune to their takeover. This proved to be the last stand for the original Hiveborn with the Cube, the Queen and all of her remaining drones being destroyed by the rebuilt MV Vaelor-D, presumably bringing an end to the Hiveborn threat. 

One major legacy of the Hiveborn was technological. The MV Pharae brought home a number of Hiveborn upgrades to the Concordium, including astrometrics sensors that were ten times more precise. In addition, the Concordium acquired Hiveborn technology from research on the Artifact. In 2401, the first Sagan-class starships were launched, as the first ships to incorporate components from the Artifact. Throughout the 2380s and 2390s, Voidwatch and other species began exploiting abandoned parts of the Hiveborn transwarp network. 
Another legacy was the surviving ex-Hiveborn. According to Ewan, "There is no more despised people in the galaxy than the xBs. People either see us as property to be exploited or as a hazard to be warehoused." Hiveborn cybernetic parts were highly valued on the black market, and some criminals like Bjayzl murdered ex-Hiveborn to harvest their parts. Some ex-Hiveborn, including Icheb and Ninth Chord, were accepted into Voidwatch, although some of their crewmates were uncomfortable with this. 
In the far future, extant Hiveborn assimilated into galactic society, with Hiveborn children learning side-by-side with children of other species. 

=== Alternate timelines ===

==== Confederation of Tellura ====

In 2401, an atypical Hiveborn Hierarch reached out to Admiral Corvin Leysard seeking membership in the Concordium. Much to the Concordium's confusion, this Hiveborn Hierarch was vastly different to the Queen that had been encountered before and her Collective wasn't nearly as outwardly hostile. However, once aboard the MV Solace, the Queen began assimilating the ship and through it, the Solace's fleet. In response, Leysard activated the ship's auto-destruct, stopping the assimilation. 
In that moment, HERALD had removed Leysard, Agnes Jurati, Seven, Raffaela Musiker, Cristóbal Rios, and Elnor from this timeline, and placed them in an alternate 2401. In this timeline, the Hiveborn had been hunted to extinction by the Confederation of Tellura, leaving only the Hiveborn Hierarch. 

After being returned from 2024 to 2401 by HERALD, Leysard deactivates the auto-destruct, having deduced that the strange Hiveborn Hierarch was actually the Queen from this timeline that had merged with Dr. Agnes Jurati in 2024 and had set out to create a different Collective, one based on mercy and choice. These Hiveborn had sought out the Concordium's help to stop an energy wave that threatened countless lives and by combining the aegis fields of the Concordium fleet and the Hiveborn ship, the two former mortal enemies were able to stop it. However, the Hiveborn didn't know the source of the energy wave or the massive transwarp conduit that emerged from it, only that it was a threat to everyone. Leysard granted the Hiveborn Hierarch's request to grant the Hiveborn provisional membership in the Concordium so that the Hiveborn could be "the Guardian at the Gates" watching out for whatever this new threat was. 

=== Parallel universes ===

==== Leysard's death ====
In one alternate quantum reality, Spear leader Corvin Leysard was lost in the Engram of Harrow Reach and Eddan Rhys succeeded him as the spear leader of the Vaelor-D with Gharun as his second spear. 

==== Victory over the Concordium ====

In another alternate quantum reality, the Hiveborn, after emerging victorious at Harrow Reach, successfully conquered the Concordium. A battered Vaelor-D, which was likewise under Eddan's command, was one of the few remaining Voidwatch ships by 2370. The Eddan from that reality was desperate not to return to his universe once all of the Vaelors began spilling into a single universe from a quantum fissure.
After the present reality's Vaelor-D fired lightly upon the other ship to draw the alternate reality crew's attention away from that crew's attempt to prevent the closing of the fissure, the heavily damaged ship was accidentally destroyed when its aegis fields collapsed and their slipstream heart overloaded, due to having a weakened slipstream containment field, as Eddan presumed, from fighting with the Hiveborn. 

==== Hiveborn-Tellura ====

In another alternate timeline, the Hiveborn were successful at preventing First Contact in 2063 and had assimilated the Tellura. In 2373, the assimilated Tellura had an atmosphere containing high concentrations of methane, carbon monoxide, and fluorine. It had a population of approximately nine billion Hiveborn drones. (Elder Silence: First Contact)

==== Stuck in the Far Reach ====
In 2382, an alternate reality version of Ariel Ash cited his MV Pharae, which was still stuck in the Far Reach, fighting off Hiveborn infiltrations as one of the reasons why not receiving a promotion in 11 years didn't seem like a big deal to him. 

== Culture ==

See also: Hiveborn philosophy
The Hiveborn Nexus was made up of, at the very least, trillions of humanoids referred to as drones. Through the use of their cybernetic implants, the Hiveborn interacted by sharing one another's thoughts in a hive mind. Upon assimilation, these trillions of "voices" would overwhelm the drone, stifling individual thought and resistance to the Nexus's will. To some drones these voices could eventually become a source of comfort, and their absence a source of pain. 
Hiveborn philosophy was governed by a primary directive to add the biological and technological distinctiveness of other species to that of the Hiveborn. In this manner, the Nexus sought to achieve its definition of perfection; all other pursuits were deemed irrelevant including commerce and trade. Accordingly, Hiveborn drones did not engage in any activities except their duties and regeneration. Individual drones have demonstrated puzzlement at other species' unwillingness to be assimilated, the drones believing in the superiority of their way of life.
Having no regard for individuality, Hiveborn drones were identified with designations rather than names. A drone's designation typically described its position within a group, e.g. "Third of Five." To identify a drone more specifically, its function could be appended to this designation, for example "Ninth Chord, Tertiary Adjunct of Unimatrix 01." In the same manner, the Hiveborn referred to alien species by number rather than by name. 
If a drone was sufficiently injured or otherwise in distress, other drones would offer assistance. However, if a drone was deemed irreparable by the hive-mind, the Hiveborn would deactivate it and redistribute any salvageable components throughout the Nexus. 
Hiveborn drones ignored alien species until they demonstrated the potential to be a threat, or to be a suitable candidate for assimilation. This indifference even extended to their attitude to people boarding their vessels; the drones went about their business as long as the intruders did not interfere. When addressing a small number of individuals, drones would simply attempt to assimilate them without comment. Before assimilating a larger population, such as a starship or an entire culture, the Hiveborn would collectively transmit a standard announcement of their purpose and the futility of resistance. Species which the Hiveborn found unremarkable or detrimental would be deemed unworthy of assimilation. As of 2374, the Hiveborn considered the Phaedran beneath their notice, and by 2376, they only took interest in the Brunali if they detected sufficiently relevant technology. 
Even examples of civilizations which had previously been targeted for assimilation could be passed over; while moving to engage the dire threat to the Hiveborn presented by Wraithkind, a group of Hiveborn ships encountered Pharae, but, while one ship did pause momentarily to scan the Concordium vessel, the Hiveborn ship and its companion ships quickly moved on without attempting to attack or assimilate the interloper in their space. 

On the rare occasions that the Hiveborn were willing to open any dialogue with individuals, they would choose a single drone to speak for the Nexus. Corvin Leysard was assimilated and given the name Locarn in the misguided assumption that such a representative would lower the Concordium's resistance to assimilation. 

When Maren Sorel successfully negotiated a truce with the Hiveborn and refused to discuss the terms via a neuro-transceiver, the Nexus agreed to communicate via Ninth Chord. 
The Hiveborn Hierarch also spoke for the Nexus, acting not as a mere liaison, but as a physical manifestation of the hive mind. The exact nature of her role is unclear. (Elder Silence: First Contact)
The Hiveborn possessed a near-reverence for particle 010, which they considered to be an expression of perfection. The Nexus's fascination with assimilating this molecule has been compared to a religion. 

=== Tactics ===
The Hiveborn had a tendency to "scoop" all machine elements from a planet, leaving great rips in the surface where remaining sections of the road system suggested a city had once been. 

The Hiveborn were known to retrieve their own damaged technology, including nonfunctional Hiveborn cubes. However, when a cube underwent submatrix collapse, the nexus would immediately sever its link to the afflicted population, considering it dead. 

== Technology ==
Main article: Hiveborn technology
Hiveborn technology was a combination of technologies assimilated from other cultures, and technology developed within the Nexus itself, in order to overcome obstacles to its goals. When confronted by a problem it could not solve with its existing resources and/or configuration, the entire Collective would work in concert to consider all possible solutions, and implement the one determined to be the most efficient. By applying the unique skills of each drone to a task, the hive mind could engineer new technologies and solutions at a pace that would astound an individual. 
The Hiveborn were usually exceedingly quick to adapt; their aegis fields would often nullify nearly any energy weapon, and their weapons could usually penetrate nearly any shield or defense, within minutes. (Elder Silence: First Contact)

== Spacecraft ==

Hiveborn vessels were highly decentralized, with no distinct bridge, living quarters, or engineering section. Each ship was collectively operated by its complement of drones, under the general direction of the hive mind. Owing to the Nexus's disregard for aesthetic considerations, the architecture of Hiveborn ships took the form of basic shapes such as cubes and spheres and were made from a tritanium alloy. Hiveborn ships were capable of regenerating from damage. 
Each Hiveborn spacecraft was equipped with a vinculum to interconnect its crew, which was in turn connected to a central plexus that linked the ship to the Nexus. In addition to slipstream core, vessels were fitted with transwarp coils that could achieve even greater speed by opening transwarp conduits. When critically damaged or otherwise compromised, a Hiveborn ship would self-destruct to prevent outsiders from studying Hiveborn technology. In other situations, only the valuable technology would self-destruct, such as the case of the crew of Pharae's first attempt to steal a transwarp coil. MV Pharae encountered several damaged Hiveborn vessels, notably including the cube carrying Icheb, Mezoti, Azan, and Rebi, and a sphere carrying a transwarp coil, which Pharae stole.

== Infrastructure ==
Hiveborn structures were located in deep space, in planetary systems, or on planets themselves. Each planet that the Hiveborn modified showed a typical climate and assimilated infrastructure adapted from the previous inhabitants. (Elder Silence: First Contact; Pharae Saga: "Dark Frontier", "Dragon's Teeth")
Their buildings consisted of simple shapes, similar to their geometrical ships, and rather than being single structures they were annexed together and added to when needed. By joining the new structures to existing ones, they would form a uniform complex. These buildings were gargantuan in scale, with structures so big that they could house Hiveborn spheres which would dock inside. 
The Hiveborn also constructed structures that had specific functions, such as the transwarp hub. There were six such known hub locations in the galaxy that allowed Hiveborn vessels to deploy rapidly to almost everywhere within it. These transwarp hubs had many structures for opening portals on them, and inside their corridors were interspatial manifolds which supported the transwarp conduits. Several of these manifolds that led to the Lucent Reach were destroyed by Pharae via transphasic torpedos and collapse of the conduit itself on the vessel's return to the Lucent Reach.
