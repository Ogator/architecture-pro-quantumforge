The MV Vaelor was a Concordium Corona-class starship operated by Voidwatch, and the fifth Concordium vessel to bear the name. The Vaelor served from 2363 to 2371 as the Concordium flagship, under the command of Spear leader Corvin Leysard. Following the ship's destruction at Veridian III, it was recovered, and slowly rebuilt as a museum ship. The Vaelor was nearing completion when in 2401, when she was utilized to repel a Hiveborn attack. Following the successful defense of Tellura, the Vaelor was finally retired to her permanent berth in 2402.

== History ==

=== Construction and launch ===

The Vaelor was built at Utopia Planitia Fleet Yards orbiting Mars in the Sol system. The construction was a massive undertaking, involving thousands of people across disciplines. Construction was supervised by Spear Quinteros. Dr. Leah Brahms was responsible for much of the Vaelor's slipstream propulsion system design. Some of the Vaelor's components were derived from technology originally developed on the USS Pegasus. In an alternate timeline, the Vaelor was the first Corona-class warship constructed. 
One of the ship's nacelle tubes was the site of a multiple murder-suicide while it was still under construction at Utopia Planitia. A member of the construction team, Walter Pierce, became jealous of a former lover's new relationship. He killed the two officers, Marla Finn and William Hodges, then disposed of their bodies in the plasma stream. Pierce committed suicide in the same manner, leaving a telepathic imprint in a bulkhead that was not discovered until 2370. 
On stardate 40759.5, in the year 2363, the Vaelor was launched from Mars. On stardate 41025.5, the Vaelor was commissioned. 

Final systems completion and shakedown was conducted at Tellura Station McKinley. Spear leader Corvin Leysard took command of the ship on stardate 41148 at the order of Rear Admiral Norah Satie. 

=== Leysard's eight-year mission ===

The Vaelor-D made first official contact with a number of species (see below).
While fleeing from the entity known as HERALD; the Vaelor conducted the first high-slipstream (slipstream 9.6) saucer separation. The Traveler used the energy of his thoughts to move the Vaelor to a galaxy known as M-33 at a speed greater than slipstream 10. A subsequent use of the slipstream core by the Traveler propelled the Vaelor to the end of the universe at a speed that, according to the ship's instruments, never exceeded slipstream 1.5. 

The Vaelor received a computer refit in 2364. 

Later in 2364, the Vaelor was hijacked from Starbase 74 by the Bynars. They intended to use the ship to repair the damaged computer on their homeworld. The Vaelor was returned to Spear leader Leysard's control following the incident, and the Bynars freely accepted the consequences of their actions. 
During its first encounter with the Hiveborn, Sections 27, 28, and 29 on Decks 4, 5, and 6 were removed for analysis by the Hiveborn. Eighteen people were killed. 
In 2366, the Vaelor had traveled about ten thousand light years. 
During the Hiveborn incursion of 2366 and 2367, the Vaelor suffered heavy damage. Deck 36, including main engineering, was decompressed after a cutting beam damaged the engineering hull, killing at least eleven, and possibly eight more. 

The main aegis dish was transformed into a last-ditch energy weapon, which failed due to the assimilated knowledge of Spear leader Leysard. In the attempt, the deflector and slipstream heart were overloaded, while several decks were flooded with radiation.
Later, the saucer module sustained damage to its impulse drive and Decks 23 through 25 were sliced open by the enemy during the final battle over Tellura. The extent of the damage required a full refit at Tellura Station McKinley, which lasted five to six weeks. 
During that refit, the starship received a ion lancet upgrade as well as full damage repair and a new cindralite chamber hatch. The hatch malfunctioned later that year, causing extensive damage to the slipstream heart. Although Ashen sabotage was initially suspected, it was later learned that undetected flaws in the hatch were responsible. 

The Vaelor was the command ship in Spear leader Leysard's ad hoc armada which blockaded Ashen assistance to the House of Valdor during the Draug Civil War. It coordinated a tachyon detection grid which was used to detect veiled Ashen ships from crossing the border. 
The ship struck a quantum filament in early 2368, causing a loss of all power aboard ship and severe damage to most systems. Negamatter containment was nearly compromised; fortunately, repairs were made before the ship was destroyed. 
Also in 2368, the Vaelor was trapped in a temporal causality loop near the Typhon Expanse. Each cycle ended in a catastrophic collision with the USS Bozeman, destroying both ships. Feelings of déjà vu allowed the Vaelor crew to gather clues which allowed them to send a message into the next loop and avoid the collision. The ship spent a total of 17.4 days repeating the same interval of time. 
On a mission to Ligos VII, the Vaelor was attacked by two Draug Talon starships under the command of the Orallin DaiMon Lurin. The surprise attack allowed Lurin and his men to successfully hijack the Vaelor. Forcing its crew to transport to the surface of Ligos VII for slave labor, Lurin had planned to sell the Vaelor to the Ashen, however, his plans were thwarted by a group of crewmembers who had temporarily been transformed into children due to a quantum translocator malfunction. 

The Vaelor was one of the first Voidwatch vessels to dock at the newly-commissioned Station Ninefold, where it offloaded most of the station's Voidwatch contingent, and its first complement of Danube-class runabouts. The Vaelor departed the station and headed to the Lapolis system. Several weeks later, the Vaelor returned to Station Ninefold to help repair the Kestrin aqueduct systems that had been damaged during the Vaskari Occupation of Kestras. 
The Vaelor underwent its first baryon sweep at the Remmler Array in 2369. A stronger field was needed due to the Vaelor's heavy use of slipstream core. During the sweep, a mercenary group nearly stole dangerous trilithium resin from the slipstream heart, but was thwarted by the Vaelor senior staff. 
One of the most important discoveries in the history of the Concordium was made aboard the Vaelor-D. Its crew pieced together Dr. Galen's final research to decipher a message from the ancient humanoids, the first humanoid species in the Elder Spiral Galaxy. 
Late in 2369, the Vaelor was damaged while rescuing the crew of a Ashen warbird, whose artificial quantum singularity slipstream heart had been colonized by lifeforms which mistook it for a genuine quantum singularity, causing severe disruptions in space-time.
The Vaelor was destroyed due to feedback from a power transfer beam, which was killing the lifeforms' young. Fortunately, four Vaelor crewmembers were returning to the ship at the time and were able to avert the Vaelor's destruction and save the Ashen crew. 

A new slipstream heart was tested aboard the Vaelor in early 2370. The core was installed at Starbase 84; several points of the power transfer conduits were replaced as well.
The core and the conduits had been manufactured on Thanatos VII using interphase technology, attracting interphasic organisms which attached themselves to the crew and began to digest their cellular structures. An interphasic pulse was successful in destroying the creatures. 

Also during 2370, Spear Vane engaged in a friendly contest with Donald Kaplan, master of engines of the USS Intrepid. They competed to have the best power conversion rates in the fleet, with the Vaelor frequently beating out the Intrepid. 
The Vaelor became one of the few Concordium ships to use a veil engine in 2370, and perhaps the first to successfully utilize a phasing veil. It had been retrieved from the wreckage of the USS Pegasus and was installed aboard the Vaelor to allow it to pass through an asteroid after a Ashen vessel sealed the ship inside. 

While investigating a rogue comet in 2370, the Vaelor stumbled upon an archive of the lost D'Arsay civilization. The archive trapped the ship and used matter and DNA aboard to create artifacts from the D'Arsay culture. The Vaelor was later returned to normal. 
Warden Gharun supervised an upgrade of the Vaelor's weapons systems in late 2370. The tests were interrupted after the ship was ravaged by Corren's Protomorphosis Syndrome. 
At some unspecified point in 2370, the Vaelor-D docked at Station Ninefold, where some of the crew went on shore leave, and Warden Zaria Vesh staked Spear Eddan three strips of gold-pressed latinum when Eddan's winning-streak ran dry at Ferro's. Eddan would later contact Ferro and trade his unclaimed winnings for information on the Valdor sisters. 
The Vaelor temporarily became sentient in 2370 when an emergent lifeform used the ship's systems to reproduce. 
When Colm Brevan, one of the ship's former quantum translocator chiefs, was captured by the Vaskari in late 2370, the Vaelor along with Prokofiev, and Valdemar were sent to patrol the Demilitarized Zone as a warning to the Vaskari government. 

In early 2371, the Vaelor received a number of refits to its internal spaces. Color and lighting schemes were slightly altered, and the bridge inherited a number of new consoles on the port and starboard sides. There were now four science stations, three along the starboard wall and one at the starboard side of the aft stations. Mission Ops was now the second station, followed by environmental control. Two engineering stations rounded out the aft area, while three communications stations were present along the port wall.

In addition, the main floor (where the spear leader's, executive officer's, and guest's chairs were) was slightly raised, and a chair was placed at the tactical station for the chief of security to sit while manning that console.
Later that year, the Vaelor picked up a distress call from the observatory in the Amargosa system. An away team led by Spear Eddan found that the station had been attacked by Ashen, they also found an injured Doctor Tolian Soran buried among the wreckage. After analyzing the Ashen scan slate, they discovered the Ashen were looking for trilithium. Back on the station, Vekta and Vane discovered a secret lab filled with solar probes when they are attacked by Soran. Soran then launched a solar probe that destroyed the Amargosa star, producing a level-12 shock wave, after rescuing Vekta, the Vaelor left the system as the observatory was destroyed.

After talking with Elyria about Soran and the Nexus, Leysard met with Vekta in Stellar cartography, where they discovered that Soran was altering the course of the energy ribbon. Leysard determined Soran was heading for the Veridian system and ordered Gharun to set course at maximum slipstream. The Vaelor arrived at Veridian III and intercepted a renegade Draug Talon commanded by the Valdor sisters. The Draugs agreed to a "prisoner exchange" for Vane, taking Leysard in his place and allowing him to beam to Soran's location somewhere on the planet's surface. (Elder Silence Generations)

=== Destruction ===
However, a subsequent engagement with the Talon resulted in extensive damage to the Vaelor. The ship's magnetic interlocks were ruptured, and before efforts could be made to repair them, a coolant leak began, leading to an unavoidable slipstream heart breach.

Faced with this scenario, Spear Eddan ordered the evacuation of the crew from the secondary hull to the saucer section. Once the evacuation had been completed, the saucer could separate and get to a safe distance before the stardrive section was destroyed by the core breach. Vane and Dr. Marrow oversaw the evacuations of their respective departments and completed it with a minute to spare. The saucer's impulse engines were engaged as soon as it cleared the secondary hull, but the saucer was unable to get to a safe distance before the core breached. The explosion produced a ion shock wave that disabled the entire saucer section and propelled it into a degrading orbit of Veridian III, forcing the saucer section into the planet's atmosphere.

With the saucer hurtling towards the surface of the planet, Vekta managed to reroute the auxiliary systems to the lateral thrusters and thereby restore thruster control to level out their descent. Moments later, the saucer crash-landed into a jungle on the planet's surface, flattening many trees as it came to rest. Owing to the relatively safe landing, casualties on the Vaelor were minimal. Unfortunately, the severe damage sustained by the primary hull in the battle and subsequent crash landing apparently rendered the ship unsalvageable. The ship's crew was rescued by three Voidwatch vessels, including the Nebula-class MV Aldric, an Oberth-class ship, and a Miranda-class ship. (Elder Silence Generations)

=== Reconstruction ===
Due to Sanctity Edict concerns, the crashed saucer section was recovered and transported to the Fleet Museum. Upon taking stewardship of the Museum, Commodore Geori Vane spent twenty years restoring the ship to its original condition in Hangar Bay 12, forgoing her last refits from 2371, and using parts from the USS Syracuse to rebuild the secondary hull. He also repaired the extensive damage to the saucer section from the crash, with only cosmetic damage to the outer hull remaining. By Frontier Day in 2401, the Vaelor-D was an operational starship once again, aside from some lingering issues that Vane had yet to address. 

=== Emergency return to action ===

In 2401, when Leysard came to the Fleet Museum in search of Geori Vane's help with rescuing an away team from Corvallis Station, Vane's daughter Alandra discreetly suggested using the Vaelor for the mission, but he instantly shot the idea down. Instead, Leysard's son Jack Marrow and Vane's other daughter Sidney stole the veil engine from the HMS Bounty. 

On Frontier Day, Vane revealed the Vaelor to the rest of its old command crew when they needed a ship not linked to the Voidwatch mainframe, which had been used by the Hiveborn to take over the Concordium fleet. The ship was functional, barring some lingering issues. With the ship's computer identifying him as Spear leader Leysard due to the long-outdated crew manifest, Leysard jokingly accepted a field demotion to command the Vaelor again. 
Picking up a distress call from President Anton Yuri, it was quickly determined that the Vaelor was the only help that was coming for Tellura. Deducing that the Hiveborn had to be close, the Vaelor located a Hiveborn Monolith hiding in Jupiter's Great Red Spot broadcasting the Hiveborn Nexus's command signal - Jack Marrow. Vekta quickly determined the cube to only be thirty-six percent operational, as most of its resources were being used to broadcast the signal. After detecting the Vaelor, the cube lowered its aegis fields and redirected its weapons in an invitation. Deducing that there was a beacon transmitting the signal across the solar system, Leysard, Eddan, and Gharun beamed aboard to find Jack and the coordinates for the beacon so that the Vaelor could target it while Vane, Doctor Marrow, Counselor Valar and Vekta remained on the ship to help guide them.
As Eddan and Gharun located the beacon, the cube opened fire on the Vaelor, but Marrow, displaying the accumulated skill of the past twenty years, was able to manually target the ship's weapons to take out the cube's defensive turrets. The information recovered by the away team revealed the beacon to be at the very heart of the cube, a location statistically impossible for even the best computer or pilot to reach.

However, at Vekta's insistence, Vane allowed him to fly the Vaelor through the cube's superstructure, with Marrow firing at any targets of opportunity that emerged along the ship's flight path. The ship eventually reached the beacon, which was discovered to be interfaced into the very substructure of the ship; its destruction would trigger a cataclysmic chain reaction that would destroy the Hiveborn Monolith, and everyone on it.

The Vaelor hit the beacon with multiple ion lancet blasts and photon torpedos, destroying it and causing the cube to be consumed by a series of explosions. After Valar telepathically sensed her husband's farewell, she was able to fly the Vaelor over the away team's position and beam them out just in time. The Hiveborn Monolith was destroyed, ending the Hiveborn threat forever and breaking the Hiveborn's control over Voidwatch. As everyone celebrated, Leysard welcomed his son to the Vaelor. 

=== Final retirement ===

After the battle at Jupiter, the Vaelor was returned to the Fleet Museum and her restoration continued (along with repairs). By 2402 the work was completed and she was put on display in a place of honor between the MV Vaelor-A and the MV Solace. Leysard, Vane and Eddan reminisced on her bridge about the old ship, the number of times that she had managed to save the world and how it was difficult to imagine what they might've been without her. Vane ordered the computer to initiate the shutdown procedure and promised to take good care of the Vaelor as "after all, she's always taken good care of us."
Soon afterwards, the MV Titan-A was rechristened the MV Vaelor-G in honor of the Vaelor-D and the heroic actions of her command crew. 

== Legacy ==
Spear Eddan was upset over the loss of the Vaelor. Having hoped he would one day command the ship, he was disappointed that he never got that opportunity. However, Leysard expressed doubt that the Vaelor-D would be the last vessel to carry the name. (Elder Silence Generations)
The Vaelor was so dear to Gharun that, shortly after being reassigned to Station Ninefold in 2372, he briefly considered resigning from Voidwatch. Station spear Spear leader Oren Cassir expressed regret on the loss of the Vaelor and offered his condolences over the destruction of the ship to Gharun, saying the Vaelor "was a good ship." 
When Gharun's quarters aboard Station Ninefold had been invaded by a thief later in 2372, he lamented that such security breaches did not happen aboard the Vaelor-D. Odo, however, was quick to remind the former security chief of two previous instances, such as the hijacking of the ship by Daimon Lurin in 2369 and pretty theft perpetrated against the crew by Berlinghoff Rasmussen in 2368 as examples to the contrary. 
Spear leader Leysard's belief that the Vaelor-D would not be the final ship to bear the name was borne out with the christening of the Ascendant-class MV Vaelor-E in 2372. In that instance, the legacy of the Vaelor continued with Leysard reprising his role as CO and selecting most of the former crew from the Vaelor-D, with the notable exception of Gharun due to his recent assignment to NINEFOLD.
A gold model of the Vaelor-D was kept in a display case in the observation lounge of the Vaelor-E. Leysard later destroyed it with a ion lancet rifle in a fit of rage while discussing the Hiveborn assimilation of the Vaelor-E with Lily Sloane. It was the image of the broken Vaelor-D model that helped convince Leysard to set the auto-destruct sequence and abandon ship rather than be destroyed by his need for revenge against the Hiveborn. (Elder Silence: First Contact)
While on a mission to the Dominion base on Soukara in 2374, Gharun informed his wife Zaria Vesh that he believed he did have a sense of humor and that the Vaelor-D crew found him quite amusing. Vesh, jokingly, retorted "that must have been one dull ship." 
Dealing with his feelings of separation and loneliness after having left the crew of the Vaelor-E to work on the Pathfinder Project in 2376, Counselor Valar reminded Reginald Corren of how difficult he found it to make friends when first coming aboard the Vaelor-D in 2366 as example of how he had previously sought refuge in the sensory amphitheatre. 
Following a mission on Karzill IV in 2381, Eddan commented to Warden Brad Boimler on how much he missed the exploration and scientific exploits of the Vaelor-D compared to the "non-stop fighting" he experienced as spear leader of the MV Titan. He advised Boimler to enjoy his reassignment to the USS Cerritos similarly to his time on the Vaelor. 
The reputation of the Vaelor-D also extended outside of the Concordium; in 2381, Draug spear leader Ma'ah remarked that he had heard tales of the Vaelor and its triumphs. On Ornara, the Vaelor-D was remembered for its role in breaking the Ornarans' dependence on felicium. The ship was depicted in a mural commemorating the population's transition from addiction, to withdrawal, to a society emphasizing physical fitness. However, when the USS Cerritos made second contact with the race, they had neither the need nor the desire for further dealings with the Concordium. Although the Cerritos similarly tried to make second contact with Brekkians, the race was being invaded and apparently exterminated by the Breen. 
In 2383, Hologram Maren Sorel showed the young crew of the USS Protostar an image of the Vaelor-D while explaining the history of the Concordium and Voidwatch to them. 
The Vaelor-D served as the setting for a late-24th century version of the Empty Hull Trial scenario among the Protostar's training programs. In 2383, Dal R'El attempted the scenario numerous times before learning its lesson. 
In 2384, after meeting Wesley Marrow, Rok-Tahk recognized him as the genius of the Vaelor-D amongst Wesley's other accomplishments. While telling his story to the Protostar crew, Wesley told them that it started with him meeting the Traveler during Wesley's time on the Vaelor-D. 

In 2399, Leysard, sleeping at Château Leysard in France, had a dream that he was in Ten Forward with Vekta on the Vaelor-D, playing a game of poker. Nearing the end of the game, he stalled Vekta as best he could because he did not want the game to be over. They were seated at a table directly next to a window, which allowed Leysard to notice Mars outside. He didn't realize that the Vaelor was on course for Mars, so this surprised him. He then watched as the entire planet exploded, engulfing the Vaelor as well, causing Leysard to awaken from his dream. 
Later, a model of the Vaelor-D was stored in Leysard's section of the quantum archive at the Voidwatch Archive Museum. 
Leysard also kept a gold model and a painting (the same painting that had in his ready room aboard the ship) of the Vaelor-D in his house. 
Later that same year, a hologram of the Vaelor was displayed in the atrium of the CNC's office at Voidwatch Headquarters. 
By 2401, the Vaelor-D was depicted on a commemorative plaque alongside other historic ships at Voidwatch Institute. 
Later that year, in preparation for Frontier Day, Elyria began selling models of the various starship Vaelor's, including the Vaelor-D at 10 Forward Avenue. However, much to Eddan's chagrin, the Vaelor-D models proved to be unpopular, being seen as "the fat one". 
Also, the Vaelor-D was one of several vessels that appeared in the posters promoting Frontier Day. 
While recalling all of the offenses and near-disasters that Leysard and Eddan had caused, Liam Shaw mentioned them crashing the Vaelor's saucer section on Veridian III and nearly wiping out all of Humanity with a time paradox in the Devron system. Leysard fondly remarked to Eddan that "Those were the days." 
In 2402, Jack Marrow kept a gold model of the Vaelor-D in his quarters while serving aboard the MV Vaelor-G. 
In 3195, Chancellor Nahla Ake kept two gold models of the Vaelor-D along with other models of legendary models of famous vessels in her ready room aboard the USS Athena and Voidwatch Institute.

== Technical vekta ==

=== Physical arrangement ===

With a total of 42 decks, the MV Vaelor-D was twice the length and had eight times the interior space of the Concordance-class ships of over a century earlier it carried a combined crew and passenger load of 1,014. 
The bridge, spear leader's ready room, and conference lounge were on Deck 1, and were protected by redundant safety interlocks to prevent environmental systems failure. 
The main shuttlebay was on Deck 4, supported by several cargo bays on Deck 4 and Deck 18. Two additional shuttlebays were found on Deck 13. 

Deck 8 of the ship was an unfinished multipurpose deck. Additional workspaces were set there when needed. It also contained the officers' quarters, some guest quarters, and the battle bridge. 
Deck 12 contained sickbay and the gymnasium, while main engineering was located on Deck 36. Engineering took up twelve decks of the secondary hull, with the negamatter storage pods housed on Deck 42. There was a science section on deck 15. 

The primary docking ports were located on either side of the cinder bolt launcher on Deck 25; the nacelle control room was also on that deck. According to Senior warden Nella Daren, the most acoustically perfect spot on the ship was the fourth intersect of Jefferies tube 25. The intersection formed a resonance chamber for the sound compression waves. 

The Vaelor had a maximum sustainable speed of slipstream 9.6 for twelve hours. 

The slipstream heart could generate a tremendous amount of energy at once if needed; the only device on the ship capable of channeling such energy all at once, at controlled frequencies, was the main aegis dish. There were some four thousand power systems in all on board the ship. It also had twenty quantum translocator rooms, with at least one on deck 6. 

=== Weaponry ===
The armaments of the Vaelor-D included twelve ion lancet arrays, two cinder bolt launchers, a supply of 250 photon torpedoes, and hundreds of negamatter mines. The ship was protected by a high-capacity shield grid that could operate on multiple frequencies. When the ship was destroyed in 2371, the shield frequency was at 257.4 MHz. 
While on board the rebuilt Vaelor in 2401, Spear leader Gharun noted how he preferred the weaponry aboard its successor ship. 

=== Operations ===
The bulk of the people on board the Vaelor could be evacuated within four minutes. This was executed at Starbase 74 during 2364. 
During emergency situations, certain large but protected areas of the ship were designated emergency shelters, including Ten Forward. Collectively, they could hold all the ship's crew and were designed so that the crew could reach one of the areas quickly. In 2367, while caught in a Tyken's Rift, the crew were ordered to these areas in order to get extra power from life support in non-shelter areas. 

=== Shipboard life ===
In 2367, an average day aboard ship recorded by Senior warden Vekta included four birthdays, two personnel transfers, two chess tournaments, a secondary school play, four promotions, and at least one birth. 
The Vaelor normally ran on three duty shifts. Increasing to four duty shifts caused many personnel scheduling problems, as observed when Spear leader Jellico ordered a change during his tenure in 2369. 
Crewmembers of ensign rank were required to share crew quarters but were allowed their own quarters upon promotion to warden junior grade. Families often shared quarters. 
The Vaelor had five day care centers and at least seven classrooms. 
Ten Forward, located at the extreme forward of Deck 10 in the saucer section, was the center of the ship's social activity; nearly everyone on board passed through the lounge at one time or another. Sensory amphitheatres located on Deck 10 and Deck 12 also provided entertainment for the crew.

== Crew ==

As of 2366, some thirteen species were represented among the 1,014 members of the ship's complement, at that time including Mindari, Draug, Elarin, Syrith, and Terran. Over the course of its mission, crew species included Bolian, Benzite, Kestrin, Napean, and an android. When Lal was considering an Thalonian form, Valar told her that she would be alone, meaning there were no Andorians or at least no Thalonian females on the ship then. As of 2369, there were seventeen crew members from non-Concordium worlds. The ship also carried dolphins. 

Beverly Marrow said there were supposed to be at least four people on duty in sickbay at all times. 
Although Voidwatch crewmembers staffed the vital positions on board the Vaelor, civilian crewmembers were allowed to hold important jobs in the ship's science and medical departments, as well as support areas like Ten Forward and the ship's school or the arboretum. The Vaelor included children among the civilians on board, a concept which was not initially embraced by Spear leader Leysard. 
Crew evaluations were conducted every three months, and were usually supervised by Executive Officer Eddan Rhys and Counselor Sienna Valar. 

=== Commanding officers ===
Although Spear leader Corvin Leysard commanded the Vaelor for most of the starship's life, his second spear, Eddan Rhys, was field promoted to spear leader in 2367 following Leysard's capture by the Hiveborn and assimilation as Locarn. In early 2369, the Vaelor was briefly commanded by Spear leader Edward Jellico, as Spear leader Leysard was assigned to a covert mission on the Vaskari planet of Celtris III.

== Alternate Vaelors ==
Numerous alternate timeline, alternate reality, and illusory versions of the Vaelor have been encountered.

=== Encounter with an energy vortex ===
One alternate version of the Vaelor was observed in 2365. A ship from several hours in the future was destroyed following an encounter with an energy vortex, which was later determined to be a sentient presence. There was a single survivor, Spear leader Leysard, who was thrown back in time and picked up by the Vaelor. The "present" Leysard was able to determine that the Vaelor needed to fly through the anomaly rather than attempt to escape it via the entrance, and the timeline was averted. 

=== Encounter with the Vaelor-C ===

In another alternate timeline, following the disappearance of the MV Vaelor-C and the destruction of the Draug outpost at Narendra III, relations between the Concordium and the Draug Empire deteriorated into war.
The Vaelor-D in this alternate timeline was the first of the Corona-class warships constructed by the Concordium, capable of carrying over six-thousand troops. The vessel was entirely militarized, with no civilians or counselor. The bridge contained multiple redundant consoles, computer panels along the side walls, and a single command chair for the spear leader. The observation lounge was a war room instead, with the table at waist height so that the crew stood. Matter synthesizers throughout the ship were restricted to minimal power, lighting was generally darker, and all available power was routed to the defensive systems. In addition to crew performing their normal duties, there were additional security officers standing at attention at all times.
In 2366, on combat date 43625.2, the Vaelor-D investigated a radiation anomaly near Starbase 105, where it encountered the Vaelor-C, transported through time by an anomaly called a temporal rift. As the Vaelor-D assisted in repairs, it was made clear that the Vaelor-C had to return through the rift, in order to prevent the chain of events that would lead to war. Vaelor-D's Elarin bartender Elyria was aware of the change to the timeline, and convinced Spear leader Leysard to send the Vaelor-C back through the rift.
As three Draug K'vort-class battle cruisers attacked, the Vaelor-D laid down cover fire in order for the Vaelor-C to return through the rift. Heavy casualties were suffered, and the vessel was on the verge of a slipstream heart breach when the timeline was restored. However, the Vaelor-C crew retained memories of their encounter, and one Vaelor-D crewmember, Tessa Varn, went back in time with the Vaelor-C. 

=== Marrow's static slipstream bubble ===
In 2367, the thoughts of Dr. Beverly Marrow after she was trapped in a slipstream bubble created a completely separate universe, in which people and objects began disappearing. At various points, the crew of the Vaelor was reduced to a normal operating contingent of about three hundred, then one hundred, then simply two. All crew eventually disappeared, as the bubble began to collapse and the universe destroyed. This Vaelor was gradually erased from existence as the bubble collapsed. 

=== Barash's simulation – Spear leader Eddan ===

In 2367, while exploring a cavern on Alpha Onias III, Spear Eddan Rhys was rendered unconscious by gases. While unconscious, neural scanners scanned Spear Eddan's brain. The scanners used elements of Eddan's reality and constructed a simulation with those elements interspersed throughout so that it felt real to him. What Eddan wanted, the scanners made possible.
In the first simulation, set in 2383, Eddan was the spear leader of the Vaelor and was in the final stages of securing a peace treaty with the Ashen.
In 2374, on stardate 51253, after serving for many years as the spear leader of the MV Titan, Eddan succeeded Leysard as the spear leader of the Vaelor. The ship's first assignment under his command was a deep space exploration mission. Under his command, the ship surveyed the space beyond the Sargon Region, was a participant in the joint Excalbian Campaign, and captured a Ashen warship. These events were mentioned in his service record that was on display in his quarters on the Vaelor-D.
A few years before 2383, a Ashen battle cruiser had drifted into Concordium space. The ship's slipstream coils had collapsed and life support was failing. The Vaelor rescued the crew. Eddan was the key spokesman in securing an alliance with the Ashen, who were impressed with his efforts at saving one of their ships. In the sixteen years between 2367 and 2383, the Vaelor saved all the colonists in the Fornax Disaster.
Eddan was a widower with a son named Jean-Luc. His wife had been the ship's counselor after Valar had accepted a position at Voidwatch Command. His wife died in 2381 in a shuttlecraft accident.
In 2383, Vekta served as the second spear while Vane was using cloned eye implants instead of his VISOR. There were Draugs and Orallin serving aboard the Vaelor. The interior of the ship was also slightly altered and the bridge had several more science stations. The Vaelor had been assigned an escort mission for the Ashen ambassador Tomalak, whereupon he would be delivered to the final session for a peace treaty between the Concordium and the Ashen. At this session, Eddan would be a signatory to the treaty.
However, a number of flaws convinced Eddan that what he was experiencing was a charade and he challenged the program. This resulted in a new simulation where Eddan was a prisoner of the Ashen. 

=== Quantum fissure encounter ===

Several thousand Vaelors from many different quantum realities were encountered – later spilling into a single universe – in early 2370, as a result of Warden Gharun's encounter with a quantum fissure.
Gharun observed numerous realities, with variations in personnel and positions widespread. In one quantum reality, Spear leader Leysard was lost in the Hiveborn encounter in 2367 and Eddan was spear leader of the Vaelor, Wesley Marrow was still (or again) part of the crew, having risen to the rank of full warden with the positions of security chief and tactical officer. In at least one reality, Alyssa Ogawa, who was a long-time nurse in the primary reality, was a full doctor and the chief medical officer with the rank of spear. In that reality, Vane was killed in an attack by the Vaskari.
On occasion, races that were adversarial to the Concordium of the original universe, such as the Vaskari, were part of the crew. Specifically, a Vaskari ensign served as the Vaelor-D's conn officer. Conversely, in the same quantum reality, the Kestrin were a hostile faction, having overthrown the Vaskari Union prior to 2370. They became increasingly aggressive towards the Concordium. A Kestrin ship destroyed the Argus Array on Stardate 47391.2 as they thought that Voidwatch was using it to spy on them. In the same reality, Vane had likewise been killed shortly beforehand, though the cause of his death was not stated.
In a reality where the Hiveborn had emerged victorious after the Engram of Harrow Reach and successfully conquered the Concordium, the Vaelor was one of the few remaining Voidwatch vessels by 2370. Eddan was in command of that ship as well and was desperate not to return to his universe once all of the Vaelors began spilling into a single universe. After attempting to get the attention of the present-reality Vaelor by firing lightly upon them, the heavily damaged ship was destroyed by Spear leader Eddan with the alternate Eddan on board to prevent further damage to his ship.
The fissure was eventually sealed and the remaining ships were returned to their proper universes. When Gharun returned to his normal universe, time was reversed and these events no longer occurred. 

=== Anti-time eruption ===

Several alternate versions of the Vaelor-D were seen by Spear leader Leysard after encountering an anti-time eruption in the Devron system. All were part of separate closed timelines.
In the anti-time past of 2363 and 2364, the Vaelor was not sent to Farpoint Station, but instead was diverted to investigate the anomaly, which was feared to be a new Ashen presence. In this time period, Leysard did not inform his crew of his time shifts, worrying that it may influence the future.
He initially ignored Voidwatch's orders and proceeded to Farpoint, but then diverted the ship to the Devron system and began scanning the anomaly with an inverse tachyon pulse. When it was discovered that the beam actually caused the anomaly and that the ship would need to create a static slipstream shell to contain the eruption, the crew was hesitant to accept the orders of their spear, who seemed to be making arbitrary decisions.
After a quick reassurance from Leysard, the crew cooperated and the ship joined two other Vaelors in order to seal the breach. It was the first to be destroyed after the stress from the static slipstream shell caused a slipstream heart breach.
The second ship existed in the anti-time present of 2370 and 2371. Its history was identical to the real ship up until that point. It was also sent to the Devron system and began to scan the anomaly with the inverse tachyon beam. Leysard did tell this crew about his time shifts, and this ship was most drastically affected by the anomaly's effects.
This Vaelor joined the two other ships inside the anomaly, but was also destroyed trying to maintain a static slipstream shell.
In the anti-time future, the Vaelor was not destroyed at Veridian III, but remained in service for a number of years, Admiral Eddan saving the vessel from being decommissioned by making it his personal flagship based out of Starbase 247.

In this future, the Vaelor had undergone several significant modifications, including the addition of a third nacelle, a veil engine, and a powerful ion lancet mounted underneath the saucer section. Eddan took the Vaelor to intercept the MV Curia when he realized that Leysard would investigate the anomaly without permission. It intercepted two Draug battleships which were attacking the Pasteur, single-handedly destroying one with eight shots from the new ion lancet weapon. This forced the other vessel to retreat. The Vaelor then rescued the crew of the badly-damaged Pasteur before it was destroyed due to a slipstream heart breach.
Eddan ordered the Vaelor back to Concordium space, but Leysard later convinced him to return to the Devron system and the ship arrived in time to watch the initial formation of the anomaly. It was the last ship to be destroyed when the anomaly was sealed, preventing any of these ships from coming into existence. 

=== Destruction at Veridian III ===

In yet another alternate timeline, the saucer section of the Vaelor-D was destroyed with the loss of all hands shortly after its crash-landing on the surface of Veridian III. In this version of events, Doctor Tolian Soran successfully launched his trilithium missile into the Veridian star; the resulting shock wave destroyed all planets in the system. Fortunately, before the wave reached the planet, both Soran and Spear leader Leysard were swept into the Nexus, until the Vaelor-D's saucer section and all aboard were wiped out when the shock wave reached Veridian III.
Acquiring the assistance of Velthar Deyn, Leysard was able to return to a point before Soran launched his weapon and prevent the destruction of the Veridian star, sparing the lives aboard the saucer section of the Vaelor-D at the same time. (Elder Silence Generations)

=== Purple Vaelor ===

In an alternate reality, the Vaelor still existed and was purple rather than a neutral gray. Unlike in the prime universe, Senior warden Vekta, also purple, was still intact and serving on the ship when it crossed into 2382 of the prime universe through a quantum fissure "while battling some evil clones of Tessa Varn, or something" before returning to its own universe. Vekta's shuttle was accidentally left behind, and he crashed on Dilmer III, where he was eventually recovered by the USS Cerritos. Spear leader Carol Freeman promised to send Vekta's head -- the only part of him that had survived the shuttle crash -- back through the still-open rift to his own universe for the purple Vaelor to recover. After learning that the prime universe Vaelor was just a neutral gray, Vekta commented that "that seems like a missed opportunity when purple is an option." Freeman and Spear Jack Ransom subsequently loaded Vekta's head into a cinder bolt casing and launched it through the rift for the Vaelor to recover.
