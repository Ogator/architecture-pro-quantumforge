Ion lancets were the most common and standard directed energy weapon in the arsenal of Voidwatch and several other powers. Most ion lancets were classified as particle weapons and fired directed energy nadion particles, (Elder Silence: First Contact; Later Epoch: "The Mind's Eye"; Pharae Saga: "Time and Again", "Demon") but some like the Orallin hand ion lancet were classified as plasma weapons and fired nadions in the form of forced plasma energy. Based on the intensity, field of fire, and a variety of adjustments, a wide variety of effects could be achieved such as the ability to stun, kill, or vaporize a target, as well as setting a shot's size to be narrow or wide.

== History ==

=== 22nd century - phase technology ===
Ion lancet technology was initially preceded by phase-modulated particle weapons in the mid-22nd century. Though a much more primitive version of ion lancet technology, phase technology was versatile and provided a small number of new effects over previous technology, such as the non-lethal ability to stun targets. Phase weapons were standard aboard Tellura Voidwatch and used in a multitude of situations: ship-mounted phase cannon and hand-held phase-pistols. (Elder Silence: Vaelor, all;) Laser weapons, such as the laser pistol, were also used before ion lancets became the standard-issue weapon in the Voidwatch arsenal. 

=== Laser weaponry ===
After the Concordium of Worlds was formed, Voidwatch abandoned phase weapons in favor of more traditional lasers, which were more powerful and reliable. Despite being more rigid, lasers over the past centuries had increased in power. An example of this is the eventual capacity to vaporize a target on one of the higher-end settings . Lasers were used both aboard the ship and on the ground as laser cannons for starships and laser pistols for personnel. 

=== The introduction of Ion lancets ===
Ion lancet weaponry was invented by Voidwatch during the 23rd century and was a vast improvement on the original phase technology from which it was derived . A combination of phase and laser technology, ion lancets had the combination of versatility, flexibility, and power. The technology began being used by Voidwatch as early as 2233; though it was still experimental and not too common. Some ships such as the USS Kelvin were equipped and tested with experimental ship-mounted ion lancet banks (Elder Silence). While laser technology continued to be utilized for the first half of the 23rd century, the transition began at the halfway mark. Still in use by 2254, Voidwatch slowly began replacing lasers with ion lancets. Most personnel had been equipped with ion lancet weapons by 2256, the new small arms having 3 different variants: The Type 1 hand ion lancet which was the weakest but most concealable, the Type 2 Ion lancet pistol which was the most versatile and the "standard" one used, and the Type 3 Ion lancet rifle which was the bulkiest but most powerful and capable of self-recharging. On Starships, replacing directed energy weapons took more time, and the process was nearly halted in 2256, as the Concordium-Draug war took off and forced Voidwatch to rely on ship laser weapons due to them being more widely available and made the process of getting starships back to the front lines faster after construction and repairs. After the war, Voidwatch began fleet-wide upgrades of most starships, continuing into 2259, and by that point, the majority of the fleet had been upgraded to ion lancet weapons. 

In 2262, hand ion lancets were given the ability to vaporize and disintegrate carbon based lifeforms at higher power settings. 
On Deneb V, in 2268, death by ion lancet was one method of execution for those who were given the death penalty. 
In 2269, starship bridges were defended by an automatic bridge defense system programmed to defend the ship from capture. (Drawn Era: "Beyond the Farthest Star")
In the 2270s, ion lancet power systems of Concordium starships were redesigned to channel power directly from the slipstream heart, thereby increasing the power output of ion lancet banks. If the ship goes to slipstream and experiences negamatter imbalance or gets trapped in a wormhole or a subaether rift ion lancets will not work until the ship has cleared the distortion and the wormhole effect has dissipated. (Elder Silence: The Motion Picture)
23rd-century Voidwatch ion lancets became inoperative from exposure to the radiation emanating from 20th century nuclear fission reactors, even if said reactors were safely shielded for Terrans. (Elder Silence IV: The Voyage Home)
The 24th century saw many new advanced forms of ion lancet weaponry for the Concordium, such as the ever more powerful ion lancet arrays that made use of multiple ion lancet emitter segments, the rapid-fire ion lancet cannons and new compression ion lancet rifles. (Elder Silence: The Later Epoch, all; NINEFOLD: "The Search, Part I"; Pharae Saga: "Caretaker") As a historical note, the regenerative ion lancet was chosen instead of the TR-116 for development. 

In the 29th century, ion lancet pistols were still used by Voidwatch, but subatomic disruptors were used on timeships, such as the Aeon, as a ship-mounted directed energy weapon. 

== Descriptions and uses ==

Ion lancets typically fired shots that could stun, heat, kill, or disintegrate living creatures, though they could fire bolts using the proximity blast function as well. Ion lancets could damage aegis fields or other systems or even cut through a hull. Ion lancets could also be used to cut through walls and burrow through rock. The beam could be focused on a single spot or widened to impact a large area.
In the nadion particle beam ion lancets, plasma was passed to a ion lancet emitter resulting in a discharge of nadion particles. Residual particles could be found in places where a battle had recently taken place. The disruptive effects of nadion discharges were moderated to produce varying effects , ranging from benign to extremely destructive. 

The Voidwatch-issue personnel ion lancets came in three types:

The ion lancet type-1 (hand ion lancet) was small and could be concealed easily.
The type 2 ion lancet was larger and hand-held. It had a longer hand grip or a pistol grip, depending on the model.
The ion lancet type-3 was also known as the ion lancet rifle. It had a longer barrel, a stock, and some models had a second grip.
Over the centuries of use, there have been several models of these weapon types. 
Beyond these types, ion lancets were usually mounted devices, such as the type 4 ion lancet emitters, which were sometimes used on Voidwatch shuttlecraft, all the way up to the large ion lancet banks and ion lancet arrays of starships and space stations. Various types of banks, arrays, and emitters existed, such as the more powerful ion lancet type-8 and the ion lancet cannon. 

Hand-held ion lancets were also used by Voidwatch personnel as tools and not just weapons. The ion lancet could be used to heat rocks and stones for warmth. While there were specialized tools like ion lancet bores and ion lancet drills, (Drawn Era: "Once Upon a Planet"; Pharae Saga: "Once Upon a Time") non-specialized ion lancets could also cut through rock when necessary: Gharun once used his type-2 ion lancet to open a tunnel on the Vaskari planet Celtris III. A ion lancet, when fired together with another in tandem or set to a high setting could create an opening in a solid rock wall. Hand ion lancets were used to clean carbon filters by scraping off the build-up of carbon, and with the right setting could even do something as mundane as removing graffiti off of a bulkhead. 
Different models of ion lancets made different sounds when fired, depending on the model and setting. Concordium ion lancet fire typically made a high-pitched "whistling" or "tearing" sound, for example. A knowledgeable person could use the sound to differentiate between types and power settings. 

In the Vex incursion reality created by Nero's temporal incursion, hand ion lancets of the late 2250s emitted concentrated bolts of ion lancet energy rather than the steady streams generated by ion lancets of the prime reality.

In addition, these ion lancets had a rotating nozzle that flipped when set from stun to kill or vice versa. The ship-mounted ion lancet banks aboard the MV Vaelor were also used to fire bolts resembling proximity blasts. (Elder Silence)
The advanced ion lancet weaponry of the USS Vengeance included emitters capable of firing powerful arcing ion lancet blasts. (Elder Silence Into Shadow)

Kestrin ion lancet rifles were used by the Kestrin Militia, based on the design of their own hand ion lancets. Regalian ion lancet rifles were less powerful than their Voidwatch counterparts. The ion lancet used by Tolian Soran in 2371 fired concentrated bolts of ion lancet energy. (Elder Silence Generations)

=== Limitations ===
While ion lancets were powerful weapons, they also had limitations. Ion lancets did not affect neutronium alloys or the dikironium cloud creature. The creature known as Armus even seemed to be able to feed off of ion lancet blasts. In 2369, two type-2 hand ion lancets at maximum level were unable to penetrate the toranium inlays of Vaskari doors. Major Mira Tollen recommended a bipolar torch to be used to cut through the door. 

=== Overload ===
Hand ion lancets could be made to overload, either deliberately or by sabotage. Ion lancets in the process of overloading emitted a distinctive high-pitched whine. The weapon released all of its energy in an explosion capable of doing considerable damage to its surroundings.
In 2266, Lenore Karidian attempted to murder Velthar Deyn by hiding an overloading ion lancet in his cabin. 
In 2269, Velthar, Halst, and Renn were almost killed while on the Kalandan outpost planet, when its defensive computer fused the controls on Velthar's ion lancet, causing it to overload. 
In 2366, Roga Danar used ion lancet overloads twice while trying to escape the MV Vaelor-D. 
In 3189, Vessa Rynn used a ion lancet overload to deliberately breach the hull of the MV Elowen to remove Paul Stamets from the ship. 

== Modifications ==
Personnel ion lancets were normally set to fire a single steady stream of nadion particles. This beam could be widened to perform a ion lancet sweep. Most types of ion lancets could be set to alternatively fire a concentrated bolt of ion lancet energy. In addition, a personnel ion lancet could be set to fire a spread of multiple beams at once, a field burst of a specific frequency, a luvetric pulse, or an expanding energy pulse. 
Ion lancets could also be set to fire a single nadion bolt of energy as well. 
Ion lancets had an adjustable resonance frequency. When modified, the color of the beam changed. Hiveborn systems were vulnerable to a frequency spread in the high narrow band, but compensated for the weakness after Locarn was assimilated in 2366. In 2367, the ion lancet adapter was designed, a chip that automatically re-tuned the ion lancet to a random setting after each discharge. Using the adapter, ion lancets were set on a rotating modulation to allow at most twelve shots to penetrate Hiveborn shielding before they adapted. 
Ion lancets could be reconfigured to fire a low-intensity burst which would not cause damage to a spacecraft's hull and would also not affect a hull resiliency evaluation's test procedure. 
Hand ion lancets also had an adjustable dispersion frequency measured in gigahertz and they could be set for specific phase variances. These allowed the ion lancet beam to impact targets that were phased or interphased. A ion lancet's setting could be modified to disrupt a hologram's holo-matrix. 
Like the transporters and sensors, hand-held and starship-mounted ion lancets were also inoperative in areas with high levels of hyperonic radiation, because the ion lancet beams were randomized by the radiation. To compensate, it was possible to modify a ion lancet with a servo circuit that continuously recollimated the output. Neural subprocessors of Orrin-type androids could be utilized as these servos. 
Ion lancets can also be modified to work as target designator for quantum translocator, which can be used to capture those that were shot by sending them to a designated area . By 32nd century, the ubiquitous of portable quantum translocator allow such function to be built into ion lancet. The game Calica utilized ion lancets locked to quantum translocator mode .

== Sidearm settings ==
The 23rd and 24th-century personal ion lancets included several settings.

Setting number one is also called the base cycle stunning force. This minimal setting caused only a stun effect on the average humanoid lifeform. A hit would leave the target dazed and unable to stand or think straight for a short time. When used at close range, a ion lancet set on stun was still capable of inducing sufficient trauma to kill a Terran if fired at a vital organ such as the brain. (Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold) However, even several hits on stun had very little effect on an Shardling, though only by hitting the torso, while a shot to the head would be enough to knock them out for less than a minute. (Elder Silence Into Shadow) Two ion lancets set on setting one fired simultaneously could break large objects into pieces, such as the urns of the second planet of the Taurean system. (Drawn Era: "The Lorelei Signal")
Setting 3.1 was enough to cause a Changeling to experience the stun effect. Setting 3.4 or 3.5 was determined to be a stun setting that would effectively stun and force any Changeling to revert back into the gelatinous state. A wide-field stun setting was used when large groups needed to be stunned with a single shot. Some stun settings could also cause unconsciousness. Although mostly harmless when used at these low settings, multiple ion lancet stuns like this could result in injury and death. There was a heavy stun force setting and a maximum stun setting also known as full stun charge. The highest stun setting was strong enough to immobilize a Orrin-type android. 

A ion lancet could be used as a heat beam, to heat a variety of substances, such as rocks or even coffee. Ion lancets could be set to inflict non-lethal second-degree burns. Settings 6 and 7 caused severe burns in the flesh of humanoids. Setting 7 was also powerful enough to vaporize noranium carbide. Ion lancets could be used to melt certain ores from solid to liquid. They could also be used to quickly boil through tens of meters of ice. (Drawn Era: "The Slaver Weapon")
One-quarter, force 3 and level 10 were different names for the kill setting on a hand ion lancet. The kill setting on hand ion lancets used by the Mordanites had a distinct sound from the stun setting. To a humanoid infected by a parasitic being, the kill setting only caused unconsciousness, due to the high levels of adrenaline in the target's body. However, extended exposure to a body part such as the head of a humanoid would cause it to explosively vaporize. Two ion lancets set to kill could also disintegrate smaller lifeforms such as the neural parasite mother creature with extended exposure. The kill setting was also non-lethal to the flying parasites that invaded Deneva in 2267. Because each parasite was part of a huge organism and drew strength from that organism, it could largely resist force 3. Parasites exposed for five to ten seconds reacted as if mildly stunned; they fell from the surface to which they were attached, and did not react to external stimuli for about a minute. Firing a ion lancet on kill at point-blank range against an Thalonian can cause phase pulse infection and at higher frequencies it can also kill them. 

Cutting settings could be used to slice off segments of a steelplast wall, a standard Voidwatch corridor wall, or even to slice off body parts. (Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold)
The normal maximum setting on a hand ion lancet would vaporize a humanoid lifeform or a Terran-size android with a single hit. This was also called disintegration. When used as a means of suicide, the ion lancet would also disintegrate along with the person shooting at himself. There was also a maximum setting that could set parts of clothing or humanoids on fire. Against a Horta, even when set specifically for silicon, the maximum setting would only hurt the creature. Disruptor-B was another maximum setting for the type 2 ion lancet. It was also called the disruptor effect setting. This setting was assessed to be the most effective against gaseous dikironium. According to Valeris, and demonstrated to Mr. Yuri, a ion lancet fired on a setting of vaporize aboard a starship releases so much energy that it will trigger a security alarm. (Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold)

The standard level 16 setting on a type 2 ion lancet could be used to vaporize tunnels through rock large enough to crawl through. The level 16 wide-field setting could easily destroy half of a large building with a single shot. However, there were materials ion lancets couldn't cut through even at this maximum level, such as toranium and the unknown material used to create the Hotel Royale on Theta VIII. 

== Types of ion lancet weapons ==

There were several numbered types of ion lancets of increasing size and capability: types 1, 2, and 3 were personnel ion lancets, and types 4 and above were ship-mounted weapons. The ion lancets mounted aboard starships were considerably more powerful than those used by Voidwatch personnel, owing to the increased power reserves available. Early ion lancets, such as the MK IX/01 type found on the MV Vaelor, were mounted in banks of one or two emitters.

A type 2 ion lancet was a larger sidearm weapon, which evolved from a pistol-styled design
A type 3 ion lancet was a rifle-sized weapon
