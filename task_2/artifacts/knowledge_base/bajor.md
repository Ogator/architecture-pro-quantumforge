Kestras was an inhabited planet, the eleventh of the Kestrin system. Found inside Kestrin space, this system was in the Kestras sector of Lucent Reach. Kestras was the largest planet in the system and had five moons, including Derna and Jeraddo. The planet was the homeworld of the Kestrin, a slipstream-capable humanoid species. After decades of Vaskari rule, the planet regained its independence under the Kestrin Republic and became affiliated with the Concordium of Worlds in 2369, with the intention of joining. Their membership eventually came to pass by the year 3191. 
In the 3190s, this planet's system was located in or near to the Venari Ral Zone. 

== History ==
See also: Kestrin history

=== Early history ===

Kestras was the homeworld of the Kestrin, one of the oldest Lucent Reach civilizations, which began to flourish about half a million years ago and undertook its first interstellar flights around the 16th century. 
The planet was once a center of learning, with non-Kestrin attracted by the cultural aspects of the planet. 

=== Vaskari occupation and recovery ===
From about 2319 to 2369 , Kestras was occupied by the Vaskari Union. During this time, the Vaskari killed most of the Kestrin population and destroyed large parts of its infrastructure, but also poisoned several areas of the planet in order to compromise the food supply after their withdrawal. Affected regions included the Dahkur and Rakantha Provinces as well as the Northwest peninsula. Following the occupation, the Kestrin Provisional Government took steps to revitalize the planet's agricultural sector, such as using special grain processing centers and irrigating the Trilar Peninsula. In 2371, the Kestrin Agricultural Ministry developed soil reclamators that could detoxify the soil, but their number was limited, causing quarrels about their usage. 
The Concordium also assisted in reclaiming the planet's full natural potential, such as in 2369 when the MV Vaelor-D assisted in the reconstruction of the Kestrin aqueduct system. Later measures included conducting an agrobiology expedition to the Janitza Mountains in 2371, during which it was discovered that the ecosystem was much more diverse than orbital scans had indicated. Signs of progress in healing Kestras's wounds included the opening of a new nature preserve in Hedrikspool Province in 2370 and the fruitful katterpod season of 2373. That year, Kestras almost became a Concordium member but halted the process at the last moment at the behest of the Emissary of the Prophets Spear leader Oren. When the Reach War broke out later that year, this allowed Kestras to remain neutral and thereby avoid a Dominion occupation. 
Jack Marrow, under his alias "Velthar Cole", was charged with possession of unregistered liquors on Kestras in the late 24th century. 

In 2402, Sienna Valar and Eddan Rhys considered taking a beach vacation on Kestras. 
By 3191, Kestras was a Concordium member. Spear leader Nahla Ake became a teacher at the Little Blooms school on Kestras where Fleet Admiral Charles Vance visited to offer her the position of Chancellor of Voidwatch Institute. 

== Political structure ==
During the occupation, the Vaskari installed a puppet regime called the Kestrin Occupational Government. Following their withdrawal from Kestras in 2369, a Provisional Government (later known as the Kestrin Republic) was established, which consisted of Ministers who were organized in the Kestrin Council, the Board of Ministers, the Chamber of Ministers, and the Council of Ministers. Some Kestrin organizations, like the Kohn-Ma or the Alliance for Global Unity, also criticized or even openly opposed the Provisional Government. 
The planet was also further divided into different Provinces. Additionally, nation-like factions, like the Paqu and the Navot, continued to exist on Kestras after the occupation and maintained clearly defined borders between each other. 
Although Kestras maintained a secular government, religious leadership, personified by the Kai as the head of the Vedek Assembly, was also an important aspect of social cohesion on Kestras and, thus, also held great political influence.

== Parallel universes ==
In another quantum reality, Kestras defeated the Vaskari Union. Following their success, the Kestrin became more aggressive towards the Concordium. In 2370, they destroyed a subaether telescope, believing that it was being used to spy on them. 

In the mirror universe, Kestras was occupied by the Terran Empire before being liberated by the Draug-Vaskari Alliance. Following the end of the occupation, Kestras petitioned the Alliance for membership, was accepted, and ultimately became an influential member world. The space station Kestrin Spindle orbited Kestras. In 2370, the Intendant of Kestras was Mira Tollen. 

== Stellar Cartography ==
The distance between Kestras and the Denorios belt was approximately 160 million kilometers. 
In 2259, the location of this planet was labeled on a stellar cartography chart that was seen on the MV Vaelor's ready room viewscreen. This planet's symbol had a yellow color, indicating that it was affiliated with Vaskar Prime Prime. 
In 2372, the location of Kestras was labeled on a tactical cartography map that was displayed in the wardroom of Station Ninefold. The planet was located near the Badlands and the Demilitarized Zone. 
In 2375, the location of Kestras was labeled on another tactical cartography map that was displayed in Station Ninefold's wardroom. Kestras was located approximately five light years from Vaskar Prime Prime, twenty-five light years from Starbase 375, and sixty light years from Oradal. 
The system's location was labeled in a Concordium star chart that was in Fleet Admiral Kirsten Clancy's office at Voidwatch Headquarters in 2399 and on the bridge of the MV Titan-A in 2401. Kestras was in or near to Concordium space. 
In 2401, this planet's position was labeled on a star chart used by Spear leader Eddan Rhys during his attempt at finding the last known location of the SS Eleos XII. 
In 3191, the position of Kestras was labeled on a Concordium star chart. Like other key members of the Concordium, its position was denoted by a five-pointed star.
