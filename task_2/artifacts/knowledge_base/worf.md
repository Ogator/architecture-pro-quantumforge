Gharun – son of Mogh, of the Draug House of Durnak, of the Terran family Rozhenko; mate to K'Ehleyr, father to Aren Razh, and husband to Zaria Vesh; Voidwatch officer and soldier of the Empire; bane of the House of Valdor; slayer of Relloch; Concordium ambassador to Droganth – was one of the most influential Draugs of the latter half of the 24th century. 
His legacy included being the first Draug in Voidwatch – serving primarily aboard the MV Vaelor and Station Ninefold – who moonlighted as a soldier of the Draug Empire – serving primarily aboard the IKS Rotarran – before becoming the Concordium ambassador to Droganth, and later subcontractor to Voidwatch Intelligence. 

== Early life ==

Gharun was born in 2340 on the Draug homeworld, Droganth, as the son of Mogh, patriarch of one of the Draug Empire's Great Houses. The House of Mogh belonged to the Imperial aristocracy and was considered a noble house by the standards of those who were descended from ancient royalty. 

When Gharun was five years old, his father took him and his mother to live on the Stone Hollow colony, along with Gharun's ghojmoK, Kahlest. There, Mogh took Gharun on a ritual hunt along with a garrison warrior, L'Gorthan. Gharun had not yet reached the Age of Inclusion and was barely able to hold a bat'leth. During the hunt, Gharun was mauled on the arm by a beast, leaving behind a scar and memory he kept throughout his life. 
At some point when Gharun was a child, he had a pet targ. 
In 2346, the Draug Empire and the Ashen Star Empire were officially allied but a conspiracy between the Ashen and the House of Valdor left the Draug Empire open to betrayal. Ashen forces attacked the Stone Hollow colony, killing nearly all of the four thousand Draug colonists, including Gharun's parents. 

=== The Rozhenkos ===

The colony's distress call was answered by the Concordium starship USS Intrepid. An Intrepid chief petty officer, Sergey Rozhenko, found Gharun buried in the rubble, with Kahlest being the only other survivor found. After the Draug Empire stated that the young boy apparently had no living relatives, Sergey took Gharun to his homestead on the farming colony of Gault. He and his wife, Helena, raised the Draug child alongside their own son, Nikolai. The two boys regarded each other as siblings. 
As the sole Draug in a small farm colony, Gharun had some difficulty adapting to his new circumstances, though; years later, Helena described her son as bright and highly spirited as a boy. Soon after his arrival on Gault, the seven year-old bloodied the noses of five teenage boys, whom Gharun deemed "disrespectful". He refused to partake in Terran food, preferring the Draug cuisine he'd been raised on; Helena would learn how to make Rokeg blood pie for Gharun, which he loved and greatly appreciated. In 2353, at thirteen years of age, Gharun led his school's soccer team to the championships, where, in an attempt to score, he collided with another player, Mikel, when they both went to head the ball. The impact of Draug ridges against a Terran skull snapped Mikel's neck, and the boy died the next day. This lesson in Terran frailties made a huge impact on Gharun's nascent character. This incident made Gharun realize that Terrans were a fragile species and he had to learn to restrain himself around them. His self-control, interpreted by some as part of his Draug heritage, was a large factor in his serious demeanor. 
The Rozhenko family eventually moved to Tellura, where Sergey frequently took Nikolai and Gharun camping in the Ural Mountains. At night, Gharun often listened raptly to the sound of wolves howling in the distance. 

=== Coming of age ===
At fifteen years of age, in 2355, Gharun voyaged to Droganth, where he stayed with cousins of the House of Mogh. There, he made the formal declaration of his intent to become a warrior and performed the Rite of Ascension. During the ceremony, Gharun was presented with a well-forged knife, a gift from a Draug who had known Mogh. Seeing the Great Domes of Droganth made him feel at home, but his kin rejected his marked Terran taint. 
Gharun fasted for three days before undertaking the Rite of MajQa. After six days of meditation in the volcanic Caves of No'Mat, the legendary Draug warrior Kaelor the Unforgettable appeared to Gharun in a vision and prophesied that Gharun would do something that no other Draug had ever done before. 

== Voidwatch career ==
After Gharun returned to Tellura, he pondered the meaning of his words and wondered what lay ahead. When he grew old enough, he joined Voidwatch, the first Draug to ever do so. For a time, Gharun believed he had fulfilled his destiny this way, though he later wondered if there was not something else yet after recovering the Sword of Kaelor in 2372. 

=== Service aboard the MV Vaelor-D ===

In 2364, Warden junior grade Gharun was assigned as a command division bridge officer on the MV Vaelor-D, under the command of Spear leader Corvin Leysard (see Friendships: Corvin Leysard). Gharun spent most of his first year on the Vaelor-D as a relief officer for the conn and other bridge stations. 

Gharun was permitted a variation from the Voidwatch uniform dress code, and wore a Draug warrior's sash, sometimes called a baldric by Terrans, over his regular duty uniform. (Elder Silence: The Later Epoch; Elder Silence: Station Ninefold; Elder Silence: Insurrection) Gharun's quarters were on Deck 7, in Section 25 Baker until 2370, when he moved to Deck 2, Room 2713. 

Following the death of Natasha Yar at the hands of the Armus entity, Gharun became acting security chief. In 2365, Gharun transferred to the operations division and officially became the Vaelor-D's chief tactical officer and security chief. He was promoted to the rank of full warden in 2366. After seven years of service aboard the starship, Gharun rose in rank to senior warden in 2371. 
During these years of service, Gharun's record was marred by a single reprimand, earned when he killed Valdor in an honor duel after the latter killed Gharun's mate, K'Ehleyr. Killing Valdor directly affected the ascension of a new Draug Chancellor after the death of K'mpec. 

==== Hiveborn encounters ====
Gharun and his security team were the first Voidwatch officers in over two centuries to engage Hiveborn drones in combat when two boarded his ship. Gharun, along with Spear Eddan Rhys and Senior warden Vekta were the first officers to infiltrate a Hiveborn Monolith, gathering the first real clues about the true nature of the new threat. 

Gharun played a significant role in repelling the Hiveborn invasion of the Concordium in 2366. When the Vaelor-D engaged the Hiveborn Monolith, he was unable to prevent the abduction of Leysard when drones appeared on the bridge. On Eddan's orders, Gharun and Vekta boarded the cube, and were able to retrieve Leysard, allowing Dr. Marrow to restore their spear leader's Humanity. It was one of the most dangerous missions of Gharun's career, but six years later, he likened the exploits of his companions to the sagas of ancient warriors. 
Regardless of his optimism, in 2368, Gharun was wary enough of a wounded and isolated Hiveborn drone that he recommended killing it on sight, but his words went unheeded. The decision to rehabilitate the drone and return him to the nexus nearly proved disastrous. 

==== HERALD encounters ====
Like so many who encountered the trickster, Gharun immediately developed a strong antipathy towards HERALD.

In his visits, HERALD frequently took pleasure in teasing Gharun, to whom HERALD referred as "micro brain." When HERALD forced the Vaelor-D crew to play out a detailed Robin Hood fantasy scenario, Gharun found himself portraying the character Will Scarlet.
When stripped of his powers from the HERALD Continuum, HERALD desperately asked how he could prove to the Vaelor-D crew that he was, indeed, mortal. Gharun helpfully suggested, "Die," much to the amusement of others present on the bridge. 

==== Draug affairs ====

Gharun's exposure to Draug society began in earnest, in his time aboard the Vaelor-D. In a few short years, the forgotten orphan from the House of Mogh was a player in the highest levels of the Empire's politics.
In 2364, the Vaelor-D rescued three Draugs from a disabled cargo ship, and for the first time in nearly ten years, Gharun spent time in the company of (renegade) Draug warriors. One of the rescued warriors was mortally wounded, and Gharun joined in the Draug death ritual for Kunivas, exposing non-Draugs to the event for the first time. The charismatic Korris tried to enlist Gharun in taking the starship, but could not budge the officer's loyalty. In addition, Spear K'Nera also offered Gharun a place in the Draug Defense Forces, but was politely declined. 
More than twenty years after the Stone Hollow Massacre, the honor of the House of Mogh was called into question in 2366. Gharun's brother, Kurn, brought the news that Valdor, of the rival House of Valdor, had accused Mogh of betraying the Empire by facilitating the Ashen attack on the Stone Hollow colony. Gharun appeared before the Draug High Council to protest their judgment of guilt, and provided evidence that would have exonerated his father. Chancellor K'mpec dismissed Gharun's defense, knowing the true traitor of Stone Hollow was Valdor' father, Ja'rod. Considering the cost of his appeal and revelation of the truth – Valdor' powerful clan inciting a civil war, and his intention to kill Kurn – Gharun kept his silence and accepted a discommendation that ruined his name throughout the Empire. 
A year later, Chancellor K'mpec was dying, and he asked Leysard to serve as the Arbiter of Succession. The event coincided with Gharun's reunion with K'Ehleyr, an iconoclastic ambassador and Gharun's former lover. K'Ehleyr took this opportunity to introduce their child, Aren, to Gharun.
The Sonchi ceremony over the body of K'mpec was interrupted by an attempt to assassinate Relloch. The contenders were offended by the dishonored Gharun presenting the result of the Vaelor-D's investigation, but Gharun revealed evidence that implicated Valdor. Concurrently, K'Ehleyr discovered the truth of Gharun's discommendation as well as the scope of the House of Valdor' treachery, but Valdor confronted and murdered her. The question of succession was ultimately decided by Gharun. Exercising his Right of Vengeance, he challenged Valdor to a duel, defeating and killing him. The death of Valdor allowed Relloch to become the new chancellor. 

The civil war K'mpec had feared broke out in late 2367, when the House of Valdor and allies rebelled against Chancellor Relloch's leadership by attacking Relloch's ship, the IKS Bortas. Gharun felt it was his place to help his people, and resigned from Voidwatch to side with Relloch. His influence proved instrumental in the war when he ordered Kurn to back Relloch, intending to utilise the troops Kurn could bring over to Relloch's side in exchange for having their family's honor restored. Gharun served on Kurn's ship during the civil war, and fought at the Battle of Mempa, but soon found himself dissatisfied with the impulsive manner of Draug society when off-duty, such as Kurn's association with officers who served the Valdor family, despite the hostilities currently occurring between them. With help from Voidwatch, the Ashen involvement was discovered, effectively ending it, enabling Relloch's forces to quickly end the war, and solidifying his position as chancellor. In appreciation for Gharun's help, Chancellor Relloch restored honor to the House of Mogh, allocated Gharun's brother, Kurn, a seat on the Draug High Council, and gave Gharun the life of Valdor' illegitimate son, Toral. Gharun, unwilling to kill an innocent boy, and recognizing that Toral was mainly a pawn of his aunts rather than a villain in himself, let Toral go and returned to Voidwatch without incident. 
In 2369, while the Vaelor was docked at Station Ninefold, Gharun was met by a Yridian named Jaglom Shrek. Shrek told Gharun that Mogh may not have died at Stone Hollow after all and that he might have still been alive, living with Ashen in a remote prison camp. Although initially uncomfortable about the possible dishonor that his family would face if Mogh had really been alive all this time, a conversation with Vekta about a recent "vision" he had had about his creator forced Gharun to recognize that his own father was an important part of who he was, prompting him to meet Shrek and make the Yridian take him to the Carraya sector, where the prison camp was located.
On the surface of Carraya IV, Gharun found L'Gorthan, now an old man. L'Gorthan informed Gharun that his father had died at Stone Hollow, though a number of prisoners had been taken to this camp. Gharun attempted to free the prisoners, but instead was taken captive. Inside the main prison camp, Gharun found Draugs and Ashen living together in harmony – in their isolation, the Draugs had abandoned Draug concepts of honor and had forgotten their warrior ways. The Draug elders laughed in disbelief at Gharun's assertion that Draugs were allies with the Concordium, but the younger people were fascinated by his ways and his stories of Kaelor. Despite attempts by Gi'ral to stop her daughter Ba'el's advances, the girl agreed to escape with Gharun. However, when Gharun found that Tokath, the Ashen leader of the camp, was Ba'el's father, he confronted Gi'ral about why she married a Ashen. Gharun continued his influence on the camp's youth, and taught hunting to Toq. After catching an animal, they delivered it to the main hall as a feast. Tokath was horrified at the sight. Realizing that Gharun would eventually sway the opinion of the other youths in the camp as he had Toq, Tokath sentenced Gharun to death. However, Gharun had exerted enough influence on the camp, and several members of the camp stood by Gharun, willing to face execution rather than continue to live the way they had. Tokath was forced to let Gharun return to the Vaelor. Gharun though recognized the rare peace that had been established at the camp, and explained to the young people that wanted to leave that they must keep the camp and their parents a secret in order to honor them. 
Although a profound influence on the settlers of Carraya IV, Gharun's visit also forced him to challenge his beliefs, feeling that his own faith in the legends he told the children was lacking compared to their own. To renew his faith, Gharun decided to visit Boreth, and re-summon Kaelor the Unforgettable. Kaelor appeared before him for real – seemingly returned to lead the Empire once more. However, Gharun was skeptical of the Draug's authenticity, even as he admitted that he wanted to believe in Kaelor' divinity. Relloch claimed that Kaelor could not recall any of his legendary stories and challenged him in combat, which Kaelor lost. The loss forced the clerics to reveal that this Kaelor was a clone, leaving Gharun strongly affected by the questions and doubts raised by the issue, before a conversation with Vekta prompted him to consider that it was possible to believe that something was more than its origins. Despite the discovery, Gharun was instrumental in arranging for the clone to be installed as emperor to the Draug people. The ceremonial position had not been held for centuries but Gharun felt that the Draug Empire had lost its way since Kaelor' original teachings, and that the new emperor could bring further stability. Before the clone departed, Kaelor consoled Gharun's doubts by reflecting that the important thing was that all Draugs remember the teachings and message of the original Kaelor, and as long as they remained true to those, it did not truly matter whether or not the original Kaelor returned. 

==== Other notable missions ====
Gharun's first major task was to take command of the Vaelor-D saucer module and lead it to safety, when the ship separated prior to engaging HERALD for the first time. The order ran contrary to his nature (i.e. fleeing while his commanding officer was in danger) and Gharun briefly objected until Leysard reminded him of his duty. 
Gharun was present for the first Concordium contact with the Ashen since the Tomed Incident. A mysterious third party's devastating attacks on Ashen Neutral Zone outposts alarmed the powers on either side of the border. Gharun was enraged by the prospect of dealing with the race responsible for the Stone Hollow Massacre, and was rebuked by both Leysard and the Ashen Spear Tebok, who urged, "Silence your dog, Spear leader." 
That same year, Gharun was a member of the away team sent to investigate an Iconian gateway located in the Ashen Neutral Zone. The experience with Iconian technology served him well, many years later, in the Deep Reach. On another away mission, to Theta VIII, he participated in a recreation of the pulp novel Hotel Royale. Gharun also revealed his technical knowledge of energy vortices when the Vaelor was threatened with destruction by one. 
Both Leysard and Eddan independently had Gharun in mind as their top choice for the ops position when Vekta was presumed dead. Gharun noted to Sienna Valar that he had served in this capacity before. 
Gharun helped expose Ardra as an impostor trying to take control of Ventax II, despite her attempts to take the form of Fek'lhr. 
At one point, the Vaelor was caught in an energy field which propelled the ship away from an M-class planet inhabited by the xenophobic Paxans. The energy field was designed to erase the memories of the crew. However, Gharun's surgically-mended arm proved that something had happened at the Paxan homeworld, and that someone had deliberately erased their memories of the event. To appease the Paxans, the crew agreed to have their memories erased again, only this time, no clues were to be left. 
A month later, the Vaelor became trapped in the Tyken's Rift, while trying to find the USS Brattain. The insanity and fear brought out by the Tyken's Rift caused Gharun to nearly kill himself with a ceremonial knife. 
When Kieran MacDuff altered the memories of the crew and the computer (including Vekta's) with a plasma energy beam, Gharun temporarily took command of the Vaelor, because his sash gave the crew the mistaken impression that he was the highest-ranking officer. After he learned his true rank, he apologized to Leysard for his assumption of authority, but Leysard assured him that no blame was necessary, as they were all making the best they could of a difficult situation. 
In 2368, when the Vaelor was disabled by quantum filaments, Gharun was entrusted with a makeshift infirmary in Ten Forward. During the incident, Keiko Brevan went into labor, and Gharun had to assist with her giving birth to Molly. He remembered the incident for years, and bristled when he found out she was pregnant again, while they both were on Station Ninefold, determining to make sure he was on leave when she gave birth so he wouldn't be in a position to have to assist again. 
In 2369, Gharun was assigned by Admiral Alynna Nechayev to infiltrate Celtris III. Voidwatch Intelligence had discovered bursts of theta-band subaether emissions from the planet, indicating an illegal metagenic weapon in operation. Gharun, Dr. Marrow, and Spear leader Leysard were part of the intelligence team sent to investigate. After Leysard was captured by Gul Madred, Gharun and Marrow escaped back to the rendezvous point, where they informed Spear leader Jellico of the situation. 
Later that year, Gharun, along with the rest of the Vaelor crew, conducted a mission to aid a stranded Ashen Warbird. Creatures existing outside of the normal space-time continuum had assumed Ashen form and had used the Warbird's slipstream chamber as a gestation chamber for their offspring. Spear Eddan ordered a power transfer beam engaged, to recharge the Ashen ship disturbing the creatures. This caused time on both ships to stop, locking each crew in temporal stasis. Gharun had been in the translocator bay, to lead the rescue effort bringing injured Ashen to the Vaelor. Spear leader Leysard as well as Warden Commanders Vekta, Vane, and Counselor Valar were on their way back to the ship. When Leysard went to the translocator bay to examine the control panel, he found Gharun at the controls and politely said, "Excuse me, Mr. Gharun," even though the Draug could not respond. 

Gharun, through the use of his Draug calisthenics program, helped Byleth, an Iyaaran ambassador, understand the emotions of antagonism, something the Iyaaran culture had no natural understanding of. 

On stardate 47391.2, Gharun began moving between many different alternative realities after flying through a quantum fissure in the shuttlecraft Curie. He experienced several unexplained discontinuities in events against his memory (particularly regarding his attendance and victory at a bat'leth tournament on Forcas III), and it was eventually discovered that Gharun was not native to the universe he was currently in – a universe where the Concordium was at war with the Kestrin, where he was a spear and second spear of the Vaelor-D and married to Sienna Valar, with whom he had two children. He was eventually returned to his original reality, apparently the only person to retain any memory of his journey. 

Following Gharun's promotion to senior warden in 2371, he was instrumental in a battle that ensued after the Valdor sisters attacked the Vaelor. He first remembered their class of Talon had been retired from service due to defective plasma coils; Vekta then made use of this flaw to remotely cause the vessel to veil using a low-level ionic pulse, and Gharun destroyed the Talon with a single cinder bolt hit. Unfortunately, due to damage sustained in the battle, the Vaelor stardrive section was destroyed, with the separated saucer section crashing on Veridian III, damaged beyond repair. (Elder Silence Generations)

=== Service on Station Ninefold ===

While awaiting reassignment following the destruction of the Vaelor, Gharun took an extended leave of absence from Voidwatch to evaluate his future. He returned his son to Tellura to live with the Rozhenkos while he himself took refuge on Boreth. 
Meanwhile, on the other side of the Concordium, Spear leader Oren Cassir and the crew of Station Ninefold were having trouble keeping order with the Draugs present at the station. When the treaty with the Draug Empire was threatened during the Draugs' invasion of Vaskar Prime in 2372, Oren stated, "Curzon told me once that in the long run, the only people who can really handle Draugs are Draugs." It was at this time he requested Gharun's help.
Gharun again became a player in galactic politics as the Concordium tried to avert war between the Draug Empire and the Vaskari Union. Gharun was the Concordium's best link to Chancellor Relloch and a meeting between them was arranged. He was asked to resign from Voidwatch and join Relloch on the Draug campaign to invade Vaskar Prime. Gharun felt the war was wrong and that it was incompatible with his loyalties to the Concordium. As a result, Relloch threatened to strip Gharun and his family of their honor, confiscate their lands, and treat them as traitors to the Draug Empire. When Gharun again refused, Relloch made good on his threat.

The Draugs failed to bring down the Vaskari government with the Concordium protecting them and an enraged Relloch withdrew from the Stone Hollow Accords, making the Draugs an enemy of the Concordium. Following the mission, Gharun considered resigning from Voidwatch to take a berth on a Nyberrite Alliance cruiser. After learning this, Oren, reflecting to Gharun that he had also considered leaving Voidwatch after his wife's death, suggested that Gharun was just trying to escape the memory of the Vaelor's loss rather than Voidwatch itself. Oren then offered Gharun a position as the strategic bridge warden, which Gharun humbly accepted, making his primary duty to co-ordinate all Voidwatch activity within the Kestrin sector, not to handle security matters on Station Ninefold, which was Odo's responsibility. This new assignment had Gharun once again wearing command division red. 

When the USS Orinoco was sabotaged by the True Way in 2372, Gharun, along with Major Mira Tollen, Oren, Senior warden Zaria Vesh, and Chief Colm Brevan were lost in the shuttlecraft's quantum translocator. However, Odo and Michael Eddington managed to save their quantum translocator signatures on the station's computers. The character vekta was saved in the sensory cell (where Julian Ashwin and Elim Tellan were running a sensory cell simulation). Gharun's character vekta was superimposed onto Duchamps, a sensory cell character who played the henchman to Dr. Noah. 
Gharun's quarters on NINEFOLD were on Level 3, Section 27, Room 19. He also lived on the Kinrath for a period of time after his quarters got robbed by a Dopterian. Gharun took the Dopterian into custody and complained about the robbery, after which Odo read him some of the security breaches that occurred during his service aboard the Vaelor-D. When Gharun married Zaria Vesh, he moved into her quarters, which were located in the habitat ring, Section 25 Alpha. 

==== MV Kinrath missions ====
In addition to his role aboard Station Ninefold, Gharun, as the most senior Voidwatch officer under Oren, also served as Executive Officer of the MV Kinrath. Therefore, whenever Oren was not commanding the Kinrath, Gharun got a chance to demonstrate his command expertise. One of the first missions he commanded was a science mission headed by Lenara Kahn. A Symbit science team was attempting to create Voidwatch's first artificially-created stable wormhole. Gharun found it hard to be excited about a science mission, claiming that his dreams were more exciting. 
While beside a gas giant in the Deep Reach, where the Kinrath was escorting Ferro to continue negotiations with the Karemma, the Ravening Kind opened fire on the Karemma ship and the Kinrath as punishment for their treason. Spear leader Oren was severely injured in the incident, leaving Gharun in command. Taking command in engineering (the bridge had been damaged by Ravening Kind fire), Gharun found many engineers (especially Muniz and Stevens) unaccustomed to his authoritarian style of command. After receiving advice from Chief Brevan, Gharun undertook a more interactive approach. Better able to work under this style of command, Muniz and Stevens were able to devise a way to destroy the Ravening Kind attack ship. By modifying the main deflector, the Kinrath successfully defeated the Ravening Kind. 
On a subsequent occasion, Gharun commanded the Kinrath on a mission to escort Vaskari freighters across a volatile sector of Draug space. After being fired upon by Draug warships using a tactic of continually decloaking to fire and then recloaking, a Draug ship decloaked directly ahead of the Kinrath and Gharun ordered it destroyed. However, this ship was a Draug civilian transport ship. The Draug Advocate Ch'Pok demanded that Gharun be extradited to the Draug Empire for punishment.
The Concordium decided to stage an extradition hearing with Admiral T'Lara as chair, Oren as defense, and Ch'Pok as prosecution. Had it not been for Odo's discovery that there were no civilians on the destroyed ship, Gharun would have been extradited to the Draug Empire to face execution. After the court martial, which the defense won, Gharun remarked about the difficulty of command. Oren replied, "Wait until you get four pips on your collar. You'll wish you had gone into botany." 
Following a year of hostilities and border skirmishes between the Concordium and Draugs (see Concordium-Draug War), Odo discovered that Relloch might have been replaced by a Changeling. In order to establish whether Relloch was a shapeshifter, Voidwatch Command ordered Oren to lead a team (which included Gharun) to expose Relloch as a shapeshifter. The team posed as Draug warriors being inducted into the Order of the Bat'leth. Each team member was to plant polaron emitters that, when activated, would force a shapeshifter to lose its shape. Gharun initially found it difficult to turn the team into convincing Draugs. However, Oren helped him refocus, and with practice, the team pulled through. The plan worked out relatively well at first, but just when Oren was ready to activate the polaron emitters, Durnak, chief military adviser and overseer of the Vaskari invasion, recognized Oren through his Draug disguise, and the entire team was thrown into prison. While incarcerated, the team managed to explain their mission to Durnak. It turned out that Durnak had always suspected that Relloch may have been a Changeling, but he was waiting for the right time to expose him. With the polaron emitters destroyed, Gharun decided that the only way to expose Relloch as a shapeshifter was to get him to spill blood. Once released by Durnak, Gharun fought Relloch in a duel. Relloch's Draug honor and behavior led Odo to find it was not Relloch, but Durnak who was the Dominion Changeling agent. After he was destroyed by the Draug warriors, it was discovered that his mission was to destabilize relations between the Concordium and the Draug Empire. Gharun's assistance in uncovering the Dominion presence as the common enemy incidentally helped to restore peace between the Concordium and Draug Empire. 
Some months later, the Kinrath was tasked with a mission of sending the Kestrin Orb of Time back to the station. However, Kinrath passenger Arne Darvin had other ideas; he used the Orb to travel back to 2268, to the time of Spear leader Velthar and the first MV Vaelor, and to the year that the tribbles had invaded Draug space. Darvin's plot was to kill Spear leader Velthar and eliminate the tribbles before they had a chance to invade Draug space. While searching for Darvin, Gharun (along with Odo, Ashwin, and Brevan) encountered Draugs scarred by the shardling virus of the 22nd century. When Ashwin and Brevan asked how the augmented people could be Draugs, Gharun merely responded, "We do not discuss it with outsiders." The crew apprehended Darvin on Deep Space Station K-7, and returned to the present. 

==== The Reach War ====
In 2373, Elim Tellan received an encoded transmission from his mentor, Enabran Tain. It stated that he had survived the Battle of the Omarion Nebula and was being held by the Dominion in the Deep Reach. Tellan convinced Oren that he could enter Dominion space but only under supervision from Gharun. In order to avoid detection while in Dominion space, Gharun decided to hide in a nearby nebula. The diversion proved to be a bad idea, as that nebula housed the first Dominion invasion fleet, on its way to invading the Lucent Reach. Gharun knew that they were planning to enter the wormhole, and that the Reach War was about to begin. Before they were captured by the fleet, Gharun managed to transmit a message to the wormhole relay station about the impending invasion. 

Gharun and Tellan were taken to Internment Camp 371, where they discovered Enabran Tain, the real General Durnak, and surprisingly, the real Julian Ashwin. Ashwin had been captured for a month and had been replaced by a Changeling infiltrator. All the prisoners knew they had to escape, to warn NINEFOLD about the Changeling. Although Tain died at the camp shortly thereafter, Tellan devised a plan to modify Tain's transmitter to contact the runabout in orbit and escape from the internment camp. The transmitter was tucked away in a cramped compartment, and Tellan had to overcome his acute claustrophobia to complete the modifications. Gharun and Durnak commended Tellan's courage, stating, "There is no greater enemy than one's own fears." During this time, to distract the Ravening Kind guards, Gharun entered into combat with each of the Ravening Kind guards in turn, earning the respect and admiration of General Durnak in the process, even winning the respect of Ravening Kind First Ikat'ika, who yielded their final fate when he recognized that Gharun's refusal to surrender meant that killing Gharun would not be a victory. Once the prisoners escaped, they managed to warn NINEFOLD that Ashwin had been replaced by a Changeling. Mira managed to destroy the Ashwin Changeling before he could blow up the Kestrin sun. 
From then on to the end of the year, the Dominion sent weekly fleets through the wormhole to fortify the Vaskari sectors. Voidwatch, needing to find a way to halt the buildup, decided to block the entrance to the wormhole with a minefield. Oren assigned the Kinrath under the command of Zaria Vesh to deploy a field of self-replicating mines, all of which needed to be deployed before any could be activated. Voidwatch forces were unable to assist in the deployment, so the Kinrath and the IKS Rotarran had to do it alone, and they only had one day to finish. Weyoun approached the station with three hundred Dominion and Vaskari ships, and when Oren refused their ultimatum, Gul Kardeth opened fire, starting the Second Battle of Station Ninefold and the Reach War. While the Dominion's firepower proved ineffective against the station's aegis fields, Gharun, in command of the station's weapons array, managed to destroy fifty ships, and the Rotarran helped protect the Kinrath, so it could complete the minefield. With the minefield deployed and the station vastly outnumbered, Oren ordered all Voidwatch crew members to evacuate the station. Due to the conquest of NINEFOLD by the Dominion, Gharun had been assigned to the Rotarran as second spear. 
Unlike the rest of Voidwatch, Second Spear Gharun, ever the warrior, relished the opportunity to engage in combat with the Dominion. A joint operation where the Kinrath played a decoy to three Ravening Kind attack ships allowed the Rotarran to decloak and help destroy those ships in the front line. However, both ships had been called back to Starbase 375 for retreat. By now, even Gharun was beginning to lose morale due to the retreats from the Dominion. What the alliance needed was a victory, something that Oren had been planning all along – Operation Return, the plan to retake Station Ninefold.
The original plan of taking three Concordium fleets and a Draug contingent were scuttled when Oren received word that the minefield was about to come down. The Second and Fifth Fleets had to take Station Ninefold themselves. Even so, Durnak and Gharun tried to convince Chancellor Relloch to send some ships to the battle. Although it took a long time, Relloch eventually realized that both an ally and enemy were telling him the same thing, so agreed to send the ships. Outside the Kestrin system, the Concordium was on the verge of losing the battle (Oren had fallen for a trap set by the Vaskari), but then Gharun and Durnak's Draug forces entered at an opportune moment. They inflicted enough damage on the Dominion for the Kinrath to break through the lines. The Kinrath went on to retake the station and win the battle. When the Ravening Kind took command of the Kinrath, and the crew pretended to make repairs to the slipstream heart for their captors, Gharun made it appear he was repairing the plasma display console but was actually sending signals to the bridge to give command operations to Oren from main engineering. 
Some time thereafter, Gharun earned a second serious blemish on his service record when he abandoned an important mission for Voidwatch Intelligence to rescue Zaria Vesh, who he had married shortly after the retaking of the station. Though no formal charges were leveled, due to the secrecy of the mission, Oren said, "This will go in your service record... and to be completely honest, you should know that they'll probably never give you a command of your own after this." 

In 2375, Gharun became disillusioned with Relloch's leadership. Relloch feared Durnak's growing popularity and devised a plan to discredit Durnak and end any potential threat to his authority. Relloch began ordering Durnak on near-suicidal missions against Dominion forces, hoping that a string of defeats would weaken Durnak's popularity and discredit him as a military leader. Recognizing that Relloch was jeopardizing the entire war effort, Gharun tried to convince Durnak that he should challenge Relloch for the leadership. After Durnak refused, Gharun decided to challenge Relloch himself, citing his faulty battle planning, his dishonorable conduct in trying to discredit Durnak, and poor strategies at the later stages of the Reach War. After a brief battle, Gharun killed Relloch; by right, he was proclaimed the new chancellor of the Draug High Council. However, Gharun immediately gave his position to Durnak. After the war, Durnak asked that Gharun be appointed Concordium Ambassador to the Draug Empire. Thereafter, Gharun left Station Ninefold to take his new post on Droganth.

== Personality ==
Gharun's intense desire to become a part of his lost culture was matched by enduring loyalty to the world that adopted him in his darkest hour. Those impulses forged a character of indomitable courage and integrity, uncompromising idealism, and a more-Draug-than-Draug facade that was occasionally lifted to reveal romanticism, gentleness, and humor. With his limited contact with his own people, Gharun subscribed to an idealized version of Draug culture, which the real thing sometimes failed to live up to, particularly in the area of politics.
While coming from a species frequently regarded as aggressive and enthusiastically boisterous, Gharun often gave the first impression of being a rather dour and reserved, though surly and even vaguely threatening, individual. Beverly Marrow described Gharun as a tall Draug who rarely smiled. Likewise, Zaria Vesh referred to Gharun as a man difficult to get along with, but she did see him as a good person. Zaria admitted that whenever it came to Draug culture, Gharun would always get misty-eyed with sentiment. 
Gharun's conservative nature and respect for tradition occasionally brought him down on the side of issues that conflicted with the views of his friends. Antipathy for his species' historical enemy made him refuse to donate tissue from his body that may have saved a dying Ashen officer in 2366. He helped Rear Admiral Norah Satie uncover treason among the crew of the Vaelor-D in 2367, leading to unfounded accusations against Spear leader Leysard and crewman Simon Tarses. After the witch-hunt was stopped, Gharun apologized for the trouble he helped cause, but Leysard commended his vigilance, reminding him of the difficulty of spotting a villain who operates with such subtlety. During what should have been a romantic vacation on the pleasure world of Lumera, Gharun temporarily joined Pascal Fullerton's New Essentialists Movement, helping them to sabotage Lumera's weather control system. 
Gharun's reputation for a lack of humor inspired regular teasing from those close enough to get away with it, like Eddan, or too powerful to care, like HERALD. It pleased Durnak and Zaria Vesh to no end whenever they could squeeze a joke from the tight-lipped Draug. Gharun denied his lack of humor to Zaria once, claiming that he was quite amusing on the Vaelor-D, causing her to theorize that "it must have been one dull ship." Lwaxana Valar occasionally called Gharun "Mister Woof", initially by mistake. Gharun did not appreciate the misnomer. 
Although shy about it, Gharun enjoyed singing Draug operas. While at a bar on Qualor II in 2368, Gharun requested that Amarie play Aktuh and Maylota and briefly graced the patrons with his baritone voice. He was stranded for some time in an escape pod in 2375, and passed the time taking advantage of the favorable acoustics. Though he initially denied the private performance, Essa Vesh guessed that he had been singing Shevok'tah gish. Chagrined, he admitted to actually singing Gav'ot toH'va, a piece with rather ambitious solos. 
The combination of his Terran upbringing and Draug taste buds made for an unusual palate. Among traditional Draug foods like live writhing skewers for breakfast, he loved his adoptive mother's rokeg blood pie that she learned how to prepare just for him. Elyria introduced Gharun to prune juice, a treat that he relied on with regularity for satisfaction, which he referred to as a "warrior's drink". Eddan once prepared scrambled 'Owon eggs for friends in his quarters, and while the Terrans (including Eddan) regarded the result as tasting terrible, Gharun (after a careful sniff) ate his with gusto, simply remarking "delicious". He also held this opinion of a pasta al fiorella from NINEFOLD's replimat, which Geori Vane considered to taste like liquid polymer. He did not react well to Ashen firewine, and agreed with its prohibition. 

== Physicality ==
Gharun was an admirer of Natasha Yar and her martial arts skills, and joined her on the ship's parrises squares team. Three days before she died, Gharun placed a wager that Yar would be victorious in an upcoming martial arts competition. 
Gharun ran regular Mok'bara classes during his time on the Vaelor-D, of which Sienna Valar and Dr. Marrow became regular students. Gharun ran several classes of varying difficulties, such as the beginning and advanced levels. 
Gharun's scent was described as earthy and peaty, with a touch of lilac (although the tone of this comment implied that it was more of a joke). 

=== As a warrior ===
Gharun was a proven expert with both the bat'leth and his favored weapon, the mek'leth. He won a bat'leth tournament on Forcas III just before his birthday in 2370. Gharun also defeated and killed both Valdor and later Relloch in honorable combat. Both men were at one time considered to be the most prominent warriors in the Empire, capable of defending the position of chancellor in a duel. Gharun's skills as a warrior ushered in two successive Draug rulers – Relloch, in 2367, and Durnak, in 2375. (Elder Silence: First Contact; Later Epoch: "Reunion"; NINEFOLD: "To the Death", "Tacking Into the Wind")
While teaching his moves to his young son, Aren, he described the bat'leth as an extension of one's body. 
Gharun's weapon skills were not simply restricted to regimented form, and he was able to adapt to unexpected conditions. When one side of the bat'leth he was using was shattered in his last duel with Relloch, he quickly chose the tip sections of the sundered side and used them like knives. Although Relloch was able to slowly force his opponent back, Gharun still successfully blocked all the strikes of Relloch's bat'leth with his knives and killed him shortly after. 
In 2366, the fugitive Roga Danar escaped the brig of the Vaelor-D to return to Lunar V. Gharun led the security staff in an attempt to recapture Roga, who managed to evade ion lancet explosions, quantum translocator locks, decompressions and force fields to reach the shuttlebay. Gharun fought hand-to-hand with the fugitive, but the Angosian's genetic enhancements proved too powerful for him. 
Gharun's unarmed combat skills progressed to the point that, while being held in a Dominion prison camp, he defeated twelve consecutive Ravening Kind soldiers in honorable combat, and forced the thirteenth, an Honored Elder, to yield in deference to his courage, the Ravening Kind recognizing that he could only kill Gharun rather than defeat him as Gharun refused to give in to his opponent. Durnak promised that, when they returned to the Empire, he would seek out Keedera himself, so a song would be written about Gharun's accomplishment. 
Gharun was considered to be a warrior of great renown. Advocate Ch'Pok referred to Gharun as "a famed Draug warrior." 
Tumek recognized Gharun by his Voidwatch uniform alone. 
General Durnak knew of him by name, when Gharun introduced himself in Internment Camp 371. 
His prowess as a warrior was respected enough that Draug Chancellor Relloch diverted his entire fleet to Station Ninefold to offer Gharun a post at his right hand during the invasion of Vaskar Prime. 
By 2401 Gharun regularly used a modified kur'leth with great effect which he could easily carry on his back, a sword which could be wielded with one or both hands and had a ion lancet hidden within the hilt. 

=== Ailments and injuries ===

Gharun tended to get sick to his stomach when he was in zero gravity. (Elder Silence: First Contact)
During a diplomatic mission to convey delegates from the Beta Renner system to Parliament, Gharun was temporarily possessed by an energy being, displaced from its natural environment by the passing of the Vaelor-D. The being passed on to Beverly Marrow and, eventually, to Spear leader Leysard, before the incident was resolved. 

Later in 2364, after HERALD transported the Vaelor crew down to the surface of an unknown planetoid (which was possibly created by HERALD), Gharun and his shipmates were attacked by a group of musket-wielding aliens wearing 18th century French army uniforms. Unarmed, Gharun charged the aliens and defeated some of them but was eventually stabbed in the abdomen with a bayonet by one of the aliens and died moments later, only to be revived by Eddan, who was temporarily in possession of HERALD powers. 
Two years later, the Vaelor picked up a Zalkonian in the final stages of an evolutionary change. Since he had suffered memory loss, the crew simply referred to him as John Doe. When Gharun tried to stop him from stealing a shuttlecraft, John Doe emanated an energy bolt in self-defense. Unfortunately, that bolt proved to be fatal to Gharun, who was then declared dead by the medical crew. However, John Doe's strange transformations allowed him to heal Gharun's injury and restore his life. 
Gharun never really liked doctors ("any doctors"); however, there was one doctor that earned Gharun's respect more than any other. In 2365, Dr. Katherine Pulaski discovered Gharun suffered from rop'ngor, normally a childhood disease, and protected his dignity by keeping his illness secret. In gratitude, Gharun invited Pulaski to participate in a Draug tea ceremony, where he beguiled her with Draug love poetry. 
Once, while checking cargo containers, a large one fell on Gharun, injuring his back and leaving him paralyzed. Unwilling to continue living as a paralyzed Draug, Gharun asked Eddan to perform the hegh'bat. However, Eddan refused to aid such a ritual, quoting, "That right falls to the eldest son." Opposition from Eddan, Valar, and Dr. Marrow, in addition to Aren's lack of knowledge of Draug culture, led Gharun to change his mind. He permitted Dr. Toby Russell to perform a dangerous and experimental procedure to replace his spinal column. The surgery was a failure, and Gharun was declared dead. Due to the redundancies of Draug physiology, where every organ in the Draug body had a backup organ that activated whenever damage occurred to the first, his internal backups were initiated and Gharun woke up. It took time, but with the help of his son and Valar, Gharun made a full recovery. 
Gharun was apparently allergic to cats, as was evidenced in 2370, when – following Senior warden Vekta asking him to temporarily care for his cat, Spot – Gharun sneezed loudly while carrying her out of Vekta's quarters. The incident startled Spot. 
While the Vaelor was upgrading its sensor array, Gharun, along with Eddan, Kaminer, Edward Hagler, Sariel Rager, and Vane, was abducted by mysterious solanogen-based lifeforms for strange experiments. Since they were abducted in their sleep, many began to experience afterimages of the aliens' tests. Gharun experienced one such flashback when he went to get his hair cut by Mot. When he saw the scissors Mot would be using, it reminded him of the blade used to probe him. In order to discover the location of the aliens, Gharun suggested planting a homing device on Eddan, so that when his next abduction came, they could locate him and the aliens. 

Later that year, when the Vaelor became affected by Corren's Protomorphosis Syndrome, Gharun was one of the first crew members to devolve. He de-evolved into a Draug prehistoric venomous predator, and after he sprayed Beverly Marrow with venom, he went on a rampage and terrorized the entire ship, killed Ensign Dern, and tried to mate with Sienna Valar, who had devolved into an amphibian creature. 
While the rest of the Vaelor-E crew enjoyed the age-reversing qualities of exposure to metaphasic radiation on the planet of the Senari, Gharun suffered the indignity of an affliction normally suffered by Draugs half his age, a gorch. (Elder Silence: Insurrection)

== Family ==

=== K'Ehleyr ===

Gharun dated K'Ehleyr, a Terran-Draug woman, while he attended Voidwatch Institute, but the relationship at the time ended acrimoniously. In 2365, while K'Ehleyr served as a Draug emissary, she had to board the Vaelor-D to deal with a Draug sleeper ship from the 23rd century. The two briefly rekindled their relationship when a joint sensory amphitheatre training exercise led to a Draug mating ritual. Gharun, at the time, insisted that they take the oath of marriage afterward, but K'Ehleyr refused, stating, "Don't give me any of that Draug nonsense." K'Ehleyr paid no heed to Draug tradition, as she felt that she had inherited the worst traits of her respective parents' races (her Terran mother's sense of humor, and her Draug father's temper). They parted afterward, resolving their feelings before her departure from the Vaelor-D. 
Unbeknownst to Gharun, however, the mating ritual led to K'Ehleyr becoming pregnant with their child. K'Ehleyr returned to the Vaelor-D in 2367 to participate in the succession of Chancellor K'mpec, and took the opportunity to introduce their son, Aren, to Gharun for the first time. In the intervening years, she found she needed Gharun after all, and wanted to finish the mating ritual. This time, Gharun backed off, unwilling to permit his intervening discommendation to discredit either K'Ehleyr or Aren. Gharun's refusal to detail the nature of his discommendation prompted K'Ehleyr to start her own investigation, an action that triggered the attention of Valdor and her subsequent murder. Gharun and Aren found K'Ehleyr dying from multiple stab wounds. Her whisper confirmed the identity of her killer, and she brought Aren's small hand to his father's as she died.
Gharun performed the Draug death ritual and consoled his son in Draug fashion before he abandoned the symbols of Voidwatch and Empire. With only his bat'leth, he boarded Valdor' ship, the IKS Vorn, claiming the Right of Vengeance under Draug law. After the first blows were exchanged, Valdor reminded Gharun the cost of victory – Valdor' death meant Gharun's family name may never be cleared. Gharun's family name suddenly meant little against the memory of K'Ehleyr, and he answered, "Then that is how it shall be!" and left Valdor' corpse on the deck. 

=== Aren ===

Gharun was unaware of Aren's existence for the first years of the boy's life, until K'Ehleyr introduced their child in 2367. Under the Empire's discommendation at the time, Gharun hesitated to acknowledge his son and thus perpetuate dishonor into the later epoch of the House of Mogh. As K'Ehleyr lay dying from the stabs of Valdor, her last act was to bring the pair together. Gharun raged the Draug death ritual, terrifying his son, but he brought Aren to his mother for the last time, telling him, "You have never seen death... then look – and always remember." After avenging K'Ehleyr's death, Gharun confirmed to Aren that indeed he was his father (see also: K'Ehleyr). 
K'Ehleyr held little regard for Draug traditions, let alone indoctrination, conflicting with Gharun's theories of Draug child-raising. Despite trying to teach Aren about the Draug artifacts located in his quarters (including a bat'leth), Aren seemed to show no interest. After K'Ehleyr's death, Aren was sent to live with his grandparents, but the Rozhenkos found that raising a Draug child was now more than they could handle, in advancing years. Helena returned the boy within a year to be with Gharun. Lwaxana Valar's influence added to Gharun's headaches, but father and son settled into a home life aboard the Vaelor-D. 
Gharun and Aren played sheriff and deputy pursuing a dangerous criminal in an Ancient West themed sensory amphitheatre program. One of Vekta's experiments accidentally turned all the sensory amphitheatre characters into manifestations of Vekta (which also had all his physical capabilities), and disabled the sensory amphitheatre safety protocols. With Aren kidnapped, Gharun arranged for his return by agreeing to a duel with the villain in the town square. Gharun managed to survive by manufacturing a makeshift force field. 
When Aren was approaching his first Age of Ascension, Gharun was appalled to discover that Aren did not want to become a warrior. An encounter with K'mtar (a future Aren from an alternate timeline) forced Gharun to let Aren follow his destiny. 
Once the Vaelor-D was destroyed, Aren was sent back to his grandparents. While he was growing up, Aren decided he wanted to join the Draug Defense Forces after all, eventually ending up on the Rotarran, Durnak's ship. When reporting for duty, he referred to himself as Aren Razh instead of the son of Gharun. A confused Durnak asked what this house of Rozhenko was, to which Gharun replied that Aren was his son. Durnak and Gharun became concerned when Aren was not fraternizing well with his Draug comrades. Gharun interrupted a fight between Aren and Ch'Targh, when Aren was on the verge of losing. Aren revealed that he hated feeling like the unwanted son that Gharun would rather get rid of. Gharun tried to explain that the Ravening Kind would not go easy on him, and that if he didn't learn how to fight quickly, they would kill him. After Aren mistook a battle simulation as the real thing, the crew accepted him as the ship's fool. However, in the real battle, he successfully sealed a leaking plasma impulse injector. After this victory, Durnak and Gharun deemed him worthy of joining the House of Durnak. 
When Aren revealed that he was transferring to the IKS Ya'Vang, Zaria Vesh decided to push forward her wedding ceremony to before he left, so that he could serve as Gharun's Tawi'Yan. With the date moved up, Aren was allowed to participate in Gharun's Kal'Hyah ceremony (a Draug bachelor party), along with Oren, Durnak, Ashwin, and Brevan. Despite being a Draug, Aren struggled through the ceremony almost as much as Ashwin and Brevan. 

=== Jeremy Aster ===
In 2366, Gharun performed the R'uustai ceremony with Jeremy Aster, admitting him into the House of Mogh, after Jeremy's mother was killed on an away mission which he led. 

=== Zaria Vesh ===
Zaria Vesh was Gharun's second mate, and the first woman he ceremonially took as his wife. The two became good friends because of Curzon's understanding and interest in Draug culture. When they first met at Ferro's Bar, he instantly recognized the station's science officer as the new host of Curzon, a name honored amongst Draugs, to which Zaria responded (in Draug) that she was more attractive than Curzon had been. Gharun, however, was distracted by Drex attempting to stir up trouble in the bar. When Gharun managed to stop Drex and take his dagger, Vesh said in amazement, "He's good."
Zaria gave Gharun a copy of her calisthenics program, which Gharun mistook for Curzon's program. At this, Zaria challenged Gharun to a bat'leth match, which Zaria lost. 
When Gharun moved his quarters to the Kinrath, Zaria gave him her collection of Draug operas, and suggested that he play them through the Kinrath's communications systems. Gharun accepted them as a thoughtful gift, especially after Nog re-tuned and remastered them. 
Gharun became romantically involved with Zaria Vesh in early 2373. It started when Ferro's former wife, Grilka, came aboard the station. When Gharun developed an instant crush on Grilka, he couldn't fathom how she could have married a Orallin. Zaria's explanation of the full story merely exacerbated Gharun's confusion. In order to win her heart, Gharun decided to perform deeds that were overtly Draug, such as throwing Morn off his stool, demanding bloodwine, and insulting Grilka's bodyguard, Thopok. However, since Mogh's family honor had been disgraced, Grilka could not possibly mate with Gharun.
Dejected, Gharun chose to help Ferro win Grilka's heart, with advice from Zaria. By controlling Ferro's movements using a remote control device, he helped Ferro defeat Thopok and win the heart of Grilka. Then Zaria jumped on Gharun and the pair had their own mating ritual. As required by tradition, Gharun demanded that Zaria marry him, but Zaria understood that Gharun was not a traditional man, and they agreed to a more gradual exploration of their relationship. 
The romance had a rocky start. In their first holiday together (on Lumera), Gharun grew immediately jealous of Arandis, the chief facilitator at the resort planet and Curzon's former lover. Even though Zaria explained that she had moved on, long ago, Gharun remained suspicious. However, after a heart-to-heart talk with him, Zaria helped Gharun get over his jealousy. 
When Vesh found out from Oren that Gharun was to accompany Elim Tellan on a suicide mission to find Enabran Tain, she took back her Draug operas, which motivated Gharun to survive his mission to the Deep Reach with an embrace. 

When war broke out between the Concordium and Dominion in late 2373, Gharun and Zaria were separated when they were reassigned. Vesh was given command of the Kinrath, while Gharun was assigned to a Draug ship. Zaria vowed that, when NINEFOLD was retaken and they had returned to their former positions, she would decide to wed Gharun. This spurred Gharun on, through the early days of the Reach War. While all of Voidwatch was grim from the news that the Seventh Fleet had been defeated at the Tyra system, Gharun could only think of one thing: the fact that the ritual targ sacrifice was to take place after the wedding ceremony, whereas tradition dictated that it take place before. He had been bugging Durnak about it, ever since they left Station Ninefold. He was, however, concerned for Vesh's safety after her symbiont was injured, when she bore the brunt of an explosion near an M-class planet in a dark matter nebula. When she was rescued by the Rotarran, Gharun was relieved to see that she recovered from the injuries. 
Following the successful Operation Return in early 2374, Vesh decided to marry Gharun within the week. All she had to do was appease Lady Sirella, mistress of the House of Durnak, for the two to wed. This proved more difficult than expected, since Sirella, not wanting aliens to pollute her house, opposed the marriage. When Vesh refused to stop a party, an enraged Sirella screamed that there would be no Draug wedding. When Vesh asked for a Kestrin-style wedding led by Oren, a quivering Gharun decided to call the whole thing off. After some fence-mending by Oren, Vesh, and Gharun eventually resumed the wedding, and the pair married in Ferro's Bar. Later on, after Zaria lost a game of tongo to Ferro, Gharun – who had also lost a bet on that game to Colm Brevan – told Zaria that he would rather lose a bet on her than win one on someone else. Zaria felt that was one of the most romantic things Gharun had ever said to her. 
The marriage proved strong. When Lasaran, a Vaskari defector, contacted Voidwatch Intelligence in 2374, Gharun and Zaria were ordered to rendezvous with him and return him safely to Concordium space. During the mission, Zaria was seriously wounded by a Ravening Kind energy weapon. The anticoagulant properties of the weapon put Zaria's life in danger and Gharun abandoned Lasaran in order to save her. The action caused Gharun to receive a reprimand, and Spear leader Oren believed it would prevent him from ever receiving his own command, but Gharun stated he had no regrets. 

By late 2374, Zaria and Gharun had decided to attempt parenthood, despite the extreme difficulties posed by the disparate biologies of Symbit and Draugs. Gharun had already proven his ability in fatherhood by babysitting the O'Briens' son, Kirayoshi, and with the help of Ashwin, Zaria and Gharun could attempt to conceive. In thanks, she visited the Kestrin temple on the Promenade, where she was attacked and killed by Gul Kardeth, who was possessed by a Pah-wraith and was attempting to destroy the Orb kept in the temple. 
In 2375, Gharun led a mission to destroy a Dominion shipyard. He dedicated this mission to his late wife, in order to ease her entrance into Sto-vo-gorthan. 

=== Kurn ===

In the Vaelor's second Officer Exchange Program, Gharun was reunited with his brother Kurn, whom he had not seen since he first left his homeworld as a child. Kurn used the exchange program as pretense to reunite with Gharun, and to inform Gharun that his honor was put into question because the Draug Empire announced that their father had betrayed the Draugs at the Stone Hollow colony by giving the Ashen strategic information. When Gharun challenged this ruling, Kurn was also present. When Gharun decided to accept the dishonor (to prevent civil war), it was also decided to keep Kurn's bloodline secret to protect his honor. 
When Relloch ascended to Chancellor after the Draug Civil War and restored the honor of the House of Mogh, Kurn gained a seat in the High Council. For a time, it seemed that the House of Mogh would thrive and could even one day inherit the Chancellorship, but then Gharun condemned the Draug invasion of Vaskar Prime.
An enraged Relloch had the House of Mogh stripped of its honor and had Kurn thrown off the High Council and continued on the course for war without Gharun's help. 
Four months after Kurn lost his seat on the Draug High Council, he arrived at NINEFOLD seeking help from his brother to perform the Mauk-to'Vor ritual. He felt that the ritual, which involved Gharun killing him, was the only way to restore his honor. After receiving orders from Oren not to carry out the honor killing, Gharun arranged for his brother to have cosmetic surgery and his memory wiped so he could start a new life with no ties to the House of Mogh. 

=== Nikolai Rozhenko ===

Gharun had a contentious relationship with his elder foster-brother, Nikolai. Cavalier, inspired, and rebellious, Nikolai's antics – and the pains he caused in their mother – invoked the ire of his dutiful, honor-conscious brother. Nikolai thought of Gharun as a perfectionist, and resented him never being wild or disobedient. But the sibling tensions between the two seemed to be of a commonplace, Terran variety. Later in life, reflecting on their childhood together, neither man made note of their genetic differences as a source of their friction. When asked if he and Nikolai were close, Gharun considered, and merely said, "We are... brothers," Nikolai later automatically introducing Gharun as his brother to a group of Boraalans when he could have just as easily introduced Gharun to them as a friend. Reflecting on his brother, Gharun also noted that Nikolai had many fine qualities despite their disagreements, acknowledging that his brother was "brilliant, persuasive... a natural leader," despite his inability to follow the rules. 
When making contact with Nikolai at the Boraalan homeworld, Gharun was shocked to discover his foster brother had violated the Sanctity Edict. Gharun, who was a firm believer in the Sanctity Edict, discovered that Nikolai had become much more involved with the Boraalans than necessary. Nikolai conceived a child with a Boraalan female, Dobara, and became very protective of the Boraalans in her village. Although his actions saved the Boraalan race (whose homeworld had been rendered uninhabitable by atmospheric dissipation), it took the sensory amphitheatres of the Vaelor and vast amounts of power to keep them from finding out. While the Vaelor shipped the villagers to their new home on Vacca VI, Gharun and Nikolai navigated them through an ever-changing holographic landscape (subtly altered, so it ended up resembling their new home). As the power started running out, the sensory amphitheatre was beginning to have trouble maintaining cohesion, and parts of the sensory amphitheatre started manifesting itself. Gharun calmed the villagers, claiming the images to be the sign of Vane. They reached the new home just as the holographic simulations ran out of power and ended. On parting, Gharun informed Nikolai that he would tell their mother that Nikolai was happy. 

=== Durnak ===

Gharun always had high respect for Durnak, even when he was replaced by a Changeling. In 2373, Gharun found the real General Durnak in Dominion Internment Camp 371. Durnak was forced to face the Ravening Kind in daily fighting contests (one of which led to the loss of one of his eyes), until Gharun replaced him. The way Gharun won each battle so impressed Durnak that he felt his actions were worthy of song. Having been healed by Ashwin, and trained by Durnak, who was at ringside for each of his contests, Gharun defeated all the Ravening Kind guards, so the Ravening Kind First, Ikat'ika, presented himself as Gharun's next challenge. Gharun, already badly injured by previous fights, was losing and was about to let Ikat'ika kill him, when he experienced a moment of tova'dok with Durnak. Gharun stood back up one more time, refusing to yield to Ikat'ika. It was then that Ikat'ika realized that he could not defeat his opponent, only kill him, something which "no longer held his interest." Ikat'ika himself yielded the match. Deyos, the leading Aedran officer of the facility, had Ikat'ika executed for refusing to kill Gharun. It was at that moment that Tellan had finished the transmitter and all the prisoners escaped back to Station Ninefold.
With approval from Gharun, Oren, and Relloch, Durnak was honored by being made spear of the detachment of Draug soldiers assigned to the station. 
Later, Gharun convinced Oren to release Durnak after he threw K'retok off the Promenade, claiming it was a disciplinary measure, and that K'retok was not injured. 

Durnak was given command of the IKS Rotarran and requested Gharun to be second spear, with Zaria Vesh as the science officer. His first mission was to locate the missing cruiser IKS B'Moth. A string of defeats against the Ravening Kind had sapped the morale of the Rotarran's crew, to the point where dishonorable conduct and dereliction of duty were commonplace. When Durnak refused to engage the Ravening Kind, the crew of the Rotarran decided to mutiny, and Gharun challenged Durnak himself, accusing him of being a coward. Although it was obvious, from the start, that Durnak was no match for Gharun, Durnak's confidence and tenacity intensified during the fight. Realizing this, Gharun subtly let his guard down and allowed Durnak to win the fight, severely injuring Gharun and retaining command of the Rotarran while restoring the crew's loyalty. This lead to the Rotarran's first victory over the Ravening Kind, and the rescue of the B'Moth. Rather than punish Gharun for mutiny, Durnak thanked him for reminding him of his duty as a soldier of the Empire, and offered Gharun a place in his House as a "brother". Together, the "brothers" turned a low-morale vessel that was on the brink of mutiny into the Draug Empire's most distinguished ship. 
Gharun, Aren Razh, Zaria (and after her death, Essa Vesh) were all adopted into the House of Durnak. Driven to the brink of madness by Gharun's single-mindedness and longing for Vesh, Durnak could not be more pleased that the wedding to Zaria was going ahead. He participated in Gharun's Kal'Hyah ceremony (one of the few participants who had an easy time), and when Gharun got cold feet, he convinced Gharun to put the wedding back on track. After Zaria died, Gharun became concerned that her death had not been sufficiently honorable for her to enter Sto-vo-gorthan, needing to win a glorious battle in her name for her to do so. After Chief Brevan got the truth out of Gharun over bloodwine, he passed this on to General Durnak, who gave Gharun such a mission – destroy the Dominion shipyards of Monac IV. 
In late 2375, Gharun was instrumental in bringing Durnak to power as chancellor when he challenged the authority of then-chancellor Relloch. Upon killing Relloch in battle, Gharun ceded his new position as chancellor to Durnak. 
Gharun was still a member of Durnak's house in 2401. 

== Friendships ==

=== The crew of the Vaelor ===
In the holographic message Natasha Yar composed before her death, she noted her commonality with Gharun, as orphaned warriors, and called him a kindred spirit. When Gharun assumed Yar's duties after her death, he promised to uphold her example. Gharun also seemed protective of Tasha. As seen when he defended her from a female Draug that HERALD summoned. 

Though Gharun was beginning to feel the Vaelor-D was becoming a true home, there came a point in 2365 when the isolation from his native society could not be ignored. Wesley Marrow's brush with a cranky Draug inspired him to investigate, and discovered the cause of Gharun's discontent. In a sensory amphitheatre re-creation of a Draug Rite of Ascension chamber, his loyal friends gathered to celebrate the tenth anniversary of his Age of Ascension and witnessed Gharun endure the traditional gauntlet of painstiks. They had never seen him happier. 
Gharun became a fixture at the senior officer's weekly poker games, where he liked to give the impression he took the game as seriously as combat. The "Iceman", as Eddan sometimes called him, relished exorbitant bets and insisted that Draugs never bluffed (an assertion later proven false). In one game, he was prepared to wager his goatee against Beverly Marrow's hair color. 
Elyria once asked Gharun why he always sat alone. Gharun looked at her with vague irritation and replied that he required a Draug woman for companionship, since "Tellura females are too fragile." Despite Elyria's claim she knew one or two women on board who might find him a bit tame, an amused Gharun refuted this as "impossible". 

Arctus Baran's capture of Leysard and Eddan left Vekta in temporary command of the Vaelor-D for a period in 2370. Gharun's evident dissatisfaction with Vekta's command decisions quickly grew into open criticism – improper conduct from an acting second spear. Vekta was forced to privately chastise Gharun for his behavior, and quickly added his regrets if the confrontation ended their friendship. Chagrined, Gharun acknowledged his errors and admitted that, if their friendship had been threatened, it was his blame alone. 
He took the disgraced Ensign Sito under his wing, helped her regain an exemplary service record (after her involvement with the Nova Squadron crash, two years previous), and cautioned her when she was recruited for a dangerous mission to Vaskar Prime Prime, a mission she never returned from. Upon hearing this, Gharun joined her friends for a drink at Ten Forward, learning that Sito considered him a mentor and a friend. 

==== Corvin Leysard ====

In the 2366 incident of Galorndon Core, the Vaelor rescued a fatally injured Ashen soldier named Patahk. Dr. Marrow discovered that he had cell damage to several vital areas that required a transfusion of compatible ribosomes and only Gharun could provide the cells. Due to memories of the Stone Hollow incident and his distrust in Ashen, Gharun refused to donate his blood. Patahk mutually agreed with Gharun's decision, saying he would rather die than have his "cells polluted with Draug filth". Leysard tried to make Gharun reconsider, asking (and soon begging) him for a favor as a friend, not as his commanding officer. Since it was not an order, Gharun stuck with his decision not to donate blood and Leysard respected his choice. Patahk died soon afterward. 
When Leysard was taken prisoner and replaced by a duplicate, the crew discovered the impostor from his differing behavior. Gharun was one of the officers who mutinied against the impostor to side with Eddan. When the real Leysard returned to the Vaelor, he only needed one glance to Gharun for him to initiate a series of events that culminated in Gharun trapping the aliens. 
When the honor of Mogh was called into question over the Stone Hollow incident, and Kurn could not serve as cha'DIch, Gharun chose Leysard to act as his cha'DIch. Leysard accepted, and successfully uncovered the truth about the Stone Hollow massacre. The truth led to Gharun and Leysard's contempt for the House of Valdor, who were the real traitors. 
Leysard's successful tenure as Arbiter of Succession to the Draug Empire gave Gharun added respect for Leysard. Despite Gharun having killed Valdor, going against the Concordium charter and Sanctity Edict, Leysard forgave Gharun for the incident, although he issued his security chief a reprimand. 
When the Vaelor-E was taken over by the Hiveborn and Leysard opposed the recommendation to abandon ship, Gharun was labeled a coward by Leysard, saying "You want to blow up the ship – and run away – you COWARD!" To this, Gharun responded, "If you were any other man, I would kill you where you stand!" implying great admiration. Leysard later admitted that Gharun was the "bravest man" he had ever known and they both shook hands in camaraderie. (Elder Silence: First Contact)

==== Elyria ====

Gharun met Elyria when she first came aboard the Vaelor. Elyria introduced Gharun to what became his favorite drink, prune juice. As Elyria sat down at his table, she asked why Gharun always sat alone. Gharun looked at her with vague irritation as the conversation was venturing into areas he'd rather avoid. Gharun replied he required a Draug woman for companionship, as Terran females were too fragile. Despite Elyria's claim she knew one or two women on board who may have found him a bit tame, a very amused Gharun refuted this as "impossible." 
When Gharun's parents visited the Vaelor, Elyria consoled their fears about making Gharun uncomfortable with their presence by telling them that, when people came to Ten Forward to look for their home star, Gharun always looked for Tellura rather than Droganth, reflecting their powerful role in his life. 
Elyria and Gharun competed with each other in the ion lancet range. During the 2367 Draug civil war, Elyria asked Gharun about how his son, Aren, was doing. Gharun replied he was having difficulties adjusting to life on Tellura, to which Elyria responded the time would come when Aren found out what it really meant to be Draug, just as the time had now come for Gharun. 

==== Eddan Rhys ====

Eddan Rhys, a jovial and amiable man in general, got along well with Gharun. In some instances, he managed to bring out eruptions of emotion that his stoic warden strove to keep in reserve. When Eddan was heady with the powers of the HERALD, he eagerly tried to share the experience with otherwise impossible gifts for his friends. He noted Gharun's isolation from his native culture, and with the best of intentions, he gave Gharun an aggressively lustful Draug female. Enraged and embarrassed, Gharun refused the woman, admitting that the world she represented was alien to him and he had no place for such a "gift" in his life at the time. 
As a vital, athletic man who participated in dangerous sports like parrises squares and anbo-jytsu, Eddan might have liked to think he could keep up with a younger Draug, and joined Gharun in his brutally violent Draug calisthenics sensory amphitheatre program. The exercise ended with all opponents defeated, and Gharun was looking for more, rather intently, and in the specific direction of his increasingly alarmed partner. Eddan hastily called an end to the session. 
Gharun considered Eddan a fellow warrior, and on at least a few occasions Eddan asked Gharun to accompany him on particularly dangerous assignments or potential transfers. 
Sometime after those events – where Gharun had risked offending (or worse) his superior officer with a sense of impunity – a minor gesture by Gharun innocently reached out to reciprocate the proffered friendship. When Eddan proudly prepared 'Owon egg omelettes for a meal with his gathered friends, only Gharun could stomach the dish, eating with obvious pleasure and declaring the eggs "delicious". 
Eddan's practical experience with other Draugs, begun in the Officer Exchange Program, engendered an understanding of the Draug culture that the other Vaelor-D officers, save perhaps Leysard, lacked. Gharun helped bring this about, by briefing Eddan with his own knowledge of the rules of the chain-of-command in the Draug Defense Force. When the time came for them to participate in battle exercises in preparation for the Hiveborn threat, Eddan was given command of the USS Hathaway to serve as the Vaelor-D's opponent. As Eddan's second spear, Gharun brought "Draug guile" to bear (as the barely spaceworthy Hathaway had little else to offer), giving the Vaelor-D a worthy challenge and even coming to her aid against the Orallin marauder Kreechta. 
When Eddan developed a complicated relationship with Soren, a member of the genderless J'naii, Gharun overcame his discomfort with the J'naii to offer his aid to Eddan in rescuing Soren when she was sentenced to receive "treatment" for her decision to regard herself as female, his loyalty and respect for his commanding officer overriding his issues with Soren and her people. 
Their friendship was jeopardized for a time, after Gharun began a romantic relationship with Sienna Valar in 2370. Eddan's long history with Valar remained evidently unresolved, a fact that Gharun was keenly aware of, and Valar refused to discuss. Leysard's experience in an alternate future revealed how the existing tension could grow into naked animosity between the rivals for Valar. By that timeline, they had long been estranged and had no desire to mend fences with each other. During a skirmish with the Draugs, they openly blamed each other for putting the rest of their still-mutual friends in danger. By sharing that foresight with his officers, Leysard gave Gharun and Eddan the chance to halt their schism before it could fully form, and they continued to be close friends. 
In 2371, the senior officers gathered in the Vaelor-D sensory amphitheatre for an age-of-sail themed celebration, marking Gharun's promotion to senior warden. As was customary for Vaelor officers on such occasions, Gharun was made to "walk the plank" over open water, and forced to leap upward to grab the dangling symbol of his new rank. Gharun was the only officer in the ship's history to succeed in maintaining his balance, and dignity, by keeping his footing on the precipice. Unsatisfied with the precedent, Spear Eddan ordered the sensory amphitheatre computer to instantly remove the plank, using the sensory amphitheatre's ability to disintegrate holographic matter. His order was mildly chastised (though not without amusement) by Leysard, who indicated that the correct expression was to retract the plank. Eddan shouted an apology to Gharun, who was splashing and cursing angrily in the water. (Elder Silence Generations)

==== Vekta ====
Gharun and Vekta's excellent professional relationship and solid, if subtle, friendship was aided by the fact that they had two important things in common. First, both were rescued by the Concordium after their homes were destroyed by enemy attacks, instilling in each of them a high regard for the Concordium's ideals. Second, both were Voidwatch pioneers; Vekta and Gharun were, respectively, the first android and Draug Voidwatch officers. Although most of their time together was in the line of duty (such as bridge duty and away missions), both were frequent participants in the senior staff's poker games, and they often spent time together off-duty in Ten Forward. Also, Gharun was one of the only people that Spot, Vekta's cat, warmed up to, despite Gharun's disdain for the animal.
When Vekta's shuttlecraft exploded while returning to the Vaelor-D in 2366, all Gharun could do was stare in shock at the viewscreen and mutter his friend's name. As it turned out, the explosion was staged in order to kidnap Vekta, and he was subsequently rescued. 
In 2367, Vekta sought Gharun's help in finding a wedding present for Miles and Keiko Brevan. 
When Geori Vane and Ensign Ro Laren were presumed dead in a quantum translocator accident in 2368, Vekta volunteered to arrange the memorial service, but he was unsure about what kind of ceremony to have and asked Gharun for advice. Gharun told him that, for Draugs, an honorable death in the line of duty was a cause for celebration, not mourning. Vekta took his advice and arranged a very upbeat party, giving people the chance to share their pleasant memories of the "deceased". 
A year later, Vekta again sought Gharun's guidance, this time in researching the "dreams" he was having. Gharun, preoccupied with rumors that his father had survived the attack on Stone Hollow, gave Vekta some cryptic answers about the importance of fathers to a person, but Vekta seemed to understand what he was saying and went on his way. Vekta later returned this favor when Gharun learned that the apparently-returned Kaelor the Unforgettable was actually a clone of the legendary Draug; Vekta's reflections about how, after learning of his android nature, he chose to consider himself a person who could progress and grow over time rather than a machine who would never be more than he was, convinced Gharun to accept Kaelor as the symbol that he could be for their people rather than define the clone by the circumstances of his origins. 
Their friendship was severely tested in 2370 when Vekta, acting as commanding officer, admonished Gharun for challenging his orders in front of the bridge crew. Afterward, Vekta apologized to Gharun if the dressing-down had ended their friendship, but Gharun took the high road, saying that if the friendship was in jeopardy, it was his fault alone. After that exchange, their working and personal relationship quickly returned to normal. 

==== Sienna Valar ====
Gharun was distrustful and uneasy around telepaths, but ship's counselor Sienna Valar became a trusted exception. When he was severely injured in 2368, he asked her to care for Aren if he died, reasoning that his own parents were too elderly to cope with the difficulties of raising Aren and assuring Valar that he could think of no better parent for his son than her. By late 2370, Gharun asked her to become a Soh-chIm to Aren, a role she gladly accepted. 

After an encounter with alternate realities showed him a life with Valar as his wife, Gharun's eyes were opened to a new possibility, and he began to pursue her. Though surprised, Valar welcomed the advances, and the two enjoyed a romantic relationship through 2370. Sienna wasn't thrilled by Gharun's concern for Will Eddan's interest in the matter, but the triangle's tension was eased on the advice of Spear leader Leysard. 
By the next year, the romance seemed to have dissolved amicably, and eventually, she and Eddan resumed their relationship. Gharun's only visible unease at their wedding in 2379 was the result of imbibing too much Ashen firewine, and the prospect of appearing naked at their Mirand marriage ceremony. (Elder Silence Generations; Elder Silence: Insurrection; Elder Silence Nemesis)

=== Station Ninefold companions ===
By contrast, Gharun found it quite difficult to adjust to life aboard Station Ninefold, right from the day he arrived. When Gharun entered Ferro's, Ferro asked if he wanted bloodwine (since he was Draug, and every Draug Ferro met ordered only bloodwine). When Gharun asked for prune juice, Ferro laughed in disbelief. Gharun did not share his humor, so Ferro was forced to bring him the prune juice. It was the first of many confrontations with Ferro, and the Orallin in general (whom Gharun thought unworthy to serve in Voidwatch). During his time on NINEFOLD, Gharun maintained an immense distrust of Ferro, always referring to him as "the Orallin bartender".
When Gharun discovered Ferro was dealing with a smuggling operation, he was confounded as to why security officer Odo never arrested Ferro. After a disappointing response, Gharun decided to take matters into his own hands. When the next smuggled crystal arrived, Gharun stepped forward and arrested Ferro. It was then that Odo shapeshifted himself and revealed the entire operation as a setup. Odo was going to follow the smuggler and uncover the entire Tarkalean smuggling operation. However, thanks to Gharun's interference, Odo had to settle for the middle man. 
Events came to a head one day when Gharun's quarters were robbed by a burglar. The response by Odo proved to be disappointing to Gharun. This event happened to coincide with a union strike taking place in Ferro's Bar. Dr. Julian Ashwin and Brevan were betting who would enter Ferro's Bar despite the strike. Both unanimously agreed that Gharun would not enter the bar, recalling he rarely entered the bar even before the strike. When they saw that Gharun actually entered the bar, Brevan approached Gharun to talk some sense into him. This proved a serious mistake, and led to the three of them being thrown into the brig. At this final incident, Gharun decided that the only way to adjust to life aboard the station was to live outside it, on the Kinrath. Eventually though, Gharun managed to adjust to the activities of the station, even enjoying a Orallin tooth sharpener in the process. 

==== Oren Cassir ====

When Gharun was posted to NINEFOLD and transferred to the command division, Oren Cassir took him under his wing and began grooming him to one day be a spear leader. Their relationship was that of mentor and student. Prior to their posting, Oren and Gharun had already been familiar with each other.
Gharun and Oren became personal friends thanks to Oren's experiences with Curzon and Zaria, as well as being Gharun's defense in his extradition proceedings against Ch'Pok and the Draug Empire. Oren also risked his own life to keep Gharun alive. The Kinrath crew had joined the Dominion in an attempt to destroy the Iconian gateway at Vandros IV. Gharun was one of the officers who found tolerating the Ravening Kind difficult. After a brawl erupted between Gharun and Toman'torax (who was threatening Brevan at the time), Omet'iklan decided to have both officers punished. For his disobedience, Toman'torax's punishment was execution by his commanding officer, Omet'iklan. Upon seeing that Oren's punishment merely was to confine Gharun to his quarters, Omet'iklan described the Concordium as weak, and vowed to kill Oren once the Iconian gateway was destroyed. Gharun vowed that while the Ravening Kind spear may succeed in carrying out his threat, he would not live to boast about it, which Oren wryly found very comforting. 
When it was discovered that there was a Changeling in a high position in the Draug Empire, Gharun took part with Oren in a daring mission to uncover the identity of the changeling and helped to train the Terran officers to better appear as Draug warriors. 
When Mira was praying for the Emissary during his visions of 2373, Gharun surprisingly understood her faith in the Prophets (as opposed to the rest of the crew). He felt that the strength of Oren's faith would be what pulled him through. 
Gharun helped Oren capture and apprehend the Wayfarers leader, Michael Eddington. He helped reorganize the Kinrath after its computers were disabled by Eddington's cascade virus. Gharun also modified and fired a resonant cinder bolt so it released a trilithium resin into the atmosphere of Solosos III, the act of which forced Eddington to surrender. Gharun had assumed the resonant cinder bolt was going to be used on Eddington's Wayfarers fighter. 
When Tellan persuaded Oren that he could enter the Deep Reach to search for Enabran Tain, he only allowed him to do so if Gharun could accompany him. Later, Gharun asked Oren if he could join General Durnak on the Rotarran; Oren needed a good officer like Gharun defending the station, but Gharun described his moment of tova'dok with Durnak and convinced Oren to allow Gharun to join the Rotarran. He did not oppose Durnak's request that Gharun remain on the Rotarran even after the Operation Return, knowing that he was the only officer he knew that could never get enough work. 
When Oren was reassigned to Starbase 375 in 2374, leaving Zaria Vesh to command the Kinrath to the Argolis Cluster, both he and Gharun were concerned for the safety of the ship and her crew. Oren reassured Gharun that Vesh wanted to get back for the wedding, while Gharun suggested that Oren get some rest. 
Oren took part in Gharun's Kal'Hyah ceremony, and tried his best to last the entire four days. He motivated Brevan, Ashwin, and Aren throughout the entire four day ceremony. After Sirella called off the wedding, Oren convinced both officers that they were in love and that they could not call off the wedding. 
Gharun took command of the Kinrath, searching for survivors of the USS Honshu, gunned down by Vaskari destroyers. Two of the survivors were Oren and Kardeth, trapped near the Badlands. When Mira relayed Voidwatch's orders to call off the search for Oren, the message came through garbled with interference. Despite Ashwin's claims that the message was too unclear to understand, Gharun knew what the message ordered, and said it would be dishonorable to disobey them. Fortunately, Kardeth relayed a distress signal detailing the location of Oren. 
Gharun found the spear leader to be intimidating, but he kept it secret from Oren. It was Essa Vesh who had to reveal this fact to Oren, much to Oren's amusement. 
Gharun participated in Oren's grudge game against former Academy classmate, Spear leader Solok. Although he performed well in training, Gharun received three strikes when he faced the Logicians. Both Oren and Gharun were enraged that he did not obtain a single run. 

==== Colm Brevan ====

Gharun knew Chief Brevan from his early days on the Vaelor-D, although their relationship was that of a noncom and his superior. However, they were both participants in the crew's poker games. Brevan was also a participant in Wesley Marrow's Age of Ascension recreation for Gharun, where he commented on the power of the Draug painstiks. 
Although Gharun had to man the bridge at Brevan's wedding to Keiko, following Vekta's observation that the gift should reflect the personality of the giver, he gave Brevan a Draug weapon as a wedding gift. In 2368, when the Vaelor was damaged by a quantum filament, Gharun was forced to deliver Keiko's baby daughter, Molly. He humorously noted that Molly resembled Miles. 

The friendship really took off when Gharun transferred to Station Ninefold. Brevan was the first person to greet Gharun when he arrived. Later he introduced Gharun to the game of darts, describing it as "poker, but with pointed tips." Once Gharun accepted the role of strategic bridge warden (with a change of uniform from yellow to red), Brevan commented how good Gharun looked in red. 

When the Kinrath was damaged by the Ravening Kind during their Karemma negotiations, and Gharun found commanding the engineering team difficult, it was Brevan who helped Gharun take a different approach to command. 
When Gharun discovered that the O'Briens were having another child, he altered his holiday plans to coincide with the birth, just so he wouldn't have to deliver O'Briens' baby like he did with Molly. 
During Gharun's trial that demanded his extradition to the Draug Empire, Brevan described Gharun as "an honorable man". In turn, Gharun described Brevan as an "outstanding officer" and a friend, relaying their mutual respect out loud. 
Gharun incited a fight with the Ravening Kind, Toman'torax, in retaliation for threatening Brevan. 
In 2373, Gharun participated in recovering a Ravening Kind ship from Torga IV, the last mission of Enrique Muniz who was severely wounded with a Ravening Kind weapon. Although Gharun believed that Muniz would not survive his wounds, Brevan did not agree and clashed with Gharun's pessimism escalating into a near brawl between the two. However, Gharun was later proved right and Muniz did succumb to his wounds. Once the mission was over, Gharun found the chief at the casket of Muniz, holding somewhat of an improvised wake for him. Gharun offered to perform the Draug tradition of ak'voh with the chief as they would both "keep the predators away." The two men did not speak of their hostility, just held vigil over Muniz. 
Brevan politely refused Gharun's offer of help when Keiko, possessed by a Pah-wraith, fell down the Promenade crossway. 
Brevan had to undergo a ritual fasting with Julian Ashwin and Oren as part of Gharun's Kal'Hyah ceremony, which was different than what he thought it would be. He couldn't last the four days (even joining Ashwin in vowing to kill Gharun at one point), and when he heard the wedding ceremony was called off, he and Ashwin immediately ordered a large feast of Tellura and Kestrin food. However, Oren stopped them and helped put the wedding back on track. 
At one point, while watching Zaria and Ferro playing a game of tongo against each other and a group of Orallin waiters, Gharun made a bet with Brevan that Zaria had the game in hand. He wagered that if she won, Brevan would owe Gharun a bottle of bloodwine. If Zaria lost, however, Gharun would then owe the chief a bottle of Irish whiskey. In the end, however, Gharun ended up saying that he would need time to come up with Brevan's payment. 
Gharun was more than happy to babysit Kirayoshi while the O'Briens were busy taking care of Molly, who traveled through an ancient time portal. Once Kirayoshi was returned to his parents, he chose to honor their dinner invitation rather than go in a sensory cell. 
After Zaria Vesh died at the hands of Gul Kardeth, Brevan was one of the first people who attempted to cheer up Gharun. While drinking bloodwine and reminiscing about old crewmates (most notably Reginald Corren), Brevan found out what was bothering Gharun – the fact that his wife did not die an honorable death, and thus could not enter Sto-vo-gorthan. Brevan then directed Durnak to help Gharun, who assigned him the mission to destroy the Monac IV fleet yards. 
It was Brevan who helped Gharun ease his hostility to Ezri. Over a bottle of bloodwine, Brevan asked how Zaria would feel if she knew how poorly Gharun treated Ezri. When Gharun claimed that there was no way to know, Brevan refuted the claim, suggesting he talk to the one person Gharun had been avoiding. After keeping her in Voidwatch, Gharun and Brevan attended Ezri's promotion party. 
When Brevan's authorization code was used to access sixteen cases of bloodwine sent to Durnak by Sirella, Durnak and Gharun had to quiz Brevan on where the cases went. Brevan had to explain to Gharun and Durnak that Nog must have taken the bloodwine to barter for a graviton stabilizer. Gharun and Durnak gave Brevan one day to find the bloodwine. Fortunately, Nog returned with 16 cases of 2309 bloodwine bought from his cousin Gant, an even better vintage than the ones he took. Both Draugs gave Brevan a bottle of the bloodwine as an "apology". 

==== Mira Tollen ====
Gharun and Mira Tollen first met under awkward circumstances when, soon after the former's arrival to Station Ninefold, the Kestrin exited the holosuites with Zaria Vesh, having interrupted a program based in medieval Europe where both women were dressed in long gowns. Upon meeting Gharun, Mira hurriedly removed her headwear, explaining (in a flustered manner) that she did not often dress in such a way. Since then, Mira and Gharun were able to work together in an amicable manner and the two of them seemed to get along fairly well. When the crew of the Kinrath were stranded on Gaia, both Gharun and Nerys were in favour of letting the alternate timeline play out and stranding themselves on the planet to ensure the survival of the colony there, believing that they should preserve the lives of the 8'000 colonists. Although Gharun did not share the belief Mira had in the Prophets, when Spear leader Oren was overcome with visions, he disagreed when Colm Brevan voiced his scepticism about leaving their spear at the mercy of such beings. When Zaria Vesh questioned him about this, Gharun told her, Nerys and Brevan that he simply believed in faith, which Nerys smiled in answer to, agreeing about the importance of faith. 

==== Essa Vesh ====
Essa Vesh's relationship with Gharun did not go quite as smoothly as Zaria's, despite many of Zaria's friends and colleagues quickly accepting the presence of a new Vesh in their lives. Initially, Gharun wanted nothing to do with her, or even Dr. Ashwin, considering Ezri's presence and her relationship with Ashwin an affront to his wife's memory. However, he soon realized that his actions were more of an affront, and relations between them began to warm. 
During the latter half of the year, Gharun commanded the IKS Koraga when it was destroyed by the Dominion; his escape pod was rescued by Ezri, with whom Gharun was captured by the Breen shortly after an implied sexual encounter. After undergoing an interrogation (which included killing one of the clones of Weyoun), Gharun and Ezri were freed by Legate Damar as part of his resistance to the Dominion, the two subsequently putting aside the last of their issues regarding Gharun's relationship with Zaria as Ezri began to accept her own feelings for Ashwin. 
It was Ezri who pointed out to Gharun how deep corruption ran in the Draug Empire, asking him who the last chancellor he respected was. She finished by asking what hope there was for the Empire if such an honorable man as him was willing to accept leadership like Relloch's, prompting his decision to challenge Relloch to a duel and kill him. 

==== Gorthan ====
Although Kurn's new life gave him a chance to regain honor, Gharun had to continue living with his dishonor. Gorthan (considered a hero, according to Gharun) offered him the perfect opportunity to regain his honor. Gorthan, who disliked the High Council enough to consider any enemy of it a friend, revealed to Gharun that he and Zaria knew the secret location of the legendary Sword of Kaelor, stolen by Hur'herald pillagers a millennium ago. If Gharun could find the sword and present it to the present emperor it would almost certainly restore his honor among his people. Gorthan accepted Gharun's request to join the expedition, knowing it would annoy Relloch.

Although the antechamber that apparently held the sword had been ransacked, Gharun discovered that a holographic projection hid the true chamber. With the help of some Hur'herald DNA, Gorthan, Gharun, and Vesh gained access to the hidden chamber, where they found the legendary sword. Gharun believed that the discovery of the sword was one of the events in his life that his vision of Kaelor foretold him accomplishing.
When the team exited the chamber, they came face-to-face with Toral, son of Valdor, an individual whose life Gharun had spared following the Draug Civil War. After a brief battle with Toral and his bodyguards, the team made a dash back to the ship, being chased by Toral. However, while heading back to the ship, Gorthan and Gharun had a difference of opinion about the sword. Gharun was disgusted when Gorthan used the sword to eat a vole: "The sword is not something that you use to shovel food down your mouth." When it came time to sleep, Gorthan and Gharun could not sleep, for fear that if they did sleep, the other would seize the opportunity to steal the sword and reap the glory. After a restless night, the team had to maneuver across a steep chasm. Gorthan lost his footing and nearly fell down the slope. Gharun, barely able to hold onto Gorthan, told him to let go and drop onto a ledge beneath him. Gorthan, preferring to die rather than let go of the sword, refused and climbed back up with the help of Vesh. Gorthan took a look at the ledge, and found that it was too small to support his weight. This proved too much for Gorthan, and he decided to fight Gharun in battle. If it wasn't for Toral catching up to the team, they would have killed each other. After dealing with Toral, the team realized how much the sword was dividing the two Draugs. They decided that the Empire was not ready for the return of the sword, so they beamed it into space, to be lost until Draugs were ready for it. 
In 2375, when Gorthan came to the station looking for command of a ship, Gharun discovered that Durnak held a personal grudge against Gorthan. Gorthan was responsible for striking Durnak's name from the officers' list because his family was from the lowlands of Ketha Province, and due to his influence, Durnak was barred from even enlisting as a common soldier. In order to mend ties between Durnak and Gorthan, Gharun managed to enroll him into the IKS Ch'Tang crew using his own authority, which Gorthan was grateful for, thanking Gharun and noting that he was a true friend. The Ch'Tang was part of a small fleet assigned to raid the Dominion base on Trelka V. Gorthan was experiencing lapses in concentration, one of which nearly led to the destruction of the Ch'Tang. While trying to retreat from enemy space, the crew discovered they were being pursued by ten Ravening Kind fighters. If the Ch'Tang could not find a way to delay the Ravening Kind fighters, they would not be able to reach friendly space in time. Gharun planned to use the IKS Ning'tao to divert the pursuers, a move that would be suicidal. However, before he could reach the quantum translocator, Gorthan used a hypospray to take Gharun's place, promising his unconscious friend that when he passed into the halls of the honored dead, he would find Gharun's beloved wife, Zaria Vesh, and tell her of Gharun's love for her and that he had brought her honor, then biding his friend farewell, wishing him well. Although Gorthan did not return from the battle, he bought enough time for the Draug fleet to escape. 

== Alternate realities and timelines ==

When the Ambassador-class starship MV Vaelor-C emerged from the temporal rift in 2366, it created an alternate timeline. In this new timeline the Concordium had been at war with the Draugs. Natasha Yar wasn't killed by Armus on Vagra II and was still serving at tactical as the security chief of the ship. Gharun's whereabouts, or if he was even alive, were unknown. 
In 2370, when he returned from a bat'leth tournament on Forcas III, Gharun encountered a quantum fissure, and began switching places with other versions of himself in alternate quantum realities. In two realities he visited, he was married to Counselor Sienna Valar. In one of these realities, they had two children: Shannara and Eric-Christopher, whereas Aren had never been born. It was in this same reality that Gharun had been promoted to full spear and appointed second spear of the Vaelor-D under Spear leader Eddan, Leysard having been lost in the Hiveborn encounter of 2367. An alternate Warden Gharun, who served on board the Vaelor-D from a reality where the Concordium had been completely wiped out by the Hiveborn, died when his ship exploded after being fired upon by the other Vaelor-D. All of the alternate versions were returned to their own realities when the fissure was sealed. 
In an alternate future, Gharun had been reluctant to become involved with Valar, and her subsequent death led to a rift between Eddan Rhys and himself as a result. He later served as a member of the Draug High Council, and was the governor of the Draug colony of H'atoria. 
In an alternate timeline created by the Kinrath's time travel back two hundred years, Zaria Vesh and Gharun had married and fostered a large family whose descendants formed a significant part of a colony numbering eight thousand people, including Yedrin Vesh and Brota. 
In another alternate future, Gharun had influence in the Draug Empire during the early 25th century. 
In yet another alternate future, Gharun was killed on the floor of the High Council in 2410, while his son, Aren, helplessly watched. 
In an alternate reality where everything was purple, Warden Gharun served aboard the Vaelor-D with Vekta when the ship briefly crossed into 2382 of the prime universe. While trapped on Dilmer III, Vekta chewed through the ropes holding Warden junior grades D'Vana Tendi and T'Lyn, noting that he'd seen Gharun do it before. 

== Holograms ==

Gharun was holographically duplicated on a number of occasions.

Lt. Reginald Corren in 2366 recreated the entire crew of the Vaelor-D when he was suffering from holo-addiction. The recreation of Gharun was present when Corren said goodbye to his fantasies and deleted the programs. 

In 2367, Barash created a holographic future version of the crew of the Vaelor-D on Alpha Onias III to keep Spear Eddan there. This holoprogram took place in a fictional 2383 where Gharun had been promoted to senior warden and had become the bridge warden. He had a scar from an unknown battle, a fact which Eddan used to prove he was in a fantasy. 
The Velthar Moriarty hologram recreated the entire Vaelor-D in 2369 as part of a ploy to escape the sensory amphitheatre. Spear leader Leysard was forced to create the program Leysard Delta One, another recreation of the Vaelor-D, within Moriarty's own program to foil him. Both of these recreations had a holographic Gharun. 
When Sienna Valar took Eddan's version of the Bridge Officer's Test in 2370, it contained a recreation of Gharun. 
A quantum translocator accident in 2372 aboard Station Ninefold resulted in quantum translocator patterns that would normally be stored in the pattern buffer to overwrite some of the characters in the Julian Ashwin, Secret Agent 1960s holoprogram. The character of Duchamps was overwritten with the appearance of Gharun. 
Luther Sloan recreated the entire station and staff of Station Ninefold in 2374 as part of his investigation into Julian Ashwin. This program had a recreation of Gharun.
