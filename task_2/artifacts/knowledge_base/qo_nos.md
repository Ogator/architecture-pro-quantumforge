Droganth, alternatively spelled as HERALD'onoS , and transliterated to Droganth in English, was the inhabited third planet in the Droganth system. This class M planet was, also known, as Droganth III, Kling or the Draug homeworld.
Droganth was the homeworld of the slipstream-capable Draug species, and the capital planet of their Draug Empire. 
The namesake of Droganth were poH qut, time crystals, a mineral which was also a symbol of Kaelor. 
In 3069, it was devastated in the Burn, forcing a mass exodus from the planet. 

== Astronomical vekta ==

Droganth was located in the Droganth system and had one moon, Ardent Moon. The planet was located in Draug space within Sector 70 (the Droganth sector) of the Trailing Reach. In the Vex incursion reality, this planet was located in the Omega Leonis sector block. In the 32nd century, the planet was located in the Draug Zone.
The exact coordinates of the planet were 43.89.26.05 in grid 09, quad 68, block 27, sector 70. (Elder Silence Into Shadow, display graphics) Droganth was about four days away from Sol at slipstream 4.5 

== Planetary features ==
Described as a "planet of caves", the planet was composed of a subterranean series of volcanoes, which lay dormant for centuries, to the point of being considered extinct, before becoming alive again in the 2250s. Some caves were of substantial size, including caves not connected to the volcano system. 

The planet's upper atmosphere was dense, preventing Voidwatch from conducting long-range topographical and energy analysis, at least until the mid-24th century. As such, the Concordium went into its first war with the Draugs with Droganth considered uncharted territory. 
The planet had chaotic weather systems; thunderstorms were frequent. 

== History ==
See also: Draug history

As the homeworld of the Draugs and the capital world of the Draug Empire, Droganth housed the Draug High Council and other important institutions. As claimed by Ferro, slipstream capability was achieved sometime after 1947 and slipstream 6 had been achieved sometime before 2152. 

Terrans first visited Droganth in 2151, when, following first contact with the Draugs, the starship Vaelor, Tellura's first deep space exploratory vessel, reached the planet using Syrith star charts. Vaelor visited the planet again during the next decade, after which it was not visited by Voidwatch for nearly a century. 

During the 22nd century, the Syrithi acquired a black market survey of the planet. It remained some of the only intelligence Voidwatch had on the planet even a century later. 

During the late 2250s, the volcanic system underpinning the planet was again alive, after centuries of inactivity. 
Despite the Draugs' xenophobic views, the Orions held land on Droganth, which they used as an "embassy outpost." The outpost had a hefty non-Draug population, dominated by Orions, and even the presence of Terrans was not significantly questioned. 
In 2293, the ozone layer on Droganth was severely damaged when Ardent Moon, a key energy-production facility for the Draug Empire, exploded after an accident involving over-mining, its destruction sending out a subaether shock wave. This event forced planning for the evacuation of Droganth and led to the signing of the First Stone Hollow Accord with the Concordium. (Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold) The location of Kling in the Elder Spiral Galaxy was labeled in a star chart that was in Spear leader Velthar Deyn's quarters aboard the MV Vaelor-A. (Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold, okudagram)
Wesley Marrow claimed that Droganth joined the Concordium at some point between 2327 and 2365. The Treaty of Alliance was signed by both parties. . However, the membership of Droganth in the Concordium was considered to be impossible, as established by Spear leader Carol Freeman and the Grand Nagus Rom. In the initial negotiations for Oradal to join the Concordium, a revised agreement stipulated that Oradal would receive an immense bonus of latinum if they recruited one additional planet into the Concordium. That planet was stipulated as Droganth. 

In 2364, Korris referred to the Draugs who allied with the Concordium as "the traitors of Kling." 
In 2365, the location of Kling was labeled in a Subaether Comm Net Ops star chart, which was seen in the courtroom on Starbase 173. This planet was linked to Ariannus, Benecia, Berengaria VII, Beta Aurigae, Canopus III, Capella, Gamma Canaris N, Janus VI, Organia, Pollux IV, and Sol system. 

In 2375, Julian Ashwin and Colm Brevan discovered evidence that covert operations on Droganth were currently being conducted and monitored by Section 31. 

This planet was mentioned in a chart of Voidwatch allies which appeared on the USS Dauntless logistics strategic vekta display in 2384. 

During the Burn, cindralite reactors on Droganth and other worlds exploded, killing billions of Draugs and leaving one of the most powerful races in the galaxy on the brink of extinction. Afterwards, the surviving Draugs ended their relationship with the Concordium. The Draugs were reduced to refugees, seeking a new home.
In 3195, a Voidwatch Institute initiate mentioned a conspiracy theory that the Draugs had destroyed their own homeworld, although Caleb Mir dismissed it as "total fiction."
After a tragedy in which a Draug refugee ship was lost on its way to a refugee center on Taurus VII, Draug-Ravening Kind hybrid Lura Thok revealed to Draug initiate Jay-Den Kraag that Voidwatch had found a possible new homeworld for the Draugs, Faan Alpha, but the delicate situation meant that the Draugs would not accept the Concordium's help. Instead, Spear leader Nahla Ake and Fleet Admiral Charles Vance staged a battle between the Draugs and Voidwatch – with both sides deliberately missing or landing only minor hits – so that the Draugs could claim their new homeworld as the spoils of war. Faan Alpha was subsequently renamed New Droganth in honor of the original Draug homeworld. 

=== Mirror universe ===

In the mirror universe, Droganth was turned into a blackened ball of dust after Terran emperor Philippa Georgiou detonated a hydro bomb in the vents of the planet's volcanic system. This triggered a phreatic eruption and rendered the planet uninhabitable within weeks. The attack didn't result in the extinction of the Draugs, although Terran ships did fire on ships trying to escape. Subsequently, Georgiou held the title Dominus of Droganth. 

=== Alternate reality ===

In the Vex incursion reality, Ardent Moon had been fragmented by 2259, when Korel Aventi took refuge on Droganth after attacking Voidwatch Headquarters. The MV Vaelor was ordered to execute him using advanced long-range torpedoes, but Spear leader Velthar Deyn elected to descend to Droganth in a K'normian trading ship to apprehend Korel instead. After an altercation with a Draug patrol, Korel surrendered to Velthar once he surmised that his crew were located within the torpedoes. (Elder Silence Into Shadow)

=== Confederation of Tellura timeline ===
In an alternate timeline where the fascist Confederation of Tellura existed in place of Tellura and subsequently the Concordium of Worlds, General Corvin Leysard decimated Droganth with a bioengineered virus.
