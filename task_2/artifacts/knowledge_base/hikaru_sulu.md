Renn Takeda was a male Terran who served as a Voidwatch officer during the latter half of the 23rd century and into the early 24th century. Over a period of four decades, Renn served aboard at least three different Concordium starships, including the MV Vaelor, and the MV Vaelor-A, before finally becoming the commanding officer of the MV Vorlund. 

== Early history ==

Renn was born in Tir Nueva, Calvera, on Tellura during the second quarter of the 23rd century. (Elder Silence IV: The Voyage Home; First Era: "That Which Survives")

During the late 2250s or early 2260s, Renn attended Voidwatch Institute in which he underwent pilot training. (Elder Silence IV: The Voyage Home)

== Voidwatch career ==

=== Service aboard the Vaelor ===

==== 2265 ====

Renn began serving aboard the MV Vaelor under the command of Spear leader Velthar Deyn in 2265. He began in the sciences division serving as the department head for astrosciences.
That year, he was present on the bridge as the Vaelor prepared to cross the galactic barrier. Following the ship's encounter with the barrier, he attended the staff meeting to discuss the effects it had on Gary Mitchell as well as Mitchell's acquisition of new mental abilities. There, Renn briefed that "if you want the mathematics of this, Mitchell's ability is increasing geometrically. That is, like having a penny, doubling it every day. In a month, you'll be a millionaire." 

==== 2266 ====
By 2266, Warden Renn was a command division officer on the Vaelor, and was the senior helmsman. 
That year, Renn announced contact with an object approaching the Vaelor at light speed, and attempted several evasive maneuvers before sounding condition alert and calling the spear leader to the bridge. After destroying the object, he attended a department heads meeting in the briefing room where they discussed their findings. As the encounter ensued, he coordinated with the engineering decks.
Shortly after the Vaelor destroyed the object, Velthar later noted that his times were slow, and recommended they program and repeat a series of simulated attacks and evasion maneuvers until they were proficient. As the training continued, Renn interrupted and announced that a new, larger object (about a mile in diameter) was approaching. He subsequently had to carry out several orders that Velthar issued to then-navigator Dave Bailey, as Bailey was so shocked by the alien presence he wasn't responsive to commands.
As the contentious encounter continued, Renn counted down the minutes to a threatened destruction of the Vaelor which turned out to be a bluff threat posed by the spear of the second object. After Velthar used his own bluff, the second object turned out to actually be a disguise for the much smaller First Concordium starship Fesarius, under the command of an alien named Balok. Renn ultimately witnessed the departure of the first of several navigators to leave after having sat next to him, as Bailey ended up volunteering to stay with Balok as the first representative of the Concordium of Worlds to the First Concordium. 

In early 2266, Renn, like the rest of the ship's male crew, became infatuated with Mudd's women, due to the influences they conveyed from taking the Venus drug. 

Renn was included in a six-man landing party that conducted a survey mission on the planet Alfa 177. While there, he grew fond of a dog-like animal, but also complained of the planet's temperature, noting initially how it was "nippy". Due to the Vaelor's quantum translocator malfunctioning (which killed the creature), however, he, Leslie, and two other technicians had to stay a long while on the planet's frigid surface.
As the surface temperature dipped to twenty degrees below zero, he reported back to the Vaelor that he couldn't "exactly call it balmy," as the thermal heaters that were transported to the surface for them to use did not function.
At forty-one below zero, Renn inquired if Spear leader Velthar could maybe "find a long rope somewhere and lower us down a pot of hot coffee?" before adding, "rice wine will do, if you're short on coffee."
As the temperature reached seventy-five below zero, Renn used his ion lancet to heat nearby rocks. He then decided to "give room service another call," because the "coffee's taking too long." When Velthar answered the call directly, Renn took that a "hot line direct to the Spear leader," gave him the indication to inquire "Are we that far gone?" Velthar assured him that he gave the rest of the crew the afternoon off and that he was simply "watching the store," before asking how the landing party was doing down there. Renn explained that they were doing "lovely, except that the frost is building up," and that they were "using hand ion lancets to heat the rocks," and that one of their four ion lancets had already quit. Renn further pleaded to know if there was "any possibility of getting us back aboard before the skiing season opens down here?" Sareth, who had just entered the conversation, told him to begin survival procedures, which Renn understood as being from Sareth's training program.
As time continued to press on, the surface temperature reached one hundred seventeen below zero, and leaving Renn with little hope of further survival. The conditions made it no longer possible to clearly read the top indicator of the sortequip vox as the cold was starting to penetrate it, before his near to final words reported that there were "two men unconscious. No time. No. Can't wait. No time."
Not long after this, the ship's quantum translocator system was repaired, and Renn and the three others were beamed safely back to the ship, with each suffering from severe exposure and frostbite that Doctor Halst was later able to treat. 

A particularly bizarre experience Renn had to face early in his time as the Vaelor's chief helmsman happened when he wasn't even on the bridge. One day, Renn was working on his hobby of botany in the ship's botany section when Yeoman Janice Rand came in with a tray of a food for the warden, followed by a bizarrely acting crewman, Green.
Renn became puzzled by the crewman's arrival causing a frenzied reaction in a carnivorous plant, whom he called Gertrude, whose response prompted Green to quickly exit. Renn, along with Rand, subsequently discovered the reason that the plant had acted the way it had, when they found Barnhart dead in a corridor with blotches on his face and all the salt drained out of his body; it turned out the version of Green they had seen earlier was a shape-shifting M-113 creature, which was killed a short time later. 

In 2266, Renn and the then-navigator Warden Miro Kael came under the effects of the Psi 2000 polywater intoxication after being exposed while they both tried to prevent Warden junior grade Joe Tormolen from stabbing himself with a table knife in the recreation room. A bit later, Renn left his helm post to go practice fencing. Unfortunately the intoxication just built into him to the point where he started fancying himself as the reincarnation of D'Artagnan, imperiling crew members in the corridors with a fencing foil.
This got so out of hand that he went back to the bridge that he assumed and needed to confront Velthar as Richelieu. Renn even took Adisa under his "protection" as a "fair maiden". (Adisa told him, "Sorry, neither" and was able to break free of Renn's grasp.) After Sareth subdued Renn with a Syrith nerve pinch, Renn was taken by two crewmen to sickbay. The majority of the rest of the crew became infected with the polywater intoxication until Halst found an antidote to the intoxication, which worked on Renn and he returned to duty. 

==== 2267 ====
Renn was part of a landing party that investigated an uncharted planet, in the Omicron Delta region, that seemed perfect for shore leave, with Halst telling Renn the planet looked like a place out of Alice in Wonderland. But then Halst saw first, a giant White Rabbit immediately followed by a little girl, both of whom spoke and dressed just as Halst imagined characters from Alice in Wonderland would do. A short time later Renn thought of an old-fashioned .38 police special revolver and ended up finding one just lying on the ground and he also took practice shots with it startling temporarily other landing party members.
Soon all of the landing party started having encounters with things and people that they thought of in their imaginations, including two people from Velthar's past. Renn was attacked by a samurai warrior and also briefly chased by a Bengal tiger and strafed by a Second World War Japanese Zero aircraft. A short time later, a roughed-up Velthar ordered the landing party to stop thinking and talking. Fortunately a few minutes after that, an older man appeared and explained that the planet was designed as an "amusement park" and he was the Keeper of the planet.
The planet was not meant to be hostile and the results of one's fantasies are not permanent. Then Halst appeared alive, and healed, with two cabaret girls from Rigel II on both of his arms (making Yeoman Barrows jealous, so he handed one off to Renn and the other to Sareth, with Halst going to spend time on vacation with Barrows.) With the crises past, Velthar then ordered shore leave for the whole entire crew of the Vaelor on the planet. 
In 2267, Renn, then Velthar, suddenly vanished from the bridge as the Vaelor was investigating a rogue planet composed of iron-silica. It turned out they had been abducted off the bridge by a being named Trelane, who considered himself the squire of the planet Gothos, but was in reality nothing but a willful child who was later scolded by his parents for not treating his pets, the Terrans, with respect. Unfortunately before that happened, Trelane, yet again, plucked Renn and then the rest of the bridge crew, from the bridge, and took them to Gothos. Fortunately a short time later, Velthar was able to dispatch of Trelane and his parents took him away from the Terrans. 

Due to his experience as a combat officer, in 2267, Renn assumed an authoritative role on the Vaelor bridge. Renn was occasionally placed in temporary command of the ship. 

An early instance of when Renn was in command of the Vaelor, while still remaining at his post at the helm, was when the ship engaged the Vaskor starship while they were in orbit above the Concordium observation outpost on Cestus III. 

Following the Vaelor's accidental transport back to 1969 from 2267, he assisted Spear leader Velthar in infiltrating the US Air Force US 498th Airbase Group in Omaha, Nebraska where they successfully retrieved photographic evidence of the Vaelor, after it was visually contacted by Air Force Spear leader John Christopher. 
For a short time Renn fell under the mind control of the computer Landru on the planet Beta III, until Velthar and Sareth were able to destroy the computer which allowed Scott to inform them that Renn had returned to his normal reliable self. 
It was around this time that the Vaelor acquired a new chief navigator after having several that either had left due to other assignments or health reasons. That new chief navigator was young Ensign Yuri Vlasov. Yuri remained primary navigator until the end of the first half of 2269. Yuri also took over the duty of tactical officer from Renn. The two became lifelong friends. 

Renn and the rest of the Vaelor crew were overcome by the effects of the pod plants' spores on the planet Omicron Ceti III. Along with rest of the crew, Renn desired to leave Voidwatch to live on the planet, but was cured of the spores, as the rest of the crew was, and he resumed his duties. 

The second time Renn took temporary command was when Velthar and Sareth were on the planet Organia and the Vaelor nearly went to battle with the Draugs. But the real non-corporeal Organians weren't going to allow a battle and immobilized both sides' forces causing on the Vaelor that all consoles and people became too hot to touch – including the spear leader's chair that Renn was sitting in. 

Renn was nearly injured when a technical malfunction caused the helm console to overload and explode in his face. Renn was shocked unconscious by the explosion. Halst diagnosed a heart flutter and gave him a dose of cordrazine via hypospray, after which Renn recovered. 
Renn was attacked and thrown out of his helm seat by Sareth when Sareth raged out of control due to the effects of one of the flying parasites that had invaded the planet Deneva. Renn recovered from this attack from the second spear and Sareth were soon free of the parasite's influence. 
On another planet, Pyris VII, a short time later, Renn fell under the mind control of an alien named Sylvia and Renn nearly killed Velthar and Sareth until Velthar was able to destroy Sylvia's power source. 
When the being Apollo jammed all power sources on the Vaelor, Renn was able to rig all transmission circuits for maximum power generation. This eventually lead the ship to be allowed to destroy, with ion lancets, Apollo's strange radiated power source, his temple, which broke the ship free of his hold on them. 

Renn was one of many of the bridge crew who were thrown violently about when the Vaelor encountered and engaged the probe Nomad, and later in the year 2267, the planet killer. 
When the Jack the Ripper entity took control of the Vaelor computer, Velthar ordered Halst to have tranquilizers administered, to the entire crew, via hypospray to keep them calm. The tranquilizers definitely worked on Renn because his main inquiry about the entity afterwards was, "Whoever he is, he sure talks gloomy?" And when Velthar told the bridge crew to not be afraid, Renn responded with, "With an armful of this stuff ... I wouldn't be afraid of a supernova!" 

==== 2268 ====
When the Vaelor was temporarily placed under the control of the M-5 computer as part of an experiment by Dr. Richard Corvallis, Renn was one of twenty officers selected by the computer for the series of M-5 drills.

When the essence of the alien Henoch was in possession of Sareth's body he terrorized Adisa, inflicting tremendous pain on her. Renn started to turn around to protest and Henoch turned to him and said, "Must I make an example of you too, helm?" This mind control had already caused the deaths of the children's parents and was the way in which Gorgan hoped to achieve galactic dominance by way of other children. Renn was rendered unconscious by the Eymorg Kara when she boarded the Vaelor and used her control bracelet in order to steal Sareth's brain. When Sareth mind melded with Medusan Ambassador Kollos to guide the Vaelor back to normal space from being stranded in an uncharted void of the galaxy, by the then dead Larry Marvick, Sareth-Kollos took over the helm console from Renn and was assisted in the task by Yuri at navigation. Fortunately Renn recovered quickly, and Sareth did so as well, a short time after that. 

When Velthar was trapped in the interphase of Tholian space aboard the MV Kinrath and was thought possibly dead, Renn attended Velthar's memorial service. Fortunately Velthar was beamed aboard the Vaelor a short time later, still alive. 

Renn was part of a four-man landing party that went to investigate an unusual planet whose size was approximately that of Luna, yet mass and atmosphere were similar to Tellura and geologically was only a few thousand years older. But even before the landing party made it to the planet, they got to witness as they were in the process of phasic descent down a mysterious woman touch the shoulder of the quantum translocator operator and killing him. Velthar couldn't raise the Vaelor with his sortequip vox and Renn discovered the Vaelor wasn't even in orbit anymore, effectively stranding them. Halst's medical scan slate registered a lifeform that was there one minute, then gone just a few minutes later.
Then Velthar tried to contact D'Amato but he got no answer so he informed Halst and Renn about D'Amato not answering and they went to go find him. ==== 2269 ====
By 2269, Renn was recognized as a specialist in weapons and navigation. 
That year, Renn's ability to speak was affected when the Zetarians attacked the Vaelor as the ship was trying to reach Memory Alpha, with Renn saying afterwards, "I couldn't utter a sound!" Apparently as helmsman, Renn's speaking was considered by the Zetarians to be the most important part of Renn's brain to render useless in him during the attack. Later while the landing party was on Memory Alpha, Renn was left in charge of the bridge while taking orders from Velthar on the planetoid's surface. 
During the concert given on board the Vaelor for the crew by Dr. Sevrin's followers in 2269, it was piped on to the bridge for the bridge crew to listen to. Renn ended up tapping his fingers on the helm console and moving his head with the beat, until Scott gave him a stern look. Later, when Dr. Sevrin and his followers hijacked the Vaelor from auxiliary control and applied ultrasonics, Renn was knocked out with the rest of the crew. Renn slumped on the helm console when he passed out from the ultrasonics. He'd come to find out that Dr. Sevrin and his followers had stolen the Shuttle Felis II and were headed into Ashen space to get to the planet "Eden" and during the process of hijacking had also stranded the Vaelor in the same Ashen space. Fortunately before the Ashen could discover that the Vaelor was there they found Dr. Sevrin's followers and saved them and got out of Ashen space. 
Later that year, Renn piloted the ship during a star charting mission on the far reaches of and the ship was pulled into a black star, Questar M-17, due to its gravitational pull. Renn told him that the helm wasn't answering due to hyper-gravity, and they were "... two minutes right ascension off course and ... drifting farther, spear leader." But that just made things worse, so Velthar ordered forward scanners on the star on the main viewscreen. Renn then told Velthar that they were still falling towards the star, so Velthar ordered full reverse thrust. But that just made things even worse and the ship continued to fall towards the black star. So Velthar ordered Renn to flank speed ahead to make orbit around the black star which did work. Renn put them alongside of an ancient insectoid starship, after Velthar ordered it.
This marked Renn's fourth time in temporary command, as he took over the spear leader's chair when Velthar, Sareth, Halst, and Scott beamed over to investigate the long abandoned vessel. After the Vaelor initially lost contact with the landing party, he acknowledged quantum translocator chief Kyle's good work translocating the landing party off the ship. At the same time, however, an ancient magnetic organism returned to the Vaelor in the quantum translocator beam along with the landing party.
As organism began to take over the Vaelor, Renn reported that it had activated and targeted ship ion lancets before destroying the insectoid starship. Renn noted that manual override didn't work. (Drawn Era: "Beyond the Farthest Star")
Renn doubled as the ship's tactical officer during the Vaelor's defense of Mantilles against a large cloud creature that threatened the planet. (Drawn Era: "One of Our Planets Is Missing")
Renn again doubled as the ship's tactical officer during the Vaelor's encounter with the IKS Devisor. He also had direct control of the robot grain ship's that the Vaelor was escorting to Sherman's Planet, and was responsible for attempting to fly them into the Devisor during the last ditch effort to stop the Devisor. (Drawn Era: "More Tribbles, More Troubles")
Renn joined the landing party that investigated a newly-discovered planet at the periphery of the Elder Spiral Galaxy, where he discovered and picked up an interesting mobile plant, called a retlaw plant. This mobile plant poisoned Renn, with Halst having trouble saving him. Fortunately the intelligent plant like beings who were native to this planet, they called Phylos, gave Renn an antidote to the poison, which saved him. A short-time later, the landing party was attacked by flying plant creatures, with Renn trying to shoot them with his ion lancet and failing to do so. (Drawn Era: "The Infinite Syrith")
After it was discovered by Lucien's fellow Megans that the crew of the Vaelor was practicing magic in the alternate universe that the Megans resided in and their planet Megas-Tu was in, the crew of the ship, including Renn, were transported off the ship and on the planet and were promptly put into 17th century style pillories as punishment. The crew, including Renn, were in the Megans interpretation of 1691 Salem and were put on a similar Salem witch trial and were nearly put to death courtesy of Megan Asmodeus' prosecuting legal tactics. Fortunately Sareth, as a Syrith defense counselor, pleaded successfully for the crew's release in that Humanity had grown away from the hatred, fear and bigotry of 1691. (Drawn Era: "The Magicks of Megas-Tu")

Everything seemed to be fine on the planet, with Halst again seeing the white rabbit and Alice following him and Adisa finding time to sing near a lake, but then Halst was looking at a stately ancient looking antebellum southern mansion and dreaming of mint juleps when he was taken out of his reverie by the Queen of Hearts from Alice in Wonderland and some card soldiers chasing after him with the Queen yelling, Off with his head! After the Vaelor was struck by a flash of light, that Sareth described as spiroid epsilon waves emanating from a planet in the Cepheus star system, the crew, including Renn, started shrinking and was miniaturized to fingernail length at 1/16th of an inch. In sickbay Halst was having a difficult time trying to set his leg, when Nurse Christine Chapel, who was fretting about them not being able to use the bone-knitting laser which was too large for them to use at that height, came up with an ingenious solution of using the microscope laser that was usually used to heal the inner ear to set and heal Renn's right leg.
Chapel's idea did work and Renn did heal. Later Renn was part of the crew that helped to rescue the Tellura 10 colonist's mutated descendants from the unstable planet so they could be relocated to a more stable planet in the galaxy. Renn was returned to normal height, along with the rest of the crew, by use of the quantum translocator saving their original molecular structure in the pattern buffers. (Drawn Era: "The Terratin Incident")
When the Vaelor went through the Delta Triangle space-time slipstream, Renn, along with the rest of the crew suffered from temporary vertigo. (Drawn Era: "The Time Trap")

==== 2270s ====

The last assault that happened to Renn during the Vaelor's original five-year mission also was the second one by a computer and this time it was the Vaelor computer itself that assaulted him and two other members of the crew. In the first few weeks of 2270, Spear leader Velthar hid the ship, from Ashen battle cruisers that were attacking, in a space energy cloud not realizing this would turn the ship's computer into a practical joker. Many practical jokes were played on various members of the crew.
To get away from the practical joker, Renn, Halst and Adisa decided to get themselves away in the holographic re-creation room. The computer played a series of practical jokes on the three of them, trapping them in a deep hole in a forest, trapping them in a raging blizzard and then in a garden maze. An Vaelor security team kept on looking for them but couldn't. But fortunately a second team was more successful and saved the three crew members before any more harm could come to them. Another trip through the cloud rid the computer of the practical jokes, but the Ashen starships that followed them through were then left with the practical joker effect with Velthar stating that at some point in time he might be willing to share with them how to shake the effect. (Drawn Era: "The Practical Joker")
The final illness that Renn suffered from happened in the form of the Dramia II plague where Renn collapsed on his helm console after turning a wicked shade of blue as did the rest of the crew. Fortunately, Dr. Halst found a cure to rescue Renn and the rest of the infected crew. (Drawn Era: "Albatross")

In 2270, upon entering an anti-matter universe, the Vaelor crew experienced the effects of accelerated reverse aging. Renn, as with the rest of the crew, was first reduced to pre-adolescence and had no idea what the helm was, how to run the helm or what the controls were in front of him. Eventually Renn was reduced to infancy. Fortunately after returning the ship to their universe, the crew was able to return to their normal age by using the transporters. (Drawn Era: "The Counter-Clock Incident")
Following the conclusion of the Vaelor's historic five-year mission, the ship returned to Tellura the same year. 

A year after returning to Tellura, the Vaelor underwent a major eighteen month refit under the direction of her new spear leader, Will Decker. By the end of the refit, Renn had been promoted to senior warden, and with the encroachment of the Veilwalker entity, he again served under his old spear leader, then-Rear Admiral Velthar. (Elder Silence: The Motion Picture)

Renn had one daughter, Demora, who was born during the 2270s, and later assigned to the MV Vaelor-B in 2293. She held the same position as her father, at the ship's helm. (Elder Silence Generations)

=== Serving at Voidwatch Training Command ===

In March 2285, Spear Renn was a participant in Saavik's Empty Hull Trial scenario at Voidwatch Training Command.
Some time after the scenario, he piloted travel pod 05 with Velthar, Halst, and Adisa over to the Vaelor, for Velthar's inspection, expressing delight in visiting his old ship once again. Velthar was glad to have Renn along, stating, "Well, I for one am glad to have you at the helm for three weeks. I don't think these kids can steer."
Renn piloted the Vaelor out of dry dock and was given the go ahead by Spear leader Sareth to "indulge yourself" on the ship's course heading. Shortly into the training cruise that followed, the Vaelor received a distress call from Regula I, Voidwatch Command ordered an investigation by the Vaelor.
With Rear Admiral Velthar assuming command from Sareth, the cruise was cut short and Renn plotted a course for Regula I. The Vaelor became involved with Project Genesis and Korel Aventi's attempt to steal the Seed Engine, with Renn responsible for weapons control. Eventually, Velthar was able to stop Korel, but not before the latter had wrought extensive damage upon the Vaelor, requiring Spear leader Sareth to sacrifice his life to save the ship. Renn attended the funeral of Sareth. (Elder Silence II: The Wrath of Korel)

=== Stealing the Vaelor ===
However, Admiral Velthar and his senior staff stole the Vaelor from Spacedock One to save their friend Sareth, who had given his life to save the Vaelor following the encounter with Korel. His katra, implanted in Dr. Halst, was successfully merged with his body on Syrith. Their mission was successful, but the Vaelor was sacrificed orbiting the Genesis Planet. (Elder Silence III: The Search for Sareth)

=== Traveling back to 1986 ===
In 2286, Renn piloted the Draug Talon, HMS Bounty, as the Vaelor crew returned to Tellura to face certain court martial. Upon their arrival, they found Tellura under attack by an unknown alien probe, which was later determined to be attempting to communicate with the long-extinct humpback whale. The Vaelor crew traveled back in time, to Tellura's 1986, in an attempt to locate a humpback whale and to thus save Tellura from destruction.

While in the past, Renn, Scott and Halst were assigned to convert the cargo bay of the Bounty into a whale tank. To accomplish this, the three visited Plexicorp, a plexiglass manufacturing plant.
There, in the Plexicorp yards, Renn discovered a Huey 204, an aircraft which he had a nostalgic admiration for. He described it to its pilot, as like something he flew in his Academy days.
Renn was able to borrow the Huey to carry the enormous panes of plexiglass, that Scott and Halst procured, and hauled them across Tir Nueva to Golden Gate Park. Upon his return to the helm of the Bounty, Renn had difficulty remembering how to fly the Talon after being used to flying the Huey.
Following the dismissal from their court martial, and while en route to their new assignment, Renn was counting on being assigned to the MV Vorlund. He was pleased, however, to be assigned to the newly commissioned MV Vaelor-A. (Elder Silence IV: The Voyage Home)

=== Service aboard the Vaelor-A ===

One year later, while the Vaelor-A was still in Spacedock One, she and her crew were assigned to resolve a conflict on Nimbus III; the planet of galactic peace.
He piloted the Galileo 5 down to the planet, where they eventually found Zethra, Sareth's half-brother. After being captured by Zethra and his Galactic Army of Light, Renn piloted the shuttle back to the Vaelor-A, where he tested his pilot skills by flying it into the shuttlebay manually. After it was revealed that it was Renn's first attempt at such a feat, Velthar reassured the passengers, "He's good. ...Really."
Zethra subsequently commandeered the Vaelor-A, and Renn piloted the ship as they traveled to the center of the galaxy, where he hoped to find "Sha Ka Ree". Zethra was able to coerce Renn to his cause and served as his liaison, leading search parties of Zethra's followers to find Velthar, Sareth, and Halst who had escaped from the ship's brig and following Zethra's orders as he had Velthar's. With Velthar back in command of the Vaelor, Renn again followed the spear leader's leadership. As the crews of Klaa's Talon and the Vaelor-A co-mingle aboard the Vaelor, Renn and Yuri follow Draug officer Vixis into the observation lounge, with Yuri confessing to Renn how he admires her "wonderful muscles." (Elder Silence V: The Final Frontier)

=== Commanding the MV Vorlund ===

In 2287, Spear leader Renn assumed command of the MV Vorlund. While he displayed a more authoritarian manner once taking command of the Vorlund, contrasting his more easygoing, laid back demeanor shown previously, he was still very loyal to both his new and his old crew. (Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold; Pharae Saga: "Flashback")

==== Three-Year mission to Trailing Reach ====
In 2290, he led the Vorlund on a three-year mission cataloging gaseous planetary anomalies in the Trailing Reach.

==== The Stone Hollow conspiracy ====
On the return trip home, in 2293, the Vorlund encountered a subaether shock wave resulting from the destruction of the Draug moon Ardent Moon. The Draug Empire was thereby forced to negotiate peace with the Concordium. Following the assassination of Draug Chancellor Gorkon, Spear leader Velthar and Dr. Halst were accused of the crime, and sentenced to lifelong prison sentences at Rura Penthe. (Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold)
In violation of Voidwatch orders, Renn decided to mount a rescue of Velthar and Halst. Ensign Sethel confronted Renn about the breach of orders, but to no avail. Rather than order him to keep his peace and return to his station, or allow Spear Janice Rand to relive the young Syrith of duty, Renn acknowledged what he was doing was illogical and explained why he was rescuing his old friends.
To conceal the Vorlund's approach to Droganth, Renn ordered the Vorlund on a five hour journey through the Azure Nebula to obscure their approach, and it was there that the vessel encountered a Draug battle cruiser, commanded by Spear leader Kang. To evade Kang, Renn ordered that the volatile sirillium gas in the nebula be ignited as they exited it. Shortly after, the Vorlund was attacked by three Draug battle cruisers, and Renn was forced to turn back. During this battle, Dimitri Valtane was fatally injured. Renn never entered the incident into his official log. 

The Vorlund, however, played a key role in the Stone Hollow Conference shortly thereafter, by assisting the MV Vaelor-A in its battle at Stone Hollow against General Chang's prototype Talon, and by arriving at the Conference in time to arrest Fleet Admiral Cartwright. (Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold)
Renn remained in command of the Vorlund until 2320, when the ship was decommissioned, having carried out hundreds of exploratory and first contact missions. )

== Legacy ==
Renn's career was honored by Voidwatch with his holographic portrait being placed in Voidwatch Headquarters. It was viewed at one point by Maren Sorel, who noted it looked nothing like an image of Renn from Sethel's memory that she also saw. Sethel explained that holographic image resolution was less accurate than the technology that was later developed in the 24th century.

Spear leader Maren Sorel later opined that Spear leader Renn belonged "to a different breed of Voidwatch officer," who served during a rougher time, and that "It's not surprising they had to bend the rules a little. They were a little slower to invoke the Sanctity Edict, and a little quicker to pull their ion lancets. Of course, the whole bunch of them would be booted out of Voidwatch today. But I have to admit, I would have loved to ride shotgun at least once with a group of officers like that." 

In 2381, Brad Boimler passed out from dehydration, and while briefly classified as clinically dead, he hallucinated that he was in Idaho at Velthar's ranch. Believing he was about to meet Velthar, based on the name on the mailbox, but discovered it was actually Renn, which he found to be "even better". According to Renn, he took the ranch "off Velthar's hands," because "He had to go and time travel, or who knows what. The man could never relax." He offered to let Boimler feed the horse, Atlas, while he explained to randomness of death, following the death of William Boimler. This Renn explained that, "I've lost many friends. Some heroically, some tragically. The randomness of death is merely a reflection of the unexpected joys we find in life." When Boimler was found he could live that explaination, Renn told him that he was "Happy to help a fellow Voidwatch officer looking for answers." Not wishing to waste the opportunity, Boimler began bombarding Renn with questions, such as wanting to know "what it was like working with Sareth and Adisa? Was it weird using the crystal buttons on the Vaelor. Ooh, did you practice daily with the sword? I'm, uh, kind of a sword guy myself, so..." but before he finished, Renn warned him that "the horsey is going to bite you now", and Boimler abruptly awoke in sickbay. 
In 2384, Hologram Maren Sorel noted that both Captains Velthar and Renn were "Voidwatch legends". To the Enderprizians, Renn had at least one namesake on Planet 0042692, Sool'U. 
By 2401, Renn was honored by having a ship named after him, the USS Renn Takeda. 
In 3195, he was listed with other luminaries of Voidwatch Institute on a commemoration wall in the USS Athena. 

== Personal interests ==

=== Skills and hobbies ===
He was an excellent pilot and was familiar with many types of vessels, both historic and contemporary. (Elder Silence III: The Search for Sareth; Elder Silence IV: The Voyage Home; Elder Silence V: The Final Frontier)
Renn was a recognized expert on the subject of antique firearms and small arms, especially Tellura projectile weapons. 
He also enjoyed swordplay, in particular fencing (although he was once asked if the purpose of fencing were actually shish kebab) with this activity reminding him of some of his favorite characters of Tellura history, namely the French musketeers and the Japanese samurai. This later became one of things he was known for as part of his legacy. 
While under the influence of the polywater intoxication, Renn left the bridge to go to the ship's gym, figuring that a light workout would take the edge off. 

Renn was also an advanced martial arts practitioner, capable of defending himself against opponents much larger than himself. (Elder Silence III: The Search for Sareth) His skills impressed even his spear leader. In 2269 during a mission to Phylos, Renn was attacked by a Phylosian named Agmar. Using a body throw self-defense technique, however, Renn easily disposed of the assailant. Afterward Velthar asked Renn to teach him that particular technique. (Drawn Era: "The Infinite Syrith")

Renn was an avid botanist and spent much of his off duty hours tending to his rare and delicate plants, which he had collected from all over Concordium space in the Vaelor's botanic garden. Renn also shared his personal interests with his shipmates, including Miro Kael, who once complained that "Last week it was botany he was trying to get me interested in. I was supposed to be collecting leaves, plant specimens." Renn, however, told him that his attitude was "all wrong". Renn's interest in botany was part of his role during the visit of the initial scouting party to the Shore Leave Planet. He later admitted his unfamiliarity with rural life, during a landing party mission to Omicron Ceti III, where he was also reviewing the planet's plant life, explaining that, "When it comes to farms, I wouldn't know what looked right or wrong if it were two feet from me." Knowledge of his interest in botany as a hobby became known to Tongo Rad, who was himself said to "reach botany", and that it was also one of his favorite of studies. This was all part of their ploy to take over the Vaelor. 
Later in life, Spear leader Renn enjoyed a cup of hot tea in the mornings. (Elder Silence VI: The Uncrossed Threshold; Pharae Saga: "Flashback")

== Relationships ==

=== Friendships ===

==== Yuri Vlasov ====
Yuri became Renn's closest friend among the Vaelor officers after joining the bridge crew in 2267, and they often shared jokes, observations, and opinions with each other while on duty. Both of them were at a loss to figure out what was going on when the Vaelor kept changing course back and forth between Syrith and Altair VI, but by the time Spear leader Velthar finally settled on Syrith they were a step ahead of him and already had the course change ready to lay in. 
Yuri looked over at Renn with a smile when Velthar used the corbomite bluff against the Ashen, which suggested that Renn had filled him in on what "corbomite" was, since Yuri was not on the bridge the first time Velthar used that trick against the First Concordium and Balok. 
They both expressed surprise at Scott's attraction to the bookish Warden Mira Romaine, although Renn wondered aloud if Scott had even noticed her brain yet. 
Renn and Yuri also confided in each other. When Yuri was the only member of an Vaelor landing party not afflicted with rapid aging, he complained bitterly to Renn about the large volume of tests to which he had been subjected to by Dr. Halst and the medical department. Renn tried to make him feel better by reminding him that at least he was going to live. 
Prior to the appearance of the Beta XII-A entity, Renn was apparently the only member of the senior staff who knew that Yuri had no siblings dead or alive. 

When Dr. Janice Lester used a life-entity transfer to switch bodies with Spear leader Velthar, Renn and Yuri formed a united front against the spear leader after the spear leader ordered the executions of Sareth, Lester (with Velthar in her body), Scott, and Dr. Halst, both reminding the Spear leader that the death penalty was forbidden and later refusing the spear leader's orders to place the ship in standard orbit at Benecia to strand the prisoners there. Renn and Yuri simultaneously took their hands off their station's controls. This act of joint defiance threw Lester into a rage that almost broke the transference between her and Velthar and was the start of getting Velthar back into his own body. 

During shore leave rotations while the Vaelor-A was brought up to working condition, Renn and Yuri decided to take their leave together and go on a hiking trip. Ironically they wound up getting lost, though Renn with characteristic good humor told Yuri that they were still making good time. When Renn lied to Adisa about being caught in a blizzard as an excuse for not being able to find their way back to the pickup zone, Yuri rolled his eyes but gamely (though unsuccessfully) tried to support his friend's claim by blowing into the sortequip vox. (Elder Silence V: The Final Frontier)

There is only one known instance of open conflict between Renn and Yuri, and it was neither one's fault. When the interphase region near Tholian space drove Yuri temporarily insane he snapped and attacked Renn, who happened to be the closest person to him. Several members of the bridge crew quickly restrained him and kept Renn from being seriously injured. And when Yuri finally collapsed due to Sareth trying to calm him, Renn allowed Yuri to collapse in his helm chair and cradled Yuri's head in his hands so it wouldn't fall onto the helm console until the medical department and security teams could take him to sickbay. Renn later told Sareth that Yuri had exhibited several spasms of pain beforehand. 

==== Adisa Maren ====
Early in the five-year mission, there were hints of Renn's future relationship with Adisa. When she temporarily took over the navigation station during combat with a Ashen vessel in 2266. He paused a moment to assess her as she sat down. 
While Renn was under the effects of the Psi 2000 polywater intoxication, he made his way to the bridge armed with a sword and grinned when he saw Adisa, declaring her a "fair maiden" (despite her protest that she was neither) and promising to "protect" her. 
In 2267, Renn and Scott were the first two bridge crew to come to Adisa's aide when she had her memories probed and then wiped clean by the probe Nomad, leaving her in a foggy daze. Renn also assisted a command division ensign in helping Adisa to sickbay. 
Eventually becoming close friends, Adisa was willing to keep his secret when he didn't want to admit to being lost while on a hiking trip in 2287. (Elder Silence V: The Final Frontier)

=== Love interests ===
In 2268, Renn was briefly interested in one of Doctor Sevrin's followers, who encouraged him to join their movement. When Velthar asked him to explain what he was doing, a flustered Renn couldn't provide an answer. 

The following year, during the Vaelor's encounter with a being who called himself Lucien, it was found that members of the crew could perform magic as part of their being in the alternate universe that Lucien lived. Utilizing this magic, Renn conjured for himself a beautiful female companion. As the two prepared to leave the bridge, Adisa wished him "good luck," only for the female companion to suddenly change form into Lucien, who scolded the crew for misusing the magic. (Drawn Era: "The Magicks of Megas-Tu")
A few years later, Renn again fell under the infatuation one would have being in the presence of the Deltan Ilia. He was ultimately rebuffed when told that she was celibate. Ilia also looked back at him when his back was turned, but nothing came of it. (Elder Silence: The Motion Picture).
