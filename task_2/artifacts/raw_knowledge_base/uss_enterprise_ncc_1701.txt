The USS Enterprise (NCC-1701) was a 23rd century Federation Constitution-class starship operated by Starfleet, and the first Federation starship to bear the name Enterprise. During its career, the Enterprise served as the Federation flagship and was in service from 2245 to 2285. During the latter years of its life, the Enterprise was converted into a Constitution II-class starship to continue its missions of exploration, then assigned to Starfleet Academy as a training vessel until its destruction in 2285.

== Service history ==

=== Construction and launch ===

In the early- to mid-23rd century, at least twelve heavy cruiser-type starships, the Constitution-class, were commissioned by the Federation Starfleet.  Constructed at the San Francisco Fleet Yards in San Francisco, California, the Federation vessel registered NCC-1701 was christened "the Enterprise", the latest in a long line of ships of the same name. 
Captain Robert April oversaw construction of the ship's components as well as its initial trial runs. His wife, Sarah April, designed several tools for the ship's sickbay. (TAS: "The Counter-Clock Incident") Larry Marvick was one of the designers of the Enterprise itself, while Doctor Richard Daystrom designed its computer systems. 

A piece of the Enterprise NX-01 was incorporated into the newer craft, as it was the previous ship to bear the name. 

=== Robert April's command ===

The Enterprise was launched on April 11, 2245, under the command of Captain April.    
Sarah April served as the ship's first chief medical officer, with Christopher Pike serving as Captain April's first officer. (TAS: "The Counter-Clock Incident"; DIS: "Brother"; SNW: "Strange New Worlds")  
Sarah had to frequently come up with new ideas for medical tools. (TAS: "The Counter-Clock Incident")

In 2246, April chose to break General Order 1 and warn a pre-warp civilization, the Perricans, about a possible apocalyptic meteor shower that was due to hit their planet. 
In 2248, April sent his science officer to the industrial-age planet Na'rel to solve the imminent threat of an extinction-level drought by sharing Federation technology. 
Una Chin-Riley was assigned to the Enterprise as April's science officer.  
One deep-space cruise took the Enterprise within half a light-year of 99 Pegasi. (ST: "Q&A")
April also visited Man-us II without his security officer, during which he chose to reveal the Enterprise to the Ohawk, a pre-warp civilization. 

=== Christopher Pike's command ===
In 2250, April left the Enterprise and command was turned over to Pike. Pike also chose Chin-Riley to be his first officer. 

==== Early voyages ====

At one point, the Enterprise visited Pragine 63, where science officer Lynne Lucero was transferred to the USS Cabot to serve as its new captain. (ST: "The Trouble with Edward")
In 2253, Pike and the Enterprise traveled to Starbase 40 to receive the ship's new half Human-Vulcan science officer, Spock, who went on to serve under Pike's command for over eleven years. 

In 2254, on stardate 2496.4, the Enterprise visited Rigel VII as a routine exploration of a remote class M planet. Pike, along with a landing party consisting of Spock, Yeoman Zac Nguyen, Ensign C. Plummer, M. Aberth, and several others, went down to the planet's surface, where they were attacked by the Kalar. Nguyen, Plummer, and Aberth were presumed killed, while Spock and six others were injured. The mission lasted all of four hours. The remaining members of the landing party returned to the Enterprise and set course to the Vega colony to get medical aid for those injured. Unbeknownst to the crew, Nguyen had actually survived and had been left stranded on the planet. 

While traveling to Vega, the Enterprise learned there were survivors of the SS Columbia that were marooned on Talos IV nearly eighteen years earlier. Following the stop at Vega, the Enterprise traveled to Talos IV to rescue the Columbia survivors, only to find out it was a trap devised by the Talosians. 

==== Federation-Klingon War ====
In late 2256, Michael Burnham encouraged Cadet Sylvia Tilly to improve her physical conditioning so she would stand out amongst her peers and be assigned to the Enterprise or one of its "sister ships". 
At that time, the Enterprise was on a five-year mission under Pike. With the Federation-Klingon War underway, Starfleet elected not to recall the starship and leave the vessel as an instrument of last resort, so ordered Pike and his crew to remain out of the fighting. 
Pike later accused Starfleet Command of not recalling Enterprise because they knew he would steadfastly remind them of Federation values, but Admiral Katrina Cornwell, the only Starfleet Command representative present to hear the accusation, countered that they "wanted the best of Starfleet to survive" if the war was lost and they had deemed that to be Enterprise. 

==== The red bursts ====

Following the conclusion of the  Federation-Klingon War in 2257, the Enterprise was dispatched by Starfleet to investigate one of a series of unidentified signals detected throughout the galaxy.  En route, the ship suffered multiple catastrophic systems failures, leading Pike to issue a priority 1 distress call.
The call was received by the USS Discovery, en route to Vulcan.  Pike and two senior officers transferred to Discovery to continue their mission, while a team was dispatched to tow the Enterprise back to Spacedock for a diagnostic. The damage was assessed as severe, with no estimate for repair. 

A week later, Chin-Riley reported to Pike that the holographic comm system was the source of the failures, which had compromised primary systems. Pike ordered her to tell Chief Engineer Louvier to strip the system from the Enterprise in favor of viewscreen communication. 

==== Battling Control ====

After the Discovery acquired data from a Sphere that would ultimately lead to the evolution of Section 31 artificial intelligence Control which would threaten all sentient life in the Milky Way Galaxy, Pike was left with no other option but to set the Discovery's auto-destruct system and call the Enterprise for immediate evacuation of the ship's crew. 
On stardate 1051.8, the Enterprise finally rendezvoused with the Discovery and began taking on the ship's crew. When all personnel were fully evacuated, Pike ordered Discovery's destruction. However, the Sphere intelligence utilized Discovery's defensive systems to block the Enterprise's torpedo volley. Unable to eliminate Discovery, the crew determined that sending the Discovery into the future was the only method to secure the Sphere data from Control.

With the appearance of a fifth red burst, both ships traveled to the planet Xahea, which was ultimately the position Cornwell and Pike decided to fight Control's armada. After the decision was made to destroy Discovery to prevent its capture by Control,  Discovery deployed evacuation corridors to transfer its crew to Enterprise. 
During the decisive battle with Control, the Enterprise lost a significant portion of her saucer section when an undetonated photon torpedo penetrated it and later detonated. Cornwell was killed while sacrificing herself to stop the torpedo from destroying the entire ship. As Discovery traveled into the future, the Enterprise and a fleet of Klingon and Kelpien reinforcements provided cover fire against the attacking Section 31 drone ships and then finished them off when Philippa Georgiou neutralizing Control left the enemy ships dead in the water. Following the battle, the Enterprise returned to Earth for repairs and debriefing. 

==== Continuing mission of exploration ====

After roughly four months of repairs, the Enterprise resumed its mission of exploration, once again under the command of Pike. Their first stop was a shakedown run to a newly discovered moon in orbit of Edrin II. 

In 2259, the Enterprise was back at Earth undergoing scheduled maintenance and system upgrades, during which time the crew was given downtime. It was pulled out of drydock early, forced to skip the redundant system checks, and cutting crew downtime off early by April, now a fleet admiral. Pike and the Enterprise were assigned to follow up on Chin-Riley and the USS Archer after both went missing during a first contact mission to Kiley 279. After arriving at the planet, in an effort to force the Kiley not to destroy themselves, Pike violated General Order 1 and ordered the Enterprise into the low atmosphere, showing it off to the Kiley as a proof of his claims.

After the mission to Kiley 279, the Enterprise returned to Starbase 1, where the command crew was reprimanded by April but let off on a technicality. On stardate 2259.42, the Enterprise left Starbase 1 to officially resume its third five-year mission of exploration. 

One of the Enterprise's first stops on its mission was surveying the Persephone system. During this mission, the crew managed to save a planet in the system from a comet impact. This came at the cost of starting a quarrel with a species known as the "Shepherds", though through subterfuge, relations were able to be patched up. 
The Enterprise visited an abandoned Illyrian colony at Hetemit IX to find out what had happened to the colonists. However, an Illyrian light-based virus broke out aboard the ship, forcing the crew to shut the Enterprise down. After becoming infected, chief engineer Hemmer nearly destroyed the ship after disengaging the ship's warp core containment field. 

The Enterprise was tasked with delivering an AP 350 atmospheric processor to the colony world Finibus III. Upon arrival in the system, the Enterprise discovered a ravaged colony, with no indications of planetside survivors. The Enterprise did, however, establish contact with an ore cargo vessel carrying a small number of survivors. Due to the cargo vessel's heavy radiation shielding, the Enterprise docked with the cargo ship by using its transport tubes. While docked, the vessels were ambushed by Gorn hunter ships. In an attempt to escape, Pike ordered the ship to hide in the gas clouds surrounding the system's brown dwarf. After managing to destroy the first Gorn ship, more Gorn ships appeared, and Pike put forward another unconventional idea: slingshot the Enterpise around the neighboring black hole and leave behind something that would explode in their wake, making seem as if they had been destroyed by being sucked into the black hole. The maneuver proved successful, and Lieutenant Erica Ortegas dubbed it the "Pike Maneuver". 
After the fight with the Gorn, the Enterprise returned to Starbase 1 for repairs. While the crew enjoyed some shore leave, Pike engaged in negotiations with the R'ongovian Protectorate. 
The Enterprise was assigned a routine cartographic survey of Majalan system. While there, the Enterpise encountered a shuttlecraft under attack by presumed pirates and assisted, inadvertently involving Pike in the succession of the First Servant of the planet Majalis. 

The Enterprise hosted the scientist Dr. Aspen and ventured past the edge of Federation space to assist a colony convoy which had fallen on difficult times, dropping subspace buoys along the way. Arriving at their destination, the Enterprise found the wreckage of the colony ships and was temporarily caught in an energy trap while investigating. After some time searching, the Enterprise found the long surviving ship, which had been commandeered by pirates. While an away team visited the ship, Aspen--in reality the pirate captain Angel--led another set of pirates to seize control of the Enterprise. With the assistance of T'Pring's cruiser, the crew of the Enterprise was able to regain control of their ship. 

The Enterprise was tasked with surveying the Jonisian Nebula. While there, the ship encountered a sentient non-corporeal lifeform speculated to be a Boltzmann brain, later named Debra by Rukiya. 

The Enterprise was sent on a priority one mission to deliver vidium power cells to Deep Space Station K-7. En route, the Enterprise was contacted by Starfleet Command with a new priority one mission: to travel to Valeo Beta V to follow up on a distress signal sent by the USS Peregrine, rescue the crew and, if at all possible, retrieve the ship. The Enterprise deployed a landing party, including Pike, to investigate the crashed Peregrine. Chin-Riley was left in command of the Enterprise and tasked with finishing the mission to K-7. Upon the Enterprise's return, it was able to tractor the Peregrine off the planet. 
The Enterprise and the USS Cayuga were assigned to help with resupplying and retrofitting the Earth Outpost Stations along the edge of the Neutral Zone. While there, Pike witnessed an alternate timeline version of the Neutral Zone Incursion and became became aware of James T. Kirk, recognizing he had the potential to be a good captain for the Enterprise in the future. Chin-Riley was arrested shortly thereafter after Starfleet had discovered she was an Illyrian. 

The Enterprise again returned to Starbase 1, where Chief Fleet Inspector Commander Pelia and her team from Operational Support Services ran comprehensive inspections, systems checks, and upgrades on the starship. During this time, most of the crew were granted leave. Pike took a shuttle to search for a lawyer for Chin-Riley, leaving Spock in command of the Enterprise. During inspections, the Enterprise received a distress call from Lieutenant La'An Noonien-Singh on Cajitar IV, a planet on the edge of Klingon space, about an "anti-Federation threat". However, April refused to allow the ship to investigate, fearing it could reignite the Klingon War due to the Klingons currently being in control of the planet following a painstakingly-negotiated treaty. Under Spock's leadership, the crew faked a potential warp core breach in order to evacuate the inspection team, steal the Enterprise, and go to La'An's aid. Although Pelia deduced their deception, she aided the crew in stealing the ship rather than stopping them and acted as Chief Engineer. 

After arriving at the Cajitar system, the crew uncovered a false flag operation by the Broken Circle to reignite the Klingon War using the rebuilt NCC-1279. The Enterprise engaged and then destroyed the rogue ship, which foiled the Broken Circle's plot. Afterwards, Pelia expressed an interest in sticking around as a part of the crew and April let Spock off easy, concerned about a potential upcoming war with the Gorn that Starfleet would need every good officer they had for. 
The Enterprise returned to Earth for Chin-Riley's court martial. After Chin-Riley was acquitted, she was allowed to return to duty aboard the ship. 
The Enterprise and the Cayuga were assigned to a joint mission charting a newly discovered binary star system. During the survey, Starfleet Command reassigned the Enterprise to investigate and correct cultural contamination on Rigel VII, as a depiction of a Starfleet delta was discovered in a garden on that planet. After arriving in orbit, Pike led a small away team down and Chin-Riley was left in command of the Enterprise. While in orbit, the crew of the Enterprise began experiencing tinnitus, headaches, and acute memory loss. The command crew's personnel files were distributed to them as a reminder of their identities in cause the memory degradation became worse. The crew theorized some sort of radiation from the planet as the cause, so they attempted to shield the ship from it by hiding in the nearby Stecora debris field. With it providing at least some beneficial effect, the crew was able to develop a shield harmonic to fully block the radiation. Pike made the determination that the asteroid that had caused the radiation effect impeded natural development, so therefore its removal would not violate General Order 1. After shuttlecraft tractored the asteroid off the planet, the Enterprise used its tractor beams to hurl the asteroid away from the planet. 
The Enterprise was assigned to the Vulcan system to survey the Kerkhovian moon as part of ongoing archeological efforts to understand the fate of the ancient inhabitants of that world. The crew were able to activate a piece of alien technology which enabled them to make contact with the Kerkhovians, whom the crew learned had become energy beings and had moved to extradimensional space. During this mission, the Enterprise hosted T'Pring, her parents, and Amanda Grayson. 
As part of a mission to fully establish a station in Bannon's Nebula, Pike was temporarily promoted to Fleet Captain and given command over a fleet consisting of the Enterprise, the USS Farragut, the under-construction Bavali Station, and several support craft. While there, the Enterprise used the nebula's natural supply of deuterium to replenish its reserves. After determining that there were lifeforms bonded to the nebula itself, the Enterprise destroyed Bavali Station, and both the Enterprise and the Farragut vented their collected deuterium back into the nebula. 

On its way to deliver food supplies to Setlik II, the Enterprise performed  a routine survey of Krulmuth-B. While there, they discovered a time portal, which expunged a Starfleet officer from 129\0 years into the future named Brad Boimler. The Enterprise detected an incoming Orion vessel, and Boimler talked Pike out of the Enterprise firing on the Orion ship. The two ships communicated instead, and then the Orions beamed the portal to their ship and fled. Boimler made modifications to the Enterprise to enable to ship to track the Orions and pled with Pike for a diplomatic solution. Pike wound up trading the Enterprise's cargo of Triticale in exchange for the portal. The Enterprise returned to Krulmuth-B and returned the portal to its original location, but as Boimler was attempting to go home, a shipmate of his, Beckett Mariner, exited the portal, using its last reserves of horonium fuel in the process. After attempts to resolve the situation failed, the Enterprise set course for Starbase 1 to hand off responsibility of the wayward time travelers. However, en route, Boimler and Mariner determined that there was horonium on the Enterprise: a piece of the Enterprise NX-01 ceremonially included in the Enterprise during its construction. The crew of the Enterprise excavated the component and reversed course back to Krulmuth-B to send Boimler and Mariner back home. 
The Enterprise traveled to the Prospero system to rendezvous with the USS Kelcie Mae, pick up Federation ambassador Dak'Rah from that ship, and transport him to Starbase 12. Dak'Rah was treated as an honored guest, but his presence made the crew extremely uneasy. As tensions rose, Chin-Riley proposed a shortcut through the Chantico Nebula, which Pike agreed to. Before the Enterprise could arrive at Starbase 12, Dak'Rah had died in a physical altercation with Dr. Joseph M'Benga. 

On stardate 2398.3, while the Enterprise was traveling in the far edges of the Alpha Quadrant, they encountered a naturally-occurring subspace fold. During this time, James T. Kirk was temporarily transferred to the Enterprise to shadow Chin-Riley as part of his command training. While conducting experiments attempting to use the subspace fold as a communications medium,, Ensign Nyota Uhura sent a message containing music through the fold. This caused the fold to release a quantum uncertainty field that created a musical reality aboard the ship. Crewmembers started to express themselves through uncontrollable singing, which was deemed a security threat by Noonien-Singh. The crew devised a means to seal the subspace fold "like a zipper", but the effort ultimately failed and the fold began spreading further across the entire region. The crew investigated the possibility of destroying the fold, but determined that it could cause widespread catastrophic damage across the entire area of effect, which was spreading rapidly. The Enterprise detected Klingon ships also affected by the fold inbound, with intent to destroy it, so the Enterprise prepared for combat. Uhura, however, realized that a large enough musical number might produce the effects needed to shatter the fold and managed to lead the entire crew to sing in unison, shattering it successfully. 

The Enterprise responded to a distress call from the Cayuga at Parnassus Beta just outside Federation Space. Upon arriving in the Parnassus system, it found the wreckage of the Cayuga and were approached by a Gorn hunter ship.  April contacted the Enterprise, sharing a map that had been transmitted by the Gorn and showed they had annexed the system. In contradiction to orders not to enter Gorn-claimed space, Pike assembled an away team to search the planet for survivors from the Cayuga, leaving Chin-Riley in command. The remaining command crew managed to disable Gorn planetary defenses and rescue several members of the away team, but the remaining members of the away team and the survivors located were beamed away by an incoming Gorn ship. The Enterprise charged the Gorn ship, breaching its shields, and managed to tag it with a modified torpedo they could use as a tracking device. With no other options, the Enterprise then fled. 
The Enterprise rendezvoused with the USS Pablo  Picasso, which was carrying April. April ordered the Enterprise to stay out of Gorn territory and not start another war, but also suggested that if the Enterprise could find a way to fight back without causing a war, they should take it. The Enterprise then departed for Gorn space and tracked the Gorn ship that had taken their people to an unfamiliar-to-Starfleet binary system. After learning Gorn behavior responded to CME activity, the decision was made to use the Enterprise to attempt to trigger a CME X-flare by flying it between the stars and channeling radiation through the ship itself. This ultimately proved successful and, as best the crew can tell, managed to put the Gorn into a long hibernation cycle, though at the cost of significant damage to the Enterprise. 
The Enterprise spent three months at Starbase 1, undergoing repairs from the damage that it had sustained. During this time, Captain Marie Batel remained on the Enterprise, continuing to recover from her Gorn infection. In addition, Lieutenant Montgomery Scott, who was stationed on the USS Stardiver before it was destroyed, was officially assigned to the Enterprise. 

Before Batel could leave the ship for her new command, her Gorn infection returned, threatening her life. With the only chance to save her being the chimera blossom, the Enterprise underwent an off-the-books mission to Kenfori which was declared off-limits to both the Federation and the Klingons after the Klingon War. Pike and M'Benga went on the away mission to the planet alone, yet again leaving Chin-Riley in command. When the Enterprise lost contact with Pike and M'Benga, Ortegas forced Chin-Riley to authorize a dangerous maneuver where the Enterprise warped directly into the planet's thermosphere in order to beam Pike and M'Begna out. In the process, the Enterprise's illegal presence was revealed to the Klingons and the ship suffered from enormous stress. Ortegas was temporarily relieved of duty for her rash actions.  
Batel subsequently remained on the Enterprise on medical leave as she underwent treatment. 
The Enterprise was assigned a dual mission: to study a neutron star in the Kitolian Belt, and to test out a prototype holodeck. However, the combination of proximity to the neutron star interfering with ship systems, the drain on ship computing resources to run the holodeck, and the large amount of energy required to run it led to widespread issues across the ship. This included unreliable power, loss of shields and helm controls, and failure of holodeck safety protocols. These issues jeopardized the safety of the Enterprise and its crew when the neutron star released a gamma ray burst. The Enterprise report on the holodeck project recommended it be shelved due to the design flaws. 
The Enterprise traveled to Vadia IX in M'Kroon space to assist Roger Korby with an archeological project. In addition to providing personnel, the ship also channeled a beam of energy through its navigational deflector to help reveal an ancient site. After an apparent injury, Ensign Dana Gamble was beamed back to the ship, inadvertently bringing a malicious extradimensional alien onboard as well, which, after Gamble's death, was trapped in the Enterprise's transporter buffer. 
When the Farragut was heavily damaged by Helicon Gamma's destruction, the Enterprise came to its rescue, only to be captured by the planet's destroyer: a mysterious Scavenger ship that was considered a legend and a Destroyer of Worlds by many cultures. Despite no other ship having escaped the Scavengers before, the Enterprise managed to escape through a clever coordination of its crew and the Farragut, partially manned by Enterprise personnel, temporarily disabling the enemy ship. After the Scavenger Ship's destruction, the crews of the Enterprise and the Farragut were upset to learn that the Scavengers were the Humans descendants of scientists who had departed Earth in the mid-21st century on an exploratory mission. Subsequently, the Enterprise towed the Farragut back to spacedock for repairs. 
The Enterprise was ordered to non-Federation space, to transport cargo from Tychus-B to Lutani VII. This cargo primarily consisted of a Jikaru from Tychus B, which as far as the Enterprise was aware was categorized as a type of livestock, but in actuality was a heavily modified sentient lifeform intended to be used as a weapon. The Jikaru escaped when a Lutani scientist sabotaged its neural dampener. The crew of the Enterprise discovered the true nature of the creature, as well as the fact that it was locked in a state of great suffering, the Enterprise fulfilled the Jikaru's request for euthanasia by luring it into the system's star. These events later became the focus of a documentary. 
The Enterprise was given a mission to Tezaar, to assist in preventing a nuclear meltdown that would affect the entire planet. Due to the technological level of Tezaar's people, and their pre-existing knowledge of Vulcans, only Vulcans could interact with the planet, so several members of the crew used an experimental serum to temporarily transform into Vulcans. The mission was a success, though the serum had complications after the away team's return. 
The Enterprise was tasked with a time-sensitive delivery of vaccines to Epsilon Indi III. Complications arose when Lieutenant Ortegas disappeared. {SNW: "Terrarium")

After Batel was cleared to return to active duty as the new Judge Advocate General, the Enterprise was to ferry her back to Earth. However, during routine maintenance, it was discovered that the Vezda that had possessed Ensign Dana Gamble was able to escape from the pattern buffer and make its way to Skygowan, intending to release the rest of its kind trapped on Vadia IX. Kirk convinced Captain V'Rel to lend the Farragut's aid to the Enterprise which proved to be crucial when the combined firepower of the two ships was required to open the portal for Pike and Batel. Using a Vulcan mind meld, Spock and Kirk flew the two ships in perfect synchronicity and opened the portal. On the other side, Pike and Batel were able to defeat and reimprison the Vezda at the cost of Batel becoming their eternal guardian.
Before departing the ship, Doctor Roger Korby provided the crew with data that he had recovered on Skygowan revealing enough uncharted planets for another five-year mission. The Enterprise set out on its new mission, beginning with a promising M-class planet chosen by Ortegas. 
In the following six months, the Enterprise went on a streak of fifteen back-to-back missions with very little rest or downtime available to the crew. The last of these fifteen missions was to Tulaxar, where an away team was sent to covertly deliver medical supplies. 
The next mission for the Enterprise was assigned what was meant to be a uneventful and enjoyable mission - to monitor a category 10 interstellar squall, a rare spatial phenomena. While observing the squall, the Enterprise detected and responded to a distress call coming from inside the phenomenon, only to be thrown 65 million years into the past. After exiting the storm, the ship had taken severe damage. While part of the crew retrieved the crucial repair material iridium from the dinosaur-filled Earth, the ship was briefly engaged by Martian Commander Cassell, who mistook the Federation ship as an enemy. At war with the arachnid Dol'drm, Cassell learned from the ship's database that Mars would become a barren wasteland. In a desperate attempt to save her people from what she believed to be destruction from the Dol'drm, Cassell stole the Enterprise's primary antimatter fuel pod and fired it into the core of Dol'drm, destroying the planet in an act of genocide. Pike ultimately chose not to intervene, as he realized the planet Dol'drm did not exist in the future. The Enterprise managed top use the power of the explosion to return to its correct time. A fragment of the destroyed planet became the asteroid that wiped out the dinosaurs, paving the way for the evolution of the human race.
Afterwards, it was determined that the Enterprise's journey was a predestination paradox, as the ship answered its own distress call in a storm that was created after it sent the Enterprise back in time. Pike chose to turn down a promotion to Commodore in favor of staying onboard the Enterprise, but Una Chin-Riley was promoted to Commander on his recommendation. 
The Enterprise received a long-range signal from its shuttle, the Alvarez, which was scheduled to be returning with Spock and La'An. Upon rendezvousing with the location described in the signal, the hulk of the USS Griffin was found, and Spock and La'An, as well as tagalong James Kirk, were rescued from the ship. 
The Enterprise was assigned to host and mediate peace talks regarding the Rodaxian civil war. Recent events had left the crew highly stressed, and the decision was made that shore leave was required. On the Enterprises path was the resort world Cesnar, known for its intoxicating atmosphere, and arrangements were made to stop there. However, under the influence of the gases, Captain Pike requested that the Rodaxians move the talks forward, leaving insufficient time for preparations. While the talks went badly, a shuttle explosion was mistaken for the Formal Rite of Peace, resulting a a positive resolution to the peace talks. 
The Enterprise was instructed to retrieve the Federation probe Angelo-1, which had shut down and drifted into Klingon space. The Enterprise was specifically told not be to caught crossing the demarcation line, out of fear of reigniting the war with the Klingons, so the shuttle Cervantes was deployed to collect the probe. 
The Enterprise set course for Vulcan, with a route that took them through Rizonian space, a region of space that had been experiencing increased Orion pirate activity. Due to this, the Enterprise was ordered to reduce speed and take any opportunities that presented themselves to help make the sector safer. 
The Enterprise fell into a slipstream wormhole, causing massive internal damage, widespread casualties, as well as threatening to push the ship past the warp threshold which would destroy it. With nearly all of the deuterium fuel irradiated, the Enterprise experienced critical power shortages. At the suggestion of Nurse Christine Chapel, the Enterprise collected strong force neutrinos from within the wormhole which allowed her to heal the mortally injured Doctor M'Benga while Scott replenished the fuel supply. As the warp threshold was reached, Ortegas was able to crash the ship out of the wormhole and return to a stable warp. During this time, it was revealed that M'Benga was considering leaving the Enterprise and had requested the return of former interim CMO Phil Boyce. Chapel, who had only been a temporary duty assignment, requested a permanent posting to the ship. 
Not long after, the Enterprise took part in the Kiriaki mission. After its conclusion, Trelane visited the Enterprise and trapped Captain Pike in an illusory soap opera. 
The Enterprise was sent to investigate an incredibly rare white hole which Starfleet believed to be connected to a black hole. During the mission, Uhura became linked via quantum entanglement with herself a year in the future aboard the Enterprise under the command of Captain Kirk which was investigating the linked black hole. This resulted in the Enterprise getting caught up in a corrupted alternate timeline and aiding in the birth of the "space baby." Afterwards, the Midwives reverted the timeline, leaving only the two Uhuras with any memory of what had happened. 

=== James T. Kirk's command ===

In 2263, after nearly twenty years of service aboard the Enterprise, Captain Pike was promoted back to fleet captain and left the ship. 
Command of the Enterprise was transferred to Captain James Kirk. Helmsman Erica Ortegas was eventually promoted to captain and became a flight instructor on Bindar XII while La'An Noonien-Singh no longer appeared to be a part of the crew.
That year, the Enterprise was sent to investigate a black hole which was linked to the white hole the ship had investigated in 2262. Ortegas was called back to fly a shuttle to perform readings of the black hole, but both the shuttle and the Enterprise were struck by energy waves from the birth of the "space baby," creating a corrupted alternate timeline in which Montgomery Scott died in 2262 while linking Uhura with her past self by quantum entanglement. After the birth of the space baby, the Midwives reverted the timeline so that no one but the two Uhuras remembered what had occurred. Uhura, Scott and Ortegas were safely beamed off of their exploding shuttle after successfully deploying a probe to gather data on the black hole. 
In 2265, the Enterprise was outfitted with the latest technologies which included a small structural redesign before being assigned to another five-year mission of deep space exploration. 
The ship's primary goal during this mission was to seek out and contact alien life. Kirk's standing orders also included the investigation of all quasars and quasar-like phenomena.
Beyond its primary mission, the Enterprise defended Federation territories from aggression, aided member worlds in crisis, and provided scientific expeditions and colonies in its patrol area with annual examinations and support. 

==== Discoveries ====
From 2265 to 2270, the Enterprise visited over seventy different worlds and encountered representatives of over sixty different species. More than twenty of those were first contacts with beings previously unknown to the Federation, including stellar neighbors like the First Federation and Gorn, voyagers from the Kelvan Empire in distant Andromeda, and powerful non-corporeal entities like the Thasians, Trelane, and the Organians.  Two discovered species were the first known examples of silicon-based lifeforms: the Horta and the Excalbians. 

The Enterprise was the first Federation vessel to survive an encounter with the galactic barrier. The ship's warp drive and other systems, however, were critically damaged (which later prompted the ship to be refit and repaired, changing its appearance slightly for the rest of Kirk's mission) and casualties totaled twelve crewmembers and officers. By stardate 4657.5, the Enterprise was traveling through space in a region hundreds of light years further than any Earth starship had explored. 
The reality of time travel, externally influenced, had been known for over a century, but following two accidental temporal displacements, the Enterprise became the Federation's first deliberately-controlled timeship. Observing the death-throes of Psi 2000, the crew suffered from polywater intoxication and the Enterprise nearly lost orbit after an engine shutdown. A previously untested "cold start", via controlled matter-antimatter implosion, saved the ship, but the high-speed escape from the planet's gravity well caused the ship to travel three days into the past. 

In 2267, while escaping the gravitational pull of a black star, the Enterprise was hurled through space and time to Earth of 1969. The crew developed and executed a method to return to their own time, by warping around the sun's gravity well in a slingshot maneuver. A year later, the Enterprise was ordered to repeat the recently proven slingshot effect, and returned to Earth's past on a mission of historical observation. 

Some missions of discovery confronted Enterprise with entities and mechanisms that threatened great swaths of Federation and neighboring space.
An ancient "planet killer", fueled by the consumption of planets it destroyed with its antiproton weapon, approached Federation population centers in 2267. It required the combined efforts of the Enterprise and its "sister ship", USS Constellation, to destroy the invader. 
One year later, in 2268, a single-cell organism of colossal scale emitted negative energy, toxic to humanoid life, killing the entire Vulcan crew of the USS Intrepid. The Enterprise penetrated the cell interior and destroyed the organism before its imminent cell division threatened to overwhelm the rest of the galaxy. 
In around 2268, the Enterprise visited Planet 0042692 where they detected an approaching catastrophe, but couldn't intervene directly due to the Prime Directive. Ensign David Garrovick volunteered to embark upon a solo mission in the shuttle Galileo, but the Galileo ended up crashing. The Enterprise departed the planet without Garrovick after which no other Starfleet ship would visit that sector for over a hundred years and there was no record of first contact. Having survived the crash, Garrovick predicted that Starfleet would return one day and sent out a distress call. Learning about Starfleet from Garrovick, the planet's natives began calling themselves Enderprizians, named their settlement New Enda-Prize, and adopted versions of the Enterprise crew's names. 

==== Battles ====

The nature of its mission of exploration meant the Enterprise was frequently the only Federation military asset in a little-known, otherwise undefended frontier. When called into harm's way, the ship regularly did so with little chance of immediate support against previously unknown enemies and threats.
Happily, the Enterprise's earliest engagement of its five-year mission, against a deceptively powerful starship called the Fesarius, ended with an amicable first contact with the First Federation in 2266.  Following the destruction of a colony on Cestus III, a surprise attack – from a previously unknown species – led the Enterprise to battle and pursue an evenly matched Gorn starship in 2267. 
The Enterprise played the fox for four of its "sister ships" in a war games problem on stardate 4729.4, as part of a series of M-5 drills. Equipped with the new M-5 multitronic unit computer and stripped of most of its crew, the Enterprise became a killing machine – crippling the USS Excalibur and killing its entire crew – before Kirk could re-assert control. 

===== Klingon engagements =====

Warships of the Imperial Klingon Fleet were frequent opponents of the Enterprise. Commander Kor held the Enterprise and Kirk in high professional regard, and relished the prospect of battle. Lower ranks chose to mock the starship; on one such occasion, Korax compared the vessel to a "garbage scow" before he corrected himself, adding, "It should be hauled away as garbage." 
While Starfleet rallied its forces at the outbreak of a Federation-Klingon War in 2267, the Enterprise was sent forward to secure a border region anchored by the planet Organia. The vessel destroyed a Klingon ship and prepared to engage an approaching Klingon fleet, before the Organian Peace Treaty precluded a full-scale war. 
The Enterprise sporadically engaged Klingons throughout its voyage. A warship failed in an attempt to blockade the Enterprise from Capella IV in 2267. Sabotaged during a diplomatic mission to the Tellun system in 2268, the ship successfully fought off the assault of a harassing D7. The same year, the Enterprise was forced to destroy a battle cruiser that Kang had commanded but had recently abandoned, and the rescued Klingons (influenced by the Beta XII-A entity) subsequently made an unsuccessful attempt to wrest control of the Enterprise from Kirk. 

===== Romulan engagements =====

The Romulan Star Empire re-emerged from a century of isolation to antagonize the Federation with the Neutral Zone Incursion of 2266. The Enterprise responded and was victorious against a new Romulan Bird-of-Prey, which was equipped with a cloaking device and a plasma torpedo system. 
In later encounters, the Romulan fleet used strength of numbers in their efforts to overwhelm the Enterprise. When Commodore Stocker took temporary command and violated the Neutral Zone in 2267, up to ten Birds-of-Prey swarmed and pummeled the starship until Kirk's "corbomite" bluff inspired their withdrawal. 
In 2268, the Enterprise again violated the Neutral Zone – for the purpose of espionage – and was quickly surrounded by three Romulan D7-class battle cruisers. The Enterprise escaped by becoming the first Federation vessel to install and successfully utilize a (stolen) Romulan cloaking device. 
Near Tau Ceti in the following year, Kirk employed the Cochrane deceleration maneuver, allowing the Enterprise to defeat a Romulan vessel. 
In the final year of Kirk's original mission, the ship was ambushed by a trio of Romulan battle cruisers while on a routine survey. The Enterprise managed to escape through an energy field that adversely affected the ship's main computer. The malfunctioning systems were corrected by another pass through the field, this time with the Romulan ships in pursuit. The attackers then became incapacitated by the same computer malfunctions, and the Enterprise managed to escape. (TAS: "The Practical Joker")

=== Refit of the 2270s ===
The Enterprise underwent a major refit into what would later be known as the Constitution II-class. The refitting took eighteen months of work, and essentially a new vessel was built onto the bones of the old, replacing virtually every major system. This ensured Enterprise's continued service for the next several years, enabling the ship to continue to serve in its prominent role.

Refits and overhauls with new technologies after long deployments were far from unusual in the ship's history. However, the Enterprise's overhaul of the early 2270s became a nearly keel-up redesign and reconstruction project. Weapons system upgrades included the phaser banks having power channeled directly from the warp engines. A double photon torpedo/probe launcher was installed atop the secondary hull.
Extra egress points were added for better access/exit from the ship and now included a port-side spacedock hatch, dual ventral space walk bays, four dorsal service hatches, and a standardized docking ring port, which was aft of the bridge on the primary hull. Also included were four more docking ring ports, paired on the port and starboard sides of the launcher and secondary hulls respectively, and service hatch airlocks on the port and starboard sides of the hangar bay's main clam-shell doors. Unlike from before the refit, these new egress points were not covered by retractable hull plating.
A new bridge module reflected the modern computer systems, operating interfaces, and ergonomics that ran throughout the ship. Following Kirk's promotion to rear admiral and posting as Chief of Starfleet Operations, his successor, Captain Will Decker (whom Kirk himself picked to succeed him), oversaw the refit, assisted by chief engineer Commander Montgomery Scott. (Star Trek: The Motion Picture)

=== V'ger ===
After eighteen months in drydock for refit, the Enterprise was pressed into service, weeks ahead of schedule, in response to the V'ger crisis, once again under Kirk's command.
Decker was temporarily demoted to commander and posted as an executive officer because of his familiarity with the new design. Incomplete systems had to be serviced during the vessel's shakedown en route to V'ger, including the first test of the new warp engines.
Shortly after launch, a matter/antimatter intermix malfunction ruptured the warp field and led to the Enterprise entering into an unstable wormhole. Commander Decker belayed an order from Admiral Kirk to destroy an asteroid in their path, which had been dragged into the ruptured warp field along with them, with phasers. The refitted phasers now channeled power directly from the main engines at a point beyond the dilithium/magnatomic-initiator stage.
Because of this refitted function, both the intermix malfunction and the resultant antimatter imbalance within the warp nacelles caused automatic cutoff of the phasers, a design change of which Kirk had not been aware. Decker ordered the use of photon torpedoes, instead; as a backup, they had been designed to draw power from a separate system in case of a major phaser loss. Commander Spock arrived at a timely point and brought correction to the intermix problem. (Star Trek: The Motion Picture)
Once the V'ger threat was averted, Captain Decker was listed as "missing in action", and the Enterprise remained under Admiral Kirk's command for an interim period. (Star Trek: The Motion Picture) At some point, Kirk passed command on to the newly promoted Captain Spock. (Star Trek: The Motion Picture, Star Trek II: The Wrath of Khan)

=== Khan's return ===

In 2285, the Enterprise had been pulled from the front lines of Starfleet and the Federation and, near the end of its life, was relegated to a training vessel. The ship participated in a low-tempo training cycle, based in the Sol system. Admiral Kirk boarded his old command to observe a cadet training cruise.
Meanwhile, Khan Noonien Singh had escaped from exile on Ceti Alpha V and hijacked the USS Reliant, after which he stole the Genesis Device from the Regula I space station.
The Enterprise was tasked to investigate, and Spock deferred his command to Admiral Kirk. Subsequent engagements with Reliant left the ship badly damaged, with cadet and crew deaths, including Captain Spock. (Star Trek II: The Wrath of Khan)

=== Final mission ===

Upon the Enterprise returning to Earth, Starfleet Commander Fleet Admiral Morrow announced that the starship, at that point forty years old and heavily damaged, would be decommissioned. When Morrow denied Kirk requesting permission to return to the Mutara sector, Kirk conspired with his senior officers and stole the Enterprise from Spacedock One, in order to recover Spock's body from the Genesis Planet – to bring it and Spock's katra, the latter possessed by Leonard McCoy, to Mount Seleya on Vulcan. As part of the plan, Kirk had Scott rig up an automation system to run the Enterprise so easily that "a chimpanzee and two trainees" could have handled the craft.

At the Enterprise's destination, the ship was attacked by a Klingon Bird-of-Prey operated by Klingon Commander Kruge, an assault that left the Enterprise disabled; Scotty's automation system was not designed for combat and overloaded when the ship was attacked. After setting the auto-destruct sequence, Kirk and his crew abandoned the ship for the surface of the Genesis Planet. Demolition charges in place on the bridge, and elsewhere throughout the ship, exploded, killing a Klingon boarding party. The battered secondary hull (with what was left of the saucer) fell from orbit and blazingly streaked across the planet's atmosphere. (Star Trek III: The Search for Spock)

Being forty years old at the time of its destruction, the Enterprise had surpassed its designer's original projected eighteen-year endurance by twenty-two years, when the ship was launched back in 2245. DIS: "Brother" graphic display) The next USS Enterprise, a Constitution II-class heavy cruiser USS Enterprise-A, was launched a year later. (Star Trek IV: The Voyage Home)

== Legacy ==

The Enterprise's long history would be remembered for the next century.  
Captain John Harriman of the USS Enterprise-B would learn of Kirk's missions when he was in grade school. (Star Trek Generations)
In 2293, Captain Kirk kept a model of the Enterprise in his quarters aboard the USS Enterprise-A. (Star Trek : The Undiscovered Country)
In the 2310s, a young Jean-Luc Picard kept a model of the Enterprise when he was living in his house in La Barre. 
In 2364, a sculpture of the Enterprise was on display in the observation lounge of the USS Enterprise-D along with other starships named Enterprise. It was later removed in 2368. (Star Trek: The Next Generation )
In 2369, when Montgomery Scott was rescued from the crash landed USS Jenolen, and his surprise that he was found by the USS Enterprise-D, Scott's immediate response was "Enterprise? I should have known. I bet Jim Kirk himself hauled the old girl out of mothballs to come looking for me," even though he, along with Pavel Chekov and Kirk were on the USS Enterprise-B during its maiden voyage when Kirk was presumed killed. 
Between 2373 and 2379, a gold model of the Enterprise was on display along with other Federation starships named Enterprise in the observation lounge of the USS Enterprise-E. (Star Trek: First Contact, Star Trek: Nemesis)
In 2383, Hologram Janeway showed the young crew of the USS Protostar an image of the Enterprise while explaining the history of the Federation and Starfleet to them. 
In 2384, the Protostar responded to Ensign David Garrovick's distress call that had been sent from Planet 0042692 over a hundred years before, discovering that Starfleet had no record of the Enterprise's mission to the planet, but learning of it from the natives, the Enderprizians. The Protostar crew made second contact and fulfilled Garrovick's promise that Starfleet would some day return to save the Enderprizians from the curse of "the Gallows," in reality toxic runoff caused by the crashed shuttle Galileo. 
In 2399, retired Starfleet Admiral Jean-Luc Picard observed holographic images of the original Enterprise from 2258 and the Enterprise-D in the central lobby of Starfleet Headquarters. 
Kirk's use of the slingshot maneuver using the Enterprise in order to travel through time was mentioned as an example by Picard as one of the methods for traveling back through time in order to repair changes made in 2024 by Q. 
In 2401, while in preparations for Frontier Day, the Enterprise was one of several vessels that were shown in the promoting posters of Frontier Day. 

== Alternate timelines and realities ==
In an alternate timeline shown to Commander Michael Burnham by the time crystal, Enterprise failed to destroy the USS Discovery with photon torpedoes after Discovery's auto-destruct didn't go off. As in the current timeline, Enterprise was hit in the saucer section with an undetonated photon torpedo during the Battle near Xahea, which presumably destroyed the ship as Control won in that timeline. Having been shown this vision moments before Enterprise's failure to destroy Discovery, Burnham stopped the crew from even attempting to do so. Later, Admiral Katrina Cornwell sacrificed herself to save Enterprise from destruction by the torpedo which she predicted would kill at least the bridge crew if it went off and maybe everyone else on the ship. Burnham and Spock came to the conclusion that the time crystal had showed her this possible future so that she could change it. 

In an alternate timeline in which Captain Christopher Pike managed to avoid his crippling fate, he was still in command of the Enterprise in 2266 during the Neutral Zone Incursion while James T. Kirk was the captain of the USS Farragut instead. In this timeline, the ship suffered heavy damage from the Romulan warbird and took on the Farragut's survivors before Pike negotiated a cease fire between the two sides. When the Romulans showed up with a full armada in response to the perceived weakness of the Federation, the Enterprise barely escaped, taking severe damage to several more decks and incurring several casualties, most notably Spock. The incident resulted in an endless war between the Federation and the Romulans, before the Pike of this future traveled back in time and showed his past self the consequences of avoiding his fate. 
In an alternate timeline created by the death of Spock at the age of seven, Thelin served as the first officer and science officer of the Enterprise. Upon learning of the damaged timeline that Kirk and Spock decided to correct, Thelin was supportive. Upon hearing that, Spock wished Thelin a long and prosperous life in whatever circumstances the corrected timeline placed him in. (TAS: "Yesteryear")

== Crew ==

=== Complement ===
The number of the Enterprise's crew complement more than doubled over the duration of its service.
In 2254, the Enterprise had a complement of 203, this stated following the loss of three crewmembers at Rigel VII. 

In 2257, scans showed the entire crew complement was also stated to be 203, yet in a display graphic specifically stated that the crew compliment was 430; 43 officers and 387 enlisted. 
In 2265, the Enterprise's complement consisted of "almost a hundred women." 
In 2266, Captain Ramart commented to Charlie Evans that the Enterprise was "like a whole city in space," compared to his ship, the USS Antares, which had a relatively meager complement of twenty. Ramart further explained that there were "over 400 in the crew of a starship," which was clarified by Kirk as actually "428, to be exact." 
Jadzia Dax observed to Benjamin Sisko, after they transported aboard the Enterprise, that "they really packed them in on these old ships." 

=== Casualties ===
Service aboard the Enterprise proved to be hazardous duty. Between 2265 and 2269, individuals who were killed while assigned to the ship included at least fifty-eight officers and crew. Nine crew members were killed when the Enterprise encountered the galactic barrier in 2265. Gary Mitchell, Lee Kelso, and Elizabeth Dehner later died on Delta Vega. 
Two of seven crewmembers assigned to study Murasaki 312 on the shuttlecraft Galileo – Latimer and Gaetano – both met an unfortunate end, by the hand of a large creature on the planet Taurus II. 
In 2267, Enterprise security officers Hendorff, Kaplan, Mallory, and Marple were killed on planet Gamma Trianguli VI.  Further incidents with multiple fatalities included four security guards killed by Nomad in 2267, as well as five security guards killed by a dikironium cloud creature on Argus X in 2268.  An outbreak of Rigelian fever, in 2269, killed three crewmen and imperiled the rest until a source of ryetalyn could be obtained. 
As well, two Enterprise security officers were beamed out into open space while the ship was under the control of Gorgan.  Lieutenant Galloway was vaporized by Captain Tracey on Omega IV,  but later was somehow resurrected.  Yeoman Thompson was reduced to a dry cuboctahedron solid. She was killed when the Kelvan Rojan crushed the object in his hand. 
At some point after stardate 3619.2 in 2268, but before 2270, security officer Ensign David Garrovick vanished along with the shuttlecraft Galileo from the ship. His ultimate fate on Planet 0042692 was not discovered until 2384. 
In the mid-2270s, Commander Sonak and an Enterprise officer were killed in a transporter accident while beaming to the ship. (Star Trek: The Motion Picture)

=== Background information ===

The Enterprise and its interiors in The Original Series were designed primarily by Matt Jefferies. A three-foot demonstration model was completed in November 1964 by the Howard Anderson Company to show to Gene Roddenberry. After getting his approval, an eleven-foot model was then constructed by Richard C. Datin, Jr., Mel Keys, and Vern Sion at Volmer Jensen's model shop, and was finished in December 1964. The eleven-foot model was modified for "Where No Man Has Gone Before" and again for the regular series effect shots. Re-used footage of all three stages of the eleven-foot model's appearance are shown mixed together in TOS.
In the final draft script of "The Naked Time", the Enterprise was somewhat poetically described thus; "Sleek... efficient... the look of man in space... tooled... equipped..."
For Star Trek: The Animated Series, the color of the Enterprise was limited. D.C. Fontana commented, "For the purposes of animation you can't do the light white, silver kinds of colors. So they made the Enterprise gray and it came off all right." (Star Trek: The Magazine Volume 1, Issue 16, p. 68)
Some distinctive effects shots of the Enterprise from TOS were recreated in animation for Star Trek: The Animated Series. Depicting the ship performing any new, impressive maneuvers would have been too costly for TAS and would have taken the animators too long to show, despite frequent TAS Director Hal Sutherland later implying that a desire to portray the ship doing "barrel rolls and that kind of thing" was quite common. (Star Trek: The Magazine Volume 1, Issue 16, pp. 63 & 64)
The 2270s configuration of the Enterprise depicted in the films Star Trek: The Motion Picture, Star Trek II: The Wrath of Khan, and Star Trek III: The Search for Spock was designed by Richard Taylor and Andrew Probert, based on designs for the vessel made by Matt Jefferies for the undeveloped television series Star Trek: Phase II. The design for the movie version was the basis of a design patent issued by the US Patent and Trademark Office.
The Enterprise was to have appeared in Star Trek: The First Adventure, which would have revealed that the design of the ship in TOS was actually a refit; the original design resembled Enterprise NX-01, though that vessel was created years earlier. When Star Trek: Discovery season 1's finale aired, this idea was continued, as when the Enterprise showed up at the end, it had elements of the NX-01 incorporated into the new model.
The Enterprise was recreated as a new physical model for the DS9 Season 5 episode "Trials and Tribble-ations". The ship's interior was represented with sets built on Paramount Stage 11. (Information from Larry Nemecek) The CG model of Constitution-class USS Defiant, created for "In A Mirror, Darkly", was relabeled as the Enterprise for the final scene of "These Are The Voyages...", the last episode of Star Trek: Enterprise.
A new CG model, built from caliper measurements of the original eleven-foot physical model, was created for use in the remastered and recreated version of The Original Series (for more detailed treatises on the studio models used, see Constitution-class model (original).
Visual effects artist Gabriel Koerner created a re-imagined version of the pre-refit Enterprise from the movies. The design is more contemporary, while keeping the design of The Original Series version of the ship. A video showing the ship from various angles can be seen on YouTube. The model was also featured as the August image for the 2007 Ships of the Line calendar, as well as in the Ships of the Line coffee-table book, placed between TOS and TMP images, which included text from Michael Okuda suggesting it as one of the ideas on how to refit the ship.
The Enterprise was pictured on three paintings during the Star Trek franchise: on a painting in the recreation deck of the 2270s configuration of the vessel, in Star Trek: The Motion Picture; on a painting in the waiting area of the sickbay aboard the USS Enterprise-D, in the Star Trek: The Next Generation fifth season episode "Ethics"; and on a painting on the wall of Kirk's kitchen in the Nexus, in Star Trek Generations.
Upon preparing to view the bridge of the Enterprise in the first draft script of TNG: "Relics", Montgomery Scott specified, "Show her the way she was before stardate 5928," referencing the stardate on which TOS series finale "Turnabout Intruder" takes place. Consequently, this line of dialogue would have established that the holographic simulation of the Enterprise's bridge in "Relics" was definitely contemporaneous with the exact setting of TOS. Scott did not specify that in the final draft of the script, however. [3] The line is also not spoken on screen.
The Enterprise was to have been referenced in the first draft script of VOY: "Flashback", in connection with its near-destruction at Eminiar VII. However, all mention of the vessel was eliminated from the episode by the time the final draft of the script was written.
A new CGI model was created for the appearance of the USS Enterprise in the Season 1 finale of Star Trek: Discovery, "Will You Take My Hand?". This model updated the appearance of the USS Enterprise to better match the style of other Starfleet ships depicted in the show, most noticeably changing the Enterprise's nacelles to have visible, glowing blue cutouts on the inner surface. The impulse engine was also different, and the nacelle pylons were swept back with openings in their centers similar to the 2270s configuration. The new model also had a longer "runway" before the shuttlebay doors.
